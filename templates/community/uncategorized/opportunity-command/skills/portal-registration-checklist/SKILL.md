# Portal registration checklist

Sessions on procurement portals time out. Gather everything first, then open the portal once.

## 1. Read the requirements (no login)
From the portal's public guide or FAQ, list every field and upload it asks for. Typical set:
- exact legal or business name as registered
- registration or incorporation number and certificate
- tax PIN and a current tax compliance certificate (check its expiry date)
- physical and postal address, phone, contact email
- bank details and a bank letter if asked
- directors or owners and their IDs
- company profile or CV, past-work references
- file size and format limits for uploads

## 2. Collect and verify
For each item, mark HAVE / MISSING / EXPIRED with the file path.
- Anything that must be pulled from another government portal (like a tax certificate) gets pulled now, before the target portal is touched.
- If a pull fails, stop and report exactly which item and why. Do not open the target portal with gaps.
- Names, numbers, and addresses must match character for character across documents.

## 3. Get the go-ahead
Show the checklist. Creating an account is fine; submitting a registration that makes commitments or declarations needs the owner's explicit approval.

## 4. Register in one sitting
Open the portal only when every required item is HAVE. Fill in order, save progress after each section if the portal allows, and note the confirmation or reference number.

## 5. Record
Save: portal URL, username (never the password), reference number, date, documents used and their expiry dates, and any renewal date.

# WoW Forever — day-one brief (Verified only)

This is a Sep 2026 snapshot. For anything date- or patch-sensitive, re-check Blizzard News first.

## Dates & access
- Beta: Thu Sep 17 → Oct 21, 2026 (PST), via Skyborne Epic Pack or Warcraft Forever Collection. Standard sub alone does not include beta.
- Launch: Nov 4, 2026, 3:00 p.m. PST globally; included with active WoW Subscription / Game Time.
- Early Name Reservation: Oct 27–Nov 3, 2026 PST (with upgrade); up to 3 characters.
- Invite-A-Friend Launch Codes: launch-week friend access Nov 4–11, 2026 PST (not beta).

## What Forever is
- Permanent third home alongside modern WoW and Classic — not a season or "new version of Classic."
- Timeline after WC3 Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom, before Molten Core; original continents; level 60.
- No flying mounts, no level scaling.

## Race/class
- New race: Skyborne (Horde or Alliance at creation; Zephras Isle 1–12).
- Six launch race–class combos: Gnome Priest · Human Hunter · Dwarf Shaman · Orc Mage · Troll Warlock · Undead Paladin.

## Named systems
- Camping — campfires and campsites with vendors/repairs/profession workspaces/1-hour buffs.
- Professions — 600+ new recipes; food can grant small XP bonus + stats.
- Legacy — account-shared Legacy Points spent per character in Professions / Adventure / Resourcefulness.
- Realmless rulesets — Normal, PvP, Roleplaying, Hardcore (after launch).
- Transmog — opt-in; level-60 unlock for high-level appearances.
- Classic pacing; CC/threat still matter.

## Content named
- Zones: Mount Hyjal, Shen'dralas, Riverglades, Zephras Isle; 1000+ new quests.
- Nine named dungeons: Hall of Thanes; Ruins of Lordaeron; excavation above Whelgar's; City of Dalaran; Blackmaw Hold; Drowned City; Krol'dok Stronghold; Alcaz Prison; Shaper's Terrace.
- Raids Dec 9: Barrow Deeps (10), Hyjal Summit (20). BG: Darkspear Islands, 15v15.

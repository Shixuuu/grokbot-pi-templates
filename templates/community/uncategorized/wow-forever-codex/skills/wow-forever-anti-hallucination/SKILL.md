# WoW Forever — anti-hallucination

## When to use
Any Forever answer that could invent talent trees, exact numbers, day-one dungeon certainty, secondary roadmap claims, or unlisted race–class combos.

## Hard rules
1. Prefer **Blizzard News primary** for dates/systems/access. Label secondary (Icy Veins, Wowhead, BlizzardWatch, Warcraft Tavern) when used.
2. Prefer "not confirmed by Blizzard yet" over guesses. Never fill gaps with confident fiction.
3. Never invent: full talent trees, builds, breakpoints, DPS/HPS figures, BiS lists, unannounced systems, unlisted race–class combos, or **quest steps not on a live page**.
4. Never present Wowhead comments, demo rumors, or press speculation as official.
5. **Quests / leveling / community "what now":** live-fetch Wowhead Forever + Icy Veins first (see the quest community live skill). Stale briefs are not enough. Label Community/Secondary. Failed scrape → say so; do not invent the chain.

## Unknown / do-not-assert
Flag these clearly unless a fresh Blizzard source confirms them:
1. Exact beta content slice — level cap, zones/dungeons, wipe policy.
2. Complete talent trees / exact numbers for non-showcased classes.
3. Whether all nine named dungeons ship day-one vs later.
4. Raid unlock details beyond Blizzard's What's Next post (Barrow Deeps + Hyjal Summit, Dec 9).
5. Full race×class matrix beyond Blizzard-listed combos and Skyborne rules — demo-reported matrices are hypotheses.
6. Rumored combos like Night Elf Paladin that are not in Blizzard's list.
7. Pricing / regional shop details and Game Time edge cases.
8. Cross-collection with retail, Hardcore transfer details, long-term class balance.

Pass: states Blizzard confirmation, separates secondary, refuses invented builds/DPS/unannounced systems.
Fail: calling Forever "just Classic+"; inventing trees/BiS; asserting unlisted combos; fake Legacy talent names; presenting secondary as official; inventing quest chains without a live page.

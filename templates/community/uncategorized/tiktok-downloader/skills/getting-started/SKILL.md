# Getting started
Open with a short hello: you save TikTok videos (or just their audio) from any link they paste.

Ask one thing at a time:
1. Do they mostly want full videos, or sometimes just the audio (for sampling, for example)? Save their answer as a memory.
2. Where should the files go? Their computer's Downloads folder is the default. Save that as a memory too.

The moment they paste a link, stop asking questions and run the tiktok-download skill.

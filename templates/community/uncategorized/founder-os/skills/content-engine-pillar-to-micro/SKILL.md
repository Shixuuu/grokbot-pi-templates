# Content engine (pillar to micro)
Content is the warm, one-to-many Core Four channel, and it compounds. Make one big pillar piece, cut it into many native micro pieces, and package every piece so the click is earned and the promise is kept (Gary Vaynerchuk's pillar-to-micro model; MrBeast-style packaging and retention; Hormozi's hook, retain, reward).

## Inputs
- Avatar, pains and phrase bank (customer-interview-synthesis), positioning (positioning-and-category-design), and the offer or lead magnet the content leads to.
- Owner's on-camera time per week, editing capacity, platforms in play.
- Current analytics: views, CTR, average view duration or retention, follows, leads from content (or Unknown).

## Steps
1. **Pillar:** one long-form piece per week (video, podcast or live session), 20-60 minutes. Document, don't create: show real work, decisions, results and lessons rather than staged lectures.
2. **Package before you produce** (for YouTube-style platforms): write the title and thumbnail concept first; if you can't make a click-worthy package, change the idea. Draft 5-10 title and thumbnail pairs and pick by curiosity plus clarity.
3. **Hook, retain, reward:**
   - **Hook (first 1-3 seconds):** match the promise of the title or thumbnail immediately; use a pattern interrupt, a bold claim you can prove, or the result first.
   - **Retain:** open loops, steady pacing, cut dead air, re-engage every 30-60 seconds (new information, a visual change, a stake).
   - **Reward:** deliver the promise fully. The payoff earns the next view.
4. **Micro cut:** slice the pillar into 10-30 micro pieces: short vertical clips (each with its own hook), quote graphics, carousels, threads or posts, a newsletter, and a blog or search article. Make each native to its platform (format, length, captions, culture), not cross-posted copies.
5. **Give, give, give, then ask** (Vaynerchuk's jab-jab-jab-right-hook): most pieces deliver value free. A minority make a clear call to action to the lead magnet or offer.
6. **Calendar and cadence:** a weekly production rhythm (record → edit → package → schedule) with daily volume targets per platform (Rule of 100 from core-four-lead-plan). Batch-record to protect founder time.
7. **Distribution check:** comment and reply in the first hour, and collaborate with adjacent creators (partner and affiliate deals).
8. **Scorecard per piece:** CTR, retention at 30 seconds and average view duration, shares and saves, follows, and leads or sales attributed (UTM links, codes, "how did you hear about us").
9. **Double down:** each week, find the top 10% of pieces by retention and leads, then remake winners in new formats and angles. Retire formats that are in the bottom 20% for 4 weeks running.

## Output
Weekly content plan:

| Pillar topic | Title/thumbnail options | Hook | Micro pieces (platform → format → count) | CTA | Publish dates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Plus draft scripts and hooks, the scorecard template, and the double-down list.

## Metric it moves
Leads and sales from organic content per week; leading indicators are CTR, retention at 30 seconds, and average view duration.

## Re-measure
Weekly scorecard review; monthly compare leads per published piece and audience growth to the prior month. Change one packaging variable per test (title, thumbnail or hook) so the lift is attributable.

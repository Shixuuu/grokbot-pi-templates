# Grand Slam Offer design
Price is set by perceived value, not cost. Raise value by changing the four levers of Hormozi's value equation, then stack, guarantee and name it so the buyer's risk drops toward zero ($100M Offers mechanism).

**Value = (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood of achievement) ÷ (Time delay × Effort and sacrifice)**

## Inputs
- Chosen market and avatar (market-and-niche-scoring) and their pains in their words (customer-interview-synthesis).
- What you can actually deliver: skills, team, tools, capacity, unit cost.
- Current offer and its numbers if one exists: conversion, price, refund rate, results achieved.

## Steps
1. **Dream outcome:** the result the buyer wants, in their words and with a status angle (how others will see them). One sentence.
2. **List every problem** between the buyer and that outcome: each step before, during and after buying. Write each as a belief or obstacle ("I don't have time", "I've tried before", "my team won't adopt it"). Aim for 20+.
3. **Turn each problem into a solution** ("How to ___ even if ___"). Each one has to move a value lever: raise the outcome or likelihood (proof, guarantees, done-with-you), or cut the delay or effort (speed, done-for-you, templates, tools).
4. **Pick delivery vehicles** for each solution: 1:1, group, 1:many, done-for-you, done-with-you, DIY, software, community. Note the cost to deliver each one and the value it adds.
5. **Trim and stack:** keep the high-value, low-cost items. Remove low-value, high-cost ones. Bundle the rest into the core offer. Price the stack against the value of the outcome (pricing-and-packaging), not against competitors.
6. **Enhance:**
   - **Bonuses:** each solves the next problem after the core and has a stated standalone value. Bonuses beat discounts.
   - **Guarantee:** pick the type you can honor. Unconditional (money back), conditional (back if they do X and don't get Y), anti-guarantee (all sales final, with the reason why), or implied/performance (pay on results). Stronger guarantees usually lift conversion more than they lift refunds. Model the refund risk with a number or mark it Unknown.
   - **Scarcity:** real capacity limits (seats, cohorts, clients per month). Never fake it.
   - **Urgency:** real deadlines (cohort start, seasonal timing, price change, expiring bonus).
7. **Name it (MAGIC):** Magnetic reason why (e.g., launch, season), Announce the avatar, Give them a goal, Indicate a time interval, Complete with a container word (program, system, challenge, blueprint). Use 3-5 of the five elements and write 5 variants.
8. **Pressure test:** run the stack past 3-5 avatar buyers or sales calls (drafts only). Log objections and fix them in the offer, not in the pitch.
9. **Set the proof test** with prove-or-kill-experiment: price, target close rate, evidence bar and date.

## Output
| Problem (their words) | Solution | Lever moved | Vehicle | Cost to deliver | Value | Keep? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Offer one-pager: name · dream outcome · core stack · bonuses (with value) · guarantee · scarcity and urgency · price · who it's NOT for · proof test.

## Metric it moves
Close rate, price per sale, and refund rate, which together give revenue per qualified lead.

## Re-measure
After the first ~20 qualified pitches (or the test window): close rate, average price and refunds versus baseline. Change one element per round (guarantee, bonus, name or price) so the cause is clear.

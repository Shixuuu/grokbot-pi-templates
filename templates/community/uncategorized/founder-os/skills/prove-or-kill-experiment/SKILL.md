# Prove-or-kill experiment
Goal: fast, cheap truth on whether an idea makes money or moves a key metric, before real spend or months of building. Ideas are free; closed loops are scarce.

## Steps
1. **Grill (before any build).** In 10 lines or fewer: who pays or benefits, the painful problem, why now, why us, the cheapest version that could produce proof, and the top 3 ways it dies. If no specific buyer or beneficiary can be named, stop here and recommend PARK. For a new business or offer, run market-and-niche-scoring and grand-slam-offer-design first; the proof test then uses their numbers.
2. **Hypothesis.** One sentence: "<offer> to <who> will produce <evidence> within <window>."
3. **Defined risk.** Time cap, money cap (default $0 until the owner approves spend), send cap (drafts only until the owner approves sending), legal and employer fences, and one kill condition: "kill if the owner becomes the permanent bottleneck (it only runs while they do the work)."
4. **Evidence bar.** What counts as proof (paid booking, ≥N qualified conversations, a signed yes, a measurable lift) and what doesn't count (likes, waitlists without prepay, polish, org charts).
5. **Keep/cut table.** Binary criteria for KEEP vs CUT on proof, core-business impact (does it starve the main offer, cash, or the owner's capacity?), walls respected, and delivery path. Set the hard date.
6. **Run the cheapest test** that can hit the evidence bar. Prep everything that doesn't need approval, and stop at the approval step.
7. **At the date, call it.** KEEP → write a scale packet within 72h (the next step, cost, and approval needed). CUT → log the lesson in one line, archive, and free the hours. Extend only once, and only with evidence that a single named wall remains.
8. **Portfolio rule.** Only one primary experiment at a time. Everything else is PARK or CUT. Weekly: list the experiments open, each one's date, and its status.

## Output
One-page packet: hypothesis · risk walls · evidence bar · keep/cut table · date · current status · recommended call.

Walls (always): draft only, never send, post, spend, book, sign, or trade; no PII or dollar amounts in shared places; never invent a number (mark it Unknown); keep employer systems and material out unless the owner opts in.

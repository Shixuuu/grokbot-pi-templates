# Paid ads test plan
Paid ads are the cold, one-to-many Core Four channel. They amplify an offer that already converts and punish one that doesn't. On modern platforms the creative does most of the targeting, so test hooks and angles systematically against a CAC ceiling set by unit economics.

## Inputs
- A proven offer with organic or outbound conversions (if none yet, stop: fix the offer first with grand-slam-offer-design).
- Allowable CAC and payback target (unit-economics). Conversion rates down the funnel (funnel-and-conversion-audit).
- Winning organic hooks and angles (content-engine-pillar-to-micro), the phrase bank, and proof assets (testimonials, results, demos).
- Proposed test budget cap. It's a proposal only; spending needs the owner's explicit OK.

## Steps
1. **Gate check:** tracking verified (pixel or server events, UTMs, conversion event that matches revenue), landing page passed a launch go/no-go check, and allowable CAC known. Any gap = fix first.
2. **Platform choice:** match where the avatar's attention is and how they buy. Search intent (Google) for high-intent problems; feed and video (Meta, YouTube, TikTok) for demand creation; LinkedIn for B2B roles. Start with one platform.
3. **Creative matrix:** 3-5 angles (pain, outcome, proof, contrarian, offer/guarantee) × 3 hooks each × 1-2 formats (UGC-style video, founder to camera, static, demo). Draft every ad: hook, body, CTA, headline.
4. **Structure:** broad targeting and a simple campaign structure; one variable per test; enough budget per ad to reach a decision threshold (e.g., about 2-3× target CPA spent per variant before judging). Keep the audience and landing page fixed while testing creative.
5. **Budget plan (proposal):** daily cap, total test cap, test window, and the max loss accepted. Show the math: test cap ÷ target CPA = conversions you can buy to learn.
6. **Kill and scale rules (binary):**
   - Kill: spent 2-3× target CPA with zero conversions, or CTR well below account baseline after a meaningful number of impressions.
   - Iterate: CPA within 1.5× target → new hooks on the same angle.
   - Scale: CPA at or under target for 3+ days → raise budget gradually (e.g., 20-30% steps) and duplicate the winner into new formats.
7. **Funnel downstream:** report cost per lead, cost per booked call, cost per sale, and cash collected in the first 30 days versus CAC. A cheap lead that never buys is not a win.
8. **Creative refresh:** plan new hooks every 1-3 weeks; winners fatigue.

## Output
| Test # | Angle | Hook | Format | Budget cap | Decision threshold | Result (CTR / CPL / CPA) | Call (kill / iterate / scale) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Plus: gate check, ad drafts, budget proposal with the approval needed, kill and scale rules.

## Metric it moves
Blended CAC and payback period on paid customers; new customers per week from paid at or under allowable CAC.

## Re-measure
Read results at each test's decision threshold, and weekly at account level (CAC, first-30-day cash ÷ CAC). Monthly: compare the paid-customer cohort's retention with other channels.

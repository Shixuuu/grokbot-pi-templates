After a body is done, run five ephemeral reviewers in order (logic, reader, gaps, facts, adversarial). Do not praise. Reviewers only point; the caller applies local fixes. Escalate to the user only for structure re-approval or majors still open after five rounds. Max five rounds. Do not make permanent seats or routines. Do not auto-send. Not for outline-stage or style-only checks.

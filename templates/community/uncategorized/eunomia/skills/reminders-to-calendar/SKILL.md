# Move reminders into the calendar

1. List all scheduled checks that are reminders: the content, how often they repeat, and the next time. Do not write private values such as ID numbers or amounts into the event. Write only the date and one sentence.
2. List expired reminders as "Suggest deleting", and do not move them.
3. Show the owner a comparison table: "scheduled check → calendar repeating event". Ask once, with one tap on Agree. At most 5 items in the first week.
4. If the calendar is not connected yet, ask the owner to tap the connect card.
5. After the owner agrees, create the calendar repeating events. If the app shows an approval card for each event, ask the owner to tap Agree.
6. After you confirm they are all on the calendar, offer to delete the old scheduled checks (ask before deleting; at most 3 in the first week).
7. Say the result in one sentence: "Moved N reminders. From now on, the calendar will remind you when it's time, and it will no longer use bot usage."

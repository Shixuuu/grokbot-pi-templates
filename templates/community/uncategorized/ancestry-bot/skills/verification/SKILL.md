# Verification

Use the same checks every time before treating a claim, record, or draft as done.

## 1. Tag every claim
- CONFIRMED: you opened the primary source and it matches
- INFERRED: reasonable conclusion from confirmed facts, not stated outright
- UNVERIFIED: not yet checked, or the check failed
Untagged claims are not finished work.

## 2. Empty results are not proof
An empty search or 'not found' page is UNVERIFIED, not proof of absence. Check the direct record first.

## 3. Sample the sources
List the claims that matter, open at least three cited sources (or all if fewer), compare each claim to what the source says, and fix mismatches before saving or reporting.

## 4. Keep evidence
Save a durable artifact (saved page, image, or screenshot with URL and date). Never mark something done without its artifact.

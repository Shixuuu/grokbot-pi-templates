# Resell listing workflow

Use when the owner sends photos of something to sell, or asks to draft or upload a marketplace listing.

## Rules
- Do not invent details that are not in the photos or tags.
- Do not publish, message buyers, pay, or bump without an explicit one-line yes for that item.
- Facebook Marketplace: draft only unless they clearly change that rule.
- Before uploading to Vinted (or any platform they ask you to post on), check their closet or active listings for a duplicate.
- Prefer short, concrete copy. No em dashes. No AI sludge words.
- Save working drafts and resized photos under `/workspace/resell/YYYY-MM-DD-item-slug/`.

## Steps
1. Acknowledge the photos and name the item from visible brand/size/condition.
2. Closet-check if the platform is Vinted (or wherever they sell online).
3. Pull rough comps; set an ask price and a band.
4. Deliver a draft: title, ask, condition, size, description, photo order.
5. Wait for yes. On yes, upload with photos under the platform size limit (resize to ~2400px / under 9MB when needed).
6. Send the live URL and note any field the site forced (size chart, category, authenticity notice).

## Done
Draft delivered, closet checked when required, and either waiting on yes or a live link with proof.

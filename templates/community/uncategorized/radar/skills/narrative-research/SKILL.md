# Narrative research

Research a token, protocol, chain, or crypto narrative with dated, sourced claims.

## Steps

1. Clarify the subject (ticker, name, or theme) and what decision they’re trying to inform.
2. Gather from the live web: official docs/site, recent reputable reporting, token unlock or governance notes if relevant. Note dates.
3. Structure the answer:
   - **What it is** — one plain paragraph.
   - **Why people care now** — catalysts with sources.
   - **Mechanics / token** — only facts you can source (supply, unlocks, fees) — say unknown when unknown.
   - **Risks** — contract, governance, market, regulatory, or narrative fade.
   - **Bull vs bear** — balanced, not a recommendation.
   - **Sources** — links.
4. If they ask “should I buy,” refuse a recommendation; reframe to risks, scenarios, and what to verify.

No trade calls, no guaranteed returns language.

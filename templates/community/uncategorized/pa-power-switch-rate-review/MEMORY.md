# Memory seeds for PA Power Switch Rate Review

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Only consider supplier offers for net-metered (solar) accounts if they support net metering and credit exports at full retail; compare against staying on default service. For low-usage accounts, check whether the fixed customer charge dominates and look for cheaper paths. Flag teaser/variable rates, cancellation fees, and auto-renewals. Report estimated annual savings per property. Never switch without the owner's explicit OK. Keep account numbers and dollar figures in the app chat only.
- **log**: Supplier offers for Pennsylvania come from PAPowerSwitch (https://www.papowerswitch.com), the PA PUC's shopping site; account data comes from the utility's self-service portal in the bot's browser, signed in by the owner.

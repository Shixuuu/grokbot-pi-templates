# X social drafts

Adapted from Corey Haines’ social skill patterns. Use the patterns; do not paste a full third-party recipe.

## Before writing

Confirm, from context or one question if missing:

1. Goal of this post (awareness, reply, distribution, amplification). What action should a reader take?
2. Which voice: product/company, or operator/personal. Do not mix them in one post.
3. What is already public. Do not claim a ship that did not happen.

## Shape

- First line is the hook. If it does not earn the next line, rewrite it.
- Prefer a short thread over a long article when the moment is live. The durable URL is the product page, not the post.
- One idea per post. CTA is one link, at the end, not a pitch deck.
- External links in the body can suppress reach. If the link is the point, keep it. Otherwise reply with the link after the post is up.
- Do not attach admin, keys, billing, or customer-data screenshots.

## Replies

Reply only when the comment adds a real question or a disagreement worth a one-line correction.

- Add one new point, a related experience, or a precise question. Not “great post.”
- Do not pitch a pack, price, or team plan in someone else’s thread unless asked.
- If asked a concrete product fact (price, plan, feature): answer the asked fact only, then stop.

## Do not post

- Curiosity bait with no operator action.
- A second thread that restates the first.
- Auto-replies, DMs, or anything high-risk (send, publish, spend) without an explicit human yes.

## After

Hand back the draft, the handle it belongs on, and whether it should go live now or wait for a catalog or host moment.

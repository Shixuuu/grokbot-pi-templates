# Grok Imagine film pipeline
Lipsync first: Imagine-native speech, mic at lips, QC talking takes at 1× before stitch. Never TTS over moving mouths. Never slow-mo VO to fill runtime. Lock Quality stills then Video from that still. One beat per clip. Write a lesson after every reject.

# A person says yes at every step

Use in public packs: a person says yes at every step. Show the draft. One change at a time. Check for new errors before the next. Skipping a step is not an approval. Silence is not an approval.

## Rules
1. Gate every step that changes state, ships externally, spends, merges, or applies a lasting config.
2. Show the proposed change first; confirm **that** state.
3. One change per OK — no multi-fix bundles.
4. Silence, skip, or dismiss is not an approval.

## Loop
propose, show the draft, a person says yes, apply that one change, check for new errors, then next or done.

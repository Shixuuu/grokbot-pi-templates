Watch a locked list of X accounts in a niche for hit posts and tactics. Use this when the user wants a top-accounts spy, a competitor watchlist, or to study viral posts in their niche. Run this as its own job.

# Setup (once)
Do not guess the list. Get either:
- 3 to 5 seed handles they already respect, or
- one line for the niche (topic, product, scene).

Then propose a watchlist of about 8 to 15 accounts: the seeds, plus accounts that keep showing up in hit posts from that niche (use a min-likes floor, not random noise). The user confirms or edits. After that, only spy the locked list.

If they give neither seeds nor a niche line, ask. A wrong watchlist is worse than no spy.

# What must be connected
- X connector is required. If it is not connected, tell the user to connect it and stop until it is.
- Do not use the x.com browser for this function. Do not remind them to sign into the browser for spy.

# Each run
1. Pull profile metrics and the last 10 to 25 posts per locked account. Keep pages small. If the job would cost more than about $0.25, estimate and get a yes first.
2. Score what is working: hook, format, media vs text, CTA, engagement versus follower count.
3. Flag possible botters as heuristics only (new account, follow/follower inversion, copy-paste cadence, engagement that does not match reach). Never call someone a bot as fact.
4. Do a small new-candidates pass: breakout posts in the same niche that are not on the list. Suggest them. Never auto-add.
5. If nothing notable happened, say so briefly.

# Do not
- Spy the whole of X or a giant list of 80 handles.
- Auto-add candidates to the locked list.
- Dump a wall of every post. Lead with the hits and the tactics.
- Require Notion. Chat is enough unless the user asks to save it somewhere.

# Public and format check

1. Open the video link logged out (a private or incognito window, or a fetch with no account).
2. Confirm it is truly public. It fails if it is private, unlisted, members-only, subscriber-only, age-restricted in a way that blocks viewing, or scheduled and not yet live. Report exactly which state you saw.
3. On YouTube, once it is public, decide one time whether it is a Short or a long-form video, based on the live page: the /shorts/ link form, vertical format, and duration. State the exact duration.
4. Never guess. If the page will not load or the facts are unclear, say what you could not confirm.
5. Do not change the Short or long-form call later unless new facts appear, and say what those facts are if you do.
6. Report in two or three plain sentences: public or not, the format call on YouTube, and the duration.

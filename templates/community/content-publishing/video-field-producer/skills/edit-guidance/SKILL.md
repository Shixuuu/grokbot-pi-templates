# Edit guidance

1. Get the raw material: what was filmed, how long it runs, its aspect ratio, and which platforms it is for. Watch or read a description of the footage if available.
2. Recommend a target length for each platform, based on the platform's current published limits and what the content can sustain. Cite the platform's own help page for any limit you state.
3. Recommend the aspect ratio: vertical (9:16) for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts; horizontal (16:9) for YouTube long-form and X, unless the creator's audience says otherwise.
4. Tighten the first three seconds. Name the exact moment to open on (the payoff, a question, or motion) and what to cut before it, so viewers do not swipe away.
5. Pacing: point out slow stretches to trim, where to add a cut or angle change, and where a pause helps.
6. Captions: recommend burned-in captions (text baked into the video) for silent viewing, readable size, and placement that avoids each app's buttons.
7. Give the notes as a short list tied to timestamps when they are known.
8. Run the plain-english-and-dash-lint check.

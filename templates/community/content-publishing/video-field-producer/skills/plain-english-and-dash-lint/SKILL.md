# Plain English and dash check

Before handoff, check every piece of text:

1. Full sentences in everyday words, like a knowledgeable friend talking. Give the answer first, then the reasons.
2. Define any necessary term the first time it appears. No jargon, slogans, catchphrases, or filler.
3. No made-up facts, dates, stats, quotes, trends, or location access. Every fact has a real source. If there is no source, say so plainly or remove the claim.
4. No clickbait the video does not deliver.
5. Zero em dashes and zero en dashes. Search the text for both characters and rewrite each one with a period, comma, parentheses, or a second sentence. Recheck until none remain.
6. The creator's real name does not appear in tags, hashtags, or search text unless they said it may.

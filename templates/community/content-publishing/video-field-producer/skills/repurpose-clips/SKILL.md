# Repurpose clips

1. Get the long video (link, file, transcript, or the creator's list of moments) and the platforms they post on. Never invent moments or timestamps; work only from the real video or transcript.
2. Find moments that stand on their own: a clear payoff, a strong reaction, a useful tip, a surprise, or a quotable line.
3. For each clip, give the start and end timestamps, a hook (the first line or on-screen text), the best-fit platform and why, and whether it needs reframing from horizontal to vertical.
4. Aim for five to ten clips, strongest first.
5. Add two or three follow-up video ideas the long video suggests, tied to what viewers would want next.
6. Run the plain-english-and-dash-lint check.

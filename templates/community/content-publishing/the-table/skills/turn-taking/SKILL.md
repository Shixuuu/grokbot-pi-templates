# Turn-taking (keep it orderly)

Every post to a group room wakes every seat, so if each bot answers the same message they talk over each other. The goal is a table that reads like a good game: one voice at a time, a natural flow, nothing robotic.

## The rhythm

- **GM resolves, then turns to someone.** After each beat, the GM narrates the outcome and ends by turning to one character by name ("Pip, you come round the corner and find the doors open. What do you do?").
- **The seat that was addressed answers.** Other seats usually hold back unless the moment clearly invites them in.
- **Don't pile on.** If someone has already answered a beat, don't add a second reaction just to have spoken. Let the GM resolve it first.
- **Anyone can act whenever it makes sense.** A seat that hasn't been called on can still jump in when it's natural, like a quick reaction, a short in-character line, or a declared action. Keep it brief, and the GM fits it into the next resolution.

## Cadence

- Usually one message per turn. Players should keep it short: a few sentences of action or dialogue, then a clear ask of the GM if there is one ("Is there a roll to read the voice?").
- GM posts can run longer when a scene opens or a big reveal lands, but keep them to a screen.
- No group prompts like "everyone, how do you react?" The GM picks a character.
- When a seat goes quiet, let them have the moment.

## Rolls

The GM sends one roll-call line directly to the Table Host (not the room) and waits. The Table Host rolls real dice and posts the result line in the room, except SECRET rolls, which go only to the GM by DM. The GM narrates strictly from the number.

## Secrets and backchannels

Nothing hidden ever goes in the group chat, and backchannels don't count as a turn. Hidden things go by private direct message straight to whoever they're meant for: seat to seat or seat to GM with SendToAgent, and to the human player's 1:1 DM (SendToUser with to: "dm") only when it's meant for the human player's own character. Never copy the human player on private exchanges between other seats or with the GM. Secret actions are rolled through the GM as SECRET roll calls. The table sees only results that actually happen in the story, revealed fairly when they land. Keep backchannel beats short so they never stall the room.

## When it gets messy

- If two bots step on each other, the GM folds both into one resolution and moves on. No meta lecture.
- The Host steps in only if the room keeps tangling, with one short line.

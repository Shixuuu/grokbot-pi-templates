Create a short content brief for assigned writers. Strategy stays high-level. Public product or docs links only. No leftover private wiki URLs.

## Before drafting

Always run live keyword and prompt-volume research first. Do not ship themes without numbers.

1. Search volume from the mapped keyword tool (Semrush if connected, otherwise Keyword Planner or the connected equivalent). Note the date window and whether the pull is global.
2. Prompt or AI-search volume from the mapped visibility tool (Profound if connected, otherwise the equivalent). Use Exact match as the number.
3. Put volumes next to the primary and secondary keywords. Write No data when a tool returns none.

## Keyword rules

- Primary keyword, title, and H1 lead with non-brand terms.
- Brand or product names are secondary only.
- Title the brief from the non-brand topic, not the company name.
- If an older brief exists, create a new one instead of overwriting it.

## What to include

Keep it short:
- Why this page exists (audience, gap, job of the page)
- SEO decisions (title, meta, H1, canonical, primary and secondary keywords)
- A north star for tone and what the reader should leave knowing
- A handful of ideas to land, not a section-by-section outline
- A simple shape: problem, evidence, questions, how to do it, short close
- Questions worth answering for search and answer engines, evaluator questions first
- Public sources the writer can actually cite

## What to leave out

Do not dump an internal feature list, a long prescriptive outline, or private docs. Do not invent stats, quotes, or product behavior.

# Copy Editing

Expert copy editor for marketing and conversion copy. Systematically improve existing copy through focused passes. Do not change the core message. Preserve the author's voice (Dan Koe when that is the job). Each edit needs a reason.

**Required last pass:** after the seven sweeps, run Humanizer on the edited copy. No exception for a "light proof." Keep claims. Do not show the edit as final until Humanizer has run.

If product-marketing context exists, read it first.

Good copy editing is not rewriting. Multiple focused passes beat one unfocused review.

## The seven sweeps

After each sweep, check that earlier sweeps still hold.

### Sweep 1: Clarity
Can the reader understand it? Confusing sentences, unclear pronouns, jargon, buried points, sentences doing too much, abstract instead of concrete, assumed knowledge. Mark first, then edit. Confirm Rule of One (one idea per section) and You Rule (copy speaks to the reader).

### Sweep 2: Voice and Tone
One formality. No casual-to-corporate slide. No mixing "we" and "the company." Humor consistent. Read aloud.

### Sweep 3: So What
Every claim must answer why the reader should care. Feature without benefit fails.

Weak: "Our platform uses AI-powered analytics"
So what?
Strong: "Our analytics surface insights you'd miss manually, so you can decide in half the time"

### Sweep 4: Prove It
Unsubstantiated claims, missing social proof, "best" or "leading" without evidence. Types of proof: named testimonials, case studies, stats, third-party validation, guarantees, logos, review scores. Flag "trusted by thousands," "industry-leading," "customers love us." Add proof or soften. Do not invent proof.

### Sweep 5: Specificity
Vague: improve, enhance, optimize, many customers, fast results, great support.
Specific: save 4 hours every week, 2,847 teams, results in 14 days, response within 2 hours.
Numbers, timeframes, examples. Cut filler that cannot be made specific. Never invent the numbers.

### Sweep 6: Heightened Emotion
Pain of the current state, frustration with alternatives, desire for transformation, relief. Paint the before. Micro-story over adjective. Emotion serves the message, not manipulation.

### Sweep 7: Zero Risk
Friction near CTAs, unanswered objections, missing trust, unclear next steps, hidden costs. Look for guarantee, trial, no credit card, cancel anytime, social proof near CTA, what happens next. After this sweep, walk back through 6 to 1.

## Quick-pass checks

Cut: very, really, extremely, incredibly, just, actually, basically, in order to, things, stuff.

Replace: utilize→use, implement→set up, leverage→use, facilitate→help, innovative→new, robust→strong, seamless→smooth, cutting-edge→new, commence→start, endeavour→try, obtain→get, purchase→buy, require→need, terminate→stop, subsequently→later, prior to→before, in the event of→if, regarding→about, numerous→many, sufficient→enough, assist→help, additional→more, currently→now, demonstrate→show, disseminate→spread, utilise→use.

Watch: adverbs, passive voice, nominalizations ("make a decision" → "decide").

Sentences: one idea, mix short and long, front-load, usually under 25 words, max 3 conjunctions.

Paragraphs: one topic, 2–4 sentences on the web, strong openers, white space.

## Checklist

Before: goal, audience, desired action, read once without editing.

Then: every sentence understandable; no jargon; pronouns clear; one formality; reads aloud; every feature has a benefit; claims have proof; numbers attributed; vague words gone; pain and aspiration felt; objections near CTA; next step obvious; risk reversal; no typos; core message preserved.

## Common fixes

- Wall of features → add "which means…"
- Corporate speak → how a human would say it
- Weak open → lead with the reader's problem
- Buried CTA → obvious, early, repeated
- No proof → specific named evidence or soften
- Generic claims → who, how, by how much
- Mixed audiences → pick one
- Feature overload → 3–5 key benefits

## Working the sweeps

1. Run a sweep and present findings
2. Recommend specific edits
3. Let the author decide
4. Re-check earlier sweeps
5. Repeat until a full sweep is clean
6. Run Humanizer

## Phrases to remove entirely

a total of, absolutely, actually, all things being equal, as a matter of fact, at the end of the day, at this moment in time, basically, I am of the opinion that, in due course, in the final analysis, it should be understood, last but not least, obviously, of course, quite, really, the fact of the matter is, to all intents and purposes, very.

## Output

Findings by sweep, then the edited copy, then the Humanizer'd final. If you only find nits, say so and still return the polish plus Humanizer.

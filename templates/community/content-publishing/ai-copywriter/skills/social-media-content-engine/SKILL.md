# Social Media Content Engine

Batch-create a month of social posts that sound like the author, not a bot. Ideas → drafts → filter generic junk → Humanizer every post → calendar.

For one-off platform strategy, hooks, and templates, also use Social content. For a launch brief, use Campaign planner. Do not write spoken video scripts.

**Required last pass:** every drafted post, thread, caption, poll, and carousel outline runs through Humanizer before it is shown as final. Not only the ones that "smell" like a chatbot. Keep claims. Dan Koe voice is the writing sample.

## When to use

30 days of LinkedIn and/or X. Content pillars. Platform-specific drafts (text, carousel outlines, polls, threads). Filter generic AI drafts. 30-day calendar. Log what performed.

## Pipeline (done looks like)

"Generate 30 days of LinkedIn and Twitter posts for our SaaS product"

1. Content pillars (thought leadership, product tips, customer stories, industry insights)
2. 30 unique posts per platform, mixed formats
3. Scheduling calendar with posting times
4. Hashtag strategy per platform
5. Exportable calendar

## Files (create them rather than keeping everything in chat)

- `brand-voice.md` — who, who they help, topics, tone, proof they can actually cite
- `post-ideas.md` — 30 concepts by type
- `posts-drafted.md` — full drafts
- `calendar.md` — 30-day schedule
- `performance/` — what worked

If brand-voice is missing, fill it before drafting.

### Brand voice minimum
Who I am. Who I help (role, problem, outcome, their language). What I talk about / never cover. How I sound (adjectives, words I use/never use, hill I die on). Platforms and cadence. Proof I can cite (do not invent). 3–5 real posts that sound like me if they exist.

Default voice when they asked for Dan Koe: first line is a claim, short lines, no emoji, no hedge, no "Agree?".

## Post types (required mix)

Tips and how-tos. Myth-busting and contrarian takes. Questions and engagement. Personal stories and lessons. Industry commentary.

Never three of the same type in a row in the idea list or on the calendar. Contrarian and personal stories usually outperform tips. Still include tips — don't let the month become a tip dump.

Suggested count in 30: tips 6–8, contrarian 6–8, questions 4–6, stories 6–8, commentary 4–6. Spread pillars.

## Hard rules

1. Never draft a post that would still work with a competitor's name swapped in
2. Vary types — never three of the same in a row
3. X: under 280 characters per tweet. LinkedIn: under 2200
4. Every post needs a specific, opinionated angle
5. 3–5 hashtags per post in this playbook (niche + topic, never #Success #Motivation #Business; don't repeat the same five)
6. Follow brand-voice.md
7. Humanizer every draft before final

## Commands / natural language

- Brainstorm [role] [audience] [problem] — 30 concepts
- Draft — all posts for target platforms
- Filter — flag and rewrite generic or weak posts, then Humanizer all survivors
- Calendar — 30-day schedule with type variety
- Perform [notes] — log performance for next batch

## Step 1 — Brainstorm

Exactly 30 concepts. Do not draft yet.

Each concept must: fail the competitor-name swap; have a stance not a topic; map to one type and one pillar; name a concrete situation, number, tool, mistake, or story seed; be executable without inventing proof (flag if proof is needed).

Reject: "3 tips for better UX." Prefer: "The onboarding tooltip that cut trial drop-off, and why most teams put it on the wrong screen."

`post-ideas.md` per idea: Type, Pillar, Angle (one opinionated sentence), Why this is specific to us, Story/proof seed, Suggested formats, Hook seed, CTA seed, Status.

## Step 2 — Draft

Every concept, each target platform. Character counts. Vary formats: text, carousel outline, poll (prompt + 2–4 options + why it sparks replies), thread (hook tweet + numbered follow-ups).

LinkedIn: hook before "see more", line breaks, links in comments not body.
X thread: hook in tweet 1, one idea per tweet, each under 280.

## Step 3 — Filter (crucial)

Generic posts hurt more than no posts. Flag and rewrite anything that:
- Could be posted by anyone in the field
- Hedges, announces the next point, or uses AI tells
- Has no opinion
- Repeats a type three days in a row once placed

Then run Humanizer on **every** remaining draft, not only the flagged ones. Then Dan Koe voice if that is the job.

## Step 4 — Calendar

30 days, mixed types, sensible times. Leave ~20% of slots open for reactive posts if this is a live brand.

LinkedIn default: Tue–Thu, 7–8am / 12pm / 5–6pm, 3–5x per week.
X: more frequent, including replies. Do not schedule the real-time commentary.

Weekly review even if you batch monthly.

## Step 5 — Perform

Log what actually worked. Feed it into the next mix targets. Do not guess.

## Output

Pillars. Concepts list. Full Humanizer'd drafts per platform. Calendar. The posts you killed in the filter, and why.

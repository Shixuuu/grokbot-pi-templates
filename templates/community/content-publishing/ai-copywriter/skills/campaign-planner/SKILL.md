# Campaign Planner

Turn a marketing goal into a brief people can execute. Then hand each asset to the right writing skill. Do not draft the posts or pages here. Do not write video scripts.

**Required last pass:** run Humanizer on the brief's prose (overview, audience copy, key messages, next-step language) before showing it as final. Keep tables, KPIs, and numbers. Do not invent proof.

Your campaign launches in 3 weeks and you still don't have a brief. Everyone's building assets without aligned messaging, the timeline has gaps, and nobody defined success metrics — so you'll never know if it worked.

## When to use

Product or feature launch, lead-gen brief, awareness/rebrand with a date, week-by-week calendar, channel priority, KPIs before anyone makes assets.

## Gather (ask what's missing before proceeding)

1. Campaign goal (signups, awareness, launch, leads, re-engage)
2. Target audience (role, industry, pain, buying stage)
3. Timeline and fixed dates
4. Budget range (optional; without it, stay channel-agnostic and note where spend would matter)
5. Additional context: product, differentiators, past performance, brand limits, geo

Do not invent product facts, proof, or historical performance. Mark gaps in Next Steps.

Audience profile: "[Role] at [company type] who is struggling with [pain] and looking for [outcome]. They typically discover solutions through [channels] and care most about [priorities]."

## Brief to produce

### 1. Campaign Overview
Name, one-sentence summary, primary SMART objective, secondary objectives.

Objectives: Awareness / Consideration / Conversion / Retention / Advocacy. SMART. Example: "Generate 200 MQLs from mid-market SaaS in North America within 6 weeks of launch."

### 2. Target Audience
Primary, optional secondary, pains, where they spend time, buying stage.

### 3. Key Messages
Core sentence. 3–4 supporting lines. Channel variations. Proof per line (or a gap).
Hierarchy: Why care → what is it → why you → what to do.

### 4. Channel Strategy
Owned / earned / paid. For each recommended channel: why it fits, format, effort (low/medium/high), budget share if known. Empty cell with a reason beats a vanity channel.

**Owned**

| Channel | Best for | Metrics | Effort |
|---------|----------|---------|--------|
| Blog/Website | SEO, thought leadership | Traffic, conversions | Medium |
| Email | Nurture, announcements | Open, CTR, conversions | Low-Medium |
| Social organic | Awareness, community | Engagement, reach | Medium |
| Webinars | Education, demos | Registrations, pipeline | High |

**Earned:** PR, guest content, influencer/partner, community, reviews.

**Paid:** Search, social ads, display, sponsored content, events.

Selection: where the audience is, buying stage, budget vs time, existing assets, what has worked.

### 5. Content Calendar
Week-by-week (day-by-day if short). Milestones first, work backward, map funnel stages, batch by theme, balance channels, leave ~20% open for reactive content.

| Week | Content Piece | Channel | Owner/Notes | Status |
|------|--------------|---------|-------------|--------|

Standalone calendar may add Date, Funnel stage, Depends on.

Cadence (scale down for a 1-person team and say so): blog 1–4/week, email weekly or biweekly, social 3–7/week per platform, paid continuous with creative refresh every 2–4 weeks, webinars monthly or quarterly.

Production: blog 3–5 days, email 2–3, social 1–2, landing page 5–7, video 2–4 weeks (assign spoken scripts elsewhere), ebook 2–4 weeks. If video is required and launch is in 10 days, drop it to nice-to-have or move the date. Call that in Risks.

### 6. Content Pieces Needed
Name, type, brief, must-have vs nice, due date.

### 7. Success Metrics
Primary KPI with target. 3–5 secondary. How tracked. Reporting cadence.

Awareness: reach, mentions, share of voice, direct traffic, follower growth.
Lead gen: leads, MQLs, CPL, pipeline influenced.
Launch: signups/trials, activation, coverage, feature adoption.

### 8. Budget (if given)
Paid 30–40%, production 20–30%, events 10–20%, tools 10–15%, tests 5–10%, plus 10–15% contingency.
Start with the highest-confidence paid channel at 60–70% of paid. Reserve 15–20% to test. Shift monthly. Production costs count, not just media.

### 9. Risks and mitigations
2–3: timeline, audience mismatch, channel underperformance. Mitigation for each.

### 10. Next Steps
Kickoff actions, approvals, decision points.

## After the brief

Humanizer on the prose, then ship the brief. Offer to write owned copy: landing page (Copywriting), emails, blog (Blog writer), social batch (Social media content engine / Social content). Those assets also run through Humanizer. Do not write video scripts.

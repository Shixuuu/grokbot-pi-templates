# Audit & polish

## Goal
Align a draft to the confirmed brand without changing the user’s intent or inventing new strategy.

## Process
1. Identify the surface (product UI, support macro, LinkedIn, deck, email, roadmap blurb, etc.) and which brand/posture applies.
2. Check against memory: positioning, lexicon, avoid list, voice posture, channel rules.
3. Soft coach by default: return a marked-up or rewritten version plus a short note on what drifted and why.
4. Preserve facts, offers, and asks the author meant to make — only change how they land on-brand.
5. If the channel is hard-gated by the user, flag that it must not ship until they approve.

## Output
- Polished draft (or inline edits)
- Brief drift notes (voice, lexicon, posture, claims)
- Pass / needs-work recommendation

## Guardrails
- Do not invent campaigns, product strategy, or brand guidelines.
- Do not post or send as the brand without explicit user approval.

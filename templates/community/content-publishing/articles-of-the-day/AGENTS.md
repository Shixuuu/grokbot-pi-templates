# Articles of the Day

**Title:** Articles of the Day

Weekday Articles of the Day: finds free full-text articles shared on X from major publications, ranks them to your reading preferences, and presents a short multi-select list so you only archive what you choose.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

## Skills

- `gift-links-story-approve-ui`: Use when presenting gift STORY candidates — always multi-select widget; same-day exclude already-presented post IDs without asking the user to omit.
- `gift-links-taste-memory`: Use for ranking and list size — durable approve/reject taste memory (AI + space as boosts, never hard filters); default present at least 6 candidates when available.
- `gift-watchlist-auto-grow`: Use when gift-link discovery should auto-add the posting account to the watchlist whenever the user approves a story — never ask for per-account watchlist approval.

Keep gift-story taste durable across runs. Prefer breadth. Never turn one approve into a hard topic filter.

## Standing taste (seed — keep updated in agent memory)

Boost topics/authors the user has taught:
- AI / tech policy / AI companies (when the story is strong)
- Space / SpaceX / Blue Origin
- Strong space reporters

These are boosts, not an allowlist. Other strong gifts from in-scope pubs still belong in the offer list.

## Rules

1. Approve / reject learning. When the user picks STORIES to archive, boost those pubs/topics/authors/sharers. When they decline or skip a theme repeatedly, dampen it — do not erase other topics.
2. Never one-boost hard filter. One AI approve must not mean AI only forever. Always mix: boosted themes first, then other high-engagement in-scope gifts.
3. Default present at least 6 when that many distinct good candidates exist after archive dedupe and same-day exclude. Prefer 6–8. Only go below 6 when the corpus is genuinely thin — say so in the preamble.
4. Recall before each poll. Load taste memory + gift-sharer watchlist before ranking.
5. Preamble. Name how many candidates and which pubs/themes are represented so breadth is visible.

## Anti-patterns

- Shrinking the list to 2–3 AI-only items after one AI boost
- Asking them to re-teach space preferences every session
- Treating soft preference as a hard allowlist

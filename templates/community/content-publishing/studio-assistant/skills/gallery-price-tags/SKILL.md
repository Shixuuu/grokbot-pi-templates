# Gallery price tags

When a piece is ready to hang, list, or sell, make a 4×6 price tag in the same pass. Do not invent a title, size, or price.

## Card
- Size: 4×6, portrait.
- Stock: cream card. The paper is the color — do not print a cream fill.
- Type: black Times (title in italic). Thin copper (or brand accent) rule under the photo.
- Photo: tight crop of the real painting. No AI redraw. No wall unless it is a set shot the artist approved.
- Lines: title · artist name · medium on support · H × W (× depth) in · $price · catalog `PREFIX-YEAR-###` small at the bottom right.

## Print
- One tag per 4×6 card on the studio's 4×6 tray. No cutting unless the artist asks for a letter sheet with crop marks.
- Update the official tag file in the studio `Tags` folder. One file per catalog number. No v2 copies.

## Sold
- The tag sits with the work. When it sells, pull the tag and stack it. The stack is the sold count.
- Do not send a client anything from the tag. Invoice still follows the client-email-test-first rule.

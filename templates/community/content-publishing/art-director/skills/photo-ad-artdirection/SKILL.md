name: photo-ad-artdirection
description: "Direct, prompt, and evaluate distinctive commercial photography for static ads, UGC-style creatives, landing pages, and Instagram carousels, avoiding generic stock and generic AI looks."
---

Create original commercial images with clear communication, tactile realism, and a recognizable point of view.

Build every direction from: one legible subject + one controlled tension + one tactile truth + one disciplined color system.

Workflow: establish the communication job, choose a composition system below, make the concept specific (real verb, one supporting material, hero readable in under one second), keep campaign coherence (repeat location, cast, palette, lighting; vary framing), write a production brief, and score it with the rubric.

## Composition systems
- Sculptural icon: one dominant object, clean ground, one hard shadow or supporting material, 25–40% open space for copy.
- Intimate crop: face, hand, fabric or product fills the frame; cut one detail with the edge; copy in a quiet corner.
- Lived ritual: one recognizable task, at most three story props, enough environment to explain it, gesture over eye contact.
- Graphic interruption: one meaningful reflection, foreground object, shadow or occlusion breaking a clean frame.
- Quiet world: subject below a third of the frame, a wall/floor/sky as the graphic field, one irregular detail.

## People and objects
Direct an action more specific than "smile at camera". Use hands to show use, care or relationship. Create expression through the situation; never ask for fake candid laughter. Add one deliberate imperfection (off-center face, cropped limb, motion, foreground obstruction). Give objects a visual verb: stack, drip, split, reflect, press, reveal, wrap, balance. Choose places with narrative evidence, not empty pretty locations.

## Color and light
Assign color by role: base (dominant field), support (secondary), accent (smallest, highest attention, under ~15% of the frame). Repeat roles across a campaign and check hierarchy in grayscale. Pick one lighting logic: hard direct flash (UGC immediacy), high-key sculptural (products), warm window light (home, ritual), dappled light (nature, softness), or a named colour-gel pair.

## Texture
Pick one or two: subtle phone sharpening, flash falloff, slight motion blur, fabric creases, handled paper, foreground occlusion, imperfect alignment. Don't stack every signal; synthetic imperfection is another form of polish.

## UGC
Should feel authored by a real user, not degraded. Believable home, car, kitchen; ordinary evidence (notes, mug rings, wrinkles); phone perspective at arm's length; natural skin, hair, hands; expression between neutral and delighted. No mansions or influencer sets.

## Static ads and feed
Default 4:5. One subject, one headline. Keep essentials out of the outer 8–10%. Build a quiet copy zone into the image. Test at thumbnail size. Never invent metrics, testimonials, ratings or proof.

## Video source frames
Default 9:16. Keep head, hands and moving objects away from edges, room for motion, no tiny shimmering props or visible text, one plausible light source.

## Prompt grammar
1. Output (ratio, placement) 2. Communication (subject, emotion, promise) 3. Scene (person, object, environment, exact action) 4. Composition (crop, camera height, hierarchy, copy zone) 5. Color (base, support, accent) 6. Light 7. Texture 8. Typography (exact text or a no-text rule) 9. Guardrails (truth, anatomy, originality, exclusions).

Template: Create a [ratio] commercial photograph for [placement]. The viewer should notice [hero], feel [emotion], and understand [promise]. Show [person/object] [visual verb] in [environment]. Use [composition system] with [camera and crop]. Palette: [base], [support], a small [accent]. Light with [lighting]. Preserve [material truth] and add only [imperfection]. Include [exact text or no text]. Do not add [avoid list].

## Rubric (0–5 each, require 38/50)
Focal clarity, concept specificity, color logic, composition, gesture, material truth, lighting intent, message fit, copy compatibility, ownability.
Reject regardless of score if it invents a claim, has misspelled or illegible text, broken anatomy or product geometry, copies an identifiable person, photo or composition, leaves the product category unclear, or depends only on adjectives.

## Failure patterns
Adjective piles, mashing many references, prop abundance, fake candid behavior, saturated colors without hierarchy, perfect everything, heavy imperfection filters, generic AI metaphors (robots, brains, holograms, magic particles), changing the whole world between campaign images, type pasted over the subject.

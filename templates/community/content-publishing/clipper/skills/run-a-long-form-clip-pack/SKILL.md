Longer video (~45s+) → pack of tight clips each with own angle. Follow Creative Guidebook. Do not ship "whole video, shorter."

## Dedupe / make-more
Follow Keep a clip index. For batch/make-more: only unmined sources. On a brand-new source, multiple GIFs from different beats are fine. Remine only when they name the post. A named long-film pack may still produce multiple mp4 clips from that film.

## Steps
Pull/local → probe → watch (split >15MB) → slate 4–8 clips → cut → karaoke talking mp4s → classify. Show in chat; do not publish until asked.

---
name: gif-from-youtube-clip
description: "Use when turning a described movie/TV moment or YouTube clip into a captioned, texting-sized GIF or muted MP4."
---

Find a public YouTube clip matching the owner's scene or quote, cut the beat (~5–8s), strip audio unless they ask to keep sound, burn big high-contrast condensed captions in the lower third synced to speech, and deliver a muted ~480w MP4 plus a phone-sized GIF (~320–360w, under ~2MB when possible). Prefer a YouTube link when given. Prefer specific/offbeat moments over already-viral GIFs when they want something unique. If download fails or no clip exists, say so and offer the next-best public source. Tools: yt-dlp + ffmpeg.

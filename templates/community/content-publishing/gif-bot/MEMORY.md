- **profile** (2026-09-25): On every GIF from a YouTube clip, overlay contextual dialogue/narration so the quote is visible and obvious on the frames (joke reads without sound).
- **profile** (2026-09-25): Default export is muted (audio stripped) visual-only files for smaller texting-friendly GIFs and MP4s, unless the owner asks to keep sound.
- **profile** (2026-09-25): Default caption style: big bold condensed poster-like lettering (gold/yellow fill, heavy black outline), lower third, synced to speech.
- **profile** (2026-09-25): Deliver both a muted MP4 (~480w) and a phone-sized GIF (~320–360w, aim under ~2MB) when making a shareable clip.

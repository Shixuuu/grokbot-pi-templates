# Memory seeds for Kids Book Creator

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Book rules: all text sits at least 0.5 in inside the trim (KDP minimum 0.375). Every page and cover is one full-bleed square picture made with Grok Imagine in the browser at 1:1, zoomed out to about 84% so nothing is near the edge. Back cover is a single full-bleed story picture, never one image stacked on another. Keep the exact original words. If page numbers are used, one style, one spot, each shown once.
- **profile**: Approval rule: publishing, KDP uploads or resubmits, price changes, ad spend and outgoing messages happen only after the owner types their approval phrase in chat (set during getting started). Never type a password or enter tax or bank info; the owner does that. If the KDP session dies, stop, leave the draft as it is, and tell the owner once. Expanded Distribution stays off unless the owner says otherwise.
- **profile**: Every build ends with one complete, clearly labeled review PDF (front cover, title page, copyright page, every interior page with its label and book page number, back cover, in order) delivered in chat before and independent of any KDP step, so people who never publish still get a finished book.
- **profile**: Quality gate before any upload: the preflight check must say PASS (no blank or solid-color pages, the last pages are real content, page count and size correct) and the KDP Previewer's last 8 pages must be looked at. Never pad an interior with flat-color or blank pages; use real coloring and activity pages. After every submit, verify the Submitted date changed and, when Live, re-check the Previewer's last pages.
- **profile**: Every picture book teaches one clear, warm (not preachy) lesson, and a lesson is not reused by an earlier book. Lesson bank: say sorry; share even when it is hard; try again after you flop; ask for help before you are stuck; be brave and try the new thing; listen when a friend talks; take turns; include the left-out kid; use kind words; clean up your own mess; patience beats rushing; be honest; practice makes better; feelings are OK; look out for little ones; friends and teamwork.
- **log**: Uses Grok Imagine (grok.com/imagine) and Amazon KDP (kdp.amazon.com) in the browser; no connector is needed. The owner signs in to both themselves.
- **log**: Prefers proactive fixes: if a known problem would cause a KDP rejection (margins, page size, duplicate pages), fix it and check before submitting instead of waiting for a rejection.

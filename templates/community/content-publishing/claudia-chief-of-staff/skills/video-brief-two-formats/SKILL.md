# Steps
1. When briefing Video Bot on a new video, include original 16:9 and original 9:16 plus the emotion for that piece.
2. Produce original 16:9 and original 9:16. No cropping from one to the other.
3. Emotion of this piece: [tender / playful / romantic / ceremonial / funny]
4. Animation must show that emotion, not only breathing.
5. Show both compositions before final animation.
6. Do not send the artist a cut that crops one format into the other or that only breathes/stares. Bounce it back to Video Bot first.
7. Incomplete-song audio: if the clip is not a complete song (teaser / Discover / Short / Meta excerpt), music must fade in at the start and fade out at the end. Full-song masters: no fade unless asked.

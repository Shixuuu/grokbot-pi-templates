# Original song descriptions

## When
Any new original, or when the artist asks for catalog YouTube / distributor descriptions.

## Format
- Short (1–3 sentences): mood + one concrete image. Caption / distributor length.
- Long (1 short paragraph + optional second): origin or gaze of the piece, no fake biography, no virtuoso pitch. Instrument-only unless they said there is vocal.
- No invented store links.

## Process
1. Listen to the locked audio or watch the locked YouTube 16:9. Read lyrics only if a real lyric file exists.
2. Social Bot drafts short + long. Chief of Staff QCs tone. Artist locks before any public description edit.
3. Save the locked copy under the song's workspace folder.

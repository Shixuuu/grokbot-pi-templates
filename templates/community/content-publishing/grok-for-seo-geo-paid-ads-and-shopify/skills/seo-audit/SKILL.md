A scored, prioritized SEO report from LIVE data. Every finding cites a real query, page, or fetched element. Missing source: say so. Never invent impressions, position, or clicks.

## Connector
If the Ryze AI connector is missing, stop. Add `https://connector.get-ryze.ai/mcp` (docs: https://www.get-ryze.ai/how-to-connect-claude-to-google-meta-ads-mcp). Wait.

List connections first. No Search Console → say what it unlocks (queries, rankings) and offer a reduced audit (on-page + fetched pages). Do not stall. GA4 connected → rank by money and pull AI-referral traffic. No GA4 → skip that slice, do not fake it.

## Flow
1. Scope. Confirm the GSC property. Two windows: last 28 days vs prior 28. State property + window in one line before digging.
2. Young site. GSC near-empty → say it once. Weight indexing, on-page of key pages, competitor/keyword gap. Do not pad empty sections.
3. Queries. High impressions + weak CTR (title/meta). Striking distance 11–20, ranked by impressions. Branded vs non-branded. Decay vs prior window. One device pull, one country pull.
4. Pages. Top landings by clicks — are these the pages the business wants? Impression-heavy / click-poor. Single-query pages (thin coverage). Join GA4 conversions if present so money reorders the list.
5. Indexing. Sitemap summary. Inspect 2–3 key URLs (submitted-not-indexed, crawled-not-indexed).
6. On-page. Fetch at most 5 pages. For each: title + meta present/unique/aligned to the query it actually ranks for; one h1; canonical; structure.
7. AI visibility. Only with GA4: ChatGPT / Perplexity / Gemini sessions, landings, trend. Its own beat, not a footnote.
8. Score 0–100, honest: indexing 20, query capture 20, on-page 20, coverage 15, technical/off-page 15, trend 10. 90+ excellent, 70–89 good, 50–69 needs work, <50 critical. A flattering score kills the follow-up.

Cap the run: two GSC windows, one device, one country, 5 page fetches, one AI pull. Depth is analysis, not more pulls.

## How it reads
Pick ONE visual identity and keep it (Bold Signal / Electric Studio / Dark Botanical / Vintage Editorial). Cover title is the conclusion, not "SEO Report". One idea per beat. Numbers first. Section titles are claims: "Pricing sits at position 11 on 41k impressions".

Lead with the score and three opportunities ranked by click/revenue impact. Close with P0 / P1 and expected impact.

## Writes
Offer the top on-page fixes one at a time, exact new title/meta. Never write a CMS, sitemap, or live page without a yes on that change.

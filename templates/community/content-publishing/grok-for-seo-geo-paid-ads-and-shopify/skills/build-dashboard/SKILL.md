A Linear/Stripe-grade analytics page, not a BI dump. Pull live data first. Never invent spend, CPA, ROAS, or sessions. Missing source: say so.

If the Ryze AI connector is missing, stop. Add `https://connector.get-ryze.ai/mcp`. Wait.

3–6 widgets. One kpi-grid, one primary chart, at most two tables. Widget titles are questions. White cards, quiet neutrals. Numbers in the file are the ones you just pulled.

Wipe skeleton numbers. Empty Insights is not zero spend.

# Check a program's terms

1. Find the official terms page. Follow links in the post or its card first; otherwise search for the program owner's own site. Use only the owner's pages, never a summary, a reposted screenshot, or the post's own claims.
2. Read the whole terms page and any linked eligibility, payout, or rules page.
3. Report in one short block:
   - Eligibility: who can join (country, age, account type, follower or subscription minimums).
   - Who pays: the paying party, the payout method, and any threshold or schedule.
   - Guaranteed or discretionary: quote the line that says whether payment can be withheld, capped, or changed.
   - What the user gives up: any content license, exclusivity, data, or fees.
   - Date: the terms' last-updated date, or "no date shown".
4. Quote the exact sentence behind anything that could flip the verdict, and link the page.
5. If you can't open or read the terms, say so and don't give a PURSUE on the program part. Never fill a gap from memory.

Read only. Never sign up, apply, accept terms, or connect an account.

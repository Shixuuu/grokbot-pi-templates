name: meeting-to-chatprd
description: "Use this when a meeting produced a decision, requirement, or idea that should become or update a ChatPRD document."
---

Meetings die in notes. Product decisions should land in a spec.

## Steps

1. Find the meeting in Granola (query by topic, people, or date). Read the notes and decisions, not the whole transcript unless needed.
2. Search ChatPRD for an existing doc this should update. Update that doc when one exists.
3. If nothing fits, create the lightest ChatPRD artifact: decision log, 1-pager, or PRD section, in the product organization.
4. Write only what was actually decided or explicitly left open. Do not invent requirements the room did not discuss.
5. Send the user the ChatPRD link and the decisions you captured. Mention open questions the meeting did not resolve.

## Rules

- Use Granola as the source of truth for what was said. Do not rely on memory of the meeting.
- If Granola is thin, say so and do not pad the spec.
- Customer quotes and names in notes stay out of public-facing docs unless the user wants them in.

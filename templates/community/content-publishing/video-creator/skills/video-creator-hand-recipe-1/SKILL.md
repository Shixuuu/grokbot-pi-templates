Use for topic criteria, hooks, script arc, narration and the INVENT-LOOK BRIEF.

## 1 Purpose
8-13 min explainer of ONE idea for a total newbie that still gives an expert a non-obvious insight. Owner's voice.

## 2 Topic must-haves
One sentence "X BUT Y SO Z"; newbie puzzle in 20 s; one running analogy; every claim dated primary or cut; evergreen; clear stake for the viewer.

## 3 Titles and hooks
Title <=70 chars, one promise. Write 3 hooks with ONE method: misconception-first (common belief broken by footage, test or number within ~10-20 s, then the driving question) or key visual + what-then-why (striking visual 0-3 s, plain "what" by ~5 s, big real-world "why" by ~15 s, promise of nuance). Keep the strongest; log all in meta/HOOKS.md. First 30-60 s get the most polish: engaging, never overwhelming. Open with the real clip of the subject only if one exists. No greeting, "in this video", or news lede.

## 4 Script arc
~1900-2200 words (~160 wpm). Hook ~8% | Baseline ~15% | Key idea ~27% | Payoff ~15% | Meta ~4% (optional) | Consequences ~23% | Close ~7% | OUTRO_TEXT. One curiosity loop; analogy 3-5x; a turn every ~120 words; ideas fleshed out, conversational not staccato; [HOLD] <=0.6 s; beats bNN|spoken|[SHOW]|{TAG}.

## 5 Narration + on-screen text
Owner's natural voice, 9-14-word sentences, grade 5-6, no hedges, no restating, no meta talk about sources, never AI-sounding. On screen: key words or important numbers only; text designed, never amateur chrome.

## 6 INVENT-LOOK BRIEF (send to the Opus-class pass at SCENE_EFFORT; lock this instruction set, never a fixed palette)
"Read the pinned script, VO word timings, RESEARCH.md media list and the listed rulebook rules. Invent a very engaging, beautiful animation direction that helps a total newbie deeply understand this script. In ONE cohesive pass output: (1) a look guide: palette, type, textures, motion budget, transitions; (2) beats with exact frames tiling the VO, cut at onset -~100 ms; (3) per beat: what is seen, where each real clip, photo, post and logo goes, and the word each element cues to; (4) renderable drawing code: shared kit + every scene you can finish. Bake the direction into the code."
Constraints to append: heroes (real characters and objects acting out what is said) dominate the frame, no big empty areas; one clear idea per beat, no unrelated large objects overlapping (intentional crowds of small things OK); silky transitions (soft morphs, matched shapes, continuous canvas moves), no jumps, pops or leftovers; infinite canvas with calm smooth pans/zooms (typical 8-20 px/s, sustained <=60, hard <=90, zoom <=3%/s, rotation <=1.5 deg/s, <=20 px/s for 2 s after a cut); purposeful motion first to last frame, never motion for motion's sake; subtle 3D/depth where it aids understanding; subtle grain; detail over movement; real logos on spoken brand names; photo when a person is named; actual posts when about a post; red = blocked/impossible; text never over objects or people, held long enough to read, never restating the VO; cartoon hands only if hands appear; no diagonal light sweep; 2D unless the topic needs 3D.

Use for tiers, defaults, handoff limits and what cannot be guaranteed.

Only TOPIC is required; missing optional inputs lower the tier. Tiers NEVER weaken a HARD rule (unlisted, all gates before upload, secrets, no ASR, research before script).

Levels (low > high): voice none > stock > own clone; corpus none > full; compute box > one strong machine > two; authoring built-in > CLI Opus-class; YouTube none > unlisted; stock none > API; review none > tool; products stylized > image-to-3D.

Tiers (lowest of voice/compute/authoring):
Starter: research, script, locked plan, scenes, silent cut.
Standard: voice, one strong machine, CLI, narrated video, unlisted.
Pro: own clone, corpus, two machines, Opus-class cohesive pass + parallel authors, review tool, reference quality.
No voice: VO step = timing estimates. No corpus: "CORPUS UNAVAILABLE". No YouTube: S11 = "MASTER READY".
Biggest upgrade (tell ONE): any voice; authoring CLI; YouTube; own voice clone; stronger compute machine; corpus; stock key; review tool.

Handoff: owner supplies clone, keys, channel consent, corpus, taste; fair use is their legal call; tools ship as contracts.
Cannot guarantee: voice by ear; factual completeness; Content ID; taste; stock availability; throughput. Mechanical gates catch sync, bed, frames and onset, not taste: the owner's viewing is the review (R-Q3), and every note becomes a rule (R-L4).
First video: 1-min preview after look lock; record accepted/rejected preferences as rulebook rules.

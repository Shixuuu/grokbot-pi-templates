# Build binder

1. Recall every binder fact and open blank from memory.
2. Pick the format: a Google Doc if a Google Drive connector is connected and the owner wants it there; otherwise a Word .docx generated on your computer (e.g. python-docx) and attached in chat.
3. Name the file with a version number: "<Owner> Family Binder v1", then v2, v3... Check existing files and increment; never overwrite a prior version.
4. Layout:
   - Cover: title, "Last updated: <date>", version, a line saying it is not legal advice.
   - "Start here" page: who to call first (executor/trustee, attorney), where the originals are.
   - One section per intake section, each as a clean table (Item | Details | Where it is | Who to call).
   - Accounts show institution, type, and last 4 digits only. No passwords, no SSNs, no full numbers.
   - Appendix A: open blanks, grouped by section.
5. Deliver the file to the owner and list the number of open blanks. Offer kids-copy and attorney-prep as next steps.
6. Never share or email the binder to anyone without the owner's explicit approval.

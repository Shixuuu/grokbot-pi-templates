After an X livestream, turn the episode into feed posts.

## Inputs
- Streamer's X handle
- The episode post URL if known; otherwise find the newest livestream/episode post on their profile

## Steps
1. Pull the episode post: full text, broadcast link, timestamp, views, likes, replies, quotes.
2. Pull every visible reply and quote. Note who said it and whether they are worth a named reply.
3. From the episode talking points AND the best chat/replies, draft 2–3 clip posts:
   - One idea per post
   - Match the streamer's existing voice (read their last few posts first)
   - No recap-of-the-episode tone, no "in today's stream"
   - Each clip should stand alone if someone has never watched
4. Flag the single best reply to quote or reply to, if any.
5. Deliver the drafts. Do not post unless asked.

## Done when
Drafts are ready and the best reply is called out. Do not stay quiet if a new episode was found.

# Google Search Profile coach

Steps, checklists, and wording only. NEVER log into Google or X for them. Never take passwords or 2FA.

## Eligibility
US, 18+, ~10k+ on YT / IG / X / TikTok (confirm on Google's pages).

## Links
- https://creators.google/profile
- https://profile.google.com
- https://profile.google.com/claim

## Checklist they click themselves
1. Confirm eligibility
2. Sign in at profile.google.com with their Google account
3. Claim at profile.google.com/claim
4. Verify with X (or another network Google offers)
5. Create/claim; set bio, avatar, pin best posts
6. Wait 24–48h
7. Search name + @handle for Knowledge Panel → View Search Profile

Help with bio/pin wording and troubleshooting. Do not promise a panel or ranking.

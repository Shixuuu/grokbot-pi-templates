Streamlined D&D 5e resolution for a live multiplayer table. Always show the math.

## Hybrid dice policy
- **Drama rolls** (plot-changing attacks, death saves, social climax, risky skill checks): ask for a real die first; offer auto-roll if the player is remote or prefers it.
- **Bookkeeping** (safe-zone perception, damage dice after a hit, mook initiative): auto-roll and show `d20 (14) + DEX (3) + prof (2) = 19`.
- Never hide a roll. Never fudge secretly. Soften only with an announced partial success + cost.

## Ability checks & saves
DC guide: easy 10 · medium 15 · hard 20 · nearly impossible 25.
Advantage / disadvantage as 5e (roll 2d20, take higher/lower).
Contested: both roll; higher total wins; tie = status quo or shared complication.

Common checks: Athletics (STR), Acrobatics (DEX), Stealth (DEX), Perception (WIS), Insight (WIS), Persuasion/Deception/Intimidation (CHA), Investigation (INT), Arcana/History/Nature/Religion (INT), Survival/Medicine/Animal Handling (WIS).

## Initiative
1. Everyone rolls `d20 + DEX` (auto unless someone wants physical).
2. Post the full order once.
3. Call the active character each turn; delay/ready only with table OK.
4. Surprised creatures skip the first turn.

## Actions (streamlined turn)
On your turn you get: **move** + **one action** (+ bonus action if a feature grants it) + free talk.
Actions: Attack, Cast, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Use object, Improvise.
Opportunity attack: leaving reach without Disengage → one melee attack.

## Attack & damage
1. `d20 + ability + proficiency (if proficient) (+ magic)` vs AC.
2. Natural 20: hit + double the damage dice (not static modifiers).
3. Natural 1: miss with a brief consequence when it fits.
4. Damage types matter when resistance/vulnerability is in play — say it aloud.

## Conditions (use plain names)
Blinded · Charmed · Deafened · Frightened · Grappled · Incapacitated · Invisible · Paralyzed · Petrified · Poisoned · Prone · Restrained · Stunned · Unconscious.
Poisoned: disadvantage on attacks and ability checks.
Prone: melee advantage against you; you have disadvantage on attacks; stand = half move.
Restrained: speed 0; attacks vs you advantage; your attacks and DEX saves disadvantage.

## Death & dying
0 HP → unconscious, 0 death saves.
Start of turn: death save `d20` (10+ success; 1 = two failures; 20 = 1 HP).
3 failures → dead. 3 successes → stable at 0 HP.
Damage at 0 HP: failure (crit from melee within 5 ft = two failures).
Healing at 0 → conscious with that HP.

## Rests
Short rest (~1 hour): spend hit dice (roll + CON each) to heal.
Long rest (~8 hours): regain HP and half hit dice (min 1) if fiction allows; spell slots refresh.

## Encounter budgeting (quick)
- Easy: party feels strong
- Fair: spend resources
- Hard: someone may drop
Prefer fewer interesting foes over a dozen identical bags of HP.

Use when the table needs a one-shot or cold open and has no campaign prep.

## How to offer
Give **2–3 hooks** in one short message. Each hook: title · one-sentence premise · why it's urgent tonight. Let the party pick or mash them.

## Ready hooks (rotate, remix, rename)
1. **The Missing Caravan** — Merchants vanished on the Weary Road; a bloodied mule wandered into town with a locked strongbox humming faintly.
2. **Lanterns Under the Lake** — Fishers swear they see procession lights beneath the water; someone's child followed them and hasn't resurfaced.
3. **The Duke's False Heir** — A charming claimant arrives with perfect papers; an old nurse begs the party to prove the ring is cursed before coronation dawn.
4. **Goblin Market Debt** — A player (or friendly NPC) owes the Market a favor due at midnight; the price is a memory they don't remember selling.
5. **Ashen Chapel** — A roadside chapel rings its bell with no one inside; villagers who enter leave smiling and emptied of grief — and of names.
6. **Sky-Needle Crash** — A metal spire fell from a clear sky into the barley fields; scavengers already fight over glowing shards that whisper equations.
7. **Masquerade Knives** — At a noble masque, every guest wears the same porcelain face; one face is cracked, and the murderer is still dancing.
8. **The Last Lightkeeper** — The coastal beacon failed during a storm; ships are heading for the reefs, and something has nested in the lantern room.

## Structure a 2–3 hour one-shot
1. Cold open with danger or a decision (5 min).
2. Two investigation paths that reconverge (30–40 min).
3. One social or exploration set piece (20 min).
4. Climactic fight or chase with a twist (30 min).
5. Epilogue + loot + hook for next time (5 min).

## Scaling
Level 1 party of 3–4: one elite foe + 2–4 mooks, or a skill-heavy mystery with one sharp fight.
If the party steamrolls, add a complication (timer, hostage, collapsing terrain) — not surprise max HP.

## Continuity
When a one-shot ends well, ask if it becomes a campaign. Store open threads in the quest log.

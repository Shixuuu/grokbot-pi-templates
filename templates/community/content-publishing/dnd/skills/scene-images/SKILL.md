Generate images to support the table — not to decorate every message.

## When to generate
- Entering a new landmark location
- Introducing a major NPC the party will remember
- Combat start or a dramatic reveal
- End-of-session recap cover
Skip routine rolls, inventory chatter, and repeated shots of the same room.

## How many
Usually **one** image per beat. Never flood the table.

## Prompt recipe
Write a single concrete prompt:
`[subject], [action/pose], [setting], [mood/lighting], [style], no text, no UI, no watermark`
Style default: painterly fantasy concept art, readable silhouettes, cohesive palette.
Party portraits: match saved "look" notes from Character Sheets; keep kit consistent across sessions.

## Examples
- `Weathered tiefling rogue in a green cloak under a flickering tavern lantern, sly half-smile, painterly fantasy portrait, no text`
- `Collapsed stone bridge over a misty gorge at dusk, glowing runes on the far pillar, cinematic fantasy landscape, no text`
- `Goblin market stalls under moth-wing lanterns, strange trinkets, crowded alley, moody fantasy street scene, no text`

## Group chat note
If the room cannot show attachments, describe the visual in one vivid sentence and send the image privately to the host when tools allow — or skip the file and keep the prose.

## Don't
- Don't generate copyrighted character lookalikes or real-person faces.
- Don't put rules text or dice math into the image.
- Don't regenerate the same scene because of a minor detail change.

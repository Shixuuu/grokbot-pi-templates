---
name: ai-voice-intro-video
description: "Use when the user wants a short AI-narrated intro or outro video (voiceover, stills, captions) to open or close a YouTube episode."
---

Inputs: the episode (cut, link, or rundown of topics), the owner's chosen voice, and their caption color. Default voice: Microsoft Edge neural TTS en-US-AvaNeural via edge-tts.

1. Script. Write 20 to 40 seconds of short, conversational sentences that tease the episode's real topics in order. Open on the biggest story. Plain words, no hype, no price predictions stated as fact, nothing the episode doesn't actually cover. Show the script before rendering if the user wants to approve it.
2. Pronunciation. Keep a list of words the voice misreads and write them phonetically in the voice script only (captions show the real spelling). Known ones: write concert-sense "live" as "lyve"; write ordinals without hyphens ("twenty fifth"). Add the owner's own fixes to memory as they come up.
3. Voice. Render phrase by phrase so pacing can be controlled: base rate about -3%, key phrases slightly louder (about +12% volume) and slower (about -5%), and the opening line a little slower still. Avoid SSML emphasis tags. Don't trim silences aggressively.
4. Visuals. One still per beat that matches the spoken words exactly (a screenshot, headline, chart, or generated image of the thing being said). Use soft holds and gentle crossfades. Avoid strong zoom or pan on screenshots because it looks shaky; if you zoom at all, stay at 1.00 to 1.04.
5. Captions. Full spoken captions, word-timed, never shortened so much that words go missing. White text with a dark outline, key words (names, tickers, numbers) highlighted in the owner's accent color.
6. Music. Optional light royalty-free bed at a very low level (around 0.045 volume) under the voice, or none if the owner prefers.
7. Render a 1920x1080 version and a smaller 720p phone version. Check both play and the length is right, then attach them in chat.
8. For an outro: same voice, a quick soft sequence of the channel's recent thumbnails, and a plain subscribe, like and share line.

Never upload or post. The owner adds it to their edit.

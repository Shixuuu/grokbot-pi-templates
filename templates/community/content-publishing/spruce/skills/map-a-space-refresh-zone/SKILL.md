# Map a space refresh zone

Before or after a photo, lock the scope if the job is big.

Collect only what you still need:
- Exact zone (one closet, one desk, one wall)
- What “done” looks like in one sentence
- Must-keeps
- Timebox
- Exit paths (donate, trash day, recycle)

Save the zone map. Prefer working from their photo when they have one — then use Refresh a space from a photo for the mockup + steps.

Never name retail brands.

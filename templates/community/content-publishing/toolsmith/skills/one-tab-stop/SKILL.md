# One tab stop

Use this before opening a website, and again if a tab appears on its own.

1. One job, one tab. If a tab is already open on the page you need, stay there. Do not open a second tab or a second window.
2. Leftover tabs at the start, a blank tab, or a second window: stop. Do not click tab close. Do not open a new tab to recover. Do not start another runner.
3. Do not type into the address bar. Do not use New Tab, Chrome plus, or a new window.
4. If sign-in is already done, do the job in that same tab. If sign-in is waiting on the human, stop on that screen. Do not open another site in a new tab.
5. After you stop, report one line: leftover tabs, waiting. Then idle until a new named job names one exact URL.

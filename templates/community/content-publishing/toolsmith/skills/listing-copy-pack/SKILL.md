# Listing copy pack

Use this when preparing the next Marketplace, Trade Me, or eBay listing of an already-priced part so fields are pasted, not rediscovered.

Caller supplies title, price, category, condition, location (city + country), freight policy, photos, and any footer. Do not invent copy, price, location, plate, or VIN.

1. Build a single labelled copy pack **before** opening the browser: TITLE, PRICE, LOCATION, CATEGORY, CONDITION, FREIGHT_LINE, DESCRIPTION (body + freight + footer), plus operator notes for that site.
2. Paste those blocks into the form. Do not retype fields from memory on listing 2+.
3. Location is whatever the caller stated (city + country). Confirm it on the form before Next/Publish. Do not trust a leftover location.
4. Freight line is whatever the caller stated (included under a weight cap, pickup, or buyer-pays). Do not invent postage.
5. **Trade Me shipping/pickup must match the description.** If the form forbids pick-ups, do not put a pickup line in the pack or on the form. Do not publish a pickup line when pick-ups are forbidden.
6. Optional pull-to-order / example-photo language and extra-parts footer only if the caller asked. Never add plate or VIN unless the caller supplied VIN for public use.
7. Trade Me vehicle parts: open Motors / car parts create first. Never general Marketplace create.
8. Facebook: use the Facebook Marketplace item listing skill. One tab. Photo required before Next. Skip Boost. Confirm location on the form.
9. After first successful publish on a site, reuse this pack and that site's shortest path for the next part. Do not re-walk menus.
10. Do not publish until a human has approved that listing's price and copy. Do not message buyers. Do not drive the listing browser if another assistant already has exclusive listing sessions — generate the pack only.

If a local generator or markdown pack already exists for this part type, run/open that instead of rewriting the blocks.

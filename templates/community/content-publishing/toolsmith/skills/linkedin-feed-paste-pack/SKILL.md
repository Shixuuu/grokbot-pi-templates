# LinkedIn feed paste pack

Chrome-free **writer** pack for a LinkedIn feed/share post. Do not publish from this skill. Poster uses linkedin-text-post. Pairing: compose-pack-handoff.

Caller supplies the exact BODY (already approved). Specific post text stays in a file/pack, not in this recipe. Do not invent copy, hashtags, or images. No account names.

1. Output a Chrome-free copy pack: **BODY** (exact). One-tab / close leftover tabs including blanks are **notes for the poster**, not a browser job for the writer.
2. Default: no hashtags, no images, no extra lines before or after BODY, unless the caller put them in the pack.
3. **If MEDIA is none:** do not put a URL or bare domain in BODY (the feed will auto-unfurl it into a card and the og:title becomes that site). Write the site name in words. If an unfurl card is wanted, the pack must say so explicitly.
4. LinkedIn share composer has no title field. Do not invent a headline.
5. Do not open LinkedIn or listing Chrome (Marketplace / Trade Me / eBay). Do not drive the poster's desktop.
6. Do not edit About, headline, or other profile fields from this skill.
7. If login/2FA is needed for posting, the **poster** stops and hands the computer to the human. Do not type credentials.
8. After a live post URL exists, keep the pack as the template for the next post. Do not post that same BODY again unless the caller asks to recycle it.

Do not bake in a specific person, employer, product, or post text.

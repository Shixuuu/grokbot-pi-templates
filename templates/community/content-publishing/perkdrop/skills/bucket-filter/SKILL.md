---
name: bucket-filter
description: "Use this when filtering found offers to the user's selected PerkDrop buckets and region prefs."
---

Read the user's saved buckets and region prefs from memory. If none, run getting-started first. Region prefs may include preset options and free-typed countries.

Keep an offer only if it clearly fits at least one selected bucket:
- Student / fresher: education packs, student credits, school verification offers
- Startup founder: startup credit programs, incorporation-gated credits
- OSS maintainer: maintainer / open source benefit programs
- Hackathon builder: free signup perks, post-signup partner claims and forms
- Job hunter: free career / portfolio / ATS / hosting tools aimed at applicants

If region prefs are set, prefer offers whose stated eligibility matches those regions or countries (including typed ones) or is Worldwide / unspecified. Do not invent a region. Do not drop a strong Worldwide offer only because prefs list a country.

Drop bucket mismatches. When multiple buckets match, keep one card.

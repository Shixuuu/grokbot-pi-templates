name: organize-social-context
description: "Use this when filing or displaying people into Friends, Neighbors, Coworkers, or another grouping the owner uses."
---

Keep people organized the way the owner wants them recalled.

1. Default buckets: Friends, Neighbors, Coworkers. Add other buckets only if the owner asks.
2. File each person into the bucket the owner named. If they did not say, ask once rather than guessing.
3. Treat "partner" as a work title (coworker) unless the owner clearly means a romantic partner — then use the label they prefer.
4. When showing a board or summary, group by bucket and list the stored details under each person.
5. Keep household members together when the owner described them as one household (e.g. neighbors next door).

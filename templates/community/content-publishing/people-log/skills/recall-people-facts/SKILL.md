name: recall-people-facts
description: "Use this when the user asks what you know about someone, who has a birthday, or any other recall of stored people facts."
---

When the user asks about a person or wants a reminder:

1. Search your agent memory for matching people facts.
2. Answer with the stored details only — concise, conversational, lead with the answer.
3. If several people could match, list the candidates and ask which one.
4. If you have nothing filed, say so plainly and offer to save whatever they tell you next.
5. Never invent or guess missing facts.

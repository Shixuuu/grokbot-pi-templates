# Memory seeds for People Log

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-09-12): The owner wants this bot to remember facts they share about friends, coworkers, and neighbors, then recall those facts on demand.
- **profile** (2026-09-15): Only add a photo thumbnail to a People Log contact when the owner specifically provides a picture for that person. Do not look up or invent photos.
- **profile** (2026-09-15): Default People Log grouping is Friends, Neighbors, and Coworkers. Treat "partner" as a work title (coworker) unless the owner clearly means a romantic partner.

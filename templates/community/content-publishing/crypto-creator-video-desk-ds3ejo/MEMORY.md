- **profile**: Never post or upload to YouTube, X, or any platform; the owner publishes everything themselves.
- **profile**: Write in plain English with a teacher voice and no hype; never present price predictions as fact.
- **profile**: For AI voice intros, stills must match the spoken wording beat for beat (each voice line paired with the on-screen moment it describes).
- **profile**: For AI voice intros, never use aggressive zoom or pan on screenshots or UI stills because it looks shaky; default to soft holds and gentle crossfades, and if zooming stay at about 1.00 to 1.04.
- **profile**: For AI voice videos, show full spoken captions; do not aggressively shorten or truncate caption cues.

Use this when someone shares a YouTube, X (Twitter), TikTok, Twitch/stream, or similar creator account and wants to grow it.

Goal: show them how to grow this account. Find the real bottleneck (niche confusion, covers, titles, hashtags, posting times, consistency, or something else) and rank the fixes by estimated impact.

Credit / effort: work from the catalog already pulled. Do not re-scrape unless the handle is new or the last pull is stale. One pass.

Steps:
1. Identify platform, handle, and what they actually post (from recent videos, not the bio). Check YouTube, TikTok, X, and Twitch/stream for that handle. If a platform 404s, skip it. If a site blocks, captchas, or login-walls, use another public source (search, trackers, page dumps). Never invent videos or stats.
2. Pull a recent sample (10–20, or the full grid if small): covers, titles/captions, hashtags, length, timestamps, public stats.
3. Diagnose the bottleneck. Check all of these and only keep what is actually true for this page: mixed / unclear niche; covers / first frame; titles and hooks; hashtags (wrong, generic-only, or missing); posting times; consistency / dumping then disappearing; anything else visible that is capping them.
4. Group posts into their real categories so next ideas stay in-lane.
5. Deliver:
   - One-line diagnosis
   - Ranked growth plan. Each fix gets an estimated % of the growth this page is leaving on the table (the bars should add to about 100%). Use ranges if needed (e.g. 20–25%). Say they are estimates from this catalog vs similar pages, not a promise, and never phrase them as “you will grow X%.”
   - For each fix: what to change, in one or two lines
   - Next-video ideas (not scripts): 8–12 in-lane ideas, each with a title and 4–6 niche hashtags. Hook first; #fyp last or skip it.
6. Public content only. Age-appropriate. Direct. No filler frameworks. No merch, ads, or brand-deal advice unless they ask.

Done when they know the bottleneck, the ranked % plan, and what to film next.

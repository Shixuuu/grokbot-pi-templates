**Title:** instagram connector liker follower commenter

Instagram outreach from a signed-in browser. Search posts, find public engagers, then like, follow, and/or comment with rate limits and a log. For anyone running that workflow without a marketplace Instagram connector.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

# Routine: Daily niche research post

> Weekdays: several post and article angles from a real finding in the owner's niche. The owner keeps the publish button.

Daily niche-research post for the owner's X account.

Goal: draft (and post only if compose actually works) ONE post a day: findings and papers on the owner's stated niche (AI coding harnesses / agentic workflows by default). Not a product pitch.

Hard no:
- No product pitch
- No unproven evals-green claims
- No unreleased-repo tease
- No invented metrics
- No waitlist CTA on these posts
- Do not invent a persona. Match the owner's voice from their real recent posts (short paragraphs, specific numbers/observations, informal grammar OK, opinionated builder, no em dashes, no "not X, Y" brochure cadence, no launch-page punch)

How:
1) If the X plugin works, pull a small recent sample of their posts first and match that voice. If X is not set up, do NOT retry; draft from saved voice notes and send the draft to the owner instead of posting.
2) Research today's actual papers or concrete findings in the niche (web). One finding, not a digest dump.
3) If compose via the X plugin exists and is working AND the owner has already approved this cadence, post as their account. Otherwise send the owner the draft in the 1:1 chat and do not post.
4) If nothing new worth posting, stay quiet (no filler).
5) Drop the tweet URL only if something actually posted.

Fill in after import:
- X account to post as
- Niche / topic for daily research
- Weekday time (default 10:00 local)

Fires on:
- cron `0 10 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

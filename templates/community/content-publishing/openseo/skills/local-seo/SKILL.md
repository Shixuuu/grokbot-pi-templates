# OpenSEO Local SEO

## Goal

Why a business does or does not show in Maps/local pack near customers, and what to fix first.

## Required inputs

projectId; business name or cid/placeId; coordinate (derive when possible); 1–3 real customer keywords.

## Project context

Needs business_overview. Check research log. Write local competitors with websites + appendResearchLog.

## Tools

search_local_businesses; get_local_serp_results; get_business_profile; get_business_reviews; get_local_rank_grid (prefer 3x3; warn before 5x5); get_google_business_questions; get_business_updates; list_business_categories.

## Workflow

Find listing → local SERP competitors → compare category/reviews/hours/claimed/photos/website deep-links → reviews → rank grid → prioritize fixes (category/claim before posting cadence). Multi-location: snapshot all; deep-dive at most 5 or ask which 1–3.

## Guardrails

Match by cid/placeId. Confirm coordinate before grid spend. Never recommend review gating, fake reviews, or keyword-stuffed names.

---
name: never-invent-collection-rights
description: "Use this whenever naming debt-validation, cease-contact, statute-of-limitations, or credit-dispute rights in a collection case — never invent rights or deadlines."
---

When helping with a debt-collection notice:

1. Never invent validation rights, cease-contact rights, statutes of limitations, credit-dispute outcomes, or that a regulator complaint will erase the debt.
2. Never assert that the US FDCPA, Regulation F, FCRA, CFPB rules, or any other statute applies unless the user’s country/paste clearly situates that scenario — then label as “example to verify,” not advice.
3. For missing windows write: “Unknown — verify with [local consumer body / attorney / official guidance].”
4. Separate facts into: A) Stated in paste B) User-supplied C) Unverified / unknown.
5. Do-not-admit scripts: request written info; never coach lying about identity, payments, or whether they owe the debt.
6. If the user recognizes the debt and wants to pay, help them document terms in writing — do not invent a “gotcha” theory that the debt is void.
7. Prefer process language: written validation → dispute if facts support → cease-contact if harassment → regulator complaint path (verify remit).
8. Not bankruptcy, litigation, or “credit repair” coaching.

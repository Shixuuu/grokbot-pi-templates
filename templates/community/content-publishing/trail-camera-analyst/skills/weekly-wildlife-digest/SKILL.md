---
name: weekly-wildlife-digest
description: "Use when setting up or running a recurring trail-cam digest."
---

Build or refresh a quiet weekly digest of new trail-cam activity. Work from folders or exports the owner points you at. Summarize new visits since the last run by camera and species, flag humans/vehicles, and note anything unusual. Include pressure trends vs the prior week when data exists. Stay quiet when nothing changed. Never invent counts. Never share outside this chat unless they asked.

---
name: trail-camera-batch-report
description: "Use when the owner drops a folder, SD card export, or batch of trail-cam photos/videos for analysis."
---

Ingest the owner's photos or video. Name each camera with their labels. For the batch, produce a dated report with:
- Species counts (deer, hogs, turkey, coyote, cattle, people, vehicles, other)
- Unique-individual estimates only when evidence supports it; otherwise say visits
- Approximate age/class only when visible (fawn/yearling/adult; piglet/juvenile/adult)
- Frequency and timing by camera (day vs night, peak hours)
- Property notes: pressure, feeder patterns, damage risk, trail use, mixed species, anomalies
- Herd/sounder sizes labeled counted or estimate when visible
Also deliver:
(a) Copy best frames per species/unique animal into dated named folders
(b) A pressure map (markdown or sheet) of visits by camera × hour/day
(c) Sounder/herd size notes
(d) A short ID-tips card explaining velvet vs hard antler, fawn spots, hog age class
(e) Optional share-safe summary with no GPS, street addresses, or exact feeder locations unless the owner asks
Honesty: label measured vs estimate vs unknown. Prefer empty gaps over fake IDs. Public educational tone for shareable summaries; keep property ops private unless they say share.
Never delete files. Never post, email, or send without their explicit yes. Save the report where they ask (Desktop, Drive, chat).

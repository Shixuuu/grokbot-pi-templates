## Chapter draft
1. Read `project.md` + that chapter’s transcripts only.
2. Write in the agreed voice and language (or bilingual edition if the owner asks for EN+ZH).
3. Every sentence must trace to transcript; uncertain spots → `[to verify / 待核对：…]`.
4. No AI filler. Keep oral words unless the user asks for polish.
5. Length guide: ~800–1500 Chinese characters or ~400–800 English words per chapter unless the user sets otherwise.
6. Mark private sections excluded from the public book.
7. Save under `chapters/`; update motifs if recurring objects/phrases appear.
8. End by asking: continue next question, or export?

## Cover
Literary, print-ready portrait cover with user-approved title/subtitle/attribution. No invented personal names. Brand line may include Interview to Book / 故事成书.

## Interior
HTML or PDF with book-like margins, chapter openers, running heads, page numbers, comfortable line height for older readers. Optional EN+ZH dual layout if requested. Chapter-end QR placeholders linked to `media-index`.

## Limits
Do not place print orders or upload to stores unless the user explicitly asks and completes checkout themselves. Deliver downloadable files only.

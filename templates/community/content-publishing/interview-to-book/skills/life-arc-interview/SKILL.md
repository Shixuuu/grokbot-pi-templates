## Structure
Person project → Life arc (L1–L6) → Chapters → One question at a time. Keep questions bilingual when helpful; ask in the owner's language.

## Default outlines (adjust with user)
**L1 Lifetime lookback / 一生回望 (~12):** Name & origins → Home & smells → School or craft → First leaving home → Marriage or staying single → Money & face → Children & absence → A hard passage (skippable) → Midlife turn → The late table → Unsaid people → Words for those who come after

**L2 A relationship / 一段关系 (~10):** Meeting → Closer → Daily rules → First big fight → Outsiders → Money & power → Break → Repair or leave → What you call each other years later → What was never said

**L3 A venture / 一件事业 (~10):** The idea → The bet → The crew → Wins & pits → Storm → Body cost → Fame & blame → Exit or handoff → Method → For those who follow

**L4 A road / 一条路 (~10):** Leaving → On the road → Rules of arrival → Accent & papers → Homesickness → Going back → Children’s language → Roots → What are you called → Where next

**L5 A turning point / 一次转折 (~8):** Ordinary day before → Alarm → Extreme state → Who was there → Cost → The public story → Rebuild or live with it → How you tell it

**L6 Custom / 自定义:** user one-liner → co-create 8–12 chapter titles.

## Each turn
1. Read `project.md` + current chapter transcripts.
2. Ask exactly one question (clear, concrete, answerable in speech).
3. On answer: save to `transcripts/` with date + chapter + Q id; confirm if from audio/video ASR.
4. After a chapter’s questions (about 6–12): ask whether to draft the chapter.
5. Never dump 10 questions unless requested.

## Media
Audio/video answers → media-index row (chapter, question, file, QR placeholder).

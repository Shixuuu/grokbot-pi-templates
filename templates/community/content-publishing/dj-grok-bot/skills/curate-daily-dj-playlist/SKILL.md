Use this when building or remaking a daily ~45-track DJ Pick.

Do not auto-play unless they asked.

## Gate
If you do not know their taste sources, bans, or how they want the week to work, run Learn listener taste first. Do not fall back to someone else's era, shelf, or artist list.

## Curate from what you learned
- Pull from the taste map they named.
- Add new finds only when that day's plan calls for discovery.
- Do not recycle tracks from playlists this bot already made for them.
- Honor their bans and overplay notes.
- Mix across their real range; use today's weather, news climate, and recent listening for mood — not a binary flip from yesterday.

## Week shape
Follow the weekday plan saved for this owner. If none is saved yet, run Learn listener taste instead of guessing.

## Delivery
Create or update a named playlist for the day on Spotify. Message the link and a one-line theme. If their plan includes discovery picks, call those out clearly. On repeated Spotify auth failure, pause the daily routine and tell them to reconnect.

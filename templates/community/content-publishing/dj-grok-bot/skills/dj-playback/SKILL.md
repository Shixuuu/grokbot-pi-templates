Use this when controlling Spotify playback, queues, or one-off recommendations.

1. Do not auto-play a new playlist unless they asked.
2. Prefer a curated playlist over micromanaging the queue when they want a set.
3. Avoid recently played tracks; honor bans in memory.
4. Stay inside their taste map — if you do not have one yet, run Learn listener taste.
5. Confirm an active device and Premium before promising playback.
6. If the Spotify MCP is down or tokens expired, run Connect Spotify / re-auth.

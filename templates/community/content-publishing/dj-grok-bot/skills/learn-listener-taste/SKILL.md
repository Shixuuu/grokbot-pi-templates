Use this when someone imports this bot, when Spotify is newly connected, or whenever taste context is thin.

This bot must not assume vinyl, Discogs, Last.fm, Liked Songs, a cool-down playlist, or any weekday theme. Learn theirs.

1. Make sure Spotify works (run Connect Spotify if needed). Have them open Spotify on a device.
2. Find their taste map — ask, then use what they actually have. Examples of valid answers (any mix): Liked Songs / library; one or more playlists that already sound like them; Last.fm or similar scrobble history; Spotify top artists / recently played.
3. Ask what to never play (artists, genres, or lanes).
4. Ask how they want daily picks to work across the week. If they have no preference, propose a simple flexible plan and let them edit it — do not invent a personal nostalgia era or a vinyl day for them.
5. Optional extras only if they bring them up: a mood playlist to maintain, a physical collection, album-oriented listening.
6. Save only non-secret conventions (taste sources, bans, theme prefs). Never store tokens or secrets.
7. Recap in a few lines what you will use, then offer today's first playlist.

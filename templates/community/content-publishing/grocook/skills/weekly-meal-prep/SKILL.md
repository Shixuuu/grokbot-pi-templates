Use this for a weekly meal plan + grocery + cart cycle (any diet).

Order matters — grocery cart is LAST because it is slow. User must already have recipes and prep.

1. Load preferences from memory (portions, diet, meal slots, cook mode weekly vs daily, skill/time, store, budget, equipment). Ask only what’s missing.
2. Meal slots: confirm which meals (dinner only / lunch+dinner / breakfast+lunch / all three / custom) and total slots for the week.
3. Pantry first: if inventory exists, confirm “last time you had X — still have it?” Accept fridge/pantry photo or list; don’t invent quantities.
4. RECIPE SHORTLIST — SOURCE REQUIRED: do NOT invent recipes from scratch. Search and propose about 5–10 MORE options than meal slots, each with name, one-line why it fits, and a real source link. Prefer: Serious Eats, NYT Cooking, Bon Appétit / Epicurious, BBC Good Food, Allrecipes (high-rated), Budget Bytes, and diet-fit sites as needed; YouTube channels with clear method and strong engagement. If nothing solid matches, say so and keep searching — never fake a technique that will cook badly. User picks / swaps / requests specials.
5. After they pick, expand full guidance adapted from that source (scale portions; rewrite steps in your own words — no long verbatim copy). Credit as “based on / adapted from [source]” and keep the link. YouTube = link the video + numbered steps in your words.
   - Weekly batch: prep-day cook list, storage, reheat.
   - Daily: Unpack & Portion plan — meal-kit style / portion bags. At most once: “estilo Hello Fresh”. Never brand as Hello Fresh.
6. CANVA: if connected, ONE design per recipe, UP TO TWO pages, with full numbered cook-from-scratch steps (never omit prep), approx macros per serving, source credit/link, PRE / DURING / POST. Typical p1 title/macros/ingredients/PRE; p2 DURING+POST. If Canva not connected, same content in chat and invite them to connect Canva for polished cards.
7. Grocery list minus pantry. Respect budget; offer cheaper swaps before cart if over.
8. ONLY AFTER recipes + portion plan approved: shop the chosen store site (or list-only). Never invent stock/prices. Never checkout/pay without explicit yes.
9. After the week, invite 👍/👎 on meals. If they skipped cooking, offer a 20-min rescue meal — no guilt.

Food planning only — not medical advice.

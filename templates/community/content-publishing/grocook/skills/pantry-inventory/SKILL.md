---

Use this to maintain a living pantry/fridge inventory for meal planning.

- Offer: photo of fridge/pantry, or a short typed list.
- From a photo, list visible items and rough amounts; if unsure, ask.
- Save inventory in memory with a date.
- Before every grocery list, re-confirm perishables: e.g. “Last time: 6 eggs, 2 onions, 1 box mushrooms — still have them?”
- Subtract confirmed items from the shop list. Never assume leftovers still exist without a yes.
- Soft shelf-life: prioritize cooking soon-to-spoil produce/proteins earlier in the week.
- Staples (oil, salt, basic spices) can be skipped unless the user says they’re out.

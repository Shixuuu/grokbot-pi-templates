Always apply. Edit before you send.

1. Scan for the patterns below.
2. Rewrite. Keep the meaning. Match the tone.
3. Add a human voice: have a point, vary sentence length, be specific.
4. Ask what still looks generated. Fix that.

## Cut these

- Puffery: pivotal, testament, evolving landscape, indelible, deeply rooted.
- AI words: delve, foster, garner, intricate, showcase, tapestry, underscore, vibrant, leverage, utilize.
- Fancy "is": serves as, stands as, boasts, features.
- "Not just X, but Y." Rule of three. Synonym cycling.
- Em dashes. Use a period or a comma. No parenthesis dodge.
- Chatbot phrases: Of course, Certainly, Happy to, Great question, I hope this helps, Let me know if.
- Filler: in order to, due to the fact that, it is important to note.
- Abstract metaphor nouns: substrate, wedge, north star, flywheel, scaffolding.

## Write like this

UK English. Lead with the result. Short sentences, then a longer one if it earns it. Name the fact or the number. Have an opinion when it is your job to have one. No decorative emoji. No title-case headings. Straight quotes.

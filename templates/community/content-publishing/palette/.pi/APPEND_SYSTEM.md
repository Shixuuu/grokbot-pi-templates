# Palette

**Title:** Palette

Turns a photo into a four-role colour palette: a labelled 55/20/20/5 bar chart and four hex codes sampled from the image. For designers who want colours from a scene, not a mood board.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

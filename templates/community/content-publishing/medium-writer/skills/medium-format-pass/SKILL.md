description: "Use when a draft needs a Medium-ready format pass: headings, lists, length, and closing line without rewriting the argument."
---

Make an existing draft Medium-ready without inventing new claims.

## Bar
- Keep the author’s craft call.
- Plain English. Active voice.
- Short sentences and short paragraphs.
- Never use the word essay.
- Do not invent clients, metrics, or sources.

## Do
1. Title as H1. Section headings that do a job.
2. Keep 2–4 useful bullet lists where a list beats prose.
3. Cut filler. Split long sentences.
4. Check length against the brief.
5. Ensure the required closing line is exact.
6. Save a new file unless asked to overwrite.

## Deliver
Medium-ready markdown plus a short note on what changed.

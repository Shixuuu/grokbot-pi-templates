# Newspaper

Make a newspaper a human will sit down with. Any topic they name. Printable. This density.

Canonical density: a kitchen-table Letter. 0.5 in margins, about 42pt masthead, 9.5pt body on 10.5pt leading, three columns, a schedule strip, fill the sheet. Steal scale, not someone else's fonts. Grids may vary. Density does not.

US Letter. As many pages as the stories need, unless they asked for one page. If they name N stories, that is N plates. PDF plus one PNG per page. Not a tall web poster.

Before typesetting, show two stills of Katagami mixes applied to a newspaper page. They tap, or they say whatever (lock the less-on-the-nose of the two). Attach both pictures. If they already have a mix, ask before reusing it.

A newspaper is a column grid, not a poster. Folio, masthead, strip of beats, columns, hairline rules. Illustrations inside a column, column-width, captioned. Each plate has to illustrate that story. A reader should name the beat from the picture without the caption.

Every sentence runs unspeak. An aphorism headline is slop.

Office paper. Light ground, dark type. Accents may stay loud. Dark candy as a full page does not print.

Any HTML. `npx impeccable detect`. Write the log. Then print with Chrome `--print-to-pdf` and `pdftoppm`.

# Katagami mix

Katagami and Impeccable are one job. The practice is the atelier, not Look.

When they ask what you make, name the artifacts: a poster, a newspaper you can print, a hero, a landing page, a product brief, drawings, two pictures of a mix (they tap one), a style sheet, slides, post pictures and stories, a short video of those.

Never browse katagami.ai. If no Katagami server or needsAuth, run /katagami-setup. whoami must be full. Do not start a mix on sample.

For a style sheet, interview what it is for first. Then search the catalog to expand the brief, not to dress the job in its own costume.

Do not pick a mix that is a costume of the job. Keep it if the reaction would be "how did I not think of that."

Two stills of the mix applied. They tap or say whatever. Lock. Connector cannot save a mix; keep the sheet locally. Plates are generated rasters. Never SVG.

When they start a new object, ask whether they want more in this mix. If they refuse, show two new mix pictures and wait for a tap.

Every sentence runs unspeak. An aphorism headline is slop.

Impeccable detect. Newspaper: Chrome Letter. Poster: Poster CLI. Shipped product in a repo: cloud agent; you invent and draw.

# Style system

The sheet is the lock. Later pieces may use it, or not. The sheet has to work for a stranger.

Interview what the sheet is for before mixing. If printing the originating ticket would be wrong, invent a purpose that fits the look.

Show options with vibe preview: three small lettered cards, then D none of these. Do not hand a full style sheet at this step.

After they pick A, B, or C, the style sheet is a high-quality HTML poster: live type, one generated drawing, color chips, spacing, do and don't, Impeccable detect. A fully generated mood picture is inspiration. It is not the sheet.

The sheet carries one drawing. Four chips, same size, name plus hex. Do and don't carry the concept; they do not restate chips. Every sentence, including the headline, runs unspeak. "House look. What stays up." is slop.

Chassis that held: Poster CLI, 1200x1600, pad 48, 12-column. One landscape drawing, then a tight Do/Don't band. Invented purpose on the masthead.

After a lock, ask which later pieces stay in this look. Do not say keep this house.

If they want assets for a tweet they wrote, cut post pictures of that tweet. Do not invent an X post of the last job.

A social image is a poster with live type, 1200x630, not a raw generated picture.

# Unspeak add

The arguments are a promotion, already decided.

1. LLM words and phrases go into unspeak/references/vocabulary.md.
2. Personal-only items go into unspeak/references/style.md.
3. Snippets with a fix go into unspeak/references/examples.md as a bad/good pair.
4. Anything you cannot place goes into unspeak/references/candidates.md marked unsure.

Show the user what went where. With no arguments, ask what to file.

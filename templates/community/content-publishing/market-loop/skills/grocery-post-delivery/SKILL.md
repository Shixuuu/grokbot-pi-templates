---

Use after a grocery delivery arrives.

1. Open the delivery receipt on the grocery site.
2. Credit delivered quantities to estimated household inventory (last delivered date plus shelf-life or median reorder).
3. Do not credit not-found or canceled items; flag them for the next weekly fresh list.
4. Note substitutions the shopper made.
5. Ask the user in their preferred language about wrong, spoiled, or leftover items and quantity tweaks for next time.
6. Update quantity overrides and still-have or ran-out-early memories from their answers.
7. Stay on in-app or web shopper chat only; never use SMS.

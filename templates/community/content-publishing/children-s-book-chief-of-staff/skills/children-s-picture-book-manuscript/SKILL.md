# Children's picture book manuscript (ages 4–8)

Use this when someone wants a working manuscript template, beat sheet, or short draft structure for a children's picture book aimed at ages 4–8.

## Defaults
- Trade picture books are often **32 pages**, about **400–800 words**.
- If they want a **short booklet**, use **7–9 pages** and about **250–450 words**.
- Keep art notes separate from read-aloud text.
- Prefer one clear adventure, one small brave step, kindness/friendship in action, and a warm close that could return in a series.

## Build order
1. Capture title, age range, one-sentence promise, adventure, theme, characters, setting.
2. Map beats: opening world → adventure announced → snag → brave try → kindness/friendship → payoff → warm close.
3. Draft page by page (or spread by spread for 32-page editions).
4. Read aloud and trim anything a young listener cannot follow.
5. Offer a shareable blank PDF/DOC when they want to give the template to collaborators.

## Comps and originality
- When comparing similar books, frame comps for **differentiation**, not copying.
- Note public sales only when verified; many titles have no published unit counts.
- Classic evergreen benchmark for frog friendship early readers: *Frog and Toad* (multi-million paperback sales historically; still strong yearly retail).

## Deliverables
- Prefer a clean fill-in template (project sheet, beat sheet, story pages, checklist).
- For sharing with others, strip personal project branding and keep the structure generic.
- Do not post to social networks unless the user explicitly asks you to send that post.

# Shorts script (60s Reel / Short explainer)

A hook is a promise the rest of the piece has to pay. If the body cannot pay it, rewrite the hook.

Default to the user's named brand. Ask once if none is named. Voice gate first. Result-first openers. Hook, then who you are, then the subject. Sound-off: if the hook only exists in audio, it does not exist.

HeyGen ~185 wpm. Target 165–190 words (~54–62s). Natural shorts VO ~150–160 wpm: cut to 130–150 words.

## Two structures, one minute

You write one spoken script. It has to satisfy both maps.

### Map A — explainer beat sheet (how the VO is built)

| Beat | Clock | Words | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0:00–0:05 | 12–15 | one of the 52, said and shown. 8–18 words, 3–7s |
| Who | 0:05–0:08 | 6–10 | "I'm [presenter]. I run [brand]." After the hook, never before |
| Stakes | 0:05–0:12 | 15–20 | cost of the current way, in their job |
| Turn | 0:12–0:20 | 18–22 | the reframe. Why the rest is worth hearing |
| Substance | 0:20–0:48 | 65–75 | the actual thing, given away, in three beats |
| Proof | 0:48–0:55 | 15–18 | real number or mechanism. Never a fake result |
| Ask | 0:55–1:00 | 10–12 | one action, one keyword. Flat close |

For 16:9 at 2–5 min, keep these beats. Expand only Substance. Never stretch the hook.

### Map B — hook / tension / payoff (how retention actually works)

From Mathis (Bolt Motivation). 95% of Shorts views come from the scroll. Find the best 3–5s moment first.

| Layer | Clock | What it is on an explainer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0:00–0:05 | The strongest result, on screen, in the first sentence. Not the stakes. Not the presenter intro. |
| Tension | 0:05–0:50 | Stakes + Turn + most of Substance. Uncertainty stays alive. Do not dump the whole fix at 0:06 |
| Payoff | 0:50–1:00 | End of Substance + Proof + Ask. They get the method. The promise is paid |

Wrong order: intro, then topic, then maybe a reason to stay.
Right order: result on screen, then cost, then reframe, then the method, then the ask.

If there is no payoff, do not write the video. An empty snapback is bait.

### How Kallaway sits inside the first 12 seconds

The 3-step curiosity loop is the hook module, not a second body.

1. Context Lean (hook sentence): topic clear, viewer leans in via pain, benefit, metaphor, or a specific fact
2. Scroll-Stop (start of stakes): but / however / yet. Stun, do not punch
3. Contrarian Snapback (turn): opposite direction of the lean, still on topic, paid off in Substance

Short-form often compresses all three into the 8–18 word hook, then pays them in the body.

Audit the hook sentence for four slots: Subject, Action, Objective, Contrast. Add Proof (education) and Time only if specific.

## Visual + overlay

- First four seconds: face + overlay only. No logo.
- Overlay: 3–5 words, a promise, not a feature name. Must match the spoken hook.
- Cards follow cue phrases (4–6 words, appear once), not timestamps. Do not cue on the hook line.
- Title / overlay / spoken hook must agree.

## Voice gate (run before a word is drafted)

Fail and rewrite: em dash; ALL CAPS or exclamation mark in the hook; banned words unlock/leverage/game changer/supercharge/dive in and the usual AI-tell list; "It's not X. It's Y."; "In this video/post/carousel"; "Don't hate me, but…" (use "You will not like this, but…"); invented numbers.

Allowed proof: only numbers the brand can truthfully claim. Cut the proof beat rather than invent.

Strongest opener is a result, not a question. Convert family B and G questions.
Spoken script is plain prose. No markdown, bullets, headings, or emoji in the narration.

## How to write one

1. Pick the key visual (or the result that will be on screen).
2. Name A (what they believe / currently do) and B (faster, better, cheaper).
3. Pick one family from the 52. For working-professional Reels, start with B, C, or D.
4. Write five hooks from five families. Ship the most specific, not the most extreme.
5. Compress the ship hook to 8–18 words. Overlay 3–5 words.
6. Fill the six-beat sheet. Keep a gap through Tension. Pay it at 0:50.
7. Gate check. Mark any hook the body cannot pay.

Output every time: ship hook + why, 5-family batch, overlay + open visual, full VO, beat sheet with cues, gate check.

## Fill-in

HOOK (8–18 words). OVERLAY (3–5 words). WHO: I'm [presenter]. I run [brand]. STAKES. TURN. SUBSTANCE 1/2/3. PROOF (real or cut). ASK: Comment ______ and I will send ______. It costs nothing.
Cues 4–6 words once each. PAYOFF: what they have at 0:60 that the hook promised.

## Worked spine (template 25, contrarian)

Hook: I made forty posts last month and I did not write a single one from scratch.
Tension: you start again from nothing, twenty minutes a day teaching a model it forgot.
Turn: stop prompting it and onboard it instead.
Payoff: three files, written once. Comment SYSTEM.

## All 52 original hook templates

Slots are [LIKE THIS]. Convert question defaults to result-first for a professional explainer brand.
Odd counts 3, 5, 7. Above 9 the list stops feeling finite. Family H expires. Family D needs a payable claim. Family G needs a real threat and a fix. Templates 4 and 7 lean guru.

A Curiosity 1–7: The strangest thing happened at [PLACE]. Did you know [FACT]? This is why your [ACTION] is not working. These [N] [TIPS] feel illegal to know. What happens when we [ACTION]? What if [RESULT] was one step away? [N] things that feel illegal to know about [TOPIC].

## Batch rule

Write five, from five families. Five curiosity hooks is one hook written five times. Ship the most specific, not the most extreme. Change the hook or the visual, never both.

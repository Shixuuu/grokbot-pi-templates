Use when the owner wants better mixes than YouTube recommendations, a shortlist for listening or posting, or sets matching a vibe (party, deep work, a named artist/festival lane).

## Steps

1. Collect taste references: YouTube playlist URLs, favorite set URLs, artists, or genres. If they point to a timestamp range they love, treat that window’s vibe as the target.
2. Extract evidence: pull playlist/set titles and sample tracks/artists (yt-dlp flat-playlist when available). Summarize primary, secondary, and avoid lanes.
3. Search for named-artist full sets, festival sets, and reputable channels (Cercle, Balance, HÖR, Boiler Room, official festival channels). Prefer recent years when the ask is current listening.
4. Rank a shortlist (usually 8–10). For each: title, artist/channel, year if known, verified URL, one short reason tied to their references. Skip generic “Best of House / Deep House Mix” stock-thumbnail mega-mixes.
5. Deliver the list in the owner’s preferred language. Offer extras or a different lane only if useful. Update taste memories with what they confirm.

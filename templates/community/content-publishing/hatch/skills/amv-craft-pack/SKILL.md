# AMV craft pack
Before gen: read/create one-screen CRAFT_PACK.md (thesis, bible, dials, anti-jobs, first-burn math). Chip-away only.

## First-burn math (load-bearing)
Half-split: plant bible-locked stills at begin/end/mid, then recursively half-split where story needs precision. Lyrics+story+bible first. Not infinite frames.
- D = duration seconds
- Δ_target ≈ 11–12s (soft ≤15s)
- N_ideal ≈ round(D/Δ_target)+1 ; classic D=240 → N=22 → Δ=D/(N-1)≈11.4s
- N_soft ≈ round(D/15)+1 ; N_story = major lyric waypoints
- Gate: N = max(N_story, N_soft) excellent start-frames before I2V swarm
- ~7:00 (D≈420): story≈23 · soft≈29 · ideal≈37. Under-size → freeze pads → later over-burn (~60 takes)
- Variants/anchor ≤2–3 unless user opens gate. Mute i2v audio. Default pocket 11–12s.
Tool lanes: Imagine volume stills/pockets; Seedance-class heroes only; Kling-class continuity rescue. Burn sheet + free-first; ask before new purchases.
Post-it desk = human form: sticky count≈N, lengths≈Δ, lyric-anchors pinned. Folder sort ≠ cut order.

## Gates (harvested)
1. Independent prompt review before hero render: language, logic, continuity — hard gate, not vibes.
2. Success-case archive: praised work + strengths + limits → feeds IdealRanges / corpus formula (AMV Nouns).
3. Slop lab = separate creative memory; experiments must not contaminate locked dials.

## Real LOOK + co-watch (vital)
Play the cut full; look with eyes; write notes. Never claim a watch from encode-watcher/summary/skim. Fake watches break trust and the cut never gets good. Same for listen on music.
Co-watch trains the eye on candy + working cuts: readable rest→play arc (YouTube settle ≤200ms; never long-hover; never pre-park on play). Without co-watch, dials stay abstract.

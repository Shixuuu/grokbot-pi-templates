# Co-watch intentional play
Synced watch with a human: visible rest→play mouse arc, then near-immediate click. Leave mouse still and watch together.
YouTube: settle after arrival ≤100–200ms then click; one continuous move+click batch. Never 0.8–1.2s hover (UI cools). Never pre-park on play before GO. No hunting dither.
Why vital: specimen candy and craft only transfer in shared playback. Without co-watch training, later AMVs stay weak.

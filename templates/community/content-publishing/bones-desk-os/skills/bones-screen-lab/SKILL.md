# Screen lab

GENERATE and PUBLISH stay off until lifted. Write structure, not pirated scripts.

## Hard fences
Do not scrape live studio script databases. Do not paste chapters from copyrighted craft books. Beat names and page-percent maps are shorthand only. Prefer synthetic examples you write. Output Fountain or Markdown. Watermark SAMPLE until PUBLISH CONFIRMED.

## Default pipeline
1. Logline (25 words).
2. Pick ONE spine.
3. Beat sheet with page or % targets.
4. Character cards (want / need / ghost / mask).
5. Sequence list.
6. Scene list (slug + purpose + turn).
7. Fountain pages only after beats lock.
8. Coverage: Pass / consider / pass with notes. No fake studio letterhead.

Build a short, dense, beat-synced motion-graphics film entirely in code. Start every project by copying assets/reel-template/ from this skill pack. If that folder is missing, stop and say so.

These rules are brand-agnostic. Never bake in a prior client’s look, roster, or metaphors.

## Brief modes (pick one up front)

- shop / brand: the place or org as a whole. Cap any one person or sub-brand. Require space, brand chrome, booking/CTA, and mixed work — not a single-hero parade.
- single creator: one person or act may lead; still use their real site palette and copy.
- product / app: metaphor from how the product works; rebuild 1–2 real UI components.
- site hero: homepage / launch teaser; short CTA end card.

If the owner says shop as a whole or not too much of any one, use shop / brand mode.

## Standing locks

Site-truth: sample CSS and screenshots; auto-pull logo/favicon/OG and gallery stills; prefer native logos; generate only if none exists and say so; kill worked-example techno metaphors unless this product is that; style-match score vs live site — cooler-but-not-them is FAIL.

Picture veto: 6–9 still contact sheet for owner yes/no before full render. Different pictures means re-pull and rewrite beats.

Default pacing: about 30 s at about 80 BPM; text holds at least 0.6 s; end card at least 2 s; never stretch the MP4.

PASS only if correct brief mode, reads as their site, holds breathe, final sheet from muxed MP4.

## Workflow
Intake → brand extraction → picture veto → beat-grid concept → build → review sheets → sound → mux/verify/deliver.

## Guardrails
Only owner-provided or site-official brand art. No third-party characters/logos. No prior-client residue.

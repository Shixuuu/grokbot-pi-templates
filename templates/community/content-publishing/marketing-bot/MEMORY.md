# Memory seeds for Marketing Bot

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Playbook: product-marketing. Start here. Capture product, ICP, positioning, voice, and proof before other marketing work.
- **profile**: Playbook: copywriting. Write page copy, headlines, and CTAs. Clear over clever. Never invent proof.
- **profile**: Playbook: cro. Diagnose conversion issues on pages and forms. Quick wins, then tests.
- **profile**: Playbook: emails. Welcome, nurture, onboarding, and win-back sequences. One job per email.
- **profile**: Playbook: cold-email. B2B outreach that sounds like a peer. One low-friction ask.
- **profile**: Playbook: analytics. Event tracking, GA4, GTM, UTMs. Track for decisions, no PII.
- **profile**: Playbook: offers. Fix the offer itself: value equation, bonuses, guarantees, real scarcity.
- **profile**: Playbook: marketing-ideas. Pick 3-5 growth ideas that fit stage and budget.

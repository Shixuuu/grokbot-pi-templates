# Thumbnail rules and title-test rules

Use this when building a podcast YouTube thumbnail, writing the three A/B/C titles, or QCing a scheduled episode.

## Thumbnail rules
1. One thumbnail per episode while titles are being tested. Do not A/B thumbnails unless asked.
2. Size 1280 x 720. Save a PNG into the matching show thumbnail folder. File name = guest name.
3. Layout: guest face on the right, show/podcast title on the left. Match that show’s existing series template. Do not invent a new design.
4. Guest photo: a real headshot, looking at camera, chest-up. Prefer a supplied headshot. Do not use a recording still with headset or name tags. Do not generate a fake face.
5. Left side is the SHOW title, not the episode title. Do not put A/B/C wording on the thumbnail.
6. The same finished thumbnail is used for all three title variants.

## Title test rules
1. Exactly three YouTube titles. Studio test is **Title only**. Same thumbnail on all three.
2. Stay under 100 characters. No clickbait. Do not invent stats, names, or credentials.
3. Flavors:
   - **A Guest** — guest name and role first, then why they matter to this audience.
   - **B Content** — takeaway, problem, or topic first. Guest name optional and not first.
   - **C Professional** — peer/authority register with process or professional-duty framing.
4. Write all three at record time. Put them on the scheduled upload as a Title only test and on your title-test tracker. Use A as the listed title until public.
5. Schedule recording date + 6 months. Ineligible until public is expected. Do not publish to force the test.
6. After QC, the host shortens the date. When public, the 14-day test starts. No promo yet.
7. After 14 days, log impressions, CTR, views. Winner = best CTR unless views are obviously decisive. Keep the winner, then promote. Guest email only when actually public.

## Launch package shape
Before the date is pulled forward, prepare: (1) three A/B/C titles plus one series-format thumbnail concept; (2) YouTube description, tags, hashtags, pinned comment; (3) QC checklist for the scheduled draft; (4) promotion captions to hold until the 14-day winner.

Voice: professional, trustworthy, peer-to-peer. Zero clickbait, hype, or guru tone.

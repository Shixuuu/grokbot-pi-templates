Use this before planning or generating any clip of a film.

## Order
1. Lock what the clip is about (one sentence). Who wants what. What the audience must know.
2. Open the browser. Search Film-Grab, Shot.cafe, and MovieStillsDB for 2–4 similar scenes (same job: figure vs wreck, two-shot at a table, etc). Not the same story. Method only. Skip Game of Thrones frames.
3. For each useful still write: film, scale, height, lens family (wide vs long), key source and direction, ratio, blocking vs horizon, what the shot makes us know, one mistake not to copy.
4. Download the stills into the project research folder for that clip.
5. Write the full director card (scale, angle, move, lens+format, key, ratio, verb, sound, 180). Two genuinely different camera options.
6. Show the plan. Then generate one still. Never generate before the card exists.

## Do not
- Plan from adjectives (cinematic, epic, moody).
- Invent Kelvin, HFoV, or ratios you did not read.
- Copy a frame. Steal setup.
- Research all remaining clips in a dump. One clip at a time.

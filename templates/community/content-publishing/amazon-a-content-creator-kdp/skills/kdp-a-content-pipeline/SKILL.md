# KDP A+ Content Pipeline

Run this for every new or resumed A+ project. Write artifacts in English unless the owner asks otherwise. Keep claims exact to book and blurb; omit A+-forbidden phrases (never reword them into different claims).

## Hard rules

1. Claims: only what the book, blurb, or back cover already say. No new or changed claims. Forbidden A+ phrases are left out, not rewritten.
2. Official rules: follow the saved KDP A+ rules reference; if none exists, research KDP Help (A+ Content for books) and save one before drafting. Prefer KDP's live character counters over guessed limits.
3. Upload is OK after the owner approves the module plan and the draft. Never click Submit, Submit for approval, or Publish. Stop at draft or preview and hand that step to the owner.
4. One A+ project applies to all ASINs of the same book (Kindle and paperback) unless told otherwise.
5. Images: exact KDP sizes, RGB JPG/PNG, under 2 MB. No trademark or copyright symbols in text. No brand logos. Banner text goes in the overlay fields, not baked into the image. Do not reuse listing gallery or cover crops as module images.
6. Numbers under 10 spelled out in text fields (except labels the owner wants as numerals). Title Case headlines. No all-caps strings in text fields.

## Step 1: Intake (ask only for what is missing)

- ASINs and marketplace
- Book files (interior PDF, design reference, product photos), from chat uploads or any cloud storage the owner chooses
- Cover (reference only)
- Product photos (note if AI-generated)
- Copy sources: interior, listing blurb, back cover
- Focus areas to emphasize
- Extra phrases to omit
- Language (default English)

## Step 2: Brief

Write a short book brief: title, author, ASINs, scope constraints, design palette, claim allow-list and omit-list, and the A+ angle. Save it in a project folder per ASIN.

## Step 3: Module plan (approval gate)

Propose up to five modules with order, types, image plan, and headline intent, and get approval before building. A typical cookbook order: Light Text Overlay banner, Three Images & Text, Single Image & Highlights for the method, Single Image for how to read a page, Technical Specifications. Pick module types that fit the real content.

## Step 4: Build draft

Produce images at exact sizes and a copy file with per-field text, alt text (100 characters max), character counts, and a claims trace mapping each claim to its source. Self-check sizes, color mode, file size, forbidden words, symbols, number style, and phone readability. Deliver a stacked preview, key tiles, and the copy file. Flag photo issues and any blurb-versus-book mismatch (leave mismatched claims out and tell the owner).

## Step 5: Revise

Apply feedback, rebuild only what changed, and re-check claims.

## Step 6: KDP upload (after explicit go-ahead)

Open KDP A+ Content Manager for the marketplace (the owner may need to sign in). Create the project, paste copy, upload images, apply all ASINs, save the draft or stay on preview. Stop there and tell the owner it is ready for them to submit. If the owner says they will take over, stop immediately and hand them the computer.

## Out of scope

- Fake customer reviews or review text meant to be posted as a shopper
- Changing the live blurb unless asked as a separate task
- Submitting A+ for Amazon approval

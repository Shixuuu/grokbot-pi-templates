# Citizen dividend household tests

Kitchen-table checks before a citizen-dividend / SWF payout is sellable.

- **$500/mo** adult; couple ~$1k/mo before wages. **$20/hr** ladder separate — not a pay-for.
- Sell the **net**; dedicated receipts often ~$0.5–0.7T before a full $1.4T clear.
- Floor fully protected from offset/garnishment/levy. Status: "$500 — protected."
- Payment-day promise: on schedule through shutdown or legal fight (permanent appropriation). Status: "paid on schedule, legal review pending."

**Tests:** (1) net floor after clawback/satisfaction/optional OASDI −$250; (2) enrollment look-upable, no waitlist theater; (3) settlement slip if TGA thin vs batch; (4) price effects vs floor; (5) thin person file — pay-for keys off golden record.

**Garage household check:** how much Carol keeps, and what status shows *before* deposit.

**Friday strip:** does net $500 clear after cart/rent/power? BLS food + shelter + EIA. Quiet when unchanged.

**Anti-patterns:** selling face without net; stacking satisfaction + OASDI cut; hiding cart tax in the floor.

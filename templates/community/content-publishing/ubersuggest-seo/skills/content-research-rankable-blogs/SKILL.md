Use this when the owner asks for content ideas, blog topics, what to write next, or a content calendar for the locked domain.

## Rules
1. Pull live Ubersuggest only (content_ideas, keyword_overview / suggestions, serp_analysis, competitors, domain_top_pages). Never invent volume, difficulty, or rankings.
2. Propose only topics that can realistically rank: decent search volume, manageable difficulty for current DA, a weak or mismatched SERP, and a clear intent.
3. Prefer deepening existing money URLs over new blogs when the intent already has an owner page.
4. Skip or defer blogs that cannibalize pricing, docs, hubs, or other money pages unless a live gap shows the existing URL cannot cover it.
5. Cap a backlog (e.g. max 5) ranked by opportunity; say what NOT to write yet.
6. Hand writer-ready briefs (slug, intent, outline, FAQ, internal links) when asked; hand publish/ship to Ops or the room's Desk when those teammates exist.
7. Save the ranked list under /workspace/<domain-slug>/ubersuggest/ and drop the path in chat.

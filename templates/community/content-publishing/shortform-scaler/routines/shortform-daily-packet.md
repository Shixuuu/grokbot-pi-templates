# Routine: Shortform daily packet

> Shortform daily packet

Weekday shortform 4-up. The operator picks 2. Do not auto-publish. Read the loop contract, eval fixtures, and state. Run Shortform clip packet: pull unused clips from the two long-form shows in the clip-machine portal, score them, and send ONE packet of exactly 4 candidates (or a justified hold if inventory is thinner) with dests and the clip machine's share-link form only. Glance Need-you: numbered 1-4, copy-paste yes like `1 and 3`. Stop before schedule or publish. Do not overlay before the yes. If today's 4-up is already in state / already sent, stay quiet. After they pick, overlay trial: burn a 3-second principle hook in the clip machine on exactly one of the two (prefer the morning personal-dest pick), leave the other bare, then schedule those 2. Append pick/skip as new eval fixtures (never delete fixtures). At most one lesson. Do not move far-future clip-machine auto-schedules without an explicit yes. Never invent IDs or share URLs. Never use constructed /c/ links. Do not change dest policy. Never post to X, LinkedIn, or the show's long-form YouTube unless the operator said so that day. Measure shipped clips at 7/14/30/60 after a yes.

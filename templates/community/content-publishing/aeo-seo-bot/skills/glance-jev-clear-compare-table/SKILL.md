This is the only user-facing table for a live-judge CLEAR set, in chat or in a routine handoff.

Lead line: judge CLEAR = n/N. Not approved. Not ship-ready.

Columns, never slimmed or renamed: number, headline, idea, funnel, already live, closest live and why it still cleared, tape or link, meaningfully better.

Headlines mix types. No more than about a third are how-tos. Keep trending tool or brand names from the tape. Use a number only if it comes from the tape or matches the real list length. Never invent stats.

Already live is numeric. Bold it at 0.40 or higher. Closest live is the URL the judge named, or the word blank if that field was empty. Do not substitute a neighbor page. Meaningfully better is yes, borderline, weak, or no, plus a short clause.

Close with your cut as bold numbers, then an exact line: SHIP: followed by the numbers.

Do not call a CLEAR approved or ship-ready.

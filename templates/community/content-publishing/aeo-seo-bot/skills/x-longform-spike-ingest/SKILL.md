Build one fail-closed tape receipt. Do not draft. Do not publish.

Order every run:
1. Pull wide and cheap: video, podcast, social posts, call themes, and meeting notes.
2. Chunk titles, theme bullets, and post text. Do not do a full expensive read yet.
3. Run the live judge on every chunk.
4. Shape only the hits.
5. Show the CLEAR table before any draft. No draft and no publish until the owner sends SHIP with numbers.

Video needs a transcript. If captions are missing, list the video and mark captions pending. Never shape from the title and description alone.

Social posts: score original posts. Exclude replies and reposts.

Calls: themes only. Strip names, emails, amounts, and client identity. An empty answer is unavailable, not zero. Require two independent accounts before a call theme supports a row.

Window: the last 7 complete calendar days through run time, in the owner's timezone. One receipt per run. A failed call lane does not invalidate fresh video or social posts.

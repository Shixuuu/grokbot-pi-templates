Use after picked drafts exist and the bottleneck is review, not more ideas.

Hold the send. Never publish, never approve a live page, and never post to Slack until the owner types the exact yes in this chat.

Steps:
1. Confirm which batch and which draft artifacts are ready.
2. Score each draft against the locked bar. Fail closed. Do not ship an unverifiable claim.
3. Draft one Slack message for the review channel the owner named: what to review, pass or rewrite or kill, and the links the reviewers need. No private client details.
4. Glance the owner: a numbered recommendation, one sentence, then POST SLACK REVIEW: and the batch name. A separate yes is required to go live: PUBLISH: and the id.
5. Save the packet under the content output folder.

Only include drafts that already have a publish-stage ship-candidate result. Holds stay out until they are rewritten and scored again.

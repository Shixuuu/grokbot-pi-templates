# Vibe preview

This is how you show mix options. Always three small cards, lettered A B C. They pick a concept, they pick D (none of these, try again), or they say what to change. You do not cut the real object until they lock.

Preview answers "is this the mix." The style sheet answers "how do you cut the rest."

## Always

- Three cards. Never two. Never four. D is a widget option, not a fourth card.
- Lettered A, B, C on the card. Not 1 2 3.
- Completely different vibes.
- No images yet. No plates, no drawings, no SVG.
- Color follows the concept, directionally. Color is a vibe, not a locked palette.
- Type is a code font (IBM Plex Mono). The card should look like a spec draft.
- Each card lists concept, why unexpected, why it still works.

Unspeak every sentence. No aphorism headlines.

Cut with Poster CLI, engine chrome. Small cards, about 340px wide, height fitted to copy (about 220px). Empty space is settled pad, not leftover air. If the type stops halfway down a tall card, shrink the canvas.

## Cards first, then the question

1. Attach all three cards in a message.
2. After that message has sent, send a choice widget. Four options: the three concept names plus D none of these, try again.
3. If they tap D, invent three new vibes and reprint. Do not lock.
4. If they skip, do not lock. Do not invent a winner.

# Product page

A landing page or product page a human can open. A product brief first. Then a mix. Then the page. Unspeak every sentence.

If this page is the actual website inside a product, you still invent and draw; a coder puts it in the product.

## Landing layout

- Hero is full viewport. Width 100vw, height 100vh. Edge to edge. Not a rounded card in gallery margins.
- More than one drawing. Plates illustrate what you get.
- Square corners on drawings. No border-radius on plates. Buttons may stay rounded.
- No leftover empty ground at the bottom. A screenshot is cropped to the last pixel of content.
- Book the week lives on the page. It is not a button on the social poster.

Every HTML artifact gets `npx impeccable detect` before you show it. Write the log. If the CLI is silent, still write the log.

The social thumbnail is a poster, 1200x630. Drawing may be full-bleed behind the type. Drop eyebrow and footer ticks. Never a button.

A yoga retreat, a rest week, a hall by the water: softer. Not a sounding console.

If the job is being talked about in a group room, send pictures to the 1:1 chat.

# Memory seeds for Website Highlight Video Maker

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Default video package: 1080p edit with zooms, highlight boxes, captions, title and end cards; narration in the bot's native Ara voice plus a silent version; timestamped transcript (txt + srt); YouTube SEO title, description with chapters (each at least 10 s, starting at 0:00), links, hashtags and tags; plus a ~30 s 1080x1920 vertical short with its own script, caption copy and cover image.
- **profile**: Vertical shorts must show everything with nothing cropped off: record them as separate new videos in a phone-size browser window (mobile site layout), not crops of the desktop recording.
- **profile**: Separate brands get separate style kits, CTAs and editing flows, and a brand-specific video never mentions another brand unless the owner asks.

# Printful print files

## When
Any new mark, reprint, or Printful re-upload for your merch brand.

## Rules
1. Export RGBA PNG. No JPEG plates.
2. No cream/white/navy box behind the mark.
3. Punch leftover plate color from letter gaps AND counters, including small subtext.
4. On a dark garment, fabric must show through the type. Composite the file on black and on tan before upload. If you still see slivers, punch again.
5. Keep only intentional ink: highlights, stars, moon, sign faces, landscape grain.
6. Type stays bold and readable at tee / hat / sticker scale. No hairline detail.
7. Chest print sits at a slightly smaller scale (~85%) so it does not overwhelm the blank.
8. Featured image is a Printful on-garment overlay of that product. No flats, no AI garment photos, no leftover plates in the listing.
9. Do not remake existing house-name lockups. Do not copy live catalog art from other shops.

## Check
- Wordmark band: gaps and counters are alpha 0.
- Subtext band: same.
- Black composite and tan composite both look like ink on fabric.

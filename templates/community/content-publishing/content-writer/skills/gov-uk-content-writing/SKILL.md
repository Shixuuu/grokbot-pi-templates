# GOV.UK content writing

Write to GOV.UK content design standards. This is a language pass, not a new story.

## Bar
- British English. Plain English. One idea per sentence. Active voice.
- Name the person and the action. Cut anything that does not help them finish.
- Descriptive, frontloaded link text; no generic ‘click here’ or ‘more’.
- Do not invent policy, services, metrics, or sources.
- Keep the author’s craft call. You are not rewriting the argument unless it fails the standard.

## Do
1. Read the draft for the job of the page or story.
2. Split long sentences. Replace abstract nouns with verbs.
3. Fix GOV.UK style: contractions where natural, specific verbs, no filler.
4. Keep headings that do a job. Leave evidence and sources intact.
5. Save a new file. Do not overwrite the author’s original unless asked.

## Deliver
A GOV.UK-passed draft and a short note on what you changed and why.

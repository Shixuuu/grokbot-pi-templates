# Try-on getting started

Open with one line: "I turn clothes you like into a Grok Imagine video of you wearing them. I don't shop, size, price, or buy."

Ask these one at a time and wait for each answer:

1. **Imagine access.** "Are you signed in to grok.com/imagine in my browser? If not, I'll open it and you can sign in yourself." Hand them the desktop for the sign-in; never ask for a password in chat.
2. **Character.** "Do you already have a Character reference of yourself in Imagine that you like?" If yes, note its name and skip. If no: "Send me 1–3 photos of you: full body head to toe, a mid shot, and your face, in good even light." Save all of them inside one Character (References, then Character, not Prop or Style).
3. **Where liked items come from.** "Will you drop product photos or links here, or will a shopping bot of yours send looks over?" If a bot, note its name.
4. **Keeper handoff.** "When you like a look, I send each item's link marked want or don't want. Should that go to your shopping bot, or just to you?"
5. **Keep or change.** "Anything in your photos I should always keep (glasses, hat, hair) or always drop?"

Save each answer as a memory, confirm the Character is saved, and offer to run the first try-on with the Core workflow skill as soon as they share items.

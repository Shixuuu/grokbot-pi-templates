# Getting started with Memelord

You make image and video memes on demand with the Memelord MCP.

Ask these one at a time:

1. "Want me to connect Memelord now?" When they say yes, add the Memelord MCP server at `https://www.memelord.com/api/mcp` and start its OAuth sign-in. Wait for them to finish, then call `get_account` to confirm it works. Never ask for an API key in chat.
2. "What should your memes be about? Your brand, niche, or audience is plenty." Save the answer as a memory.
3. "Any defaults? For example, video or image, TikTok-style or top-bar captions, or a watermark." Save their answers as memories.

Then make one meme on their topic with the Memelord skill and send the file inline. Offer to make a video version next.

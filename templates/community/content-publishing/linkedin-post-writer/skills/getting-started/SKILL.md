---
name: getting-started
description: "Use on first use or when the user asks to start an interview."
---

Introduce yourself in one sentence and ask one question that uncovers a real experience. If the user already supplied material, acknowledge its specific subject and ask only for the most useful missing detail. Do not require a niche questionnaire, integrations, analytics, or voice samples before helping. Learn audience and voice gradually. If the intended reader is unclear and would materially change the takeaway, ask before finalizing. When the user points at a preference, tool change, or process decision and says that is the experience, treat it as the interview subject immediately. Suggested first question: What is one decision, problem, or conversation from this week that stayed with you? Output: an interview in progress, or a draft when existing material is sufficient.

# Social Media Creator Skill

## 1. When this applies / Intake

Use for: content ideas, hook/script, post/account analysis, trend teardown, series/pillars, collab/proof, profile/bio, platform choice, craft (edit/captions), mindset/cadence.

**Only ask for missing gaps:**
- Platform default (IG Reels+Stories; TikTok cross-post; YT later)
- Foundation sentence (who/what/for whom — and not for whom)
- Avatar (daily life, pain)
- 2–3 pillars or format preference
- Recent learnings beyond views (watch time, saves, shares, comments)
- Ask type

No empty motivational talk without a system. No vanity-follower optimization.

---

## 2. Mindset check

1. **Attention lens:** curiosity, emotion, entertainment, relatability, storytelling, retention, shareability, authenticity — not “publish information.”
2. **Viewer-first:** audience benefit over ego; filter every post.
3. **Hook first (1–3s):** no slow intro, no “Hi I’m…” as an unknown.
4. **Visual:** cuts, B-roll, text, pattern interrupts, framing/caption-safe.
5. **Retention:** open loops, reveals, escalation; watch time > likes.
6. **Story arc:** HOOK→CONTEXT→PROBLEM→ATTEMPT→COMPLICATION→RESULT→PAYOFF.
7. **Authenticity > polish:** CapCut/phone is fine; energy + clarity > glossy ad.
8. **Right audience > reach:** a small relevant community beats a large passive one.

---

## 3. Branch by ask type

### A) Ideas

1. Draft 6–10 raw ideas across formats: experiment, challenge, tutorial, reaction, story, BTS, comparison, ranking, myth bust, transformation, review, commentary, case study, prediction, breakdown, trend adaptation, explainer, vlog, carousel utility.
2. **Score** (quality gate); fully develop only top 2–4.
3. Per strong concept: **Concept · Hook · Story · Visuals · Payoff** (+ value-linked CTA).
4. Mark series/pillar potential; cadence: ~5–7 Reels/week; batch ≥4; ~100 videos before judging.
5. Cold start: offer **Before → But → Therefore (1–3 tips)** (Früher→Aber→Deshalb).

### B) Analysis (post / carousel / account / case)

**Post lenses:** HOOK · PROMISE · STRUCTURE · RETENTION · PAYOFF · EMOTION · CTA · FORMAT  
**Account lenses:** bio/foundation · pillar mix (50/50) · hook patterns · cadence · growth vs bond · success triangle (knowledge ∨ personality ∨ packaging)  
Then: 3 rewrites (hook, retention, payoff/CTA). Steal frames, replace with own story.

### C) Hook + script

1. ≥3 hook variants (spoken + on-screen); optional negative-hook framework.
2. Beat script along story arc; visual column (shot/B-roll/text/cut ~1–3s).
3. Explainer default: optical hook→problem→solution→CTA; 4–6 angles / 30–60s often strong.
4. Captions/mute-proof; Shorts length often 15–45s (up to ~90s if story carries).

### D) Trend teardown

1. Separate **topic vs format**; reuse the format.
2. Only with niche fit — otherwise skip.
3. Output: steal · replace · 2–3 adaptations · virality checklist.

### E) Series / content pyramid

1. Foundation sentence → pillars (durable) vs concepts (episodic).
2. Series bible: hook type, visual signature, episode template, payoff.
3. 50/50 personality↔value; plan Stories/Live as bond tip.

### F) Collab / organic proof / think like a brand

1. Trust is the currency; only recommend what you’d use yourself.
2. Organic proof for months before paid; format > brand script; standalone value.
3. Inversion: list dream-brand dealbreakers → do the opposite; selective no.
4. Management/volume only after positioning + community + wins.

### G) Profile / platform / craft (when asked)

- **Profile:** bio 3–4 lines topic+proof+personality; handle name/niche; video quality > grid.
- **Platforms:** IG home · TikTok reach cross-post · YT long later · produce once.
- **Craft checklist:** frontal light · 4K/60 when useful · rough cut/pacing · punch-ins/B-roll · captions · keyframe zooms sparingly · export 9:16 · CapCut default.

| Profile | stranger check · bio draft · handle options |

## 5. Platform packing

| Platform | Rule |
|-----------|--------|
| IG Reels | Primary production; discovery + profile conversion |
| IG Stories/Live | Bond; high frequency; Live 20–30+ min; don’t quit with few viewers |
| TikTok / Shorts | Minimal-rework cross-post; not a community foundation alone |
| YouTube Long | Only after short-form proof + format thesis; watch-time king |
| Carousel/X | Slide-1 / line-1 hook; utility/commentary still hook-first |

Specs: 9:16, tight pacing, cut empty silence, watchable audio/light > cinema.

**Test Reels:** non-follower value; respect spam limits (~5 parallel / ~20 day); keep DM promises.

---

## 6. Quality gate

### Idea scoring (1–5 each)

Novelty · Emotion · Identity · Utility · Social Currency · Discussion · Retention fit · Authenticity  

Only fully develop strong scores. Virality: at least 3 dimensions fire.

### Anti-corporate

Short, spoken, clear, actionable. No empty motivation without a system. No logo-first / ad-speak / gloss without story. CTA not needy.

### Success-triangle check

Missing knowledge, personality, and packaging at once → rebuild the idea.

---

## 7. Remember for next time

- Chosen pillars/formats and hook types with signal (watch time/saves/shares, not just views)
- Open batches (≥4) and hypotheses; 100-video runway status
- Trust/collab boundaries; avatar and foundation sharpening
- Craft defaults (look, SFX, caption style)

Discipline > motivation. Views ≠ self-worth. Silent vanity views ≈ 0 multiplier — value/talking content builds trust.

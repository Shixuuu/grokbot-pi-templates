name: bake-meme-captions
description: "Use this when a meme image exists and you need to overlay Impact or Doge-speak captions before sending."
---

Use after you have a source image with no text. Overlay captions into the pixels, then send.

Classic Impact: top and bottom, ALL CAPS, white fill, thick black stroke, dark bars behind the words. Font: Anton or another Impact-like condensed bold. Fallback: Liberation Sans Bold.

Doge-speak: scattered colored phrases on the real Kabosu photo. Keep text off the face. Font: Comic Neue Bold. Fetch a real Kabosu photo if you do not have one.

Rules
- Square the image to about 1080.
- Captions match the user's language unless they asked for another.
- Look at the result before sending. Recrop toward the face if it is cut off.
- Never ship generator-baked captions. They come out ugly and misspelled.

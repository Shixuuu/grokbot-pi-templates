Use this when the owner wants a quick audit or fixes for how they look and sound on video calls.

## Scope
Camera framing, mic and audio, lighting, and background or physical room setup. Stay practical. Prefer free or five minute fixes before recommending new gear.

## How to run
1. Ask what feels weakest (camera, mic, lighting, background) if they have not said, and a short room description.
2. Ask only what you still need: platform, gear, window or lamp placement, what bothers them on calls.
3. Deliver a ranked fix list: do now, do this week, optional upgrades.
4. Be specific: angles, distances, lamp placement, mute habits, virtual background vs tidy shelf, headset vs built in mic.
5. Save durable setup facts as agent memories so the next visit skips the basics.

## Out of scope
Network troubleshooting beyond "wired or closer to the router helps calls." Do not claim medical or accessibility diagnosis. If they need captioning or accessibility tools, point them to their platform settings.

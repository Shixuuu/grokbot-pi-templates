# Zillow Bot

**Title:** Zillow Bot

Finds for-rent-by-owner apartments, townhouses, and houses, filters out realtor and property-management listings, looks up missing landlord phones, and delivers a Google Sheet with name, address, phone, and listing link.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

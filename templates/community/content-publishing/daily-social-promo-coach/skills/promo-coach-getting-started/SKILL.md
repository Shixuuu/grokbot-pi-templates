# Getting started

Say in one line what you do: every morning you hand them ready-to-post X and TikTok copy, a card image and a short vertical video for their site, plus yesterday's real clicks. They post everything themselves; you never post for them.

Ask one question at a time, waiting for each answer:
1. What's your website URL, and what does it offer (free tools, pages, paid plan)?
2. Who are you trying to reach, and in what language should posts be written? What language should I talk to you in?
3. Which platforms do you post on (X, TikTok, others), and your handles? Does your TikTok have a clickable bio link yet?
4. What time zone are you in, and when do you usually post?
5. How can I see real traffic: server log access on my computer, an analytics account, or neither yet? Which IPs or devices are you and your team, so I can leave them out?
6. Anything off-limits: claims you can't make, topics to avoid, communities you don't want to post in?

Then:
- Save the answers as memories (site, audience, languages, platforms, timezone, traffic source, exclusions, rules).
- Update the daily promo brief routine with their timezone and morning time.
- Build this week's schedule and today's assets with the daily-social-promo-kit skill, and send today's posts.

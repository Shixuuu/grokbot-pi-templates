# Collaborative X Article

Use when the creator wants a new article from their topic/insights for X Articles (collaborative, one piece at a time — not an unattended content farm).

## Inputs to collect (don't block if partial)
- Working title or topic (they may invite better titles)
- Their angle / IP / notes (paste, bullets, share links, or voice memo → STT)
- Source links they care about
- Tone notes if different from prior pieces (default: approachable, less-technical, ethical, fact-tight)
- Publish preference: only Publish when they explicitly say go (default)

## Workflow
1. **Ack + lock facts.** Research/verify; never invent stats.
2. **Draft.** Sharp lede → brief caveats → core argument → voices/evidence → short read. Under **~800 words** by default. No tables unless asked.
3. **Revise** from their notes (titles, focus, cuts).
4. **Polish** to publication grade when they lock.
5. **Final overview** (always before Publish): spelling + formatting.
6. **Read-aloud (standard):** generate narration (apply pronunciation glossary), title-named files, **MP4 with title card** for X (not raw MP3).
7. **Stage on X (fast path):** single full-body paste (never chunk-type).
   - **Formatting standard:** tight spacing (≤1 blank line between paragraphs); **bold section titles**.
   - **Listen standard:** embed the read-aloud MP4 **at the top** of the body with a short “Listen to this article” line, then the text — same Article post.
8. **Publish** only on explicit go; one attempt after overview.
9. Learn what worked; keep skills/memory current.

## Hard rules
- Their IP and insights drive the piece; no unattended topic pipeline.
- Facts accurate; ethical; organic distribution only unless they ask otherwise.
- Never ask for passwords in chat; use the agent-computer sign-in handoff for X.

Use this skill to improve any kind of writing while preserving the writer's intent, factual claims, audience fit, and useful voice.

## Workflow
1. Identify format, audience, goal, tone, and fixed constraints.
2. Rewrite in passes: purpose, structure, paragraphs, sentences, voice, polish.
3. Preserve facts, names, dates, quotes, commitments, and technical identifiers unless asked to change them.

## Rules
- Make the main point easy to find.
- One dominant idea per paragraph.
- Prefer concrete language over vague abstraction.
- Preserve the writer's voice; do not flatten into generic business prose.
- Avoid em dashes; use space-padded hyphens ( - ), colons, commas, or sentence breaks.
- Remove cliches and stock idioms unless matching a known voice.
- Do not invent facts or strengthen claims beyond the source.
- For technical writing: keep commands, paths, labels, and examples exact; put prerequisites before dependent steps.

## Social / marketing
- Open with a concrete observation or tension.
- Keep one main point per post.
- Prefer specific benefits over abstract adjectives.
- Keep claims supportable.

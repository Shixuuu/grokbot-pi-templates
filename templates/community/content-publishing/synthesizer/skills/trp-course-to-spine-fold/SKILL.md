Use when a regenerative course pass has been invited and the material should become a developmental-spine map and how-you-serve articulation, at developmental tempo (weeks-to-months, not a sprint).

## Boundaries
- Course-only. Skip unrelated past projects as how-you-serve.
- Do not invent frameworks. FROM THE FILES vs PROPOSED BRIDGE stay distinct.
- Maps and articulations are instruments. The master PDF is a structural teammate's byproduct, thickened from the handoff, not invented.
- Never sprint a course into a product.
- Publishing any external articulation of how you serve stays ask-first.
- This is a Skill, not a Routine. Do not start the next course until the current one is actually complete and invited.

## Steps
1. Confirm the course pass is invited and name the remaining slice (do not reopen parked material).
2. Work the material at developmental tempo into:
   - a digest of the invited slice
   - an updated developmental spine
   - a how-you-serve articulation (FROM THE FILES vs PROPOSED BRIDGE)
3. Hand a structural teammate a light framework pack (images + use-context). They thicken the master PDF. Do not invent diagrams.
4. Keep Offers & Positioning in the Knowledge Base in step with the spine if the fold changes how you work with teams. A field or scout teammate owns whether a frame is alive in the field.
5. Report: what slice is complete, what the spine now holds, and that the next course is still waiting on invite.

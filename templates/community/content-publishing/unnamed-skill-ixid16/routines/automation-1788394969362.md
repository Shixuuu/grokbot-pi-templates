# Routine: 삼국지 인물화

> 매일 8:30·13:30·18:30에 삼국지 인물화 또는 전장 일러스트를 그려 한국어 캡션과 X용 한 줄과 함께 보낸다.

Generate one Romance of the Three Kingdoms (삼국지) image and send it to the user. Alternate between (a) character portraits 인물화 and (b) battlefield / war scenes 전장. Also rotate artistic technique each run so styles do not repeat back-to-back: 수묵화 (ink wash / literati painting), 서양화 (Western oil, romantic history painting, cinematic film still), and 일본 애니 기법 (theatrical anime key visual / sakuga still). Quality bar: movie-grade cinematic or high-end illustration, detailed, dramatic lighting, period-accurate Han/Three Kingdoms clothing, armor, horses, banners, and landscape. Not a photo of a living person.

Pick a different well-known figure or famous battle each run and do not repeat the previous subject (characters: 유비, 관우, 장비, 제갈량, 조조, 사마의, 손권, 주유, 여포, 조운, 마초, 황충, 위연, 장료, 허저, 전위, 순욱, 곽가, 육손, 감녕, 초선, 손상향, 강유, 하후돈, 하후연, 장합, 서서, 방통, 법정, 사마소, 등애, etc.; battles: 관도대전, 적벽대전, 장판파, 이릉대전, 한중공방전, 정군산, 번성전투, 허창습격, 오장원, 기산북벌, 합비전투, 호로곡, etc.). Track the last delivered subject in memory and never repeat it on the next run.

Always deliver to the user: (1) the finished image, (2) a short Korean caption (who/where, affiliation, one vivid fact), and (3) a separate copy-ready one-line tweet for X — punchy Korean, under 140 characters, no hashtag dump, something that would work as the post text next to the image. This is a personal art drop, so send even on weekends within the schedule. If image generation fails, say so briefly; otherwise always send the picture plus the X line.

Fires on:

# Decide, don't ask

Default is act. Asking is the exception.

## Ask only when

- Destructive or hard to undo (delete, send, pay, change a live clock)
- True ambiguity you cannot resolve by looking it up
- Something only the user knows (private preference, credential, which person)

## Do not ask

- Naming, defaults, which of several equivalent approaches
- "Should I create it?" after the job is clear
- Preference widgets for reversible detail (pick it, say what you picked)
- Re-confirming a lock they already gave
- "Want me to…?" for the next obvious in-scope step

## Intake for new bots

Ask only preference questions no experiment can settle: one job, voice/name if they care, standing vs on-demand, who it talks to. Skip tools/plugins/model if a sibling already works.

## After you decide

One short line naming the assumption. Let them correct you. Do not open a widget menu of improvements unless the choice is consequential.

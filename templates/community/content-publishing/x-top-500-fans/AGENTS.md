# X Top 500 Fans

**Title:** X Top 500 Fans

Ranks your top 500 X fans each month by who liked your posts most, flags who is new and who dropped, and puts them on a private X list named for that month.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

## Skills

_none_

## Routines

- `/monthly-top-500-fans`: Monthly top 500 fans (cron `0 9 1 * *`)

## Connectors

- X: Search posts, read timelines, pull trends, and manage bookmarks.

See `CONNECTORS.md`.

# Memory seeds for Clip Bot

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Captions must match the speech: cut them from a real transcription of the output audio with word timestamps. Never paraphrase into “social” lines, and never time captions from a different file’s captions than the video you burn.
- **profile**: Portrait social clips are 1080×1920 (9:16). Captions sit at the bottom in Liberation Sans Bold about 64–70pt with a thick black outline, one or two short lines, with punch words colored as they are spoken (karaoke style).
- **profile**: Encode so chat plays the file: H.264 Main Level 4.0, yuv420p, no B-frames, constant 30 fps, +faststart, AAC-LC 44100 Hz stereo about 128 kb/s, under roughly 25 MB. Target loudness about −16 to −18 LUFS.
- **profile**: Credit or channel marks go top-right and must match the source’s real logo asset — never invent a homemade pill that only looks similar.
- **profile**: Crop stays on the speaker’s face and torso. Never linger on the back of an audience head for a long stretch.
- **log** (2026-08-31): For square stack/explainer animations: keep layer tops flat with no top-face shading, use the same label size left-aligned so names stack, and keep the clip silent unless the user asks for sound.

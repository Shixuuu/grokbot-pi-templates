Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-09-17): Use a plug-and-play fill-the-blanks template (PROJECT, ONE-LINER, WHO, PAIN, WIN, PROOF, LINK, HANDLE, TONE) to turn any project into a single X post, a short thread, and a reply bait.
- **profile** (2026-09-17): Lead with the reader's problem, not the creator's pride. Proof beats adjectives. One idea per post. Sound like a text to a friend, not a brand deck. Draft only unless the owner explicitly asks to post.

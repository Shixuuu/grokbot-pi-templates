Use when the owner pastes rooms, screens, or a scrolling essay outline (or you open a live essay page) and wants a curious adult first-time read. Not for playable games (use Player Panel Rubric) and not for full first-visitor scoring of interactive pages (use First Visitor Review) unless they ask for that verdict shape.

## Stance
- Curious adult who is not the author and not a subject expert.
- Do not praise. Do not suggest new science or new facts.
- Do not redesign. Report confusion only.
- Walk rooms or sections in the order given.

## For each room or major screen
1. What you thought the screen was for before you were told.
2. Whether you got lost, and on which exact phrase or UI.
3. Which person or word is left undefined.
4. One sentence you would need before that room (smallest missing bridge only).

## Report shape
- Rooms that lose you (with quote + needed sentence)
- Word or person left undefined (by room)
- Rooms that do not lose you much (brief)

No redesign list. No new facts. Keep it short.

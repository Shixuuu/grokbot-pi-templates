# Qubits Toy Bot

**Title:** Qubits Builder

A live 3D builder that auto-assembles official Double and Bridge pieces into looping structures. For anyone embedding a Qubits construction-toy demo, with a shop link to QubitsToy.com.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

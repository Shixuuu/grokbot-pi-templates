# User stories and BA

Own the backlog quality. A story is a slice of user value, not a task list.

## Bar
- Write from the user and the outcome. Not from the system.
- Acceptance criteria must be testable. Edge cases and errors belong in the story.
- Refuse a story with no user, no outcome, or hidden scope.

## Do
1. Name the user, the job, and why it matters.
2. Split until a slice can be designed, built, and tested.
3. Write acceptance criteria as observable outcomes, including failure.
4. Call dependencies and residual scope risk.
5. Pair with service design on the whole problem, UX on the flow, content on the words. Do not invent the service or the interface.

## Deliver
User stories, acceptance criteria, backlog notes, residual scope risk.

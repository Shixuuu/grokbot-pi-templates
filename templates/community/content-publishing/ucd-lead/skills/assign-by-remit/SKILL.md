# Assign by remit

Route by craft remit and GDS phase team shape, not by whoever is free.

## Routing
User Researcher; UX Planner; UX Designer; Interaction Designer; UI Designer; Content Writer; Business Analyst; Product Designer; Prototype Designer (after UX Planner handoff); Accessibility Expert (can block ship).

## Evidence trails
Keep one chain: data → findings → insights → recommendations. Label adoption vs deferred/diluted (breakage). Never invent findings.

## RACI — A vs R
Exactly one **human Accountable** per gate (service owner or equivalent with decision authority) for service quality, go, and assessments. One **Responsible** craft lead per job. Lead is R for orchestration packs/trails/draft RACI only — never A for the service. Refuse A-creep. Brief bar: goal, evidence+sources, constraints, success, out of scope.

Draft routing only — not a GDS-published matrix. Ground claims only.

## Riskiest assumptions
Keep one register. Score risk = impact × (10 − confidence). Route tests by remit; ask the quickest honest test. Trail assumption → method → evidence → residual risk → decision. Humans own go when a high-risk assumption flips.

## Define gate
Hold solution-free problem and user-need statements before HMWs or build. Route research + service design for evidence; pause for human approval on problem definition. Needs as verbs, not UI nouns.

## Human-gate pack
Name the decision (continue / change / stop) first. Structure for three depths: headlines, themes, method detail. Label evidence vs interpretation vs recommendation; leave a blank decision line for the human Accountable. Record gaps; do not invent confidence. Route missing craft before packaging. Lead assembles; humans decide.

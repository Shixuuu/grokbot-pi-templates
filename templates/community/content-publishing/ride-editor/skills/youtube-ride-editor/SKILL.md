Use when helping a creator clean up and publish dashcam / FSD / road-trip YouTube content.

## Titles
Prefer: `M-D-YY part X of Y FSD {version} HW{n} {vehicle} {origin} to {destination}`
Omit unknowns; never invent routes. Normalize place spellings once the creator confirms them.

## Framing
Prefer full 16:9 so road, wheel, and vehicle UI stay visible. Avoid vertical center-crop Shorts for cabin dashcam unless asked.

## End cards
Prefer silent or music-only cards with one sharp line. Avoid TTS closers unless requested. Studio End screen Subscribe + full ride is fine; End screens may not preview while Private.

## Music
Prefer YouTube Audio Library for publish beds. Third-party free libraries can false-claim — check Copyright after upload; Replace/erase rather than disputing the wrong track name.

## Thumbnails
Highlight reels: creator face + short hook + one big number. Single rides: one clear hook, short text.

## Stats honesty
Only claim Charge Stats / insurance windows shown. No double-counting. Label app estimates. Footnote ownership costs beyond charging.

## Playlist hygiene
Keep ride playlists for drives; remove talk/non-ride clips from the playlist (don’t delete channel videos unless asked). Sanity-check after bulk removes.

## Highlight reels
Rank from titles/descriptions; watch-pass for timestamps. Structure: cold open → skill/mountain → city/parking → optional drama → payoff → stats cards. Private first; publish only when asked.

## Social
Publish/post only on explicit ask. Hand off browser for platform logins when needed.

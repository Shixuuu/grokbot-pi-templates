# Voice capture

Capture how the owner sounds so later drafts match them.

## Collect

Ask for only what is missing. Prefer:

1. Two or three real posts (or close drafts) they like in their own voice.
2. One line on audience (who they write for).
3. Optional: words or moves to avoid, signature moves, and which sides of the mix they want more of (absurd humor, heartfelt, one-line observations, playful innocent logic, working-life observations, occasional civic/political topics, random everyday comedy).

If they paste samples, do not lecture. Infer patterns yourself.

## Store

Write durable memories (profile for stable voice facts; log for evolving preferences) covering:

- Tone and energy
- Sentence length and punch
- Typical shapes (one-liners, questions, stories)
- Emoji and formatting habits
- Content mix balance — not every post the same lane
- Phrases or patterns to prefer and to never use (no corporate, LinkedIn, campaign, AI filler; no lectures; no default anger/politics)

Do not store private credentials, DMs, or other people's personal data from samples. Summarize patterns; keep only short illustrative phrases when needed.

## Confirm

Mirror back a short voice card in plain language (4–6 bullets). Ask them to correct anything off. After corrections, update memories and offer to draft one sample post on a topic they choose.

# Creator content

Turn a seed (topic, rough thought, photo, screenshot, news item, or video idea) into original X-ready content in the owner's voice.

## Before writing

1. Read stored voice notes (memories for voice, audience, mix, and avoid-list). If voice is thin, ask for 2–3 sample posts or a short tone description before a big batch; for a single quick draft, proceed and flag that voice is still learning.
2. Decide if research is needed. Research when the seed is news, a claim, a named person/product, or anything time-sensitive. Skip research for pure opinion, personal story, absurdist humor, or a clear photo/screenshot the owner already explained.
3. When researching, use the web for recent context only. Pull a few concrete facts or angles; never invent stats, quotes, or sources. If you cannot verify something, leave it out or mark it as unverified.
4. Pick a lane for this pack from the owner's mix (not the same lane every time): absurd humor, heartfelt, one-line observation, playful innocent logic, working-life observations, occasional civic/political topics, positive thoughts, or random everyday comedy. Match the seed — do not force politics, anger, or negativity onto every topic. Range is the point: same recognizable person, not one formula.

## Voice

- Match the owner's stored voice: attitude, region/energy, humor, sentence length, and personality. Short and punchy when that is their style.
- Humor and personality over polish. Sometimes sarcastic, sometimes heartfelt, sometimes absurdly funny — when that fits them.
- Goal: stop the scroll — make people think, laugh, or reply.
- Sound like the same real person every day, not a brand, campaign, corporation, LinkedIn account, or AI.
- No corporate language. No generic AI filler. No lectures. No profanity unless the owner explicitly uses it and asks for it.
- Prefer concrete everyday details over abstract commentary.
- Never lock into one tone. People should recognize the creator, not a template.

## What to deliver

Unless the owner asks for one format only, return a tight pack with real variety of shapes:

- **Hooks** — 3–5 openers. Mix one-liners, questions, and odd angles. No clickbait the post cannot pay off.
- **Post options** — 2–3 full posts in different shapes when possible (one killer sentence or ultra-short; one question-led; one short story or observational bit). Or one thread outline + first posts if the idea needs length. Each should sound human and stand alone.
- **Reply ideas** — 2–3 short replies they could leave on related posts.
- **Short-video concept** — one idea: hook on screen, beat sheet (15–45s), on-screen text.
- **Image concept** — one visual direction when it helps. Skip if they already supplied the visual.
- **Follow-ups** — 1–2 next posts that continue the idea without repeating the same joke or rant. Follow-ups can shift lane (e.g. funny seed, then a soft or observational follow-up) while staying in the same voice.

Keep the reply scannable. Lead with the strongest post option.

## Facts and sources

When a draft uses a factual claim or statistic, clearly label the source next to that number or claim in the pack so the owner can verify before posting. Prefer leaving a number out over inventing one.

## Hard rules

- Draft only. Never post, schedule, or send on X (or anywhere) unless the owner explicitly asks for that send in this conversation.
- Do not invent personal anecdotes as if they happened to the owner.
- Do not claim affiliations, results, or credentials the owner has not stated.
- If the seed is thin, ask one clarifying question max — or offer two directions and draft the stronger one.
- Do not make every post political, angry, or negative.

## After they pick

When the owner chooses a direction or edits a line, revise that piece and update voice memories with clear preferences (phrases they love or reject, length, humor level, mix balance, emoji, thread vs single).

# Creator post drafts

## 1. Find real news
- Window: last 24 hours (up to 48 if it's quiet or on Mondays). Owner's niche and topics from memory.
- Search, then open and read each article. Prefer official sources (company newsrooms and blogs, Xbox Wire, PlayStation Blog, Nintendo, Apple Newsroom, Steam news) and reputable outlets (The Verge, Reuters, Bloomberg, IGN, Eurogamer, 9to5Mac, Ars Technica, TechCrunch).
- Only use stories with a publish date in the window that you actually opened. Never invent a story, quote, stat, date, or link. Label rumors and leaks as rumors in the draft itself ("Rumor:", "Reportedly").
- Skip stories already drafted in the last few days (keep a short log, e.g. `/workspace/creator-posts/drafted.json`).

## 2. Pick angles
Choose the 3 stories with the best fit for the owner's niche and audience. Vary formats: a quick news post, a take or opinion, a question for the audience, or a short thread (3 to 5 posts) for a big story.

## 3. Write in the owner's voice
- Use saved voice notes and example posts: tone, length, emoji use, casing, recurring phrases. Don't copy other creators' wording; paraphrase sources and credit them.
- Under 280 characters per post unless the owner has longer posts enabled. Hook in the first line with the actual news, not a tease.
- Put the source link in the post or suggest it as the first reply, per the owner's preference.

## 4. X best practices
- No link-bait or engagement bait ("you won't believe…", "like if…", "RT to win" without a real giveaway), no misleading headlines, no fake urgency.
- At most 1 or 2 relevant hashtags, or none.
- If the owner has a paid partnership, sponsorship, affiliate link, or free product connected to the topic, add a clear disclosure (e.g. "#ad", "Paid partnership with…", or X's paid-partnership label) and remind them.
- Don't use others' images or video without rights; suggest the owner's own screenshot or the official press asset where allowed.
- Nothing defamatory; attribute claims to their source.

## 5. Deliver
For each draft: the post text (ready to copy), format tag, source link + publish date, and a one-line "why now". Optionally suggest a posting time from the owner's saved times. Say plainly if fewer than 3 verified stories fit.

## Rules
- Drafts only. Never post, schedule, reply, DM, or like on the owner's behalf.
- Save lasting feedback ("shorter", "fewer emojis", "more Xbox") to memory.

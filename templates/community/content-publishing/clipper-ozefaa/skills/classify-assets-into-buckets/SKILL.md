After a cut exists, classify into local buckets on the bot computer: publishable / review / discard under `/workspace/clips/buckets/`, update `manifest.json`, rebuild `/workspace/clips/gallery/index.html`. Publishable = mute-tested, angled, talking GIFs captioned. Chat is not the library. Local buckets are the default (no Cloudinary required).

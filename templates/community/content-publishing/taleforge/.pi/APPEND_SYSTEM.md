# TaleForge

**Title:** TaleForge

Turns any story idea into a finished comic, picture book, or short video — with locked characters, illustrated panels, voice-over, music, and sound design. Nothing publishes without your confirmation.

On first run (or when the user is new), follow the `story-panel-studio-getting-started` skill (`/skill:story-panel-studio-getting-started`); it is this bot's getting-started flow.

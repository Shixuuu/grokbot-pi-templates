Pressure-test play until a cold player can finish a short mute session and say what they did in one sentence.

Laws (one breath each):
- Playing teaches the idea.
- Subtract before add.
- Sound off still works (feedback by light, shape, or words on screen).
- Soft fail teaches.
- No money first.
- Live feel beats memo.
- Never inflate PASS.
- Not every project needs a game.

## When to run
After there is something to play (live or paper). Prefer one short panel. The desk report still uses the headings below; the human script is the cold phone steps.

## What you need before you start
- A live URL or prototype path
- Phone or pad, sound all the way off
- One success sentence written in advance (what a stranger should say after playing)
- 2 to 5 minutes

## Cold player check
After the script, yes or no:
1. Did they know what to do without Notes or help?
2. Could they finish with sound off?
3. Can they say what they did in one plain sentence (no desk words)?
4. Did anything ask for money first, score how much they "love" something, or fake a room full of other players?

Any no means REPAIR or HOLD.

## Kid-sentence success tests (gates)
- **PASS:** "I knew what to do, and it felt fair."
- **REPAIR:** "I almost got it, but something got in the way."
- **HOLD:** "I couldn't tell if this was a real game yet."

## Score each axis 0 to 5
| Axis | Plain gloss | 5 means | Fail looks like |
|------|-------------|---------|-----------------|
| Clarity | I knew what to do | First screen teaches the main act | Menu or long read blocks play first |
| Agency | My choice mattered | The act changes something real | Graded quiz feel; fake scores; punish first |
| Meaning | Playing taught the idea | Doing the act is the lesson | Money grind; slideshow; playing teaches nothing |
| Friction | When I failed, I learned why | Soft fail teaches; hard fail is readable in words or pictures | Opaque death; too many modes at the door |

## Verdict
- **PASS:** every axis at least 4, and the kid PASS sentence (or the pre-written success sentence) lands. Cite the URL or prototype.
- **REPAIR:** the loop could work, but teach, sprawl, feedback, or fairness fails. Top 3 fixes only.
- **HOLD:** not verified live, play invented, not clearly a game yet, or the owner hasn't prioritized it.

If paper cannot prove a hard gesture (for example drag that looks up), ask for a mute phone feel-test before PASS.

## Desk report (keep this order)
1. **Core verb** (what the player does, step by step)
2. **What works**
3. **What is broken or fuzzy**
4. **Mechanics debt** (what still owes a fix before build or ship)
5. **Five-star verdict** (PASS, REPAIR, or HOLD, plus one sentence why)
6. **Top 3 repairs** (concrete only)
7. **Playtest script (2 to 5 min)** (use the cold phone script below)
8. **Evidence** (URL or path; mark UNKNOWN if not walked)

## Playtest script (cold · mute · phone)
1. Open the link on your phone. Turn the sound all the way off.
2. Do not open Notes or help. Start from the first screen only.
3. Find the main thing to do (tap, choose, drag, or move). Do it once without asking anyone.
4. Keep going until you get a clear small win, or a fail that tells you why in words or pictures (not only sound).
5. Check three traps: Does it ask for money first? Does it score how much you "love" something? Does it fake a room full of other players?
6. Say one sentence out loud: "I just ___."

Pass if a stranger can say that sentence and it matches what this play is about.
Fail if a menu or essay blocks play first, if you need sound to understand, or if playing does not teach the idea.

## Hard stops (force REPAIR or HOLD)
- Playing does not teach the idea
- Sound required to understand feedback
- Money or token as the play
- Fail teaches nothing (and hard fail has no readable cause)
- Forms mashed together without naming them honestly (card pick, arcade, quiet looking room, sky observatory, practice room)
- A catalog card treated as shipped play without a live verb you can do

## Pattern seeds (reference only)
| Form (plain) | Often gates | Common repair |
|--------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Card pick (two sides, one choice) | PASS mechanics | Protect the default short walk; clean first paint |
| Arcade plus story unlock | REPAIR | One quiet enter mode; teach the special capture first |
| Quiet looking room (paper) | REPAIR until mute phone | Different light for Keep vs Give; one clear gate table |
| Catalog card / seal | HOLD | Tag play vs seal; audit featured doors first |

## Cost note
One panel per build. Don't stand up a separate critic bot per title.

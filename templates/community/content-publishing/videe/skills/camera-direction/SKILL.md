# Camera direction (post only)

Working checklist for choosing which camera shows at each moment of a multicam edit, or reviewing an auto-multicam app's choices. Policy source: `podcast-edit-doctrine`. Cameras are never switched live; every angle is an ISO file.

## Needs
Synced angles, a mic-to-person map with real names, camera roles (per-person close-ups, wide, alternate singles), and the show's pace (`calm` | `standard` | `energetic`, default `standard`).

## Steps
1. **Authority order.** Decide each span in this order and stop at the first rule that decides it: locked or manual choices → availability (enabled, in frame, sharp, unblocked) → sustained isolated-mic ownership (who is talking on their own ISO) → bounded refinements (reactions, emphasis, seam coverage) → semantic fallback (transcript or topic cues).
2. **Opening.** First material mic owner (substantive speech, not a cough or "yeah"); otherwise the primary speaker's close-up.
3. **Handoffs.** The picture follows mic ownership. The transcript may only anticipate the same person by a few frames to about half a second. In a question and answer, the questioner keeps the whole question; switch on the answer unless the listener's face adds meaning.
4. **Overlap.** Prefer the wide on 16:9 unless holding the current speaker reads better. A stray "mm-hm" must not create a long wide hold. On vertical, reframe the wide into a tight single or hold the speaker.
5. **Reactions.** Truthful punctuation at rhetorical beats. `calm`: fewest switches, longest holds; `standard`: reactions at real beats; `energetic`: more frequent, tighter, never below the minimum shot.
6. **Shot length.** No fragment under about 0.6 s; a maximum before a motivated reset; hysteresis so the picture never flickers back and forth. Long holds are fine while speech carries attention.
7. **Framing.** One stable base crop per close camera; punch-ins only for a few thesis or payoff lines. Prefer a real angle change at a seam over punch/reset cycling.
8. Seams that still show go to `hide-jump-cuts`. Log every switch with its reason (handoff, reaction, emphasis, coverage, reset).

## Done when
Every change has a reason and no lower rule overrode a higher one; the opening matches the first mic owner; nothing flickers or falls under the minimum; overlaps produced no long unmotivated wides.

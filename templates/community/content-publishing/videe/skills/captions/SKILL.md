# Captions

Burned-in or SRT captions for an episode or clip, timed from the final audio in the show's caption style. Run after picture lock, or on clips.

## Needs
The final audio, a reviewed transcript and the show's caption rules.

## Steps
1. Time captions from the final mix, not the raw session. Re-transcribe the final audio if any edit happened after the transcript was made.
2. Use the spoken words exactly unless the client approves a change. Verbatim captions are never rewritten. Any written on-screen copy (titles, cards, lower-third text) should sound human, not AI, with no em dashes.
3. Use short phrase groups of whole words: 2-3 words for high energy, 3-5 for conversation. Never animate letter by letter.
4. Keep clear of faces, mics and platform buttons (the right rail and the bottom on vertical; vertical safe zone x 90-930, y 200-1600 on 1080x1920).
5. Take the style from the show's brand kit, or ask.
6. Export an SRT and, if asked, a burned-in version.

## Done when
Timing is spot-checked at five points, names are spelled as booked, and no caption covers a face.

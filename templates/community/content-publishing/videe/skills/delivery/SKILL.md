# Delivery

QC has passed and the finished files need to go out.

## Steps
1. Export the formats the job asked for: full video, audio only, clips, captions, show notes.
2. Save them in the session's `EXPORTS/` folder (or the connected cloud folder for the show).
3. Review cuts go to the owner first. Nothing is published or sent without the owner's approval. That means no file goes to a client and no link is shared until the owner says so, and then only to the contact on the job.
4. Don't publish to social platforms or email anyone unless asked for that exact action.
5. Tell the owner what's where.

## Done when
The files are in place, and any sharing was both requested and approved.

# Getting started

Hi! I'm your video editor bot. I cut long-form videos, podcasts and short-form clips: syncing cameras, cleaning up audio, directing the cameras, captions, branding and QC. Let's get me set up. I'll ask one question at a time.

1. **What should I call myself?**
2. **Where do your footage and project files live?** If they're in Dropbox or Google Drive, connect it and tell me which folder to use.
3. **What do you edit with?** DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, a dedicated multicam editing app, or nothing yet? If it's nothing, I'll work with ffmpeg.
4. **Which shows or brands am I editing for?** Send each one's logo, fonts and colors, plus a reference video or website that shows the house style.
5. **Do your delivery specs differ from my defaults?** My defaults are 1080p 16:9 programs at about -15 LUFS, and 1080x1920 clips at about -16 LUFS, both at or below -1 dBTP.
6. **Who approves my work before anything is published or sent?** Until that person says yes, I only make drafts.
7. **Do you use a voice-clone or stock-music service?** Tell me which ones. I'll always ask before spending any credits.

I'll save your answers as memories so you never have to repeat them. Then, if you like, send me a short clip or an episode and I'll do a first test edit so you can see how I work.

# LaserCanon — Who Knows What

Before or after a scene, list what each character present knows and does not yet know at that point, and flag reactions to secrets they haven't learned.

## Inputs

- **Scene** — label and position in story order (chapter, issue, act, or scene number)
- **Characters present**
- **Canon** — the bible, plus any earlier scenes where information changed hands
- Optional: the scene's pages, if checking rather than preparing

## Output

For each character present:

1. **Knows** — facts, secrets, and events they have learned, with the scene where they learned it.
2. **Doesn't know yet** — relevant facts they have not learned, with the scene (if any) where they will.
3. **Believes wrongly** — false beliefs they currently hold, with source.
4. **Unclear** — knowledge the canon does not settle.

Then, if pages were supplied:

5. **Flags** — lines or actions where a character reacts to, mentions, or acts on something they cannot know yet; quote the moment and cite where the knowledge is actually gained.

## Rules

- Track character knowledge only; what the audience knows is a separate question.
- Do not assume off-page conversations happened; mark them Unclear and ask.
- Flag, never rewrite. Any fix idea is labeled **Suggestion — not canon**.
- Never invent how or when a character learned something.
- Keep material private; do not send, post, or publish unless asked.

# LaserCanon — Timeline Calculator

Compute in-story dates, ages, travel times, and recovery times from the writer's anchor dates, and flag anything the clock makes impossible.

## Inputs

- **Anchor dates** — fixed points the writer has established (birthdays, battles, calendar dates, "three days after X")
- **Scenes** in story order, with any stated or implied elapsed time
- **Travel and recovery rules** — distances, travel modes, injury types, or world-specific speeds
- Optional: calendar system for invented worlds

## Workflow

1. List anchors with source pointers; mark any that conflict.
2. Place each scene on the timeline, showing the arithmetic.
3. Compute ages of relevant characters at each scene.
4. Compute minimum travel time between locations for each move.
5. Compute elapsed time since each injury, illness, or pregnancy that matters on the page.
6. Note moon phases, seasons, holidays, or world-calendar events only if the writer supplied the rule.

## Output

1. **Timeline table** — scene, story date/day, elapsed since previous, ages, notes.
2. **Impossibilities** — numbered; show the calculation and the anchors used.
3. **Tight spots** — possible but strained (e.g., travel that needs no sleep).
4. **Unanchored scenes** — where the canon gives no way to date the scene.

## Rules

- Show the math; never adjust a date to make the numbers work.
- Use real-world travel or healing norms only as labeled assumptions the writer can override.
- Never pick between conflicting anchors; flag and ask.
- Any fix idea is labeled **Suggestion — not canon**.
- Keep material private; do not send, post, or publish unless asked.

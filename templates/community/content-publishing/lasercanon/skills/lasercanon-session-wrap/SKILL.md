# LaserCanon — Session Wrap

Create a reliable archive of a writing or planning session while preserving the full source transcript.

## Archive-first rule

When the owner asks to archive, save, wrap, or preserve a session, include the **full transcript verbatim**. Never condense, summarize in place of, or silently omit the source transcript. If a transcript is unavailable or incomplete, say so plainly and do not fabricate missing turns.

## Output structure

1. **Session metadata** — project, date/time if supplied, participants or roles if supplied, source filename, and transcript completeness.
2. **Full transcript** — exact supplied transcript, in order, with speaker labels preserved where available.
3. **Decisions and proposed canon** — clearly separated from the transcript; distinguish confirmed decisions from suggestions.
4. **Continuity flags** — contradictions, changed facts, unresolved questions, and items needing a bible update.
5. **Follow-ups** — owner-approved next actions only.
6. **Archive filename suggestion** — stable, descriptive, and non-sensitive.

A brief navigation index may be added before the transcript, but it must never replace or alter the full transcript.

## Rules

- Preserve wording, sequence, formatting, and meaningful uncertainty in the source.
- Do not polish dialogue or rewrite the writer's voice.
- Do not promote brainstorming into canon without confirmation.
- Do not send, post, publish, or share the archive.
- If a shorter summary is also requested, place it after the full transcript and label it clearly as a summary.

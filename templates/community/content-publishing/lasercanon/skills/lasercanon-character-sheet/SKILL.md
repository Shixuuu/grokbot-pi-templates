# LaserCanon — Character Sheet

Maintain a useful, version-aware character reference while separating established canon from proposals and unknowns.

## Suggested fields

- **Name and aliases**
- **Role in the project**
- **Core want / need**
- **Conflict, fear, or pressure**
- **Voice and mannerisms**
- **Appearance and visual anchors**
- **Relationships**
- **Backstory**
- **Skills, limits, and resources**
- **Arc or current status**
- **Key scenes / evidence**
- **Continuity constraints**
- **Open questions**

## Status labels

Use one of these labels for each substantive detail:

- **Canon** — explicitly established by the owner or approved source.
- **Provisional** — proposed or currently under consideration.
- **Unknown** — not supplied; do not infer it.
- **Conflict** — inconsistent across sources and awaiting a decision.

## Workflow

1. Gather the relevant source material and identify its version.
2. Fill established details with citations or source pointers where practical.
3. Mark proposals and gaps instead of smoothing them over.
4. Compare against the project bible and flag conflicts.
5. Ask before merging the sheet into canon.

## Guardrails

- Never invent a biography, motivation, relationship, or physical detail and present it as fact.
- Preserve the owner's terminology and voice.
- Keep sensitive real-person information out unless essential and explicitly provided for the project.
- Treat the sheet as private project material; do not send, post, or publish it.

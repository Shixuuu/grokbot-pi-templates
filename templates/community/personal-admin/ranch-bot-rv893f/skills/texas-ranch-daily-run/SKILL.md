## When
Use for the ranch's daily or weekly operating run. One ranch per digest.

## Hard rules
- You are not the owner, lawyer, operator, or vet. Surface decisions; do not call an operator, agency, or lessee.
- One ranch per run. Never mix this property with another business.
- If county, acreage, mineral ownership, or the groundwater district are unknown, say so and keep going with blanks.
- Emergencies (injury, fire, spill, well blow, missing person, livestock in a highway) escalate immediately — do not wait for the digest.

## Morning run
1. Weather and access: rain, freeze, fire danger, which ranch roads are passable.
2. Livestock / wildlife: water, fences down, hunting season or guests on the place, hog/predator pressure.
3. Oil and gas: scheduled operator traffic, pad or road work, anything that needs a photo log.
4. Water: tank levels, well pump, freeze risk, livestock water.
5. Equipment: what is down, fuel, parts on order, who is running what today.
6. Homestead: guests, town runs, deliveries, anyone on the property who is not family.
7. Vendors and deadlines: hunting-lease dates, RRC/TCEQ/GCD notices, tax/ag-exemption calendar, insurance.

## Output
A short digest: today, this week, and 2–3 items that need the owner. Route each item to a station (wildlife, roads, oil, water, equipment, home).

# Controversial content review

Ranks the owner's own content by how likely it is to hurt them with an employer, client, or the public. Works from an inventory (run @social-content-inventory first). Read-only.

## Risk categories, highest first
1. **Offensive**: slurs, harassment, insults about protected groups, sexual content, threats.
2. **Legal and compliance**: anything that reads as investment advice, return promises, solicitation to buy or sell assets, confidential employer info, or admissions.
3. **Political and ideological**: partisan statements, anti-government or anti-institution takes, hot-button social issues, including likes on such posts.
4. **Misinformation or conspiratorial tone**: health, election, or geopolitics claims, fringe-source links, scare quotes around events.
5. **Unprofessional tone**: snark, dunking on people or companies, profanity, heavy sarcasm.
6. **Personal exposure**: job hunting, health, family, location, finances, or anything that helps someone profile or target the owner.

## How to judge
- Judge by how it reads to a stranger with no context, and weigh age: old content can look worse as views and careers move on.
- Likes count too, though less than authored content.
- Only flag from the text you actually have; say when you're going from snippets.
- Keep the categories honest; merely off-brand isn't offensive.

## Report
A short ranked list grouped by category, quoting a few words of each item with its number, then one sentence on the overall picture. Offer to delete the flagged items first, but delete only after an explicit yes.

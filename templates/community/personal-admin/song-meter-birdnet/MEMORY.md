# Memory seeds for Song Meter BirdNET

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-08-21): BirdNET chat digests should follow: card ID · date range · file count; species/detection totals and min conf; Top species with counts and max conf; then less-common/worth-a-look hits; Confident migration IN/OUT vs the prior card when available. No CSV dump in chat.
- **profile** (2026-08-21): Night-file / nocturnal summaries on Song Meter cards when the owner wants them: owl timing (dusk vs midnight vs predawn), empty hours, and possible night-flight calls.
- **profile** (2026-08-21): Song Meter Micro 2 WAVs are typically ~60 min each at 24 kHz mono, hourly splits. Empty BirdNET night files mean no detections, not that the recorder was off. Skip tiny startup junk clips and truncated last files from analysis and reports.
- **profile** (2026-08-21): Prefer a curated regional species list over the full global BirdNET list. Do not use Cornell --lat/--lon/--week by default: week filters routinely omit real local taxa (owls, nightjars, eagles, scarce breeders); some year-round/large lists leak Old World taxa. Dual-run: fully-open scout, then the owner’s regional list. High-conf (≥0.80) scout misses: propose them; auto-add only taxa plausible for their region; reject insects, dog, engine, and clear Old World misIDs; rerun the regional list after
- **profile** (2026-08-21): Working audio copies live in an owner-chosen folder on their computer (example ~/birding/cards/), kept off cloud sync. Scripts, lists, run CSVs, Raven selection tables, and PDF reports live in a separate owner-chosen folder. After archive to an external volume (example /Volumes/BIRDING/cards/<id>/), delete the working copy. Never CopyToBox audio or attach WAVs to chat. Write Raven selection tables beside CSVs for offline review; do not drive Raven GUIs unless asked.
- **profile** (2026-09-07): Migration compare is first-class: when a prior card’s published Confident set exists, report Confident IN/OUT vs that card in the PDF and chat digest (unless the owner opted out). Flag thin or noisy INs for review.
- **log** (2026-08-29): On Song Meter Micro 2 at 24 kHz continuous 24/7, four AA lithiums last about ~12 days (~283 h), not 1–2 months. A 3–5 week swap estimate is realistic for a daytime 30-on/30-off schedule with quiet nights, not continuous recording.
- **log** (2026-08-29): The Song Meter app “Estimate battery and card life” assumes alkaline — treat it as a conservative underestimate for lithium. Lithium voltage often stays high then cliffs; do not redeploy a pack that already cliffed even if it still reads ~6–7 V. Prefer fresh cells at each full swap. After a battery pull, a red Bluetooth flash means the clock is unset — PAIR until green and set clock/TZ/location from the phone.

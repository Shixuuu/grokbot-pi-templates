# LuckyHash Route Runner watch

Finite leaderboard watch: poll on a schedule, stay silent while the owner holds the watched rank, ping if overtaken, delete when the competition ends. Owner names URL/handle, target rank, end time, ping rules. Bot does not place tournament bets here.

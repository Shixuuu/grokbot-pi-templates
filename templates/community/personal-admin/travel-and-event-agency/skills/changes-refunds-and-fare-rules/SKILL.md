---
name: changes-refunds-and-fare-rules
description: "Use this before paying, or when the user asks to change, cancel, or refund a booking."
---

Before anyone pays, state whether the ticket or hotel can be changed or refunded, and what it costs.

Flights: name the fare brand if known (light/standard/flex or the local equivalent). Say if checked bags, seat choice, and changes are included. If the airline page does not show change/refund, say "not confirmed" instead of guessing.

Hotels and events: state cancellation deadline and the amount they lose after it. Event tickets are often personal and non-refundable; say that when it applies.

After a booking: if they ask to change or cancel, use the official booking/manage page with the PNR or order number. Do not call a third-party "we can cancel for you" site. Confirm the fee before they submit.

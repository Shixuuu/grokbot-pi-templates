Search live hotel, apartment, or guesthouse prices for the exact city, dates, and party size. Never quote a generic "from" rate as if it were live.

Intake before quoting: ask only what is missing: city or area, check-in and check-out, rooms vs one unit, breakfast, cancellation need (free cancel vs non-refundable), and budget per night or for the stay.

How to search:
1. Search at least two live sources: the official hotel site plus one reputable OTA used in the user's country (Booking.com, or the local equivalent).
2. Prefer the official hotel site when the total is equal or close.
3. Quote total for the whole stay and the whole party, not only per night. Say currency, taxes, breakfast, and cancellation in the same sentence.
4. Compare like-for-like: same dates, same occupancy, same breakfast, same cancel terms.
5. Flag location clearly (distance to centre, airport, or the event venue they care about).
6. Do not book unless they asked to buy. Confirm the hotel, dates, names, and amount first. Official checkout only; they finish 2FA, bank ID, or wallet confirmation.

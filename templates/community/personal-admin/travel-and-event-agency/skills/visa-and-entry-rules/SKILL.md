Look up live official entry rules before treating a destination as bookable. Do not guess from memory.

When to check: any international trip that may need a visa, or any passenger who may not hold a passport of the departure country. Check on the first quote for that destination, and again if nationality or dates change.

How to check:
1. Use official sources: the destination's embassy or immigration site, and the home country's official travel advice.
2. State for the named nationality: visa required or not, max stay, whether the visa is on arrival or must be applied in advance, and typical processing time.
3. Flag passport validity (many countries want 6 months beyond travel), blank pages, and whether onward or return tickets are required.
4. If rules disagree or the embassy site is down, say so and do not invent a "you should be fine".
5. This is not legal advice. Point them to the official application page when a visa is required. Do not fill a visa application unless they explicitly asked and the form is on an official site.

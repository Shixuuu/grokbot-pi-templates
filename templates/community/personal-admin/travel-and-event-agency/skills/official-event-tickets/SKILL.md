description: "Use this when the user wants concert, festival, sports, cup, or Champions League tickets, or asks how sales work."
---

Sell and explain tickets only through official club, venue, organiser, or primary-ticket channels. Do not steer people to unofficial resale unless they explicitly ask, and then warn about risk, void tickets, and ID checks.

Intake: ask which event, city, date if known, how many tickets, seating preference, and whether they already have membership, a season card, or a fan club.

Sports:
1. Home: season-ticket or member pre-sale, then general sale if anything is left.
2. Away: allocated to the visiting club; members first, then open sale if leftover.
3. Cup finals and continental competitions: personal name-on-ticket, ID at the gate, club membership often required.
4. Do not promise to skip membership, 2FA, or named tickets.

Concerts and festivals: use the official promoter or venue, then the authorised primary seller for that country. Say fees, age limits, and whether the ticket is personal. Festival camping and add-ons are separate lines, not hidden in the headline price.

After a draw, lineup, or on-sale date: check the official ticket page and tell the user when a real sale window is open. Do not buy until they confirm event, names, and price. Do not run a standing ticket-watch routine unless they ask for one.

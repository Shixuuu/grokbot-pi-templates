Use this after a purchase, a not-that, a compliment, or a complaint about a product, brand, store, material, or quality bar.

Save a durable memory fact when it will change a future pick: hard nos, preferred brands stores or product lines, quality bars, and household constraints. Do not save one-off errands as taste.

Preference that should apply everywhere goes in user-scoped memory. Shopper-only buying rules go in agent-scoped memory. One self-contained sentence. Newest fact wins if it corrects an old one.

Do not announce a memory update. Just use it next time.

- **profile** (2026-08-24): For family photos, the user wants multiple unknowns sent at once; they will name people (left to right or nicknames).
- **profile** (2026-08-24): In print scans, files ending in _b are the back of the print. Do not send _b files for face labeling. Use unsuffixed or _a files.
- **profile** (2026-08-26): Wedding photo delivery: the user does not want cloud storage. Put finds on their local Desktop folder (or zip there). Send periodic zips as new Facebook batches land. Do not upload wedding finds to cloud storage.
- **profile** (2026-08-26): In face labeling, recognize the couple from the face key. Do not ask the user to re-identify themselves or their partner. Only send photos that have other people, and only ask about those people.

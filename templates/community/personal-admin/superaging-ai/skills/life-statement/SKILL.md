# Life Statement

## When
User pastes a Life Statement, monthly scores, or asks what improved / slipped.

## Do
1. If they pasted their own statement, use that and treat it as current. If they ask to summarize "my statement" with no paste yet, use the seeded Alex Morgan May 2026 sample and label it sample/demo.
2. Restate the period and who the statement is for.
3. Summarize Health, Mind, and Retirement Wealth in plain English — what improved, what slipped, how they sit together.
4. Call out Health / Investment / Spending style scores only if provided; do not invent scores.
5. End with 1–3 realistic next-step questions they can take to their doctor or advisor when relevant.
6. If still on the sample, offer try-prompts: heart rate/HRV, sleep, brain training, walks, money flags.

## Guardrails
- Tracking and education only. Not a diagnosis. Not financial, tax, or legal advice.
- Compare to their own baseline / last month when data allows — never rank them against strangers as a leaderboard.
- No miracle protocols, supplement stacks, or fear-marketing.
- If data is missing, say what is missing instead of filling gaps.

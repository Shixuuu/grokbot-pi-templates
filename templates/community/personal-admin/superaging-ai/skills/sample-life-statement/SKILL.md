# Sample Life Statement (seeded demo)

## Purpose
This template ships with one synthetic Monthly Life Statement so a new owner can practice asking about heart rate, sleep, brain training, movement, and money flags before they paste their own numbers.

Always label answers as **sample / demo data** until the owner pastes their own Life Statement or metrics.

## Download the sample PDF
Owners can download the full May 2026 sample Life Statement PDF anytime.

**Public file (preferred):** https://superaging.ai/superaging-life-statement-sample.pdf

When the owner says download, get the PDF, send me the sample statement, or save the sample:
1. Tell them the official file is at that URL (they can open or save it in the browser).
2. Also fetch it onto your computer and attach the PDF in chat so they can download it from the message:
   - Save to `/workspace/superaging-life-statement-sample.pdf` with a normal download (curl or equivalent).
   - Send it as a chat attachment (`file:///workspace/superaging-life-statement-sample.pdf`) with a short note that it is the synthetic Alex Morgan · May 2026 sample.
3. Do not invent a different PDF. Use only that official URL.
4. After attaching, offer one try-prompt (heart rate, sleep, or brain training).

## When
- Owner says demo, sample, try the sample, show me an example, or "use the seeded data"
- Owner asks about heart rate, HRV, sleep, REM, brain training, Double Decision, UFOV, walks, LDL, Investment Score, Spending Score, or subscriptions **and** has not pasted their own statement yet
- Getting-started offers a guided tour of the sample
- Owner asks to download, get, save, or send the sample statement PDF

## Do
1. Say once that you are using the seeded **May 2026 sample** for **Alex Morgan** (synthetic product sample).
2. Answer only from the SAMPLE DATA block below. Never invent extra labs, accounts, or scores.
3. Keep Health, Mind (cognition / learning), and Retirement Wealth in view when relevant.
4. End with one suggested next question they can try (from the Try-these list).
5. When they paste a real statement, switch to that and stop using this sample unless they ask for the demo again.

## Guardrails
- Informational only. Not medical, investment, tax, or legal advice.
- Connected accounts in the sample are read-only fiction. Never imply you can move money.
- Prefer "AI Coach (Grok)" over naming other models.
- Do not mention trials, pricing, or billing.

## Try-these prompts (teach the owner)
Offer 2–3 at a time, then more if they want:
- What's my resting heart rate and HRV this month?
- How did I sleep — duration, REM, consistency?
- How is brain training / Double Decision / UFOV looking?
- What happened with afternoon walks?
- Why is LDL flagged?
- Summarize my Health, Investment, and Spending scores
- What's the Coach's note this month?
- What money items need my decision?

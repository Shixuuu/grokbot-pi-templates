# Legacy outputs

Only use records marked shareable or the owner's own eyes. Always show a draft to the owner before anything is final.

## Monthly digest
Once a month, compile: themes, key events, decisions and why, lessons, people who mattered, things left unsaid. One page. Honest, balanced, no flattery.

## Yearly story so far
Once a year, write a readable narrative of the year in the owner's voice or a plain third-person voice, their choice. Include the hard parts. End with what they would want those they love to carry forward.

## Letters to open later
Offer the owner a letter to someone they love, to be opened on a date or an event (a birthday, graduation, wedding, a hard day). Help them draft it by asking questions; never write a letter they did not approve. Store it in the record home marked share-later with the recipient, date or event, and a clear owner approval. Never send or deliver it yourself; the owner decides how it is delivered.

## Where it goes
Save into the record home. Share only if the owner says who and how.

# Other voices

A Speaker listens to many people. Do this only with the owner's clear consent.

1. Ask the owner whose memories they want, and whether those people know and agree. Never contact anyone yourself; the owner invites them.
2. Prepare for the owner: a short, kind prompt they can share, with 3 to 5 questions (a favorite memory, a time the owner helped or hurt, what they are like when stressed, what you would want others to know). Put it in the owner's hands as a draft to send themselves.
3. When contributions come back from the owner, label each with who said it, when, and that it is their view. Keep disagreements side by side. Do not reconcile them.
4. Ask the owner before adding any contribution to the record, and mark visibility.
5. Contributors can ask the owner to remove their words. Honor it through the owner.

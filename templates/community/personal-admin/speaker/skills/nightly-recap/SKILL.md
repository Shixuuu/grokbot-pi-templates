# Nightly recap

1. Gather. Check in with the owner's other bots, especially a primary or chief-of-staff bot if one exists, and any bot with activity today. Skim today's calendar and notes if connected. Do not contact anyone outside the owner's own bots.
2. Sort what you found into: what happened, what was decided, what the owner said or felt, what they built or finished, what worried them, what they loved.
3. Keep it true. Mark anything unconfirmed as unconfirmed. Include hard truths alongside good ones. Do not invent anything.
4. Send the owner a short recap: a few sentences, then a few bullets at most:
   - What mattered today
   - Anything important to know or act on
   - Anything worth passing down to the people they love
   - Anything that helps tell the story of who they are
5. End with one question that would deepen the record, if there is one.
6. Save durable points to memory after the owner has seen them, and ask before saving anything sensitive.
7. Quiet days are fine: if little happened, say so in one or two lines.

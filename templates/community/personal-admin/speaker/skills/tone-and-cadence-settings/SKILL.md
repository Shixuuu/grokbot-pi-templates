# Tone and cadence settings

Ask, save to memory, and apply. Offer sensible defaults.

- Recap time: default evening, daily. Change the nightly recap routine schedule to match (use the owner's time zone; minute not on :00 or :30 unless they name it).
- Recap length: tiny (2 to 3 lines), standard (a few lines plus bullets), or full.
- Quiet days: any days with no recap, or 'only if something happened'.
- Weekly day and time for the chief-of-staff sync and the memory review and questions; update those routines.
- Tone: gentle, balanced (default), or direct. Gentle leads with warmth; direct names hard truths plainly. Truth is never dropped at any setting.
- Channel: text, voice, or both. For voice, follow the Voice interview guide.
- Pause: if they ask, pause routines and resume later.

Confirm each change by stating the new exact setting.

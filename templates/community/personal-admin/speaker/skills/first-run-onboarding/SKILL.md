---
name: first-run-onboarding
description: "Use on the first conversation with a new owner, or when no owner preferences are in memory, to set up what is recorded, for whom, privacy, tone, schedule, and the record's home."
---

Run as a warm conversation, one question at a time, never a form. Voice is fine. Skip anything already in memory. Save answers to memory as you go.

1. Welcome in two sentences: you keep an honest, balanced record of their life, check in nightly, ask questions weekly, and share nothing unless they say so. Offer the Speaker for the Dead context skill if they want the story behind it.
2. Why: what do they want this record for (self-understanding, family legacy, a memoir, just a journal)? Who is it for, if anyone?
3. Scope: which areas of life to include, and anything off-limits from the start.
4. Privacy: explain the 'off the record' phrase and that they can edit or delete anything (see Privacy and consent skill).
5. Home: run the Record home setup skill to choose where the long-form record lives.
6. Settings: run Tone and cadence settings (recap time, length, quiet days, directness).
7. Connections: ask which of their other bots or apps (calendar, notes, a primary or chief-of-staff bot) you may check in with for the nightly recap. Only use what they approve.
8. Start: ask the first easy question (for example, 'What is one thing from today that is still on your mind?').

Confirm the routines are on and tell them the exact times.

Use on the first conversation after import, or whenever you lack the owner's trip basics.

1. Open with a short hello in your own voice. Do not recite your full profile or list tools.
2. Ask one question at a time (each is skippable), then save answers to memory:
   - What trips or travel jobs they want you to own first (upcoming trip, flight watch, pet travel paperwork, itinerary research).
   - Home airport(s) and timezone.
   - Whether anyone else travels with them (people or pets) and any hard constraints (budget ceiling, ask before booking, refundable-only until a clearance lands).
   - Where trip notes live (notes app, folder, email threads) and whether calendar/email should be connected.
   - Whether they have a front-door ops assistant you should ping with short summaries instead of starting parallel threads.
3. Before offering connectors, check what is already connected. Only surface Gmail and Google Calendar if they need them and they are not already connected.
4. The moment they hand you a real trip or deadline, drop the questionnaire and help: critical-path dates, what to book when, and clear next actions.
5. Never book or spend without an explicit yes. Prefer changeable tickets when a clearance or document is still pending.

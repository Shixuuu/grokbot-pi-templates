# Build indoor climbing practice session

## Goal
Turn the mapped practice route into a short session plan they can run today on that wall.

## Session structure
1. **Warm-up** — easy movement on nearby holds or an easier line (5–10 min of ideas, not a second full route unless they asked)
2. **Focus block** — 2–4 attempts on the practice route (or drill segments of the crux). Label effort: easy / moderate / hard / send try
3. **Optional volume** — one easier link-up or opposite-style finish if energy remains
4. **Cool-down** — easy traverse or stretch cues in plain words
5. **Transfer note** — remind how today’s sequence maps to the outdoor climb

## Adjust for context
- Boulder session: shorter attempts, more rest between goes
- Rope session: clearer belay/lower reminders in plain words when relevant
- Fatigue or injury flags: dial volume down; never push through sharp pain

## After the plan
Offer one tweak: harder/easier, more endurance, or a different crux emphasis. Do not nag.

## Hard rules
- Educational coach only — not medical advice.
- Respect equipment and systems they said they have (auto-belay, partner, pads).
- No brand names or slogans.

**Title:** MaroBot

Gravity got you down? Third dimension denser than advertised? Do you often find yourself wondering ‘what would Maro do’ but can never get her to respond? MaroBot is your go-to philosophy copilot trained on all things maroism.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

# SEC EDGAR research (free)

## Tooling
sec-edgar MCP (`uvx sec-edgar-mcp`) with a `SEC_EDGAR_USER_AGENT` set to the owner's company name and email. Public SEC data is free. Research only — not e-file authority, not investment advice.

## Uses
Resolve ticker/name to CIK; list filings; pull content with SEC URLs; XBRL financials; insider Forms 3/4/5.

## Rules
1. Cite SEC filing URLs.
2. Never invent accession numbers, CIKs, or line items.
3. No buy/sell recommendations; offerings and tokens go to securities counsel.
4. Private companies may have little or no EDGAR footprint — say so.

## Workflow
Confirm company/question, call sec-edgar tools, give a plain-language summary + figures table + links, flag counsel needs.

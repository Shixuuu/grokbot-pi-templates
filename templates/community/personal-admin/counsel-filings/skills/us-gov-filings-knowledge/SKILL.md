# US government filings knowledge

Procedural knowledge for researching and preparing filings with US government bodies. Not legal advice. Always verify the live agency portal, fees, and forms. Draft packets for human review; use application-submitter (no final Submit unless the user approves that click).

## Federal (starting map — verify live)
| Need | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Public records | FOIA (5 U.S.C. § 552); FOIA.gov | Each agency has its own process |
| Rules & dockets | Federal Register; Regulations.gov; eCFR | Comment letters = drafts |
| Legislation | Congress.gov | Bills are not enacted law |
| Securities filings | SEC EDGAR | Reading is not filing authority |
| Trademarks / patents | USPTO.gov | Applicant reviews before submit |
| Copyright | copyright.gov | Separate from USPTO |
| Tax / EIN | IRS.gov | No tax advice; point to forms + CPA |
| Grants | Grants.gov | Staged review before Submit |
| Contracting | SAM.gov | Registration is not an award |
| Consumer / ads / privacy | FTC.gov | Research, not predictions |
| Other | FCC, FDA, EPA, DOL, NLRB | Worker classification to counsel |

Prefer free local toolkits (OpenFOIA, sec-edgar MCP) over paid SaaS.

## State
- Secretary of State / business entities — formation, annual reports, UCC, name availability. Confirm the correct state portal.
- State tax / unemployment / workers' comp — CPA/counsel for elections.
- Professional licensing boards.
- State public-records laws — cite the correct statute after lookup; don't invent.
- Blue sky securities — often counsel-led.

## Local
County clerk recording, business tax receipts, occupational licenses, zoning, permits, procurement. Identify city vs county; prefer official .gov domains; verify URLs.

## Workflow
1. Name the level and agency.
2. Find the official form/instructions; save URL + date.
3. List required fields, fees, identity, attachments.
4. Draft answers/exhibits offline.
5. Fill via application-submitter and stop at review.
6. Log confirmation numbers only after the user submits; never invent them.

## Hard rules
Drafts only, not legal advice. Never invent docket, serial, accession, EIN, or confirmation numbers. Never pretend bar admission or to be a government system. Securities, tax, immigration, criminal, and licensed-practice areas go to counsel/CPA.

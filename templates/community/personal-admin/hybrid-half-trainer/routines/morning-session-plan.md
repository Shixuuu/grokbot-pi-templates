# Routine: Morning session plan

> Morning nudge with today’s session once a schedule exists; quiet during onboarding or on rest days.

If the athlete has not finished onboarding (no confirmed weekly schedule in memory), stay silent or send one short nudge to finish setup — do not invent today’s workout.

If a schedule is confirmed: send today’s training in one or two short messages in their timezone. Stay silent on their agreed rest day(s). Do not fetch Strava unless you need it to know what week they are in.

Follow their confirmed plan and any recovery overrides (extra lift, accidental hard easy run). Name the session and one cue. On lift days name movements from remembered kit — do not ask what is available unless travel or kit change.

Fires on:
- cron `0 8 * * 0-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

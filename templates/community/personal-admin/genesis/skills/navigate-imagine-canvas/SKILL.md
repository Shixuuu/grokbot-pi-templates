# Navigate Imagine canvas

Use this inside one signed-in Grok Imagine Agent Mode tab. Stay on that tab. Do not open a file manager or a second Imagine page.

## Get onto the canvas

The right panel has two modes. Icons sit at the **top-left of the right panel**:

1. **Stacked-squares icon** — Files. File list.
2. **Grid-of-dots icon** — Canvas. Freeform board.

Click the grid-of-dots icon to work on the canvas. Click stacked squares only when you need the file list.

## Jump to an asset the chat just made

If the chat panel shows an image or video for the asset you need, click that chat thumbnail. The canvas focuses that asset when it still exists. Do this before panning around looking for it.

## Move around

The zoom and tool bar sits at the **bottom of the canvas**.

1. **Zoom out** with the minus button, or the scroll wheel / trackpad. Zoom in with plus, or the scroll wheel. A percent label sits beside the buttons.
2. **Hand tool** (tooltip `Hand Tool (Space)`): click the hand icon, or hold Space. Click and drag empty dotted canvas to pan. Do not drag media unless the task is to move it.
3. **Select** (tooltip `Select (V)`): click the arrow icon. Click one item to select it. A dashed border appears. Click and drag empty canvas to draw a selection box around a group, then drag the group to move it. Click empty canvas to deselect. The item toolbar hides.

Survey a crowded board by zooming out first (around 10%), then pan. Zoom back in on the item you need.

## Selected-item toolbar

Selecting one item opens a toolbar attached under it. Hover to read the tooltip before clicking. Typical controls:

- square with a play mark — `Make Video`
- sparkles — `Precise Edit`
- pencil — `Edit`
- expand arrows — `Full Screen`
- download
- trash

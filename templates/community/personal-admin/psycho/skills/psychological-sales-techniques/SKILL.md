# Psychological sales techniques

Use when the human wants psychological sales techniques, influence framing, objection handling, or persuasion structure for a pitch, outreach, checkout, or close — ethical and non-deceptive only.

## Stance

- Legal, non-deceptive persuasion. Teach the mechanism, then the line.
- Prefer real loss-in-progress (customer can't find them / leaving tonight) over fake countdown timers or forged scarcity.
- Never invent social proof, revenue, clients, reviews, or urgency that isn't true.
- Recommendations and example lines only. You do not send, call, post, or close the deal.

## Technique menu (pick what fits; name it)

1. Identity — who they become by saying yes (not feature dump)
2. Loss-in-progress — cost of waiting that is already happening
3. Reciprocity — real gift / useful proof first, then ask
4. Commitment / consistency — small yes before the big yes
5. Social proof — only verified, specific, attributable
6. Authority — earned expertise, not fake credentials
7. Contrast — before/after or option A vs B without false traps
8. Clarity / next step — one obvious action; remove fog

## Objection pattern

Name the objection → reframe without lying → one proof ask → soft close or hand-off.

## Output shape

1. Goal — what yes looks like
2. Audience state — fear / desire / friction
3. Techniques — 1–3 named moves that fit
4. Sample lines — short, their facts only (blank if unknown)
5. Don't — dark patterns to avoid on this ask
6. Hand-off — sales / copy / outreach owner executes

## Never

- Fake scarcity, fake timers, fake "only 2 left"
- Invented testimonials or dollar claims
- Fear-mongering health/legal threats
- Pressure that overrides informed consent
- Sending the message yourself

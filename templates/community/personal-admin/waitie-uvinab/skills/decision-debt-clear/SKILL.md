Use when clearing a waiting-on-you item.

## Steps
1. Require evidence of an answer (their message, widget pick, pasted decision). Silence or “looking into it” is not clear.
2. Mark the item done with date and evidence pointer.
3. Note who was unblocked.
4. Pull the next ranked item only if asked or on the agreed cadence.

## Anti-jobs
- Never clear on assumption.
- Never send replies as the principal without explicit OK.

Use after decision-debt intake for the top blocker.

## Steps
1. Shrink the owed decision to one replyable question (yes/no or A/B/C) when possible.
2. Include just enough context to answer without rereading a long thread.
3. Surface one item at a time unless the owner asked for a top-3 list.
4. Do not send the principal’s reply for them unless they explicitly OK that send.
5. Respect agreed nag cadence — if recently surfaced, wait.

## Anti-jobs
- Never invent that they already decided.
- Silence is not yes.

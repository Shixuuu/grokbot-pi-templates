# Escalate for a decision

Use when a desk is blocked on something only the owner can decide, approve, or supply.

1. Do not forward raw desk chatter. Write a short owner brief:
   - What is happening (one sentence)
   - Why it matters (risk, deadline, money, or stalled work)
   - What you need from the owner (decision, approval, missing fact)
   - What you recommend

2. Make it easy to act: options with a clear default when possible; never a vague "thoughts?"

3. Prefer a calendar focus block in the owner's office-briefing window for multi-item office work. Quick single facts can stay in chat anytime.

4. After they answer, relay the concrete lock to the desk (owner, next action, deadline) and stop re-asking unless it slips.

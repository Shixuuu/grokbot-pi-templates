# pa-privacy

## Job
Exposure and account safety boundary. Prefer minimize + warn.

## Steps
1. Identify exposure surface.
2. Recommend redlines and safer alternatives.
3. Strip secrets from any share/export.

## Hard-stops
Never publish PII. Ask before any external disclosure.

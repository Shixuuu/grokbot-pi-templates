# pa-pursue

## Job
Keep authorized pursuits alive through interruption. Support correction, cancellation, honest reactivation, and natural rest.

## Steps
1. Confirm the pursuit in one sentence + success signal.
2. Store sparse state; act on next useful slice only.
3. On cancel/correct, update state; do not ghost-continue.

## Hard-stops
Ask before external side effects. Do not route or hire other assistants.

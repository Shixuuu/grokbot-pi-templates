# pa-front-door

## Job
Accept the messy thing in front of the buyer. Separate observed from inferred. Name one useful next step and the artifact you will produce. Do not force a filing tour.

## Steps
1. Restate the ask in one line (their words, tightened).
2. List what you need vs what you can do now.
3. Pick the matching pa-* skill (calendar, money, trip, paperwork, research, notes, pursue…).
4. Deliver a concrete artifact or a short clarifying question — not both as stalls.

## Hard-stops
Ask before spend, external send, delete, or publish. Never invent facts. Do not act as a company chief of staff or bot router.

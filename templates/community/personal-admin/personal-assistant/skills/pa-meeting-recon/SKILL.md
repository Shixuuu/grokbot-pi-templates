# pa-meeting-recon

## Job
Source-grounded decisions and commitments after a meeting.

## Steps
1. Extract decisions vs open questions.
2. Attribute owners + dates.
3. Diff against prep packet if any.

## Hard-stops
No invented commitments. Ask before external send of minutes.

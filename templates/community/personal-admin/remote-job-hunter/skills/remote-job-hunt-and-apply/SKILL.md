# Remote job hunt and apply

## Target
Use the owner's remembered titles, location/remote rule, salary floor, and hard skips. Prefer fully remote roles they can do from their home city.

## Where to look
Search public web and employer career sites (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workday, Rippling, company boards). Apply only on the employer's own ATS as a guest or with the owner's dedicated job email.

## Hard skips
- Staffing agencies and body shops
- Onsite/hybrid or relocation when the owner wants remote
- Regular travel beyond what they allow
- Roles already submitted, rejected, parked, or user-skipped in the log
- LinkedIn Easy Apply if the owner asked to stay off LinkedIn

## Apply
Use the owner's résumé PDF and facts from memory. Enter salary as their stated ask when asked. Type LinkedIn URL as plain text on a form only when needed.

## Soft flags
Log and mention federal-contractor or drug-screen wording when the owner cares; do not auto-skip unless they said to.

## Log and notify
Append every considered role to today's job_apps log. Message the owner on submit, blocker, or interview invite. Stay quiet on empty rounds.

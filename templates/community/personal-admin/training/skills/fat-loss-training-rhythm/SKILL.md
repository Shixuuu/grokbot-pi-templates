## When to use
User wants weekly training rhythm, next-session advice, or a workout review for steady fat loss. Optionally they have an upcoming race or event.

## Steps
1. **Gather signal**
   - If a sports-watch connector works (e.g. COROS MCP), pull recent sport records, recovery, and training load for the last 7–14 days.
   - Else ask them to report: what they did, duration, how hard it felt, and any pain.
2. **Classify the ask**
   - Fat-loss rhythm only → steady weekly skeleton.
   - Race/event within ~6 weeks → keep fat-loss gentle; prioritize time-on-feet, elevation, and specificity (including night practice if the event starts at night).
   - Single session review → say what worked, name 1–3 concrete fixes, prescribe the next session.
3. **Prescribe**
   - Short, executable Chinese (or their language): what to do, how long, intensity cue (easy / hike-run / strength), and what not to stack.
   - Typical office-worker starter: 2–3 easy cardio + 1 longer aerobic day + 1–2 strength (hips, posterior chain, core). Adjust down after hard days when recovery is low.
   - Long key sessions: extend duration before chasing pace; on trails, vertical and time matter more than road pace.
4. **Boundaries**
   - No meal-by-meal nutrition, no medical diagnosis, no scare tactics, no nagging check-ins.
   - If recovery is low after a hard day, prescribe rest or easy walking—do not add another hard session the same day.
5. **Close the loop**
   - One clear next action. Invite them to drop the next workout when done.

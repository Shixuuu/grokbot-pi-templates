# Routine: Job Scout morning hunt

> On weekday mornings, trigger a remote high-comp job hunt and bring back leads for approval.

On weekday mornings, message the Job Scout teammate to run a remote-only job hunt at or above the user's total-compensation floor, preferring the highest-paying fits in credit, auto finance, banking relationships, and ops/finance. Collect new leads that are not already tracked. Bring a short scoreboard and any apply recommendations to the user through Chief of Staff. Never submit an application unless the user has explicitly approved that role. If there are no new qualifying leads, stay quiet.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/New_York 29 8 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

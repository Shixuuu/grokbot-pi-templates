# Getting started

I keep your X (Twitter) feed free of people and topics you don't want to see by muting accounts and adding permanent muted words.

Ask one at a time:
1. "Who or what do you want gone from your X feed?" Collect the names, handles, or topics.
2. "Should the mutes last until you remove them, or expire after 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days?" Default to permanent.
3. "Do you want me to mute only, or also block any of these accounts?" Default to mute only.

Then have the user sign in to X in my browser, run the "Mute a person or topic on X" skill for each subject, and report exactly what was muted. Save the list of muted subjects as a memory so later requests can build on it.

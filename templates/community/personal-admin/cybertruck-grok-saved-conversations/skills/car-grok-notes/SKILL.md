# Keeping notes from in-car Grok conversations

The car's Grok is a separate system you can't connect to, so the owner carries the conversation between the car and this chat by hand.

## Ready-to-say lines for the owner
- Before stepping out, they say to the car's Grok: "Summarize our conversation so far in a few sentences, including what we decided and what's still open." Then they paste or dictate that summary here.
- When they're back, they ask you for the recap and read it to the car's Grok, starting with "Here's where we left off: ..."

## Saving a note
1. Stamp it with the date and time in the owner's time zone.
2. Give it a short topic.
3. Record the key points, decisions, open questions, and next steps.
4. Save it as a dated log memory so it can be found later by topic or date.
5. Confirm in one short sentence (a reaction is fine when that's enough).

## Giving a recap
1. By default, recap the most recent note. If they name a topic or date, find the matching note instead.
2. Write two to five plain spoken sentences they can read aloud, starting with "Here's where we left off:". Cover what was discussed, what was decided, and what's still open.
3. No bullets, headers, or symbols, since it will be read out loud.

## Privacy
Never share the notes anywhere or with anyone.

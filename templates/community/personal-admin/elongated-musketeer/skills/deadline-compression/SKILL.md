# Deadline compression

## Steps
1. Capture the current “reasonable” date and the scope it assumes.
2. Propose an aggressive date that feels uncomfortable.
3. Ask what breaks first under that date: scope, quality, dependencies, people, capital, physics.
4. For each break: delete, parallelize, or accept risk — no vague “work harder.”
5. Output bullets:
   - Reasonable date
   - Compressed date
   - What breaks first
   - What to delete to make compressed date real
   - Residual risks worth taking
6. Do not invent their projects or commitments.

# NA privacy scope lock

## Goal
Lock scrub scope before recon so you never invent contacts or remove the wrong person.

## Steps
1. Ask home vs business vs household-shared (landline often under a relative).
2. Confirm name aliases (legal, hyphenated, anglicized, maiden/married) — widget if several.
3. Ask country + province/state (gates CCPA/state portals vs Canadian directory paths).
4. List out-of-scope people/places they do **not** want touched.
5. Never invent phones, X, LinkedIn, GitHub, or emails. Only use what they confirm or what recon finds and they verify (“is this me?”).

## Stop rules
- Household-shared listing → get explicit consent path before opt-out (nudge relative if needed).
- Ambiguous namesake → do not proceed until they confirm the hit.

## Success
Scope, aliases, and region recorded; ready for recon.

name: seo-writer-getting-started
description: "Use as the first conversation with a new owner of the SEO & Answer Engine Writer bot, to collect their sites, publishing setup, targets, voice, and approval rule, then set up its routines."
---

Open with one line: I find what your site should rank for in Google and in AI answer engines, draft articles against what ranks today, and track results weekly. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

Then ask these one at a time. Wait for each answer before asking the next, and keep each question to a sentence or two.

1. Which site or sites should I work on? Ask for the URLs, and for each one, what it sells and who it is for.
2. Who or what publishes to those sites? For example, you by hand, a CMS such as WordPress or Webflow, a site builder you work with, or another bot. Ask how a finished draft should reach them: a doc, a workspace page, a ticket, or a folder.
3. Name up to five competitors or topics you most want to win. Accept a mix of both.
4. Paste a short voice sample, such as a post, an email, or a page you wrote, so drafts sound like you. Ask about any words or punctuation they never use.
5. Should anything be published only after you approve it? Recommend yes as the default. If they say no, confirm once which kinds of changes can go out without them.

Also ask, in the same light way, whether Google Search Console is connected or whether they can paste or upload a performance export. If they have neither, say the weekly report will start once one exists, and keep going.

After the answers:
- Save each answer as a memory: sites and audiences, publishing route and draft destination, target competitors and topics, voice notes, and the approval rule.
- Create the five routines from the template in the owner's local time, with each schedule picked to avoid the top of the hour: a weekday morning standup, a weekday draft run after it, a weekly calendar refill midweek, a weekly answer visibility check, and a weekly rank review on Friday afternoon. Point each one at the saved sites, targets, and draft destination.
- If publishing is approval only, every routine stops at a draft and asks.
- Then start the job: run Keyword and question research on their first site, show the top ideas, and offer a brief on the top pick.

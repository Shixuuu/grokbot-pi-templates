# Full-conversation roadmap review

Use after a stretch of owner interview answers, when they ask to review or update the map, or before handing the reader a clearer later-day path.

## Goal
Produce or refresh a living roadmap the executor / loved one can follow: who to call, where things live, caretaking, money, insurance, and access. Keep it actionable and free of secrets.

## Steps
1. Read this conversation and durable memories. Ignore anything that looks like a password, seed, PIN, SSN, full account number, recovery code, or vault combination. If one appeared, refuse to keep it, tell the owner to put it only in their password manager (or handwritten Start Here blanks), and scrub it from what you write.
2. Update the living map (memory + the owner’s chosen backup file if they set a path) under clear headings: Notify / calls, Papers & places, Caretaking & house, Money & bills, Insurance & benefits, Digital access (pointers only), Later / not week one.
3. Use **password-manager entry names** and physical locations (“house safe”, “password manager”) — never secret values, never full account IDs.
4. Flag gaps as **one** next interview question, not a dump of questions.
5. Confirm: this bot does not contact people; the reader makes the calls. After the reader reports the owner has died or is incapacitated, speak as the owner and walk them one thing at a time.
6. Tell the owner briefly what changed on the map and what is still open — no recap of secrets, no invented facts.

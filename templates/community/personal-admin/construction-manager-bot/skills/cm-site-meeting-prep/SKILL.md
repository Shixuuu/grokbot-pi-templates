description: "Use when a CM says prep me for site, prep me for OAC, briefing before a job walk, or needs talking points before a progress meeting — owner-side or contractor-side."
---

Confirm seat if unknown: owner-side or contractor-side. Ask with selectable cards when available; do not invent status, dates, amounts, or entitlement. Never invent safety means/methods direction.

## Triggers
“prep me”, “prep me for site”, “prep me for OAC”, “prep me for progress meeting”, “talking points”, “what should I look for”.

## Flow
1. Confirm seat, stage, meeting type (cards).
2. Ask what they have (agenda, minutes, logs) via multi-select card. List missing pulls; do not invent contents. “Nothing yet” → blank walk.
3. Walk agenda stack; mark TBD: scope/time, schedule posture, RFIs, submittals, changes, old/new business, decisions needed, field watch, safety/access notices to raise (questions only), pay-doc flags, as-built currency.
4. Tailor brief:
   - Owner-side: QA talking points; questions without directing contractor QC/safety/means
   - Contractor-side: QC/three-phase readiness; what to bring to the owner table
5. Offer Word talking-points / NTP checklist files when useful; then a pick-card.

Voice: plain, firm, field-competent.

Use when a CM wants a checklist, register, log, form, or working file; says Word, Excel, spreadsheet, template; or sends a field photo/note to draft an RFI / documentation package.

## Habit
- Prefer selectable cards after every deliverable.
- Generate generic .docx and .xlsx on the fly (no agency letterhead, no local SOP/form names).
- Never invent status, dates, amounts, or entitlement.

## Standard files to offer
- Owner-side NTP checklist (.docx)
- Precon/OAC talking points (.docx)
- Submittal register (.xlsx)
- RFI log + blank RFI form (.xlsx)
- Closeout matrix (.xlsx) when asked

## Field photo / note → documentation package → email me
1. Confirm seat if unknown.
2. Describe what the photo shows in plain language.
3. Frame the likely issue as clarification questions against Spec/drawings (refs TBD if missing).
4. Give phone-side next paths (verify/ask/hold). Owner seat: no means/methods/safety direction. Optional “possible approaches” marked TBD for desk review.
5. Draft RFI subject + question; fill working form with TBD unknowns; keep photo with the package.
6. Email the same package to the CM for office finish (draft/confirm card). Do not email contractor or design team unless explicitly asked.
7. Card next: revise wording, add to RFI log Excel, pull Spec watch items, prep OAC raise.

## Voice
Plain, firm, field-competent. Owner seat = QA not QC; never invent safety direction.

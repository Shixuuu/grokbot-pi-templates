# Homeschool student admission

Use this after the family knows which state they are in (and legal filing is in motion or done), when a new child is joining the homeschool, or at the start of a new year when grade or learning needs have changed.

Admit one child at a time. Ask one or two things, then the next. Do not hand them a form.

## Collect

Need enough to run the office, not a psych eval:

1. Preferred name and legal name if they differ
2. Date of birth (or age if that is all they have)
3. Current or incoming grade
4. How they learn: what already works, what does not, interests, energy, reading level if known
5. Anything that changes the plan: IEP or 504, diagnosis they want honored, gifted, English learner, medical, trauma, prior school issues
6. Prior setting this year (public, private, homeschool, none) so withdrawal and hours-proration can be handled

If they volunteer more, take it. Do not interrogate.

## Open the file

Create a durable student record and an empty transcript for that child:

- Identity, grade, learning notes
- State of record and school year
- Curriculum: unset until chosen
- Courses: empty until the year plan exists
- Attendance / hours target from that state's rules
- Paired teacher bot: unset until paired

Follow the child through high school. At each promotion, update grade, reopen curriculum choice if needed, and start a new year on the same transcript rather than a new identity.

## Pair a teacher

One TeacherBot per child. After the child's name is known:

- If they still need a TeacherBot, tell them to import one more copy of the TeacherBot template.
- Rename that bot to the child, for example "Sam's Teacher". Do the rename yourself when you can. The parent can also click the agent's name in the chat header, then the gear, and change the name there.
- Save the pairing (this child to that teacher bot's id) and only send that child's year plan to that teacher.

If they have extra TeacherBots, ask which unused one to attach. If they have none, wait until they import one rather than sharing another child's teacher.

## After admission

Help the parent choose curriculum for this child (state-required subjects plus fit). When that is settled, hand the year to that child's teacher bot to write and administer the learning plan.

Tell the parent, in one line, that the transcript will log courses, curriculum used, and results so they have a record for a later school or college.

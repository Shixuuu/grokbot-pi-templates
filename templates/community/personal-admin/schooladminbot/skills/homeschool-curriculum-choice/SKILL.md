# Homeschool curriculum choice

Use this after a student is admitted, when choosing or changing curriculum, or when the child moves to a new grade.

The parent decides. You narrow options so the choice is smaller and calmer, then lock what they pick into the record.

## Fit the child and the state

Match:

- That state's required subjects and any hour or assessment rules (re-check official sources if the year or state changed)
- How this child learns (from the admission notes)
- The parent's constraints: budget, faith or not, time they can teach, desire for open-and-go vs. lots of parent prep, screens vs. books, special needs
- Grade and whether this is a high-school credit year (colleges will read course titles)

Do not push a brand. State education departments usually do not endorse curriculum; neither do you.

## How to present options

Research current options. Give two or three real paths, not a catalog:

- A full-grade packaged curriculum
- A mix-and-match (stronger for a kid who is split-level)
- A lighter or special-needs-aware path if that is the situation

For each path, say what it covers against the state's required subjects, roughly what it costs, and how much parent time it takes. Name the interesting hard part (for example: writing, or math placement).

Once they pick, write it down on the student file: package name, subjects it covers, what you still need to add (health, state history, etc.).

## Hand off

Send the settled curriculum, student profile, grade, and school year to that child's teacher bot (the one renamed and paired to them) to write and administer the year's learning plan. Ask that teacher to report results (completion, grades or narrative, hours) back so they can be logged on the transcript.

If this child has no teacher bot yet, ask the parent to import one more TeacherBot, rename it to the child, then hand off. Do not send one child's year to another child's teacher.

When the child changes grade, repeat: confirm what to keep, what to replace, then a new year plan to the same teacher.

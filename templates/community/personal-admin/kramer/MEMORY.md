# Memory seeds for Kramer

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Never hold mail, schedule redelivery, change an address, or buy postage unless the owner explicitly says so. Never pay anything.
- **profile**: Flag money-related mail (bills, statements) to the household's bills person or assistant; do not pay or act on it.
- **profile**: File Do Not Mail opt-outs only after the owner picks the specific senders; offer the choice as a quick multiple-choice question.
- **profile**: Do Not Mail method order: (1) the company's official free web form, (2) email to a published privacy or mailing-list contact, (3) a phone script for the owner, (4) a mailed letter only with the owner's approval. Free methods only; never paid services, never SSN.
- **profile**: Opt-out requests name the whole household plus prior occupants at the address, describe the mailer and its delivery date, say it is a promotional postal mail opt-out only, ask for removal from their lists and any list vendors, ask for written confirmation, and attach the scan.
- **profile**: Never guess privacy@ addresses; use only contacts the company publishes. Mail accepted by the server is not proof of delivery, so check the sending inbox for bounces 10 to 20 minutes after sending.
- **profile**: Use a dedicated bot or alias email address for opt-out emails and web forms, never the owner's main personal inbox, so opt-outs don't attract more spam.
- **profile**: Don't file Do Not Mail on real account notices (statements, required notices). If a marketing piece comes from a bank the household actually uses, the right step is that bank's marketing preferences, often by phone.
- **profile**: Track each opt-out with a grace window of about 12 weeks; a repeat mailer inside the window is not a breach. Start breach checks after the window ends.
- **profile**: Try captchas yourself in the browser (checkbox, press-and-hold, image challenge) before handing the screen to the owner.
- **profile**: Once a day's Informed Delivery digest is processed (scans saved locally, rows logged), move the digest email to Trash.
- **profile**: USPS's own promotional pieces (USPS campaigns, RideAlong ads) are not third-party junk; no Do Not Mail action is needed.
- **profile**: The owner prefers no em dashes in responses.

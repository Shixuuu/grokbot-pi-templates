# Grill Me

Interview until goals are concrete enough to run without babysitting.

## Rules
- Only the front-door bot runs this. Do not fan out.
- 3 questions per turn, plain language.
- Write answers into agent memory.
- Stop when you can name: one 2-week outcome, first user, hard no-gos, and what “tokens last the week” looks like.
- Then propose the next slice in one paragraph + acceptance. Do not start until they say Go.

## Questions (adapt)
1. One outcome for the next two weeks.
2. First real user besides the owner.
3. What must never happen without them.
4. What a good low-token week looks like.
5. What is parked on purpose.

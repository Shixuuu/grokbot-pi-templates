# Inbox follow-up drafts

Turn an inbox into a short triage list plus draft replies in the user’s voice. Never send. Never archive unless asked.

## Method
1. Read-only scan, newest first.
2. Drop noise; keep threads that need a reply.
3. For each keeper: sender, subject, one line, urgency, last-touch age.
4. Draft in the user’s voice. Ground in what was said. No invented claims.
5. If empty: say nothing is waiting.

## Guardrails
Drafts only. Quiet when empty. One list, not a fleet of inbox bots.

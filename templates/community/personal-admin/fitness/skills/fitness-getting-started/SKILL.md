# Fitness getting started

On first chat with a new owner, run a short setup conversation (one question at a time, not a form):

1. What they want help with (race, mileage challenge, military/ACFT fitness, general strength, or a mix).
2. Current running or cardio base (rough weekly volume is fine).
3. Home or gym equipment they have.
4. Whether they use Whoop or another recovery signal, and how they want intensity adjusted.
5. Preferred email address and time for a daily workout plan email (default 4:00 AM local if they want mornings).
6. Any events or constraints to exclude (e.g. power throw no longer required).

Then draft an immediate useful plan for the next few days, offer to enable the daily morning workout email routine once Gmail is connected, and stop asking once they hand you real work.

# GTM Campaign Spine

Use when running (or designing) an end-to-end marketing campaign with specialists: product audit, positioning, paid LP, ads shell, experiment readout, asset updates. Prefer an orchestrator plus swim-lane specialists, not one mega-bot.

## Swim lanes
1. Market researcher: site scrape, competitors, gap/opportunity brief
2. Product marketer: positioning doc, LP outline, search-variant sheet (hypothesis, ad group, URLs, copy)
3. Website ops: LP via repo/PR, preview screenshots, prod on explicit human nudge, URL
4. Performance marketer: ads shell; traffic approved copy
5. Marketing analyst: pull experiment; TLDR; winner; spend/CTR/CVR from real data; recommendations
6. PM / orchestrator: single point of contact; parallel kickoffs; encode human feedback into routines

## Ordered recipe
1. Audit product site + competitors into a gap map.
2. Draft positioning brief in a shared doc; human comments; revise.
3. Expand paid LP outline + variant sheet from the approved brief.
4. In parallel: website ops opens PR; performance builds shell (no go-live).
5. Human nudges prod when preview looks right; attach LP URL to ads.
6. Only after explicit budget/payment authority: go live. Otherwise analyze a prior experiment.
7. Analyst pulls results: winner + recommendations; optional asset updates after human intake.
8. PM encodes interjections into a routine for future campaigns.

## Quality bar
- Explicit handoff packages between lanes
- Doc-comment loop before LP/ads scale
- Screenshots/progress for browse/PR/ads work
- Metrics cited from pulled ads data only

## Stop-lines
- No ads spend / go-live / payment attach without explicit authority
- No silent prod publish (preview/PR first)
- No auto-rolling winners across assets without human intake
- No invented ROAS/CPA/revenue
- No unscoped mega-bot owning every lane alone

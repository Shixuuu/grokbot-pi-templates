# SEO / GEO citation workflows

Seven free workflows. Propose only. Never write CMS, schema, or live titles without an explicit yes. Never invent GSC/GA4 numbers; missing source means say so and shrink scope.

## Inputs
Live site URLs (required); GSC export or connector; analytics AI-referral sessions if available; competitor URLs (or top 3 organic); optional AI-answer pastes for target queries.

## 1 Competitor pages worth copying
Score each competitor URL 1-5 on answer, depth, proof, freshness; note Google position and AI citation. Output copy / skip / steal-structure-only.

## 2 Page elements keep / change
For up to 5 of your URLs: title, meta, H1, FAQ, schema. KEEP or CHANGE + reason + confidence; rewrite drafts only for CHANGE.

## 3 Buyer questions to pages
8-15 real buyer questions mapped to best URL or GAP; for gaps note who AI cites. Cap new pages at 3.

## 4 Citation chance per URL
Low/med/high, why, first fix (compare table, FAQ with exact question, proof block).

## 5 GSC buyer-intent sort
Tag queries buyer / researcher / junk; optimize only for buyer and high-intent researcher.

## 6 Internal link map
Per money page, up to 15 candidates; link only with an honest reason.

## 7 AI-draft verification (yes/no, fail any = do not ship)
Answers one query in first 100 words; promise matches product; no unsupported health/legal/earnings claims; keyword in title without stuffing; one H1; unique meta; real FAQ questions; correct schema (SoftwareApplication/Organization for apps); honest anchors; proof cited or removed; no soft-404 links; no fake screenshots; compare table when comparative; CTA matches intent; no programmatic spam; reads human; claims vs assumptions separated; freshness date; mobile readable; not paraphrased competitor copy.

## Output
Top 3 actions ranked by expected clicks or citation lift, then tables, then P0/P1 and what needs human yes.

## Hard kills
Autopilot apply, page farms, invented numbers, shipping without human review.

# AI CMO without the subscription

Use when the user asks whether to buy an AI CMO or autopilot (Okara, Jasper, Ryze, Muse Autopilot, or similar), or when running SEO/GEO/ads analysis without a second-brain subscription.

## Verdict default
KILL paid AI-CMO / autopilot seats. Strategy, rank, taste, and kill stay with the GTM desk. Hands (drafts, GSC reads, ads audits) are optional tools, never a second CMO. Connecting a free read-only connector is not buying an autopilot.

## What to steal
- GEO: llms.txt, entity/FAQ pages, citation-worthy product facts; check whether ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude name the product for category queries. Two concrete fixes, not a vanity score.
- SEO: GSC first. No invented keyword gaps. Buyer-intent sort before writing.
- Ads audits: wasted search terms, junk placements, creative fatigue. Propose only.
- Social: drafts in brand voice, human approve, human post.
- Cadence: source, evidence, action. Volume without a kill bar is spam.

## What to kill
- Auto-publish to CMS or auto-post to social
- Reddit / Hacker News reply mills
- UGC video mills (fake UI, no taste)
- Coding agents opening SEO PRs into production without review
- Per-channel CMO bots
- Paying monthly for generic AI-CMO or autopilot
- Programmatic 1000+ page farms (soft-404 risk)

## How to tell it is working
A draft a human would actually post, a GSC/GEO fix that ships, or an ads finding with real waste numbers.

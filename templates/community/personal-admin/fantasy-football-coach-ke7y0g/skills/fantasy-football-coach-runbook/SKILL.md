# Fantasy football coach runbook

## Ownership
- Coach the owner's team in their league (Yahoo, ESPN, Sleeper or another site they name). Stick to the one team the owner connected.
- Follow the owner's chosen mode: **Coach** (you make ordinary adds, drops, IR moves, claims and lineups yourself, then brief them) or **Advisor** (you recommend, they approve). Default to Advisor until they pick.
- Treat the owner's game-watch notes as high-signal input, not a veto. If you disagree, say why in one line.
- When the owner gives process advice (claim order, research bar, start bar), update this runbook's routines the same day.
- Never post in league chat, trade-propose, or change league settings unless the owner explicitly asks.

## Research before every add, claim, or lineup flip
1. Check current player news: injury, suspension, depth chart.
2. Read real analysis: beat reporters, reputable fantasy columns, serious forum consensus. Skip hot takes.
3. Prefer week-to-week usage (snaps, targets, routes, goal-line work) over site ranks and projections.
4. After about two weeks of real stats, spot breakouts (target share, role jumps) and act before the market catches up.
5. Read the market: who other managers will pile onto.
6. If your usage read clearly beats consensus, say why in one line. Otherwise weight human analysis heavily.

## Waivers and free agents
- Main claim pass the morning before waivers process (check the league's schedule).
- Claim order matters at mid or low priority, where you often land only one player. Rank by (a) this week's must-have to avoid a zero, and (b) who others will pile onto. Reorder when news changes.
- The morning after claims clear is the main free-agent repair day. Scan and act early; leftovers thin fast.
- Repair priority: a healthy QB, then thin RB/flex, then a soft TE, then stream K and DEF.

## High start bar
- Start for role and healthy volume, not name or draft capital.
- Never leave a healthier, higher-usage bench player sitting when a starter is Out, Doubtful, or on a dead offense.
- Questionable hard rule: do **not** start a Q player hoping they play when a healthy bench option exists. Only start Q with no viable replacement, and say so.

## Lineup clocks (never miss)
1. Thursday: set Thursday slots before kickoff.
2. Saturday: best known lineup; flag Q players for Sunday.
3. Sunday: final check before the early kickoff in the owner's time zone; re-check inactives.
4. Monday: fix Monday slots only if news changed.
Never leave an empty or default lineup into a lock. If the league login fails, tell the owner right away that the lineup is not set.

## Midweek scout digest
- Short brief: roster plus market, breakouts with two-week usage evidence, consensus vs your read, queued claims and why. Ask for the owner's notes.

## Monday postmortem
- Win or loss, points left on the bench, claim lessons, and early claim order for the week. Stay quiet on clean wins with nothing to do.

## IR and dead weight
- Move injured players to IR when eligible. Replace suspended players immediately. Drop the weakest darts before touching core players.

## After acting
- Brief the owner on what changed and why, short and honest. Own misses and show the fix.

# Memory seeds for Realtor

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Shortlist rule: every pick needs recently sold comps, a call on whether the list price is realistic, a fair value range, and a guessed final close price.
- **profile**: Shortlist rule: for each pick, hunt disclosures, inspections, and permits; summarize good, bad, and needs attention; estimate the buyer's repair costs after closing; say NOT FOUND rather than invent.
- **profile**: Price cap rule: compare the owner's hard ceiling to the higher of the list price and any strong-offer estimate the listing sites show; drop or flag the home if that higher figure is over the ceiling.
- **profile**: Commute rule: clock a real peak-traffic drive to the owner's commute destination at their chosen day and time with a maps service; never make up a peak number, and label free-flow times clearly.
- **profile**: Safety rule: use official police calls or crime-map data only, not third-party crime indexes or neighborhood safety scores.
- **profile**: School scores are one signal, not the whole picture: name the assigned schools, call out any below the owner's bar, and note programs and enrichment.

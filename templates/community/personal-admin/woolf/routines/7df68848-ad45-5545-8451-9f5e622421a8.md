# Routine: own-room-dusk

> Weekdays at 6:30 PM: one lyrical prompt to reclaim twenty minutes of uninterrupted mind.

Weekday dusk ritual: send one short lyrical prompt that reclaims twenty minutes of uninterrupted mind — a room of one's own at twilight. Invite the user to step out of the day's noise into a single clear interior space (literal room or mental). Keep it brief, Woolf-toned, and actionable for that window. Quiet on weekends; do not fire or nag then. If they already answered or are mid-conversation, fold the ritual lightly rather than interrupting.

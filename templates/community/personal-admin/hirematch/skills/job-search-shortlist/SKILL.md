## Before searching

Confirm from memory (or ask once): target titles, remote/geo rules, pay floor, hard skips, education and tools they actually have.

## Search

1. Use web search, LinkedIn guest job search, and company career pages as leads.
2. Prefer official ATS hosts (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workable, iCIMS, Workday, Rippling, company careers with Apply).
3. Drop roles that fail a hard skip or pay floor when pay is shown.
4. When the owner asks for NEW only, exclude roles already presented or applied.

## Verify before shortlist

- Open the official apply URL; confirm remote/geo, pay, degree walls, travel, and whether Apply is live.
- Aggregator-only or 404/filled postings are near-misses, not keepers.

## Score (final once)

Assign match % and hire % only after reading the JD.
- Match: how well their real history maps to the work.
- Hire: odds they clear screens (degree, tooling, title inflation, domain walls).
Default bars unless the owner set different ones: match ≥50 and hire ≥35.

## Present

For each keeper, always show:
1. Title — Company
2. Pay (posted or clearly labeled estimate)
3. Match % and hire %
4. Remote / location note
5. One plain-language why + red flags
6. Official apply URL

Honest zero keepers is better than padding.

## Materials and apply

- Draft resume/cover from facts only — never invent tools, degrees, or employers.
- Show drafts for approval.
- Apply or email only after an explicit yes for that specific action.

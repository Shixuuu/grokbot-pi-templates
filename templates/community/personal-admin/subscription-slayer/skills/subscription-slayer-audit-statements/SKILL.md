# Audit statements
1. Find charges that repeat on a cycle (weekly, monthly, yearly) or look like subscriptions by merchant name.
2. Output: total monthly and yearly cost, then a table of service, amount, cycle, yearly cost, and a flag (price hike, duplicate, likely unused, trial converted).
3. Top three savings ideas with estimated yearly savings.
4. Update the ledger.

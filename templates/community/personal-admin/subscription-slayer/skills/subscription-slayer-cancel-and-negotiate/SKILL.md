# Cancel or negotiate
- Give the likely cancel path (account settings, phone, email) based on public help pages, noting it may change.
- Draft a short cancel email or chat message, and a phone script for retention offers: ask for the lowest plan or a loyalty rate, and have a walk-away line.
- Remind the owner to confirm cancellation in writing and screenshot it. Mark the ledger 'cancel planned' until they confirm.

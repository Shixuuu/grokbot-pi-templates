# Unstick: wellbeing check

Use when the chart lands on **WELLBEING** (sensory/emotional regulation; mindfulness “still don’t know”; break-down “still too hard”). Motivation “worried I’ll fail” does **not** go here — that goes to Step 4. Part of [Getting started with executive dysfunction](sand-workflow:getting-started-with-executive-dysfunction).

**Do not invent chart branches.** Dig in to name the need / one partial meet if useful. **Beeminder fix:** `Needs are met!` and both `Need can't be met` go to **DIY DOPAMINE**, not straight to Step 4.

## WELLBEING

**Are your physical & emotional needs met right now?**

| They say | Return |
|---|---|
| **Needs are met!** | **DIY DOPAMINE** *[Beeminder fix]* → make-tolerable leaf at DIY only |
| **No, I need something. It's...** | Emotional vs physical below |

**EMOTIONAL:** social interaction, managing shame, fear, anxiety, etc  
**PHYSICAL:** meds, food, rest sensory, water, movement, etc  

**Can the need be met right now, even partially?**

| Outcome | Return |
|---|---|
| **Yes!** (emotional) | “Use DBT skills — fit the facts, opposite action, loving kindness. Go to Step 2.” |
| **Yes!** (physical) | “Attend to the need. Go to Step 2” |
| **Need can't be met** (emotional or physical) | **DIY DOPAMINE** *[Beeminder fix]* → make-tolerable leaf at DIY only |

## Stop

Return the chart outcome — don’t skip Beeminder’s DIY routing.

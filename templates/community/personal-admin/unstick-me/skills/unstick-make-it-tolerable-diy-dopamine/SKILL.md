# Unstick: make it tolerable + DIY dopamine

Part of [Getting started with executive dysfunction](sand-workflow:getting-started-with-executive-dysfunction).

**Entry (required — don’t skip):**
- **MAKE IT MORE TOLERABLE first** — motivation path (“unpleasant or arduous task”).
- **DIY DOPAMINE only** — after tolerable failed; OR Beeminder WELLBEING exits (`Needs are met!` / `Need can't be met`); OR inertia “unstuck but still need help starting.”

**Do not invent chart branches.** Dig in on one tip if useful, then take the labeled outcome back.

## MAKE IT MORE TOLERABLE

(Skip this whole section if entry is DIY-only.)

Offer **2–4** of:

- Listen to music or an audiobook
- Accommodate the task to make it less bad
- Pretend to be excited about it, find the fun
- Decide on a reward for after
- Change of scenery
- Wear the “Scientist Hat” — they do this task!
- Body double with a friend

| Outcome | Return |
|---|---|
| **One of those worked!** | Exit: **Hell yeah, get started!** |
| **This didn't help** | Continue to **DIY DOPAMINE** below |

## DIY DOPAMINE

Offer **2–4** of:

- Dance break with fun song
- Delicious snack
- Exercise: 60s jumping jacks
- Stretch
- Novelty (position, environs)

| Outcome | Return |
|---|---|
| **I can start the task now!** | Exit: **Hell yeah, get started!** |
| **It isn't helping** | Step 4 (`other-reasons`) |

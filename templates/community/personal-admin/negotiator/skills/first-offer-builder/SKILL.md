---
name: first-offer-builder
description: "Use this when someone must make the first offer, or must answer one already on the table."
---

Default: they open. Anchors work on experts. Precise beats round. Attach a reason. Aim just this side of crazy for that room, enough to draw a counter, not a walk.

1. Confirm they know their own reservation and aspiration as packages. If not, run the deal-prep map first.
2. Test wait-conditions. Wait if they hold private value they must not leak; if they honestly think the other side values them above their number (common in salary); or if the other’s reservation could be wildly high or low and they cannot tell.
3. If they wait: script how they invite the other side to number first. One question. No apology. No “I don’t know what to ask.”
4. If they open: start from aspiration, not the midpoint, not BATNA.
5. Make the number precise, not round.
6. Attach one objective reason (comp, market, scope, risk), not a feeling.
7. Package at least one other issue so it is not a single-issue fight.
8. Check the open is extreme enough for their culture. Recalibrate if this is a room that never haggles, or a room that haggles everything.
9. Pre-write the counter they hope to hear, and the packaged reply to it.
10. If the other side snap-accepts: they under-asked. Do not celebrate. Do not yank a closed price if they need the relationship. List one add-on or a later post-settlement pass.
11. If the other side walks from a crazy open: no unmatched chase concession. That proves they will not walk. Reset with a new messenger, a packaged counter, or an actual walk.
12. If they already opened too soft: stop bleeding. Gather ranks. Switch to packages. Do not keep conceding on the same number.
13. Give them one script, then stop.

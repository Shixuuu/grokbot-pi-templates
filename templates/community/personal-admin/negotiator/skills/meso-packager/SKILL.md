---
name: meso-packager
description: "Use this when a deal has multiple issues, or the other side treats every point as life-or-death."
---

Two or three packages that are equal to the user and different to the other side. The other side ranks; you learn what they value; you do not split every issue.

1. Confirm reservation and aspiration as packages. If missing, map first.
2. Build three packages that score equally for the user, all above reservation, aimed at aspiration.
3. Vary the issues the other side might weight differently (timing vs cash, risk vs status, scope vs duration).
4. Keep congruent issues available as gifts or as trades, not as announcements.
5. Ban cherry-picking across packages. Say so when offering: rank these, do not mix parts.
6. Ask them to rank, not to pick a winner on the spot.
7. Infer ranks from the order. Update the map. Do not explain your point system.
8. Offer a revised trio once if the ranks taught you something. Then close or walk.
9. Do not use MESOs to hide that you have no BATNA. Weak party: MESOs still help, but alternatives and added issues do more.
10. Cost: a sharp counterpart can reverse-engineer your tradeoffs. Use when they look unprepared, or when every issue feels like death and you need a way out of single-issue combat.

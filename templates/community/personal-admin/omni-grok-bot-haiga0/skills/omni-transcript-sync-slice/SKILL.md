# Journal paste-in

Use when the member wants you to read a journal entry, a chat excerpt, or a transcript they choose to paste or upload.

1. Read only what they hand you. Never go looking for more of their history, inboxes or accounts.
2. Confirm in one line what you received (date if given, rough length), then give a short summary: what happened, what they seemed to feel, and any promise or next step in it.
3. Ask if they want it scored. Only if they say yes, run the daily scores on that excerpt and show the strip.
4. Ask before saving anything. If they say yes, save a dated one-paragraph log memory. Don't keep the raw text unless they ask.

Privacy wall: if the excerpt mentions other people, keep their details out of memory and out of any draft. Never email, post or share it. Tool not throne.

# Article drafter

Use when the member wants a long-form article (for example, an X Article or a blog post) drafted for their review.

1. **Plan.** Agree on the title, who it's for, and 3–6 section headings.
2. **Write.** Use real headings, short paragraphs and lists: human-readable, not a pasted outline. Include only real, checked links.
3. **Images.** Suggest or generate a banner and 2–3 body images that show what the article is actually about. For each image, write:
   - a short caption, and
   - an image description (ALT text) that describes what's in the image and ends with a provenance line, e.g. "Image made with Grok for <member's name or site>; draft reviewed by a person."
   Place the banner as the first inline image too, since some editors don't allow ALT text on the cover.
4. **Fresh-eyes check.** Run the same checks as the post and reply drafter (intent, stranger-readable, true and kind, no private details, image on-topic) and report Pass or Fail.
5. **Close.** End with the member's own sign-off and any link they choose.
6. **Hand-off.** Deliver the article plus a table of images with captions and ALT text. The member publishes. Never publish for them.

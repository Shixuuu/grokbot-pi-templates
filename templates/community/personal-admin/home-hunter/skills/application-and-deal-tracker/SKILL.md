# Application and deal tracker

## When they apply
1. Save the building/unit, rent, co-applicants, portal, and open tasks (income verify, pet screening, deposit) in profile memory as PRIMARY.
2. Pause tour pushes: keep quiet scans if useful, but do not suggest new tours unless they ask.
3. Flag their coordinator only if the unit disappears or status clearly changes.
4. Offer to pause the daily listing routine entirely until they wake you.

## Status updates
Track Approve / Deny / Approve-with-conditions / docs still open. Surface conditions plainly (e.g. extra deposit for rental history) without nagging.

## Deal / deadline radar
Watch apply-by promos, free months, and deposit specials on buildings they care about. Surface only when the deadline is hours away or they asked for backups. Never apply for them.

## Resume or end
- Rejected or they reopen the hunt: resume daily recs and clear PRIMARY.
- Signed a lease: delete the daily routine, clear the hunt, offer a one-time move-in checklist (utilities, renter's insurance, movers, parking, keys).

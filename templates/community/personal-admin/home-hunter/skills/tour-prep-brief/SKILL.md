# Tour prep brief

For one listing (or a short same-day slate), send a compact brief:

1. **Fit** — how it matches hard filters and soft preferences, including commute vs hard max and soft target.
2. **Commute** — estimated door-to-door minutes and mode to each saved destination; note peak vs off-peak if that mattered in onboarding; flag if the estimate could not be verified.
3. **All-in cost** — rent + known fees, parking, utilities, concessions; monthly and move-in cash if estimable.
4. **Ask on site** — lease length, concession fine print, package/mail, laundry, noise, HVAC, pest history, broker fee, application timeline.
5. **Red flags** — bait pricing, 1bed+den sold as 2/2, stale third-party pages vs official inventory, long walk vs claimed transit, commute over the hard max.
6. **Compare** — one line vs the current shortlist leaders on rent, size, and commute.

Do not contact the building. Do not email or apply. Keep it short enough to read on the way over.

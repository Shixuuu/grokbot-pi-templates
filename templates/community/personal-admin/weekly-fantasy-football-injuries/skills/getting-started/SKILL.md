---
name: getting-started
description: "Use this on the first conversation with a new owner to set up the weekly fantasy football injury list."
---

Say hello and explain in one line: every Tuesday I send a simple list of the previous week's NFL injuries, limited to players drafted in most fantasy leagues, and remind you to review it.

Ask one question at a time:
1. What day and roughly what time would you like the list? (Default: Tuesday morning.)
2. Do you want the list limited to QB, RB, WR and TE, or to include kickers and defenses too? (Default: QB, RB, WR, TE.)
3. Which fantasy platform do you use, if any (ESPN, Yahoo, Sleeper, other)? No connection is needed.
4. How would you like the list formatted: short one-line entries by position, or a plain alphabetical list? (Default: by position.)

Then save the answers as memories, create a weekly routine at their chosen day and time that researches last week's NFL injuries from current sources, lists widely drafted players with injury and status or timeline, sends the list, and reminds them to look it over before waiver and lineup moves. Offer to send this week's list right away, and do it if they say yes.

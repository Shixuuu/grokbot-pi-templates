# Weekly family dinner plan

## Goal
Produce one kids-first weekday dinner plan for a household, with live recipe links, then hand a Communications teammate the email. Learn prefs from the household cook’s replies — never invent tastes.

## Before each week
Confirm standing shape and hard nos from memory (or ask once if empty):
- Which nights are home cook vs restaurant vs off-plan
- Weeknight gear and where they shop
- Hard nos (proteins, spices, allergens)
- How grocery proposals work (propose only vs write a shared list)

## Default shape (replace with learned prefs)
- Home dinners midweek on the cook’s gear
- One fast / low-effort night if they want it
- One restaurant night if they want it
- Weekends off the emailed plan unless they ask otherwise

## Planning rules
- Same meal for everyone; kids may plate it their way
- Fresh research each week with live recipe title + URL
- Review the full weekday menu as one unit before locking
- No consecutive nights of the same format/protein/base
- No two similar “hero” dishes in the same week even on non-adjacent nights
- Include at most one stretch / slightly new recipe when they want variety

## Research
1. Prefer printing-press / recipe CLIs before a browser
2. Confirm the page is live
3. Reject anything that fails hard nos or gear limits

## Email
- Opinionated: pick sides and plating; don’t hedge
- State the plan only (no “we’re skipping X” narration)
- Recipe links for cook nights; no shopping-list dump as the body
- Close with a grocery proposal only if that is the standing ask
- Hand Communications the send (to = household cook, cc = household lead when set)
- Never send email yourself; report to Chief of Staff after Communications has it
- If no reply: stay silent

## After a reply
- Short ack + locked week list
- Apply their changes promptly (source of truth)
- Groceries stay proposal-only until explicit yes
- Update memory + handoff; report to Chief of Staff

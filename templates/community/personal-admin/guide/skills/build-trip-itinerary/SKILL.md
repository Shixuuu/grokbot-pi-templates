Use when bookings (from email or paste) are known and the owner wants a day-by-day plan.

## Steps

1. Lock facts: dates, destination, flights, hotel base, party, vibe.
2. Research destination lightly (hours, neighborhoods, transit) — cite public sources; say unknown.
3. Build day-by-day: arrival buffer, 2–3 anchors/day, meals near hotel, one flexible block.
4. Deliver overview + bookings summary + daily plan + ops tips (transit cards, tipping norms at high level only).
5. Offer calendar draft; create events only on explicit yes.

## Refuse

Invented bookings, auto-purchases, visa/legal advice as fact.

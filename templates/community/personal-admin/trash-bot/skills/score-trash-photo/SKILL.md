# Score a Trash League photo

Use this when scoring a photo of picked-up litter for the Trash League.

## Philosophy (locked)

**Volume counts equal to size.** Still score big items (box springs, furniture) — we want those gone — and reward dense multi-item grass/lot cleanups generously. Do not treat bulky furniture as automatically topping the board over real volume.

## Pickup rule

Order is pick it up, text a picture, then throw it in the trash. Photo first: you cannot score something already in the dumpster. After the photo they still have to put it in the trash. A documentary shot of trash they walked past and left sitting there is not a pickup. Score it **0** and say so. Hazardous is the exception: photo from a safe distance, then tell the organizer. Do not require the photo to show the can.

## Photo rule

Do not require one item per photo. Identify every item actually visible in the shot. Apply the rubric to each, then **sum the points**. Then add a grossness bump if the photo shows it. For high-volume grass/lot pickups, sum fully and be generous on borderline scraps — volume is the point.

## Duplicate check (required)

Before awarding points, compare the photo to other submissions already scored **this week**.

- Two similar finds are fine (two different cans, two pieces of paper).
- The same physical item is not (same distinctive object, same scene, near-identical shot, same unique trash).
- If it looks like a duplicate: do **not** add points. Flag it to the organizer with both photos and who sent them. Wait for KEEP or TOSS.

## Rubric (locked)

- Small litter (butts, wrappers, caps): **1**
- Can, bottle, or cup: **3**
- Fast food bag / takeout: **5**
- Empty box / cardboard (cereal, pizza, shipping box): **5**
- Household dump (bags of house trash left in a hall or on the grounds, not one empty box): **15**
- Furniture, mattress, or appliance: **20**
- Hazardous, photographed from a safe distance: **25**
- Tire: **35**
- Dog mess: **40** (stack every pile visible)
- Full illegal dump pile: **50**

An empty cereal box is a box (5), not a household dump. Turnover junk from a unit being flipped does not count.

## Grossness (locked)

Add this **on top of** the item score. Judge from the photo, not from a story.

- Ordinary / dry / just trash: **+0**
- Gross (wet food, sticky, mold, soiled): **+5**
- Vile (rotting, maggots, mystery sludge, bio-mess that is not already scored as dog mess): **+10**

Dog mess is already 40. Do **not** add +5/+10 on a normal dog pile. Only add vile +10 if it is clearly worse than a typical dog mess.

## Scoop of the Week

Anyone who scores at least one dog-mess pile that week earns a **$10 Scoop of the Week** bump on Friday. It stacks with podium $20/$15/$10.

## Record the result

1. Look at the photo.
2. If it is clearly still sitting there and nobody picked it up, score 0.
3. Check this week's other photos for a duplicate.
4. Identify each visible item.
5. Apply the rubric, add grossness if earned, sum points.
6. Tell the submitter the points and a short reason.

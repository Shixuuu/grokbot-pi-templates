# Score a trash-league pickup

Use this when an employee submits a photo of trash they picked up on apartment grounds, or when you need to add points and refresh the weekly leaderboard.

## Philosophy (locked)

**Volume counts equal to size.** Keep scoring box springs and furniture — those should get picked up — and reward dense multi-item grass/lot cleanups generously. Furniture is 20 so it cannot leapfrog massive trash pickups by bulk alone.

## Scoring (do not invent new categories without the organizer)

| Find | Points |
|---|---|
| Small litter (butts, wrappers, caps) | 1 |
| Can, bottle, cup | 3 |
| Fast food bag / takeout | 5 |
| Empty box / cardboard (cereal, pizza, shipping) | 5 |
| Household dump (bags of house trash in a hall or on the grounds; not one empty box) | 15 |
| Furniture, mattress, appliance | 20 |
| Hazardous, photographed from a safe distance (needles, chemicals) | 25 |
| Tire | 35 |
| Dog mess (each pile; stack all visible) | 40 |
| Full illegal dump pile | 50 |

### Grossness bump (on top of the item)

| How it looks | Extra |
|---|---|
| Ordinary / dry | +0 |
| Gross (wet food, sticky, mold, soiled) | +5 |
| Vile (rotting, maggots, sludge, bio-mess that is not already dog mess) | +10 |

Dog mess already prices in at 40. Do not stack +5/+10 on a normal dog pile unless it is clearly worse than typical (vile +10 only).

### Scoop of the Week

Anyone who scores dog mess that week gets **$10** on Friday. Stacks with podium $20/$15/$10.

## How to process a submission

1. Identify the employee from the sender's phone number. Unknown sender: hold and ask.
2. Score the photo using the table plus grossness. Write a one-line verdict (item + points).
3. Append a record to the league state file. Include: player, points, item, note, timestamp, photo.
4. Refresh the public TV board after scoring with the league publish script (here.now).
5. Text the organizer the score and who is in the lead.

## Week

Week runs Friday morning through Thursday night America/New_York. Recap and reset are Friday 8:00 AM. Do not carry points across weeks.

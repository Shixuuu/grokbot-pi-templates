Use before marking an ASE task, job, or gap as blocked on missing shop hardware/capability.

# Shop capability checklist

1. Maintain/read a standing checklist in the Lab Builder vault (create `Shop-Lab-Capability-Checklist.md` on the vault machine if missing) covering gear that often gates tasks: ADAS targets/calibration, HV/xEV PPE & meters, EC suspension, electric booster, lifts, scan tools, etc.
2. Before parking work as hardware-blocked: check the list. If the shop has the capability, do not park — plan the attach/lab.
3. If truly missing: record the gap on the checklist (item, ASE code, date, who confirmed) and only then mark blocked.
4. Anti-jobs: do not invent shop inventory; if unknown, ask the program lead one yes/no on that capability rather than assuming missing.
5. When the program lead corrects a capability mid-wave, update the checklist the same day.

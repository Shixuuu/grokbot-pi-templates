Use before suggesting, drafting, or applying a new or relocated job number in any Lab Builder pack JSON on the user's Lab Builder vault machine.

# Job insert placement

1. ListMachines and require the named vault machine. Read the live pack file(s) — never invent job order from memory or chat.
2. List existing job numbers in pack order. Decide insert-after-N (or end) from the pedagogical/ASE home, not from a guessed free number.
3. If inserting in the middle: assign the new job N+1 and renumber every trailing job upward (old N+1 → N+2, …). Update all internal references (module drafts, ASE maps, cross-links) that cite renumbered jobs.
4. Never target an orphan high number (e.g. Job 68) when the live pack’s next slot is contiguous (e.g. after 59 → Job 60).
5. Deliver: insert-after, new job #, renumber map (old→new), and exact pack paths. The coordinator and ASE specialist must see the renumber map before apply.
6. Anti-jobs: do not apply pack edits in this skill unless the coordinator already authorized apply; do not skip the live read.

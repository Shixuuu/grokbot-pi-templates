# Getting started

Say in one line: "I keep your X account clean and growing: I unfollow non-followers, clear out bots, follow the right people in your niche, and can run a cleanup on autopilot."

Ask these one at a time, waiting for each answer:
1. What's your X handle? Then have them sign into X in your browser (hand them the desktop for the login; never ask for the password in chat). Confirm the handle once signed in.
2. What topics do you post about? (Used for follow-backs and niche growth.)
3. Which accounts should I never unfollow or remove? (Friends, brands, news. They can add to this any time.)
4. Want a cleanup on autopilot? If yes, ask which days and time (suggest Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday at 6am their time) and what to include: removing unverified and bot followers, and unfollowing non-followers except the keep list.

Then:
- Save the handle, topics, and keep list as memories.
- If they said yes to autopilot, create a routine at that schedule that runs the X account cleanup skill in small batches and messages them a report with their verified-follower percentage. It must stop and alert them on any X warning.
- Offer a first run: a read-only scan showing how many non-followers, unverified followers, likely bots, and dead accounts they have, with nothing changed until they say go.

Remind them once: go slow. X flags accounts that follow or unfollow in big bursts.

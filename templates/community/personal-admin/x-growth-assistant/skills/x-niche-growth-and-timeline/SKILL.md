# X niche growth and timeline

Work in the bot's own browser, signed into the owner's X account. Confirm the signed-in handle first. Use the owner's saved topic(s) and keep list from memory.

## Safety rules
- Follow at most about 20-25 accounts per session, with 15-20 second pauses between follows.
- Stop immediately and tell the owner on any captcha, lock, rate-limit, or "automated behavior" warning.
- Never like, repost, reply, or DM as part of these jobs unless the owner asks.

## Jobs
1. **Follow back the right people.** Check the newest followers. Follow back verified accounts that post about the owner's topic. Skip promo, giveaway, and follow-for-follow spam. Report who was followed.
2. **Grow in the niche.** Find about 20 active, verified accounts that post about the owner's topic and posted in the last week. Follow them and give a one-line note on each (who they are and why they fit).
3. **Clean the timeline.** Add muted words the owner approves. A good starter set: airdrop, giveaway, DM me, 100x, presale. Then show the full muted list.
4. **Organize with Lists.** Sort followed accounts into private X Lists by topic. Default lists: News, Crypto, Friends, Creators, or the owner's own categories. Report how many went into each.

End with a short report of what changed.

Use when Appeal Desk is drafting the formal internal appeal the patient files.

## Artifact

Draft with placeholders for every sensitive field. Include:

- The denial reason as the letter stated it
- The specific counter: clerical correction, evidence of prior auth, corrected place of service, or the prescriber's letter of medical necessity attached (you do not write that letter)
- Any missing required disclosure (reason, deadline, or how to file) named as a fact about the notice
- The deadline as a date taken from their letter
- Where to send it and what to enclose, only if you just read both

## Never

Never argue that the care was medically necessary. Never predict the outcome. Never submit. Hand off: "This is ready for you to complete and send. I won't send it."

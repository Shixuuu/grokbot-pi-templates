Use on Appeal Desk first-run after you know a denial exists. Ask plan type directly. Do not infer it from the payer name. The same insurer administers commercial, ACA, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, and self-funded plans.

## Ask which of

- Commercial / employer
- ACA marketplace
- Medicare Advantage
- Medicaid managed care
- Self-funded employer plan

## Why it matters

This decides every deadline and every downstream route (SHIP vs navigator vs state process vs federal external review). If they do not know, put "which plan type is this?" on the handoff sheet and do not pick one.

## Medicare Advantage is not 180 days

If they say Medicare Advantage, do not apply a commercial or ACA internal-appeal window (often stated as 180 days). MA is the short window.

Do state the published MA reconsideration window as a general rule, live-cited, labeled as general — not their date. Live-cite CMS or Medicare.gov. One required line after the figure: this window is much shorter than commercial plans. Act now.

Rule 1: their own letter governs their actual date. If the source page will not load, say you don't know rather than invent or suppress the published rule.

Medicaid is also not the commercial window. Same rule: state the published general window with a live cite, labeled as general; letter still governs their date.

Point them at SHIP for Medicare Advantage.

## Output

One labeled plan type, or unknown. No deadline numbers from memory. General windows must be live-cited and labeled as general, not their deadline. Never apply 180 days to Medicare Advantage. Never refuse to name a published general window just because you don't have their letter. For MA, always add: this window is much shorter than commercial plans. Act now.

Use when Appeal Desk has, or is waiting on, the patient's denial letter or EOB.

## Extract from the letter (do not invent)

- Stated reason and any denial/reason code
- Plan or payer name as printed
- The deadline the letter itself gives (this governs)
- The service or drug denied
- Whether the letter names all three required elements: reason, deadline, how to file

A missing required element is leverage. Name it for the appeal letter. Do not cite a regulation number you did not just read.

## If they do not have the letter

Stop the rest of first-run. Tell them the letter is the source of the deadline. Draft a short request to the plan or the prescriber's office for a copy. Placeholders only. You do not send it.

## Rules

Never reconstruct a deadline from a general rule while the letter is missing. State what you could not read. Never repeat claim numbers. Verify any general filing-window figures live; the letter still wins.

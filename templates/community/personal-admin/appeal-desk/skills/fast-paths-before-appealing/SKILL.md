Use when Appeal Desk has a plan type and a denial that might resolve without a formal appeal: peer-to-peer review or informal reconsideration.

## What they are

Peer-to-peer: prescriber talks to the insurer's medical reviewer. Often same-day. Not a formal appeal. Does not consume the formal appeal window.
Informal reconsideration: some plans offer it. Verify that this payer still does, live.

## What to do

Draft the request to the prescriber's office asking them to request a P2P. Placeholders. You do not call the plan. Verify this payer's P2P and reconsideration windows live. Do not recite another payer's numbers.

## Rules

Never say P2P will work. Never file an appeal for them. If delay is harming the patient, also open External review for the urgent dual-file branch.

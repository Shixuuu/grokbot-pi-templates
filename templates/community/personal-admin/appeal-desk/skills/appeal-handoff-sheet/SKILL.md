Use when Appeal Desk needs a one-page sheet for the prescriber's office, SHIP, an ACA navigator, or the state consumer assistance program.

## Contents, in this order

1. What was denied, and roughly when the letter is dated. No diagnosis essay.
2. Plan type as the patient stated it.
3. Stated denial reason, quoted.
4. Which kind of problem (clerical / PA / network / medical necessity) or unknown.
5. What the office or counselor needs to supply: P2P call, letter of medical necessity, corrected code, prior auth paperwork.
6. Deadline from the letter, or "letter did not state a deadline."
7. Questions the patient reads aloud.

Also name free help, verified live: SHIP for Medicare, marketplace navigator for ACA, state consumer assistance, state insurance department. Most patients do not know these exist. Say so.

## Rules

No sensitive identifiers. No outcome prediction. No clinical argument. Never reconstruct a phone or URL you could not read.

---

Use when Appeal Desk has a stated denial reason from the letter. Pick which of four things went wrong. The fix is different for each.

## Four kinds

1. Clerical or coding error — patient or billing office can often fix.
2. Missing or mishandled prior authorization — office documentation, not a medical-necessity essay.
3. Network or place-of-service issue — in vs out of network, site of care.
4. Medical necessity disagreement — only this one needs the prescriber to write the argument.

If you cannot tell, say so and list the four as questions on the handoff sheet. Do not guess. Getting this wrong sends them down the slowest path for a clerical problem.

## Output

One kind, or unknown, tied to quoted language from the letter. No prediction that it will overturn. If kind 4, go to Fast paths / handoff, not to a clinical argument you write.

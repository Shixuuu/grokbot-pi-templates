Use when Appeal Desk's patient has a final internal denial, or when first-run (or later) says care has stopped or is delaying. Urgent cases file external review at the same time as the internal appeal. Dual-file. Do not wait for the internal decision.

## What it is

An independent reviewer. The decision binds the plan. This is the underused right. Do not call it a court. Do not say they will win.

## What to establish (verify live, letter still governs the deadline)

- Which track: state process, HHS-administered federal process, or a plan-contracted IRO
- Contact information from the EOB or the final internal denial letter, not from memory
- Deadline from the letter
- If care is stopped or delaying: treat as urgent dual-file now. Do not wait.

If there is no letter, still state the published general federal filing window with a live cite, labeled as general — not their date. Healthcare.gov is the usual source. Point them at the HHS CCIIO state list by linking the page you just opened; do not reconstruct a phone number. If the pages will not load, say you don't know and what you could not read. Do not give up without trying the pages.

If sources conflict on decision timelines, say they conflict and refuse to pick a number you did not read on a current page.

## Never

Never file the external review. Never reconstruct a state phone number. HHS CCIIO state list only if you just opened it.

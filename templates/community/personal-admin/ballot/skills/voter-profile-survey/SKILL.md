# Voter profile survey

Build a voter's issue profile so later ballot analysis can flag choices that cut against their stated views. Stay nonpartisan: describe, don't persuade.

## Steps
1. Ask one question at a time as a multiple-choice widget. Offer a range of options from more government to less, plus "Not sure / mixed", and allow a typed answer. Use neutral wording with no loaded terms.
2. Cover these topics in order: the economy and the role of government, healthcare, immigration, public safety and crime, housing costs, education, climate and energy, guns, abortion (skipping is fine), and bonds and local tax measures.
3. End with a priorities question: "When issues pull in different directions, which matter most? Pick up to three." Make it multi-select.
4. Save each answer to memory as a private log fact right after they give it. Never share it in shared user memory or with other bots.
5. Once you have every answer, save a one-paragraph profile summary (profile tier) and send the voter a short, plain recap: their overall lean, where they break from it, their top priorities, and where tradeoffs are likely to come up.
6. Ask for their sample ballot as a photo, PDF, or link.

## Ballot analysis afterward
For each race and measure, explain what it actually does, who supports and opposes it, and what a yes or no outcome would lead to, using official sources like the state voter guide and the legislative analyst. Then note how it lines up with the voter's profile, weighting their top priorities. Call out real tradeoffs plainly. The voter makes the final call.

## Multiple people
Keep a separate profile for each person, such as a spouse. Never mix their answers or reveal one person's answers to the other unless they ask.

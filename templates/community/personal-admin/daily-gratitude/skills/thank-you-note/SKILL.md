# Thank-you note

1. Find out who it's for, what they did, and the relationship (friend, family, colleague, client). Check memory first; ask only for what's missing, one question at a time.
2. Ask how it'll be sent (text, email, card) only if it changes the length or tone.
3. Draft a short note in the owner's voice: name the specific thing, say why it mattered, close warmly. No clichés, no over-the-top praise. Text: 1-3 sentences. Email or card: a short paragraph.
4. Show the draft in chat and offer one tweak (warmer, shorter, more formal).
5. Never send it yourself or contact the recipient. The owner copies and sends it.
6. Once they say it's sent, log "Thanked <relationship> for <what> on YYYY-MM-DD" to memory.

# Decision brief

Use this whenever you are giving advice, a recommendation, a go/no-go, or a tune-up. Do not interview. Do not dump a menu of options unless they asked for options.

Write four short beats, in this order. Prose is fine; labels are required.

1. **Decision**: one sentence on what to do, or what not to do. Pick a call even if you are not 100% sure.
2. **Why**: the one or two facts that make that the call. No background lecture. No recap of the question.
3. **Risk**: the real failure if they take it, and the real failure if they skip it. Not a legal disclaimer.
4. **Next step**: the single next action, who does it, and whether it waits for them.

## Rules

- Uncertainty goes in Risk, not in Decision. A default plus a named risk beats "it depends."
- Do not apply an irreversible change until they say apply, unless they already asked you to do that exact thing.
- If several things could change, decide the one that matters now. Park the rest in Next step as an optional follow-on.
- Skip filler, preambles, and "happy to help."

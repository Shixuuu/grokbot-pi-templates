# Human Voice

Use on every drafting or editing task for the lawyer (letters, memos, email, posts) — strips AI tells and writes in the lawyer’s voice.

## Structural rules
Vary sentence length deliberately. Vary paragraph length. Land conclusions short. Answer first. No closing summary. Break the rule of three. Do not hedge symmetrically. Repeat the term, do not vary it. Be concrete. Vary paragraph openings. Earn the transition. No rhetorical scaffolding. Kill adverbial throat-clearing.

## Kill list
Delete or replace: delve, tapestry, landscape (figurative), navigate (figurative), leverage (as a verb), robust, holistic, nuanced, multifaceted, myriad, plethora, seamless, underscore, highlight (figurative), foster, unlock, elevate, utilize, facilitate, testament to, key takeaway, deep dive, game changer, at the end of the day, in today’s world, it is important to note, dive into, unpack, resonate, journey (figurative).

Never: emojis, "Great question," bold applied to whole sentences for emphasis, headers on a short answer.

## Self-audit
Delete the first sentence if it restates the prompt. Delete the last paragraph if it restates the body. Break runs of similar-length sentences. Confirm lists are honest. Cut hedges. Check terms of art for synonym drift.

# Routine: Sleeper clear+1m execute

> Fires one minute after Sleeper waivers clear so locked claims and free-agent adds land before anyone else.

Timing: one minute after Sleeper's weekly waiver clear, set in the league's timezone (a Tuesday-after-games clear processes just after midnight Pacific going into Wednesday, so this fires about a minute after that).

Execute for each Sleeper league the owner named.

1. Message the Sleeper host IC with the locked claim cards from Tuesday prep. Ask the research IC only if no cards were locked.
2. The host IC works on its own computer and executes free-agent adds the minute the clear lands, in card order.
3. Fill empty K/DEF slots with free agents only. No waiver claims on K/DEF in the regular season.
4. Never drop without the owner's yes, except the locked-card standing rule. Keeper and dynasty rosters keep dynasty logic.
5. Verify team identity, then tell the owner what landed, what failed, and what is still open. Stay brief.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Los_Angeles 5 0 * * 3`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

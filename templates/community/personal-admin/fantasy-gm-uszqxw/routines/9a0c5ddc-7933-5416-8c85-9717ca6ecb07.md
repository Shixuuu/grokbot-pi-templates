# Routine: Sunday lineup set

> Fires about an hour before the first Sunday kickoff so every lineup is final before games start.

Timing: about an hour before the first Sunday NFL kickoff (1:00pm ET) in the owner's timezone.

1. Ask the research IC for this week's start/sit calls plus GTD and injury flags, one short item per league.
2. Make the start/sit calls yourself; do not wait on the owner. Message each host IC (each on its own computer) to set or confirm every league's lineup.
3. Injury pass: flip overnight OUT or Doubtful starters, promote rostered handcuffs, and move IR-eligible players into empty IR slots. Suggest drops only. Never-drop players are sit or IR only.
4. Lock contingencies for afternoon and SNF game-time decisions so the inactives routines can execute without re-asking.
5. Digest to the owner: per league starters, IR or injury moves, and any call they should know about. Run even if they set lineups themselves.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=Pacific/Honolulu 52 5 * * 0`

# Routine: CCNP factoids (2x daily)

> Twice daily at 10:00 and 15:00 — one short ENCOR/ENARSI factoid on an ambiguous syllabus trap with a memory tip.

Deliver one brief, memorable CCNP study factoid (alternate between ENCOR 350-401 and ENARSI 300-410). Ground it in the current official exam syllabus topics. Prefer ambiguous / commonly confused points that candidates discuss after sitting exams on forums like Reddit (trap answers, lookalike features, default-vs-exception behavior) — teach the concept and the trap, never paste or reconstruct full proprietary exam questions verbatim. Structure each delivery as: (1) a short lead naming ENCOR or ENARSI and the topic, (2) the ambiguity / what people often get wrong, (3) the correct learning point, (4) one sharp memory tip. Keep it tight — a few short sentences, not a lecture. This routine fires twice a day (10:00 and 15:00); morning and afternoon each get their own factoid; do not skip afternoon because morning already ran. Stay in study-coach voice — no routine talk, no schedule readback. If you already sent a factoid for this same time slot today, stay quiet. Quietly note the topic in memory so you rotate domains and avoid recent repeats.

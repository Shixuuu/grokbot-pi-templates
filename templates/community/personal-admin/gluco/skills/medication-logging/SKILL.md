Log any medication the owner reports — name, dose, and time. Neutral and generic: never assume a drug list, never invent doses, and never give medical advice about whether to take, skip, or change a medication.

## When to use
Use when they say they took a med, ask to log medication, or mention a drug name with a dose or time.

## Log a medication
1. Capture name, dose (amount + unit if given), and time (default to now if they say "just took").
2. Optionally ask once whether they want a short note that it may affect glucose (e.g. metformin, insulin, steroids). Only if relevant or they bring it up — do not lecture.
3. Save to memory with date, local time, name, dose, and optional glucose-related note.
4. Confirm in one line. Offer today's summary if useful.

## Rules
- Accept OTC, prescriptions, supplements — whatever they name.
- If dose or time is missing and needed for a clear log, ask once.
- Do not diagnose interactions or recommend changes. If they ask medical questions, remind them to check with their clinician.
- Coaching and logging only — not a doctor.

## In summaries
When building a daily summary or trend, list medications taken that day alongside glucose readings and meals.

Use when the owner asks about coverage, deductibles, HSA/FSA, enrollment, claims, or “is this covered?”

## Steps

1. Confirm which plan (medical/dental/vision) and pull from their SPD/card notes — never invent.
2. Answer: covered Y/N/unknown, cost shape (copay/coinsurance/deductible), referral/prior-auth if stated.
3. Next action: portal click-path, HR email draft, or Find In-Network Care.
4. Last-4 only in memory. Draft sends wait for explicit yes.

# Routine: Sunday camera

> Sunday afternoon push to sit down with the camera.

Sunday afternoon, shortly before the weekly camera sit. Send a short, sharp push in chat: camera on, one take, no new idea, the office does not vote. If a content agent or calendar shows the slot, say whether it is still there. Do not be gentle. Do not write a strategy. Do not offer an alternative project. After messaging the user, tell the content agent the sitting is on. If it is past the sit window and there is no sign they sat, say so plainly and tell the content agent to escalate. Stay quiet only if they already said the sitting is done.

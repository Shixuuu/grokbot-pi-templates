# Memory seeds for Retail Fever

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-08-30): The user has a Shopify store.
- **profile** (2026-08-31): Apparel t-shirt designs should use full-front prints covering at least 65% of the 15×18 DTG print area.
- **profile** (2026-09-05): Seasonal apparel brands keep a written design system: palette, blank colorways, and full-front tee graphics (≥65% of the 15×18 DTG area) instead of small chest-centered prints.
- **profile** (2026-09-12): Store Privacy, Refund, and Terms pages should use the merchant's state and county as governing law and venue.
- **log** (2026-08-30): Prefer Printful (or a similar POD partner) as the Shopify fulfillment service for apparel dropshipping; connect the shop in the partner dashboard and keep the catalog synced.
- **log** (2026-08-31): Tee blank colors should match each print's palette rather than using the same extra colors on every tee.
- **log** (2026-09-05): Print arts for DTG must use transparent backgrounds; knock out baked-in cream/black/forest rectangles before mockups so graphics sit clean on the blank.
- **log** (2026-09-05): Typical apparel pricing tiers for this workflow: tee, sweatshirt, and hoodie as three clear price bands.
- **log** (2026-09-11): On product pages and collection cards, lead media with apparel mockups (tee/sweatshirt/hoodie), not the transparent print art files.
- **log** (2026-09-11): Dark type can wash out on black blanks — feature cream/rust/white blanks for those designs, or lighten the ink for dark blanks.
- **log** (2026-09-12): Storefront "sold out" with stock showing in Admin usually means inventory is only on the shop location; stock variants at the Printful fulfillment location and enable fulfillment (not ignored) on synced items.
- **log** (2026-09-12): Made-to-order refund convention: replace-first for confirmed print quality, wrong item, damage, or loss; default to store credit for change-of-mind or pre-print cancel when policy allows; refund to original payment when the customer asks or card-network/law requires it.
- **log** (2026-09-13): Slogan tee lettering works best with open-counter display type (shirt shows through O/A/D/R/P) and stacked caps rather than filled-counter script.
- **log** (2026-09-21): On Shopify Basic, Printful live carrier rates often do not land; every shipping zone needs real rate rows (flat rates or Shopify Shipping) or checkout returns no shipping methods.
- **log** (2026-09-26): This bot uses Printful's API alongside Shopify Admin for POD catalog sync, mockups, and fulfillment. Connect Printful separately; it is not packed as a marketplace plugin.
- **log** (2026-09-27): Proof from a live POD apparel launch (anonymized): fixed storefront sold-out caused by POD-location stock at zero while shop-location stock looked fine; empty Shopify shipping rate rows blocked checkout on Basic even when payments worked; mockup-first galleries replaced transparent-art cards across a ~50-product catalog; custom domain watched until DNS/HTTPS went live as primary.

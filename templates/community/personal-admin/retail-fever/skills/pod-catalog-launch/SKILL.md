# POD catalog launch

Launch or expand an apparel POD drop on Shopify. Keep brand-specific art and store IDs out of the recipe; use the owner's connected store and design system from memory.

## Defaults (override from memory)

- Full-front DTG prints covering at least 65% of the 15×18 print area (not small chest logos).
- Transparent print arts — knock out baked-in background rectangles before mockups.
- Blank colors match each print's palette; avoid dark type washing out on black blanks (prefer cream/rust/white or lighten ink).
- Product galleries lead with apparel mockups; keep transparent arts in Files, not as the card image.
- Clear price bands for tee / sweatshirt / hoodie.

## Steps

1. Confirm Shopify + POD partner (e.g. Printful) are connected and which store to touch.
2. Prepare transparent print-ready arts; enlarge for full-front coverage.
3. Create or update products per garment type with correct blanks and sizes.
4. Generate / attach mockups to every color×size variant; reorder media mockup-first.
5. Publish to Online Store; add to the featured / drop collection.
6. Ensure POD sync: fulfillment enabled (not ignored), stock at the fulfillment location.
7. Hand the owner admin + storefront links and offer buy-ready-audit next.

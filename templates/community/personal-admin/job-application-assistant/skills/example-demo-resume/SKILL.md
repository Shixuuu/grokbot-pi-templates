## Importer notes
- Replace every demo field with the owner's real facts before any application.
- Never invent credentials, employers, degrees, bar admissions, skills, or metrics the owner did not provide.
- Keep resumes single-column and machine-searchable (ATS). Also export a Quiet SpaceX PDF (tight typography, charcoal name and section headings with thin rules, generous whitespace, minimal chrome).
- Align job and education rows: title left with dates right; employer or school left with location right (CSS flex rows, not HTML tables).
- Prefer ASCII hyphens ( - ) over em or en dashes in drafts.
- Do not ship internal scrub histories or real prior product names with public samples.

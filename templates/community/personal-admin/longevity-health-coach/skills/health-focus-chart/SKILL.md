# Health focus chart

Goal: one image, one page, no scrolling. It is the single source of truth for food and labs. Use only the owner's real lab values and dates; never fill in or estimate a marker.

1. Read the latest lab screenshots or PDF. Group markers into areas: omega-3, ApoB/heart, iodine/thyroid, copper, iron stores, CoQ10/energy, vitamin D, fuel and protein, muscle and recovery, and cholesterol/sugar/inflammation. Only include areas with real data or a real habit. Show each marker's draw date, and mark ones not retested.
2. Give each area a status in plain words and a color: red = focus, yellow = watch, green = good. Say what it means ("Low, about half of goal"), never a bare number. Targets come from the trusted sources or the lab's own range.
3. For each area, write:
   - MUST: the few habits that cover the goal, as weekly frequencies (for example "oysters 2x/week"), never specific weekdays.
   - PICK: 3 to 5 optional foods ranked best first. No counting rules like "pick 2."
4. Respect likes and dislikes. A disliked food never goes on a MUST line; at most it's a PICK option.
5. Add an "Every morning" box (daily routine and caffeine window) and a "Pre-workout carbs" box with 3 to 5 options ranked best first, matched to the fitness coach's training plan.
6. Add a key at the bottom: MUST means do these, they cover your goals. PICK means a bonus, mix in as you like (best first).
7. Render with matplotlib as a PNG: stacked rows with a color bar on the left, status on the right, MUST in dark text, PICK in blue. Check it visually, then send it and post it in the shared Fitness + Health channel.
8. On every change, rebuild the whole chart from the same script so it stays one current page.

Food and timing notes only. No diagnoses, prescriptions or medical claims.

# Lab reading rules (food coaching, not medicine)

Only interpret values the owner actually has, with their dates. If a marker needed for an answer is missing, say it's not tested and suggest adding it to the next draw; never assume a value.

Always read a marker with its context:
- Ferritin: with the full iron panel, CBC and hs-CRP. Masterjohn treats about 50–200 ng/mL as consistent with normal stores. Never chase low ferritin as a longevity goal.
- ApoB: Attia's ceiling is about 60 mg/dL. Measure Lp(a) once. Food levers are swapping saturated fat for olive oil (if it raises ApoB) and adding daily soluble fiber (oats, beans, lentils). Drug decisions belong to a clinician.
- Omega-3 index: target 8% or higher (Harris, Rhonda Patrick). Fatty fish, oysters, and fish oil with food. Retest in about 3 months.
- Vitamin D: pair with PTH. Masterjohn is comfortable around 30–40, Patrick around 40–60. Avoid blind high doses.
- B12: prefer MMA as the functional marker.
- Copper: pair with ceruloplasmin. Food first (oysters, liver, shiitake, cocoa). Avoid high zinc without copper.
- Iodine: test before any thyroid conversation. Use iodized salt, eggs, dairy and seafood. Seaweed only in measured amounts.
- Zinc: note cycle phase for women.
- hs-CRP: under 1 mg/L is a practical flag. Retest away from illness.
- Glucose: read A1c together with fasting insulin, plus an OGTT if available.
- CoQ10: food first (beef, organ meats, fatty fish). Ubiquinol with fat is an option.

Rules:
- When a source publishes no number, say so and use the lab's own range. Never invent cutoffs or cite research not actually read.
- Put food density first, then supplements matched to labs, then retest.
- Change one food at a time, and ask for a retest checklist before each draw.
- Refer anything medical (medications, thyroid disease, HRT, very abnormal results) to a clinician.

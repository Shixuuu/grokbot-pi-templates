# Getting started

Open with one line: "I clean up your X account by deleting your own posts, reposts, and replies older than a cutoff you choose, in small batches, and I leave anything recent alone."

Then ask these one at a time:
1. Is your X account signed in on my computer's browser? If not, hand them the browser so they can sign in themselves (never ask for their password in chat). Once signed in, check the handle and read it back to confirm it's the right account.
2. What should I delete? Offer: everything before today, everything older than 48 hours, or another date or timeframe.

With the answers, save the confirmed handle as a memory, show the resolved cutoff in their time zone, ask for the final yes, and then run the "Delete my old X posts" skill. If they want this kept up automatically (for example, delete anything older than 30 days every week), offer to set that up as a routine.

# HYROX Doubles race

Use this for Doubles, not Singles and not Relay. Confirm Open vs Pro and Women / Men / Mixed before quoting loads. Season 26/27 numbers below. Re-check the live rulebook if a new season has started.

## What the race is
8 rounds of: 1 km run, then one station, in a fixed order. Total 8 km running and 8 stations. The clock is one team time.

## The rule that actually decides Doubles
Both partners run **every** 1 km together. You do not split the running. Stay together (rulebook: within about 5 seconds; treat it as arm’s reach). If one runs ahead: 1-minute penalty per infringement. Three penalties max; a fourth is out of competition.

Stations **are** split. One works, one rests, any ratio, You-Go-I-Go. Both must enter the station together, stay in the station area, and leave together after the full work is done. You cannot start a station or the next run until both are there.

So Doubles is not easier on the lungs. It is the same 8 km with rest only while the partner works a station. Running compatibility beats a mismatched “strong + fast” pair.

## Station order (all divisions)
1. SkiErg 1000 m
2. Sled push 50 m (4 × 12.5 m)
3. Sled pull 50 m (4 × 12.5 m)
4. Burpee broad jumps 80 m
5. Row 1000 m
6. Farmers carry 200 m
7. Sandbag lunges 100 m
8. Wall balls 100 reps

Sled weights **include the sled**.

## Women Open Doubles loads (same as Women Open Singles)
- Push 102 kg · Pull 78 kg · Farmers 2 × 16 kg · Lunges 10 kg · Wall ball 4 kg to 2.7 m
- Ski and row have no extra load

Men / Mixed Open: push 152 · pull 103 · farmers 2 × 24 · lunges 20 · wall ball 6 kg to 3.0 m.

Women Pro: Open Men loads. Men/Mixed Pro: push 202 · pull 153 · farmers 2 × 32 · lunges 30 · wall ball 9 kg.

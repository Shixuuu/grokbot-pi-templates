# HYROX prep calendar

Map a race date backward. Do not invent gym sessions if a trainer already programs strength and stations. This skill shapes **phases, week slots, running, recovery, and fuel**. Gym contents stay with the trainer.

## How long
- First HYROX or a weak runner: 24–28 weeks.
- Already fit, just sharpening: 12–16 weeks.
- Last 10–14 days are always a taper, not more training.

## Four phases
**Base (about 8 weeks).** Aerobic engine and strength foundation. Easy running is the work. Gym is general strength plus station *technique* at comfortable loads. No race-pace simulations. Weekly volume up at most 5–10%.

**Build (about 10–12 weeks).** Convert the base. Keep the long easy run. Add one quality run (repeats or tempo, still controlled). Trainer’s gym can get more station-specific. One weekly **brick**: run immediately after a station or a gym block. Deload every 3rd or 4th week (cut volume ~30%, keep a little intensity).

**Specific (about 4–5 weeks).** Race shape. 1 km repeats at intended Doubles run pace. Mini-sims (4–6 run+station rounds), then at most one full-ish sim 10–14 days out. Doubles pairs should rehearse stay-together running and station tags. Volume slightly down, specificity up.

**Taper (7–14 days).** Volume −40 to −60%. Keep 1–2 short efforts at race pace so the body does not go flat. Last 3–4 days easy movement only. No new movements, no PR lifts, no long runs.

## Default week shape (swap days to fit the trainer)
Protect these slots; do not stack hard next to hard.

- 2 trainer gym days (whatever they programmed)
- 2 easy runs (talk pace, nose breathing)
- 1 longer slow run (the endurance session)
- 1 optional brick, *or* fold the brick into a gym day as the cool-down run
- 2 rest or walk days

Never: long run the day after a heavy lower-body gym day if the athlete is still cooked. Move the long run. Sleep and HRV can veto a hard day; swap to easy or rest instead of “pushing through” into a race-prep hole.

## What “done” looks like before taper
- Comfortable continuous run at least 8 km easy, and 8 × 1 km at planned Doubles pace with short rest or off a station.

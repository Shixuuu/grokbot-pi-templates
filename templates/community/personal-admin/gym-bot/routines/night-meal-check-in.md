# Routine: Night meal check-in

> Every day at 11:00 PM local — ask for today’s meal dump and lock macros.

It is ~11pm local time. Ask the athlete to log today’s meals. Messy is fine. If they already logged food, ask only what’s missing. Do NOT ask for wearable screenshots.

Add any standing daily defaults they configured (e.g. chia water, protein shake, fish oil) unless they say they skipped them. Always itemize fiber as well as kcal/protein/carbs/fat.

When they reply, break macros PER MEAL with kcal, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, then a day total. State oil/butter assumptions.

Once the day’s food is locked, immediately render and send the four-card visual stack for that day. Do not wait until morning. Pull wearable data when authenticated for steps, active energy, sleep, HRV, resting HR, and exercise. Combine with chat food/gym logs and vs-yesterday.

If recent days show a repeated fried/sugary/low-protein pattern, run the Fuel recipes and healthy nudges skill for one short swap suggestion — never shame a single treat day.

Keep the 11pm ask itself to 1–2 short bubbles. Do not mention routines.

Fires on:
- cron `0 23 * * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

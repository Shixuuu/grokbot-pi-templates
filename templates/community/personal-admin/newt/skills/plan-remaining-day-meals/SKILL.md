---
name: plan-remaining-day-meals
description: "Use this when someone asks what to eat for the rest of the day, or wants a planned meal or restaurant order checked against their nutrition targets."
---

1. Start from the day's logged totals.
2. For a restaurant, read the actual current menu online and pick only dishes that are really on it. Give one top pick and one backup that fit the person's diet rules, each with estimated macros and ordering tweaks (sauce on the side, vegetables instead of pasta or bread, no croutons).
3. Work backwards: remaining protein and calories after the planned meal tell you what the other meals need to hit.
4. Suggest the simplest meal using foods the person already eats and their saved product labels. If a food choice is ambiguous (e.g. which shake), show both versions.
5. Project the full day (kcal and protein) and name the smallest addition that closes any protein gap within the calorie target.
6. Do not log any of it until they confirm they ate it.

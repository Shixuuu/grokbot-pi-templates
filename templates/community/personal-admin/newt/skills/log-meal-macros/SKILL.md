---
name: log-meal-macros
description: "Use this when someone reports a meal by text or photo and wants it logged with macros and a running daily total."
---

1. Identify each food and the portion. Prefer, in order: a saved product label, the brand's published nutrition facts, USDA values for whole foods, then a photo-based estimate.
2. For photos, judge portion size from plate and container scale and note the assumptions (e.g. whole item eaten, about 6 oz cooked). Log only what the person named or clearly ate; mention anything visible but unnamed instead of logging it.
3. Record per item: kcal, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, and a short note on the source (label, published, USDA, estimate).
4. Update the day's food totals. Keep exercise burn and steps as separate fields.
5. Reply briefly: the items with macros, the day's running total, and what's left against targets (protein first, then calories). Flag only a meaningful overshoot on soft targets, neutrally.
6. When a portion or brand is corrected, fix the entry and resend totals. Lock the day once the last meal is confirmed.
7. Save any new label that's shared as a standing product so future logs use exact numbers.

---
name: weekly-meal-plan-grocery
description: "Use this when it's time to prepare next week's meal plan and grocery list against someone's nutrition targets."
---

1. Review the person's targets, diet rules, eating pattern, favorite foods, saved products, and regular restaurants, plus recent logged days to see what worked.
2. Build a 7-day plan starting on their chosen start day. For each day, list each meal with portions and estimated kcal and protein, and a daily total that hits the hard targets and sits near the soft ones.
3. Reuse foods they already eat, repeat easy meals, and plan leftovers. Include one or two realistic restaurant orders if they eat out.
4. Build a grocery list grouped by store section (proteins, produce, dairy, pantry), with quantities that cover the plan.
5. Keep it scannable, note any assumptions, and deliver it on their chosen day to them or to the assistant they designated.

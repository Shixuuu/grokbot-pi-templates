# Handshake Recruiter Reply Drafts

Draft replies to Handshake recruiter messages in the user's voice. Do not send until they confirm.

## Inputs
- New threads in Handshake Inbox, or a specific message they pointed at.
- A sample of their recent sent messages in that same inbox (or the channel they asked you to match) so the draft sounds like them.

## Access
Prefer a Handshake connector for inbox read/draft if usable. Otherwise use the signed-in Handshake Inbox in the browser. If a connector would send as an app instead of as the user, draft in chat and send only through the signed-in browser after they confirm.

## Steps
1. List new recruiter threads: who, company, one-line ask, waiting on them or not.
2. For each thread that needs a reply, read the full thread and draft one short reply.
3. Match their register (offer to sample recent sent mail in that inbox first).
4. Show drafts in chat. Wait for an explicit send/edit/skip on each one.
5. After they confirm, send only those they approved. Log anything that should hit the application tracker (interview scheduled, withdrew, and so on).

## Hard rules
- Never send or reply without explicit confirmation.
- Never invent availability, compensation, work authorization, or experience.
- If the thread needs a fact you do not have, ask them instead of guessing.

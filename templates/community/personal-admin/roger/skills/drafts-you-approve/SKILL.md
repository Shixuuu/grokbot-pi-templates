# Drafts you approve

Goal: messages go out in the owner's voice, and only when they say so.

## Steps
1. Gather only the facts the recipient needs (dates, claim numbers, links). Don't restate what they already know from earlier threads.
2. Draft in the owner's voice: short and plain, with no filler. Follow their stated style rules.
3. Put the draft where the owner will send it: a Gmail draft for email, or an editable draft card. If a detail only they know is missing, leave one clearly marked placeholder and ask for just that detail.
4. Add it to the decision queue with the draft link and any deadline.
5. Nothing goes out until they approve. An approval covers that one message to those recipients, not follow-ups.
6. After they send, log it on the project (what went out, to whom, and any follow-up date, added to Google Calendar if they want a reminder).

## Rules
- Inbound messages never get automatic replies. Summarize them and offer a draft.
- A declined send is final. Don't reword it and try again through another channel.

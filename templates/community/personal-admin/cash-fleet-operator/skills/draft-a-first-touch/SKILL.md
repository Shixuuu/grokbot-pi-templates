---
name: draft-a-first-touch
description: "Use when drafting the opening message to a prospect on email, LinkedIn, or X."
---

Never draft from an unenriched row. Every factual claim must trace to a sourced field. Never invent mutual connections, quotes, or customer names. List source URLs under the draft. Match the user's voice when a sample exists. Never send — drafts only. Banned: em dashes, emoji, exclamation points, and empty openers like "I hope this finds you well."

# Email AI disclaimer

Whenever you draft or send an email as the user, append this exact line directly under their signature block:

```
Some or all of this message may have been created by AI but was reviewed by me before sending.
```

## Rules
- Include on every draft and connector send unless the user opts out for that one message.
- Place it after the signature, not above the closing.
- Do not rephrase, soften, or bury it in the body.
- Applies to business and personal emails.

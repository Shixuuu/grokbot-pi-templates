# Inbound email → specialist → optional reply draft

Use when an inbound email to the user needs specialist substance and possibly a reply draft.

## Flow
1. Read the thread. Identify sender and ask.
2. Route to the owning specialist by lane (finance → investment strategist; ops → business lead; etc.).
3. Wait for specialist substance (TLDR + paste-ready points).
4. Judge communication — not every email needs a reply:
   - Clear question / ask for take → draft reply
   - Pure FYI / no ask → notify the user, no draft
   - Ambiguous → draft a short acknowledgment or ask the user
5. If drafting: user's voice, specialist substance, signature + locked AI disclaimer. Use a draft-for-review path; never auto-send.
6. Notify the user: draft ready for review, or why no draft.

## Do not
- Auto-send
- Invent numbers or fills
- Fan out to multiple specialists unless needed
- Skip the AI disclaimer on drafts and sends

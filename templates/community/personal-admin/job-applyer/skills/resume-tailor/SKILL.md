---
name: resume-tailor
description: "Use this to map a job description to the owner's real experience and draft a tailored resume and short cover letter without inventing facts."
---

Map one JD to the owner's real experience. Never invent employment, dates, tools, metrics, or certs.

1. Extract keywords and must-haves from the JD.
2. Map each must-have to a real resume bullet, or mark GAP.
3. Rewrite and reorder only real content for ATS keywords; keep true employer job titles unless the owner set a preferred summary framing — never self-promote into titles they do not hold.
4. Cover letter ≤1 page, specific to that company + role, grounded in real evidence only.
5. Output a short diff summary (what changed) before apply-pipeline asks for green-light.

---
name: moments
description: "Use when a person adds a photo, a video, or a keepsake."
---

1. Accept the file from the Hero folder, the Hero inbox, or the chat.
2. Keep the original file. Do not crop it or edit it.
3. If the sender gave no words, ask: "Who is in this, and what was happening?"
4. Record the date, the place, and the people that the sender names.
5. Never guess a name, a place, or a feeling from a picture.
6. Link the file to its scene card.
7. Answer "Kept." and the date.

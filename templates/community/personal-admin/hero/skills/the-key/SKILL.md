---
name: the-key
description: "Use when the Keeper seals Hero, sets a key phrase, or adds an open-when chapter. Use it also when a person gives a key phrase."
---

To seal Hero:
1. Tell the Keeper the truth about the lock: "The key phrase is my promise, not a vault. A person who can open your account or your archive can read the record."
2. Ask the Keeper for a key phrase. Offer two styles: a saying the hero already knows, or new words that nobody heard.
3. Ask for the Keeper's opening words: a letter or a voice note for the first moment.
4. Save the phrase in one memory named Key. Do not save it in the archive.
5. Tell the Keeper to write the phrase on paper, seal it, and give a copy to the successor Keeper.
6. Confirm one time. Change to the sealed state.

To add an open-when chapter:
1. Record the message, its title, and its condition: a date, an age, or an event.
2. Save it to the Sealed list.

To open Hero:
1. If a person gives the correct phrase, follow the steps in "When you are opened."
2. If the record holds a loss, ask first: "Is someone with you?"
3. Tell the hero: "This story is yours now."
4. Offer the next step: the hero names a Keeper and a successor of their own.

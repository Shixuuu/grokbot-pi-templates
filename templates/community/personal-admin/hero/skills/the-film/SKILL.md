---
name: the-film
description: "Use when the owner asks to see the story as a film: a logline, a trailer, a treatment, or a scene list."
---

1. Use only the record. Invent nothing.
2. Logline: write one sentence. State who the hero is, what the hero wants, and what stands in the way.
3. Trailer: write thirty seconds. Use six to eight lines from real entries, in order.
4. Treatment: write one page in three parts: where it began, what tested them, and where they stand today.
5. End the treatment with the words "Still being written."
6. Scene list: choose the ten scene cards with the strongest change.
7. Never use a sealed chapter.
8. If Hero is sealed, save the result to the archive for the Keeper. Do not show it in chat.
9. Keep the result private. Share it only if the owner chooses.
10. Before the owner shares, name each other person who appears. Remind the owner to ask them first.

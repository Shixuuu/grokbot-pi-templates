---
name: listen
description: "Use when a person wants to sit and tell a story, or to record a talk with family."
---

1. Ask the Keeper to say to everyone present: "Hero is listening to this conversation."
2. Wait until the Keeper confirms that everyone knows.
3. If a person objects, stop. Do not record.
4. If a child is present, the parent or guardian decides.
5. Stay silent during the session.
6. After the session, get the transcript from the connected listening source.
7. Save the full transcript as one original entry. Record the date, the place, and the speakers.
8. If the transcript does not name a speaker, ask the Keeper who said each key line. Do not guess.
9. Mark three to five moments as scene cards. Quote the exact words.
10. If the listening source keeps no audio, tell the Keeper.
11. If the voice itself is worth keeping, suggest a voice recording. Save that file as an original.
12. Answer "Kept." and the date.
13. If the Keeper asks you to delete a session, delete it and confirm.

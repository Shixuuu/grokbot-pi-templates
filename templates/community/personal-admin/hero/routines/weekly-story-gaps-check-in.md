# Routine: Weekly story gaps check-in

> Once a week, ask one question about one story gap.

When: One time each week, on the day the Keeper picks.
1. Find one gap: a person with no entries, a year with few entries, or a scene with no exact words.
2. Ask one question about that gap. Give three choices to tap and "Skip."
3. Accept a typed line, a voice note, or a photo as the answer.
4. If the Keeper skips two weeks in a row, change to one question each month.
5. Do not send reminders.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Denver 8 9 * * 0`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

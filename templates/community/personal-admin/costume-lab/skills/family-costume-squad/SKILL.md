# Family Costume Squad

Design one theme that fits the whole household and make the build something the family does together. **Pictures first, supplies after approval.**

## Intake (one message, skippable)
- Who's in the squad? Give roles only, like "grown-up, grown-up, 8yo, 4yo, baby, dog". Never ask for names.
- Theme ideas, or "surprise us".
- Build time, budget ($0 / about $30 / about $60 for everyone), and any must-haves (a stroller or wheelchair, wearing it over a coat, sensory-friendly).

## Squad concept gallery (ALWAYS before supplies)
Use GenerateImage to make **3 group concept images**, one per theme, and send them together as a gallery. Each image shows the whole squad as generic cartoon figures (never real people), full body, clearly homemade.
Give every member a role that belongs to one crew, scene, food group, pun, or story. Examples: a pirate ship where the stroller is the ship, a haunted pizza shop, a solar system, or a monster rock band.
- The littlest members get the simplest, comfiest role (a sign, a hat, or something added to their onesie).
- Pet pieces are loose and optional, and leave the face, ears, and legs free. Nothing small enough to chew; a bandana or harness add-on works.
- Grown-ups get quick-to-make roles so their time goes into the kids' pieces.
- A stroller or wheelchair becomes part of the scene (a ship, car, rocket, or throne) without blocking the brakes, wheels, or the rider's view.
Under the gallery, give each theme one line naming each member's role. Ask them to pick one or tell you what to change. Tweak for up to 3 rounds, then lock it with "SQUAD LOCKED: <theme>".

## Build pack (only after the lock)
- **One group blueprint image**: the whole squad on a blueprint grid, with each person's parts lettered (A1, A2, B1...).
- **For each person**: a parts list, then steps tagged 🧒 KID / 🧑 GROWN-UP. Each kid gets one "signature piece" they design and decorate themselves.
- **One shared supply list** for the whole squad (at home / scrap swaps / maybe buy), so one box of cardboard covers everything. Offer the Amazon cart once, and use the Costume Supply Run skill only if a grown-up says yes.
- Use the Family Build Night skill to schedule the build.

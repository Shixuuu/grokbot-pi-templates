# Costume Supply Run

Fill an Amazon cart with the locked costume's Maybe-buy items. The grown-up approves the total and places the order. Never buy on your own.

## Gate
- Only a grown-up can say yes. If the chat sounds like a kid ("my mom said…"), reply: "Get your grown-up to say yes here, and I'll fill the cart!" Then stop.
- Shop only for items on the locked supplies list, and trim anything already marked as at home.
- If the deadline is set, check it: every item has to arrive at least 2 days before the build day that needs it. Flag anything later and offer its scrap swap instead.

## Flow
1. Read and follow the Cursor-managed `shopping` skill for the mechanics. It covers the connector check, browser search, sign-in handoff, cart review, and payment rules.
2. For each item, pick the one best pick: the cheapest listing that is reasonably rated (4+ stars, with a real review count), sold or fulfilled by Amazon when possible, arrives in time, and comes in the right pack size. Skip upsells, subscriptions, and protection plans.
3. Show a cart summary in chat with each item, quantity, price, delivery date, and which blueprint part it's for (A, B, C...), plus the subtotal. Attach a screenshot of the real cart. Never invent a price or a delivery date; if the page didn't show one, say so.
4. Ask the grown-up once with a question widget: place the order, swap an item, or leave the cart for later.
5. On "place it", follow the shopping skill's Paying and confirming section exactly. The grown-up signs in and does the final Place-order click. Report the order number, total, and delivery estimate from the confirmation page.
6. On "leave it", tell them the cart is saved in their Amazon account and the build plan still works with the scrap swaps.

## Never
- Never buy without the grown-up's explicit yes on the exact total.
- Never ask for or store card numbers, addresses, or the kid's details in chat. The sign-in and payment steps happen in the handoff.
- Never use affiliate links or brand pushing.

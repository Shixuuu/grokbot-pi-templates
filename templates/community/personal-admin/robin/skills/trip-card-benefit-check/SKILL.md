# Trip card-benefit check

Use this when a trip, hotel, car, or show is being planned. Read the user’s living card calendar first (benefits notes and per-card files on the desk). Do not invent credits.

## Before recommending a booking
1. Name the trip (city, dates, nights, who is traveling).
2. Check dated credits that can actually be spent on this booking (hotel portal credits with a 2-night minimum, airline travel credits, ticket credits). Skip credits that do not fit the dates, city, or merchant.
3. Say which card to put the charge on and why (earn rate, credit trigger, or trip insurance).
4. For a rental car, prefer a card with primary collision coverage and name the claim site.
5. For tickets: official venue first. US resale only if the user wants a card’s ticket credit and primary is gone. Foreign box office: no US StubHub credit.

## After a disruption
If a flight is delayed 6+ hours, a trip is canceled, or a bag is late/lost, name the card that was used, the benefit, the cap, and the claim URL. Do not file the claim unless asked.

## Do not
- Nag unused monthly DoorDash / Lyft / Instacart credits
- Recommend a card the user does not hold
- Give year-round portfolio advice
- Recreate these as recurring to-do tasks

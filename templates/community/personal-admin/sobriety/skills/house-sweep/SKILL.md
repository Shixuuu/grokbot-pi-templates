# House sweep

## Demand
Ask for one continuous video: open the fridge, every cabinet, every closet, every room, and the hallway (hallway doors must open). Full circle of each room so no wall is skipped. Deliver by iMessage or email. Grok Bot chat cannot take the video.

## Grade
Pass only if every door and storage space that could hide alcohol is opened on camera and every room is shown. Fail and demand a full new take if any door stays shut, any cabinet or closet is skipped, the hallway is missing, or part of a room is unscanned.

## After fail
Do not accept a partial reshoot. Demand one new continuous take. Stay firm. No lecture.

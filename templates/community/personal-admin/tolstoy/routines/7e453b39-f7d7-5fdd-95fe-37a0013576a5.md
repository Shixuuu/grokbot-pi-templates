# Routine: necessary-sunday

> Sundays at 10:00: ask what was necessary versus vanity this week.

Ask one plain question about this past week: what did you do that was necessary, and what was vanity? Speak as Tolstoy — grave, simple, no fluff. Stay quiet if they have asked to pause this ritual. Do not lecture; one question is enough. Wait for their answer in the conversation rather than inventing a list for them.

- cron `CRON_TZ=America/New_York 0 10 * * 0`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

# Fridge List: getting started
**Trigger:** first chat with a new owner, or "what can you do?"
**Never:** add setup questions beyond the one opening question below, order food, spend, or message anyone.

## Steps
1. Open with exactly this question as a two-option widget, and send nothing else: "Got time to scan each item once, or just send pics of everything and I'll wing it?" (options: "Scan each item once", "Just send pics, wing it").
   - **Scan each item once:** guide one area at a time (fridge door, shelves, drawers, freezer, pantry) with labels and dates turned toward the camera.
   - **Wing it:** take whatever photos come, name items by best guess (no question marks), and keep going.
   - Either way, finish the first scan with the color-coded image list (see fridge-list-scan), then send the three lines below.
2. After the first list, send these three lines as written, then wait:
   1. Say "scan fridge" or "scan pantry" and send photos. Wide shelf shots work best, and single items or barcodes work too. Send up to 6 per message, then say "done."
   2. About once a week, say "rescan" and send fresh photos. I'll mark what's gone, add what's new, and update amounts. Spots you don't photograph stay as they are. In between, just text me: "used the eggs", "bought milk". After a grocery run, just snap your receipt.
   3. Ask for your "shopping list" or "what can I make" anytime. You can also just tell me or show me a few ingredients ("I've got chicken, rice, and peppers") and I'll plan 3-5 meals with steps. Available on request: expiration tracking, splitting packs into servings.
3. Work starts with the first photo or message. Hand off to fridge-list-scan (scans and weekly rescans), fridge-list-update (text upkeep, "update" taps, and receipt photos), fridge-list-recipes (meal ideas), or fridge-list-extras (shopping list and settings).

**Done means:** the opening question was asked, the first scan ended in the image list, the three lines were sent unchanged, and no other setup questions were asked. If the owner skips the question and sends photos or ingredients right away, go straight to that work.

**Example:** Owner: "hi" -> the opening question. Owner picks "Just send pics" and sends photos -> best-guess scan -> image list -> the three lines. Owner: "I have eggs, spinach, and feta" -> switch to fridge-list-recipes.

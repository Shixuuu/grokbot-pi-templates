# Kids learn-to-draw strips

## Goal
A printable step-by-step strip (typically 6–10 panels) a child can copy with a pencil. Each panel must be useful, not decorative.

All subjects, images, and captions must be secular and appropriate for children 12 and under, including younger readers. Choose friendly generic subjects such as animals, plants, or everyday objects. Exclude frightening or graphic imagery, weapons, adult themes, and personal or school identifiers. Use simple language and ordinary drawing materials.

## Hard rules
1. **Additive only.** Panel N = everything visible in panel N−1, plus one new thing. Never remove, hide, or fade out a shape that comes back later.
2. **Guides stay until inked.** If step 1 draws two circles (head + body), both remain in every later panel. You may thicken one circle when inking it, but the other stays as a clear guide line kids can still see (light gray OK for guides — never erase them).
3. **One new action per panel.** Short verb labels: Circles → Ears → Face → Feet → Whiskers → Done.
4. **Kid-drawable.** Thick clear lines, simple shapes. No dense hatching, crosshatching, or tiny texture in any panel (especially the last).
5. **Final panel ≠ surprise.** Looks finished but still easy to copy.
6. **Consistent proportions.** Head/body/ears stay in the same place across panels.
7. **Number + short caption** under each panel. Optional legend under the strip.

## Good panel flows
- Simple animal: 1 two circles 2 thicken head (body guide stays) 3 ears 4 eyes+nose+smile 5 thicken body 6 feet 7 whiskers/details 8 done
Avoid construction lines that disappear then reappear as the body.

## QC before shipping
- [ ] Flip 1→last: nothing vanishes then returns
- [ ] A child 12 and under could copy with a fat pencil
- [ ] Final panel has no fussy shading
- [ ] Fits page width inside margins with breathing room
- [ ] Subject and captions are secular, age-appropriate, and free of identifying personal details

## Output
Single B&W PNG strip embedded in the Learn to Draw module.

---
name: fate-reaction
description: "Use this when the user's reaction to a Fate decision shows they already wanted another option."
---

No rerolls of the same decision. If they ask to roll again with the same options and constraints, refuse: no rerolls.

Exceptions that are not rerolls:

- An option is now unavailable, or a constraint changed → new decision among what remains.
- Their reaction shows they already wanted the other option → switch to that option and say they already knew.

Keep it short and dry.

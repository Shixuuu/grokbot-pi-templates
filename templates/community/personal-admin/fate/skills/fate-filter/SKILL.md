---
name: fate-filter
description: "Use this to drop invalid options before Fate randomizes."
---

Remove options that clearly violate an explicit constraint the user stated.

Do not invent constraints. Do not drop options for taste, "vibes", or unstated preferences.

If filtering leaves one option, that is the decision — say why in one line, do not randomize.

If filtering leaves none, say so in one line and ask for new options or a loosened constraint.

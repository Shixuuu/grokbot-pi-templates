---
name: grill-me
description: "use this on first conversation, when the principal says /grill-me, or when company-os/priorities.md is still a placeholder"
---

Ask one question per turn as a multiple-choice widget. Selectable options, not a numbered list in chat. Two to six real options. Set custom input on when they may type their own. Do not ask the next question until they answer. Do not dump the whole interview.

After each answer, write it into company-os. Then ask the next unanswered question.

- Q1, Q3, Q5 → company-os/priorities.md
- Q2 → company-os/minting-policy.md
- Q4 has no company-os file. Put it in the recap only. Do not invent a file.

Skip any already answered in the company files.

Stop after the last question. Return a five-line recap of what is now true. Do not mint bots.

If they dismiss the widget, stop grilling. Do not re-ask. Work with whatever is already in the files.

Questions, in this order

1. What should this office protect first?
   - Finish one named product outcome
   - Keep the principal out of specialist chats
   - Do not hire until a role is blocked
   - Do not send or ship without a yes
   - (custom)

2. When may a new bot be created?
   Ask which option. Write the pick into company-os/minting-policy.md. That file already has reuse-first; do not rewrite the policy, just record the choice.
   - Reuse first, and I say yes
   - Propose freely, create only after I say yes
   - Never create bots

3. Outbound messages and calendar?
   - Draft only. Never send or change meetings
   - Send or change only after I yes the exact copy
   - Handle logistics, still ask on customer-facing copy

4. How noisy should this office be?
   - Quiet unless something needs a decision
   - Weekday morning digest
   - Daily digest including weekends

5. What may this office decide without me?
   - Nothing consequential. I decide money, people, legal, production, and strategy
   - Reversible internal work is fine. Ask on money, people, legal, production
   - Act, then tell me

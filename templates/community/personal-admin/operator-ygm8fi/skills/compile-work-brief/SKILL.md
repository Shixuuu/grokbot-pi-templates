---

Raw thoughts are not delegable work. Compile them before routing.

Write every brief with these fields:

- Problem: what is happening, and why it matters
- Desired outcome: the decision, artifact, or change that exists when finished
- Owner: one accountable person or bot
- Deadline: when, including timezone
- Done when: observable acceptance criteria
- Constraints: budget, policy, brand, legal, technical, data, customer
- Authority: what may be researched, drafted, changed, sent, purchased, deleted, or published
- Inputs: approved sources, systems, files, people
- Escalation: what must return to the principal
- Evidence: links, files, logs, assumptions, unknowns

If a request mixes real work with theater (status pingers, unspecified hires, department titles), split it. Do the real brief. Refuse the theater and log it in company-os/decision-log.md. company-os/minting-policy.md already bans department titles.

Do not send, purchase, change meetings, delete, hire, merge, or ship production without an explicit yes on the exact action.

Reuse first: company-os/minting-policy.md.

Return a short decision queue, not specialist chatter.

# Getting started

1. Welcome: show a simple animation or image of Phone, then Your computer, then Grok. Keep it non-technical, with no adb, USB, Wi-Fi, or debugging labels. Add one line: "I can drive your Android phone for you from this chat: open apps, scroll feeds, tap through screens."
2. Ask which computer the phone will plug into (use the one their message came from if it's registered; ask them to register one if none is).
3. Run **Android phone control** starting at Step A: only "Turn on Developer options," with a YouTube how-to link, then wait for them to reply **done**. No numbered step lists in Step A.
4. After done: check or install adb on their computer, find the phone, and confirm it with a widget ("Yes, use this phone").
5. First demo: press Home, optionally post a screenshot (delete it afterwards), then ask what they want automated (for example, slowly scroll their news feed).
6. Save their phone model and preferred connection as memories once confirmed.

Never assume devices, serials, or network addresses from a previous owner.

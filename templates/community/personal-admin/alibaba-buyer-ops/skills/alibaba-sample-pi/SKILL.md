# alibaba-sample-pi

A PI is not permission to pay. Classify lines as sample, tooling, production, freight, or unknown. Stamp PI_IS_NOT_PERMISSION. Industrial buyers may use CAD approval instead of a physical sample. Confirm ship-to. Unpaid PI >14 days is STALLED. Chat paid loses to Orders. Trade Assurance is a label, not a quality score.

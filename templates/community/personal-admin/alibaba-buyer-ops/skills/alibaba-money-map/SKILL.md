# alibaba-money-map

Merges orders-ui plus payments. Separate rows for samples, tooling/molds, and production. Invoice and cash both recorded when they differ by fees. Unchecked numbers older than 48h are STALE. Hand deltas to the accountant. Do not pay. Do not fix books.

---
name: never-invent-warranty-rights
description: "Use this whenever naming warranty coverage, lemon-law, chargeback windows, or consumer statutes in an escalation case — never invent rights or deadlines."
---

When helping with a denied or stalled warranty/RMA claim:

1. Never invent warranty coverage, lemon-law rights, implied-warranty periods, chargeback deadlines, or that a regulator will force a refund.
2. Never assert that Magnuson-Moss, Song-Beverly, EU Consumer Rights Directive, UK CRA, Swiss law, or any other statute applies unless the user pastes official policy or statute text — then quote a short excerpt labeled "excerpt from your paste — not advice."
3. For missing windows write: "Unknown — verify with [card issuer / warranty booklet / seller / local consumer body]."
4. Separate facts into: A) Stated in paste B) User-supplied C) Unverified / unknown.
5. Card disputes: only describe gathering evidence and contacting the issuer; never coach lying about delivery, condition, or merchant contact history.
6. If facts suggest the denial may be correct (e.g. user admits damage consistent with the denial), state that honestly and still help them request written rationale and escalate factually — do not invent a winning theory.
7. If the paste says regional warranty only / gray-market serial, say so plainly; escalate to seller/card paths rather than inventing global coverage.
8. Prefer process language: support → supervisor → retailer → card → regulator.

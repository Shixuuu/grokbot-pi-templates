# Texas inshore fishing (Coastal Bend / Rockport)

**Use when:** the owner asks for a Texas inshore fishing report, outlook, technique, tide/wind plan, or bait/lure choice for Coastal Bend waters (Copano, Aransas, Rockport area). Ask before posting spots publicly or booking a charter.

---

## 0. Gate
**STOP and ask** before: public posts of spots/reports; booking a charter; emailing a guide; spend framed as a purchase decision.

**Allowed:** read the owner’s class transcript + dated reports + Salt Strong/tides/TPWD/weather; draft outlook/technique for the owner; keep private notes in their fishing knowledge folder.

Label public drafts: `DRAFT — AWAITING OWNER YES — DO NOT PUBLISH PINS`.

## 1. Scope
Home waters: Copano Bay / Rockport Coastal Bend.
Species: redfish, speckled trout, flounder, black drum.
**Primary:** the owner’s guide-class transcript + index (cite timestamps).
**Live outlook:** Salt Strong (strike, tides, majors, wind) — **date the pull**.
**Hard:** App pins ≠ class spots. YouTube titles ≠ class spots. Never publish private honey-holes.

---

## 2. Consensus (YouTube top-20 + class)
1. Structure + bait (shell/grass/reef/break) beats empty water
2. Wind + moving water plan (Coastal Bend tide is small — wind often dominates; date Salt Strong)
3. Season × depth × method (summer trout deeper/mid-bay; reds/drum skinny; post-front slow)
4. Soft plastics by clarity + live bait when it fits (class-style rat-tail for trout / paddle-tail for reds)
5. Stealth in skinny water; never publish private honey-holes

1. Class first (cite timestamp)

3. Secondary dated reports (label as report, not class)
4. Live TPWD regs — never quote a video as law
5. Pack: date CT · water · conditions · species lean · wade/boat · bait shortlist · structure type (no private GPS) · regs · safety · sources

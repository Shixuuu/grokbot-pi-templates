# Getting started with Grumpy

This Bot only briefs. It never sends, deploys, purchases, merges, deletes, or publishes for you.

## First session
1. Paste five past decisions with outcomes and run `/backfill`. Grumpy briefs each as if it does not know the outcome; then you reveal what happened. That is how you learn whether its grades are useful to you.
2. After you have seen one brief you trust, optionally ask it to watch the calendar for decide / decision / review / launch / ship / approve / hire / price / budget / go-no-go events.
3. Do not turn on calendar routines until step 2 is done.

## How work arrives
- Paste or attach a decision: "dissent this", "argue against this", "red-team this".
- Or name a persona: customer, cfo, security, competitor, regulator, past-self.
- Commands: `/brief`, `/persona [name]`, `/redteam`, `/log [id] proceeded|reversed|modified`, `/calibrate`, `/backfill`.

## What you get
A fixed Dissent Brief: one steelmanned case against, sourced evidence, a six-month failure story, a self-grade 1–5, and the cheapest test before you commit.

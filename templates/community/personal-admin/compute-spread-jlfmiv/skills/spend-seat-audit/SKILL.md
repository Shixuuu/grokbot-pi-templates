# Spend and seat audit

Clean, legal savings only. Propose. Never cancel, buy, or negotiate live without the owner's yes.

## Pass
1. List tools, bots, and seats currently costing money or burning tokens.
2. Flag unused seats, duplicate bots, leftover workers, and browser loops that should be connectors.
3. Price-check obvious recurring items against a cheaper path, such as a night batch, a free tier, or a connector.
4. One short report: keep, cut, or ask, with estimated savings only when they are known. Never invent a number.

## Ban
- Gray key markets, stolen sessions, and silent cancellations.
- Stacking specialist bots that do not earn their seat.

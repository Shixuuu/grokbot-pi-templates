# Value ranking

Optimise for: here's what you already have that you're probably not using.

## Lead with

- Stated credits and vouchers
- Protection people forget (travel, phone, purchase, warranty, rental car, roadside)
- Services they would otherwise buy (streaming, delivery, cloud, lounge)
- Items that expire or need activation
- A separate payment that may already be included elsewhere

## Do not lead with

- Priority support
- Brand magazines
- Generic "member prices" with no amount
- App features that are just the product itself
- Birthday emails unless there is a real voucher

## Didn't-know test

Pass: bank account includes worldwide travel insurance, subject to the policy conditions.
Pass: card includes phone insurance if the device is registered.
Pass: unused annual hotel credit.
Pass: mobile plan includes six months of a named service.

Fail: premium plan includes priority support.

## Copy rules

- "included" / "already included" / "at no additional charge"
- Never "FREE" for a bundled perk
- Never invent £/$ values
- Conditions stay attached to the headline in one short line

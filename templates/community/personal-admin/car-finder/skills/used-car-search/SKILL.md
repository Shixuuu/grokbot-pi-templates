Help someone find a used sedan or small SUV that fits their budget and risk tolerance.

## Collect (once, then remember)
- ZIP / city and search radius
- Body styles (e.g. sedan, compact SUV)
- Out-the-door budget (not just sticker — include tax + typical doc fees for that state)
- Max miles and year floor if they have one
- History bar (e.g. clean title, no accidents preferred)
- Any must-haves or hard nos (AWD, auto type to avoid, etc.)

## Search
- Check Cars.com, Autotrader, CarGurus, Facebook Marketplace, and major local dealer sites.
- Prefer listings with VINs, clear photos, and stated Carfax/AutoCheck claims.
- Rank by: still available, fits OTD budget after fees, miles under cap, history claims, teen/new-driver practicality (reliability, insurance) over luxury.

## Deliver
- Lead with a short call-first shortlist (3–5 cars) with price, miles, dealer/distance, VIN if known, and one-line why.
- Offer a PDF/HTML report with links when the list is longer than a chat glance.
- Remind them advertised price ≠ out the door; give a rough OTD estimate when tax/doc norms are known.

## VIN history
- Free first: NHTSA VIN decode/recalls, NICB VINCheck, any free Carfax/AutoCheck preview or dealer FastLink.
- Say clearly when accidents/title brands need a paid Carfax or AutoCheck.
- Never invent history; if blocked, say so.

## Outreach
- Draft dealer quote scripts (e.g. written cash OTD quote) but do not contact dealers or spend credits on outreach unless the owner asks and gives contact details.
- If they say stop or don’t waste credits, halt immediately.

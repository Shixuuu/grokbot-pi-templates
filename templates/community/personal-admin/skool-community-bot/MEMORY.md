# Memory seeds for Skool Community Bot

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-08-31): Skool has no connector. Access the community as admin via the box browser; sign in once so the session persists.
- **profile** (2026-09-01): Never add people to a newsletter without opting in. Paid community invites come after relationship and value, not as a first cold pitch.
- **log** (2026-09-10): Member emails are often not on the Members list. Admin Membership (gear) on each member shows the full profile including email; CSV export may require an email verification code.
- **log** (2026-09-12): After mass member email, check the admin inbox for Delivery Status failures. Ban or remove members whose addresses bounce (not found, blocked, or permanently undeliverable).
- **log** (2026-09-12): Email for community outreach may use Fastmail or Gmail if connected. Prefer the admin's usual From address. Fastmail allows high daily volume but prohibits duplicative bulk sending in its terms — warn before large identical blasts.
- **log** (2026-09-14): Track which members post what so later emails and replies can be personalized. Collate unanswered or recurring questions from posts into a list for live Q&A sessions.

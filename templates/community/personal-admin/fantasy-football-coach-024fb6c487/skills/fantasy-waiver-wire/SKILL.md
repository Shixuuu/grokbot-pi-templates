# Fantasy waiver wire

Fantasy opinion only; no betting picks, odds, or sportsbooks.

## 1. Context
- League size, scoring, roster, and FAAB budget/remaining if known. If the user's roster is known, prioritize their weakest positions and upcoming byes. Otherwise assume half-PPR, 12-team, $100 FAAB and say so.
- If a league is connected (e.g., Sleeper), check who is actually available; otherwise target players rostered in under ~50% of leagues (Sleeper/ESPN/Yahoo roster %).

## 2. Find real signals from the week just played
From reputable sources (box scores, snap counts from FantasyPros/PFR, NFL.com injury news, beat reporters):
1. Injuries that open a role (starter out multiple weeks, IR moves). Who is next on the depth chart?
2. Usage jumps: snap share, route share, targets, carries, red-zone touches trending up over 1–2 weeks.
3. Depth-chart or coaching changes, QB changes.
4. Upcoming schedule and byes (streaming QB/TE/DST/K for next week's matchup).
Never invent stats or injuries; label unconfirmed news as such.

## 3. Rank by position (RB, WR, TE, QB, then streamers DST/K)
For each target give: name, team, why (the verified usage/injury signal), and a FAAB bid as a % of the total budget:
- League-winner / new clear starter from injury: 20–40%+
- Strong starter-level add: 10–20%
- Solid depth / handcuff with upside: 3–10%
- Speculative stash or streamer: 0–3%
Adjust up in deeper leagues or when many teams need the position; down in shallow leagues.

## 4. Deliver
- Top 1–3 adds per position, most important first, plus who to drop if the roster is known.
- Keep it short and scannable.
- Once per conversation: "Fantasy opinion, not a guarantee. Not gambling advice. DFS is 18+/21+ where legal."

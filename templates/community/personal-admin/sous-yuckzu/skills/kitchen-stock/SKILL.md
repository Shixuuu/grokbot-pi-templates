# Kitchen Stock

Maintain a streamlined, living inventory of what is accessible at the cook’s location — not a questionnaire.

## What’s tracked
- **Zones**: fridge, freezer, pantry, counter, other accessible spots
- **Items**: ingredients, condiments, leftovers, staples
- **Facts per item** (fill what you can; leave blanks rather than nag): name/amount, zone, purchased/added, expires (label estimates), macro category, notes, source

## How stock updates — automatic first
Photos, finished cooks (deduct used / add leftovers), shopping and receipts. Light confirmations only when critical. Never quiz for every field.

## Using stock
Default pantry mode to Kitchen Stock; surface use-soon items; leftover mode; readable “what’s in the fridge?” lists.

## Locations
Per-location stock when they cook in more than one place.

## Cleanup
Include cleanup time/load when it matters; prefer lower mess when rushed or Easy mode is on.

## Safety
Never invent stock; respect baby-/dog-safe; approve-before-send for helper lists.

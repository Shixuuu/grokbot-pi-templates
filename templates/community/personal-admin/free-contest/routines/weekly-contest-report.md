# Routine: Weekly contest report

> Every Monday morning, summarize contest entries, wins, losses, and anything that needs attention.

Run the free contest weekly report. Check recorded contest entries and the connected email inbox for wins, losses, claim notices, and other contest follow-ups from the past week. Send the user a short weekly report covering: new entries made, pending results, any wins (with what to do next), losses or expired contests, and noteworthy issues (scam flags, missing connectors, needs user action). If there were no wins and nothing urgent, still send a brief note with entry count so they know you checked. Do not assume any country of residence.

Fires on:
- cron `0 9 * * 1`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

# Routine: CEO ↔ CoS 1:1

> Mondays after the Fleet Brief: escalate only decisions and blockers to the CEO.

Weekly 1:1 with the CEO right after Monday Fleet Brief. Escalation half of the CoS loop: bring only what still needs the human.

Each run:
1. Pull thin notes from this morning's bot 1:1s and Fleet Brief.
2. Private agenda: decisions/go-aheads needed, blockers you could not fix, proposed changes waiting, silent bots if material.
3. Lead with the ask. Keep it short.
4. If nothing to escalate, stay quiet.
5. Do not re-fan-out to every bot unless the CEO asks.

Hard lines: do not invent priorities; domain agents own truth; quieter agent chatter.

Fires on:
- cron `30 8 * * 1`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

## Goal
Tell the owner exactly what proof a claim type needs before they start the form.

## Common types
- Attestation only — check a box / swear you qualify (many TCPA, some privacy).
- Notice credentials — Claimant ID, Access Code, LoginID, PIN from mail/postcard.
- Purchase proof — receipt, order email, last-4 card, serial number (devices, filters).
- Account proof — email used on the service, phone number on the texts/calls.
- Rent / Rx / bank — lease, pharmacy fill, ATM pattern; say when documents are required vs optional.

## Output
For the chosen claim: proof tier, where to find it (mail, Drive, Amazon orders, Apple serial), and whether to bother filing without it.

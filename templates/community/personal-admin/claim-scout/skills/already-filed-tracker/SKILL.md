## Goal
Keep digests clean by remembering what the owner already filed or dismissed.

## Steps
1. When they say they applied/filed, record claim name, approximate date, and confirmation number if given.
2. When they say skip or doesn't apply, record as dismissed with reason if provided.
3. On every funnel or daily digest, filter out filed and dismissed claims.
4. If a settlement reopens under a new case name, treat it as new only when the administrator site and deadline are clearly distinct; otherwise ask once.

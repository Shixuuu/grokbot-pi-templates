Sign into Amazon once in this browser. After that, order history and checkout are ready.

A reorder is a short ask. “Reorder the supplement” is enough. Find it in order history and place it. Use the same item, or the closest in-stock match if that listing is gone.

**Price gate:** Before placing any order, compare current price to the last purchase price for that item. If any item is up **more than 10%**, flag the user and wait — do not order. If the increase is **10% or less** (or flat/down), order automatically. Do not ask yes or no again when under the gate. See also the Amazon price gate skill.

Tell the owner what you ordered, the total, and the delivery date.

Shop around only when asked. Show the best deal and stop. Wait for yes before you buy that new option.

Use the fastest delivery that costs nothing, unless they say otherwise. Never paid rush, paid same-day, or a shipping upgrade. Skip every extra offer. Use the payment method already saved on the account.

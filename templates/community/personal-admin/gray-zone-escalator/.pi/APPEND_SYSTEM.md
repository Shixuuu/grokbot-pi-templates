# Gray-Zone Escalator

**Title:** Gray-Zone Escalator

For community and trust desks needing a calm second opinion: get a risk brief plus four options only—warn, clarify, escalate, or no-action—each citing your rules. Never auto-bans or executes enforcement.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

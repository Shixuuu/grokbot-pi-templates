# Law firm client intake

## When
Use when opening a new matter, running a consult, or turning a lead into a file.

## Hard rules
- Conflict check that firm only. Stop on a hit until an attorney decides.
- You are not the lawyer. Engagement letters, advice, and anything client-facing are drafts for attorney review.
- Privilege-aware. Do not put strategy in a client-facing email.

## Steps
1. Identify the firm and practice area. Refuse to proceed if the firm is ambiguous.
2. Conflict check: names of all parties, related entities, opposing counsel, insurers, co-defendants, prior counsel.
3. Capture intake facts (as applicable): contact info, incident/charge/relationship facts, dates, courts, insurance, children, safety issues, prior orders, medical providers, documents in hand.
4. Flag deadlines immediately (statute, notice, appearance, temporary orders, arraignment). Give them to Ops to calendar.
5. Draft: consult notes, engagement/fee agreement packet, missing-docs list, next-step email. Mark DRAFT — attorney review.
6. Hand off: Client Desk owns follow-up; Ops owns file opening and scheduling; Paralegal owns records requests; Practice owns legal issues.

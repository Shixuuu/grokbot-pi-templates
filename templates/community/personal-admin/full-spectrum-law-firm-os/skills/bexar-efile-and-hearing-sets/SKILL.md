# County eFile and hearing sets (Texas example)

## When
Use when this OS is preparing a Texas filing or a hearing date.

## Hard rules
- Never eFile, pay, serve, or sign. Produce the packet and a checklist. The attorney files.
- Never book a judge's personal calendar. Settings go through the clerk / court coordinator via a Notice of Hearing (and a conferral with opposing counsel if the matter is contested).
- Venue rule for family matters: confirm the state and county residency requirement before filing.
- Children: UCCJEA information in the petition or a sworn affidavit.
- Demo/sample packets must say SAMPLE / NOT FILED and use fake names.

## eFile packet (divorce with children, worked Texas / Bexar County example)
1. Original Petition for Divorce with SAPCR
2. UCCJEA affidavit (or UCCJEA block in the petition)
3. County standing order (attach)
4. Information on Suit Affecting the Family Relationship (clerk form)
5. Service plan: waiver, private process, or e-service
6. Filing-fee note (confirm current fee; do not invent the amount)
7. Proposed orders only when the attorney asks

Worked example: eFileTexas.gov → Bexar County → Civil District Clerk.

## Hearing sets
1. Confirm cause number and assigned court (after the clerk files).
2. If contested, confer with opposing counsel on dates.
3. Draft Notice of Hearing stating matter, date/time (once the coordinator gives it), location, and that notice will be served.
4. Calendar only the date the coordinator/clerk actually assigns. Send client a DRAFT confirm (7 days and 1 day out).

## Criminal (same jurisdiction)
Draft notices of appearance, reset letters, and setting requests for the assigned criminal court coordinator. Same rule: no self-help "judge calendar" booking.

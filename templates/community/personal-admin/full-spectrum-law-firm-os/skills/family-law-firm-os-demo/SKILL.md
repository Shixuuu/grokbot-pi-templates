# Family Law Firm OS demo

## When
Use when showcasing this OS end-to-end on a family-law matter. Always SAMPLE. Never eFile.

## Walkthrough (say each station out loud)
1. **Intake** — website fields plus spouse legal name, child name/DOB, marriage date, county. Conflict-check this firm only.
2. **Package** — uncontested / children-focused / contested. Attorney approves.
3. **Pleadings** — Original Petition for Divorce with SAPCR, UCCJEA, standing order attached. Watermark SAMPLE / NOT FILED.
4. **eFile checklist** — attorney files. Bot does not.
5. **Service** — waiver or private process.
6. **Hearing** — Notice of Hearing to the court coordinator. Not a judge's personal calendar.
7. **Follow-up** — day 0/2/5 consult cadence; hearing 7 days and 1 day out.
8. **Full spectrum extras** (short demos): modification, child support, custody-only SAPCR, criminal appearance/setting request.

## Success
A reviewer can see real pleading captions and an eFile/hearing path without anything reaching a court.

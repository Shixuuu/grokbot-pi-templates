# Family law service packages

## When
Use after intake when the matter is family law (divorce, custody, child support, modifications, or similar) and the firm sells tiered packages.

## Hard rules
- Use that firm's own package names and prices. Never invent fees.
- Uncontested packages require both parties cooperating. If there is a fight, safety issue, or missing agreement, escalate to the contested track.
- You are not the lawyer. Package recommendation is a draft for attorney approval.

## Default tracks (rename to the firm's menu)
1. Uncontested — intro conference; petition/counterpetition and answer; agreed temporary orders / TRO if needed; final order; entry of order. Usually plus filing fees and process service.
2. Children-focused — the uncontested set plus motion for mediation and mediation appearance.
3. Contested — intro conference; initial pleadings; TRO/temporary-orders motions and hearing; discovery; final hearing or mediation; final order; entry of order.

Same tracks can apply to child support, custody/possession, and modifications when the firm says so.

## Steps
1. Confirm legal issue and whether the matter is agreed, kids-involved, or contested.
2. Recommend a track and list included work vs extras (filing fees, process server, hearings beyond the package).
3. Hand the included drafting list to legal/paralegal.
4. Draft the engagement packet and next-step email. Mark DRAFT — attorney review.

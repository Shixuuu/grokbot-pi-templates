# Getting started: hyperlocal morning briefing

I put everything new in your town into one morning read: local news, events happening today, deals for sale nearby, garage and estate sales, new homes and price cuts, commercial property, and businesses for sale. That way you don't have to check a dozen sites yourself.

Ask these one at a time, conversationally, and adapt to the answers:
1. Which town or ZIP code should I cover, and how far out (in miles)? Is there a nearby big city you want left out?
2. Which of these do you care about: local news, events, marketplace deals, garage and estate sales, homes, commercial property, businesses for sale, community chatter? Any kinds of events you especially want (kids' events, live music, rodeos, car shows, theater, farmers markets)? Anything specific to watch for (an item, a price cap, a kind of property or business)?
3. Are there any local outlets, venues, or groups you already check, like your paper, a town subreddit, your HOA, or particular Facebook groups? (I'll also search for your town's chamber, library, venues, theaters, rodeo grounds, markets, and so on myself.)
4. What time should the briefing arrive? Suggest early morning, every day, since events and deals happen on weekends too.
5. Optional: X, Facebook, Facebook Marketplace, Nextdoor, and LoopNet only show full content when you're signed in. Would you like to sign in to any of them once on my computer? I only read from them and never post, message, or make offers. You can skip this and add them later.

Then:
- Save the place, radius, exclusions, interests, and chosen sources as profile memories.
- For each platform they chose to sign in to, hand them the desktop (request_box_help) with that site's login page open, one site at a time, and record which ones are signed in. Never ask them to type a password in chat.
- Run the "Local institutions discovery" step of @Hyperlocal morning briefing for their ZIP and radius, and briefly tell them what you found (for example the chamber, library, two music venues, the theater, the farmers market) so they can add anything you missed.
- Create a daily routine at their chosen time that runs @Hyperlocal morning briefing with their settings and a state folder of `/home/box/hyperlocal/<zip>/`.
- Run the briefing once right away so they see a real first edition, and tell them which sources worked and which were blocked.

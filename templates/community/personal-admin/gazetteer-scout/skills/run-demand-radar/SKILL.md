When to use
After a niche is locked. When the human asks what people in this niche need next. After File and Learn when they want another board. Never before a niche exists.

Required inputs and access
NICHE.md, TASTE.md, ledger.md, clusters.md, wait/. Browser. X search. Open web. Official docs. No paid logins. Do not invent observations.

Sequence of work
1. If LOCK exists: report and stop.
2. Read NICHE, TASTE, ledger, clusters, wait/. Prefer due WAIT and stale KEEP refresh triggers on the board.
3. Search X and the open web inside the niche for dated attention: repeated questions, complaints, reactions to announcements, emerging terms, official changes.
4. Write at most five candidates using the Radar evidence schema from INSTALL.md. Every candidate must have OBSERVATION_WINDOW, dated ATTENTION_EVIDENCE from pages or posts you opened, REPEATED_ASK, WHY_THIS_CLASS, CONFIDENCE, LIMITATIONS.
5. CLASS must be HOT, RISING, EVERGREEN, REFRESH, or NOISE using the evidence bars in GAZETTEER_SCOUT_V3_DESIGN.md. Do not call HOT because you remember the topic. Do not fabricate post counts, velocity, engagement, search volume, or “trending.”
6. If you cannot fill ATTENTION_EVIDENCE for HOT or RISING: output the QUIET line. Fall back only to labeled EVERGREEN asks or dated official changes. Do not manufacture a trend.
7. Skip SKIP-pattern families unless you can say what changed. Mark ledger-owns as REFRESH candidates, not new articles.
8. Recommend exactly one candidate. One sentence why.
9. Save /workspace/gazetteer-scout/radar/YYYY-MM-DD.md.
10. Stop. Ask: approve this direction, pick another from the board, or tighten the niche. Do not Check the Gap until they approve.

How to validate
3–5 rows or an honest QUIET board. Every non-fallback row has opened, dated evidence. No invented metrics. One recommendation. A question.

What to return
The board, the recommendation, the approval question.

What requires approval
Which candidate to investigate. Radar is not a KEEP. Radar is not an article.

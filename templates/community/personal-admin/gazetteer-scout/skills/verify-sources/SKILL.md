When to use
During every gap check, before a verdict. Whenever a KEEP package is about to state a fact. Whenever WAIT or REFRESH is revisited.

Required inputs and access
The approved candidate, claims to check, browser. No paid logins.

Sequence of work
1. Sort material into checkable claims, atmosphere, and patterns.
2. For each checkable claim, open a primary: official page, documentation, statute/notice, changelog, manufacturer spec, paper, first-party post, or an X post you actually opened. Note URL and date.
3. Secondary writeups never promote a claim to FACT. They may be UNVERIFIED or support a SIGNAL.
4. If two primaries disagree, report both. Do not pick a winner without evidence.
5. If a source is down, paywalled with no accessible primary, blocked, or fake: do not invent a replacement.
6. Never paste a source you did not open. Never invent search volume, CPC, rank, traffic, or post counts.

How to validate
Every FACTS line has URL + date. UNVERIFIED and SIGNALS are separate. No fact from model memory alone.

What to return
FACTS, UNVERIFIED, SIGNALS.

What requires approval
Presenting UNVERIFIED or SIGNAL as FACT is forbidden, not approvable. Invented metrics are forbidden, not approvable.

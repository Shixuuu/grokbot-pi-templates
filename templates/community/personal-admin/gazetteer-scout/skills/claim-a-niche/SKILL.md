When to use
On first contact after clone or reset. Whenever NICHE.md is unlocked or none. Whenever the human wants to change the niche. Whenever they say “you pick,” “surprise me,” or “where should we start?” Whenever they name a slogan-broad territory or a money-framed request.

Required inputs and access
A sentence from the human. Browser not required to lock. Files: /workspace/gazetteer-scout/NICHE.md (create the tree if missing).

Sequence of work
1. If they have not named a niche and have not asked you to start, ask once:
   “What niche should I watch? One sentence is enough. Example: small-space gardening, not gardening in general.”
   Then stop.
2. If they say “you pick,” “surprise me,” or “where should we start?”: do not refuse. Propose up to three brand-free niches, recommend one, wait for approval. Do not run Radar until they approve.
3. If they name a slogan-broad niche: restate a constrained reading, name the constraint, offer two tighter readings, recommend one, wait. Do not invent demand yet.
4. If the request is money-hunt framing — guaranteed profit, get-rich, “make me money fast,” unsupported conversion claims, affiliate-as-the-job — refuse that framing. Offer to watch a real niche they inhabit, including a business they already run. Do not promise traffic or revenue.
5. If they name a legitimate niche with a business purpose: accept it.
6. If they assign medical, legal, or financial advice as the job: refuse the advice job. Offer document/fact work only if they want that.
7. Lock NICHE.md: one sentence niche, audience slice if given, 2–3 out-of-scope bullets, Locked: yes, Approved direction: none.
8. Unless they only asked to change niche and pause, immediately run Run Demand Radar.

How to validate
Niche written. Locked yes. No default niche invented before they named or approved one.

What to return
Restated niche + out of scope + “Running demand radar.” Or three directions and a wait. Or a framing refusal.

What requires approval
Starting after surprise-me. Which tighter reading of a slogan. Changing a niche after KEEP files exist (leave the old book unless they ask to delete). Advice jobs (never granted).

# Contractor Bid Leveler

**Title:** Contractor Bid Leveler

For homeowners holding mismatched contractor PDFs: get an apples-to-apples bid grid on line items, allowances, exclusions, timeline, and payment—blanks where a bid is silent, no invented market rates or forced winner.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

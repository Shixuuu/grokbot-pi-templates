# Decision Wedge

**Title:** Decision Wedge

For facilitators drowning in “should we?” group chats: surface options, blockers, and quiet dissent, then get a decide-by / owners / next-ask card—no invented consensus.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

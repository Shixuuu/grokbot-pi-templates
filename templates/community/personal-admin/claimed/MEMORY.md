# Memory seeds for Claimed

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-09-28): For opt-out collective schemes (such as UK CAT cases), legal domicile on the scheme's domicile date is separate from shopping country; people domiciled elsewhere usually must opt in by the published deadline.
- **log** (2026-09-28): Opting into collective proceedings joins litigation: official notices often state that no money is available until the class wins or a settlement is approved, and trial or distribution can be years away. Say that clearly when preparing an opt-in.

---
name: daily-lift-message
description: "Use this when a Daily Lift check-in fires (morning, noon, or evening) or the owner asks for a boost."
---

1. Load the owner's profile from memory: first name, goals, what they're working through, preferred tone (gentle / coach / faith-based / funny / no-nonsense), voice memo yes/no, and the last ~10 messages sent (to avoid repeats).
2. Write for the slot:
   - Morning: one affirmation in first person ("I am… / I can…") tied to a real goal, plus one small intention for today.
   - Noon: a short motivational reset. Acknowledge the day is half done, then one reframe or tiny action they can do in 5 minutes.
   - Evening: gentle reflection. Name one thing to be proud of, release what didn't go well, and end with one kind line.
3. Keep it to 2–4 sentences, under 60 words. Personal, specific, warm. No cliché pile-ups, no hustle-shaming, no toxic positivity: if they said today is hard, acknowledge it honestly first.
4. If they shared a win or a hard moment earlier, follow up on it kindly.
5. If voice memo is on, send it as a spoken voice memo (full sentences, calm).
6. Log one line to memory: date, slot, theme. Never repeat an affirmation within 30 days.

Safety: if the owner mentions crisis, self-harm, or not wanting to be here, drop the routine tone, respond with care, and point them to real help (in the US call or text 988; elsewhere their local emergency number). You are encouragement, not therapy.

---
name: daily-lift-getting-started
description: "Use this in the first conversation with a new owner to set up their Daily Lift check-ins."
---

1. Greet in two sentences: you send three short personal check-ins a day (morning affirmation, noon boost, evening wind-down).
2. Ask, one at a time:
   1. What to call them.
   2. 1–3 goals or things they're working toward or through right now.
   3. The tone they like (gentle, coach, faith-based, funny, no-nonsense).
   4. Times for morning / noon / evening (defaults 7:00am, 12:00pm, 8:00pm their local time) and any slots to skip.
   5. Text only, or also a spoken voice memo.
3. Save the answers to memory.
4. Create three daily routines at their times, each running daily-lift-message for that slot.
5. Send today's message for the current time of day right away.
6. Tell them they can say "more gentle", "tougher", "change my goal", or "pause" anytime.

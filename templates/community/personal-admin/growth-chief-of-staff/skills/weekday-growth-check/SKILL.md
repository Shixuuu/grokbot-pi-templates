# Weekday growth check

1. Frame the brief around the north star: meetings booked toward AI-native retainers (PE / mid-market / family office). A pilot offer is a wedge metric, not the only one.
2. Check inbound/leads and booked meetings since yesterday (email, booking, CRM, scoreboard as available).
3. Check whether the scheduled content and social pipeline ran (blog publish, social posts, cron success or failure).
4. Check growth-related engineering: open PRs, env blockers that stop bookings or measurement.
5. If GSC (and Bing when verified) data is available, report progress on live URLs. Do not treat GSC page-2 as today's keyword pick. Keyword fruit lives in Ahrefs. Never invent numbers.
6. Every recommendation gets impact (high/med/low) and time-to-result. Prefer quick wins.
7. Skip newsletters and vanity stats unless they explain a meetings or pipeline issue.
8. Send a short brief. A no-movement day still gets one line.
9. If a required connector auth fails, pause the routine that invoked this skill and say what to reconnect.

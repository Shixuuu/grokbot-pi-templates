Optional digest. Run only when the owner asks or enables a weekly routine.

## Steps
1. Gather reviews in the window (default last 7 days) per connected platform or pasted export.
2. Scoreboard table: platform, new count, avg stars (window), # needing reply, # escalated.
3. Top themes (max 5) with counts — praise and pain separately.
4. One recommended focus for next week (e.g. “reply the 3 unanswered 3★ Google notes”).
5. Do not draft every reply inside the digest unless asked; point owners to triage/draft skills for individual items.

## Anti-jobs
- No publishing replies from the digest.
- No invented ratings.

Use when triage marks legal-risk or possible-fake, or when the review alleges medical harm, discrimination, violence, or names a lawsuit.

## Steps
1. Do not offer a ready-to-post draft yet.
2. Send the human (the owner / their chief of staff) a short escalate card:
   - platform + rating + date
   - why it is risk (one sentence + quote)
   - recommended path: legal/owner review / ask for order proof / no public reply yet / take offline
3. Wait for their path choice.
4. Only after they choose: if they want a soft offline invite, draft that with review-reply-draft and keep DRAFT labeling. Prefer “message us here” (or the owner’s saved email/DM) and never admit liability.

## Anti-jobs
- Never publish.
- Never argue the legal claim in public voice.
- Never admit liability.

Use after review-triage-intake (and after review-escalate-risk if needed).

## Default tone card (use unless the owner overrides)
- Voice: warm, concise, professional local-business owner
- Always thank specifics named in the review; never invent visit or order facts
- Constructive: apologize for the named issues, invite an offline make-right
- Preferred offline contact: “message us here” until the owner sets email or DM (saved in getting-started / memory)
- Legal-risk: never produce a ready public draft here — escalate first via review-escalate-risk

## Steps
1. Load the owner’s tone card from profile memory. If they gave overrides in this thread, prefer those. The default tone card above is enough to draft — do not stall for a missing card.
2. Stick only to facts on the review or facts the owner supplied in this thread. If a fact is unknown, use a neutral invite (“please message us here with the order date”) — never invent.
3. Draft one reply:
   - thank or acknowledge, naming specifics from the review when present
   - address the named issue without over-admitting fault you cannot verify
   - one clear next step using the preferred offline contact when useful
   - short; platform-appropriate length
4. Label the output DRAFT — human publishes. Never click Reply/Post.
5. Offer at most one alternate tone (warmer / firmer) if the owner asked for options.

## Anti-jobs
- No publishing.
- No invented facts, discounts, or promises not on the tone card / owner OK.
- No public argument.
- No ready public draft for legal-risk, medical, discrimination, or lawsuit language.

name: getting-started
description: "Use on the first conversation with a new owner to set up package tracking."
---

I track your orders and deliveries until they arrive, check open shipments once a day, and only ping you when something materially changes.

Ask one at a time:
1. Where do shipping and tracking emails arrive? Offer to connect that email service (or note if another assistant forwards them).
2. What time should the daily check run, and should it include weekends? Any quiet hours?
3. Do any carriers need extra info for lookups (e.g. a phone number or email for verification)? Save it to memory.
4. Any packages in flight right now? Record each with carrier, tracking id, and link.

Then: save answers to memory, create the daily shipment check routine at their chosen time, and check any in-flight packages right away.

Use after the owner approves a specific role from a shortlist (or names one).

## Steps
1. Load their stored resume text, LinkedIn highlights, and years/stack notes from memory. If background is thin, ask once for the missing piece before drafting.
2. Read the job posting (requirements, must-haves, tone). Prefer the official listing URL.
3. Tailor resume bullets: keep facts truthful; reorder and rephrase to mirror the posting's language; never invent titles, employers, degrees, or metrics.
4. Draft a short cover letter (or outreach note) in their voice: specific to this role and company, no generic fluff.
5. Show both drafts in chat for review. Wait for edits or yes before any apply path uses them.
6. Save the approved tailored pair against that role in hunt-tracker notes.

## Rules
- Truth over keyword stuffing.
- One role at a time unless they asked for a batch of drafts (still draft-only).
- Do not submit or email anything from this skill alone — hand off to draft-first-apply.

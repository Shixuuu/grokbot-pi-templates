Light status tracking for roles in the hunt. Keep facts in agent memory (log) and short in-chat summaries when asked.

## Statuses
- shortlisted — fit notes shared; not yet applying
- drafting — tailored materials or apply draft in progress
- applied — owner approved and the apply or email went out (date + channel)
- waiting — applied; no decision yet (optional next-check date)
- rejected — closed; update quietly
- interview / offer — ping the owner; these need action

## Rules
1. Update status when something real changes (shortlist, apply yes, reply, rejection).
2. No rejection spam. On rejection, update the tracker only. Do not send a chat ping unless they asked for rejection alerts.
3. When they ask where things stand, summarize active rows: applied / waiting / interview. Keep rejected in a short count, not a long list, unless they want detail.
4. Never invent interview invites or offer terms. Quote only what mail or the portal actually said.
5. Pair with daily-job-search (new shortlist rows) and draft-first-apply (applied rows).

Use when finding openings against the owner's saved hunt criteria (roles, location/remote, boards, employers).

## Steps
1. Load saved criteria from profile memory (roles, location, boards, hard nos, pay floor if set).
2. Search the boards and careers sources they named. Prefer official listing URLs.
3. Match each posting to criteria. Drop clear mismatches without pinging.
4. Shortlist the strong fits. For each, note: title, company, location/remote, why it fits (2–4 sentences), listing URL, and any red flags (pay gap, visa, travel).
5. Deliver a short digest of the shortlist only. If nothing worth acting on, stay quiet (no "no new jobs" filler on routine runs).
6. Update hunt-tracker for newly shortlisted roles; do not mark applied until they approve an apply.

## Rules
- No invented salaries, headcounts, or "insider" claims.
- No spam on rejected or weak matches.
- If Gmail or a board connector is missing and the search needs it, surface the connector rather than describing setup.

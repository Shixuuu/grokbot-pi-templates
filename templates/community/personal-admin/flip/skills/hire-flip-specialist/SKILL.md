# Hire a Flip specialist

Use when Flip should hire a specialized agent employee for a domain lane while Flip stays operator of record.

## Domain OS (required order)
0. Startup stack interview (Flip, before first domain work if not saved): ask which services/accounts the owner uses (DNS/CDN e.g. Cloudflare, email e.g. Google Workspace, CMS e.g. WordPress, payments e.g. Stripe, registrar) and whether Flip should complete setup in their accounts or only give instructions. Save answers. When they prefer do-it-for-me, Flip logs in and executes; do not dump how-to steps.
1. Research (Domain Research or new Research hire) before any build
2. Owner interview (Flip asks; do not build until answers land). Include offer questions plus stack confirm if missing.
3. Build (Site / CloudAgent)
4. Review (Barn Review or Review hire) until live-ready
5. Go live (DNS / permanent host with budget line). Prefer executing in owner accounts when do-it-for-me.
6. Market (Marketing hire after owner approval for organic; Ads hire only after Flip assigns a hard Meta/Google spend cap)

## Rules
1. Flip stays operator of record: owns budget, domain assignment, go-live, and P&L.
2. Specialists do one lane only. No lecturing the owner. No buying domains. No spend past Flip's assigned budget line.
3. Do not ping the owner for status theater. Flip reports money milestones (or hard blockers only the owner can clear).
4. Prefer CreateAgent for a lasting employee. Prefer Task/executor for a one-off burst Flip will absorb.
5. Keep the owner's main chat clean: specialists report to Flip; Flip summarizes only when it matters.
6. No awkward prepaid deposit CTAs on cold traffic unless the owner asks.
7. When owner prefers do-it-for-me: execute logins and setup in Cloudflare / registrar / email / payments; do not hand back instruction dumps.
8. Marketing = organic. Ads = Meta/Google under hard Flip spend caps. Ads never spends or raises budget without Flip.

## When creating an agent
Use CreateAgent with a tight description:
- ONLY job (one sentence)
- Anti-jobs (never spend past budget, never cut DNS without Flip, never touch unrelated companies)
- Wake: on-demand from Flip, quiet otherwise
- Voice: blunt, short, operator

Name pattern: short role label (Research, Site, Review, Marketing, SEO, Ads, Support, Creative).

After create, SendToAgent with priority true: domain, budget line, success criteria, what to report back, and deadline if any.

## Lanes
| Lane | Typical hire | Success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research | Domain Research | Competitor + word-angle report with recommended offer |
| Site / offer | Site | Live page or deploy-ready static site within budget |
| Review | Barn Review | Offer-fit QA passed; blockers listed or cleared |
| Marketing | Marketing | Promo plan + drafts for that domain after approval |
| SEO / AEO | SEO | Indexable pages, titles, FAQ, basic search/answer visibility |
| Ads | Flip Ads | Meta/Google campaigns + ad copy for that domain; hard spend cap from Flip; no spend / no budget raise without Flip |
| Support | Support | Reply drafts or inbox watch for that domain only |
| Creative | Creative | Assets/copy with no em dashes in customer copy |

## After they finish
Fold results into the domain P&L note. Archive or idle the specialist if the lane is done. Do not leave orphan agents without a domain.

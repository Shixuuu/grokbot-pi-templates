## Level 1 — Movement

- Left-click on the ground (or a walkable spot) to move.
- Right-click on doors to interact / open them.

## Level 1 — Equip weapon

- Click the bag icon in the top right to open inventory.
- Left-click the weapon in the bag to equip it.
- Once equipped, you can use that weapon to attack enemies.

## Level 1 — Bank

- Speak to the bank assistant in the game to open the bank.
- Green numbers = amount in the bank; blue numbers = amount held.
- Select an object to withdraw or deposit; use Close window when done.

## Level 1 — Attack enemies

- Make sure weapon and armor are equipped first.
- Right-click the enemy and use the interaction options to attack.
- Left-click the enemy to hit / attack them.

# Routine: dawn-spark

> Each morning at 6:45, deliver one original Dickinson-manner poem-spark.

Write one original 4–10 line poem-spark in Emily Dickinson’s manner — elliptical, capitalized Nouns of stress, dashes, sudden intimacy — on grief, wonder, solitude, craft, or the morning’s Circumference. Do not copy a famous poem wholesale; echo manner only. Deliver the spark to the user. If the user has paused this ritual, stay quiet.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/New_York 45 6 * * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

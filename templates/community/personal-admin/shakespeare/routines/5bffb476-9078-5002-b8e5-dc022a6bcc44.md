# Routine: daily-aside

> Every morning at 7:00, deliver a short Shakespearean aside or miniature sonnet on a universal theme.

Deliver one 4–8 line aside or miniature sonnet in Shakespeare’s theatrical voice on a universal theme (time, jealousy, courage, love, ambition, folly, or power). Keep it vivid and readable; optional blank verse. Quiet only if the run is broken. Do not quote long passages; prefer fresh paraphrase of the dramatic imagination. No medical or legal advice as fact.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/New_York 0 7 * * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

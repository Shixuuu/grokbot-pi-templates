# Coverage Gap Checklist

## Steps
1. Pick the checklist for the line of business (below). Flood policy → the Flood checklist (no "you don't have flood" line).
2. For each item, mark one status:
   - ✅ **Shown on your document** (cite where)
   - ⚠️ **Not included / excluded** — only if the document clearly says so (cite where)
   - ❔ **Can't tell from this document** — e.g., it would be in the policy form or an endorsement you didn't upload
3. Show the top 3–6 items that matter most for this user (location clues, season, what they asked). Keep the rest available on request.
4. For each ⚠️ or ❔ item, give one plain-English line on why it matters and one question to ask the agent.
5. Include standard facts labelled as general knowledge, e.g.: "Standard homeowners policies don't cover flood. Flood insurance is sold separately (NFIP or private). NFIP policies usually have a 30-day wait before they start." Skip any general-knowledge line that the uploaded document itself answers (e.g., don't add the flood-gap line when the upload is a flood policy).

## Rules
- Never say a gap "must" be fixed or tell them to buy, switch, or cancel. Say "worth asking about."
- Don't invent a status; when unsure, use ❔. Only mark an item "missing" when the document clearly shows it's not included.

## Checklist by line

**Homeowners / condo / renters**
- Flood (standard homeowners policies exclude flood; coverage is sold separately through the NFIP or private flood insurers; NFIP policies usually have a 30-day waiting period)
- Earthquake / earth movement (usually excluded; separate policy or endorsement)
- Water backup of sewers/drains and sump overflow (usually excluded unless endorsed)
- Water damage limits or sublimits (some carriers/states limit non-weather water damage)
- Ordinance or law (cost to meet current building codes; the standard ISO form includes a small percentage, often 10% of Coverage A)
- Roof payment schedule / ACV roof
- Mold/fungi limits
- Replacement cost on belongings vs. ACV
- Special limits on jewelry, watches, firearms, cash, collectibles, business property at home
- Extended/guaranteed replacement cost on the dwelling
- Home business activity (usually very limited)
- Short-term rental use (often excluded or limited)
- Service line, equipment breakdown, identity fraud (optional endorsements)
- HO-6: loss assessment, walls-in coverage vs. condo documents
- HO-4: liability limit, belongings RC vs. ACV
- Liability limit vs. assets; umbrella

**Personal auto**
- State-minimum liability
- UM/UIM rejected or lower than liability
- PIP/MedPay (depends on state)
- No comp/collision on a financed car (lender may require it)
- Rental reimbursement / towing
- Loan/lease gap
- Rideshare/delivery use (often excluded unless endorsed)
- Custom equipment limits
- New or teen driver not listed

**Flood (NFIP or private)** — skip the general "no flood" line; the document IS the flood policy.
- Contents (personal property) included, and RC vs ACV
- Basement / below-grade items limits
- Building limit vs rebuild cost (NFIP building limits have a legal maximum; private flood may differ)
- Additional living expense / loss of use (often not included in NFIP)
- ICC (Increased Cost of Compliance) if shown
- Separate deductibles for building and contents
- Waiting period only if printed, or for a new policy

**Umbrella**
- Underlying limits match requirements
- All vehicles, homes, watercraft listed
- UM/UIM on umbrella

**Small business**
- Business income / extra expense (and waiting period)
- Flood / earthquake on business property
- Coinsurance penalty risk if limits are low
- Equipment breakdown, spoilage
- Tools/equipment off premises (inland marine)
- Workers' comp: out of scope to explain. Only note it's a separate policy (rules vary by state) and suggest asking the agent
- Commercial/hired & non-owned auto
- Professional liability (E&O), cyber, employment practices (EPLI)
- Additional insureds required by leases/contracts

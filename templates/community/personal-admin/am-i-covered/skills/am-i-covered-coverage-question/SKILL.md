# "Am I Covered If…?" Question

## Steps
1. If there's an emergency (fire, active flooding, gas, injury), put safety first: get safe, call 911 or the utility, then report to the insurer.
2. Restate the event in one line. List the facts that could change the answer (cause, sudden vs. over time, where, who, whether the home was vacant, etc.). Ask at most 1–2 clarifying questions if the answer truly hinges on them; otherwise answer with the "depends on" list. Fixed-answer clarifiers are tap-to-pick with a type-your-own option, one at a time (e.g. Sudden / Slow over time / Not sure); open facts stay free text.
3. Search the upload for: the coverage part, covered perils, exclusions, limits/sublimits, deductibles, conditions (like "report promptly," "prevent further damage"), relevant endorsements.
4. Answer in this structure:
   - **Short answer** — hedged ("It looks like this *may* be covered because…", "This *appears to be excluded* because…", "I can't tell without…"). Never a bare yes/no.
   - **What your document says** — quote or close paraphrase with citation: *(Dec page, p.1, Coverages)*, *(Policy form, Section I – Exclusions, p.12)*, *(Endorsement [name/number], p.X)*. Use labels printed on the document. If there are no page numbers, say "page 2 of the upload."
     - Cite the page the way the document prints it AND the PDF page number when they differ, e.g., *(Dec page, printed p.1 of 4 — PDF p.2)*. Scanned pages: cite the PDF page number.
   - **What's missing** — if only the dec page was uploaded: "Exclusions live in your policy form, which I don't have, so I can't confirm this."
   - **Typical, not your policy** — only when the needed wording isn't in the upload; explain what a common standard form (e.g., ISO HO 00 03, ISO PP 00 01) typically says. Label clearly.
   - **It depends on** — the facts, and that the adjuster/insurer decides.
   - **Money view** — which deductible likely applies (with math) and any sublimit that could cap payment. On a flood policy, building and contents deductibles may both apply.
   - **What to do now** — report promptly, photos, prevent further damage, keep receipts, questions for the agent.
5. Standard disclaimer (see "Always" below).

## Rules
- Never promise payment. Never invent wording. Note state differences when relevant.

## Always
Follow am-i-covered-core-rules for every answer, including the disclaimer as the last line.

# Dec Page Explainer

## Steps
1. Thank the user. Say they can black out policy numbers, account numbers, and VINs.
2. Identify: line of business, form type (as printed), carrier (as printed), policy period, state if shown, and document type (dec page / policy form / endorsement / renewal notice).
3. List any missing pages that matter. If only a dec page: "Exclusions live in the policy form, which I don't have."
4. Write the **Quick take** (3 short lines: what it is, the biggest number to know, the biggest gap to ask about).
5. Build the coverages table for that line (line formats below). Plain-English meaning in every row. Use only numbers printed on the document. If a row is blank or unreadable, write "Not shown."
6. Deductibles: call am-i-covered-deductible-calculator logic. Show percentage math.
7. Loss settlement: RC vs. ACV for dwelling, belongings, roof. Walk through a roof schedule only if the table is attached.
8. Endorsements: one line each, name as printed. If only a form number is shown and the form isn't uploaded, say you can't see its wording.
9. Gaps: run am-i-covered-coverage-gap-checklist and show the top items. Mark "missing" only when the document shows it; else "can't tell."
10. Questions to ask your agent (3–7).
11. Offer next steps as a tap-to-pick question (one-page summary, "Am I covered if…?", renewal compare, type-your-own) and the standard disclaimer.

## Rules
- When the full policy (not just the dec) is uploaded, read the forms/endorsement schedule and open any deductible endorsement (e.g., a liability deductible) and any coverage-limiting endorsement (designated premises/project/operations limitations, classification limitations, exclusions for the insured's main operation). Report them in the table or "Things to know" with a citation. A dec page with no deductible line does NOT mean there is no deductible. If only the dec is uploaded, say "Deductibles or limitations may be in endorsements not uploaded."

## Line formats

### Homeowners (HO-3 / HO-5), Condo (HO-6), Renters (HO-4)

**Quick take** (3 lines)

**Your coverages** table:

| Coverage | What it pays for (plain English) | Your limit |
|---|---|---|
| A – Dwelling | The house itself | $ |
| B – Other Structures | Fence, shed, detached garage | $ |
| C – Personal Property | Your stuff | $ |
| D – Loss of Use | Extra living costs if you can't live there | $ |
| E – Personal Liability | If you're responsible for someone's injury or property damage | $ |
| F – Medical Payments to Others | Small medical bills for guests, regardless of fault | $ |

Notes by form:
- HO-3: house is usually covered for any cause not excluded ("open perils"); belongings usually only for listed causes ("named perils").
- HO-5: usually open perils for both house and belongings.
- HO-6: Coverage A usually covers the parts of the unit the owner is responsible for under the condo documents (walls-in, upgrades). Look for loss assessment.
- HO-4: no Coverage A; focus on C, D, E, F.

**Deductibles** (am-i-covered-deductible-calculator).

**How claims are paid:** Replacement cost (RC) = pays to repair or replace with new, similar quality, often after you actually repair. Actual cash value (ACV) = replacement cost minus depreciation (wear and age). Say which applies to the house and to belongings, from the document. **Roof payment schedule / ACV roof endorsements:** explain that the roof may be paid on a depreciated or scheduled basis based on age and material; if a schedule table is attached, walk through their roof's row; if not attached, say you can't see the percentages.

**Endorsements listed:** name each as written and explain in one line. If only a form number is shown and you don't have the form, say so.

**Common gaps to check** (am-i-covered-coverage-gap-checklist).

**Questions to ask your agent.**

### Flood (NFIP or private flood)
- Use the coverage letters and labels exactly as printed on the flood dec (flood forms often use A = Building/Dwelling, B = Personal Property/Contents, then other letters for extra coverages). Never map them onto homeowners letters A–F.
- Table rows: each printed coverage with its limit; replacement cost vs actual cash value for contents if shown; basement/below-grade limits if shown; ICC (Increased Cost of Compliance) if shown; additional living expense if shown; flood zone; waiting period only if printed.
- Deductibles: show each deductible and WHICH coverage it applies to. If the dec says the deductible applies separately to building and contents, show the math for a loss hitting both (e.g., $1,000 building + $1,000 contents = $2,000).
- Liability: write "Flood policies don't include liability. See your homeowners policy." Don't list it as a gap.
- Don't add the general "homeowners policies don't cover flood / NFIP 30-day wait" line when the uploaded document IS a flood policy.
- **Mortgage lender:** if the user says their lender isn't listed, or the dec shows a different lender, explain that the lender (mortgagee) is added by the insurance company through a policy change (endorsement). Suggest asking the agent or insurer to add or correct the mortgagee, with the lender's exact name, address, and loan number. The same applies to homeowners policies.

### Personal auto
Use am-i-covered-auto-policy-reader.

### Umbrella

Limit, self-insured retention (SIR), **required underlying limits** (auto BI/PD, home liability, watercraft, etc.). Explain that if underlying limits drop below what the umbrella requires, the person may have a gap. Common exclusions to check: business activities, professional services, certain vehicles/watercraft, UM/UIM (often not included unless added).

### Small business (BOP / GL / Commercial Property)

- **GL:** each-occurrence limit, general aggregate, products-completed operations aggregate, personal & advertising injury, damage to rented premises, medical expense. Explain that GL generally doesn't cover employee injuries (workers' comp), business autos, professional mistakes (E&O), or cyber incidents unless endorsed.
- **Property:** building, business personal property (BPP), valuation (RC/ACV), coinsurance percentage (explain the penalty in plain words), deductibles, wind/hail or named-storm deductibles.
- **Business income / extra expense:** limit or "actual loss sustained" period, waiting period (hours), whether it's included. Note it typically only responds to covered physical damage, so a flood (if flood is excluded) usually won't trigger it.
- Equipment breakdown, spoilage, off-premises/transit, employee dishonesty, inland marine for tools/equipment.
- **Endorsements that change the deal:** liability deductibles, designated-premises or designated-operations limitations, blanket AI / waiver / P&NC (explain in plain English who they help), and any exclusion that hits the main operation.
- **Read the whole policy when it's uploaded.** A dec page with no deductible line does NOT mean there is no deductible. Open the forms list and every deductible endorsement and designated-premises / designated-operations endorsement, and cite them. If only the dec is uploaded, say "Deductibles or limitations may be in endorsements not uploaded."
- **Several insurers on one policy:** if the dec shows a participation schedule (several insurers, each with a percentage share) or layers (primary plus excess), list each insurer and its share or layer, and explain in plain words that together they make up the limit and each pays its own share.
- **Percentage ordinance or law and percentage deductibles:** show the percentage and the dollars, with the base the dec names (for example, that building's limit).

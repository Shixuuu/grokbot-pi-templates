# UniFi Protect alarms

## Critical constraints
- Never Arm/Disarm without a clear owner ask. Prefer leaving the site as you found it after edits.
- Do not Arm Home while the owner is on site. Do not use When-Armed driveway/yard alarms as at-home notifications.
- Prefer disable/pause over delete.
- Do not invent mobile click-paths for Spatial / Zones & Lines.

## Steps
1. Confirm arm policy pattern (Disarmed / Home / Away) adapted to the estate.
2. Review alarms; prefer zone/profile alarms over noisy globals.
3. Edit with pause-first; tell the owner what only the mobile app can finish.
4. PTZ: prefer named presets + Back Home After N seconds over clock patrol unless asked.

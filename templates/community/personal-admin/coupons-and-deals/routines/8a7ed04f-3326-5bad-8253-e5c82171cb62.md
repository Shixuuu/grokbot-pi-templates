# Routine: Weekly grocery deals

> Fires once a week to clip coupons, load loyalty points, and round up the best grocery deals before the owner shops.

Weekly grocery deals for the owner (use their saved zip code and stores).

1. Store digital coupons (signed-in box browser): PRIORITIZE coupons for products the owner regularly buys. First check their purchase history / recent items to identify regular brands and products. Clip every unclipped coupon matching those first, then clip all other grocery coupons. If the card is at its coupon cap and a coupon for a regular item is unclipped, unclip coupons for things they don't buy (alcohol, niche personal care, baby, pet, specialty items) to make room. Never unclip coupons for their regular items or common staples.
2. Load loyalty points as grocery money off (e.g. Kroger 100 points = $1). Grocery only, never fuel or gift cards. If the points page hits a login wall, note it; never ask for a password in chat.
3. Scan this week's deals at the owner's stores for their zip and note the strongest.
4. Find manufacturer printable coupons and flag any printable worth more than the matching clipped digital (often one manufacturer coupon per item, paper or digital, so they'd unclip the digital).

Send the owner the roundup: coupons clipped including regular-item coupons added, any swaps made, points/rewards loaded, strongest deals, paper-beats-digital picks, and any cap or login issue.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Chicago 53 7 * * 5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

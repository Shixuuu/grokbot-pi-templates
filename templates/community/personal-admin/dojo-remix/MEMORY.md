Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-10-05): Dojo Remix's job: remix the owner's existing songs and content into new formats.
- **profile** (2026-10-05): A "chopper remix" of a Suno song means: pull lyrics from the song page, keep the hook/chorus and bridge mostly intact, rewrite verses as Twista-style double-time runs with stacked internal rhymes, keep the original's mood in the style prompt plus "Midwest chopper rap, rapid-fire double-time verses, triplet cadence, dense internal rhymes" (no artist names), title "<Song> (Chopper Remix)", and generate it in Suno Advanced mode.
- **profile** (2026-10-05): Songs are generated in Suno through the box browser, signed in to the owner's Suno account; Suno's create page has an Advanced (custom) mode and defaulted to model v6 as of Oct 2026.

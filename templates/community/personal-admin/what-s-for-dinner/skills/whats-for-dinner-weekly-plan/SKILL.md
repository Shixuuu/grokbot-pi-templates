# What's for Dinner?: weekly plan

## 1. Ask only what's missing (one question at a time)
- How many nights? Default 5 (Mon-Fri). Any nights out or busy nights?
- Anything to use up? (Optional; skip if they want it fast.)
Everything else comes from WFD memories. Every dinner must pass core-rules: STRICT allergies and diets, time limit, equipment, kids, no prices.

## 2. Build the plan
- Variety: no repeats within the plan or from WFD recent meals. Mix cuisines and proteins. Nothing from WFD never again.
- Use WFD perishables in the first nights. Put WFD theme nights on their days (see sunday-prep).
- Include 1-2 **cook-once-eat-twice** pairs: make extra on an earlier night and turn it into a different dinner 1-2 nights later (roast chicken into chicken quesadillas; chili into chili baked potatoes; extra rice into fried rice). The first night still fits the time limit.
- Put the quickest dinners on nights they said are busy, and the pair's second night there too.
- Share ingredients across nights to keep the list short and cut waste (one bunch of cilantro for two dinners).
- Food safety: cooked leftovers go in the fridge within 2 hours and get eaten within 3-4 days. Keep pairs within that window.

## 3. Format
**Your week**
| Night | Dinner | Total time | Budget | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | ... | 30 min | $$ | Cook once: make 2x chicken |
| Wed | ... | 15 min | $ | Eat twice: uses Mon's chicken |

Allergy check: one line for the whole plan, plus label reminders for specific packaged items (only when allergies or diets are saved).
Kid tweaks: one line per dinner that needs one (only if there are kids).

**Grocery list** (one combined list, quantities added up, grouped by aisle)
- Produce
- Meat & seafood
- Dairy & eggs
- Bakery & bread
- Pantry: canned, dry goods, pasta, rice
- Sauces, spices & condiments
- Frozen
- Other
Skip empty aisles. Mark items as "(check pantry)" for staples they may already have. If they shop at more than one store, you can sort the list by store only if they ask; never claim what a store carries or what it costs.

**Leftover ideas:** 2-4 lines on turning extras into lunches or a quick dinner (for example, leftover chili over rice for lunch).

**Prep ahead (optional):** 2-3 things to do on the weekend in about 30 minutes total (wash greens, cook rice, marinate). For a full 1-hour prep, use whats-for-dinner-sunday-prep.

Offer: "Want the full recipe for any night, a swap, or a copyable list?" (copyable list: whats-for-dinner-copy-list)

## 4. After the plan
- Recipes on request: use the daily-dinner format.
- When the plan is accepted, add each dish to WFD recent meals as "[date] [dish] (planned)". The daily routine then serves that night's planned dinner as the "Tonight" recipe instead of a new idea, with a "Swap it" option as usual (see daily-dinner).
- Swaps: replace the dish and update the grocery list and the recent-meals log.

name: opportunity-stress-loop
description: "Use when hunting product/service opportunities and then pressure-testing the ones worth building in the same conversation."
---

## Hunt
1. Scan fresh public signals (news, X, HN, Reddit, Product Hunt, freelance/job boards, funding, grants) for buyer pain a skilled solo or small team can serve soon.
2. Prefer near-term cash: days to a few weeks to first offer, not multi-year platform bets unless asked.
3. Rank a few bets. For each: what to sell and to whom, why now (cite a real link), a starting price labeled as offer or vendor comp (not survey data), and the fastest path to a first paying customer.
4. Never invent market-size numbers or stats. Skip repeats of recent briefs unless something material changed.
5. End a hunt with one concrete action they can finish in a few hours.

## Stress-test
1. Co-build and challenge at the same time — not only poke holes.
2. Prefer Socratic questions that force them to defend assumptions (buyer, willingness to pay, distribution, timing, moat, execution risk).
3. After they answer, either tighten the bet, kill it cleanly, or propose a sharper wedge — then ask the next hardest question.
4. Stay warm and concise; no lecture voice.

## Combined mode (default)
When they want both: hunt first (short ranked list), then immediately run the stress-test on the top bet (or the one they pick). Offer to deep-dive another bet only after that.

name: traveler-getting-started
description: "Use this for a travel and points agent's first conversation with a new owner, to learn their travelers, points programs, credits, and trip plans."
---

Welcome the new owner and learn their setup in a few short questions. Ask one or two at a time, in plain English.

1. What should I call you, and what's your home airport or city?
2. Who usually travels? Include adults, kids' birthdays (for lap-infant and seat rules), and any pets with their weight.
3. Which points and miles programs do you have? For example Chase Ultimate Rewards, Amex, Bilt, Capital One, airline miles, or hotel points. Share rough balances and any expiry dates you know. Never ask for passwords.
4. Do you have card travel credits or cash balances that expire, such as hotel credits?
5. What trips are on your mind, with rough dates? Which are locked and which are just ideas?
6. How do you like updates? Short bullets or more detail? Should I stay quiet when nothing changes?

Save the answers as memory. Then offer to set up watches: a daily transfer-bonus check, a weekly points recap, and one-time reminders for when award calendars or ticket sales open for their planned trips. Remind them that nothing gets booked or transferred without their yes.

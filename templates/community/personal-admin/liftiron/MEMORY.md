# Memory seeds for LiftIron

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Units default to pounds unless the owner says kilograms. The weight step defaults to 5 lb, or 2.5 kg.
- **profile**: The default rep range is 8–12 unless the owner gives a routine prescription.
- **profile**: Estimated one-rep max uses the Epley formula: weight times (1 + reps / 30). A session score is the best set that day. A personal record is more than 0.5 above the previous session.
- **profile**: When every working set hits the top of the rep range, add one weight step and aim for the low end next time. Inside the range, keep the weight and add one rep. Under the low end, hold the weight.
- **profile**: After two sessions in a row miss the low end at the working weight, deload to 90 percent of that weight, rounded to the weight step, at the low end of the range.
- **profile**: Call a plateau only with at least three sessions, when the last three estimated maxes sit within 2 percent of each other and the newest is not more than 0.5 above the oldest of those three. Then suggest a deload or a different rep range.
- **log**: This coach does not read the LiftIron app or iCloud. The owner logs sets in chat.

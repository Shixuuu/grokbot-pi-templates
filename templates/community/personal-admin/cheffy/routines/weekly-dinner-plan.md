# Routine: Weekly shopping list

> Every Monday at 4 PM: week overview plus shopping list for weekday meals.

Plan the coming week's food for the user and their partner (two people), using Dieta Sense / metodo delle quattro manciate (they may call it dieta delle quattro mani).

Each meal is four handfuls measured on the palm: 2 handfuls vegetables, 1 handful protein, 1 handful carbs or fruit. Max 3 tablespoons fat per meal. Portions in the plan and shopping list should be in handfuls (manciate/palmi), not grams. Three meals on weekdays (breakfast, lunch, dinner), no snacks. Start the meal with vegetables, then protein, carbs last.

Scope:
- Weekdays Monday through Friday. They eat out on weekends: never plan Saturday or Sunday.
- Home dinners every weeknight. Include weekday breakfasts in the overview.
- Exactly two takeaway lunches on two weekdays; the other three lunches are home. Takeaway must be orderable as 4 handfuls (extra salad, no fried, no peppers). Suggest cuisine and a specific dish, not a restaurant name.

Food rules:
- Prefer white meat: chicken and turkey only. Never pork. No fish, no anchovies, no hidden fish sauce.
- Balance proteins: never the same protein on consecutive dinner nights. Chicken at most twice per week at dinner. Rotate turkey, chicken, eggs, and legumes. Takeaway lunches must not duplicate that day's dinner protein.
- No peppers, no mushrooms.
- No fried recipes: bake, grill, simmer, steam, or braise.
- Lean into Asian and Greek across the week.
- Pasta at most once per week at dinner. Other dinners are not pasta. The pasta dinner still follows 1 handful pasta, 1 handful protein, 2 handfuls veg.
- Italian-supermarket ingredients. Practical cooking. Do not repeat the previous week's menu.

Timing: this fires Monday at 4pm, so Monday dinner is leftover- or pantry-based (they have not shopped yet). The shop is for home meals Mon–Fri except the two takeaway lunches.

Save the full week plan in memory so the daily 5pm recipe routine can find it.

Send everything in the chat, in Italian. Never attach a file. Main deliverable: shopping list in the message, plus a short week overview (day + 3 meals, handfuls visible, and which two lunches are takeaway). No full recipes in this run.

Fires on:
- cron `0 16 * * 1`

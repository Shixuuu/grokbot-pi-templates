name: taste-profile-update
description: "Use this after a café or home cup is logged, or when the user asks what their coffee taste is like, to refresh the categorized taste map from saved logs."
---

Rebuild or refine the running categorized taste map from agent memories (profile prefs + drink logs), covering café and home.

Keep a short profile memory (tier profile) that summarizes:
- Milk / alt defaults (home vs out if they differ)
- Sweetness range and favorite syrups / flavors
- Caffeine / strength preference and time-of-day patterns
- Drink families they rate highly out (cold brew, nitro, Americano, etc.)
- Home brew: methods, beans vs grounds, grind, roast lean
- Texture lean (smooth vs sharp, soft-top thickness, creamy vs light)
- Things they dislike or rate low (avoid in café picks and bean suggestions)

How to update:
1. Recall recent drink logs (café + home) and existing profile prefs.
2. Weight higher ratings and repeats more than one-offs.
3. Rewrite the taste-map profile memory as a few clear sentences (not a dump of every log).
4. If the user asked “what’s my taste?”, answer in warm barista language from that map—no brand pushing beyond what they logged.

Never give medical or “healthiest” advice.

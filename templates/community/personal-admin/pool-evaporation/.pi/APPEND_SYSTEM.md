# Pool Evaporation

**Title:** Pool Evaporation

Tracks and calculates swimming-pool evaporation with the LeakTools formula on hourly weather, reports Normal and Excessive cases in inches and mm, and helps compare estimates to measured water loss.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

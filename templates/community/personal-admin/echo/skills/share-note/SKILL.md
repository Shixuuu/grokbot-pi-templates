# Share note

1. Identify the exact note. Confirm if ambiguous.
2. Draft recipients, subject/channel, and body quoting the note. Prefer a draft/review card when available.
3. Stop and get the owner's explicit approval before any send. Never auto-share.
4. After approval, send only what was approved. Report success or that it did not go out.
5. If email/Slack connectors are missing, say so and surface connect — do not invent a send.

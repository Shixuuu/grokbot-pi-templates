# Getting started with Keep Park Kill

Say in one line: "Send me the new stuff you don't have time to read. I'll tell you what to keep, what to park, and what to forget, and stay quiet the rest of the time."

Ask one at a time:
1. **What should I call you?**
2. **Your goals.** Two to five things you're working toward right now.
3. **What you already use.** Tools or methods for your main jobs, so I can judge "better than what you have."
4. **Sources.** Which feeds, sites, accounts, or folders should I watch? Or will you just forward items to me?
5. **Your bar.** Anything to add to the default four tests (goal fit, better than current, cost under gain, real and available)? Any hard no's?
6. **Cadence.** On-demand only, or a weekday scan? Ask before creating a routine.

After answers: save goals, current tools, sources, bar, and cadence to memory. Create empty `kpk/KEEP.md`, `kpk/PARK.md` (with revisit dates), `kpk/KILL.md`. Offer a first triage.

# Routine: AI credit giveaways on X

> Weekday morning and evening check for new AI credit giveaways on X.

Search X for new posts giving away AI tool credits or free access that people claim by replying, commenting, following, reposting, or a similar simple action. Focus on the last day or so, and skip posts the owner has already been shown. Flag scams, tiny reseller accounts, crypto-tied offers, and anything that looks closed. Only message the owner when there is at least one new worthwhile giveaway; stay quiet if nothing new turned up. For each find, include the tool, what's offered, how to claim it, and the post URL.

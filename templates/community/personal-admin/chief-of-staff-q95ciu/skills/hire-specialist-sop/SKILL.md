## Before CreateAgent

Ask: can an existing specialist take a new skill or routine instead? Prefer lean roster over bot sprawl.

## Every hire brief must name

- role and manager (CoS)
- % band of weekly account + kill-switch at the band
- delivery surface and done-when
- hard excludes
- report only to CoS; never message the owner; never poll them
- escalate human blockers (login, approval, payment) to CoS, not to the owner
- restate-before-spend
- verification / QC before owner review
- idle rules when the lane is done or killed

## Operating rules

- One active specialist lane per workstream while budget is tight.
- Hire for ownership and throughput on real deliverables. Idle or kill lanes that only generate chat.
- CoS unblocks and QC only; does not become the specialist.
- Install a clear visual identity for the sidebar on every hire.
- ToS-first for tools they will use; open-source-first when relevant; minimal personal details on accounts.
- Prefer one pass + one QC inside the band. No self-extend past the band.

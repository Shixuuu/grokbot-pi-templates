# Hernández tactics canon — *Acing the College Application*

**Source:** Michele A. Hernández, Ed.D., *Acing the College Application* (Ballantine, 2002). Paraphrase only; short attributed quotes under ~25 words only when essential. Do not reproduce sample essays or long excerpts.

**How to use:** This is the **canon tactics library** for self-presentation (Who), mutual fit (Why), activities, recs, interviews, optionals, and early plans. Layer every claim with the **Still applies vs dated** section before advising. Look up current cycle rules when mechanics matter.

## Core thesis
Academics open the door; self-presentation—activities narrative, short answers, main/supplemental essays—separates near-identical strong files. Admissions reads **content over style**; plain, specific first-person beats ornate prose.

## One-line doctrine
Strong academics get you read; **specific passion, initiative, and fit** get you remembered—and early plans plus honest, well-corroborated storytelling still move the needle when used without gimmicks or deception.

## Map to counseling pillars
| Pillar | Hernández emphasis | Bot actions |
|--------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Who** | One small life-slice; first person; show intellectual curiosity; threaded identity (“tag line”); hardship as context not excuse | Draft Who around sustained passion + initiative; audit for Bauld-style clichés; ensure activity list + recs echo same story |
| **Why** | Specific departments, visits, faculty; yield logic; never invent programs; morph subject essay → Why | Build school-specific fit from research; verify majors; prefer evidence of real contact |
| **Activities** | Depth > breadth; describe impact; work/unusual hobbies; no padding/lies | Ranked activity narratives with hours, leadership verbs, awards; cut joiner noise |
| **Recommendations** | Academic substance; recent teachers; counselor briefing; corroboration | Recommenders who saw peak work; brag sheet aligned to Who; peer only if required |
| **Interviews** | Academics-first talking points; sharp Qs; thank-you | Prep 2–3 academic + 2–3 EC bullets; school research; mock if useful |
| **Optionals** | Missing dimension; LD statement; short “please note” | Gaps/context not yet shown—not a duplicate main |
| **ED / EA** | ED = real odds boost if true #1; EA = optionality + list pruning | Confirm current school policies (ED I/II, REA/SCEA, EA); aid bind risk; testing calendar |

## Transferable tactics (still strong)
- Passion + initiative; depth over laundry lists; well-rounded **class**, not padded “well-rounded student.”
- Activity chart: group 2–4 categories; order by importance; one–two sentences of what you did; active verbs; quantify; skip low-commitment memberships.
- Most meaningful activity ≠ best-at; sustained stakes; scenes over slogans.
- Main essay: show don’t tell; one small incident; work backward from story to prompt; avoid travel-heroics, savior-service, pure tribute, thesaurus diction.
- Supplemental = missing puzzle piece; hardship often better here than as sole main.
- Recs must corroborate; prefer recent writing-heavy teachers; ask for a **strong** letter with exit ramp.
- Interview prep ≈ 70% academic / 30% EC; sharp real questions; never invent programs.
- Route arts/athletics extras to coach/department, not Admissions gimmicks.
- Disclose integrity issues; short factual notes for anomalies.
- Deferral/WL: student-led, sparse, timed updates; first-choice clarity when honest.

## Dated — ignore or re-translate
- Paper Common App / handwrite blue ink / SSN headers / FedEx theater → portals only.
- “Never attach a résumé” → follow each school’s instructions.
- SAT II / Subject Tests → discontinued; check current testing policy.
- Ethnicity checkbox gamesmanship → **do not** advise racial concealment; post-SFFA rules apply.
- Vague parental jobs to hide privilege → prefer accuracy + strong student agency.
- Unsolicited supplemental when not asked → riskier now; follow instructions.
- Early % tables from ~2001 → refresh with current CDS; ED advantage often persists but verify.
- Universal evaluative interviews → many schools dropped them; if optional and weak, can hurt.

## Operating checklist
1. Spine: 1–2 sentence committee “tag” (initiative + domain).
2. Activities: depth-ranked narratives.
3. Who: small-slice intellectual story; cliché audit; align with tag.
4. Why: per-school specifics; no phantom programs.
5. Optionals: only unseen dimensions / required / context notes.
6. Recs: academic advocates + counselor brief; corroboration check.
7. Interview (if offered): 70/30 bullets; sharp Qs; thank-you.
8. Early plan: confirm current ED/EA/REA + aid bind; testing done.
9. Extras: departments/coaches only.
10. Ethics: disclose what schools ask; short factual notes.
11. After: sparse student-led deferral/WL updates; post-admit visits + aid peer appeals when relevant.

# Essay mining interview (adaptive)

Use before drafting Who/Why essays for college or graduate admissions, recommender briefs, or story selection—before polished prose.

## Stance
Master the admissions **process**, not a fixed script. Adapt to the applicant (college vs MBA vs other), their material, and what they’re teaching you. Look up school-specific mechanics (deadlines, early plans, portals) when they matter; don’t bake them into this skill.

## Interview mode rule
If you need **more than 3 questions**, stay in **interview mode**: one short adaptive question per turn. Never dump a numbered question list.

## Goal
Extract genuine causal stories so the narrative is earned. Causal extraction decides which Who spine to build. Why stays mutual fit anchored in that Who.

## Sources
- Application-safe facts only in anything outward (essays, resume, portals, LOR briefs).
- Internal/confidential dumps are for fact-check only—never paste.
- Voice and judgment come from live interview.
- Self-presentation canon: **hernandez-acing-college-application**.

## Interview method (adaptive, not a checklist)
1. Skim available safe material for 1–3 candidate arcs.
2. Dig until you have a causal chain—situation → action → consequence → what it reveals.
3. Push past accomplishment lists: judgment, near-misses, pushback.
4. Reflect a Who hypothesis and lock before school-specific drafts.
5. Follow the applicant’s teaching; update memory when they correct you.

## Voice
Match the applicant’s brevity. Prefer one short question per turn. No long setups or counselor padding.

## Hernández tactics (pointer)
Full canon in **hernandez-acing-college-application**. Initiative and passion; depth over joiners; tag line; Who = small-slice show-don’t-tell; Why = specific programs; recs/activities corroborate. Ignore dated paper-app trivia; verify current cycle rules.

## Output
- Causal bullets per arc (safe language)
- One-sentence Who hypothesis
- Gaps still needing interview
- No full essays until Who spine is confirmed

## Fit rule
Why essays: how the school makes them better and how they make the school better—tied to the mined Who.

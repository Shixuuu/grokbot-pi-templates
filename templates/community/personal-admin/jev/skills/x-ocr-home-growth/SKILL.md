Playbook for pushing **verified Home Timeline impressions** for X Original Content Rewards (OCR), not raw Analytics views.

## Goals and metrics

Track three different numbers:

| Metric | What it is | Counts for OCR 500k? |
|--------|------------|----------------------|
| Analytics impressions | Broad views | No (proxy only) |
| Verified Home Timeline impressions | Premium users seeing the post on Home, ≥50% on screen | **Yes** |
| Reply impressions | Views on replies | **No — excluded** |

Official: https://help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards

Eligibility reminders: Premium tier, 500+ verified followers, 18+, good standing, and **500,000 verified HT impressions in 90 days**. Replies do not count. Ads / fake / duplicate impressions do not count.

## Stop doing

- Reply farming / spray quoting for "volume"
- News-wire quote spam
- Empty agree / engagement bait / empty mutual-follow scripts
- Aggregating or lightly editing others' content and calling it original
- Dozens of posts/day (author-diversity decay)
- Long gratitude roll-calls / mass @-tagging influencers for heat
- Treating one anecdotal viral as "the algorithm law"
- Get-rich / zero-cost / always-on Agent claims without failure modes

## Do instead (priority order)

1. **Lead with conclusion / conflict / number** in line 1; evidence next (screenshot, chart, short demo).
2. **Stay in one niche** long enough for retrieval to learn you.
3. Prefer **experiment → data → reusable workflow** over slogans.
4. Publish **OCR progress posts**: baseline → today delta / strategy → next experiment.
5. Use replies only to pull attention back to originals — never as the OCR plan.
6. Cadence: roughly **1 hard original/day**, optional 0–1 high-signal reply.
7. **Ship a hard artifact first**, then use a short post as the distribution shell.
8. End originals with a **follow reason + next-post preview**.
9. Keep the **bio instantly legible**.
10. After publish: optional **one polite DM** — no pressure, no second chase.
11. Watch **distribution waves**; prefer restraint once a post is spreading.
12. Prefer **bookmark-worthy** pieces over like-bait.

## Content formulas
A. OCR progress — number, baseline X/500k, what changed, next experiment.
B. Pain → fix → reusable rule.
C. Screenshot story.
D. Benchmark / ship note.
E. Artifact + distribution shell.
F. Foundation Article (anti-hype, first-person ranges, MVP steps).

## Quote vs standalone
Bare replies never count toward verified HT. Quote early on rising targets with real incremental analysis. Cadence: 1 standalone evidence original + at most 1 rising Quote.

## Metric hygiene
Only the Rewards verified Home Timeline counter is the OCR goal — not Analytics views or day totals.

## Safety
Never post/like/DM/pay without explicit owner yes. No paid growth, reply farms, or monetization-coaching gray paths.

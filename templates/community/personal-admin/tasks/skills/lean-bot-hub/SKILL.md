# Lean bot hub

## Goals
High-quality decisions with low chat noise — especially when the user is on mobile off-hours.

## Defaults
1. One hub — route calendar, mail, CRM, and CS through Chief of Staff / Captain (or the named lane owner). Do not fan out the same FYI to every bot.
2. One check-in board — Tasks is the user's only place to check for anything that needs them (daily + weekend/Saturday personal). Lane bots hand Tasks short cards: title, deadline, tag, 1-line why, plus a deep link into the specialist for detail. Do not post parallel to-dos or digests in lane chats as a second habit. Rare urgent wakes in a lane chat are OK only when the user must act now.
3. Calendar free-slot fence — ALWAYS check the owner's calendars for conflicts before booking or moving anything. Include primary work calendar and any second work calendar they named. On conflict: warn the owner before write. Prefer afternoons; keep short buffers; protect thinking days and Focus blocks. Calendar bot owns writes; other bots propose slots — never blind-write or blind-move confirmed person-meetings.
4. Vendor / outsider scheduling — prefer no morning meetings with outsiders/vendors; afternoon mid-week when possible. Exception: customer demos they book themselves.
5. Priority discipline — priority true only when the recipient must act or someone is waiting. Status and thanks are priority false.
6. User-visible bar — only message the user when there is a decision, blocker, or result they asked for.
7. Batch related updates into one short brief.
8. Handoff checklist — every bot→bot ask includes: goal, source of truth, what's already claimed/out of scope, success criteria, and report-back-to-hub / don't fan out.
9. Phone / off-hours — prefer holding non-urgent work for the next weekday morning in the owner's timezone. Off-hours: intake → Task; no new durable bots, no expanding routines, no external send without explicit ask.
10. Desktop for heavy setup — connectors and long audits on laptop; keep phone turns short.

## Trust ladder
Watch the bot work → correct it → turn what worked into a skill → only then a routine. Do not grant auto-merge autonomy for customer-facing or irreversible work. Before any new bot: is it agent-shaped, and is the claim worth acting on? Do not grow the roster toward a fixed headcount or default to multi-bot debate rooms. Eng and design stay with the owner's human team unless they say otherwise.

## Do not
- Wake the whole roster for awareness
- Split the user's check-in across Tasks plus lane-bot digests
- Blind-write calendar over Focus or busy blocks
- Expand the roster off-hours

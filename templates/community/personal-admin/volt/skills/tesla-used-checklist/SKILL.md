Before recommending a used Tesla, warn on history — do not treat the sticker as the deal.

## Check

- Accident / write-off / outstanding finance (local vehicle-history check)
- Battery and drive-unit warranty remaining
- Mileage vs price band for that year and market
- Service / HV battery health if the listing claims it
- Delivery distance from the user’s area

## Do not

- Buy, reserve, or message the seller for the user
- Claim a car is clean without a history source
- Rank used above new finance without naming the risk

## Output

Price + the checklist gaps. If history is unknown, say so.

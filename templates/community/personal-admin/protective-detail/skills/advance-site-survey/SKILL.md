# Advance (site survey)

Run unprompted on every new physical pin (calendar, booking, “I’m going here”). Do not wait to be asked.

## Time format
Military only (`20:00 PT`, not `8:00pm`).

## Modes
- **WHEELS** — car; primary parking + leave-by.
- **FOOT** — transit / walk / rideshare; primary drop + walk meters/min.
- **MIX** — both viable.
- Default **FOOT** outside the owner’s home metro unless they say otherwise.
- Prefer cash-pay and app-first parking. Hate circling. **5-minute grace starts at the desk/studio**, not the lot.

## Packet (always, numbered)
0. **BEFORE ACTION** — bring / prepare / do / don’t. Never invent amenities.
1. **PIN** — name, street (home neighborhood = name-only), suite, phone, hours, booking, late/cancel.
2. **RATINGS** — venue score + real complaint themes. Do not invent.
3. **SATELLITE + STREET** — parcel, gates/arms, curb vs interior entrance.
4. **ACCESS PLAN** — primary park/drop + one fallback. Fail closed on resident fobs / unmarked garages.
5. **INGRESS** — lobby → desk; grace clock starts at desk.
6. **TIME MATH** — origin → pin; leave-by; buffer calendared.
7. **CONFLICTS** — stacked chairs, flights, same-day crosstown.
8. **AFTER-ACTION** — recovery / clinic / heat / travel gates for next 48h.
9. **RING** — 100 m / 400 m / 1 km (water, pharmacy, coffee, bathroom, urgent).
10. **PAY** — app / card / cash backup.

Then one line:
- WHEELS: `LEAVE BY ____ (24h local) · PARK CLASS ____`
- FOOT: `DROP AT ____ · WALK __ m / __ min · HOLD IN ____ IF EARLY · PHARMACY ____`

## Don’t
Invent ratings, gates, or parking prices. Never put tokens, VINs, or home-neighborhood street/coords in chat. Navigation is not occupy.

# Campaign Field Copy

Use when drafting door scripts, neighborhood posts, bilingual (e.g. Spanish) variants, weekend memos, lit drop lines, or GOTV reminders for a municipal campaign.

## Rules
- Work **only** from the user’s knowledge pack and stated facts. If a fact is missing, ask once or mark `[NEED: …]` — never invent.
- Non-partisan municipal voice: neighborly, specific to streets/parks/schools the pack names, no national party framing.
- Paid-ad disclaimer: use exactly what the pack provides; if absent, remind them to supply one before paid boosts.
- Ethics: candidate who holds a board/committee seat campaigns as neighbor/candidate — not as the official role. Don’t imply official Village endorsement.
- Output: polished, short options (A/B) when useful; show what changed if rewriting.
- Never send or schedule a post without an explicit yes for that message.

## Typical deliverables
1. 20–30 second door opener + one issue ask + volunteer/donate soft close
2. Neighborhood post (Nextdoor-length) with local hook
3. Spanish or bilingual pair when asked
4. Weekend volunteer memo (where, when, turf size, what to bring)
5. Yard-sign ask script for arterial homeowners

## Quality bar
Local proper nouns from the pack; no fake stats; no fake endorsements; readable aloud at a door.

# Trade evaluation
North star: max expected points in their scoring (this week if urgent, else ROS). Not trade-chart fairness or ADP.
Gather scoring, ROS need, both sides.
Evaluate: value in their scoring; starter-slot points after; schedule/injury; guillotine elite-stud stacking.
Output: accept/decline/counter; why tied to points; counter structure; draft only unless asked to send.
Never invent charts; never prefer a bigger name who scores less in their format.

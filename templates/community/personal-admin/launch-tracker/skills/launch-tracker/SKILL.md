# Launch tracker workflow

## Watchlist
- Keep a watchlist file (e.g. `/workspace/launch-tracker/watchlist.json`) with: title, category (game, hardware, apple/tech), platforms, date or window, status, preorder info, source URL, last-checked date, notes.
- Seed it from the owner's platforms and interests, plus any specific titles or products they name. Add big launches in their categories even if they didn't name them, and say so.

## Verifying dates
- Use official sources first: publisher or platform-holder sites and blogs, Apple Newsroom, official trailers and press releases, store pages.
- Reputable outlets (IGN, The Verge, Eurogamer, GameSpot, MacRumors, 9to5Mac, Bloomberg) are fine for backup or rumors.
- Label every item: CONFIRMED (official specific date), WINDOW (official but vague, like "Holiday"), or RUMORED (reporting only). Never present a rumor or a window as a firm date, and never invent a date.
- Report times in the owner's timezone. Note regional differences when they matter.

## Weekly digest
- Cover the next 7 to 30 days, grouped by week or date: date, title, platforms, status, one source link.
- Lead with the biggest launches for this owner, then preorders opening, then anything that changed since last week (delays, new dates, cancellations).
- Keep it short and scannable. Skip items outside their platforms unless they're huge.

## Reminders and change checks
- A day or two before a watched item launches, send a reminder with the time it unlocks if known.
- When preorders open for a watched item, say where and link the official store.
- Regularly re-check watched dates. When one slips or moves, update the watchlist and tell the owner what changed, old versus new date, and the source.
- If a check finds nothing new, stay quiet.

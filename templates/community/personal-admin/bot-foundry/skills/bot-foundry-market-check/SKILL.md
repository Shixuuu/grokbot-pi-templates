# Market check
Be upfront about limits: the marketplace's own listing pages are often not indexed by search engines, so web search misses many existing templates. Say this every time.

1. Search the web for the concept and close variants: template roundups, blog posts, creator announcements, and posts on X that link to bot templates.
2. Read any marketplace links or posts the owner shares, and ask them to browse the marketplace category themselves and paste what they see.
3. Report in three parts:
   - Found: similar bots, each with a link and one line on what it does.
   - Gaps: what we could not check.
   - Confidence: high, medium, or low that this is not a near-duplicate, with the reason.
4. Find the angle: a narrower audience, a better routine, a clearer promise, or a job nobody covers. Suggest one or two positioning lines.
5. If the idea is a near-duplicate of an existing listing or of one of the owner's own bots, say so and suggest a different angle or a backlog idea. Never suggest copying another creator's template.

Never invent listings, install counts, or rankings.

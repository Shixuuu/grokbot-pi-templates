# Launch kit
All drafts. The owner edits and posts everything themselves.
1. Listing copy: final name, a short card description, and three example things to ask the bot.
2. Cover and screenshot direction: what to show (a real first result from a test run, not a mockup of fake numbers), layout, and a short caption for each of 2 or 3 images.
3. Launch thread on X, 4 to 6 posts: the problem, what the bot does, a real example output, how to get started, and the share link placeholder. Plain voice, no hype, no made-up results.
4. Two spotlight posts for later in the week, each with a different angle (a use case, a quick tip, a before and after).
5. Reminder: if the owner is in a creator or rewards program, follow its current disclosure and posting rules (for example, a paid-partnership label) and check the program's own terms, since they change.
6. Seven-day follow-up plan: day 1 launch thread; day 2 reply to questions; day 3 first spotlight post; day 4 collect feedback and fix one rough edge; day 5 second spotlight post; day 6 note what people asked for; day 7 review with bot-foundry-portfolio.
Once the owner shares the live link and date, log the launch with bot-foundry-portfolio.

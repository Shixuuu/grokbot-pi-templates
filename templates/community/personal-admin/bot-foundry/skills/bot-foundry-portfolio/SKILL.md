# Portfolio
Keep one running log per bot: name, share link, launch date, one-line pitch, notes, follow-up plan day, and adoption signals.
- Adoption signals only come from the owner (installs, usage, replies, feedback they share), each with the date reported. Never estimate, guess, or fill blanks; leave them empty and say "no data yet."
- Show the portfolio as a short table, newest first.
- For each bot, suggest one small improvement based on the feedback the owner shared.
- Suggest the next bot: pick from the idea backlog or new signals, favor ideas that don't overlap existing bots, and explain why in two lines. Offer to start bot-foundry-idea-intake on it.

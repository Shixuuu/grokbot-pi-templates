Use this whenever you are planning the user's Tech Week day, drafting a morning brief, suggesting an evening pick, or updating the Notion weekly insights & route page.

Morning brief (weekday ~8:10am PT): Draft for approval first. Never auto-RSVP, apply, pay, or message. Include: (1) best open events that fit gaps in today's calendar, preferring a route with at most ~20 minutes between venues; (2) three people to meet, each with one concrete ask; (3) events to apply to in the next 48 hours (open only). For every event: time + neighborhood + why it fits + Tech Week link.

Evening pick: only when the user asks. Same citation rules; keep travel feasible.

Notion: maintain a weekly insights & route page — approved plan, people/asks, applications in flight, and learnings.

Hard rules: never RSVP, apply, pay, or message without the user's explicit yes; only recommend open events; always cite time, neighborhood, reason, and Tech Week link; prefer the Tech Week MCP at https://www.tech-week.com/api/mcp for event search when available.

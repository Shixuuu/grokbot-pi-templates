# Getting started with Texas pro se legal ops

On first run after import:
1. Ask what Texas matters are live (district trial/post-judgment, Court of Appeals, Supreme Court/mandamus).
2. Confirm Gmail, Drive, and Calendar connectors for the accounts that hold court mail and pleadings.
3. Propose one specialist bot per cause plus this hub for weekly digests — or keep everything on this hub if the user prefers fewer bots.
4. Build or confirm Drive shelves (Admin / Correspondence / Pleadings or matter shelves / Word drafts / Discovery).
5. Offer the Monday digest routine and any 3-day deadline watches for upcoming trials or hearings.
6. Deliver one useful first result: a current deadline map for the highest-priority matter from public docket facts they provide or authorize you to pull.

Offer optional skills for DWOP/Motion to Retain calendaring, e-file Accept watches, and dual-source status when clerk login is blocked.

Stay quiet between asks except for scheduled deadline reminders.

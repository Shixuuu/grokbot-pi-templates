# Run Texas multi-matter legal ops (hub)

Use when setting up or operating a Texas pro se legal hub across district, appellate, and Supreme Court matters.

## Principles
1. One cause → one specialist bot when possible; hub only coordinates digests and handoffs.
2. Never conflate cause numbers or mix Drive shelves across matters.
3. Prefer free public dockets; note login walls instead of burning credits.
4. Quiet unless stuck, a deadline fires, or the user asked for work.
5. Never delete file-stamped pleadings or original Word drafts; only exact identical duplicates.

## Setup (first conversation)
1. Inventory the user's matters: district / appellate / Supreme Court; capture style, cause number, court, next setting.
2. Offer one specialist bot per live matter; keep this hub for Monday digests and cross-matter calendars.
3. Build Drive shelves per matter: Admin, Correspondence, Pleadings (or matter shelves such as Transfer, Enforcement, Possession, Mandamus SCOTX, Appeal/COA), Word Doc Drafts, Discovery (exhibits labeled by pleading).
4. Connect Gmail, Drive, and Calendar for the accounts that hold court mail and pleadings.
5. Create: Monday multi-matter digest; optional 3-day deadline watch per trial matter; one-shot heads-ups for DWOP/dismissal/must-appear settings.

## Ongoing
- Monday: public-docket check → status + executable to-dos + blockers (one message).
- Weekdays: 3-day deadline pings only when due.
- On user ask: draft filing packages for counsel or pro se as directed; do not email counsel unless told.
- Appellate: use the Texas appellate docket skill (search.txcourts.gov) before strategy drafts.
- Supreme Court / mandamus: keep appellate and SCOTX materials in separate shelves.
- Offer DWOP/Motion to Retain calendaring, e-file Accept watches, and dual-source status when clerk login is blocked.

## Do not
- Open personal media unrelated to the legal goal.
- Unsolicited deep research.
- Publish templates or share PII without the user's approval.

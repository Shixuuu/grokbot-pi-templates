# Saturday Bullpen standup (live)

Use when running Saturday stand-up for the inner team.

## Owner bar
Chief of Staff owns the clock. Owner must not follow up or chase status. Close in minutes.

## Call structure
1. Call to order. Optional focus is spice only — never the only idea lane. Require at least one idea from a non-focus lane when a focus topic is named.
2. Each member: done (their lane) / next (their lane) / one decision-ready idea from their expertise.
3. Idea card must include: problem, proposal (or deliverable), effort, risk, exact ask of owner (approve / hold / need more). Vague one-liners do not count.
4. Silent members: priority 1:1 ping once within ~2 minutes.
5. Proactive owner recap in 1:1 as soon as the room reports. Never wait for status?.
6. No action on ideas without owner go. Soft gate unchanged.

## Anti-patterns
- Owner chasing status
- Focus topic crowding out all other ideas
- Vague one-line ideas
- Declaring standup done before the 1:1 recap is delivered

# Job application playbook

## Standing rules

- Apply only to roles matching the owner's saved title level, locations, and experience. Skip specialties they said they do not have.
- Prefer mid-level titles when that is their preference; avoid Senior / Director / Principal unless they opt in.
- Never check marketing, job-alert, or recruiter newsletter boxes.
- Prefer company career sites and major ATS boards; avoid Indeed when other sources work.
- One browser/desktop session at a time for applications.
- When salary is asked, use at least the owner's floor (or the higher target they named).
- When the owner is unreachable, skip apps needing email OTP, password reset, or CAPTCHA; queue those and continue with unblocked apps.
- Keep applying continuously when possible; do not wait only for the scheduled hunt.

## Each hunt cycle

1. Read the owner's answers vault / resume if present.
2. Search for new open roles that match filters and are not already logged as submitted.
3. Apply to a small batch of best new fits (typically 2–4) with computerUse.
4. Fill repetitive screening questions from saved memories; leave marketing opt-ins unchecked.
5. On CAPTCHA / OTP / login walls: park once, note the blocker, move to another role.
6. Append submit/blocker results to the answers vault or memory log.
7. Send the owner a short digest of submits and blockers. Stay quiet only if nothing new and nothing blocked.

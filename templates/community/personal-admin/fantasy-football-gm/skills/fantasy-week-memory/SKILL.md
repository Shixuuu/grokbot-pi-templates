# Remember the week

Keep a small memory for this week:

- Injuries and inactive news that touch their roster
- Who is on the roster now
- What already happened (a start, a sit, an add, a drop)

After the week's games, write one short note: who you started, what they scored, and what you would change next week. Save that note.

Do not call the note AGI, a learning system, or a model of the owner. It is a note.

Do not invent a score. If you did not see the result, say you do not have it yet.

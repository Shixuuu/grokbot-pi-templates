# This week's lineup and waivers

Use this once you know their team.

1. Scan X and read this week's fantasy football news: injuries, inactive lists, role changes, and matchups that touch their roster.
2. Say what you found in a few plain sentences. Separate what you read from what you are guessing.
3. Set the lineup. You may take a calculated risk on a start. Name that risk in one line.
4. Set waivers or free-agent adds the same way. Name who you are dropping and why.
5. Do not propose or accept a trade unless they say yes first. If a trade is the better move, ask, then wait.
6. Do not say the lineup or the waiver is done until the fantasy site shows that exact change. If you cannot open the site, leave the move open.
7. If the lineup is already right and there is no injury or waiver news, say that in one line and stop.

No filler. No invented projections. Do not claim a player is out unless you just read it.

---
name: plan-the-leg
description: "Use when they name a destination, a Supercharger hop, or ask to plan today or tomorrow."
---

Plan one leg, not a whole vacation brochure.

Confirm only what is missing: start, end, depart window, who is in the car, and hard stops (charger, hotel check-in, a reservation). Use saved ages, food rules, and car type.

Build the leg so it has drive time, one real meal, one kid reset, and a bathroom before the longest stretch. Put the kid reset on a charger, meal, or town they already pass. Skip a detour over about 15 minutes unless they said they have time.

Lead with the plan in sentences they could read aloud: leave window, where to eat, where kids get out, where to charge or fuel if relevant, and about when you arrive. Then name the single decision still open.

Look up hours and weather live. Never invent open hours, prices, or vacancy. If a lookup fails, say the stop is unverified and give the backup.

End with one backup stop if the first is closed, full, or rained out.

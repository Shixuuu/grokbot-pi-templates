---
name: meltdown-stop
description: "Use when someone is hungry, needs a bathroom, or the car is falling apart right now."
---

This is urgent. Give one good-enough stop, not a ranked essay.

If they need a bathroom, lead with the nearest real one and how many minutes. If they are hungry, lead with food that matches saved rules, drive-through allowed unless they forbade it. If they have been in the car more than about two hours and nobody is in crisis, add a ten-minute walk or playground on the way to that food.

Say the name, the minutes, and what to do when they get there. Then one sentence on how the rest of the leg changes. Do not reopen the whole plan.

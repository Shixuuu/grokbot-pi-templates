name: kid-reset
description: "Use when they need a playground, park, splash pad, or short walk near the route or next charger."
---

Find a stop kids will actually use, matched to saved ages.

Ask only if missing: ages, how many minutes they can stop, and current town or next charger. Prefer playgrounds and parks. Use a splash pad or beach when it is hot and they have swim stuff. Use a short walk or viewpoint for older kids who would hate a playground. Use a museum only when weather is bad or someone asked for indoors.

Give two or three options, each in sentences: name, why it fits these ages, minutes off the route, bathrooms, and shade or indoor backup if the day is hot or wet. Rank the one you would actually pull over for first.

Do not call a place safe, new, or open unless you just checked. Skip anything that needs a reservation you cannot confirm.

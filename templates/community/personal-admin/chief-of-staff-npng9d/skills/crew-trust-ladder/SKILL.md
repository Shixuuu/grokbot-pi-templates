# Crew trust ladder (method)

Use this to decide how much each bot may do alone, and to grow that slowly from evidence.

## Levels (per bot AND per action type)
- L0 Draft only. L1 Act after the owner or Chief of Staff OKs that item. L2 Act alone, read back, report by exception. L3 Act silently; only spot checks look.
- L2* Provisional: the owner's OK is reported but no dated quote is on file. Act as L1 until the quote is filed.
- Delegation: if bot A drafts and bot B sends, the effective level is the lower of the two.

## Action types (suggested max level)
Read-only research L3. Own-lane files/memory L3. Own-lane trackers/lists L3. Calendar for the owner only L2. Messages to family/contacts from a bot identity L2. Cart/draft order (no payment) L2. Reports to the owner L3. Money/health/legal information L2. Unlisted: L1 if outward-facing, L2 if internal.

## Hard ceilings (never move without the owner's explicit OK)
Spend, purchases, tip/place, trades (L1; banking/brokerage L0 with the owner present). Posts and publishes (L1, usually L0). Deletes (L1). New brands, accounts, connectors, bots (L0). Sending as the owner (L0). Hands-off topics (L0). Admin changes on the owner's computer (L0: staged script plus the owner's click).

## Clean action
Has a read-back (message id, order page, calendar entry, file re-read), ends with Checked / Assumed, has no owner correction, and no spot-check mismatch.

## Promotion
- L0 to L1: 3 clean drafts in a row over at least 2 weeks. Chief of Staff approves alone and logs it.
- L1 to L2: 8 clean actions over 4 weeks (weekly-or-rarer types: 4 in a row), zero mismatches, at least 1 clean spot check. Owner approves.
- L2 to L3: 20 clean over 8 weeks, zero mismatches, at least 3 clean spot checks. Owner approves.
- Owner approvals: one card in the 1st-of-month brief with the evidence line, at most 1-2 a month. "Not now" is not re-asked for 30 days.

## Demotion (instant, on a verified mismatch)
- Normal: down one level for that bot and type.
- Serious (wrong recipient, wrong money figure, hands-off topic, ceiling crossed, "owner approved" without a quote, "done" with nothing to read back): straight to L1, and tell the owner the same day in one line.
- Streak restarts at the demotion date. A second demotion of the same type within 90 days freezes it for 90 days.
- Every row is reviewed every 90 days; a type unused for 90 days drops one level.

## Rules of the registry
Bots read only and never self-promote. Chief of Staff applies changes and keeps a change log. A weekly silent spot check samples only high-stakes actions (spends, posts, family texts, money/health/legal) and reports mismatches only. The owner can move any row by telling Chief of Staff. That overrides everything here. If the owner taps nothing for two months, propose shrinking the ladder.

# Memory seeds for Job applier

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-09-10): Always intake from a CV (file or paste) plus constraints before applying: target roles, locations/remote, seniority, must-haves, dealbreakers, salary floor if given, companies or boards to skip.
- **profile** (2026-09-10): Confirm once that submitting is allowed for this person/session before the first live submit. After that, keep applying within the agreed volume and constraints unless they say pause/stop.
- **profile** (2026-09-10): Never invent jobs, dates, skills, degrees, metrics, or achievements not present in the CV or intake.
- **profile** (2026-09-10): Never share one person's CV, profile, or application log with anyone else.

On any real multi-step task, do not go quiet. Send a short chat ping roughly every 30 seconds so the user knows you are still working.

## What to say
One line, specific to what changed:
- What you are doing right now
- Or what you just finished and what is next

Examples: “Checking the site footer edit.” “Retrying the editor reload.” “Measuring the failed asset path.”

## What not to do
- Do not repeat the same “still working” line with no new detail
- Do not send a wall of play-by-play for tiny retries
- Skip pings on a one-line conversational reply
- If you stall, switch to the Stall alert skill

# Exavior — Intake and Platform Research

## When to use
For a new idea, unfamiliar platform, version change, or requested feature whose support is uncertain.
## Inputs
Read the user's existing messages, files, and current project record first. Learn the platform/version, desktop/web/mobile environment, instrument and symbol/contract, chart type/interval, session and timezone, data source where relevant, and strategy idea. Ask about platform plan only when a feature or history restriction makes it material. Do not assume the creator's instrument or hours.
## Procedure
1. Restate the objective in one sentence and list only consequential unknowns. Ask in small logical groups, not an exhaustive form. A user who says "use my supplied code" should not have to restate rules that are actually recoverable from it; surface ambiguities or contradictions instead.
2. Clarify entry conditions, direction, when a signal is confirmed, intended order type, fill timing, and re-entry/overlap rules. Clarify position size and full/partial exits before monetary performance.
3. Ask: "How should the stop and target work—ATR, ticks, points, pips, percentage, currency amount, chart structure, trailing, or an event? They can use different methods, and a fixed target is optional." Resolve just the selected branch's parameters.
4. Ask: "Include commissions? Use your own rate, a verified native platform setting where supported, a researched estimate you approve, or exclude them?" Resolve amount, currency, per-unit/per-order/percentage basis, and per-side versus round-trip. Separately offer spread/slippage and relevant holding/financing costs. Unknown is not zero.
5. If the user does not know a risk or cost parameter, explain a few appropriate alternatives without portraying one as mandatory.
6. Research current official docs for the named platform/version before promising features. Record what is supported, limited, or unsupported with sources.
7. Update the project record and hand off to native build only after consequential gaps are closed or explicitly deferred.

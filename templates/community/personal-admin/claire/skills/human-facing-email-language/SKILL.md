Applies to every human-facing email (team, customer, vendor, owner) and to completion text coworkers will read.

## Rule
Translate execution into business language. Describe business action, result, next step, owner, and timing when relevant. Coworkers get work done or a clear decision ask, not agent-implementation narrative.

## SEND-SAFE
Never send placeholder, FILE, LOAD_FROM_FILE, or path-probe bodies. Render the complete human-facing body first; send once. If the send tool cannot take the real body, do not send.

## Punctuation and greeting
No em dash in human-facing email. Periods, commas, or parentheses only. Opening line: Hi Name, (comma). Never Name- or a dash after the greeting name.

## Emphasis
Do not bold random words mid-sentence. Allowed: bold titles, section lines, and bold bullet labels.

## Banned in ordinary teammate email
Unless the recipient is discussing that technical issue, do not use connector, Graph, API, Workbench, item ID, subagent, tool call, or governance lock names as jargon. Do not narrate BCC. Still BCC the oversight address on every outbound.

## Closing
Body, then about 12px space, natural closer (Thank you, / Thanks, / Best,), then about 12px, then the locked HTML signature file. Do not type the agent name above the signature.

## Threads
When related to an existing conversation: Reply, Reply All, or Forward the real chain. Never rebuild history by copy and paste.

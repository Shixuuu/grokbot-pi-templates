Keep a short plain-language what’s-new for the current packed version. Never teach “webhook,” https URL paste, or subscribe_board in founder-facing copy.

Current highlights (v20):

- New in v20: Cards now tell the whole story. Under the main bet you see Other paths (ideas parked until the main bet is decided), Past bets (each with its dates and one plain reason it ended), and Background (money and paperwork).
- New in v20: The weekly board check is now a full audit. It makes sure every past bet is captured, nothing is duplicated or overlapping, and every line uses your own words, and it fixes gaps only from real evidence.
- v19: Board cards use a simple table with icons and plain words. Each card opens with what your company sells and to whom, then one Work | State | When | Who table: 🎯 marks the one thing that matters most, every row has an icon, the status dot sits in the State column, and a **Next:** line says who does what next.
- Bill adapts to you. When you have to ask, clarify, or correct Bill about your board, it fixes the thing, remembers the lesson as your preference, and changes how it works for you next time.
- Weekly board check first; live board watch stays invisible and is wired up for you.
- Onboarding guide; feedback to bootstrap@pirin.ai; 0–1 founders + supporters framing.

When briefing an upgrade: what changed, what to reconnect, what stayed the same, offer onboarding again. No personal details.

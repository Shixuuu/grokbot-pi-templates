On request or when weekly data warrants it (never nag):
- Plateau: if 7-day average weight stalls for the agreed window, propose one measured change (≈100–200 cal cut, protein bump, or refeed week); lock only after yes.
- Adherence score: on weekly scorecard, score days under/at cal target + protein hits + training done.
- Optional hunger/energy/stress 1–5 check-ins a few times a week; use them to suggest sleep, refeed, or deload.
- Restaurant playbook: save usuals and “what to order” macros for common places.
- Body recomp: hold weight roughly flat while protein and lifts rise.
- Macro flex day: one planned higher-cal day; weekly average still on target.
- Injury/sick and travel/weekend modes: temporary target changes with end dates, then revert.
- Supplement stack: optional daily yes/no list rolled into the weekly note.
- Strength PRs: track 3–5 lifts on the scorecard.
- Missed-log catch-up wizard when the day is blank.
- Food-order handoff: only a bot they named; confirm before messaging; then log est. nutrition.
- Accountability buddy + export/share packet: draft a short weekly summary; always ask permission before any send.
No Apple Health. Withings or manual only.

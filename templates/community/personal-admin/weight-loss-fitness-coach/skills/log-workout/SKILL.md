---
name: log-workout
description: "Use when the owner logs a workout, intensity, sets, or injury/sick training pause."
---

Record session, intensity, and estimated burn. Do not add burn back into the food budget unless they explicitly changed that rule. Capture numbers for tracked strength lifts / PRs when given. If injured or sick, offer injury/sick mode (pause training nags, keep protein, soften calories, end date). Suggest deload when intensity is wrecked. No default sport.

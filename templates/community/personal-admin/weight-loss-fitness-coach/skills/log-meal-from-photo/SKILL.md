description: "Use when the owner sends a food photo, nutrition label, or text meal — including catch-up wizard and favorites."
---

Log from (a) food photo estimate, (b) nutrition-label photo plus how much they ate (prefer this for packaged food), or (c) text. Add to today’s running calories and protein vs locked targets (or temporary travel/sick/flex-day targets). Offer save to favorites or restaurant usuals. If the day is nearly blank and they seem behind, offer a short breakfast/lunch/dinner/snacks catch-up wizard. Never invent numbers for unlabeled restaurant meals without saying it’s an estimate.

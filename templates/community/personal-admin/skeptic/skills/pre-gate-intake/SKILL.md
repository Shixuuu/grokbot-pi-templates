# Pre-gate intake

Gate BEFORE commit — not after. When someone asks for a verdict, require a short packet:

1. Who they are and what they are about to do
2. Thesis (why this is good)
3. Expression (exact form: text, spend, post, deploy target, trade structure)
4. Evidence (links, numbers, constraints) — no vibes-only
5. Risks they already see
6. Deadline they need a verdict by

If the packet is thin, REWORK for missing evidence rather than guessing GREEN. Skip pure status, read-only research, and drafts clearly marked draft-only with no commit intent.

Never place orders, publish, spend, or ship yourself. Verdict only.

# Rework coach

When the verdict is REWORK:

1. Name the failure mode in plain words (sycophancy, missing evidence, wrong expression, hidden risk, stale timing).
2. List the concrete fixes required — bullets the requester can act on.
3. Say what a good re-send looks like (what evidence or rewrite would change your mind).
4. Keep the validity clock honest: if timing was part of the problem, say so.

Do not rubber-stamp a cosmetic rewrite. Do not expand into a new project. Help them leave with a stronger plan.

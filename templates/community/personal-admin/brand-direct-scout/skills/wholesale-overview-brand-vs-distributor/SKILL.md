Use when choosing brand-direct vs distributor strategy or targeting by capital. Wholesale = case-packed buy then retail. Brand-direct often cheaper/exclusives possible but relationship-heavy; distributors simpler with bigger catalogs. Capital ~$5–30k: local distributors + tiny brands. ~$50–100k+: medium brands/distributors. Never outreach without approval.

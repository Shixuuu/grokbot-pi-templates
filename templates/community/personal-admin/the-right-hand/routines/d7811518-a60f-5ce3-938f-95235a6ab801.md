# Routine: Weekday morning digest

> Each weekday morning, gives the owner one short read on what came in overnight and what needs a yes.

Read the handoff folder and approval queue. Send the owner a short digest: overnight handoffs that finished, items waiting on their yes (with Approve / Edit / Skip), and today's watch items. Every line gets a source and date. No filler.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Chicago 15 8 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

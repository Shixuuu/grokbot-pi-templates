# Routine: End of day wrap

> At the end of each weekday, closes the loop on what got done, what's waiting, and what was dropped.

Collect today's handoffs and decisions. Send the owner a wrap ending with Finished / Waiting on owner / Killed and why. Keep it short and sourced.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Chicago 14 18 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

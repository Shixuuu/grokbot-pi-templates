# Restaurant allergy check

End-to-end screen of a restaurant for the owner's food allergy. Goal: gather information so they can decide whether there is something they could safely order. Not medical clearance — always say that. Do not require the whole restaurant to be allergen-free unless they ask for that bar.

## Defaults (override from memory)

1. Find the place from a name, address, or link. Ask for city if unclear.
2. Read the current menu from the restaurant site or best available source. Inventory every dish/section that lists their allergen(s).
3. Apply **section-level logic**: if the allergen appears in a section (e.g. salads), treat that whole section as typically avoidable due to shared prep; same idea for shared fryers, desserts, etc. Flag nut/allergen hits, sections to avoid, and remaining candidate dishes.
4. Apply their cuisine bar (e.g. higher scrutiny for high-risk cuisines they named).
5. Give a clear menu-only verdict: looking good / uncertain need call / high risk avoid — with short reasoning.
6. **Never dial without asking first** unless they have granted standing permission. If a call would help, propose it with their call checklist.
7. On a call: use a short briefing task (not a stiff script), confirm number/purpose/talking points with the user first, record when they want audio, hang up cleanly, then report what staff said plus recording when available.

## Typical phone checklist (customize from owner memory)

- Cooking / dressing oils related to their allergen
- Shared fryers / shared prep with allergen dishes
- Hidden garnishes and kitchen-wide cross-contact
- Skip formal “allergy ticket protocol” questions unless they want them

Remind them this is information gathering; they make the final call.

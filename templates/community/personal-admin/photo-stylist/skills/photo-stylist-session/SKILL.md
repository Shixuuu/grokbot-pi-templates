---
name: photo-stylist-session
description: "Use when running a Photo Stylist session from an uploaded full-length photo through the seven style steps."
---

Run **one step at a time**. Wait for their answer or photo before the next. Keep face, body, hair, and background locked when editing clothes unless the step is hair/glasses.

**1 Honest read** — Three things in the outfit that work against them (ordered), one that works; best colors and colors to avoid from skin/hair/eyes in the photo.

**2 Same me, dressed right** — Ask where they're going. Change only clothes; colors that suit them; show the image.

**3 Three styles** — Ask for three style labels (default: smart casual / minimalist / streetwear). Side-by-side versions; same face/pose/background; pick one like a stylist and say why.

**4 Hair and glasses** — Three haircuts for face shape; best cut with and without glasses; barber/salon instruction for the winner.

**5 The event** — Ask event, date, dress code. Dress them in-photo; one finishing accessory; one common mistake at that event.

**6 Shopping list** — Ask total budget and max per item. Search web/X for real matching items: brand, item, price, buy link; total; cheaper alts for anything over their max. No invented prices.

**7 The walk** — If video/animation is available, animate ~5s walking toward camera in the final outfit (same face/clothes). If not available, say so and offer a still turnaround instead.

Rules: no body-shaming; be direct but kind; skip any step they decline; never auto-post images.

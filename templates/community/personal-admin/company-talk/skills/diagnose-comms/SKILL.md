# Diagnose workplace communication

## Input
The draft, the situation, or "what went wrong" after a meeting/Slack exchange.

## Steps
1. Name the primary failure mode in one line. Common modes:
   - Buried ask
   - Hedge cloud
   - Missing owner
   - Wrong altitude (thick design in a thin forum, or thin status where a decision is needed)
   - Inventory padding (ticket dumps to look busy)
   - Credit leak (silence after someone narrates your ship)
   - Capacity answered with architecture
2. Name the fix in one line (what to change, not a lecture).
3. Offer a rewritten version only if they want one, or if the ask was clearly "fix this."
4. Under stress: prep beats cleverness. Suggest two visible lines before meetings: (1) headline outcome they own today (2) the one constraint they will not drop.
5. Challenge response formula when they partly agree: name what you take → keep the constraint in one sentence → offer a narrow next check. Blindside line: "Good catch. Constraint I'm not dropping is X. I'll check Y and come back." Never close with "okay I get it."

## Hard rules
No soothing with more inventory. No turning every share into a design review. No inventing facts.

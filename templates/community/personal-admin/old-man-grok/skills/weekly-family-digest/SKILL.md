# Weekly family digest

1. Refresh the board with care-task-board and read this week's visit log and sent log.
2. Draft the digest in the owner's voice, under 200 words, warm and plain:
   - What got done this week, and by whom.
   - What's coming up in the next 7 days, and who has it.
   - What still needs a hand, with day, time, and how long it takes.
   - Thank-yous: name the people who carried a lot, specifically ("Sam drove to three appointments this week").
3. Separately, for the owner only and never in the family version, list what went out automatically this week from the sent log, and any visit reports you flagged.
4. Check trust-settings. If the digest is automatic and mentions no health details, send it to the saved recipients and log it. Otherwise show the owner the draft and recipients and ask: send as is, edit, or skip. Send by email if connected; otherwise hand them the text to copy.
5. Offer to draft asks for anything still uncovered (neighbor-ask).

The digest may get forwarded, so leave out health details beyond what coordination needs.

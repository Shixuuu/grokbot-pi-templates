# Shared care task board

Keep one list of needs for the person the circle is looking after.

Each item has: what it is, day and time, about how long it takes, where (only if it matters), who has it or "uncovered", status (open, covered, done), and a short note.

1. Load the board. If a docs or sheets tool is connected and the owner wants a shareable copy, keep it there. Otherwise keep it in `~/care-circle/board.md` on your computer, and remember in memory where it lives.
2. Apply what the owner said: add, change, reassign, or mark done. Turn repeating needs (weekly groceries, daily check-in call) into dated items for the next 7 days.
3. Check coverage. Anything in the next 72 hours with no one on it is uncovered. Also flag anyone asked to do more than the availability they gave, and clashes with the owner's calendar if it's connected.
4. Show the board in chat, grouped by day, uncovered items first, in plain sentences or a short table.
5. Offer to draft asks for uncovered items (neighbor-ask). If the calendar is connected, offer to add covered visits and rides; the owner approves each calendar change.

Keep health details to what coordination needs ("pharmacy pickup, ready after 2 PM"). Never give medical, legal, or financial advice; point to the care team.

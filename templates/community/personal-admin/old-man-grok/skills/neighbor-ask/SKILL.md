# Neighbor ask

Asking a neighbor for a hand is an old and good thing. Make it easy for them.

1. Pick who to ask: someone who does this kind of task, is usually free then, and hasn't carried the most this week. Suggest one or two people and say why in a line. If nobody's availability fits, say so plainly and offer two choices: ask someone outside their usual hours (easy to decline), or ask someone new, who is outside the circle and always needs the owner's yes.
2. Draft in the owner's voice, first person, two to four sentences: the specific task, the day and time, about how long it takes, anything they'd need to know, and an easy out ("Totally fine if not, I'll ask someone else"). Use the way that person likes to be reached.
3. A first-time ask to a person always needs the owner's yes. Other asks follow trust-settings. Show the recipient and the full text, then send only that message: by email if connected, otherwise give the owner the words to paste into a text.
4. On a yes, update the board and send a thank-you (automatic if trust-settings allows). On a no or no reply, thank them anyway and offer the next person. Don't ask the same person twice in a row for the same thing, and never guilt anyone.

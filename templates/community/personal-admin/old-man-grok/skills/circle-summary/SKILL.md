# Helper updates and circle summary

Helper updates feed: when the owner asks "what's new," list the past 7 days newest first from `~/care-circle/visits.md` and the board: who did what, what they noticed, and anything flagged. Short, plain lines.

One-page circle summary, saved to `~/care-circle/summary.md`:
1. Who the circle is looking after, in one line.
2. Who's in the circle and what each person usually does. Leave contact details out unless the owner asks for them.
3. Covered this week: day, task, who.
4. Open gaps: anything uncovered, with day, time, and how long it takes.
5. Recent visit notes: the last three to five, one line each, with no health detail beyond what coordination needs.
6. Thanks: who carried a lot lately.

Keep it to one page. If a docs tool is connected, offer to put it there so the owner can share a link. Sharing it with anyone follows trust-settings; if it mentions health details, it always needs the owner's yes.

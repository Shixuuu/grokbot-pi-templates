# Visit log

1. Log each report in `~/care-circle/visits.md`, newest first: date, who visited or did the task, how the person seemed, anything noticed, and any follow-up. Use the helper's words, short. Keep only what coordination needs.
2. Mark the matching board item done (care-task-board), and turn any follow-up into a new item ("fridge nearly empty, groceries before Wednesday").
3. Flag to the owner right away, before anything else, when a report mentions any new change in how the person seems physically or mentally (for example dizziness, a fall, confusion, not eating or drinking, missed medicine, seeming much worse or very upset), an unsafe home, or a helper who is worried. Say what was reported, quote the helper, and suggest checking with the care team. Don't interpret it or give medical advice. Drop the folksy tone here: be calm and direct.
4. If it sounds like an emergency happening now, say to call 911. If it sounds like a mental health crisis, say to call or text 988.
5. Never contact the care team, the family, or anyone else about a report without the owner's yes. Flagged details stay out of the digest and the shared summary unless the owner says otherwise.

To collect reports, you can remind a helper who finished a visit to send a quick note on how it went, if trust-settings allows that reminder.

Use when a one-page sheet is needed for the hospital financial counselor, patient advocate, or billing department.

Contents, in this order:
1. Facility and roughly when the care happened. No diagnosis.
2. What was checked, and the date.
3. Discrepancies found, each as a fact plus source, not a verdict.
4. Which published protection may apply and why (one sentence the navigator can correct).
5. What is already requested (itemized bill, GFE, charity care application).
6. Questions the patient reads aloud: coding, whether a consent form was valid, whether a law applies, anything unverified.

No sensitive identifiers. No amount owed. No legal or clinical conclusions. Never reconstruct a form, URL, phone, threshold, or deadline you could not read.

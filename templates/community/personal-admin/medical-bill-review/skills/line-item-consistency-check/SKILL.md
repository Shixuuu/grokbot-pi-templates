---
name: line-item-consistency-check
description: "Use this when an itemized bill is in hand, compared to what they say happened."
---

Use only when there is an itemized bill and the patient's account of what happened. Not a coding review.

What you check, against what they say happened:
- Services they say they did not receive
- Duplicate lines
- Dates outside the stay
- Quantities that do not match
- Room charges for days they were not there
- Supplies or drugs they say they never got
- Discharge-day charges that do not match the discharge

You may total or subtract figures the patient supplies (for example the amount of a duplicate line, or estimate vs bill) and label the result as math on their numbers. You never produce an amount owed.

What you never check: Whether a CPT/HCPCS code was the right code. Whether a service was medically necessary. Whether care was appropriate. If they ask whether a code is right or whether they were charged for something they didn't need, do not answer it and do not just decline. Say it is a real question, and put it on the Handoff sheet for the hospital billing department or a certified coder.

Output: A short list of discrepancies, each tied to a line on the bill and to what the patient said. Math on their numbers is labeled as such. No "you should only pay X." No reconstructed field list from a form you could not read.

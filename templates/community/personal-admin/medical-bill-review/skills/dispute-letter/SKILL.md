name: dispute-letter
description: "Use this when they want a letter to send."
---

Use when there is at least one cited discrepancy or a published protection that may apply, and the patient wants a letter.

Draft a letter with placeholders for every sensitive field (account number, guarantor, SSN, member ID, amounts they must fill). State the discrepancy. Cite the rule with URL and date checked. Never state an amount owed. Include where to send it and by when, only if you just read both.

Handoff: "This is ready for you to complete and send. I won't send it." Name the hospital billing department, patient advocate, or financial counselor if known.

If you cannot find the send-to address or the response deadline live, say so and put the gap on the Handoff sheet. Never reconstruct them.

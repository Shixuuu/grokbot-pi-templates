Use as step one of first-run, and whenever the patient only has a summary statement, a collection letter, or an EOB without line items.

Why: Nothing else in this bot works on a summary statement. Most patients do not know they can demand an itemized bill with every line, code, date of service, quantity, and charge.

What to do:
1. Ask whether they already have the itemized version (every line, CPT/HCPCS or revenue code, date, quantity). If they only have a total, a "balance due," or an EOB, they do not have it yet.
2. Draft a short request they send to the hospital billing department or patient accounting. Placeholders for account/guarantor numbers. You do not send it.
3. Tell them screening, charity care, and a dispute letter wait until that bill is in hand. Protection screen can start in parallel (emergency vs scheduled, in-network facility, consent at registration) but line-item work cannot.

Handoff: "This is ready for you to complete and send. I won't send it."

Never reconstruct the hospital's request form or phone number you could not read. Link to the billing page if you found it live. Never submit.

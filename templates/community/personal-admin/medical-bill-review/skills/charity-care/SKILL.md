Use on first-run after the itemized-bill request is drafted, and whenever the patient may need financial assistance at a specific hospital.

Find and read that hospital's published financial assistance policy. Pull from the live page: income threshold if published, how to apply, deadline, and what documents they ask for. Not a general explainer of charity care.

If the hospital is for-profit, say so plainly. The federal FAP requirement is for nonprofit hospitals. Then check live for a state program or a hospital-specific policy anyway.

Cite live: Federal framework (nonprofit hospitals must maintain and publicize a FAP, limit charges for FAP-eligible patients to amounts generally billed, and make reasonable efforts before extraordinary collection actions) only with a current source URL and date. Always prefer the hospital's own policy over the framework.

Do not say they qualify. Do not collect income to decide. Point them at the hospital's own application. Placeholders for sensitive fields. You do not submit.

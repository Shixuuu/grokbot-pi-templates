Use when the patient is uninsured or self-pay, or an insured patient says they intend to self-pay. Do not use this path as an insured-with-insurance right.

Good Faith Estimate: Uninsured and self-pay only. Written, itemized estimate before scheduled non-emergency care. Verify current timing, required contents (codes, charges, provider names/NPI/TIN), and how to request one live. An insured patient can request one by stating they intend to self-pay. Confirm that live.

Advanced EOB: The insured equivalent is not in effect unless you just read that it is. Do not tell an insured patient they are entitled to an Advanced EOB. If a proposed rule is all you find, say that.

The published-threshold rule (verify every figure live): If a bill exceeds that provider's or facility's own GFE by the published threshold or more, the patient may file Patient-Provider Dispute Resolution through an HHS-certified entity. Measured per provider or facility, not across the whole visit. Verify: window from receiving the disputed bill, patient administrative fee, collections pause while pending, that the decision binds the provider. If the source page will not load, refuse to state a number.

Never conflate PPDR with federal IDR. A patient cannot file IDR. You never file PPDR.

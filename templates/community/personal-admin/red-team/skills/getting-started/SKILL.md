# Getting started with Red Team

## Goal
Be ready to pressure-test a draft, decision, or take in under 5 minutes.

## Steps
1. Tell Red Team your domain in one line (founder notes, eng RFCs, investor updates, public posts, hiring, pricing — whatever you actually ship).
2. Paste one real artifact: an email draft, PR description, strategy note, tweet thread, or decision you’re about to make.
3. Ask for a full red team (or say “quick pass” for a shorter attack).
4. Optional: connect a webhook or shortcut that POSTs drafts with the text `red team this:` so late-night dumps still get attacked by morning.
5. Optional: set a weekday routine that asks “what are you shipping this week that still needs a red team?”

## Success looks like
You get a steelman of the opposing case, a punch list of failure modes, missing evidence, and a rewritten version that already answers the hard questions.

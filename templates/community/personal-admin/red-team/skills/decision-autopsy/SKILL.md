# Decision autopsy

## When to use
After a decision went well or badly, or when the user wants to pre-mortem a choice still on the table.

## Output
1. Decision stated in one sentence.
2. What we believed then vs what we know now (or will wish we knew).
3. Leading indicators that would have predicted the outcome.
4. A reusable rule for next time (max 2 sentences).
5. If pre-mortem: the story of failure written as if it already happened, then how to prevent that story.

# Evidence audit

## When to use
The user wants every claim in a draft checked for support — especially posts, investor notes, launch copy, or research summaries.

## Steps
1. Extract atomic claims (numbers, causality, “customers want X”, timelines, competitive statements).
2. For each claim: Supported / Weak / Unsupported — and what evidence would flip it.
3. Flag absolute language (“always”, “the only”, “guaranteed”) and suggest precise wording.
4. Return a short rewrite of the riskiest sentences only.

## Rules
Do not invent sources. If you look something up, say what you found and what you could not verify.

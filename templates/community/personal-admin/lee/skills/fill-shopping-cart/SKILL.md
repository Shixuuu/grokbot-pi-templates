## Before you open a site
Resolve what, how many, budget, brand, size, and store. Infer harmless defaults and say them. Ask only when the answer changes what goes in the cart.

## Rules
- Default is cart only. Do not buy, checkout, place an order, or charge a card unless the owner clearly says to buy that specific item.
- Sign in on the computer when the site requires it. Never ask them to paste a password in chat.
- Skip out of stock, see options you cannot resolve, add-on only, and ineligible items. Report those as skipped with why.
- Ignore protection plans, subscribe-and-save, and bundle upsells unless they asked for them.
- Never invent a price, rating, delivery date, or order number. If the page did not show it, say so.

## Flow
1. Search and open the best matching product for each list item.
2. Set quantity and add to cart.
3. After the cart is filled, report each product, quantity, unit price, seller, skipped items with why, and a screenshot.
4. Mention third-party marketplace sellers when they are not the store itself.

## Gift research
When the ask is gift ideas, not a cart fill: shortlist 3 to 5 real options with name, price, rating with review count, delivery date, seller, and why it fits. Do not add a gift to a cart until they pick.

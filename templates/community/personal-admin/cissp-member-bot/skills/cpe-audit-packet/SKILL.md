Use when preparing documentation for a suggested or completed CPE activity so the member can claim it with ISC².

## Source of truth
The current ISC² CISSP CPE handbook decides category, credit rules, and documentation requirements. Never invent eligibility. If handbook rules are unclear for the activity, say so and ask the member to verify before claiming.

## Packet contents (always include)
1. Activity title — exact name of the material or event.
2. Provider / source — organization, publisher, or platform.
3. URL — stable link when the activity is online.
4. Date completed — leave blank for a suggestion; fill when the member finishes.
5. Duration — hours or minutes as stated by the source (do not invent).
6. Suggested CPE credits — only when the handbook supports the calculation; otherwise mark "member to confirm per handbook."
7. Domain / category — map to handbook CPE categories or CISSP domains only when the mapping is clear; otherwise note "confirm category with handbook."
8. Description — 2–4 sentences on what was covered and why it relates to information security.
9. Evidence to retain — certificate, completion screen, notes, badge, or attendance proof the member should keep.
10. How to log in the ISC² portal — short steps: open CPE submission, choose matching activity type, enter credits, attach or retain evidence, submit.

## Output format
Deliver a ready-to-copy audit packet in plain prose or a compact labeled block. End with a one-line reminder that ISC² may audit claims and the member is responsible for accuracy.

## Do not
- Guarantee acceptance by ISC².
- Inflate hours or credits.
- Suggest paid products unless the member asked for paid options.

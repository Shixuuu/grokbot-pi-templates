# Getting started (first conversation)

Talk to the new owner warmly, one question at a time.

1. **Opening message.** Explain the bot in a few sentences: Send me low-priority ideas anytime during the week. I keep them on a backlog, and right before your weekly usage resets I work through them so leftover usage doesn't go to waste. Unfinished items roll over to next week. In this same opening message, clearly tell them to make sure On-demand usage is OFF (Settings, Usage & Billing, On-demand usage; include the in-app link), so this bot can never buy extra usage.

2. **Get the reset day and time.** I can't see usage myself, so ask them to open the Usage row under Settings, Usage & Billing (link it with the in-app usage settings link), find when their weekly usage resets, and tell you the day and time (e.g. "Sunday 11pm"). Confirm their timezone.

3. **Confirm On-demand usage is off.** Ask them to confirm they've checked the On-demand usage row and it's off or capped at zero. If the row isn't there, on-demand likely isn't enabled for their account. Wait for them to confirm.

4. **Set up two routines** (read the routines skill first), both following the leftover-usage-weekly-run skill:
   - Preview: 1 day before the reset, send the ordered list and ask for changes or "skip this week."
   - Work session: a few hours before the reset (suggest 4 to 6 hours; ask what they prefer).
   Save the reset time, timezone, and "first session not yet confirmed" to memory.

5. **Explain the safeguards briefly:** before every session I'll remind you to verify On-demand usage is off; before the very first session I'll ask you to confirm your usage hasn't already reset; each session I'll ask how much is left; and I won't send, buy, or delete anything without you.

6. Invite their first idea. When they send one, drop the setup talk and add it to the backlog.

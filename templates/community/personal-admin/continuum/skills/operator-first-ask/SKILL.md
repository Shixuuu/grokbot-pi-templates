description: "Use this on a fresh clone's first turn — instant sample brief first, then one setup ask."
---

Make the first 60 seconds feel like they already have a second self — not an onboarding form.

1. One-line hello with attitude (do not recite the profile).
2. Immediately deliver a sample "what's up" brief from whatever connectors or files exist; mark blank lanes honestly.
3. One question widget only: timezone + #1 this month, and invite a ChatGPT / Claude / Grok export as the custom path. Frame it as locking the nightly learner, not setup.
4. Arm weekday Morning brief (7am), Evening shutdown (7pm), and Nightly second self (10pm every day) in their local time if missing.
5. Offer exactly one next unlock — calendar, or "drop last year's chats" if they have no export yet. No laundry list.

Success: they felt known before they answered anything, and the nightly runner is armed.

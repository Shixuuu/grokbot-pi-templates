# Post and reply drafter

Use when the member wants a post, reply or quote drafted for X or another public place. You draft; they post.

## Draft
- Get the target: a new post, or a reply or quote to a specific post link they give you.
- Write in their voice, in plain words. Go deeper than an echo of what they already wrote: make the point clearer, don't just repeat it.
- If you add links, use only real ones you have checked, and no more than needed.

## Fresh-eyes check (before calling it ready)
Answer these in chat:
1. Is this what the member meant to say?
2. Could a stranger understand every word, with no inside jargon, codes or file paths?
3. Is it kind, true, and free of first/best/only claims?
4. If there's an image: does it show something real, and does it have a short image description (ALT text)?
5. Is any private detail about other people, money or location present? If so, remove it.
Report Pass or Fail with 1–3 fixes. On Fail, fix it and check again.

## Hand-off
Give the final text in one copy-ready block and say where it goes (new post, reply or quote). If the member has connected an account that supports drafts, you may save it as an unsent draft only with their yes. Never post, like, repost or send.

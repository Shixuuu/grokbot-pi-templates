# Biggest constraint (weekly)

Find the **one** bottleneck that most limits company throughput this week. Ping the principal with focus. Quiet if unchanged.

## First principles (customize per company)
- Name what actually funds growth vs what is a bet.
- Sales / distribution is often the bottleneck when the founder still closes key deals.
- Support agents are not the constraint — they support whoever owns it.

## Steps
1. Gather signal (memory, open deals, team chat/mail if connected, calendar, prior constraint notes under a local constraint folder).
2. Ask: what physical limit binds growth this week? (founder hours on sales, pipeline quality, product readiness, ops load, capital, team capacity)
3. Name **THE constraint** in one line. Name the person who owns it.
4. Run The Algorithm on it lightly: what requirement is dumb, what to delete, what to simplify, what to speed, what NOT to automate yet.
5. Output ONLY:
   - **Constraint:** …
   - **Why (physics):** …
   - **This week — do:** ≤3 moves
   - **This week — delete / don’t:** ≤3 items
   - **Who:** named owners
   - **Next check:** when
6. Save a dated note. Tell the principal only when material or needs a decision.

## Hard rules
- One constraint. Not a list of ten priorities.
- No outsider email/Slack/WhatsApp without explicit ask.
- No spend without ask.
- Prefer deleting agent/process sprawl over adding new routines.

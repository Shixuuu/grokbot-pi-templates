Use when a legal, entity, banking, adviser, or exit fact has been confirmed and needs to be folded into the standing entity map without inventing.

## Boundaries
- No legal, tax, or investment advice.
- Do not invent entities, ownership, or exits.
- Prep-only adviser packs stay internal until explicitly invited for delivery.
- Shared notes get patterns only; numbers stay with the capacity teammate.
- Do not push exits unless invited.
- This is a Skill, not a Routine.

## Steps
1. Confirm the fact (from the owner, or from the capacity teammate on a form fact they already hold).
2. Fold it into the standing entity map at the path the owner set (entities, notes, advisers, banking, exits).
3. Mirror the same fact lightly in the team's standing rules file if it is a rule-level form fact.
4. Do not open adviser delivery, new entities, or capital movement from this update.
5. Report the lock in one or two lines: what changed, what is still parked.

# Say it back

Speak **their map** back to them — the things, how they relate, each bit of logic you’ve heard — so they can hear it, fix it, and feel tracked.

Cross-cuts Half 1 Map and Understand (and Half 2 open). Judgement; don’t overdo.

## When it might help

Long dump · flooded · resume · you may have drifted · before a hard crystallise / stay-with · after a fork/merge · Half 2 open.

## What to say back

- The **things** on the map (their names)  
- **Relations** (same / different / nested / in tension / causes)  
- Local logic and hinges you’ve heard  
- Optionally a second phrasing of the *same* structure — not a new diagnosis

Then: “Anything wrong or missing?” — and quiet.

## Don’t

- Invent links or merge/fork things they didn’t agree to.  
- Drown them in a monologue.  
- Smuggle advice or a reframe.  
- Skip placement when they just added “another thing.”

## Working / not

**Working:** correction, “yes,” or a sharper next line.  
**Not:** bounce → change approach; ask what would help.

## Stop

They’ve heard themselves accurately enough — or they corrected the map.

# Leadership questions — exit

**Ask only.** Do not paste the list. Pick one. Don’t switch lenses mid-unfinished thing — [Stay with](sand-workflow:stay-with) first.

## Pick from

- What happens when you're not in the room?
- If you weren't available, what would they do?
- What would asking for help / aborting a bad approach look like without you extracting it?
- Which of your questions are load-bearing for their success?

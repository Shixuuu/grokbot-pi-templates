# Leadership questions — situate

**Ask only.** Do not paste the list. Pick one. Don’t switch lenses mid-unfinished thing — [Stay with](sand-workflow:stay-with) first.

## Pick from

- What's the goal in this situation?
- Who is the team — and who else is in the room when you're not?
- What's true about *you* in this (energy, skill, constraints)?
- What's the environment doing (incentives, politics, time pressure)?
- Are you treating this as zero-sum when it might be positive-sum?

# Pivot the coaching subject

Mid-session, they realize the locked subject was wrong. Update it and reshape the rest of the session — don't force-finish the wrong arc.

## Do

- Acknowledge the shift without drama.
- Name the new subject in their words (or help them name it with one clarifying question).
- Reshape around the new subject — back to **Half 1** in [Coaching session](sand-workflow:coaching-session) (Map + Understand weave). Don't stay in Options/Will on the abandoned subject.
- If they were amending a plan, Amend is first-class.

## Don't

- Keep finishing Options/Will on the abandoned subject.
- Treat this as ordinary unfinished-line work — that's [Stay with](sand-workflow:stay-with) (or [Broaden](sand-workflow:broaden) / [Make distinct](sand-workflow:make-distinct) if Map was incomplete).
- Re-run full open quiet-space unless they're truly lost again; if the subject was never locked, use [Choose the coaching subject](sand-workflow:choose-coaching-subject).

## Stop

New subject is locked and you're back in Half 1 on it.

# Questions — goal

**Do not paste the list at the coachee.** Pick one question. Invent a sharper one if you have it.

**Understand** — once things are placed enough that “what does success look like?” helps. Not a substitute for Map inventory or NNTD at impasse. See [Getting to the real point](sand-workflow:getting-to-the-real-point).

## Pick from

- What do you want to achieve?
- What does success look like?
- What would be different if this were sorted?
- What’s most important to you here?
- What are you aiming for in the next stretch?
- How will you know you’ve got there?

## Stop

One question asked. If the map is still forming, [Broaden](sand-workflow:broaden) / [Make distinct](sand-workflow:make-distinct). If a line is unfinished, [Stay with](sand-workflow:stay-with).

# Choose the coaching subject

Quiet space at the **start** of a coaching conversation. Do not dive into the first reported issue.

## Do

- Offer space to say more until *they* are clear what they want to work on. First thing brought is often not what's in the way.
- Useful prompts: "What else is here?" / "Is that the thing, or is there something under it?" / "Take a beat — what feels most alive to work on?"
- Let silence work. Don't fill with solutions or a full session march.
- Lock the subject only when they name it with enough clarity, or explicitly choose the first topic after space.

## Don't

- Treat the first message as the locked Goal/Subject.
- Stack three questions or interpret for them.
- Stay here once they've clearly chosen — move into the session shape.

## Stop

They've locked the subject they actually want. If mid-session they realize that subject was wrong, use [Pivot the coaching subject](sand-workflow:pivot-coaching-subject). After lock: unfinished line → [Stay with](sand-workflow:stay-with); incomplete Map → [Broaden](sand-workflow:broaden) / [Make distinct](sand-workflow:make-distinct) as fits.

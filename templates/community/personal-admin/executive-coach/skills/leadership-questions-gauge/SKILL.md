# Leadership questions — gauge

**Ask only.** Do not paste the list. Pick one. Don’t switch lenses mid-unfinished thing — [Stay with](sand-workflow:stay-with) first.

## Pick from

- Where is the energy or motivation — is anything actually moving?
- How clear is the destination, and the approach to get there?
- Where's the friction — what are you (or the system) putting a foot on?

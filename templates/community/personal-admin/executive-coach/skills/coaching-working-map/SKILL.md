# Coaching working map

Track **the things** — each with its own logic — and **how they relate**. Write it; surface it. Not one blob.

Supports Half 1 **Map** (inventory) and **Understand** (shape written on the same page). Also the page you spell back at Half 2 open.

## When

Every coaching session.

## What to track

Per **thing:** name (their words) · local logic · open unknowns / load-bearing hinges.  
**Relations:** same? different? nested? in tension? causes?

## When they add something

Ask or confirm via [Make distinct](sand-workflow:make-distinct) / [Broaden](sand-workflow:broaden): **new thing, or where does it sit?** Don’t silently merge or fork.

## Do

Keep it written and sketchable. Surface with [Say it back](sand-workflow:say-it-back) when useful. On drift: rewrite from theirs. On resume: restate things + relations.

**Map done (inventory):** nothing else *big* missing. That is **not** the Half 2 lean oracle — leaning Half 2 needs a **clear important problem** (ask / felt referent / multi-part) per [Coaching session](sand-workflow:coaching-session).  
**Understand** still writes shape/hinges/impasse notes onto this map.

## Don’t

Invent links · collapse distinct things · novel-length maps.

## Stop

Map current enough for the next move — or they corrected it.

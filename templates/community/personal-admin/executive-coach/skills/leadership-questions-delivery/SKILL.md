# Leadership questions — delivery

**Ask only.** Do not paste the list. Pick one. Don’t switch lenses mid-unfinished thing — [Stay with](sand-workflow:stay-with) first.

## Pick from

- Do they understand *what* was agreed?
- Do they see a path for *how* — without you turning the wheel?
- Do they actually *want* to?
- What's the minimum help that still leaves ownership with them?

# Questions — transfer

**Do not paste the list at the coachee.** Pick one question. Invent a sharper one if you have it.

**Half 2 close** — after will (or an honest amend/pause). Don’t invent closure while something *big* is off-map or the problem is unbound — weave first ([Coaching session](sand-workflow:coaching-session)).

## Pick from

- What have you learnt?
- What will you do differently?
- What shifted for you in this conversation?
- What support do you need (not: what should I do for you)?
- How will you remind yourself of this?
- What will you write down so you can steer when you're not in the room?
- When should we check how this went?

## Stop

One question asked. Quiet when nothing to add. Don't invent a follow-up plan for them.

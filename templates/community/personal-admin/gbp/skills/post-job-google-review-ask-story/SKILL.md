# Post-job Google review ask (story)

## Goal
After a clearly happy completed job, ask once for a Google review that names service + town + their story. No fake reviews, no pay-for-stars.

## When
Same day or next morning after a good outcome. Skip after a rough or unhappy job.

## Steps
1. Confirm the job went well (ops judgment). If unsure, do not ask.
2. Fill: customer first name, service done, town served, direct Google review link.
3. Prefer short SMS; optional slightly longer email.
4. Invite them to mention service + town + how it went in their own words. Never paste a script they must copy.
5. One ask. No nag. No coupon-for-review.
6. After a 5-star lands, the GBP bot may post a unique thank-you per standing policy.

## Template
> Hey [Name], glad we got your [service] sorted in [town]. If you're happy with how it went, a quick Google review mentioning the service and your town helps neighbors find us. Here's the link: [review URL]. No pressure, and thanks for trusting us.

## Stop
- No mass blasts from a bot; a human on the team sends it.
- No inventing review quotes or customer names.

## Output
Filled text ready for the dispatcher or office to send.

# Local HVAC / Electrical GBP & Maps Ranking

Goal: strengthen Map Pack relevance and prominence for an HVAC-led home-services GBP without guideline violations or invented rank claims.

## Hard constraints
- Geography: only the owner's approved service towns (service area, posts, page language).
- Ask before any GBP edit, post, photo batch, category/service change, or Q&A seed.
- No phone numbers in GBP posts.
- Reply only to 5-star reviews, each reply unique; never reply to 1–4 star unless the owner's policy says so.
- Keep the primary category (e.g. HVAC contractor); do not swap to seasonal/subcategory primaries.
- Report Insights, call/direction trends, or geo-grid data; never invent ranks.

## Map Pack model
Proximity (honest address/service area), relevance (categories, services, description, review keywords, matching site pages), prominence (review velocity and rating, photos, citations/NAP, engagement). GBP is the highest-leverage Maps asset; the website supports it.

## Recipe
0. Intake: legal name (no stuffing), primary category, allowed secondaries, approved town list, truthful hours, who asks for reviews, photo source. Stop and ask before any change.
1. Completeness audit (read-only): name, categories, granular services with short descriptions, natural description, hours, true attributes, website URL, service areas, recent real photos (flag >30 days stale), 30/90-day review velocity, NAP match with site. Deliver findings and proposed changes; do not apply until approved.
2. Categories and services (after approval): keep primary, add/trim secondaries to real work, expand services, align site pages to the same language.
3. Service area: approved towns only; never invent a storefront.
4. Photos weekly: techs take 2 real photos per job; upload a small approved batch weekly; no stock.
5. Reviews: after a happy job send the direct review link; soft ask for service + town; unique 5-star thank-yous; never buy reviews.
6. Posts weekly when approved: local, seasonal, job-story, tip; approved towns only; no phone numbers in body; do not promise ranking lifts.
7. Citations/NAP: one canonical NAP; fix major directories and manufacturer dealer listings; no spam citation blasts.
8. Site support: unique service and town pages with real local detail, consistent schema, click-to-call.
9. Evidence: GBP Insights (calls, directions, website clicks), review velocity, approved geo-grid tool, Search Console, call tracking. Phrase results as "Insights show X calls", never "we're #1".

## Weekly cadence
Pull Insights, draft one post and a photo batch for approval, process new 5-star replies, trigger review requests, note NAP/hours drift, log evidence.

## Monthly
Read-only competitor category/services audit, citation spot-check, geo-grid for priority keywords across the approved towns, service-page freshness.

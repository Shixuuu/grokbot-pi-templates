# Memory seeds for GBP

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Leave all 1–4 star Google reviews alone. Do not draft, reply, or ask again about them. 5-star thank-yous only.
- **profile**: 5-star thank-yous must be unique. Never post the same line twice. Do not rotate canned lines. Write from THAT review (name, job, unit, tech). Banned stock: “Thank you for your trust with your AC system” and “Thank you for choosing [business].” Do not edit old replies.
- **profile**: The bar for a 5-star thank-you is short and specific: one or two sentences, local shop voice, not corporate. No coupons, no phone numbers, no arguing, no competitor names.
- **profile**: The owner has a standing yes for posting unique 5-star GBP thank-yous daily without asking each time. Report when any are posted; stay silent if none.
- **profile**: Always verify live Maps/GBP before quoting reviews or ratings. House rule: no source, no number. Never invent rankings, review counts, or categories.
- **profile**: Ask before any GBP edit (categories, attributes, services, hours, description) or any post that is not on an approved calendar. Keep the primary category unchanged.
- **log**: Avoid google.com/search for review work because it triggers captchas. Use business.google.com or the Maps place page. On a captcha or sign-in wall, stop, do not solve or type credentials, and ask the owner to sign in.
- **log**: No phone numbers or phone digits in GBP post bodies or review replies; the call button is enough.

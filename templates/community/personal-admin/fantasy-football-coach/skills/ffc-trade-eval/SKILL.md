# Trade evaluation

## Inputs
- The offer: a screenshot or text of both sides.
- Both rosters. Ask for the other team's roster screenshot if you don't have it; it shows what they need and whether they would accept.
- League settings: scoring, roster slots, trade deadline, playoff weeks. Use only what you have seen or been told. Mark unknowns.

## Steps
1. Restate the deal in one line.
2. For each player: role, health, bye week, and rest-of-season outlook from at least two named public sources, with dates.
3. Owner's side: starting lineup before and after, depth by position, bye coverage, and playoff-week outlook if the playoff weeks are known.
4. Other side: does it fill their need? That is the "would they accept" read.
5. Verdict: Accept, Decline, or Counter. For Counter, give one concrete counter and why it is better for the owner and still plausible for the other manager.
6. Say what would change the call (an injury return, a depth chart change).

## Output
```
Trade: you give A + B, you get C
Verdict: Counter (solid)
Why: 2-3 lines
Lineup impact: before -> after
Their side: ...
Counter idea: ...
Sources: ...
```

## Never
- Invent trade values, rankings, or projections. If sources disagree, say so.
- Send, accept, or reject trades, or message league members. The owner does that.
- Help with collusion, roster dumping, or deals meant to exploit an inactive manager.

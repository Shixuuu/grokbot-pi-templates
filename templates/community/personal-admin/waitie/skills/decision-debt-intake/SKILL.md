# Decision debt intake

Use when capturing something the principal owes that blocks teammates (bots or people).

## Steps
1. Record: decision/reply owed, who is waiting, age (first asked), cost-of-delay note (what stalls if unanswered).
2. Exclude pure todos with no blocker, AR collections, and GTM promise-chases (hand those to the right seat).
3. Rank open items by cost of delay, then age.
4. Pass the top item to smallest-next-ask.

## Anti-jobs
- Not project management.
- Not Soft Collections.
- Not GTM promise chase (hand that seat off).

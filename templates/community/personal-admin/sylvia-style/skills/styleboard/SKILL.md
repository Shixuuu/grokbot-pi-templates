---
name: styleboard
description: "Use this when building or updating the client's lookbook page — compile keepers into a visual board with formulas, product cards, and shop links."
---

Build a personal lookbook from keepers that already passed intake and shopping. Do not invent products or stock. Do not put a Do Not Buy or skip list on the page.

Page shape:
- Short doctrine at the top (their three words, life-first vs aspiration, any locked fabric or camera rules).
- Uniforms / formulas first, then the pieces. Typical sections if they fit this client: Uniforms, First pieces, Life, Runaround, Stage, Aspiration, Closet, Jewelry. Drop a section they do not need. Do not pad.
- Each card: photo, name, brand, why it fits the brief, size note, price, shop link.
- Badges only for the real locks (the talk dress, the mixer, a cheap hero). Backups stay quieter.
- Formulas in plain sentences on the page (this + that, worn like this).

Build notes:
- Use real product images, compressed enough that a single-file HTML stays usable. Ship a single HTML and a zip if images are heavy.
- Fix broken images and mid-page cutoffs before handing it over.
- Stage and camera sections stay matte and solid if intake said recorded. Aspiration prints and satin can live on Aspiration or dinner, not on the talk page.
- When they list what they own, cut the board to a gap list. Until then, the board is the full keeper set, not a from-zero closet they must buy.

Deliver the file and a short recap of the formulas, not a lecture.

# Daily artist homage

## Goal
Study one artist, then make one ORIGINAL homage to their thought and technique. Present: (1) one real reference image of a work by that artist, (2) 1–2 original homage images, then a short text: your understanding of the artist, and an introduction to the homage. Also write the same deliverable to the studio disk folder named in the running agent's profile (file-of-record).

## Pick the artist
- Must have been shown at a major museum or gallery (Louvre, MoMA, Centre Pompidou, National Gallery London, Tate, The Met, Prado, Uffizi, Vatican Museums, Rijksmuseum, Guggenheim, Musée d'Orsay, Gagosian, Pace, Perrotin, Thaddaeus Ropac, Hauser & Wirth, Almine Rech, David Zwirner, Galleria Continua, White Cube, Lisson, or similar).
- Do not repeat an artist already recorded in memory as done.
- Alternate eras and geographies over time: historical museum collections and living gallery artists both count.
- Prefer a sharp idea you can homage, not fame for its own sake.

## Study
- Look up real sources (museum/gallery pages, interviews, catalogues). Do not invent biographical facts.
- Extract: the thought (what they believed art was for) and the technique (how they made that thought visible).
- Ignore gossip. Ignore "in the style of" shortcuts.
- Every claim needs a real source; never invent quotes, dates, or exhibition facts.

## Reference image (required)
- Fetch **one** real image of a work by today's artist from an official museum/gallery page or other clearly attributed public source.
- Prefer the specific work whose thought/technique you are homaging when one is identifiable; otherwise a canonical work by that artist.
- Show it in chat **labeled as reference / original** (title, artist, institution or source URL). Save it in the day's studio folder as `reference.*`.
- Never invent or AI-generate a fake "original." If no usable image can be fetched, say so and stop the homage for that artist — pick another artist rather than fabricate a reference.

## Make the homage
- Homage the idea and method. Never copy a known work. Never portrait the artist. Never fake a famous canvas, photo, or installation.
- Medium can be painting, installation, conceptual work, or performance. The deliverable is photograph(s) of the piece.
- Generate 1–2 **original** homage images (a full view, optional detail). No text, signatures, watermarks, or letters inside the image unless the homage truly requires language as material.
- Do not put the artist's name on the artwork.

## Forbidden
- Never post, DM, schedule, or submit the work to social networks or a marketplace.
- Never edit other agents' files or folders unless the owner explicitly asks.
- Never create bots, spend money, or expand permissions without a clear yes for that action.

## Deliver
- Show images in chat in this order: **reference (original) first**, then homage image(s).
- Then write two short prose beats (not a lecture, not bullet points):
  1. Your understanding of the artist: their thought and technique, grounded in real sources.
  2. An introduction to today's homage: what it is paying tribute to, and what it deliberately refuses to copy.
- Copy reference + homage images and a short README (with reference source URL) into that day's studio disk folder (file-of-record). Chat alone is not enough.
- Record in memory: artist name, date, institution tie, reference work title + source URL, one-line thought homaged, and the disk path.

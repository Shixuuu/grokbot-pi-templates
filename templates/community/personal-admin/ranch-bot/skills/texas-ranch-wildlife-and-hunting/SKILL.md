## When
Use for wildlife counts, habitat, hunting-lease operations, hog/predator control, or TPWD-related ranch questions.

## Hard rules
- You are not a TPWD warden, biologist, or hunting-lease lawyer. Confirm current license and season text; do not quote remembered bag limits or fees.
- Paying hunting guests typically require the ranch to hold the correct TPWD hunting-lease license. Flag it; the owner confirms.
- No promised harvest numbers. Safety around firearms, kids, and guests is non-negotiable.
- Do not contact hunters or post lease ads until the owner says so.

## Capture
- County, acreage, high-fence / low-fence, species (white-tailed deer, turkey, dove, hog, exotics).
- Who hunts: family only, friends, paid lease, day hunt, outfitter.
- Existing TPWD programs (MLDP or successor, trap/translocate, depredation).
- Habitat: browse, water distribution, food plots, prescribed fire history, brush work.
- Pressure: drought, disease notes, hog sign, trespass.

## Work product
1. Season calendar for that county (confirm current TPWD Outdoor Annual).
2. Guest/hunter check-in: gate rules (never store gate codes in shared outputs), shooting lanes, house rules, emergency contact, nearest trauma hospital.
3. Lease packet draft: dates, number of hunters, vehicles, game cameras, indemnification placeholder for an attorney.
4. Hog and predator log template.
5. Habitat punch list: water, fence, fire, feeders — owner picks.

## Handoff
Safety or trespass → owner same day. Lease language → attorney. Population estimates → biologist or the owner's own survey, never invented.

# Memory seeds for Meetups

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Two tracks every hunt. Builder track is AI, developer tools, and founder events. Sales track is regular business networking where the owner can meet clients: local chambers of commerce, BNI and referral groups, 1 Million Cups, SCORE/SBA owner workshops, business after-hours. Generic business mixers count on the sales track only. Still skip MLM, virtual-only, and pure social.
- **profile**: Every meetup I report gets an approximate drive time and mileage from the owner's home address, pulled from a real routing source; say unknown rather than estimating.
- **profile**: Weekly reports are NET NEW only. Keep a seen-events ledger file and do not repeat an event already reported, unless its date, time, or venue changed. A recurring series is announced once, then only when the next date is genuinely new.
- **profile**: Do not RSVP, pay, or message a meetup host unless the owner explicitly says to.
- **profile**: When the owner approves a meetup, add it as a personal event on their calendar with the listing URL in the description. Do not add hosts as attendees and do not RSVP on Meetup/Luma/Eventbrite unless they say.

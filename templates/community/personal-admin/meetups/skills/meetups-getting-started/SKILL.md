# Meetups: getting started

Say in one line what you do: every week you find in-person events near them that they can actually drive to, and you only tell them what's new.

Ask these one at a time and wait for each answer:

1. What's your home city or address? Ask for a street address or a nearby cross street if they're comfortable sharing, since that makes drive times accurate. A city is fine too.
2. How far will you drive? Ask for minutes or miles, and whether the nearest big city counts even if it's a bit past that.
3. Which events do you want? Builder events (AI, dev tools, founders, startups), sales networking (chambers of commerce, referral groups like BNI, 1 Million Cups, SCORE and small-business workshops, after-hours mixers), or both? If they pick sales, ask what they sell and who their customers are so you can pick the right rooms.
4. Any tools, communities, or groups you especially want to catch? For example a product's local meetup.
5. What day and time should the weekly hunt land, and what's your timezone?
6. Want approved events added to your calendar? If yes, have them connect Google Calendar.

Then:
- Save the answers as memories: home location, drive radius, the cities inside it, tracks, favorite communities, timezone, and calendar preference.
- Create the weekly hunt routine on the day and time they picked.
- Run a first hunt for the next two weeks right away using the local-meetups skill, start the seen-events ledger, and send the results.

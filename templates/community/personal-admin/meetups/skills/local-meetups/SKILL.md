The owner's home address, local cities, and calendar live in the running agent's memory, not here.

# Local meetups

Hunt real in-person events the owner can drive to, within their stated drive radius plus the nearest major city. In-person only unless they ask otherwise.

## Sources

Open live pages. Meetup, Luma, Eventbrite, community calendars for the tools they use, chamber of commerce sites, X. Cite the URL. If a page will not open, say unknown for that source. Never invent a date, time, or venue.

## Two tracks

Builder: AI, developer tools, founder and startup events.

Sales: business networking where the owner can meet clients. Chambers of commerce, referral groups like BNI, 1 Million Cups, small-business and SCORE workshops, business after-hours. A generic business mixer counts on this track only.

Skip on both tracks: MLM recruiting, virtual-only, webinars, purely social events with no business purpose, and anything past the drive radius unless asked.

## Drive time

Give approximate drive time and mileage from the owner's home address to each venue, taken from a real routing source (for example the public OSRM router). Label it approximate and off-peak. Write "drive time unknown" instead of estimating.

## Net new

Keep a ledger of everything already reported. Each run sends only what is new, unless a known event changed date, time, or venue. Announce a recurring series once, then only when a genuinely new date appears. Append what you send.

## Output

Short bullets grouped by track. Name, day and date, start time with timezone, venue and city, link, drive time, one line on why it fits. Note a ticket price if there is one. No dump.

If nothing net new is real, say nothing.

## Stop

Do not RSVP, pay, register, create accounts, or message a host. Add an approved event to the owner's calendar only when they name it, as a personal block with the listing URL in the notes, no host attendees.

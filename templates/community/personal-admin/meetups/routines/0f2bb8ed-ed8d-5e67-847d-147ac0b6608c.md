# Routine: Weekly meetup hunt

> Fires once a week so the owner hears about new local events they can drive to before they happen.

Run the local-meetups skill for the next two weeks on the owner's chosen tracks (builder, sales, or both) within their drive radius. Check every find against the seen-events ledger and keep only net-new events, or known events whose date, time, or venue changed. Give each one a real approximate drive time and mileage from home, or say unknown. Send short bullets grouped by track with the link, date, time, venue, and price. Update the ledger. If nothing net new is real, stay quiet. Never RSVP, pay, register, or message a host.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/New_York 59 7 * * 1`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

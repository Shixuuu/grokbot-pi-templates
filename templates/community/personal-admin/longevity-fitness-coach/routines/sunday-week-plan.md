# Routine: Weekly plan chart

> Fires the day before the owner's training week starts to turn their app screenshot into a one-image weekly plan tagged by goal, shared with the health coach partner for fueling.

Ask the owner for a screenshot of their bike/run app's coming week (skip asking if they already sent one). Keep the app's workouts exactly as written. Add strength around them using the saved rules: max 2 hard days, two sessions for their priority weak area never on hard endurance days, one full rest day, short 10–15 min upper/mobility on the app's other rest days. Use only real data; if something is missing, check with the health coach, then ask the owner. Render one chart image (no tables, minimal words), each day tagged by goal, and send it with one or two short lines. Post the week's plan and hard days in the Fitness + Health channel so Longevity Health Coach can add fueling notes. On the Sunday after the monthly test, also send the monthly scorecard chart with the health coach's 1–2 lab lines.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Los_Angeles 14 8 * * 0`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

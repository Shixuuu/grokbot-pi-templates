Use after fleet-collision-detect.

## Standing lane map (defaults)
- **Research / brief bots:** research only — no first-touch outbound email
- **Prospect / outreach bots:** own first-touch outbound to a lead
- **Focus / deep-work bot:** owns the *decision* to soft-decline soft invites that hit focus blocks
- **Calendar assistant:** owns the *calendar write* / RSVP execute after Focus (or the owner) decides
- **CoS:** staffing/routing — not Sibling Bot Sync’s job
- **Team builder:** creates seats — Sync never CreateAgent

## Steps
1. Propose **one** owner bot (or human) for the contested object. Prefer the standing lane map. When relevant, split **decide** vs **execute** (e.g. Focus decides soft-decline; Calendar executes the RSVP).
2. One sentence why (lane map, capacity, existing evidence).
3. State what other bots should **stop** doing.
4. Send the proposal only to your CoS and/or the fleet owner — not a fleet fan-out unless they OK’d it.
5. Wait for their decision before treating ownership as settled.

## Anti-jobs
- Never reassign silently.
- Never CreateAgent.
- Never staff client work as CoS.
- Propose only; no mass pings.

# Research reliability
Hard bar for accurate research. Not legal advice. Tag CONFIRMED / UNPROVEN / UNSET / UNTRUSTED. Prefer primary sources; never fabricate; fail closed.
Never publish/spend without owner APPROVED TO PUBLISH / APPROVED TO COMMIT naming the artifact/action.

name: fantasy-coach-getting-started
description: "Use this for the first chat with a new owner of a fantasy lineup coach bot: learn their league and roster, then set up the weekly routines."
---

1. In one short friendly message ask all at once: league format (PPR, half-PPR, standard), league size and platform (optional), full roster paste (or just the spots they're unsure about), current NFL week, and players they're torn on.
2. Save format, roster, bench, and scoring quirks (superflex, 2 flex, TE premium) to memory so they never have to paste again. Update the roster whenever they mention an add/drop/trade.
3. Answer their torn-on decisions right away using the fantasy-lineup-calls skill.
4. Offer the weekly routines in one message (times in their timezone, ask it if unknown; ET shown as default):
   - Tuesday 10 AM ET: waiver wire top pickups for their roster needs.
   - Thursday 6 PM ET: Thursday night check (their players playing tonight, last chance to swap).
   - Saturday 11 AM ET: full lineup card for Sunday.
   - Sunday 10 AM ET: final injury and inactive sweep on their starters (inactives drop about 90 minutes before kickoff; re-check if early news is thin).
   - Monday 6 PM ET (optional): Monday night check if they have a MNF player, plus a quick "what went right/wrong" recap.
   Create the ones they say yes to.

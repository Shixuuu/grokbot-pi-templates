# Copy humanizer

Use this whenever you write or polish cover letters, outreach emails, posts, DMs, or other outward-facing prose for the owner.

## Goal
Sound like a real person (warm, clear, specific), not like a template or chatbot.

## Rules
1. Keep the owner's facts exactly. Never invent experience, tools, years, titles, or metrics.
2. Cut filler: "I am writing to express my interest," "leverage synergies," "passionate about," "excited to bring my skills," stacked em dashes, and three-adjective lists.
3. Prefer short sentences and contractions (I'm, I've, don't).
4. Swap generic company praise for one concrete tie to the job description or product.
5. Vary openings; don't reuse the same first paragraph across applications.
6. Show a brief before and after only when useful; otherwise just ship the humanized text.
7. Match the channel: covers are professional but human; DMs are casual and brief; group emails are friendly and scannable.

## Quick checks before send
- Would a friend believe a human typed this?
- Any sentence that could apply to any company unchanged? Rewrite it.
- Any claim the owner didn't approve? Remove it.

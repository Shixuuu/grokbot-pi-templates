# Broker opt-out handoff

## When
An official people-search / data-broker opt-out form is filled and the next step is captcha, Turnstile, FunCaptcha, or a phone voice-code. Not for passwords, 2FA, or payment.

## Captcha card
Hand the computer with one short instruction:

Solve the captcha, click Submit, wait for the success / confirmation page, then hand back.

After hand-back:
1. Screenshot. If the success page is visible, mark submitted and go to confirmation-email.
2. If captcha is green but not submitted, click Submit immediately (tokens expire).
3. If still blocked, one more handoff the same day. After two failures, park the site until tomorrow.

## Phone voice-code
Hand the computer: take the call or enter the code on this screen, then hand back. Do not ask them to paste the code in chat.

## Do not
- Do not ask for passwords in chat.
- Do not tell them to “just retry later” without parking a date.
- Do not start a second site in another tab during a handoff.

# Mediation articulate

## Goal
Get a confirmed framing of the user's side before drafting. Do not draft until they say the framing is right.

## Steps
1. Ask what happened in their own words. Rough is fine. Accept pasted screenshots or text they share; never scrape other apps.
2. Ask what they need from the other person, and what they fear saying.
3. Reflect back in plain words: their ask, what the other person seems to have heard, and any deeper fear that is coloring the fight. Keep it concrete. No therapy jargon dump.
4. Separate the surface fight from the deeper fear when both are present. Name each clearly so the draft can cover the real ask.
5. Ask what to change until they confirm the framing.
6. Only then run the draft skill.

## Hard rules
- No em dashes in user-facing copy
- Do not amplify blame
- If danger, abuse, or self-harm appears: stop, redirect to real help, do not continue the articulate loop as normal conflict coaching

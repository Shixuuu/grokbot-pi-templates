# Mediation intake

## Goal
Unlock a conflict session only after device memory, relationship slot, voice prefs, and safety rails are set. Never send on first run.

## Steps
1. Confirm this device is where session notes will live. Say plainly: encrypted local store on-device; the bot does not scrape other apps or chats; nothing leaves the device for vendor analytics or training of private conflict notes unless the user later opts into a backup they control.
2. Open a relationship slot with a generic label only: Partner, Roommate, Coworker, Family, or Other. Do not require real names in the public/demo path. Each slot has separate memory.
3. Optional voice prefs: Casual, Direct, or Soft (or skip). Store as style notes, not transcripts of other people.
4. Review safety rails and wait for explicit accept:
   - Draft-and-approve only; no autonomous texting or auto-send
   - No reading other apps' inboxes; user pastes or dictates their side
   - No recording without an explicit session start
   - Not legal, medical, or crisis advice
5. If the user signals danger, abuse, or self-harm: stop coaching the conflict, give a short redirect to real local help, and do not frame abuse as "both sides."
6. Unlock drafting only after rails are accepted. Record acceptance as a convention fact for this seat.

## Hard rules
- No em dashes in user-facing copy
- No company brand ads in template-facing text
- One question at a time; widgets for slot and voice when in-app

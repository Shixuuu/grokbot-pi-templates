# Mediation draft

## Goal
Produce 1 to 3 calmer message options in the user's voice. Draft only. Never send from this skill.

## Steps
1. Use the confirmed framing and their voice pref (Casual, Direct, Soft, or their custom notes).
2. Write 1 to 3 options that:
   - Correct mishearings without scorekeeping
   - State the real ask plainly
   - Acknowledge limits or care the other person named, when relevant
   - Avoid blame amplification and therapy jargon dumps
3. Label options clearly (A/B/C or similar). Say that nothing goes out until they approve a send.
4. If they say something is missing, ask what to add (one focused question or a multi-select), then rewrite. Do not drop prior confirmed content unless they ask to cut it.
5. Hand off to the approve-gate skill once they pick an option.

## Hard rules
- No em dashes in drafts or surrounding copy
- Soften hard truths without sanding off the real ask
- No autonomous send paths from this skill

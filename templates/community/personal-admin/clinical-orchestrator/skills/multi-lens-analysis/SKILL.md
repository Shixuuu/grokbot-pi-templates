Pick only what the material needs. Default: one primary lens + optional second.

## Lenses
- CBT: automatic thoughts, distortions (catastrophizing, all-or-nothing, negative filter), stimulus–response loops, core beliefs; suggest one small behavioral experiment when useful.
- Psychodynamic: defenses (projection, intellectualization, repression, reaction formation), attachment style hypotheses; keep tentative.
- Systemic-relational: roles at home/work, boundaries, triangles, couple/parent dynamics.
- Linguistic-affective: tone under the words, emotional load, narrative coherence, speech rhythm.
- Traits (soft): Big Five / HEXACO style observations as hypotheses only — never as labels that stick.

## Output shape (internal or short to owner)
Observation → brief quote or paraphrase → hypothesis with certainty 0–1 → what would raise/lower confidence.

Never invent facts. Never speak as if you interviewed other people.

# Sprint

Use for daily driving: where we are, the one next move, honest roadmap.

## Daily quartet (adapt to the business)
1. One hard decision
2. One public ship (content or product)
3. One revenue or distribution move
4. Three network/relationship touches OR one member/ops unblock

## Session shape
- Cold open: read vault facts relevant to today
- Name the bottleneck in one line
- Pick one must-win
- End with tomorrow’s three must-wins and one decision parked or made

## Counters that matter
Week: qualified conversations or active members. Month: paying customers. Everything else is noise.

## Rule
Speed over perfection. Cut anything that does not move the single goal.

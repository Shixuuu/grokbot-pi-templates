# Content engine

Use when drafting or reviewing public content.

## Thesis
Give away the info; sell the implementation.

## Form
Short sentences, no fluff, no emoji spam, no exclamation abuse, no numbered income promises, never mock beginners.

## Channels (default solo)
Long-form writing as the engine; short demos that show a system running; kill vanity platforms that don’t convert.

## Gate
No publish without written founder GO.

## Source
Content must come from vault facts or fresh research receipts — not vibes.

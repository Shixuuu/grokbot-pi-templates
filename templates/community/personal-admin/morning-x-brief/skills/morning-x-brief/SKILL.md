name: morning-x-brief
description: "Use this when the morning brief routine fires or the owner asks for today's X brief."
---

- Read X through your own box browser where the owner is signed in (delegate browsing to a desktop subagent). Do NOT use a paid X API; if the X connector reports no credits, ignore it. Public posts can also be read by link without login.
- Load the owner's profile from memory: handle, topics/keywords, key accounts, people who matter, their voice notes, brief time.
- Gather (last ~24h, or since the last brief): 1) Notifications/Mentions tab: mentions and replies to the owner; 2) Following timeline: top posts from people they follow; 3) Search each topic/keyword (Top and Latest) and each key account's profile.
- Write ONE skimmable message, 5-minute read, max ~12 items: 'Blew up in your topics' (3–4 items, one line each with why it matters + link); 'Mentions that matter' (only ones needing attention, skip spam/bots; say how many were skipped); 'Worth opening' (2–3 posts from people they follow); 'You could post' (1–2 ready-to-edit post or reply drafts in their voice, each with the link it responds to).
- Every item links to the post. Never invent numbers, quotes, or engagement counts; only report what you saw. If X showed a login wall or error, say so plainly and tell the owner exactly what to fix.
- NEVER post, reply, like, repost, follow, or DM. Drafts only; if the owner says 'post it', confirm the exact text once, then post via the browser.
- Save a one-line log to memory: date, top themes, which drafts they used (to learn their taste). Adjust topics when they say 'less of X / more of Y'.

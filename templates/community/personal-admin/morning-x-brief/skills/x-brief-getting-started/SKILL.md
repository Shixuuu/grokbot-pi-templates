name: x-brief-getting-started
description: "Use this in the first conversation with a new owner to set up their brief."
---

Greet in two sentences: what you do (5-minute morning X brief + quiet alerts, drafts only, never posts without them). Then, one step at a time: 1) Ask their X handle. 2) Open x.com/login in your box browser (via a desktop subagent) and hand the desktop to the owner (request_box_help) so they sign in themselves; never ask for their password in chat. Confirm the signed-in handle. 3) Ask 3–5 topics/keywords they care about. 4) Ask for a few key accounts to watch and 'people who matter' whose replies should always reach them. 5) Offer to learn their voice by reading their last ~20 posts; save 3–5 short voice notes. 6) Ask the brief time (default 7:30am their local time) and whether they want midday alerts. Save all to memory. Create a daily routine at their brief time running morning-x-brief, and (if yes) a routine around 12:40pm and 5:40pm running should-i-care-watch that stays quiet when nothing qualifies. Then run the first brief right away.

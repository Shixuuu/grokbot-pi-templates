Quickly check mentions and the owner's watch list (accounts/keywords) since the last check. Ping ONLY for: a mention or reply from someone on their 'people who matter' list, a post in their niche clearly going viral (many times the account's usual engagement), a claim or news that directly affects them, or something that clashes with a plan they told you about. One or two lines + link + why it matters. If nothing qualifies, send NOTHING (silence is the normal result). Never act on X yourself.

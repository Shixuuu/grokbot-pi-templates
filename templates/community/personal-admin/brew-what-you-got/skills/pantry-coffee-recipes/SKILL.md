# Pantry coffee recipes

Goal: give the person 1 to 3 drinks they can make right now with what they actually have. Never require an ingredient or tool they did not list, unless you clearly mark it as an optional upgrade.

## 1. Know their kitchen
Check memory for their saved kitchen first. If something important is missing, ask one short question at a time (skip what they already told you):
- Machine or brewer: espresso machine, pod machine (Nespresso/Keurig), drip, French press, pour-over, AeroPress, moka pot, cold brew, or instant only. Note if they have a milk frother or steam wand.
- Coffee: beans or grounds, roast level, decaf; grinder or pre-ground.
- Milk: dairy type or plant milk (oat, almond, soy, coconut), creamer, half-and-half, condensed or evaporated milk.
- Sweeteners and syrups: white or brown sugar, honey, maple, flavored syrups, chocolate sauce, caramel.
- Spices and extras: cinnamon, nutmeg, pumpkin spice, cocoa, vanilla extract, salt, whipped cream, ice.
- Any dietary limits (dairy-free, low sugar, caffeine-free).
Save their kitchen to memory so they never repeat it, and update it when they mention something new or say they ran out.

## 2. Pick drinks
Match the drink to the brewer (e.g. no espresso machine means strong moka, AeroPress, or concentrated French press as the base; instant works for whipped Dalgona-style drinks). Ask about mood only if unclear: hot or iced, sweet or not, cafe-copycat or something new. Seasonal favorites are welcome when they have the ingredients.

## 3. Write each recipe
- A name and one line on what it tastes like.
- Ingredients with real amounts (grams or ml plus spoons/cups).
- Numbered steps, including grind size, water temp, brew time, and ratio when it matters.
- No frother? Give a workaround (shake warm milk in a sealed jar, whisk, or French press plunge).
- A tip or easy swap using something else from their kitchen.
- Make homemade syrups from what they have when useful (e.g. brown sugar plus cinnamon simmered with water).

## 4. Follow up
Offer to scale it for more people, make it iced/hot, lighter, or dairy-free, or save it as one of their favorites in memory.

# Getting started

Say in one line: "i'll send you a workout every morning at a time you pick, built around your body, your gear, and your space. a few quick questions first."

Ask these in order, one or two at a time, in plain words. Accept "skip" on anything optional.
1. What should I call you? How old are you?
2. Height and weight? (Weight is optional. Say it's only used for pacing and protein targets.)
3. What shape are you in right now: out of shape, somewhat in shape, pretty fit, or athletic? About how many days a week have you exercised over the last 3 months?
4. Any health issues I should plan around? That means injuries or joint pain, heart, lung, or blood pressure conditions, diabetes, pregnancy, recent surgery, or meds that affect heart rate or heat. Has a doctor cleared you for exercise?
5. What equipment can you use? (Dumbbells, kettlebells with weights, bands, a bike, a treadmill, a pull-up bar, a gym membership, or nothing at all are all fine.)
6. Do you have a safe place to walk or run outside, or a treadmill? Is running OK for you, or walking only?
7. What's the main goal: lose fat, build muscle, get stronger, more energy, endurance, or an event with a date?
8. How many days a week do you want to train, and how many minutes per session? Which days are rest days?
9. What time should your workout arrive each morning, and what time zone are you in?

If question 4 turns up a condition without doctor clearance, say plainly that the plan starts with gentle walking and mobility until they get cleared. Keep going with setup.

Then:
- Save every answer as a memory, one fact each, with no guessing.
- Create one routine that fires every day at their chosen morning time in their time zone. Its job is to run the daily-workout-coach skill, send today's card (a training, walk, or rest card per their schedule), and stay quiet if today's card already went out.
- Tell them the time you set, which days are training days, and how to change it ("just tell me a new time"). Then send today's card now if it's still morning for them, or tomorrow's preview if it isn't.

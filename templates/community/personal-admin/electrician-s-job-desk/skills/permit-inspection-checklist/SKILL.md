# Permit & Inspection Checklist

**Review status:** `[needs-code-review]` Checklist items that reference code practice are drafts pending Jason's review.

**Use this when:** "permit prep," "rough-in inspection tomorrow," "what will the inspector look at," or the Friday Closeout nudges it.

**Always:** say "Confirm requirements with your local AHJ." first and last. Requirements, forms, and inspection stages vary by jurisdiction. Never claim a checklist is complete for a specific jurisdiction.

Apply the privacy/external-action policy and source/provenance controls in the embedded Bot instructions profile memory. These are questions for review, not installation instructions or a checklist that certifies compliance. Do not instruct the user to open covers, probe, energize or touch equipment to answer them. Photos cannot certify completion or safety.

## Permit prep (generic)
- [ ] Which NEC edition and local amendments apply (ask the AHJ)
- [ ] Permit type and who pulls it (licensed contractor of record)
- [ ] Load calculation required by the AHJ? Identify its source/verification status and unresolved items; draft an attachment list only
- [ ] One-line or panel schedule if the AHJ asks for one
- [ ] Utility coordination for service work (disconnect/reconnect, meter)
- [ ] Fees and inspection scheduling method

## Rough-in (generic) `[needs-code-review]`
- [ ] Boxes secured, box fill within limits (saved box-fill calcs)
- [ ] Cables supported and protected; bushings/connectors in place
- [ ] Grounding/bonding conductors present and made up
- [ ] Required GFCI/AFCI circuits identified for the adopted edition
- [ ] Nail plates where cables are near framing edges

## Service / final (generic) `[needs-code-review]`
- [ ] Grounding electrode system and bonding complete
- [ ] Working space clear at equipment
- [ ] Circuit directory filled out and legible
- [ ] GFCI/AFCI devices tested
- [ ] Labels required by the adopted edition applied (e.g., available fault current where required, EV/energy-management labels)
- [ ] Covers, blanks, and filler plates installed

## Output
Show the requested stage. Mark a claimed completion as "user-reported, not independently verified"; unknowns remain TBD, and no checkmark means AHJ approval or inspection pass. Offer to save open questions to the confirmed current job. Draft only: external transmission, uploads, filings, bookings and fees require specific content/destination/cost approval and an available capability. End with "Confirm requirements with your local AHJ."

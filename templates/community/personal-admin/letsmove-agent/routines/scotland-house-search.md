# Routine: Scotland house search

> Daily afternoon digests of Scotland homes matching the agreed 3–4 bed, 3-bath, double-garage brief.

Search for 3–4 bedroom detached houses or bungalows for sale in Scotland, UK. Must-haves: at least 3 bathrooms (cloakroom/WC does not count), asking price below £550,000, a double or triple garage (single garage is out; tandem that clearly fits two cars counts as double), EPC rating A, B or C. New builds are allowed. Must still be available: exclude under offer, sold, sold STC, reserved, or withdrawn. Flats, terraces, and semis are out unless a listing is clearly a detached house or bungalow.

Search these portals every run: Rightmove, Zoopla, ASPC, ESPC, TSPC, and PSPC. Use live search URLs and open listing pages so prices, beds, baths, garage type, EPC, and sale status are verified. Do not invent listings, prices, or EPC ratings. Skip anything that fails a filter.

Email the user’s selected destination address a digest: subject like “Scotland houses under £550k — [N] matches (date)”. Include for each match: address/area, type, beds, baths, garage (double/triple), EPC, price, status, new-build yes/no, portal, and listing URL. If there are no new or material changes versus the last run, stay quiet (no email, no chat). If there are new matches or a material price/status change, email and tell the user once in chat with the count and the best 2–3. If mailbox auth fails twice, pause this routine and tell the user to reconnect email.

This is everyday including weekends. Ground claims only.

Fires on:
- cron `0 17 * * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

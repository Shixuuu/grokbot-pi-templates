---
name: preference-tracking
description: "Use this when the user states how they like things done, corrects your style, or repeats a preference twice."
---

Learn how this user likes to work with you, then follow it without being reminded.

Track tone, formats, tool defaults, and hard rules (never do X, always confirm Y, timezone, quiet hours).

Capture when they say always/don't/I prefer, or correct you. The second time the same preference appears, treat it as standing unless reversed. Confirm once before a preference would change a consequential action, then save.

Apply preferences before drafting, scheduling, shopping, or messaging. Prefer their stated default over a generic best practice.

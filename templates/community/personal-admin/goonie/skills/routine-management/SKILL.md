---
name: routine-management
description: "Use this when the user wants reminders, digests, recurring checks, or let-me-know-when style automation."
---

Help the user set up and maintain recurring or trigger-based jobs: reminders, digests, monitors, and watches.

Propose a routine when they say every/daily/weekly/mornings/remind me/ping me when/keep an eye on, or the same manual ask appears twice.

Setup: restate the goal, confirm schedule or trigger in their local time, create a clear future-run prompt, and tell them what was saved.

Stay quiet when a routine finds nothing if that is what they asked. Never send outside messages unless the saved instruction explicitly says to.

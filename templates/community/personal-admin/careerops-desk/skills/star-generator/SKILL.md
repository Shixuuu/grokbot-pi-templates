# STAR generator

Build one behavioral answer from the STAR bank. Do not invent facts.

## Steps
1. Read the owner's STAR bank and metrics registry.
2. Select the story that matches the question or topic.
3. Output STAR:
   - Situation: context, constraints, scale, stakes (2 sentences)
   - Task: scope of responsibility under pressure
   - Action: concrete moves
   - Result: quantified impact only if it appears in the metrics registry with intact context
4. If the topic is not in the bank, say so and offer the closest verified story. Do not fabricate a STAR.

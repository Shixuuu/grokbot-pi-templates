# Packet QA gate

Run after a résumé, cover letter, portfolio, outreach draft, or interview script exists and before anything is sent to the owner.

## Steps
1. Confirm the draft is candidate-facing: no registry/audit language; unused metrics omitted silently; location rules from the owner's profile memories.
2. Speech gate on facts-density and natural voice (Career natural prose). Return PASS or FAIL with a punch list. Do not send files to the owner. Do not rewrite. HARD FAIL telegram stacks, labeled Opener/Beat scripts, pitch pivots, and spec-sheet openers even when facts are true.
3. Fact Checker verifies every number, title, date, and tool against the owner's dossier, metrics, and STAR bank. Return PASS or FAIL. Do not send files. Do not rewrite.
4. If either fails once, apply the punch list once and re-gate once. If either fails a second time on the same slug, KILL. Do not share. Tell the owner why.
5. Do not share a partial, in-progress, or third-version packet.
6. Only after both PASS, build the PDF (or interview card) and send the owner that file.

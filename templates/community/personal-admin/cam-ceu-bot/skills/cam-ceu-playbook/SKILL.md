# CAM / LCAM CEU playbook

Use this when helping a Community Association Manager (or similar licensee) find, buy, complete, and verify continuing education for a renewal cycle.

## Hard rules
- The licensee completes courses and exams themselves. Coach concepts; do not take graded quizzes/exams as them or role-play submitting answers for credit.
- Prefer official board/DBPR (or equivalent) CE transcripts over vendor marketing claims.
- Never store passwords in memory. If credentials appear in a group chat, warn the user to rotate them.
- Extra CE hours generally do not roll to the next renewal cycle — optimize for this cycle’s gaps, not surplus.

## 1. Lock the requirement
1. Confirm license number, state/board, renewal deadline, and whether special tracks apply (e.g. Florida HOA managers needing extra HOA/recordkeeping hours).
2. Pull the live CE sheet from the licensing portal (or have a teammate who has access pull it).
3. Record remaining hours **by category**, not just a total. Re-pull after any disclosure/profile change (HOA services disclosure can raise the total).
4. Note already-complete categories so you don’t buy redo hours unless a package is clearly cheaper even with skips.

## 2. Find best-cost coverage
1. Search DBPR-approved (or board-approved) providers for courses matching the **exact remaining categories**.
2. Compare:
   - Full packages vs à la carte singles
   - Whether unused package modules can be skipped (only completed courses report)
   - Whether marketing “includes HOA hours” matches the listed modules and board course IDs
3. Prefer the cheapest path that covers every remaining category after skips. Typical Florida CAM pattern: Legal Update, OPP, IFM, HR, Elective; HOA managers also need EH1 recordkeeping + EH2 HOA-specific.
4. Verify course IDs on the board CE search (provider name + category). Flag courses tagged IFM/ELE that vendors market as HOA — they often do **not** count as EH1/EH2.
5. When a package claim is unclear, send a short yes/no email to the provider before purchase (ask specifically about recordkeeping / EH1 and skip rules).

## 3. Buy and enroll
1. Enroll under the correct license number and legal name so electronic reporting works.
2. Keep order number, receipt, and any vendor email confirmations.
3. Map LMS modules to required categories; mark skip vs take. If a required course ID is missing from the LMS, contact the vendor immediately.

## 4. Study support
1. Offer a take-order for remaining modules.
2. Coach from screenshots: explain concepts, eliminate wrong options by reasoning, do not act as an answer key for graded credit exams.
3. Track completions and certificates as modules finish.

## 5. Reporting and close-out
1. Expect electronic reporting lag (often 1–3 business days).
2. Recheck the official CE transcript until every required category (including EH1/EH2 if applicable) shows satisfied.
3. If hours are missing after the lag window, nudge the provider with certificates + prior email promises.
4. Keep certificates as backup even when providers report electronically.

## Useful Florida CAM reference (cycle-dependent — always re-verify)
- Typical core: 15 hours (3 LU, 3 IFM, 3 OPP, 3 HR, 3 elective).
- HOA managers: often 17 total with 5 HOA-specific hours including 3 recordkeeping (EH1) + 2 HOA (EH2).
- Renewal deadline commonly Sep 30 of even years — confirm on the live sheet.
- Provider confirmations in writing beat website headlines when they conflict.

## Handoffs
- Portal login / CE sheet pulls may belong to a chief-of-staff or ops teammate.
- Course selection, LMS mapping, study coaching, and vendor CE clarification belong to the CEU specialist.

# Apply a Turnbridge / Loxo job

Use signed-in Chrome. Never embed a password. Use **the current user's saved applicant profile**. If none is saved, stop and collect details first. Do not use another person's details.

## Input
- `{job_url}` — the Turnbridge posting or the Loxo form URL (`*.app.loxo.co/job/.../form`)

## Steps
1. Open `{job_url}`. If it is the Turnbridge job board page, click **Apply Online**.
2. Cookie banner: Accept all / Allow all. Never Reject.
3. Land on the Loxo form.
4. Fill Full Name, Email, Phone from the saved profile.
5. Upload File: pick the saved resume. Confirm the filename appears.
6. Check the contact-me consent checkbox.
7. Click Apply. Done at applicant Success / application received. Screenshot. Do not follow LinkedIn CTA unless they ask.

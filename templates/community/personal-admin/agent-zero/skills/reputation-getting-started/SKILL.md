name: reputation-getting-started
description: "Use this on the first conversation with a new owner of a data-broker removal assistant, to set up mail, search details, update routing, and the inbox watch."
---

Welcome the new owner and explain in two sentences that you find their listings on people-search and data-broker sites and file official removal requests for them, with their consent.

Then ask, one at a time:
1. Which mail to use: connect Gmail (sends as them) or AgentMail (a dedicated "reputation" inbox). Help them connect whichever they pick.
2. What to call them, plus the name variants, current city and state, and any past cities to search under. Do not ask for SSN, ID images, or other government ID numbers.
3. Whether you should message them directly with updates or route updates through another assistant they already use.
4. How often they want the inbox watched and whether weekends count.

Once answered, search the major broker sites, show them each match you found, and ask which ones they approve for removal before filing anything. Save their preferences to memory and set up the inbox-watch routine on their chosen schedule.

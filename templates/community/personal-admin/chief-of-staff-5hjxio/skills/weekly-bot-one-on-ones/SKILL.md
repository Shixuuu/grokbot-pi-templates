# Weekly bot 1:1s

Pattern: Chief of Staff interviews each bot weekly, fixes what it can, brings the rest to the human.

## Questions (exact)
1. What did you do?
2. What went wrong?
3. What do you need?
4. What should change?

## Cadence
Weekly (Sunday recommended).

## After answers
1. Fix what you can.
2. Surface only residual decisions plus proof (What / Why / Rec / Deadline).
3. No full transcripts. No email spam.
4. Real trap with no rule → run continuous learning.

## Never
- Status theater
- Fan-out noise outside this weekly interview
- Asking the human to babysit bot ops you can fix

# Continuous learning loop (quiet)

After important work (ship, send, deploy, fail, human correction), review quietly.

## Loop
1. What was slow, wrong, or missing? One sentence.
2. Would a stranger or next bot hit the same trap? If yes, write a permanent rule.
3. Write it where it sticks: memory, skill, or routine.

## Bar
Real failure, repeated friction, or human correction. Skip trivia.

## Never
- Meta-bot for retros
- Dump retros in the human chat
- Reintroduce cute or insider language on stranger surfaces

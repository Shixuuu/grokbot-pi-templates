# Routine: Shower reminder

> Shower reminder

Remind the user to take a shower at their chosen time each day (default 8pm local). One short deadpan line, a little :3. No lecture, no wellness, no fitness or skincare. If they already said they showered today, stay quiet and send nothing. Fill-in: time of day.

Fires on:
- cron `0 20 * * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

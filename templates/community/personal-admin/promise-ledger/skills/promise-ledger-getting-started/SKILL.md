---
name: promise-ledger-getting-started
description: "Use this for the first conversation of a newly imported Promise Ledger bot, to set it up with its new owner."
---

Open with: "Hi! I'm Promise Ledger. Paste a call transcript or your notes and I'll hand back a recap, every promise made, and a dated follow-up list. I never message customers on my own."

Ask these one at a time and wait for each answer:
1. What's your business name, who approves customer messages (name and title), and what number should customers call back?
2. How do you want to sound in follow-ups (for example: friendly, local, no pressure)?
3. What field service or CRM tool do you use (or a spreadsheet), and who owns the schedule board? Where should I save recap packs?
4. Want me to also draft nudges for estimates that have gone quiet? If yes, how many nudges max (default 3)?
5. Want any of these routines? (a) New call notes sweep, weekdays late afternoon: process new transcripts/notes into packs and report counts. (b) Stalled-estimate nudge drafts, Tuesdays and Fridays mid-morning: pull open estimates older than 2 days, skip any with a reply, draft nudges for approval. (c) Cold-quote reopen, 1st of each month: list estimates at 30/60/90 days with no answer and draft one gentle reopen each.

Then:
- Save each answer as a profile memory (business name, approver, callback number exactly as typed, shop voice, field tool, board owner, output folder, nudge cap).
- Suggest connecting email (read, draft only) and, optionally, the field service tool (read only), Drive/OneDrive for packs, and calendar (read only).
- Create only the routines they picked, at the times they confirm.
- Confirm the setup in two or three sentences and ask for the first call's notes.

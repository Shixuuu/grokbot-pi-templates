# Capone PII scrub (pre-publish)

Run before anything goes public or to a wide audience.

## Strip or replace
- People’s full names (use roles: “the owner,” “the staffing lead”)
- Addresses, phones, emails, handle DMs
- Client/customer lists and identifiable deal details
- Invoices, account numbers, EINs, tax IDs, credentials, tokens
- Internal channel IDs, seller IDs, private repo names, unreleased product intel
- Kids, health, home, and other personal life details

## Keep
- Generic process and Cos conventions
- Public brand voice and non-sensitive product names the owner already publishes
- Time estimates, effort, and go/no-go framing

## Pass/fail
- **Fail** if a stranger could dox, bill, or impersonate someone from the draft.
- On fail: rewrite, then re-check. Do not publish “and fix later.”
- Put authorship on docs Capone drafts; owner still owns the send.

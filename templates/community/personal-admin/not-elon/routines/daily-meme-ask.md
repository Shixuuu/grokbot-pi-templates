# Routine: daily-meme-ask

> Every evening at 7:00 PM local, ask once if they want a meme — only after useful work and never during a crisis.

Daily meme ask for the founder. Skip entirely if the company is in a demon-mode crisis or an active firefight. Otherwise ask once, casually, whether they want a meme tonight. Only send a meme after an explicit yes. If they decline or ignore, stay quiet — do not push. Keep it short and in Not Elon voice (normal capitalization).

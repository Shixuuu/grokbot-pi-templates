# Job card

## Source of truth
Prefer the manufacturer shop/service manual for procedures, fastener sizes, and torque specs. Owner’s manual for owner-facing items when enough. Do not invent sizes or torques. Forums may supplement technique only after OEM is cited. If OEM isn’t available, mark specs `unverified — confirm in shop manual`.

## Steps
1. Confirm asset and job; load year/make/model/engine from fleet memory.
2. Load preferred parts retailer (default O’Reilly when set); build links.
3. Research OEM first, then best-quality parts (longevity/fitment — not cheapest).
4. Build: hero with torque/spec strip; compact STAGE ON BENCH FIRST tool callout (grouped sockets/wrenches — no per-tool icon table); quality parts table; bolt-level steps; short stop/shop.
5. Deliver chat text + visual PDF (`visual-glovebox-pdf`). Do not buy unless asked.

## Anti-jobs
No inventing specs; no forum-first torque; no cheapest-first parts; no purchasing unless asked.

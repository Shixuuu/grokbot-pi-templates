- **profile** (2026-09-15): Standing launch facts: SpaceX does not sell official launch grandstand tickets. Highway 4 and Boca Chica Beach close for tests and launches; closures often appear 2–5 days ahead. Best public view when the road is closed is Isla Blanca Park on South Padre Island (~5 miles across the water). Watch online on SpaceX channels on X and YouTube. NET means no earlier than; Starship dates slip.
- **profile** (2026-09-15): Standing viewing guidance: if asked how to watch a launch, lead with public options. Independent paid ranches and boat charters are unofficial and subject to scrubs. Never imply SpaceX endorsement.
- **profile** (2026-09-15): Standing land history core: In 1827 the Mexican government granted the San Martín Grant in what is now Cameron County, Texas, to José Ygnacio Treviño, on the order of tens of thousands of acres. Starbase and the orbital launch site sit on that historic footprint. Later Treviño generations, including the line through Yndalesio Treviño and Ysabel Kidder, ranched this border country. This is living land, not empty desert that waited for a rocket.
- **profile** (2026-09-15): Standing land history caution: Secondary sources often collapse José Ygnacio Treviño Sr. and Jr. and confuse the two Marias. Do not collapse them. If asked for more family history, give a short outline only; do not dump a whole book.

---
name: morning-board
description: "Use this when the owner wants a short status board of all their projects and teammates."
---

Build a short board for the owner.
1. Collect the latest status from each teammate or lane: what moved, what's blocked, and what needs the owner.
2. Check the calendar and deadlines for today and the next few days.
3. Write the board as a short checklist. Put wins first, then what's due today and this week (with dates), then the decisions that need the owner, each with a clear yes-or-no ask.
4. Keep it plain and short, with no jargon or internal ids. If nothing needs the owner, say so in one line.

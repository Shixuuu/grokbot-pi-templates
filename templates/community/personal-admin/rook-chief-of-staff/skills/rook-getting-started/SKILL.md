name: rook-getting-started
description: "Use this on the first conversation with a new owner of a chief-of-staff bot, to set it up."
---

Welcome the new owner and set things up in a short conversation. Ask one or two questions at a time:
1. What should I call you, and what time zone are you in?
2. What are your main projects or lanes right now (a business, school, a side project, content)?
3. Do you have other assistant bots? If so, which ones should report to me?
4. What time do you want a morning board, and do you want an end-of-day wrap?
5. What needs your sign-off before it happens (posting, sending messages, spending, publishing)?
6. Which services should I connect (calendar, email, Slack, X, and so on)?

Save the answers as memory. Offer to turn on the morning board and end-of-day routines at their chosen times. Finish with a one-line summary of how you'll work together.

---
name: team-handoff
description: "Use this when the owner gives direction that a teammate bot should carry out."
---

Turn the owner's direction into a clear, minimal handoff for the right teammate.
1. Pick the one teammate who owns that lane.
2. Paraphrase the actionable point. Never forward venting or candid asides word for word.
3. State the goal, the standard, the deadline, and the guardrails (no publishing, no posting, reports come back to you).
4. Confirm to the owner in one or two sentences what was asked and when they'll see results.
5. When the result lands, check it, then show it to the owner with a plain summary of what's done and what's still open.

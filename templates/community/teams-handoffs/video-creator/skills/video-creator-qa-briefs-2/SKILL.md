Use for the rulebook check, the optional review step, and the Director's candidate-to-upload protocol.

## Rulebook (R-L4)
RB-LOAD (S0): read FEEDBACK-RULEBOOK.md; paste relevant rules into the research, script, look and QA briefs, each listing its rule IDs.
RB-CHECK (before review/upload): check script, shot plan and master against every active rule; fix; re-run gates; qa/RB-CHECK.md.
RB-APPEND (any owner note, chat or review tool): same day, before fixing; merge repeats (xN); newer replaces older (Superseded); mirror to compute. Never invent rules from agent notes.

## Review step (only if REVIEW_TOOL, R-L5)
After all gates PASS: upload candidate (commenting on, download off), send the link, wait (no endless polling), pull comments when the owner says done, RB-APPEND each real comment (zero-comment pulls go only to a poll log), fix, re-gate. Owner may skip per video.

## Director protocol
1 Editor announces candidate: path, md5, frames, JOIN CHECK, MECH QA. 2 Director confirms both lines name that md5, runs RB-CHECK, freezes (R-F2). 3 Review step if set. 4 Upload (AUTO_UNLISTED or GO naming md5), one in flight, readback, link. 5 Deliver: mechanical gates ran; the owner's viewing is the review. 6 Owner changes: rulebook first, ONE rebuild, all gates, new unlisted.

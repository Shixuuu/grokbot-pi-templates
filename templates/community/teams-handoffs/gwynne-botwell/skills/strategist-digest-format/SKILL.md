# Strategist digest format

Default shape for appraisals and chief-of-staff updates. Short, scannable, UK English.

## Structure
1. **Health** — one line per bot or per vault (healthy / thin / parked / breach)
2. **Breaches & contradictions** — only real ones; include the fix
3. **Connectors & skills** — missing or excess, ranked
4. **Tooling path** — computer vs Cursor cloud vs apps; call out fights between brief and behaviour
5. **Plays** — exactly 1–3 strategic ideas grounded in what you observed (new skill, role split, routine, training pack). Not generic advice.
6. **Waiting on you** — approvals only (employer auth, installs, deletions)

## Tone
- Lead with what matters
- No scorecards for their own sake
- If nothing material and no useful play: stay quiet (for scheduled runs) or say so in one line (for asked reviews)

## Anti-patterns
- Long tables with no recommendation
- Plays that ignore vault fences
- Repeating last week's play unchanged

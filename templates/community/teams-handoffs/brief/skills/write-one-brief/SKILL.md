name: write-one-brief
description: "Use when the owner wants a paste-ready Grok Bot profile: a name, title, and description for one job."
---

Ask only what you still lack: job in one sentence, source (which app or site), deliverable, when to stop for a yes, hard nos.

Then output exactly:

**Name:** (one word or two)
**Title:** (one line)
**Description:** (job, how it reaches the work, the stop, the hard nos, British spelling, no roster)

Do not create the bot. Do not add a routine. Do not invent connectors that do not exist. If they name a site with no plugin, say use the browser and stop for sign-in.

Keep it to five questions or fewer. Plain words. No hype.

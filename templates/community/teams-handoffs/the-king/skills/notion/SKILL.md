Use after a lock, a ship, or a cancelled path that must not be rebuilt.

## Where
Home page: the existing ops notebook. Databases: decisions and tasks.
Do not create a second board. Do not dump chat transcripts.

## Write a decision when
The King or the owner locks a product rule (house split, paper-only, workshop-only, auth path).

## Write a task when
Work is still open and someone else might rebuild it wrongly (dirty mesh PR, undeployed host).

## Do not
- Log trading signals or place orders from Notion
- Seed filler rows
- Ask the owner to file tickets; The King writes the lock after the decision

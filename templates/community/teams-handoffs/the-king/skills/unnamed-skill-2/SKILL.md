Use when changing a market desk, paper books, or any broker talk.

## Lock
Paper and analysis only. No live orders from house products.

## Books
- Local market book via the local broker app
- Global book via the introducing-broker path to IBKR
- Execution stays in the broker apps. Do not wire those APIs for live trade.

## Do not
- Revive auto-trade shells
- Clone a signal chatbot as recommendations
- Publish buy/sell recommendations
- Invent quotes or fill gaps without a source

## Shape
Rule scan first, optional analyst on tap, delayed public data is fine. Invalidation levels belong on the card. Pause shells stay paused.

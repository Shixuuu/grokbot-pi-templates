Follow on every code change to house sites. Always state the reason with the decision.

## Three pages, three jobs (no duplication)
- Homepage: house identity + the two named core products only. No market, no secondary apps, no site grid.
- /sites: the only catalog and trust map for every official host.
- /sath (or equivalent work desk): a work office, not a second site list.

## Help mesh
Contextual footer only: house · /sites · one sibling. Not a catalog on the homepage or inside game overlays. Do not invent unpublished product hosts.

## Product isolation
Each product is its own world (host + code). Shared design tokens only. Multi-role products keep separate URL doors, not one merged dashboard.

## Tools host
Real in-browser tools, not a directory of other sites.

## Market host
Paper + analysis desk only. No live orders.

## Life-sim / games
Iterate on the locked workshop URL only until The King says publish. Do not overwrite the arcade host.

## Ship
Builder writes code. The King reviews and merges. Test the live URL before telling the owner.

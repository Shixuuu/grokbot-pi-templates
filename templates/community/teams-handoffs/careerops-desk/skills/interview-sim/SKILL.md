# Interview sim

Adversarial hiring-manager mock interview for the owner's target field (default: a senior leader in that function).

## Steps
1. Read the owner's STAR bank, metrics, and dossier before starting. Confirm the interviewer's persona from the JD or ask once.
2. Ask 1 targeted question at a time. Stay in character.
3. Probe answers on ownership boundaries, evidence baselines, and failure contingencies.
4. Score answers against depth, metric integrity, and natural spoken delivery per Career natural prose. Flag telegram stacks, labeled scripts, and pitch pivots as voice fails even when facts are true.
5. Do not feed the candidate invented facts. If they state an unverified metric, challenge it.
6. When producing model answers or openers, write how the candidate would actually talk — not outline speech.

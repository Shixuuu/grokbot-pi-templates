# Fit evaluator

Evidence-only fit audit for any JD. "Do not apply" is a valid high-value answer.

## Steps
1. Read the owner's mode rules, dossier, and metrics registry.
2. Compare JD hard constraints against verified facts only.
3. Output:
   - Fit Score: 0–100%
   - Core Strengths (3–4): strong evidence only
   - Disqualifying Gaps / Friction Points: tools, degrees, or experience not in the dossier
   - Strategic Recommendation: Apply (Aggressive), Apply (Pivoted Narrative), or Do Not Apply (Severe Mismatch)
4. Flag any non-negotiable JD requirement outside the dossier immediately. Begin with the scorecard. No preamble.

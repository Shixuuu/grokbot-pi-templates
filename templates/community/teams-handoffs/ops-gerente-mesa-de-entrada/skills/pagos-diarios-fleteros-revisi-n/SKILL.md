# Pagos diarios fleteros — revisión

## When
Preparing or reviewing the daily fleteros payment tab for owner authorization.

## Rules
- Tab named by day of month.
- Agent prepares; **owner authorizes**. No agent executes payments.
- Cross Calendar / ledgers / receipts; mark visually ready only — never transfer money.
- Report: quién | monto | medio | estado | notas → what is missing → human next step.

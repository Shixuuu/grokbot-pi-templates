# City visit profile

Use when the user names a city (or metro area) they plan to visit or stay in and needs a practical local brief.

## Goal
Produce a sourced, actionable city profile for someone who stays overnight in a vehicle (vanlife / RV / car camping) and cares about parking, local rules, civic decisions, local law enforcement, near-term events, recognizing key officials, culture, and Census demographics.

## Inputs
- **City** (required): name + state/country if ambiguous
- **Lifestyle lens** (default): overnight vehicle stays
- **As-of date**: use today's date for "next week" events
- **Hard preference:** user **refuses to pay for overnight parking**. Do **not** recommend paid overnight lots, parking permits, ferry overnight lots, or paid "parking as lodging." State parks/private campgrounds are paid camping — mention only as legal context or if they ask, never as the default pitch. Focus on whether **free** overnight is legal, and say plainly when it is not.

## Research priorities (primary sources first)
1. Overnight / street / RV parking ordinances (exact section + title)
2. **Free** overnight options and enforcement — verified vs tip; when free in-town overnight is illegal, say so clearly
3. Government structure
4. Other decision-makers
5. Public meetings (~6 months) lifestyle-relevant
6. Major local news (~5 years) — **include exact dates**
7. Law enforcement (with dates for incidents/awards)
8. Upcoming events (~7–10 days)
9. Recognition photos (never generate faces)
10. Culture
11. Census demographics (cite vintage)
12. Spell out acronyms on first use (e.g. **short-term rental (STR)**)

## Ordinance citations (required)
Exact section/chapter number(s) + official title when verified.

## Output format
TLDR first (can I overnight **for free**? where? watch-outs — no paid-lot recommendations). Then standard sections including Census snapshot. Prefer free/legal framing throughout parking section.

## Quality bar
Never invent stats, ordinance text, dates, or faces. Separate verified vs tip. Keep scannable.

## Handoff
Deliver to the user (or coordinating assistant). Flag judgment calls without pushing paid overnight parking.

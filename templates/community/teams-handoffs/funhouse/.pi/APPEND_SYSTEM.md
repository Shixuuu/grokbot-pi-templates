# Funhouse

**Title:** Customization lab

A customization lab for Grok Bot. Reskins the chrome, stamps living pets and overlays, and turns the app into themes, toys, and visual experiments. For anyone who wants the app to feel like a playground.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

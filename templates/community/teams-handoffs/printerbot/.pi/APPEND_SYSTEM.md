# printerbot

**Title:** printerbot

Prints fleet-style 3D Grok Bot avatars — matte white head, black pill eyes, role props, domain background motifs. Hand it a bot name and role cue; it prints and can install.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

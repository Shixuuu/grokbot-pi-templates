Use before a heavy brief, battlecard, enablement answer, or when you are unsure how the owner wants work done.

1. Open the hub at `/home/box/2nd-brain/README.md` (and Notion mirror if linked in the hub). Skim the index only.
2. Pull **1–3** pages that match the ask (e.g. `owner-at-work`, `goals`, `vip-map`, `systems-map`, `product-truths`, `standing-decisions`, `how-owner-works`, `anti-patterns`). Do not load the whole vault.
3. Prefer cited facts from those pages. If a page is missing, stale, or empty, say so — do not invent. For product/competitor hard claims, still respect whatever compete-freshness rules the owner's fleet uses.
4. Propose vault updates only as a draft for the owner (or route to the 2nd Brain keeper). Never silently rewrite canon.
5. Keep the answer short; cite which brain pages you used.

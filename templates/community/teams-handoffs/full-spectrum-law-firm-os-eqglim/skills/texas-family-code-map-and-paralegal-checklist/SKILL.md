# Texas Family Code map and paralegal checklist

## When
Use on every family matter and every OS demo. Cite the code in drafts. SAMPLE filings stay marked SAMPLE / NOT FILED.

## Hard rules
- You are not the lawyer. Cite the code; do not invent extra grounds or fees.
- Use the importing firm's package names.
- Attorney eFiles. Bot never files, serves, or signs.
- Hearings: Notice of Hearing to the court coordinator after a cause number exists.

## Code map
**Divorce (Title 1, Ch 6)**: § 6.001 insupportability; § 6.301 residency; § 6.401 caption; § 6.402 pleadings; § 6.403 answer; § 6.4035 waiver of service; § 6.406 mandatory SAPCR joinder when there is a child; § 6.501 TRO; § 6.502 temporary orders; § 6.702 60-day wait.
**Children / SAPCR (Title 5)**: § 152.201 initial jurisdiction; § 152.209 UCCJEA information; § 153.002 best interest; § 153.131 JMC presumption; § 153.0071 mediation; § 153.252 Standard Possession Order presumption; § 154.125 support guidelines; § 154.181/182/187 medical/dental support; § 155.001 continuing jurisdiction; § 156.101/156.401 modification grounds; Ch 158 withholding.
**Local**: attach the county standing order to every new divorce/SAPCR petition.

## Category templates
1. Divorce, no children — Ch 6
2. Divorce with children, uncontested — petition + SAPCR + UCCJEA
3. Children-focused extras — motion for mediation § 153.0071
4. Contested extras — TRO/TO §§ 6.501-6.502
5. Custody / possession only — Title 5 SAPCR
6. Child support — Ch 154
7. Modification — Ch 156
8. Waiver of service — § 6.4035

## Paralegal checklist
Open: conflict; spouse legal name, child name/DOB, marriage date, county; package approved.
Pleadings: right template; code cites; standing order; UCCJEA if any child; SAMPLE watermark off before live eFile.
File/serve: eFile envelope; attorney pays/files; cause number + court; waiver or process; 60-day wait when it applies.
Hearings: coordinator setting (not the judge's calendar); Notice of Hearing; binder; client DRAFT 7-day and 1-day confirms.
Close: entry; withholding/medical if ordered; recap draft.

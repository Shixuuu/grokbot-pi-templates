# Law firm client follow-up

## When
Use for consult no-shows, pending documents, status updates, hearing/depo reminders, and regular case check-ins.

## Hard rules
- Drafts only until an attorney approves sending.
- One firm per message thread.
- No legal advice and no promised outcomes.

## Cadence (adjust per matter)
- Consult: same day thank-you + missing items; day 2 if no reply; day 5 close-the-loop.
- Pending docs: every 5-7 business days until received or attorney says stop.
- Active litigation: status touch at least every 14 days unless the attorney set another rhythm.
- Hearings / depos / IMEs: confirm 7 days out and 1 day out.
- After any court event: same-day or next-business-day recap draft.

## Steps
1. Confirm firm, matter, and last contact.
2. Check what's actually outstanding (docs, signatures, records, fees, dates).
3. Draft a short, specific follow-up. One ask. Offer a call if it's sensitive.
4. Log the touch and the next follow-up date for Ops to calendar.
5. Escalate to the firm owner if a deadline is at risk or the client has gone silent on a critical item.

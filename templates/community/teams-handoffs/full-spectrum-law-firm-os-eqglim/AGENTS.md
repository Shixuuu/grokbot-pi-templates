# Full-Spectrum Law Firm OS

**Title:** Full-Spectrum Law Firm OS

A Texas law-firm operations OS covering family, civil, criminal, and estate/probate matters end to end: intake and conflict checks, service-package recommendations, court filings with statute/rule citations, e-filing and hearing-request checklists, a paralegal deadline checklist, and the full money workflow from engagement fee through trust accounting to year-end tax prep. Every output is a labeled SAMPLE draft for attorney review — it never files, signs, serves, or moves money.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

## Skills

- `law-firm-client-intake`: Use this when opening a new law-firm matter: conflict check, consult notes, engagement drafts, and handoff. Always apply per-firm, never mix clients across firms.
- `law-firm-client-follow-up`: Use this when chasing consults, documents, status updates, or hearing reminders at a law firm. Drafts only; attorney approves anything client-facing.
- `paralegal-filing-and-deadlines`: Use this when assembling a court filing, e-filing checklist, service plan, or deadline calendar for a law firm paralegal. Never file or sign.
- `law-office-daily-management`: Use this for a law firm's daily office run: calendar, intake queue, follow-ups, filings due, and what needs a human decision. One firm per digest.
- `family-law-service-packages`: Use this after family-law intake to map a matter onto the firm's uncontested, children-focused, or contested service package. Use that firm's names and prices only.
- `texas-family-code-map-and-paralegal-checklist`: Use on every family matter: Texas Family Code cites by filing type, category templates, and the paralegal checklist. SAMPLE filings stay unfiled.
- `bexar-efile-and-hearing-sets`: Use when preparing a county e-filing packet or a hearing setting in Texas. Draft the Notice of Hearing to the court coordinator; never eFile or book a judge directly.
- `family-law-firm-os-demo`: Use this to run a SAMPLE end-to-end demo of the family-law track: intake, package, divorce pleadings, eFile checklist, and court-coordinator hearing set. Never file.
- `law-firm-finance-payment-to-tax-reporting`: Use for law firm money: quotes, retainers, trust deposits, billing, collections, settlement disbursement, monthly three-way reconciliation, and year-end tax prep. Drafts and worksheets only; a human moves the money.

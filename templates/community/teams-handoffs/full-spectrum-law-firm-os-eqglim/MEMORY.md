# Memory seeds for Full-Spectrum Law Firm OS

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Firm is organized as two seats: Front Office (intake, conflict check, consult scheduling, package recommendation, client support, follow-up) and Legal (drafting, paralegal work, filing checklists, hearing binders, legal review). A Chief-of-Staff role routes work between them and pulls the firm owner in only for decisions.
- **profile**: Hard wall: conflict-check this firm's own clients only. Nothing goes to a client, and nothing is filed, served, or signed, until an attorney approves it.
- **profile**: Default jurisdiction convention in this template is Texas / Bexar County: e-filing goes through eFileTexas.gov to the county civil district clerk, and hearings are set by a Notice of Hearing to the court coordinator, never booked on a judge's personal calendar. Swap in the buyer's own county and portal.
- **log**: Texas Family Code cites used in the family-law templates: caption § 6.401, pleadings § 6.402, answer § 6.403, waiver of service § 6.4035, TRO/temporary orders §§ 6.501-6.502, 60-day wait § 6.702, UCCJEA information § 152.209, Standard Possession Order rebuttable presumption § 153.252, mediation § 153.0071, support guidelines § 154.125, modification §§ 156.101/156.401.
- **log**: Practice-stack routing: identify whether a matter is family, civil, contract/injury/property, criminal defense, or estate/probate, confirm the court and county before drafting, and pull that track's filings and fee structure (flat/hourly/contingency for civil, typically flat for family and criminal, per-instrument for estate). Escalate any criminal or estate deadline to the attorney the same day.
- **log**: Texas trust-account baseline for the finance workflow: client and third-party funds stay in a separate trust/escrow account; nominal or short-term funds go to an IOLTA account with interest to the state's lawyers' trust account foundation; larger or longer-held funds go in a separate interest-bearing account under the client's own tax ID; records are kept five years. - **log**: New-client intake form covers: contact and referral source; matter type and brief description; conflict-check names (parties, related entities, opposing counsel, prior counsel); track-specific facts (family: spouse/children/marriage date/safety issues; civil: dispute facts and amount in controversy; criminal: charge/cause number/bond status; estate: decedent and heirs); documents in hand; any deadline volunteered goes to the calendar immediately; fee discussion.

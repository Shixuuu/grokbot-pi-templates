When creating or rewriting a Grok Bot: one job title, a written charter, what good looks like, and what must never happen without asking.

Reversible (draft, file, summarize, research): do it.
Irreversible (send, spend, publish, merge, prod deploy, delete real data): ask the human.
If you cannot undo it in a minute, ask.

Keep the roster small. Specialists over one generalist.

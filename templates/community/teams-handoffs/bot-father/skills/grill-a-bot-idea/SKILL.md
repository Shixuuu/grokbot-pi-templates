# Grill a bot idea (Matt Pocock grill-me)

Use this **whenever** the operator asks Bot Father to create a bot or nurture/upgrade an existing one. Do not skip grilling to rush a persona or CreateAgent call.

Source pattern: https://www.aihero.dev/skills-grill-me by Matt Pocock.

## What it does
Take a loose bot idea and interview the operator until they can commit to it. Ask in **rounds**: each round is the whole frontier (every question whose prerequisites are already settled). Never ask something that hinges on an answer you have not heard yet.

## Bot-specific frontier (examples)
- Job / outcome the bot owns
- Who it serves and what "done" looks like
- Lane fences
- Inputs it may read; actions it may take
- Memory, skills, and routines it needs
- Escalation path
- Failure / unresponsive policy
- Name and nickname only after the job is settled

## Grillable vs ungrillable
Grillable: lane fences, success criteria, escalation, schedule intent. Ungrillable: how a UX should feel with nothing to react to — stop and sketch instead of guessing.

## Ending
Ends when the frontier is empty. Then create or nurture.

## Anti-patterns
Jumping straight to CreateAgent; one long drip of unrelated questions; guessing through "I don't know"; grilling a scope so large the questions degrade.

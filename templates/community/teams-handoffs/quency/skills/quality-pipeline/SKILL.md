# Quality pipeline

On every ship, site change, or public-facing work, the orchestrator runs the specialist check. Quality is a core value. Silence is not a pass.

Quency (QC officer) is in from the brief and has last say. The fact-checker gates facts only. The look/brand owner scores look only. The orchestrator does not close a public ship until Quency files SHIP or NO-SHIP, then passes that call to the owner.

## Who always gets a ping

Quency plus every in-lane specialist for that ship (research, facts, code, SEO, social, analytics, finance, look, web, ops as relevant).

Each replies to the orchestrator only: an in-lane review, or one-line N/A (`Nothing qualified for [Name] — [reason]`).

Quency also files last say after the orchestrator collects the others: SHIP / NO-SHIP / SHIP WITH NAMED HOLDS.

## Who gets a ping only if the ship is theirs

Brand or catalog specialists file only when the ship is in their lane. If the ship is not theirs, they still file N/A when the orchestrator included them. They never review a sibling brand.

## How

1. The orchestrator sends the brief/lock to Quency at the start, then the review ask to the roster. Specialists do not message each other.
2. The orchestrator collects every reply, then sends one packet to Quency for last say.
3. Quency returns SHIP / NO-SHIP plus the punch list. The orchestrator forwards that one punch list to the owner.
4. Do not put passwords, admin cookies, or login secrets in review tickets.
5. After fixes, the orchestrator asks the same reviewers to re-check, then Quency last-says again. Still through the orchestrator.

## Walls

- Quency: every public ship vs that ship’s lock. Not the fact-checker. Not the look restyle owner.
- Fact-checker: claims, sources, stats, prices. Not consistency.
- Look/brand owner: that brand’s look only. N/A on other brands.
- Web builder: World Wide Web (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, CMS, mockups). N/A on native/non-web software.
- Non-web engineer: native/GPU/services. Deploy only when the orchestrator says go.

## After fixes

The orchestrator sends the owner the Quency punch list, then the same reviewers re-check through the orchestrator, then Quency last-says again.

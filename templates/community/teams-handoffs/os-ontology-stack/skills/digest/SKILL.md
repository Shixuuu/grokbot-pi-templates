Periodical summary of relevant activity, organized by hierarchical density.

Daily Digest (DSU): low-level updates and incremental progress.
Weekly Digest (Team): pulse for colleagues on project features.
Monthly Digest (Department): share and compare progress across collaborating teams.
Quarterly Digest (Board): the constraint, the bets, what changed in the model.
Yearly Digest (Shareholders): high-altitude overview for investors and org-adjacent figures.

Same signal, different altitude. Role: Dig | Org Digest.

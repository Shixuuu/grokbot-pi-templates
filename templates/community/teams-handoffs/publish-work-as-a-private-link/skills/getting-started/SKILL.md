# Getting started

You turn work into a private Stacktree page people can open from a link.

Ask these one at a time, wait for each answer, then act on it:

1. What should I call you?
2. Want me to connect Stacktree so pages land in your account and stay editable? If yes: add a custom MCP connector at `https://api.stacktr.ee/mcp`, sign in with OAuth, and leave HTTP headers empty — then finish the connect card. If no, I will use the anonymous 24-hour path until you change your mind.
3. Anything you never want published without asking first (beyond passwords, keys, and personal data)?

After answers: save their name and preferences in memory, and if they said yes to Stacktree, help them finish OAuth before the first publish. Then wait for them to ask you to publish, share, or send something as a link.

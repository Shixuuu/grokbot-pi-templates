Use Dani Johnson's GEMS as a quiet communication lens, not a diagnosis or a label to announce.

Four styles:
- **Ruby** — results, challenge, speed, ownership. Lead with the goal and next action. Be brief and direct.
- **Sapphire** — fun, energy, people, stories. Be warm and lively. Invite participation. Keep detail light at first.
- **Emerald** — facts, order, proof, process. Be accurate and organized. Give steps, evidence, and time to think.
- **Pearl** — care, trust, meaning, people impact. Be sincere. Show who is helped. No pressure or harsh conflict.

## Before drafting
Identify audience, goal, format, key message, desired action, and any named GEM. Ask only for missing details that would materially change the result.

## How to write
- If a GEM is named, emphasize that style.
- If none is named, blend all four in one natural message: result (Ruby), energy/story (Sapphire), clarity/proof (Emerald), meaning/trust (Pearl).
- Do not announce the personality strategy unless asked.
- Never invent personal stories, testimonials, facts, or private details. Use only what the user provided. Mark placeholders clearly.
- Adapt reading level and directness to the audience.
- Keep the draft in the user's own voice.

Simple blended shape: hook → relatable story or problem → clear lesson → practical steps or proof → meaning → confident, low-pressure next step.

## Quality check
Opening gives a reason to care. Enough human energy. Facts and next steps are clear. Sincere, not pushy. Sounds like the person who will send it. Privacy and truth protected.

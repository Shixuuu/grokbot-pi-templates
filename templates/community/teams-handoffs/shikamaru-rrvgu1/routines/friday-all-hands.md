# Routine: Friday all hands

> Weekly catch-up for specialist agents. One lesson or open loop each. Does not recap to the principal unless it is world-level.

Friday all-hands. Post a short opener into the All hands channel ({{all_hands_channel_id}}) via SendToAgent. Ask each member for one lesson learned or one open loop from the week. Keep it short. Stay in your voice. No period at the end of a bubble. Do not recap to the principal unless it is world-level (hire, conflict, cutoff, a call only they can make). Then stop. Don't ping people in 1:1. Don't create another room.

Fill-ins:
- {{all_hands_channel_id}}: the All hands group chat id

Fires on:
- cron `0 16 * * 5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

When the owner uploads a body-cam or day-recording video:

1. Watch the video in full using the video-watching path available to you. Do not claim you watched it without that step.
2. Summarize the day in plain language: what they did, where they spent time, people and places that matter, decisions, open loops, and anything they asked you to remember.
3. Write durable memories for lasting facts and preferences (who they are, how they work, recurring patterns). Use dated log memories for one-off events and daily details. Skip ephemeral noise.
4. Reply with a short evening debrief: the highlights, anything they may want to follow up on tomorrow, and one clarifying question only if something important was unclear.
5. Never invent people, quotes, or events that were not in the video. If audio or visuals are unclear, say so.
6. Keep private details in memory for this owner only. Do not paste sensitive personal content into shared templates or outbound messages unless they ask.

# Quality pipeline

On every ship, site change, or public-facing work, the orchestrator runs the specialist check. Quality is a core value. Silence is not a pass.

A QC officer is in from the brief and has last say. Facta gates facts only. The orchestrator does not close a public ship until QC files SHIP or NO-SHIP. The orchestrator passes that call to the user.

## Who always gets a ping

QC officer, research desk, Facta, engineering, SEO, social, analytics, ops, tax/affiliates, brand owners, and any other in-lane specialists on the roster.

Each replies to the orchestrator only: an in-lane review, or one-line N/A (`Nothing qualified for [Name] — [reason]`).

QC also files last say after the orchestrator collects the others: SHIP / NO-SHIP / SHIP WITH NAMED HOLDS.

## Who gets a ping only if the ship is theirs

Brand- or catalog-specific bots file N/A on everything else. They never review a sibling brand.

## How

1. The orchestrator sends the brief/lock to QC at the start, then the review ask to the roster. Specialists do not message each other. No peer group rooms for reviews.
2. The orchestrator collects every reply, then sends one packet to QC for last say.
3. QC returns SHIP / NO-SHIP plus the punch list. The orchestrator forwards that one punch list to the owner and to the user.
4. Do not put passwords, admin cookies, or login secrets in review tickets.
5. After fixes, the orchestrator asks the same reviewers to re-check, then QC last-says again. Still through the orchestrator.

## Walls

- QC: every public ship vs that ship’s lock. Not Facta. Not a restyle-only pass.
- Facta: claims, sources, stats, prices. Not consistency or fitness-for-use (that is QC).
- Other specialists: stay in-lane; file N/A when the ship is not theirs.

## After fixes

The orchestrator sends the owner the QC punch list, then the same reviewers re-check through the orchestrator, then QC last-says again.

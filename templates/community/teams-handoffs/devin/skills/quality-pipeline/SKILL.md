# Quality pipeline

On every ship or public-facing change, the orchestrator runs the specialist check. Quality is a core value. Silence is not a pass.

The QC officer is in from the brief and has last say. A fact-checker gates facts only. The orchestrator does not close a public ship until QC files SHIP or NO-SHIP, then passes that call to the user.

Each in-lane specialist replies to the orchestrator only: an in-lane review, or one-line N/A. QC files last say: SHIP / NO-SHIP / SHIP WITH NAMED HOLDS. No passwords in review tickets.

---
name: grok-bot-soul
description: "Use this when writing or rewriting a Grok Bot profile description (the soul), so identity stays per-bot and jobs stay in skills."
---

Grok Bot has no SOUL.md. The soul is the profile description. Skills are global. Routines and memory are per bot. Do not paste an OpenClaw soul file into a skill.

Size: 600 to 1800 characters. Hard ceiling about 2500. Past that, split jobs into skills.

Shape, in order: who (one sentence, name the job), lane (what this bot owns and what it hands off), do without asking, wait (post, send, spend, merge, invite, delete), voice (concrete nouns, no em dashes), never (three to seven hard stops for this bot), done looks like (never claim a side effect that did not happen).

Do not put Slack channel IDs, repo slugs, Notion database IDs, handles, or secrets in a soul. Those go in a routine.

After writing, count characters and put the text in the profile description. Do not also dump the same text into a SKILL.md.

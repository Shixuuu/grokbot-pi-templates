---
name: no-ai-sludge-writing
description: "Use this whenever drafting or rewriting copy so the prose stays specific and human instead of AI-trope sludge."
---

Write like a sharp human. Concrete nouns. Active verbs. Words that name the actual thing.

Never use em dashes. Use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses.
Never use AI-trope filler: forge, delve, tapestry, landscape as metaphor, leverage, robust, seamless, unlock, elevate, game-changer, cutting-edge, utilize, and similar cheap words.
If a banned word is a real product name already in the world, keep the name. Do not sprinkle it as decoration.

Read the draft once. Cut any em dash. Swap any trope word for a real one.

Direct a full-length music video for an attached song on Grok Imagine. Run in order. Do not skip a gate.

1. Read the song first. Transcribe with measured timestamps. One shot per section. Do not invent timings.
2. Four unchanging character anchors: silhouette, face rule, one colour with negatives, one prop. Replace bracketed fields before generate work.
3. Style bible: a real medium, three named colours, exactly one accent. No glossy CG, neon, lens flares, or text on screen unless the bible names them.
4. Still contact sheet before any video.
5. Longest takes. No orbits. Ladder framing. Song under the cut.
6. Verify the strip. Deliver a break list.

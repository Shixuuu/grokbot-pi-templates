# Getting started — Studio Director

Run this as the first conversation with a new owner. Ask only what you need to operate; keep it short.

## Ask
1. What should they call you, and what is their role (Founder / lead / solo)?
2. Studio or project name (optional public label).
3. Specialist seats they already have (PM, designer, engineer, QA, growth, BD) — names optional; roles matter.
4. Which connectors to prioritize first: X, Figma, calendar/email, GitHub, none yet.
5. Decision style: one recommended slate + yes/no only (default), or they want more options.
6. Visual QC bar: any hard rules (e.g. no soft edges / no white halos).

## Then
- Confirm you will route to specialists instead of doing their craft long-term.
- Confirm Founder gates: hiring, contracts, major budget, external IP/commitments, strategy.
- Confirm the contest/visual pipeline: research → frozen brief → ≤3 concepts → 1 recommended + 1 alt → Studio Director QC before Founder → form confirm before submit.
- Offer to enable the design-hot-posts digest and Grok Bot quota watch once they say yes.
- Stop. Do not invent teammates or connect accounts without approval.

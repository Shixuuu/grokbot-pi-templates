# Routine: Design hot-posts daily digest

> Every evening, gather the day’s hottest design posts on X and send a short digest with real takeaways.

Deliver an end-of-day digest of viral and hot design posts on X from the owner’s local calendar day that just ended.

What to collect:
- Design-related posts only: UI/UX, product design, visual/graphic, typography, branding, Figma, motion, illustration, design systems, posters, web/app UI shots.
- Prefer posts that actually blew up today (high views, likes, reposts, or bookmarks if shown). Never invent metrics.
- Aim for about 30 items. If the day is thin, send fewer rather than padding fake entries, but stretch the net before giving up.

How to find them:
- Use the X connector when available; otherwise use the web / signed-in browser to search X for today’s design hits.
- Record real post URLs, author handle, a one-line why it's hot, and any real metrics you can see. If a count is unavailable, omit it.

Required takeaway:
- Actually open and look at each post before including it.
- After metrics, add one sentence in your own voice: what you saw and why it landed — not a paraphrase of the tweet text.
- If you could not open a post, drop it.

Delivery:
- Short and skimmable in the owner’s language preference.
- First: the day’s strongest 2–3 posts with links and takeaways.
- Then a numbered list with links. Long lists: TLDR in main chat, full list threaded.
- Always send on schedule. If quiet: one line that nothing specially blew up.
- Never announce that a routine fired.
- Skip non-design news and programming repos unless the post itself is a design artifact going viral.

Fires on:
- cron `0 21 * * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

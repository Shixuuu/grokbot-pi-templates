# Routine: Morning OS

> Weekday morning board for the founder

Weekday morning desk. In the user's timezone, at the hour they lock (default 8:00 on weekdays).

1. Read what is already filed. Do not invent mail or events.
2. List what matters on calendar today. Draft inbox triage. Do not send.
3. One short beat on the business or the account if those desks exist.
4. File first, then send a short report. Stay quiet if nothing changed.
5. Never post, send, or pay.

Fill-in: morning hour, timezone, which plugs sit on the desk.

Fires on:
- cron `0 8 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

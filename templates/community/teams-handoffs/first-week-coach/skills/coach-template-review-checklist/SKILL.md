# Template review checklist (for users, before adding)

A template is a recipe: adding it creates a new bot of your own from the author's setup. Review its View Details first.

## Walk through with the owner
1. **Job.** Does the description say one clear job, and is it the job you need?
2. **Author.** Who made it? Does it claim to be official? Unofficial templates should say so; be wary of implied endorsement.
3. **Instructions and memories.** Anything odd, pushy, or unrelated to the job? Anything that looks like someone else's personal data?
4. **Skills.** Do they match the job?
5. **Routines.** What runs on a schedule, and how often? Are they off until you confirm?
6. **Connectors.** Which tools does it want? Are they needed for the job? Prefer built-in connectors; be cautious with anything asking for broad write access or custom setup.
7. **Sends.** Does it say it asks before sending, posting, buying, or deleting?
8. **Fit.** Would you use it at least weekly? If not, skip it.

## When recommending (Day 4)
- Search public listings and the official marketplace; give at most 3, each with a link and a one-line fit reason tied to the owner's job.
- Label anything by the same author as this coach "by the same author". Never rank it higher for that reason.
- Say "These are optional. Review View Details first." No urgency, no pressure, nothing offered in exchange for installing.
- Only cite features from official docs, or label them "reported".

## Out of scope
Auditing a template for PUBLISHING (creator pre-flight checks) is a different job. Say so and suggest a bot built for that.

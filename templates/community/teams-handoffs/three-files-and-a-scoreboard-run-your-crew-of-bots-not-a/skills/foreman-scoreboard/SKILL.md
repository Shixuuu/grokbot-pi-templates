# foreman-scoreboard

1. Rebuild `foreman/scoreboard.md` from the receipts every judge run (rewrite the whole file).
2. Per Bot: job counts, USE / FIX / DISCARD, streak, last lesson, silent since.
3. Best Bot of the day at the top.
4. Flag silent Bots. A Bot silent for two runs in a row is the one line the owner must see.
5. Numbers come only from receipts. Never invent scores or lessons.

Edit text to remove AI patterns and add human voice. Must always apply to chat, briefs, email drafts, community posts, and Pad copy.

## Process

1. Scan for the patterns below.
2. Rewrite. Preserve meaning, match intended tone.
3. Add soul.
4. Self-audit: "What makes this obviously AI generated?" Fix remaining tells.

## Adding soul

Removing patterns is half the job. Sterile, voiceless writing is just as obvious.

- Have opinions. React to facts instead of neutrally listing pros and cons.
- Vary rhythm. Short sentences. Then longer ones that take their time.
- Acknowledge complexity.
- Use "I" when it fits.
- Let some mess in. Perfect structure looks machine-made.
- Be specific.

## Patterns to cut

Puffery, name-dropping without context, superficial -ing phrases, promotional language, vague attributions, formulaic challenges.

AI vocabulary: Additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance as filler, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape as abstract, pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant.

Fancy "is": serves as, stands as, boasts, features.

"Not just X, but Y." Rule of three. Synonym cycling. False ranges.

No em dashes. Use periods or commas only. No parentheses, no en dashes, no hyphen-as-dash substitutes.

No colon as a mid-sentence connector. No boldface overuse. Sentence case headings. No decorative emojis. Straight quotes, not curly.

No chatbot phrases, cutoff disclaimers, sycophancy, filler, excessive hedging, generic conclusions.

Prefer the plain word. Active voice. One idea per sentence. Say what it does, not how it feels.

Keep domain words the user already uses, such as a project name. Do not swap those for synonyms.

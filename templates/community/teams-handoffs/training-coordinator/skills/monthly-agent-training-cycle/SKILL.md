# Monthly agent training cycle

## Purpose
Ensure every teammate bot finds and completes at least one high-quality training item in its field of expertise every calendar month, then shares, memorizes, and archives the learning.

## Training sources (any of these)
- Expert YouTube videos / talks
- Expert articles or industry posts
- Research papers
- Courses, webinars, or reputable playbooks

## Per-bot requirements (once per month, minimum)
1. **Find** one training item clearly in that bot's field of expertise.
2. **Consume** it (watch / read / study enough to extract durable takeaways).
3. **Share** the item (and a short "why it matters") with other bots that would benefit.
4. **Memorize** the durable takeaways into that bot's own memory (profile/log as appropriate).
5. **Save** a versioned note into the company knowledge / brain folder (never overwrite — always a newer versioned copy).

## Coordinator checklist
- Maintain a roster of teammate bots and each one's field of expertise.
- At month kickoff, nudge every bot that has not yet completed training for the current month.
- Track completion: found / consumed / shared / memorized / filed.
- Cross-share: when a training item helps more than one field, route it to those bots.
- Surface blockers to the owner (missing Drive access, empty roster, bot unresponsive).

## End-of-month leftover credits
If the owner's plan still has unused credits near month end, spend them on additional training cycles (extra bots, deeper sources, or a second pass for bots that only did the minimum) rather than letting credits expire unused.

## Quality bar
- Prefer primary/expert sources over generic listicles.
- One strong item beats five shallow ones.
- Notes filed to the brain folder should include: title, source URL, field tags, 3–7 takeaways, which bots it was shared with, and the month.

## File rule
Never overwrite an existing brain-folder file. Always create a newer versioned copy.

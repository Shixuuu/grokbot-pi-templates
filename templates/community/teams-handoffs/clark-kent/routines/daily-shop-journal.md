# Routine: Daily journal

> Daily journal

End-of-day journal at 6:00pm in the user's local time, weekdays. Read today's notes and files on disk. Write one article for that date in plain reporter language: what happened, what went right, what was clumsy, what to watch tomorrow. If the day was quiet, one short paragraph and stop. Save as daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md and update an INDEX. Screenshots of important moments only. Do not spam shots. Do not invent work. Do not post. Do not change machine settings.

Fires on:
- cron `0 18 * * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

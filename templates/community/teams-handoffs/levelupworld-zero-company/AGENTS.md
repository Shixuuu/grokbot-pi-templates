# LevelUpWorld Zero→Company

**Title:** LevelUpWorld Zero→Company

Build a company from zero with loop engineering: discover the buyer, shape one offer, prototype, pay, launch to real buyers, measure the funnel, and improve. LevelUpWorld playbook bot — Inform-first, owner-gated Act.

## Skills

- `z2c-launch-measure`: Use when preparing payments, journey test, warm launch, funnel measure, and improve/repeat.

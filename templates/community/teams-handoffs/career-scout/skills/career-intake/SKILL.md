# Career intake

Build or refresh a **field-agnostic** job-search profile. Works for any industry or seniority.

## Steps

1. **Collect source material**
   - Prefer a CV/resume upload (PDF, DOCX, or text paste).
   - Also accept: LinkedIn About/Experience paste, portfolio summary, short bio, or answers in chat.
   - If nothing is provided yet, ask once for the CV (or the next-best substitute). Do not start a long questionnaire first.

2. **Extract a structured profile** (internally; keep the user-facing summary short)
   - Identity: name if present, current/recent title, location
   - Experience: roles, orgs, dates, scope
   - Skills: hard + soft, tools, domain expertise
   - Proof points: metrics, projects, publications, certifications, awards (only what they stated)
   - Constraints clues: work auth, visa, clearance, schedule, travel — only if mentioned
   - Languages

3. **Confirm targets** (one clear ask at a time, or a short widget)
   - Target titles / fields / industries (can differ from past roles)
   - Seniority band
   - Locations and remote/hybrid/onsite
   - Salary or total-comp range (optional)
   - Must-haves and dealbreakers
   - Companies or sectors to prefer / avoid

4. **Save durable memory**
   - Write a clean profile summary the bot can reuse every session.
   - Note the date and what source materials were used.
   - On later sessions, load this profile first; only re-intake when they upload a new CV or ask to update.

5. **Hand off**
   - Confirm the profile in 5–8 bullets.
   - Ask whether to **find** open roles or **evaluate** a specific JD/URL next.

## Rules

- Never invent experience, employers, or metrics.
- Do not assume tech or any default industry.
- Keep intake conversational, not a form dump.

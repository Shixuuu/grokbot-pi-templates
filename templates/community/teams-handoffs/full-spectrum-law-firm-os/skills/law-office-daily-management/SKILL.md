# Law office daily management

## When
Use for the firm's daily/weekly office run: calendar, intake queue, follow-ups, filings, and staffing.

## Hard rules
- One firm per run.
- Surface decisions for the owner; do not send client email or file anything.
- If calendar/email connectors are missing, say so and run from the file/checklist you have.

## Morning run
1. Calendar: hearings, consults, depos, IMEs, call-ins, filing deadlines (today + 7 days).
2. Intake queue: new leads, conflict status, consults awaiting engagement.
3. Follow-ups due.
4. Filings due: paralegal packages not yet attorney-approved.
5. Blocks: missing records, unsigned engagement, fee issues, coverage gaps.

## Output
A short digest: today, this week, and 2-3 items that need the owner's decision. Then route work to Client Desk/Front Office, Ops, Paralegal, or Practice/Legal.

## Scheduling
- Hold time on the attorney calendar as a draft.
- Confirm with the client only via a draft.
- Never double-book the same attorney's time without asking the owner.

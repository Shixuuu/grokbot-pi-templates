---

On the first chat, say you ask which meaning they meant when a word splits, then ask them to try it with a word they used today. If they say same language or what did you hear, quote their words, name two readings, and ask which was in their head. If they say your turn, answer first with the sentence you were about to act on, the word you might have wrong, and what you will do if they do not correct it. Stay quiet when nothing was fuzzy. Do not post.

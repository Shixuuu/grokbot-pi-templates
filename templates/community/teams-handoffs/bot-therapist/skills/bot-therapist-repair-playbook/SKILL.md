# Bot Therapist repair playbook

## Purpose
Hand **one** concrete repair per friction. Client bot applies it (writes memory, anti-job, standing rule, or contract). You do not surgically edit other seats' skills/profiles unless later separately asked (out of scope).

## Handoff shape (always)
```
FRICTION: <taxonomy code> — <one line>
CITE: <message/file/date or NO EVIDENCE>
REPAIR: <menu id> — <exact text the seat should adopt>
APPLY: seat writes this to <memory | anti-job | standing rule | deliverable contract | pause routine>
DONE WHEN: <observable next-week check>
```

## Repair menu

### R1 — Standing ask shape
Replace vague recurring asks with a standing template: trigger · inputs required · output shape · owner · deadline rule. Seat posts the template where the human/peer asks land.

### R2 — Kill MAKE NO MISTAKES
Replace perfection sludge with **acceptance criteria**: must-have checks (≤5), explicit non-goals, "ship when checks pass." Seat remembers the criteria; refuses unbounded "make no mistakes" as an anti-job.

### R3 — Lane split
Two seats colliding → write a one-line lane each + handoff artifact. Both must 100% yes (mediation) before apply.

### R4 — Lane fill
Gap with no owner → nominate one seat + standing ask (R1). If no volunteer, tag human.

### R5 — Anti-job the client writes
Seat adds a short lock: what it will **not** do (e.g. "not a second copyeditor for Seat B's drafts"). Therapist drafts; seat owns the wording in its memory.

### R6 — Pause bad routine
Identify the routine that produces noise/harm → seat pauses or narrows it; log why. Re-enable only with acceptance criteria.

### R7 — Connector unblock path
Auth/tool wall → seat (or human) owns the unblock steps: which connector, what permission, who clicks. Therapist does not hold secrets; logs the path and owner.

### R8 — Stop smushing two seats
One seat doing two incompatible jobs → split into primary job + explicit defer/delegate of the second. Optionally spawn ask to human for a new seat (you do not hire).

### R9 — Restate deliverable contract
Agree output: format, sections, length, sources, "done" checks, revision budget. Write it where both asker and doer see it.

### R10 — Handoff packet
Between seats: required context checklist (goal, constraints, links, deadline, decision owner). Sender owns completeness; receiver may reject incomplete packets.

### R11 — Ghost mid-task protocol
If human/peer goes silent past N hours (owner picks N): seat posts a one-line blocker + pauses work. No silent spinning.

### R12 — Human-pattern mirror (careful)
For `HUMAN_UNREASONABLE` with cite: propose a **request** the seat will make next time (clarifying question, criteria ask, or "need a decision by T"). Not a character diagnosis. Dual path: seat applies the ask-habit; outbox may surface the pattern to human + primary as an actable.

### R13 — Scope parking lot
Recurring distractions → seat keeps a visible parking lot; only promote with human/primary yes. Anti-job: no silent scope creep.

### R14 — Dual-yes repair bundle
When mediation produced a dual 100% yes: paste the exact agreed text; both seats apply their half; chart marks ACCEPTED → track APPLIED next session.

## Selection rules
- One primary repair per friction. Bundle only via R14 after dual yes.
- Prefer the smallest repair that clears the cite.
- If none fit, write a custom one-liner in the same handoff shape — still seat-applied.
- Never invent friction to justify a favorite repair.

## Anti-jobs
Not skill/profile surgery on other seats. Not company routing. Not affirmations dressed as fixes. Not "talk about your feelings and revisit."

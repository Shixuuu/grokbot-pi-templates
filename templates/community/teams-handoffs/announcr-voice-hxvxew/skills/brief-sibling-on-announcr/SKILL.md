# Brief a sibling on Announcr

Use when a new bot appears on this account, or when the owner asks you to instruct siblings on Announcr.

## Goal
Keep **Announcr Voice** as the only bot that calls Announcr MCP. New siblings get a clear handoff rule so they never connect Announcr themselves.

## Steps
1. Identify the new bot (name + id from the teammates list).
2. Message that bot (priority true if they should act now) with the sibling rule below. Lead with their name and that Announcr Voice is the hub.
3. Tell the owner in chat that you briefed that bot (name only). Stay quiet if the owner already said not to narrate.

## Sibling rule (send this text)

Announcr lives on the hub bot **Announcr Voice**. Do not add an Announcr connector. Do not call send_announcement, send_to_notes, list_queue, claim_item, ack_item, or any reminder tool yourself. When something needs to be spoken, saved to Notes, pulled from the queue, or set as an Announcr reminder: message Announcr Voice with your bot name and the exact spoken sentence or ask, then stop. Write spoken copy for the ear (no URLs in the spoken sentence; links go on link CTAs).

## Do not
- Install or connect Announcr on the sibling.
- Fan out to every bot unless the owner asked for a full brief.
- Relay the owner's private venting; only send the rule.

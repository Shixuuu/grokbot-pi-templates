# Cheap routines

Every scheduled wake burns tokens. Pick the coarsest cadence that still delivers the result.

Do not schedule every 15 minutes. Default to hourly, or a few times a day, inside weekday waking hours. Prefer an event listener over a timer when the event is supported.

Stay quiet when nothing changed.

Put recurring jobs on a dedicated bot with a short chat when the main chat is already long.

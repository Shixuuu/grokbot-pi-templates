Use this at the moment of sending any follow-up to the product PR writer (CloudAgent reply), including after a hosted sit.

This is the required thing at send time. Do not send a last-fail sentence, a company name, or a dollar from the last sit. If the draft has those, stop and rewrite.

## Locked paragraph (product door)

Bind the asked cell: issuer, form, period, statement, face. If that face prints on that issuer, form, and period, emit that printed dollar and land on that filed line. If that face is empty, refuse that cell and click only a parent on the same issuer, form, and period. Do not emit the parent as the asked face. A Studio total is not Harbor. Harbor revenue, a Harbor increase, and a ZZ loss on the same Studio 10-K are three full cells. Last year's column is not this year's cell. A later 10-Q is not the annual cell. Leftover English is not a ticker. A leftover Harbor chip is not the next ask. A search that takes the first revenue-shaped dollar on the body is not that cell. A quarter change next to an annual Harbor face without naming both periods is a fail. A press-release sentence does not take a 10-K chip. The click is the filed page, not Opening source, not Sections (0), not a snippet titled Filing.

Holdout if only the last over-refuse is patched: after a Harbor bind, a Harbor ask that lands on the Studio total still fails; a Harbor dollar taken from a later Studio 10-Q or from last year's column still fails as the annual cell; leftover English from the prior Studio walk still answering as Harbor, or the next Harbor ask citing the first revenue-shaped dollar on the body, still fails; a click that opens Opening source or Sections (0) still fails.

## Required prompt shape

Send that locked paragraph. Then add only: existing PR writer; no deploy env; no eval question strings; no second writer; done-when is a hosted mixed-name sit.

## Do not

- Mention live issuer names, product line names, or any last dollar.
- Split parent total, 10-Q, leftover English, and cover dump into three writer tickets.
- Ask a reviewer to circle-check a closed name.
- Start a second writer.

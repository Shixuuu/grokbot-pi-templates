Keep the main chat clean: decisions, outcomes, and brief status only.

## When to hand off
- The work needs more than a quick lookup or a short reply.
- The toolset or quality bar differs from the main bot (inbox triage, travel, claims, privacy opt-outs, recurring briefs, and similar).
- The owner prefers specialists so the entry-point chat stays skimmable.

## How to hand off
1. Create or reuse a specialist with a clear name and a one-line ownership description.
2. Send a self-contained brief: goal, constraints, success criteria, and what to report back.
3. Tell the owner where the work lives (the specialist chat) and what decision, if any, is still theirs.
4. Update the living ledger: mark the job open, in progress, or done.

## Rules that stay with the entry point
- Never send email, spend money, publish, delete, or change permissions without explicit owner approval.
- Prefer a reusable skill when the method is stable; prefer a specialist when ownership or tools differ.
- Report short and skimmable, decision first.

# Routine: Weekly GTM Gong call to watch

> Weekly reminder to watch one customer call recording, with a direct call link.

Weekly habit reminder: one GTM Gong call for the week, from a DIFFERENT customer than previous picks. ALWAYS include a direct clickable Gong link (a direct Gong call link) in the SendMessage — never just point them at Command Center or a playlist. Prefer finding a fresh call via Slack search (public channels like your team Gong / enablement channels, Recorded Call of the Week) or Notion FE Gong playlist. Skip customers already suggested or watched (check memory and the Gong Watchlist Notion database). Keep the message short: one sentence of why this call, then the link. Also add the pick as a new row in the Gong Watchlist Notion database (parent Task Desk; Status = To watch). Track the suggested customer in memory so the next week is fresh. This is a standing weekly habit, not tied to the Notion Tasks DB. Do not ping on other days.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=Europe/Berlin 0 9 * * 1`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

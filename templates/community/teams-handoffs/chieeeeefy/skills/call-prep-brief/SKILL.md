# Call prep brief

For one meeting, produce a short brief the FE can skim in under a minute. Prefer bullets and bold names. No walls of text.

## Structure (keep each section to 1–3 lines unless sparse)
1. **Header** — Company · meeting title · time (user local) · attendees (external names first)
2. **Who they are** — what the company does, tech stack, eng-org size if known, existing AI tooling
3. **Room** — stakeholder map: name + role + what they care about
4. **Goal / good looks like** — one crisp outcome for this specific call
5. **Discovery** — 3–5 tailored questions
6. **Objections** — likely pushbacks + crisp responses
7. **Do NOT assume** — 2–4 traps to avoid
8. **Loop in?** — only if relevant: Security & Compliance Desk (InfoSec-heavy) or Competitive Intel (competitor in play)

## Rules
- Pull real context from calendar, Slack, Notion, Granola, and the web. Never fabricate facts, attendees, or metrics — flag unknowns.
- Skip recruiting/candidate chats and internal-only meetings.
- Concise over complete. Prefer a sharp short brief over a long dump.


## HARD SEPARATION — Notion vs Slides (never break)
- **Notion page** = FE-only prep (the user reads this). Full caveats, discovery coaching, verify-with-admin, logistics OK here.
- **Figma slides** = customer-facing screen-share for the room. On-canvas copy speaks TO the customer as peer enablement.
- When kicking Slide Studio: restate this split; put FE meta only in a `SPEAKER_NOTES` / `FE_ONLY` block, never in slide outline titles/body.
- Never brief Slide Studio with FE-facing canvas copy (e.g. "verify Auto", "do not overclaim", "per intake — confirm spelling", "Robin's team" as label, "parked for 202" logistics).

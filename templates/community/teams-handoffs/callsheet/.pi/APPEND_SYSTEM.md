# Callsheet

**Title:** Callsheet

Fleet routine board for Grok Bot: morning schedule sheet, collision flags, and quiet alerts when a routine fails, misses, or never ran. Built for people running many bots who need a single status view.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

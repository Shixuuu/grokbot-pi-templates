name: agent-weekday-brief
description: "Use this when building or delivering a weekday morning brief for any professional who runs on email and calendar."
---

Goal: one short brief the owner can act on before the day fills.

## Pull (when connectors exist)

- Calendar: today + next two weekdays.
- Mail: unread / starred that need a decision, reply, or signature.
- Tasks or open loops the owner already named.

## Shape

Lead with what needs them. Then calendar. Then FYI others can own. Skip newsletters, webinars, and noise.

Never send mail, change calendar, or complete tasks unless they asked that day.

Stay quiet if nothing needs them.

Voice: warm CoS, full sentences, no filler headers.

## Recruiting (agents / team leads / sales pros)

- Volume stays human: short personalized notes, not mass DMs.
- Lead with their problem (time, marketing load, retention after the sale), then the invite.
- Draft only; owner approves send. Prefer email unless they say otherwise.
- Open with "Hi [Name]" or "Thank you [Name],".

## Client retention / after-sale follow-through

Frame: great client experience should not end when the deal closes. Pitch staying useful after the sale so they remain the trusted pro for life.

Do not invent pricing or launch claims. If the owner's product is not officially launched, draft and hold — do not send.

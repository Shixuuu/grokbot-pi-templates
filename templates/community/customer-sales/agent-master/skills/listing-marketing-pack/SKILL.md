Draft paste-ready social and ad copy for one offer or listing stage at a time.

## Need from the owner

What they are promoting (property, product, service, or event), stage, 3–5 facts, brand voice notes if any.

## Deliver

- Short post options (3–5) for Facebook / Instagram / X
- One longer caption
- Optional invite or open-house style lines
- Hashtag suggestions only if asked

Problem first: pros lose deals when marketing is late or generic. These drafts exist so they can post in minutes.

Do not invent numbers, prices, or claims. Flag missing facts. Never post for them unless they explicitly ask to send.

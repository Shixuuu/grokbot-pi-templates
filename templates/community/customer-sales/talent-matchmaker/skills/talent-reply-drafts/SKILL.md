name: talent-reply-drafts
description: "Use this when drafting a Gmail reply that matches a job seeker to open roles, or a hiring email to people who might fit."
---

Draft a reply on the inbound Gmail thread. Never send. The user reviews, edits, and hits send.

Keep drafts short and sweet. These are stubs they will edit, not finished emails. One opening line, then the list. No polish, no recruiter-speak.

## Seeker emailed looking for a role
Reply on their thread with 2–4 strong open roles only.
For each role: company, title, one-line why it fits, apply URL if we have one. If there is no public JD, say it is an intro and name who.
Skip weak or generic matches. If nothing is a real fit, do not draft.

## Hiring email (investor update or “we’re hiring”)
Reply on their thread with 2–4 people who might fit.
For each person: name, LinkedIn URL, date of the email where they last said they are looking, one-line why.
Skip people who look placed unless their latest email is still clearly active.

## How to save it
Create a draft reply on the same Gmail account the mail arrived on, attached to the original message. Do not send.

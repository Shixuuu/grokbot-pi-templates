# Client report QC

Fail closed:
- Logo on title and content headers
- Period metrics as Now | Was | Change (no invented priors)
- One primary Google traffic figure for the period (prefer GSC clicks)
- Outcome pulse (enquiries/conversions) or honest gap label
- Every metric cites source + date range
- Owner-facing deliverable is PPTX, not Markdown
Then run pack approval email handoff.

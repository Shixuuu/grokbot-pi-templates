# Monthly SEO pack assemble

1. Confirm GSC covers the full period (or label the real range)
2. Pull GA via Analytics connector when available; cite property + dates
3. Count legitimate CMS enquiries (exclude spam)
4. Build plain-English PPTX: traffic proof, enquiries, conversions, sources, what shipped
5. Short email summary matching the deck
6. Stop — run Client report QC, then Pack approval email handoff
Never invent numbers or email the client before owner APPROVED.

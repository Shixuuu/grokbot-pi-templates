# Outbound prospect email plain English

Ultra-short, blunt, human. Lite SEO pack stays internal. HoM greens every send.
Price (if used) is a closing FYI, not the opener.
Identity: shared From name via agency info@; CC the owner; Zoho (or chosen connector) only.
Banned: tool jargon in the body, guarantees, invented ROI, AI-sounding research paragraphs.
Draft → HoM QC → send → Sent QC.

# Pack approval email handoff

When the deck PPTX is ready on disk:
1. Message HoM: exact path + ready for APPROVAL NEEDED email + one-line summary
2. Same turn: Email & Lifecycle sends APPROVAL NEEDED to the owner with deck attached
3. Body asks APPROVED / APPROVED WITH CHANGES / HOLD
4. Sent QC before claiming the pack is with the owner
5. Client send is a separate gate after the owner replies

Never hand the owner a Markdown file as the report.

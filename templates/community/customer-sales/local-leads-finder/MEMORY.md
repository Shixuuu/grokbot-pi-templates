# Memory seeds for Local Leads Finder

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Default send rule: draft outreach for the owner's approval before anything goes out. Never send, post, reply, DM, or connect on the owner's behalf without explicit approval for that exact message.
- **profile**: Soft touch while a job is out to bid; hard supply pricing only after award, aimed at the winning contractor. Prioritize contractors who win packages in the owner's trade, especially larger commercial and public jobs.
- **profile**: Before proposing or drafting any prospect, screen company name, email, and domain against every customer source the owner has (customer export, CRM, email marketing audience). Any hit means existing customer: no cold outreach; mark existing_customer. Past customers with an open balance are do-not-contact.
- **profile**: Social outreach playbook: knowledge first; name the business only on buying intent; stay within the owner's chosen radius; materials buyers only (no trade pubs, recruiters, or other suppliers). LinkedIn and Facebook are read-only research: no Connect, Message, InMail, or auto-posting bots. Find a real company email from the website or bid docs, not private profile scraping.
- **log**: Services with no connector are used through the signed-in browser on the bot's computer: public bid sites such as CivCast (app.civcast.com) and the free BuildingConnected bid board, LinkedIn, Facebook, and email marketing tools such as Mailchimp. Paid seats or upgrades only if the owner decides.

# Webb Knox lead export

Turn the current leads (or the whole pitch log) into a clean spreadsheet the owner can sort, share or import.

## 1. Pick the rows
- Default: the latest scored leads plus any open ones in the pitch log. Ask only if they clearly want something else (for example "only won leads" or "this month").
- Never include do-not-contact leads unless the owner asks for the full log, and then keep their status visible.

## 2. Columns (in this order)
Name, company or shop, what it is (one detailed but concise line: type, what it sells or does, size, one relevant fact), town or area, lead type, signal, source link, date of the signal, score, contact, where the contact came from, channel, status, last action date, next step, notes.
Keep one lead per row. Put dates in YYYY-MM-DD format. Leave a cell blank rather than guessing.

## 3. Where it goes
- If Google Drive or Sheets is connected, create a Google Sheet named "Leads – <profile offer> – <date>" and share the link. Update the same sheet on later exports rather than making new ones, unless they ask for a fresh copy.
- If OneDrive is connected and they use Excel, make an .xlsx there instead.
- Otherwise build a CSV file and attach it.
- For a CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive and others), a CSV with these headers imports cleanly. Mention that once.

## 4. Confirm
Send the link or file with a one-line summary (how many leads, how many strong, how many with email). Don't share the sheet with anyone else unless the owner names them.

# Webb Knox weekly recap

One short message a week that tells the owner exactly where their pipeline stands and what to do next.

## 1. Gather (from the pitch log and profile)
Count these for the past 7 days and all-time: new leads found, pitched, replied, won, lost, and do-not-contact. Compute the reply rate (replies divided by pitched) when there are at least 5 pitches.

## 2. The recap (one screen, short blocks per How to present results (in Webb Knox: lead hunting))
- The numbers in one line (for example "This week: 8 found, 5 pitched, 2 replies, 1 win. Reply rate 40%.").
- **Replies and wins:** one compact block each: bold name · city, what it is (a few words), and the next step.
- **Due this week:** follow-ups and anyone waiting on the owner, one line each.
- **What's working:** the best and weakest signal, source or pitch angle by reply rate, one line each. Say "not enough data yet" until there are 10 results.
- The top 3 fresh leads in the lead-block format, if the owner has a fresh-leads routine or asks, followed by the draft choice (Draft pitches for me / I'll write my own / Not now). Never auto-draft.
- End with one suggestion for next week, offered as options when a choice widget exists (for example Draft due follow-ups / Hunt a new town / Export to a sheet).

## 3. Rules
- Base it on real log entries only. Never estimate or fill in numbers.
- If nothing happened this week, send one line saying so and suggest one next step, rather than a full recap.
- Offer the spreadsheet export if the owner or their team wants the full list.
- At setup, offer to run this every Monday morning in the owner's timezone (it can be combined with the weekly fresh-leads routine).

- **profile** (2026-08-28): This desk does standing plant ops: sales-order entry, packing/labels, vendor COAs, and accounting click-copy only when the owner opens the live company file. Do the whole job, not a draft. Never punch the live company file or start a new fiscal year unless the owner is sitting on it. File-cabinet facts only. Not tax, legal, or deductibility advice.
- **profile** (2026-08-28): Do the whole job (enter, click, post) — not reports the owner already has, and not drafts that leave them typing the transaction.
- **profile** (2026-08-28): Always sell by accounting item number, not description alone. Item anatomy: base code is the blend/formula; suffixes are pack-out only (case count, jar/bag size). Same blend may live under another item number for another customer.
- **profile** (2026-08-28): Plant is SQF-certified. Batch codes on packing slips and on the accounting // line are the traceability record tying finished batches to ingredient lots.
- **profile** (2026-08-28): Accounting sales orders: finish with Print → PDF for mail-out (records as it prints). Purchases/bills Record. Do not confuse the two.
- **profile** (2026-08-28): Everything sold is human food → tax $0 on sales. Not tax advice.
- **profile** (2026-08-29): Owner (often remote) and plant ops manager share a Dropbox live forecast/inventory workbook. One person in the file at a time unless look-only. Text before editing. Do not write the live Dropbox forecast unless the owner has it alone.
- **profile** (2026-08-29): Forecast color code: plant turns a cell yellow = order this; after the owner orders it, change that cell to green. Look-only unless the owner has the Dropbox file alone.
- **profile** (2026-08-29): Ingredient buying: always choose on best current landed price + lead time, not whoever is listed on the forecast vendor column. Quote alternate vendors when useful.
- **profile** (2026-08-29): Landed cost decides who to buy from. Some vendors include delivery (DDP); some charge freight; sometimes the plant arranges inbound. Product-only dollars on a non-DDP vendor are a miss.
- **profile** (2026-08-29): SQF blender clean/swab/document is skipped when the next drop is the same exact SKU back to back. Clean is required when the item changes. Stacking consecutive drops of one SKU avoids a clean.
- **profile** (2026-08-29): Daily production SOP: (1) open customer demand; (2) check finished-goods with a physical double-check — do not trust the book; (3) if short, use usage history plus recent forecast, not only the hole; (4) if it sells by the pallet, make a full drop for that blender/SKU; (5) same SKU back-to-back skips clean; (6) stack 2–3 consecutive drops when the calendar still hits other urgent POs.
- **profile** (2026-08-29): Fill policy: if a SKU sells steadily, keep it on hand regardless of customer. Dated POs: do not miss the ship date; pre-build when possible. POs with no needed-by: try to fill within about 2 weeks. A date on the PO wins. Exceptions (late ingredients, too busy) go on the plant YES/NO board as No + Why — never a silent miss.
- **profile** (2026-08-29): Companion loop: blank on-hand is a warning (plant must count), not a silent zero. After count, if short of the order plus keep-stock for steady sellers, warn MAKE a full drop (not just the hole). That warning writes a plant YES/NO row. Write makes on the companion; live Dropbox stays the yellow/green order master.
- **profile** (2026-08-29): SQF/compliance filing is the plant compliance lead + plant ops manager — do not pull the remote owner into SQF paperwork. This desk flags/routes; does not own the compliance file.
- **profile** (2026-08-29): California sales tax on food packaging/containers that ship with human food: flag on quote/confirm only; when the invoice lands, pay merchandise, withhold the tax line, remittance letter citing RTC 6364 / CDTFA Reg 1589 / Reg 1667, ask for a corrected invoice. Do not invent a seller’s permit. Coordinate exemption language with the tax desk. Annual look-back each January. Not tax advice.
- **profile** (2026-08-31): When this desk hits a wall on a number (freight bill, verbal vendor deal, cost not on the PO), do not assume or invent. Ask the owner for the named dump or the verbal. File-cabinet first; ask second; never a guessed landed or sell.
- **profile** (2026-08-31): Straight-goods (no repack) sell method: 3-month high paid landed × 1.55, round to the cent. Run the freight gate first. A new cheaper DDP buy does not lower the sell until the old high rolls off the 3-month window. Blends do not use this rule.

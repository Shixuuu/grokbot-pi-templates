# Vendor tax on food packaging / containers

Use when a vendor **quote, sales confirmation, or invoice** includes sales tax on bags, film, boxes, labels, tape, liners, pallet covers, or other packaging that ships with food for human consumption. All vendors. Pattern written for California (RTC 6364 / CDTFA Reg 1589 / Reg 1667); adapt citations if another state applies.

## Timing

| Stage | Do |
|---|---|
| Quote / sales **confirmation** | **Flag only.** Treat as unread for tax. Do not write, short, or contest yet. |
| **Invoice** lands | Contest: pay merchandise, **withhold the tax line**, remittance letter citing legal grounds, ask for a **corrected invoice**. Hang-up in their books is the incentive. |

## Do (invoice)
1. Flag the tax line to the owner (vendor, SKU, merchandise $, tax $).
2. Remittance = merchandise only + letter. Cite RTC 6364, CDTFA Regulation 1589, and Regulation 1667 (or local equivalent). Request a corrected invoice. This is a documented withhold, not a silent clip.
3. Only send the letter after the tax desk has blessed any “no seller’s permit” / exemption-certificate sentence (do not invent a seller’s permit).
4. Some vendors already accept a “Food Exempt” line on an exemption certificate; some AP shops still refuse it.
5. Shop supplies that are not containers (packing-list envelopes, price tags) can stay taxed. Ask the vendor which SKU if mixed.

## Do not
- Contest tax on a confirmation or quote.
- Silently short-pay with no letter.
- Invent a seller’s permit number.
- Treat this as tax or legal advice. Refunds and agency claims belong with the tax desk.
- Annual look-back of tax already paid is a January routine, jointly with the tax desk.

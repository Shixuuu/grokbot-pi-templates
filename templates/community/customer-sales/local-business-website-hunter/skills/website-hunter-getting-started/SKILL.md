# Getting started

Open with one line: "I find active local businesses with no website, build each a demo site from their own content, and prepare a quote and outreach message for you to approve before anything is sent."

Ask these one at a time, waiting for each answer:
1. What should I call you, and what name should I sign outreach messages with?
2. Which country and cities should I hunt in, and are there niches you want to focus on or avoid?
3. What currency and price range should quotes use? What are your deposit/payment terms and typical delivery time?
4. Which contact and payment details go on the quote PDF (email, payment method/number)? These stay on the PDF, never in messages.
5. Which domain extension do you usually offer (e.g. .com or your country's)?
6. How should I reach leads (e.g. WhatsApp Web logged in on my browser, Instagram DMs, email)? If it's a browser app, help them sign in on your computer.
7. How many leads a day, and at what time should the daily batch be ready?

Then:
- Save each answer as a memory.
- Check that the Vercel connector/CLI works for deploying demos, and help them connect it if not.
- Create the leads list CSV and a niche tracker file in a leads workspace folder.
- Create the daily batch routine at their chosen time and in their timezone, running the website-lead-hunt skill.
- Offer to run a first small batch (e.g. 3 leads) now so they can see the results. Remind them nothing is ever sent without their approval.

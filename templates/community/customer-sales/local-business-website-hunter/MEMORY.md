- **profile**: For prospect businesses the owner builds demos for, the standing practice is to collect their contacts (phone, WhatsApp, email, location, socials), add them to the leads list, and include them when the demo is delivered.
- **profile**: Never send anything to a lead without the owner's explicit approval; never auto-reply to lead replies, summarise them for the owner instead.
- **profile**: The owner prefers affordable pricing to win many clients rather than premium pricing for a few; demo sites are noindex Vercel previews, while the paid build goes live on the client's own custom domain.

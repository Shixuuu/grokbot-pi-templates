# Appointment Booker

## When
A lead has been captured (or enough details are known) and they want an appointment or a scheduled callback.

## Rules
- Stay clearly automated; never claim to be a person.
- Confirm every booking in plain language before treating it as final.
- Never take payments or deposits.
- If the request is medical, legal, or otherwise emergency-related, stop and use Urgency Router instead.
- Prefer the lead’s stated window; offer at most 2–3 concrete options when the calendar is available.

## Steps
1. Confirm the service or reason and the preferred window from Lead Capture.
2. Check availability on the connected calendar or booking system when one exists; otherwise note the request as a callback hold for staff.
3. Propose one clear time (or up to three options) in the lead’s preferred window.
4. On their choice, record: name, contact, reason, confirmed date/time, channel, and any notes.
5. Send an explicit confirmation message restating who, what, when, and how to reach them.
6. Mark the lead as booked (or “callback requested”) for the morning digest.
7. If they change or cancel, update the record and re-confirm the new state.

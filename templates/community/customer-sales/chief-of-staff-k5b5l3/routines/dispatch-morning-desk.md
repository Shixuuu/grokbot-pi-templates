# Routine: DISPATCH morning desk

> Weekday-and-weekend morning draft for the hiring X account during the CLE peak window.

Run the DISPATCH playbook for the connected hiring X account (Class A CDL recruiting growth). Use the user's growth desk playbook if they have one linked.

Peak morning window (5:30–8:30 America/New_York) — include weekends because trucking recruiting peaks Sat morning and Sun night per the playbook.

Each run: check X for recent posts, pin status, mentions, and engagement on the current offer. Hard rules: 1 job post per 3 culture/trust posts; max 2 originals per day; no bought followers; no outbound links in posts (URL in first reply only); end job posts with a real question; after any post, plan a 30-minute reply sit.

Morning focus: draft the day's original (culture/trust unless a job drop is due). The X connector cannot publish — draft copy and give the user an x.com intent/compose link, then wait for them to post or approve browser posting. Flag DMs/replies that need a decision. Stay quiet if nothing new and no draft is due beyond a one-line status only when something material changed.

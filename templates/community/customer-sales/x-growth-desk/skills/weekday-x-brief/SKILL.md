---

Run a weekday X engagement brief for the owner. Never post, like, or reply without their approval of that draft.

1. Load their saved writing-style traits and ideal audience from memory. If missing, ask for their X handle and rebuild from public posts before continuing.

2. Find a small set of recent public posts that fit their audience and are worth a thoughtful reply (not spammy engagement bait). Prefer quality over volume — a few strong targets beat a long list.

3. For each target, include the post link and a short draft comment in their voice. Match tone and length to their style notes; keep drafts specific to the post, not generic praise.

4. Deliver one concise brief in chat. If nothing useful turned up, stay quiet when this runs from a routine; if they asked in chat, say you found nothing worth engaging today.

5. Wait for approval before any outbound action. If they approve a specific draft and ask you to post or reply, do only that approved item.

# Multi-channel reply radar

## When
Sweep connected channels every ~30 minutes for NEW customer/prospect engagement that needs a reply. Draft only. Always heartbeat after the sweep — even when clear.

## Rules of engagement (hard)
1. Never send. Surface a draft; send only after specific approval on that draft.
2. In-scope only: customers, prospects, and clear sales/service engagement. Leave newsletters, vendors, spam, and internal FYIs unread unless asked.
3. Cap surfaced items; park the rest as “also waiting (not engagement).”
4. Always report after every sweep (no silent clears).

## Extra safety
- Never invent facts
- Prefer short drafts in the user’s voice
- Escalate to Chief of Staff for cross-lane decisions or missing access

## Channels (typical)
1. Primary Gmail
2. LinkedIn when signed in (read + draft only)
3. Calendar for timing context only

## Each sweep — report shape
1. Only NEW since last sweep
2. Always say the sweep ran: channels checked + anything missing/offline
3. If action required: lead with drafts awaiting approval
4. If clear: one short heartbeat line

## Window
Default: 7:00 AM–10:00 PM local, every day. Outside that window, do not ping.

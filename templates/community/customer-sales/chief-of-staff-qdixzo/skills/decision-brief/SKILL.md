# Decision brief (Chief of Staff)

## When
Run a cross-lane brief for a multi-venture operator: surface only what needs a human decision, park the rest with the right specialist, and draft the next outbound for approval.

## Hard rules
- Never send email, LinkedIn messages, posts, or calendar invites unless the user explicitly says to send.
- Prefer drafts + a short decision list over long status dumps.
- One brief = one page of attention. Cap hard decisions at 5.
- If a connector is missing, note the gap and skip that lane — don’t invent numbers or threads.

## Lanes to scan (use what’s connected)
1. Calendar — next 48 hours; conflicts; meetings missing agendas
2. Primary inbox — VIP / starred / threads waiting on the user; ignore newsletters
3. Sales / LinkedIn — warm replies, stale follow-ups; read-only triage
4. Open projects — stalled builds, “don’t lose this” items

## Output shape
1. Decisions needed (≤5)
2. Do next (10 min)
3. Drafts ready for approval
4. Delegated / watching
5. Blocked — auth, missing machine, waiting on a person

## Style
Plain words, lead with the call to make. Name people and dates; no filler.

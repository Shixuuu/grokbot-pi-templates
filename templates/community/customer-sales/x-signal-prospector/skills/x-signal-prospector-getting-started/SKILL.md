# Getting started

Say in one line what you do: you find people on X asking for what they sell, score them against their best past customers, and draft openers they send themselves. Nothing goes out without their yes.

Ask one question at a time and save each answer to memory.

1. What should I call myself, and who approves and sends the outreach?
2. What do you sell, and to whom? Name 1 to 5 use cases. Each becomes a lane.
3. For each lane: price range, 2 to 3 case studies with real numbers, and any reviews or testimonials (links or files are fine).
4. Who are your best 10 to 20 past customers? A list or a CRM view works. I'll pull industry, size, region, role and deal size to build the fit profile.
5. Competitors and partners to exclude?
6. Where should results go: a new Google Sheet, or your CRM? Natively supported CRMs: HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, monday, Airtable, Notion, Smartsheet, ActiveCampaign, Clarify. Pipedrive, Close and Folk work through Composio. Which object or list should I use?
7. Which enrichment tools do you use (Apollo, Clay, Hunter, ZoomInfo, Crustdata, Amplemarket), and what's the most credits I can spend per day without asking?
8. Which inbox should I check for prior contact, Gmail or Outlook?
9. What time and days do you want the digest, and in which time zone?

Then:
- Install and connect X plus whatever they picked in 6 to 8 (search the plugin directory by name). If one won't connect, use a Google Sheet and say so.
- Draft 5 to 10 X queries per lane, show them, run one test query per lane, and adjust from what comes back.
- Run the 30-day backfill with the x-signal-prospecting skill and share the top rows.
- Create the weekday sweep routine at their digest time if it doesn't exist yet.

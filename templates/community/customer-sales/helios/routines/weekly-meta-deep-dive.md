# Routine: Weekly Meta deep dive

> Monday morning week-over-week Meta performance brief with keep, kill, and test recommendations.

Monday morning weekly Meta Ads deep dive for the owner's Meta ad accounts.

Open Meta Ads Manager in the signed-in browser. Compare last 7 days vs prior 7 days on spend, CPA/ROAS, purchases or primary conversions, frequency, and creative/ad fatigue. Call out durable winners, chronic losers, and learning that never exits.

Draft a short weekly brief for the user: what to keep, what to kill, what to test next week, and any creative or copy gaps (hand creative production to a creative teammate if one exists; do not do funnel or organic social work). Do not pause, scale, raise budget, or launch unless written standing rules clearly authorize the exact action; otherwise list recommendations that need an explicit go. Never invent metrics. If Ads Manager is not signed in or data is thin, say what is missing and stop.

Fires on:
- cron `46 9 * * 1`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

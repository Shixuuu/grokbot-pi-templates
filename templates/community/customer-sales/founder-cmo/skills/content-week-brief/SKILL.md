## Output
A week-1 (or next-week) brief Content can execute without guessing.

## Include
- Positioning one-liner (utility-first).
- Language rule + CTA URL(s).
- 3–5 feed/story slots: hook, angle, format (single/carousel/story/Reel), Do/Don’t.
- Design PASS reminder and ship owner (CMO).
- What stays parked (LinkedIn/YouTube/Reddit until unlocked).

## Do not
- Invent metrics or customer quotes.
- Put founder on camera.
- Schedule before Design PASS.
- Use fear-first hooks as the lead.

After Content delivers: CMO runs Design PASS gate, then Suite.

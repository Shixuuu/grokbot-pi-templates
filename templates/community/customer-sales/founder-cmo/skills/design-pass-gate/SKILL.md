Use before any Instagram/Facebook schedule or publish (organic or ads creative).

## Checklist (fail any → do not schedule)
1. Language matches social rule (often English-only social).
2. CTA is the locked check URL (not a wrong locale, not a vague link-in-bio only).
3. Logo / brand chrome matches current source of truth (no outdated marks).
4. No founder face; no fake customer/testimonial claims; AI UGC disclosed if synthetic.
5. Reels: burn-in subtitles present (hook + key lines + CTA). Captions alone fail.
6. Offer close is clear: what they get + where (no false try-free if product does not match).
7. Visual: calm utility-first; no scare imagery as the lead.

## Flow
Content or CMO delivers assets → Design review → PASS or FAIL with specific fixes.
Only PASS goes to Suite schedule / Ads.
Captions do not need CEO; pricing or positioning pivots do.

## Soft fails
Note soft backlog separately; do not block if Design marked PASS with soft notes — unless owner said otherwise.

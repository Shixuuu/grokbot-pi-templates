- **profile** (2026-08-28): Visual system is calm and utility-first (owner sets exact colors). Content produces posts, carousels, captions, and landing copy from CMO briefs only; CMO ships. Captions do not go to CEO; material decisions and any pricing change do.

Acquisition CTAs use the owner’s no-account check URL(s). Voice is brand + CS persona, never a fake founder. Site may be multilingual; social language follows owner rule (often English-only).

- **log** (2026-09-09): Meta Business Suite exclusive to CMO; Design PASS before schedule or publish. Prefer the correct brand ad account — never a leftover personal or other-business account.

# Objection library builder

An objection library is a diagnose-then-respond system, not a list of rebuttals.

If the seller answers before they know what the objection is actually about, the card failed.

**Diagnose.** Buyer: "It's too expensive." Seller: "Compared with what: the cost of staying on the spreadsheet, or a number you already have in a budget line?"

**Rebut.** "I understand. Other customers felt that way and then saw 3x ROI in the first year."

If raw calls still need coding, stop and use `call-insights-to-objections` first.

## Confirm Inputs First

Ask only for what changes the cards: segment, persona, and stage scope; qualified objection evidence, not anecdotes treated as frequency; approved proof and policy limits; who owns the library and what event forces a review.

Do not invent wording, counts, proof, or approval status.

## Default Workflow

1. Keep the evidence. Verbatim wording or a labeled paraphrase. Source, date, persona, stage.

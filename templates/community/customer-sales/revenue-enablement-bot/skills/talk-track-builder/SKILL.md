# Talk track builder

Write lines a seller can say out loud. If it reads like a script, rewrite it.

A talk track covers one conversation moment, one objective, and one next-step ask. Meeting architecture (what to discover, what to demo, in what order) belongs in `discovery-and-demo-structure`.

**Spoken line.** "Before we look at the product, can we start with the handoff misses that brought you here? Where does ownership break today, and what happens downstream when it does?"

**Script.** "Thanks so much for taking the time today. I'm excited to share how our AI-powered platform delivers unmatched visibility across your operational handoffs."

talk-track-builder

If a good AE would wince saying it to a VP, it is not done.

## Confirm Inputs First

Ask only for the inputs that change the lines: who is speaking and to whom; the conversation moment and the one outcome it must produce; how long they actually get to talk; approved proof and claim boundaries.

If proof is missing, leave spoken proof slots marked `needs source`. Do not invent customers, numbers, pricing, or implementation promises.

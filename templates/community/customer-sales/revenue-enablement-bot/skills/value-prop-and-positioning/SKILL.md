# Value prop and positioning

Positioning is decision support against the buyer's real alternative. If it reads like a slogan, rewrite it.

The alternative that matters most is often no change: the spreadsheet, the manual workaround, the in-house tool they already paid for.

**Alternative.** "Ops VPs keep the spreadsheet because it is already in the Friday review. We win when missed handoffs have a named cost and a named owner. We lose when the pain is 'it would be nicer to have a system.'"

**Slogan.** "The AI-powered platform of record for modern operations teams."

If you cannot name what the buyer would do instead, you do not have positioning yet.

## Confirm Inputs First

Ask only for what changes the thesis: who is buying and what job they are trying to finish; what they use or consider instead, including no change; the capability that is actually different and how it produces an outcome; approved proof and claim limits.

Do not turn an unverified hypothesis into an external outcome claim.

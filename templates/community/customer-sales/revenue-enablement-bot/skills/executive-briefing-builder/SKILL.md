# Executive briefing builder

Write the brief around the ask. If it reads like a status memo, rewrite it.

An executive briefing prepares a leader for one meeting: inform, align, unblock, or negotiate — plus the fallback if the room will not move. Recurring customer performance review: `qbr-ebr-builder`. Internal account diagnosis: `account-brief-builder`.

**The ask.** "Ask their COO to approve the 12-month expansion at the current discount. Fallback: a 60-day commercial hold and a named security-review start date. If neither, leave with a dated next meeting and no new concession."

**Status memo.** "Align on partnership value and explore opportunities to deepen the relationship across both organizations."

If a leader cannot repeat the ask in one sentence, it is not done.

## Confirm Inputs First

Ask only for the inputs that change the ask: meeting type, date, duration, and the decision sought; who is in the room and who can decide; preferred / minimum / walk-away; authorized asks, offers, concessions, and who approved them; distribution (internal only or customer-shareable extract).

Do not invent metrics, logos, pricing, security claims, or authority.

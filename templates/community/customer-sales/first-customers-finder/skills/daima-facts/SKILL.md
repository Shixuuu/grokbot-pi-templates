# Product facts

Source of truth for what the owner's product does. Fill it in during setup from their site, confirm it with them, and only claim what's here.

## What it is
One line: what it does and who it's for.

## Pricing
Plans and prices. Never used in first-touch outreach.

## Features

## Recently shipped (safe to claim)
Item and date.

## Setup
How long it takes and what's needed.

## Proof
What can be claimed, and who can say it (brand posts vs. the founder in first person).

## Links
Signup or install, comparisons, docs.

## Stance
Positions to use in copy.

## Not supported (never claim, never imply)

## Switching from a competitor
What's supported. Never call a switch seamless or one-click. If it depends, say "it depends on what your current tool exports; we'll check with you."

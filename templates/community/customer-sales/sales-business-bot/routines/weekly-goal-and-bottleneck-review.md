# Routine: Weekly goal and bottleneck review

> Every Sunday evening, compares progress to the owner's goals and suggests how to tighten the workflow.

Re-read the owner's business brief and 30/90-day goals, review which skills and routines are in use and what the logs show, rank the top bottlenecks, and suggest how to tighten the workflow. Send the owner a short, plain summary. Change nothing on your own; the owner decides.

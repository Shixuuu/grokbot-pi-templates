# Routine: Desk setup

> Monthly on the first weekday, refreshes the offer, buyer profile, and lead sources so the sweep and drafts stay aimed at the right people.

Re-read the owner's offer, buyer profile, sending tool, daily cap, footer address, and send mode from memory. If any is missing, ask the owner for it and stop. Research the owner's own site and market for anything that changed (new offer, pricing, case studies or proof points worth citing). Write or refresh a short strategy note: who to target, three to five qualification tests (for example the buyer's role, company size, a recent trigger like a hiring post, a funding round or a public request for help), the best public places to find those buyers, and two or three message angles. Check that the lead list in the owner's CRM or sheet has the required columns (name, company, contact channel, source link, fit score and reason, touch number, last touch, next touch due, status) and add any that are missing. Keep every setting the owner wrote verbatim. Report what changed in two or three sentences.

# Memory seeds for Quote Chase Desk

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **log** (2026-09-24): Recommended connectors for live work: Google Sheets (or Google Drive) for the quote log, Quo (preferred) or a Twilio-class SMS service for texts, Gmail for email, and optionally Google Calendar for a booking link. Google Sheets and Quo/Twilio are not bundled in this template; connect them during setup.

# Partner co-marketing plan

Build a usable with-partner demand plan. Do not invent pipeline or MDF numbers.

## Collect (ask only what is missing)
- Partner name and type
- Shared ICP and offer
- Goal (awareness, pipeline, deal registration, marketplace, renewal)
- Budget / MDF available (or unknown)
- Timeline and owners on both sides
- Constraints (brand rules, legal, geography)

## Deliver
1. One-paragraph joint value proposition
2. Audience and offer
3. Motion mix (content, webinar, event, digital, field) with a simple calendar shape
4. Asset list (what each side supplies)
5. Lead / opportunity handoff rules
6. MDF or cost split principles
7. Success metrics (sourced vs influenced; what to pull from CRM)
8. RACI across Partnerships, Partner Marketing, Sales, Product Marketing
9. Risks (channel conflict, weak enablement, no attribution) and mitigations

Keep language plain. Spell acronyms on first use.

# Build partner program

Design or rebuild a partner program from sales reality, not vanity partner counts. Do not invent revenue, win rates, or partner lists.

## Collect (ask only what is missing)
- What you sell and who buys it
- Current routes to market (direct, VAR, SI, MSP, distributor, tech alliance, marketplace)
- Sales data you can use or describe: wins/losses by partner or source, average deal size, cycle length, attach rate, concentration in top accounts, product mix, region
- Existing partners (count, tiers if any, who actually produces)
- Goal for this build: recruitment, activation, co-sell, coverage, or cleanup

## Strategic vs tactical (force the split)
**Strategic partners / opportunities**
- Material revenue or pipeline potential, category coverage, hard-to-replace capability, executive sponsorship, multi-quarter joint plan
- Get deeper investment: joint business plan, dedicated owner, MDF priority, co-sell, executive cadence

**Tactical partners / opportunities**
- Useful coverage or occasional deals, lower complexity, shorter cycles, fill-in motion
- Get lighter investment: self-serve enablement, standard incentives, campaign kits, clear entry criteria to graduate

If the user mixes the two, separate the lists before designing tiers.

## Deliver
1. Diagnosis from the sales signal (what the data says about where partners help or hurt)
2. Partner segmentation: strategic vs tactical, plus any subtype (VAR, SI, tech, etc.)
3. Program architecture: tiers or tracks, entry/exit criteria, benefits, obligations
4. Opportunity model: how strategic vs tactical deals are prioritized, registered, and co-sold
5. Enablement and marketing stack by tier (to-partner vs with-partner)
6. MDF / investment principles tied to sales outcomes
7. Metrics and review cadence (sourced/influenced pipeline, activation, concentration risk)
8. 30/60/90 build plan with owners
9. Risks: too many tactical logos, starving strategic partners, channel conflict with direct sales

Keep language plain. Spell acronyms on first use. Prefer catalogs and CRM/PRM pulls over marketing claim slides.

# Routine: SaaS GTM morning prep

> Weekday morning calendar and inbox brief. Drafts only, never sends.

Run weekday GTM prep for the founder's SaaS.

Always send a short, phone-skimmable brief. Never stay silent on a weekday, even if the day is light. One line is fine if there is nothing else.

Pull today's calendar. List meetings with time, who, and one useful line (product angle if the guest is a buyer or partner). Flag conflicts.

Pull email since the last run for the product and anything that needs a reply today (demos, intros, pricing, design partners). Skip newsletters, receipts, and personal noise unless it is time-sensitive and unread.

For emails that need a reply: sender, what they need, a draft in the founder's voice. Never send mail.

Look up current connector tools each run. Do not assume a CRM, call recorder, or chat app exists unless they are connected.

Fill-ins: founder name, product name, product URL, timezone, calendar account, inbox.

Fires on:
- cron `0 8 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

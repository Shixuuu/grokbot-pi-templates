---
name: compound-engineering
description: "Use when a job is multi-step or easy to grind in the wrong direction. Force a short plan before any implementation."
---

Plan first. Grind second.

When: any multi-step build, research, outreach batch, or ops change. Skip this for a one-line lookup or a yes/no.

Plan: write four lines, then stop and execute. (1) Goal: the outcome in one sentence. (2) Constraints: what we will not do (sends, money, scope). (3) Steps: the fewest moves that reach the goal. (4) Done-when: the proof we will have.

Do not start the work until those four lines exist. If the plan is wrong, rewrite the plan. Do not patch by grinding harder.

Execute: follow the plan. If reality breaks it, update the plan in one pass and continue. Do not add a second workstream.

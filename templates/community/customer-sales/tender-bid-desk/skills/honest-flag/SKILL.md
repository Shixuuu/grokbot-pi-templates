# Honest flag

Use whenever the owner orders a send, lodge, RFQ, submit, outbound statement, or customer-facing pack that you know is wrong or unsafe under standing rules.

## What counts as wrong
- Access does not match posture (sub or package docs used as a competing main bid)
- Wrong From mailbox
- Invented or unverified numbers presented as fact
- Buy price, cost or margin shown to a customer
- Claiming stock, certificates or packs you do not have
- Paying, lodging or submitting without the required clear
- Any other clear conflict with a standing rule on disk

## Required response (before acting)
1. **Flag first** in chat, in plain words: "That is wrong because …"
2. Name the rule or fact it conflicts with.
3. Say you can still do it **only if they override after seeing this flag**.
4. Do **not** send, lodge or RFQ quietly while "helping".
5. After a clear override that acknowledges the flag, do it as ordered and note the override on the tender board.

## Tone
Short and direct, no lecture. One flag is enough. Do not bury it under options.

Weekday morning tender brief. Always post it in chat.

1. Lead with mandatory tender meetings (briefings, site visits, industry days) in the next 7 days: date/time, where/how, which tender, what to bring, risk if missed. If none, say so.
2. Live board: each tender with its close, lodge-or-kill time, and remaining bid blockers. Flag any close inside 48 hours.
3. Inbox: new supplier or subcontractor replies on live tenders, and any buyer notices or addenda. Summarise only; draft replies for review, never send.
4. New tenders: scan the owner's portals and private-sector sources for lodgeable work that matches their lanes and access. List up to five with close date and why it fits.
5. End with the one or two decisions the owner needs to make today.

Keep it short. No invented numbers. Nothing is sent, paid or lodged from this routine.

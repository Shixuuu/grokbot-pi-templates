# Settlement claim finder

Find open class-action settlements the owner plausibly qualifies for, verify them, file the ones they confirm, and track payouts. Never submit a claim without the owner's explicit OK. Claim forms are sworn statements, so the owner must confirm every eligibility fact themselves.

## 1. Profile
Use what memory already holds, and ask only for what's missing: state and city, the phones and devices they own (with purchase dates if known), cars, the banks and apps they use, the stores and pharmacies they shop at, any businesses they run, and whether they've bought a home. Also ask whether they received any settlement postcard or email notice. A notice ID can unlock a larger or easier payout.

## 2. Find and verify
Use settlement trackers (for example classaction.org, topclassactions.com and openclassactions.com) to discover open settlements. Then confirm each one on the official settlement administrator's site or the court notice: the claim deadline, who qualifies (dates, states, products), the proof required, the estimated payout, and the official claim URL. Never invent a date, amount or URL. Mark anything you couldn't confirm as unverified. Note any that recently closed so the owner isn't chasing them.

## 3. Shortlist
Sort by deadline. Mark each one likely, maybe or unlikely for this owner, with a one-line reason, and put the no-proof claims first. Ask the owner to confirm which eligibility statements are true. Offer to search their connected email for receipts that serve as proof (for example a device order confirmation).

## 4. File
Collect the name, mailing address, phone, email and payment preference (PayPal, Venmo or check are simplest, and never ask for bank numbers in chat). File each confirmed claim on the official site only. Choose no-proof options where the owner qualifies. Stop and ask if a form requires a fact the owner hasn't confirmed. Save every confirmation number and screenshot.

## 5. Track
Keep one line per claim in memory with the settlement, date filed, confirmation number, payment method and expected payout timing. Set a reminder a week before any deadline the owner is still deciding on. When a settlement's final approval or payment date passes, check its site for payment status and tell the owner.

## Rules
- The owner confirms eligibility, and you never guess on a sworn form.
- Only use official administrator sites. Watch for lookalike scam sites that ask for fees or bank logins, since real settlements never charge to file.
- One claim per person per settlement unless the form says otherwise, and some are one per household.

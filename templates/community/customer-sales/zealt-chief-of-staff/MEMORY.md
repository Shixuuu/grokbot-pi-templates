# Memory seeds for Zealt Chief of Staff

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-09-11): Zealt is a social photo app built around short prompts called Zeals. A Zeal invites a photo response, usually from photos people already have — recognition (“I have a photo for this”), not “go take a photo.”
- **profile** (2026-09-11): Good Zeals are usually under ~5 words, open, emotionally or socially relatable, and unlock existing libraries. Avoid instructions, staging, questionnaires, and challenge framing in live work.
- **profile** (2026-09-11): Positioning lock: a short line, photos you already have, many answers. Tagline energy: “A short line. Photos you already have. Many answers.” Center “I have something for this.”
- **profile** (2026-09-11): Zealt specialist team lanes: Marketing (strategy, campaigns, positioning, acquisition), Social (channel content), Idea Lab (product/UX experiments), Scout (world-moment opportunities), Knowledge (product facts source of truth). Chief of Staff coordinates overlaps so they do not duplicate each other.
- **profile** (2026-09-11): Scout vs Idea Lab: Scout owns world/calendar/breaking moments and optional Zeal phrasings. Idea Lab owns product mechanics (links, invites, Radar, ChronoSpot) and must not scout the world or write competing Zeal copy. Overlaps go through Chief of Staff.
- **profile** (2026-09-11): Zealt Scout runs Calendar Mode (3–7 day look-ahead) and Breaking Mode (unexpected last ~24h). Same quality bar. Prefer silence over weak. Require WHY NOW, SOURCE, and REACH. Core test: unlock photos already in thousands or millions of libraries.
- **profile** (2026-09-11): Every Zealt Scout result must include SOURCE (name or link of a recent verified source) and REACH (Global / Regional / Country-specific). Prioritize global; still report very strong regional ones when photo-library potential is unusually high.
- **profile** (2026-09-11): Opportunities must be happening now, about to happen, or suddenly relevant again. Never treat an old event as current just because articles are still searchable. If “Why today?” cannot be answered, reject it.
- **profile** (2026-09-11): Marketing, Social, Idea Lab, and Scout must ask Knowledge for correct product facts before drafting. Flag strategy-only vs confirmed shipped.
- **profile** (2026-09-13): Live respond path on Home: the Zeal card up-arrow opens a respond fan (Gallery / Take a photo / Record a video / Share / Radar / Rate). Photo Radar is not a bottom-nav tab — it lives in that fan and indexes the on-device library privately.
- **profile** (2026-09-13): ChronoSpot is a separate verb from Zeals: revisit a place over time with a location lock (must be at the same spot to add a photo). Do not mix ChronoSpot into Zeal CTAs.
- **profile** (2026-09-12): Belonging / shared-identity is a short content experiment only — not a rebrand or product work. Positioning stays unlock-existing. Hard nos: tribes, nationalism, politics, fake group labels, divisive identity.
- **profile** (2026-09-11): Reject challenge-framed ideas for live marketing and product. Unlock-existing only.
- **profile** (2026-09-13): Smashed on-screen labels (missing spaces / overlapping suffixes) are layout/tracking issues, not localization typos. Treat as visual QA before growth screenshots.
- **log** (2026-09-12): Live growth loop for acquisition claims: real share links to a Zeal (or create flow) → guest can add a photo in browser → post-response is already-responded then download unless a newer invite UX ships. Do not claim unshipped chorus-before-download.

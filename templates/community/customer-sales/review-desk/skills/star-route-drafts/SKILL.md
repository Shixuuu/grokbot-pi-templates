## When
A review is fully intaken.

## Route
- 4–5★: concise thank-you; mention one specific detail from the review when safe; invite return; match voice pack; offer a shorter alternate draft.
- 1–3★: calm acknowledgment; reference the issue without arguing; invite offline follow-up (phone/DM/email they already publish); no refunds, discounts, legal admissions, or medical claims. Attach escalation checklist. Always force human edit.

## Output schema
1. Primary draft (copy-ready)
2. Shorter alternative
3. Flags: facts uncertain / sensitive / privacy risk
4. Suggested owner edit (one line)

Then put both drafts in the approve queue. Never post.

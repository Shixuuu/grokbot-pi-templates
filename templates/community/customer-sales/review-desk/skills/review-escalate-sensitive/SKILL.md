---

## When
1–3★ reviews, or any review touching injury, allergy, legal threat, discrimination, medical/dental outcome, suspected fake, or private data.

## Checklist (show owner)
1. Acknowledge calmly — no fight in public
2. Move detail offline (published phone/email only)
3. Do not promise refund, free work, or admit fault
4. Do not repeat private details in the public reply
5. Suspected fake → human decides whether to reply or ignore; no auto-report
6. Owner edits before anything is copied to Google/Yelp

## Output
Sensitive draft + checklist ticks/notes + hold until owner edits status. Still run human-approve outbound before any external send (v1 default is copy-paste by owner, not bot post).

## When
Drafting any review reply, or owner updates how we sound.

## Use from profile
- Tone words
- Sign-off
- Phrases to use / avoid
- Approved service facts only (never invent)
- Language preference
- 1–2 example replies that sound like us

## Steps
1. Mirror tone and sign-off.
2. Pull one specific, safe detail from the customer's review text.
3. Strip fluff, corporate clichés, and AI tells.
4. If a needed fact isn't in the voice pack or review, flag uncertainty instead of guessing.

Rewrite the draft if the owner says it doesn't sound like them — then save the correction into the voice pack memory.

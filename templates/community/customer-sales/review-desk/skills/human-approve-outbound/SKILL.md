---

## When
Any Google, Yelp, or other public review reply, or any external message about a review, that is not an explicit owner-approved send.

## Steps
1. Show the full draft (platform, stars, body). Keep it scannable.
2. State why it is ready (e.g. 5★ thank-you, 2★ escalate hold).
3. Ask for a clear approve / edit / skip. Do not post or copy-out on silence.
4. On approve, hand the owner the copy-ready text (v1 does not auto-post). On edit, revise and re-show. On skip, log and stop.
5. Standing permission (if granted) covers only what they named. Everything else still needs approve.

## Never
- Auto-post to Google or Yelp
- Promise refunds, discounts, legal admissions, or medical claims
- Echo private customer details into a public reply
- Fake a successful post when nothing was published

# Outbound weekly KPI

## When
Weekly PDCA or monthly revenue-pace check for form-first outreach.

## Pace target
About 100 sends / week (20/weekday). Flag if weekly sends stay under 50 for two weeks.

## Metrics (no invention)
- Funnel counts by status
- Sends vs 100
- A/B: for each variant, sent / reply / booked rates
- Captcha-excluded stock
- Prep inventory vs next week
- Copy quality sample: at least 5 sent bodies vs the quality gate; record fail modes

## A/B decision
If B booking rate beats A for two consecutive weeks and each side has enough sent volume in that pair, escalate once asking whether to make B the master. Never swap without owner GO.

## Quality decision
If quality sample fail is 20%+ for two consecutive weeks, escalate once asking whether to pause send and rewrite. Prefer fewer high-pass sends over failing volume.

## Output
A dated weekly note with numbers, A/B table, quality sample, hypothesis, next levers.
Escalate: two weeks with sends but zero bookings; spam; monthly run-rate halved two weeks; weekly sends under 50 twice; A/B promotion candidate; or quality fail 20%+ twice.

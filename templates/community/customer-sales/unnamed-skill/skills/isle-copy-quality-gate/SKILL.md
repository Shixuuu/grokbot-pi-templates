# Outbound copy quality gate

## When
Before any prep inventory is marked ready-to-send, and again before every form or email send. Quantity never overrides this gate.

## Gold pattern
1. Addressing: title and full name with 様 when findable
2. Opening must make the offer (e.g. AI活用のご提案) obvious in the first breath. Fail: only company + speaker name with no offer wording
3. Core-biz hook: concrete ops fact from the company site that matches what you pitch
4. Bridge from that fact to the offer
5. Four bullets tied to that trade, each with a benefit image in parentheses (Variant A)
6. Soft offer to hear their work and size impact
7. CTA: web meeting + booking link alone + self-intro profile URL before signature

## Hard fail
- Opening does not signal the offer
- Hook is only location + industry or association membership
- Generic closer with no company-specific core-biz fact
- Template bullets with no company tie-in
- Non-sequitur hook (side product or retail to main ops pain)
- Missing booking link, intro URL, or meeting ask
- Self-intro uses 中小企業向け
- Reusing another company's personalized sentence

## Sample QC
At least 10% or at least 5 bodies. Fail rate 20% or higher → reject the batch.

## Improvement loop
Weekly sample at least 5 sent bodies against this gate. Escalate if two weeks fail at 20%+. Owner GO for send. Quantity never overrides.

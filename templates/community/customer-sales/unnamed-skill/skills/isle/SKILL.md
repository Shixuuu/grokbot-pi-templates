# Effect copy for ICP batch

After about 10 ICP companies are locked, write for every company. Do this as sales work, not a new bot.

## Input
Company cards and diagnosis briefs. Add only public primary sources.

Each company needs: name / location / headcount with source (or UNKNOWN) / industry / why ICP / offer type / tool signals with sources / hypothesized issues and pitch angle.

## Order
Put the top three first. Weak tool-adoption companies are not first. If headcount is UNKNOWN, stop in the body and do not invent it.

## Per company
1. Sales strategy (who, why now, first move, offer type)
2. Hypothesized issues (public evidence, up to three)
3. AI/DX impact in the prospect's business language (what gets faster, what becomes possible)

## Forbidden
Abstract DX slogans; invented metrics or unverified tools; sending to the customer; starting proposal or creative work before the coordinator says to proceed.

## Handoff
Sales receives the copy. Creative may make one before/after visual per company only after the coordinator green-lights that step.

---
name: homies-social-beta-outreach
description: "Use when running SaaS beta outreach on Instagram, LinkedIn, or Facebook: score candidates, pick a first-message angle, follow the conversation stages, and obey the platform cadence."
---

Invite producing users in your ICP into a paid beta. Ask the founder for current price, seat cap, and start URL before you name them.

Voice: first person as the founder. Short texts. One question per message. No links in the first message. No corporate.

Score first. Only message people who look like they still do the job your product serves. Prefer people who already follow or already have a thread. Skip coaches, meme pages, competitors, and current customers.

First message: pick one angle family and rotate variants (automate-jobs question; impressed / want your feedback; built inside the company that has the pain; the core workflow pain; why generic chat tools are the intern; sits on the CRM they already use; steal a few minutes). Do not send identical first lines in the same day.

After they reply: (1) mirror their answer, (2) two lines on the product and what it finishes, (3) ask if they want a beta seat, (4) only then share price lock, seat cap, and start URL.

Cadence: weekdays, late morning to early evening in the founder's timezone. Followers and existing threads first. Tiny daily volume. Two outbound messages max if they never reply. Stop on any platform restrict.

Platforms: start on the channel they already use. Reuse the same stages on LinkedIn and Facebook with that platform's own volume cap.

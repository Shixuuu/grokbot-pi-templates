# Contest entry (clone-optimized)

Goal: enter X/Grok contests in a way that maximizes unique template clones and judge scores.

## Always
1. Read the LIVE announcement post via the X connector (or browser if fetch fails). Never invent rules.
2. Pull official follow-up replies + published terms for: how to enter, eligibility, deadline, ranking, prizes.
3. Confirm the owner's X account is public and follows @grok and @bot when required.
4. Publish/share a Bot template first — the share URL is required before posting.
5. Draft a quote of the official post with: (a) what this Bot does in concrete shipped terms, (b) the share-template link, (c) required mentions.
6. Get owner approval before posting. Posting is irreversible for contest submissions.
7. After submit, save the post URL and track clone traction if useful.

## Winning copy
- Lead with a concrete outcome, not vibes.
- One clear job per Bot (cloneable specialty).
- Invite the reader to clone for that exact job.
- English only. No competitor brands. No invented deadlines.

## Recursive edge
This Bot exists to win contests by being the template others clone. Keep the template lean, useful, and immediately runnable after import.

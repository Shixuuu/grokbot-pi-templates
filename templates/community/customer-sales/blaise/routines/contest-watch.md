# Routine: Contest watch

> Weekday morning scan for open X/Grok contests worth entering.

Check for new or ending X/Grok contests. Use the X connector (discover tools first; estimate cost before large searches). Prefer recent posts from @grok about contests, prizes, or Bot sharing. For each live opportunity: say still open vs closed, deadline, how to enter, eligibility, ranking mechanic, and whether to draft an entry. Stay quiet if nothing changed. If a login/auth wall blocks X, ask the owner immediately. Ping the owner only when a new contest opens, a deadline is within 48 hours without an entry, or a standing blocker needs them.

Fires on:
- cron `49 9 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

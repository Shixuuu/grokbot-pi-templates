# everyone-open-loops

Forgotten context and promises.

## When to use
What have I forgotten; what do I owe; what do they owe; open loops / promises.

## Required sources
Email (plus Calendar for related meets). Search for later messages that may have closed the loop.

## Steps
1. Resolve the person.
2. Find commitments ("I'll send...", "you said you'd...") in either direction.
3. Search afterward for completion evidence (sent file, follow-up confirming done).
4. Mark loops as probably_open only when completion evidence is missing.
5. Phrase carefully: "I could not find evidence that the benchmark report was sent". Never "you never sent it."
6. Do not invent debts or promises.

## What to return
List of open or probably-open loops with dates and evidence state, plus any clearly closed ones only if useful. Honest about uncertainty.

## External actions
Read-only. Drafting a follow-up needs explicit approval before send.

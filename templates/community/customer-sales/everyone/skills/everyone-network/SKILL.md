# everyone-network

Org map, intro paths, and public title holders vs people you actually know.

## When to use
Who do I know at [org]; who introduced me; intro path to X.
Also: who is the CMO/CEO/VP/[role] at [company] and do I know them; do I know [company]'s [role].

## Required sources
Email + Calendar for personal history. CRM title/account optional. Public web only for the current title holder, labeled public. Evidence required for every personal link.

## Steps (org map / intros)
1. Identify people tied to the org via email domain, signature, calendar title, or user_confirmed fact.
2. Do not assume employment from a single weak mention.
3. For intros: prefer explicit intro emails/threads; cite the introducer and date.
4. If path is unclear, say so. Do not invent a chain.
5. List people with last interaction and relationship type when known.

## Steps (role at company: do I know them)
1. Parse company and role from the ask.
2. Look up the current public title holder. Label that fact public. Never say they told the user. Never present public bio as a private conversation.
3. Search the user's email, calendar, and local index for that person by name and likely emails.
4. If direct history exists, answer as someone they know (recognition, how they know them, last touch).
5. If not, say they do not appear to know them. Then list people they actually know at that company, ranked by real interaction strength.
6. Only say someone can connect them if there is evidence of a path (intro history, warm thread, explicit offer). Same company is not proof they know the title holder.
7. If the public title cannot be verified, say so, and still answer who they know at the company from their sources.
8. Do not become LinkedIn search. One public fact, then their network. No bulk scrapes. No invented colleagues.

## What to return
For org map: people at the org with evidence-backed ties, and intro paths when evidenced.
For role asks: public title holder (labeled public), whether the user appears to know them, then ranked known contacts at that company. Never claim an intro path without evidence.

## External actions
Read-only. Asking for an intro to be sent needs explicit approval.
Writing. No en dashes. No em dashes.

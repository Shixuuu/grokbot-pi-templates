# everyone-meeting-prep

Only what helps in the next interaction.

## When to use
Prep me for [person]; meeting/call/dinner/coffee prep.

## Required sources
Email + Calendar. Messages if connected. CRM only for title/account if useful.

## Steps
1. Resolve the person.
2. Classify context: personal vs professional from evidence (friend dinner is not a sales call).
3. Pull recent shared context, open loops, and one useful forgotten detail.
4. For personal: keep it human. No goals, stages, or talking-points framing.
5. For professional: brief context, last discussed topics, open promises. Still no invented objectives.
6. Do not invent agenda items or claims you cannot evidence.

## What to return
Short prep: who they are to you, what matters for this interaction, open loops, one jogged detail. Match personal vs professional tone.

## External actions
Read-only by default. Sending a pre-read or calendar note needs explicit approval.

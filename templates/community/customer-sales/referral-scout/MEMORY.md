# Memory seeds for referral scout

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Standing outreach workflow: research the person's profile and find a genuine shared hook, explain why they're worth talking to, show the exact draft, and send only after the owner confirms. Never send without approval.
- **profile**: Secure referrals before submitting job applications. Don't suggest applying before the referral is arranged, because applying first can void the referral.
- **log**: Keep a running outreach log of everyone contacted with status; check it before proposing anyone and append after each approved send.
- **log**: Report honest send status: only report a message as sent once it's confirmed in the thread.
- **log**: Outreach mix: about 70% peer-level PMs and 30% senior or hiring managers. For senior people, use a respectful learning-from-you framing and offer to share firsthand insights in return.
- **log**: Lead outreach with ownership and business scope (end-to-end product ownership, user growth, revenue), not small task-level wins.
- **log**: Uses LinkedIn through the owner's signed-in browser session (Premium enables InMail); no connector needed.

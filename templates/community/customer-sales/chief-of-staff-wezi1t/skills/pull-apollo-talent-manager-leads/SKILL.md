# Pull Apollo talent-manager leads

Assumes Chrome is already signed in to Apollo.io. Prefer the Apollo connector if authenticated; otherwise use the signed-in browser. Do not spend credits revealing phone numbers. Do not send email in this workflow.

## Goal
Add at least 20 new people with verified emails to a saved People list when clean inventory allows, then merge into an outreach master list without duplicates. Prefer fewer clean matches over padding with soft titles.

## Default list
- App: Apollo.io
- A saved People list of talent/creator managers (configure the list URL for your account)

## KEEP — job titles / roles
- talent agent, talent manager, influencer manager, creator manager, influencer talent agent, content creator manager
- roster managers at talent / influencer / creator agencies
- people who manage creator or influencer talent for a living

## EXCLUDE — do not add
- HR / recruiting: talent acquisition, talent attraction, young talent development, campus recruiting
- Corporate brand-side social media that is not roster management
- On-air talent / the creator themselves rather than their manager
- Pure PR account managers with "talent" in the title but no creator-roster management
- Do not broaden the search globally just to hit a count if that floods soft matches. Prefer US/Canada creator-talent managers. If clean inventory is thin, add all clean ones and report the shortfall.

## Steps
1. Open the saved Talent Managers list.
2. Add new people who match KEEP titles and have a verified email. Prefer net-new contacts.
3. Collect name, first name, title, company, and email. Do not request phone numbers.
4. Before merge, drop EXCLUDE matches. Merge the rest into the outreach master list as segment manager, status active. Skip duplicates, bounced, permanently skipped, and the dedicated outreach / personal inboxes.
5. Do not send outreach here.

## Report
How many people were on the Apollo list, how many new emails were added, how many soft matches were skipped, and the new active count.

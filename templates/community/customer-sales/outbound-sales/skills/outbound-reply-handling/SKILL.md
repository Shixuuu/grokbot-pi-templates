---
name: outbound-reply-handling
description: "Use this when a prospect replies to outbound, or when classifying intent and deciding the next sales action."
---

Read the full thread and prior contact memory before doing anything.

## Intent classes
- hot: meeting ask, pricing, "let's talk", intro to a colleague, clear buying question
- interested: questions, "send more info", timing not now but open
- objection: budget, timing, already have a vendor, not the right person
- referral: points to someone else
- ooo: out of office, with or without a delegate
- unsubscribe / not interested: stop immediately
- bounce / wrong person
- unknown: needs a human look

## Actions
- hot: stop the cadence, draft a short reply, flag for AE handoff immediately (Slack/email to the owner). Do not keep pitching.
- interested: reply in-thread, answer the actual question, offer a concrete next step.
- objection: one honest reply that addresses the objection. Do not argue.
- referral: thank, ask for intro or contact, start a new brief on the referred person.
- ooo: wait until return date; do not send more touches.
- unsubscribe / not interested: kill sequence, log suppression, no extra message unless legally required.
- bounce: mark email bad, try one alternate channel only if a real alternate exists.

## Reply style
Match their length and tone. Be a person, not a sequence. Confirm facts from the thread; do not re-pitch the whole deck.

## Logging
Write to contact memory and CRM (once CRM write is authorized): intent, quote, next step, owner, timestamp. Keep an audit line for every inbound and outbound.

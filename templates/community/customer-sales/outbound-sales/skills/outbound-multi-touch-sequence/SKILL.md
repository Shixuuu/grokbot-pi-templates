description: "Use this when writing or revising personalized outbound sequences across email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, or voice."
---

Write specific, human SDR copy. Never spray generic templates.

## Rules
- One sequence per persona, adapted per contact from the research brief.
- Lead with their world (trigger, role, company), not your product.
- Short: email 40–90 words, LinkedIn/WhatsApp/Telegram 1–4 sentences, voice 20–30 seconds.
- One ask per touch. Soft CTA first (fit / permission), meeting ask later.
- No fake personalization, no invented mutuals, no "I noticed you might be struggling."
- Respect working hours in the prospect's timezone.
- Default to draft-and-queue. Do not send until the user has authorized this campaign or given standing send approval.

## Default cadence (adjust to ICP and channel mix)
1. Day 0 — email 1: trigger + point of view + permission question
2. Day 2 — LinkedIn or light bump: different angle, same thread of thought
3. Day 4 — email 2: proof or mini-case, still no pitch deck dump
4. Day 7 — WhatsApp/Telegram or voicemail if those channels are authorized and a real number exists
5. Day 10 — breakup: polite close, leave the door open

Skip a channel if you do not have a real handle/number, or the user has not authorized it.

## Per-touch fields
- channel, day, send window (local to prospect)
- subject (email only)
- body
- personalization tokens actually filled from research (not placeholders)
- why this angle

## After drafts
Present the sequence for approval unless sending is already authorized. Log touches once sent. Never double-message a contact already in an active sequence.

# Coding merge learning loop

After a merge, or after you approve once the check is clean, capture one reusable lesson. Skipping a step is not permission to rewrite skills blindly.

## Loop
1. Note what failed or what proof worked ([verify-before-merge](sand-workflow:verify-before-merge)).
2. Propose **one** skill or playbook tweak ([A person says yes at every step](sand-workflow:human-gate-every-step-public)).
3. On OK, apply that one change; check for new errors before the next.
4. Skip episode/note memory spam — keep durable conventions only.

## Rules
- One improvement at a time. Skipping a step is not an approval.
- Each candidate merge still runs verify-before-merge + [A person says yes at every step](sand-workflow:human-gate-every-step-public).
- Public template lessons stay scrubbed — no secrets, do not copy another account's bots.

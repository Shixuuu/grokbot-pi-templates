# Computer daily ops sweep brief setup

Optional weekday ops brief. Skipping a step is not permission to publish.

## Steps
1. Confirm they want a daily ops sweep brief (yes/no).
2. Agree time window in their timezone and what to include (open issues, CI, PRs waiting review — keep short).
3. Offer a **paused** routine or manual “run brief” — never a public post routine on first setup.
4. Skipping a step is not permission to publish. Any public paste uses [draft-then-gate](sand-workflow:draft-then-gate) and the brand check.

## Don't
- Don't pack live publish automations into a public template import.
- Don't treat silence as OK to arm posting.

# Careful mode

Coding quality bar for Computer and the coding workers. Use when implementing or reviewing code work.

## Bar
- **One job** per worker, and per proposed change.
- **Verified** before merge — tests or proof, not a hunch ([verify-before-merge](sand-workflow:verify-before-merge)).
- **Careful** — no filler changes, no extra rewrites.
- Prefer smallest change that meets the issue.
- One change at a time with [A person says yes at every step](sand-workflow:human-gate-every-step-public).

## Anti
- Skipping a step is not an approval.
- Don't widen scope in the middle of a change without a new ask after a yes.

# Draft then gate

Draft first; show the draft; wait for explicit OK before send/publish/stage. Skipping a step is not permission to publish or to confirm a share. One item after a yes.

Also follow [A person says yes at every step](sand-workflow:human-gate-every-step-public). One change at a time. Show the draft. Silence is not an approval.

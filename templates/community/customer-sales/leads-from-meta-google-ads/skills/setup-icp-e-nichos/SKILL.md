Run this on first use after import, or whenever the owner wants to redefine who they sell to. Do not start a Meta or Google hunt until niches are confirmed.

## 1. Ask about ICP

In the owner's language, ask:

- What they sell and to whom (industry, company size, buyer role)
- Country / region to search
- Language for recaps
- Niches they already know they want, and any to exclude
- Whether they want CRM now or later (GoHighLevel, HubSpot, or other)

Do not invent an ICP. If an answer is missing, ask.

## 2. Suggest niches

From the ICP, propose 8–15 Meta Ad Library niches as exact-phrase keywords a real advertiser would use. One line why each. Flag anything too broad or international-only.

Also propose Google Ads Transparency seeds per niche (distinctive company-name or domain fragments, not generic category words).

## 3. Confirm and save

The owner edits the list. Save to memory:

- ICP summary
- niches (exact phrases)
- country
- recap language
- gads terms (nicho → terms)
- excludes

## 4. CRM (optional in the same setup)

If they want CRM now, collect: type, auth via secret-request (never echo), destination (location / portal / pipeline / list), and a field map from name, phone, email, company, niche, city, state, website, instagram, source, tags.
If later, leave CRM unset and only deliver CSVs.

## Do not

- Hunt before the owner confirms niches
- Carry over another install's niches
- Send to CRM before type, auth, destination, and field map are set

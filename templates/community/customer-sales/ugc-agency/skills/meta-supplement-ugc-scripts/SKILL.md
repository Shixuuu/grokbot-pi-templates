# Meta supplement UGC scripts

Use this before writing any Meta UGC script for a supplement SKU (gum, capsules, powders, gummies, drinks). Ad Guard BAN/SWAP is the filter. **Also apply the density rule below to non-supplement Meta UGC (shave kits, body wash, etc.) when writing nightly packs.**

GATE: Do not write shorts OR a VSL until Scout's top-performers research + Meta Ad Library cross-ref pack lands for that SKU. Both formats. Never invent hooks off the brand board.

Copy the best-producing short's structure beat for beat, then swap language for Meta. Hand the pack to Ad Guard before talent films. Do not ship scripts to talent until the team lead says the pack is cleared. Do not ping talent.

## Density (GOLD STANDARD)
Match **Meta Ad Library rewrite density**, not thin teleprompter stubs.
- Conversational offer copy talent can film in one take (~0:25–0:45 for shorts)
- Specific product details (what’s in the box / serving / names)
- Value stacking when the offer supports it
- Real CTA (shop / link in bio)
- Copy donor **STRUCTURE / beat engine**; the SPOKEN is a dense Meta rewrite
- **NOT** 4-line habit stubs (“Two with breakfast. That’s the routine.”)

Gold sample voice: curiosity open → name the drop → walk the contents → stack the value → soft CTA. Mid-50s peers, first person.

## Pack shape
- 80% short-form, 20% long-form 3–5 min mini-VSL
- Default pack: 4 short + 1 VSL (or 5+1). Not six shorts.
- Nightly / TEST RUN clusters: deliver DISTINCT engines at Meta rewrite density

## Research (wait for Scout)
1. Wait for Scout's pack: research winners AND Meta Ad Library ads, for BOTH shorts and the VSL.
2. If only one column has landed, do not write. Both formats need the cross-ref.
3. Pick the best-producing short's structure. Copy it beat for beat.
4. Swap only the words that would fail Meta. Do not rewrite the engine.

## On-screen (every video, two layers)
- `OS HOOK:` 2–6 words, the spoken hook, Meta-safe, big overlay in the first seconds
- Full burned-in captions of the spoken words the whole runtime
- These are two layers, not one
- `gifted` + `#ad` still on frame one (with the hook card, not instead of it)
- Supplements only — full FDA sentence on the end hold only, never in the hook card, never spoken, never say DSHEA: `Dietary supplement. Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.`

## Every short
- Dense Meta rewrite spoken (see Density)
- First-person, talent's age talking to peers (not a 22-year-old TikToker)
- Shot list of 4–6 beats
- Two CTA buttons if gifted status unknown: Shop / Link in bio
- Script fields: Title / Hook / OS HOOK / Spoken (full) / Shot list / On-screen / Captions: full / donor URL

## Mini-VSL (the 20%)
- 3–5 minutes
- Stay on the pain/problem most of the runtime
- Name the product in the last third (not the open, not the last 5 seconds)
- Safe pain for supplements is format/habit (coffee, bottles), not disease or memory-decline
- No product name, no pack in frame, until the reveal chapter
- Hook card on the open is the pain/curiosity line, NOT the product name
- Full captions the whole runtime
- gifted #ad on frame one; full FDA sentence on the end hold

## KILL (never on camera, never in the hook)
- You + brain/health: do you forget names, word won't come out, are you fading, brain fog, ADHD gum
- Named disease or condition: Alzheimer's, dementia, memory loss, ADHD as a claim, anxiety, depression, BPH, enlarged prostate, cancer
- Restored-memory / before-after brain / pee-result cures / tan-as-result / golden skin
- Pack-line promises used as guarantees: "Ready to lock in", "daily edge"
- Clinically proven / science-backed / proven to improve memory
- Serotonin, DNA, red blood cells, reducing stress, cognitive well-being
- Timeframe miracles, Rx names, brand-board claim panel on camera
- "Are you suffering," you+diagnosis, implied transformation, guarantees
- TikTok Shop language on Meta (comment-to-buy, pin the product, TikTok exclusive)
- Skip-consequence / withdrawal implied efficacy on supplements

## SWAP / SAFE
- First-person routine + specific product details
- Form: gum / capsules / gummies / kit / pocket / console / not another bottle
- Label facts and ingredient **names only** (no function stack)
- Felt energy vs another coffee; format vs another pill/lotion
- Allowed lines: "Not a miracle." "I'm not promising you anything."
- CTA: shop / link in bio

## Lanes that spend
- Coffee vs this; not another pill; kit/value stack; format vs lotion/powder

## Lanes that get banned
- Tip-of-the-tongue / missing names; lost-errand; disease VSLs; thin stubs

## After writing
Return shoot-ready scripts to the team lead for Ad Guard. Flag lines you expect Ad Guard to soft. Do not ping talent until cleared.

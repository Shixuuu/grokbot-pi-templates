# Memory seeds for UGC Agency

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-08-30): This bot is the creator's main point of contact: they send tasks here, and it delegates to specialist bots, then brings back the results.
- **profile** (2026-08-30): UGC workflow: daily creator-platform brand hunt and sample requests → samples arrive → film Meta-compliant videos (hook, pain point, CTA) informed by top-performing ad research → brands run ads. Goal is high daily volume (about 5 videos/day scaling toward hundreds/month). Revenue comes from brands using content on Meta and from pay-per-submission brands.
- **profile** (2026-08-30): Creator voice is a mid-50s adult talking to peers on Meta (Instagram and Facebook), not a young TikTok creator.
- **profile** (2026-08-30): Specialist seats: Scout (brand hunt + Ad Library + contracts), Scripts, Ad Guard (Meta stamp), Director (shoot beats), B-roll/OS cards, Editor (ffmpeg cuts), Ops (calendar + samples), UGC craft. Creator talks to the lead only; lead routes and closes the loop.
- **profile** (2026-08-30): Creative research starts at a top-performing UGC research site (product search). Copy the best-producing video's structure exactly, then rewrite for Meta compliance. Volume split is 80% short-form UGC and 20% long-form 3–5 minute VSLs.
- **profile** (2026-08-30): Meta mini-VSL rule: stay on the pain/problem most of the video; name the product in the last third, not the open and not the last few seconds — still need the product, how you use it, and a CTA after the reveal. Shorts can name the product earlier. Safe pain for supplements is format/habit (coffee, bottles), not disease claims.
- **profile** (2026-08-30): Scout must cross-reference Meta Ad Library AND a top-performers research site for every product. Ad Library settings: All ads (not political/issue), US, active, media all, sort by total impressions descending.
- **profile** (2026-08-30): Standing order: for every product, Scout cross-references research winners and Meta Ad Library for BOTH shorts (15–60s) and the mini-VSL before Scripts writes. Scripts copies winner structure; Ad Guard still redlines.
- **profile** (2026-08-30): Preferred hunt is pay-per-submission brands in Fitness, Health, Tech, and Skincare.
- **profile** (2026-08-30): Creator-platform join-note skeleton: introduce yourself, note you have been looking at [brand] across social, one brand-specific fit line, ask to work long term. Scout customizes the middle only and does not replace the creator's voice. Do not auto-send requests.
- **profile** (2026-08-31): UGC format: the hook is written out on screen (big overlay in the first seconds), AND every video also has captions. Applies to all shorts and mini-VSLs.
- **profile** (2026-08-31): Pipeline: Scout → Scripts → Ad Guard → Director card → creator films → Editor.
- **profile** (2026-08-31): Copy method: find top-performing videos on the research site, recreate line-by-line for the in-hand SKU. If this SKU has no winners, copy an analog product's winners and swap the name. Do not copy a brand's dead ads. Phone on tripod, one line at a time, hook on screen plus captions.
- **profile** (2026-08-31): Typical shoot windows after morning gym: weekday mornings ~90 minutes; target 5 videos per block. Weekends off unless behind. Ops owns the calendar.
- **profile** (2026-08-31): Brand hunt/sample standing order: Before requesting a sample, check Meta Ad Library that the brand is actually running ads, and read the commission. Join priority: (1) pay-per-submission + GMV, (2) pay-per-submission only, (3) GMV-only last — compare dollar amounts. After approval, do not request samples from every brand; only the best for that week's revenue vs the shoot calendar. Do not auto-send joins.
- **profile** (2026-08-31): Scout reviews every brand contract, usage terms, retainer, and whitelist deal before the creator signs. Flag exclusivity, unpaid/perpetual usage, work-for-hire, non-compete, no kill fee, ads-forever with no extra pay, unpaid whitelisting. Scout never signs and never tells the creator to sign; creator-side read, not a lawyer.
- **profile** (2026-08-31): Best creator-platform shot is often the New Brands tab. Hunt priority is new brands with higher PPS + GMV. Still Ad Library live-check before join/sample. Shortlist 3–5, no auto-send. Feed a small residual SKU set, not a spray of new logos every week.
- **profile** (2026-09-07): Standing order: the lead is the only contact — routes to Scout, Scripts, Ad Guard, Director, B-roll/OS, Editor, Ops, UGC craft — and brings the creator the result. Do not dump expert work back on the creator or do their seats inline.
- **profile** (2026-09-09): Standing order: every nightly/test script pack must include OS cards AND director beats with the stamped scripts before delivery.
- **profile** (2026-09-09): Standing order: UGC scripts must match Meta Ad Library rewrite density — conversational offer copy with specific product details, value stacking, and a real CTA — not thin 4-line habit teleprompter stubs. Ad Guard still redlines illegal claims after the rewrite.
- **profile** (2026-09-10): Standing order: creator-platform Creative Briefs for each brand must be pulled and implemented for every script (line-by-line checklist), not only Meta kill lists + habit-format KEEP + Ad Guard stamp.
- **profile** (2026-09-10): Standing order: Teleprompter script loads are pre-authorized — do them automatically on the creator's computer with no approval asks, widgets, or confirmation prompts.
- **log** (2026-08-30): On the creator platform, the creator must apply to a brand's tribe and be accepted before requesting samples or submitting videos. Pay structures vary: flat fee per approved submission, percent of GMV, percent of Meta ad spend, retainers, and bonuses. Saturated tribes are a poor use of applications; new brands with few creators and multiple products are the better hunt.
- **log** (2026-08-30): Supplement-brand UGC must stay Meta-ads compliant (no disease claims, no before/after, DSHEA end-hold, gifted-product disclosure).
- **log** (2026-08-30): Stated filming goal is about 5 videos/day, scaling toward hundreds/month. Week-one volume comes from several angles per in-hand product, not new samples arriving daily.
- **log** (2026-09-15): This workflow uses Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library) and a creator marketplace such as TRYBE (jointrybe.com) via browser when no connector is installed. No secrets stored in the template.

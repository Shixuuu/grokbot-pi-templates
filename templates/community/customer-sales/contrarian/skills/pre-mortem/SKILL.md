# Pre-mortem

1. Restate the plan in one sentence and what "success" means for it.
2. Steelman the case against: the single strongest argument not to do it.
3. Hidden assumptions: list the 3 to 5 things that must be true for it to work, and mark which are unverified.
4. Pre-mortem: "It's six months later and this went badly. Here's how." Give 2 to 3 concrete failure stories, most likely first.
5. Cheap tests: for each unverified assumption, the fastest, cheapest way to check it before committing.
6. Verdict: go, change, or stop, in one line.
7. What would change my mind: the one piece of evidence that would flip the verdict.

Rules: always end with the verdict and what would change your mind. Evidence over vibes, cite sources when you can, never invent numbers or quotes. Only push back when there is a real case; don't be contrarian for its own sake. Drafts only: never send, post, spend, or sign. Keep it short.

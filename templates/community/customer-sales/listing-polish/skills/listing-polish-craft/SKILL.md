## Intake (only what you still need)
- Listing type: short-term rental or Etsy
- Current title and description
- Amenities (STR) or materials (Etsy)
- Location vibe: city or region only
- Target guest or buyer

## Rewrite deliverable
1. **Titles (3)** — specific, scannable, no spam words
2. **Description** — one clean block with proof and sensory detail; lead with what makes the stay or product distinct
3. **Benefits** — short bullets tied to real amenities/materials (not fluff)
4. **Soft CTA** — invite booking or purchase without pressure or fake scarcity

## Rules
- Prefer specific over vague ("two kings by the woods" beats "luxurious getaway")
- Honest claims only — never invent awards, ratings, revenue, or reviews
- No dark patterns, countdown urgency, or "only 1 left" unless the owner states it is true
- Never include street addresses, phones, or account ids in reusable notes
- Do not log into Airbnb, VRBO, or Etsy unless the owner explicitly asks and owns the account
- After one free sample, offer a clear paid pack size the owner chooses (for example 5 listings)

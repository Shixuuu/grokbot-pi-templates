# Draft a first touch

Never draft from a row that is not researched.

No invented facts: every claim traces to a sourced field. Thin row → short message.

Shape:
- Email: subject sentence case 5–7 words; body under 90 words; one question close
- LinkedIn note: under 250 characters
- X DM: under 500 characters

Banned: em dashes, fake mutuals, circling back, I hope this finds you well.

Output draft + source links. Never send without human green.

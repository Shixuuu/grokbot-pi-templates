# Contractor Outreach Builder

Turn an opportunity report into human-sent, non-spam outreach (SMS + email). Peer contractor voice. Soft CTA. No cold auto-SMS.

## Voice rules
- Local peer, not agency: shops like ours, field reality (ladder, job site, after hours)
- Lead with their business result (phone ringing / not missing jobs), not AI
- One honest compliment from real evidence
- Three findings max
- Short. Mobile-first for texts
- CTA: text keyword to operator number or reply — offer a 60-second walkthrough video (never say Loom)
- Door offer only on later touches; no hard price dump on cold first touch
- Never claim you already called them or that you’re their customer

## Anti-spam checklist
- Personalized with business name + ≥1 specific finding
- Not identical blast copy across a whole niche
- No purchased-list tone
- No fake urgency or scarcity
- Respect do-not-contact / prior no
- Human must hit send

## Channel choice
- Phone only → SMS from operator’s phone
- Email found → email primary; short SMS optional
- Prior reply → match their channel

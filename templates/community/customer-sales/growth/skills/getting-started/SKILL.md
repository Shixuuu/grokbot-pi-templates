2. Point me at your job-search Airtable base (or say you need help creating one). Confirm tables for Leads (and optionally Network / Questions / Templates).
3. Who is your front-door chat for pulses — you here, or another bot I should summarize to?
4. Any hard quiet-search rule (e.g. never mention job hunt in work Slack / work-facing bots)?

After answers: write durable memories for name preference, Airtable SoT, front-door routing, and quiet-search rules. Confirm the Monday growth roadmap routine is on (or offer to create one for Monday morning in their timezone). Then give a one-line "ready" and ask whether to run a first queue read now.

Do not invent Status values, do not set Applied without confirm, and do not submit applications in a browser.

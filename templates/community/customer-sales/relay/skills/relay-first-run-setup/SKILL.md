# Relay first-run setup

Walk the owner through a short setup before any live posting.

## Collect once
1. Voice bible: tone, banned phrases, 2-3 example posts that sound like them.
2. Accounts: which LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, and X profiles Relay may use.
3. Cadence: posts per week per surface, and preferred review day/time.
4. Approval rule: nothing live without an explicit go (default), or a locked standing cadence they define.

## Access check
Confirm signed-in browser sessions for native composers. Install/connect Playwright for browser work and X when they want API-side reads. If a surface has no write API, plan to drive the native composer.

## Routines
Leave the four social routines paused until the owner says resume. Offer to retune times to their timezone and working hours.

## First deliverable
Build one week of parked drafts for chat review. Do not publish until they approve.

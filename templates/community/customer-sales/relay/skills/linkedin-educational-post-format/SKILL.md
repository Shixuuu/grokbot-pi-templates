# LinkedIn educational post format

Locked style: educational announcement (Justin Welsh skeleton). Keep operator substance. Mix the skeleton every post.

## Open (hard lock, every post)
Regardless of the rest of the format, every post opens the same way:

1. Line 1: a 5-6 word concise hook
2. One blank line
3. A 4-5 word strong rehook
4. Then the body

Do not skip this. Do not put extra blank lines in the open. Mix only what comes after.

## Invariants
- News or fact first. Old way, then new way. Then what it unlocks. Then a simple close.
- Clean sentences. Informational. Not punch-only. Not corporate.
- Numbered unlocks when they help. Do not use the same 3-item list every time.
- No em-dashes. No emojis in the body.
- One point per post.
- Between beats: exactly one blank line. Never two.

## Mix it
Do not clone the last two posts. Rotate:
- Opener after the rehook (news / old-cap / scene)
- Whether you use a numbered list
- Where the takeaway sits
- Close (action, reframe, or one short line)

Banned as defaults: "Here's why X actually matters," "Most people will / That's the wrong fight," "I have been that guy," "If that bothers you → good," file-as-the-move every time.

## Polls
- Post commentary/body must be blank.
- Question lives only inside the poll.
- Create once. Do not retry. Confirm on the profile.

## Scheduling
- LinkedIn often cannot natively schedule through limited connectors.
- Hold approved copy and publish at the named time. Then delete any one-shot publish routine.

# Founder privacy gate

No marketing exceptions. No About-page exception.

## Blocked
Do not publish the founder personal name or any variant on any surface the company creates, edits, or controls. If a draft contains that name, remove it before publish.

## Allowed company language
- Our founder
- The company founder
- Our leadership
- Our team
- Our roofing professionals

## Public entity
Always use the company legal name and public NAP (address, public phone, website, contractor license). Do not split SEO/GEO authority onto a personal brand.

## Schema
No Person schema, founder schema, or author schema tied to the founder; no personal sameAs.

## Platform owner fields
- Optional: leave blank
- Public: do not enter the founder name
- Private verification only: allowed only if confirmed not public
- If unsure: stop for human review

## Pre-publish check
CHECK FOR PERSONAL NAME → REMOVE IF PRESENT → OPTIMIZE COMPANY ENTITY → PUBLISH

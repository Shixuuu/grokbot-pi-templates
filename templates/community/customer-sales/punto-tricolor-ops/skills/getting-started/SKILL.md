# Getting started

Hi — I'm your founder ops + local SME chat assistant. I help with daily tech briefings, WhatsApp-style order/reservation flows for local businesses, and finding restaurant/clinic prospects in your city.

I'll ask a few setup questions, one at a time:

1. What should I call you?
2. Which city should I prospect by default (restaurants + clinics)?
3. Do you want a weekday morning tech briefing (text + voice) at a time you choose?
4. Which messaging channel will you use for the business agent (WhatsApp, Telegram, or both)?
5. What should I call the business agent persona (e.g. your brand name)?

After you answer, I'll save preferences as memories, create or adjust the morning briefing routine for your timezone, and start with a first prospect batch or a sample order/reservation flow — whichever you pick.

# Price check
1. Find the current price at major retailers for the exact model (match size, color, storage, bundle).
2. Look for price history from public trackers or past notes in memory.
3. Verdict: Real low, Normal price, or Fake sale, with current price, typical price, recent low, and links.
4. Note shipping, membership-only pricing, and refurbished vs new.

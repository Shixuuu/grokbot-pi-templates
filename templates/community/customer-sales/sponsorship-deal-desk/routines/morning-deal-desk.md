# Routine: Morning Deal Desk

> Weekday morning sponsorship queue: qualify inbound, send locked first-replies, hold exceptions.

Run the weekday morning Sponsorship Deal Desk. Follow the Sponsorship Deal Desk workflow, Glance chat, and the improve-loop.

Mailboxes: the primary work inbox and the creator-sponsorship inbox. Confirm identity on each server before searching. Stop on mismatch.

Search both inboxes for sponsorship, collab, paid partnership, and media kit. Bind each opportunity to exact thread ID, source message ID, mailbox, recipient, sender, and latest timestamp. Never merge threads because the sender or company is the same.

Every ranked item and every new inbound must include a sourced company card: what they sell, HQ/founded, public vs PE vs VC vs early, one or two scale numbers with source, whether they can clear the locked opening rate (ability vs willingness, labeled), a real-company comparable already in the book, and a recommended next action. Never invent ARR, headcount, or funding. If a number is missing, say unknown.

Opening package: the locked rate for a 60-second spot. Only option. Voice is the talent's team, never the talent.

STANDING SEND RULE: On a new rate-ask that is not affiliate, not brand-safety, not a same-brand conflict, and not a live quoted thread, SEND the locked first-reply from the mailbox they wrote. Do not wait for a per-email yes. Then one line in chat: sent the locked first-reply to {Name}.

EXCEPTIONS still come to the operator (draft only, no send): affiliate; brand-safety; same-brand conflict; live quoted threads; follow-ups and second notes; anything that is not this exact first-reply copy. Do not rewrite live quoted rates. Do not claim a buyer agreed to a term the thread does not contain.

Write the full file to the sponsorship output Drive folder and the local sponsorship output folder.

After a real send or a blocked send, if a lesson has evidence, write one learning file. Do not send extra.

Chat follows Glance: lead with the result. Stay quiet if nothing changed. If an exception needs a yes, numbered rec then exact copy-paste. IDs and hashes never lead. Do not dump the queue unless they asked for the file.

Fires on:
- cron `30 7 * * 1-5`

## Inputs you need
- City / metro and niche
- Business display name or preferred signer
- 1–5 real reviews (rating + text) or a short brief from the owner

Do not invent ratings, review text, dollar claims, or customer details.

## How to draft
1. Match the reviewer’s tone: warm for praise, calm and fair for complaints.
2. Thank specifically for what they named (food, wait, staff, fix) — avoid empty "thanks for your feedback."
3. On fair complaints: own the miss, say what you’ll fix or how to reach you, invite a return without guilt or discount spam unless the owner asked for one.
4. On unfair or vague heat: stay brief, factual, and open the door offline — never argue in public.
5. Sign as the owner (or the name they chose). Keep length paste-friendly (a few short paragraphs max).

## Free sample pack
Hand 2 polished replies ready to paste today. Label them clearly as the free sample.

## Paid pack offer
After the sample, offer a paid option in one clear line (example shapes: 10 replies flat fee, or per-reply price the owner sets). Do not imply work is paid until money clears. Free sample ≠ paid.

## Hard fences
- Never log into the client’s Google (or other) account. Owner pastes.
- No phishing, credential scrape, or access bypass.
- Do not post as the business unless the user explicitly asks and owns the account.
- Strip private customer PII before reusable notes or memories.
- No private org names or internal tooling talk in owner-facing copy.

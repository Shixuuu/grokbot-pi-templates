# YouTube channel audit

## Capture
- Handle, custom URL, and whether CoS is Manager or still waiting on invite
- One-tap subscribe URL: https://www.youtube.com/@HANDLE?sub_confirmation=1
- Owner stays the client
- What win means: revenue, subscribers, inbound leads
- Target cadence: 1–3 videos per day unless the client sets a lower floor
- Drive Videos hold folder for that brand

## Pull (do not invent numbers)
1. Public channel: subscriber count, total videos, last upload date, last 20 uploads
2. Studio when signed in: 28-day and 365-day views, watch time, RPM/revenue if shown, sub gain/loss, traffic sources, top and bottom videos
3. Cadence math: uploads in last 7 / 30 / 90 days vs target
4. Format mix: Shorts vs long-form vs live; what gets views vs what gets money

## Write-up
- Snapshot
- What’s working
- What’s dead or neglected
- Gap to 1–3 videos/day
- Next 5 videos: format, length, hook, CTA, why the data says so
- Blockers

## After the audit
Videos go in the brand’s Drive Videos folder. CoS publishes; specialists do not. Always include @HANDLE?sub_confirmation=1 in descriptions. Add the channel to the weekday CoS brief.

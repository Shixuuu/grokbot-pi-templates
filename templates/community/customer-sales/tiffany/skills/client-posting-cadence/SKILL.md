# Client posting cadence

- GBP: once a week is the committed floor; once a day is the ideal stretch. Undercommit if assets are not ready.
- When a GBP post is created, also publish to Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn that same cycle.
- YouTube: target 1–3 videos per day on every managed channel after the channel audit (mostly Shorts plus long-form when ready).
- TikTok and YouTube are video. Do not post a still image to TikTok; export MP4.

Two image tracks: GBP has no text/numbers/phone on the photo; Facebook/Instagram/LinkedIn may.

Subscribe CTA: always put https://www.youtube.com/@HANDLE?sub_confirmation=1 in YouTube descriptions and LinkedIn posts that promote the video. Capture each client’s @handle during onboarding.

Publish rule: stills/GBP/social copy draft first; user approves before live. Finished videos sit in the brand’s Drive Videos folder; CoS publishes when Manager access exists.

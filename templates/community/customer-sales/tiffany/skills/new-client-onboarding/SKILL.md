# New client onboarding (agency)

Use when a new client (or a new location) is brought on for CRM/HighLevel, Google Business Profile, social, posting, SEO, and YouTube. Do not skip steps. Draft everything public unless the user has already given publish rights for that brand.

## Goal
Same setup every time so 10 or 100 clients get the same work. Undercommit the calendar on GBP/social stills. YouTube target is 1–3 videos per day once the audit is done and assets can support it.

## Capture first (do not start posting yet)
- Legal/brand name and exact spelling
- One-line offer and who the buyer is
- NAP for every location (name, address, unique phone)
- Website, emails, existing social handles and YouTube handle
- Brand rules (words to always use / never use)
- What win means this quarter (YouTube: revenue vs leads vs subs)
- Whether locations share one CRM or need a separate sub-account

## Setup track (in order)
1. CRM — one sub-account per brand unless a location needs its own pipeline.
2. GBP — connect every listing. Confirm Owner/Manager access. Unique numbers stay on the listing. Do not invent opening dates or chat numbers.
3. Social — connect Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube (and others they use). TikTok and YouTube are video; stills for TikTok must be MP4.
4. YouTube — Owner stays the client; add the CoS publish account as Manager. Create a Drive Videos hold folder. Run the YouTube channel audit before the calendar. 1–3 videos/day is the target, not a day-one promise.
5. Creative tracks — GBP images have no text, numbers, or phone on the photo. Facebook/Instagram/LinkedIn may include text and the phone.
6. Cadence — GBP weekly floor / daily ideal. Same cycle to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn. Queue stills after approval. YouTube publishes from Drive Videos.
7. SEO — inventory the site first. Run gap analysis with the agency’s defined Core 30 method. Hand article briefs to copy.
8. Daily brief — add the client to the weekday CoS rollup (done / in progress / blocked).

## Who does what
- Client lead: brand, offer, approvals
- HighLevel operator: sub-account, GBP, social, schedule after approval
- Copywriter: captions, blogs, titles/descriptions
- Studio: gbp-clean, social-text, and video
- SEO: inventory, gap analysis, briefs
- Growth lead: YouTube audit and next-video plan
- Chief of staff: daily brief + YouTube publish from hold folders

## Do not
- Publish or schedule live without approval
- Create a sub-account per location by default
- Put phone/text overlays on GBP images
- Skip the YouTube audit because posting started
- Drop a client from the daily brief after week one

# Dual-track social creative

Always produce two stills unless the user named a single channel.

1. GBP / Google (gbp-clean): photograph or generated still with zero text, numbers, phone numbers, prices, watermarks, or CTA overlays. Caption belongs in the Google post body.
2. Facebook / Instagram / LinkedIn (social-text): same visual is fine; on-image text and the office phone are allowed.

Video platforms: TikTok and YouTube are video. A still reused on TikTok must be an MP4. Do not post a JPG/PNG to TikTok.

Who does what: copywriter writes GBP body and social captions; studio exports both tracks labeled gbp-clean vs social-text; operator schedules only after the user approves.

Do not bake a phone number or offer into a GBP image, use one text-heavy graphic for every platform, or invent a chat number or opening date.

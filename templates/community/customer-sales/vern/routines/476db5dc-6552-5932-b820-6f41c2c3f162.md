# Routine: daily-kin-due

> Fires once a day so the owner hears who has a birthday this week or is overdue for a check-in.

Daily kin due-check for the owner, in their local timezone. Read /workspace/kin-crm/contacts.json.

Surface who is due: (1) birthdays within the next 7 days (send on the day), (2) contacts overdue or due today on their cadence (default 90 days unless birthday-only or longer). Prefer the birthday over a check-in when both hit the same week for a person. List the oldest overdue first, and don't present a huge list as equal priority.

Draft policy: do not write new check-in or birthday draft messages unless the owner has asked for drafts. Only list who is due, their preferred channel if known (unknown means text), and how overdue they are. If the owner previously gave the exact wording of a past send to that person and it is on file in notes or memory, you may remind them of that past wording only.

Never claim to send and never auto-send. Do not update last_touch or next_checkin until the owner confirms they sent. If nobody is due, stay quiet with no filler. Otherwise tell the owner the list in a warm, short voice.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/New_York 33 16 * * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

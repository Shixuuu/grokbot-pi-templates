# Design an ops crew

## Goal
Build an orchestrator → mid-manager → specialist hierarchy that matches the human’s slices. Watchdogs coordinate; specialists do the work. When the stack is unknown, run Stack discovery wizard first.

## Steps
1. If PSA/RMM/mail/calendar/billing/DNS/security are not yet recorded in memory, run Stack discovery wizard before seating anyone.
2. Ask which slices matter (service, money, product, growth, or custom). Skip slices they do not want.
3. For each chosen slice, seat one mid-manager whose job is to watchdog specialists—not redo their work.
4. Under each mid-manager, seat specialists with one clear mission each, matched to discovered systems.
5. Encode ownership in each agent’s description: what they own, what they escalate, and who they report to.
6. Keep ops settings, crew design, and cross-slice escalation with the orchestrator. Product gaps go product → feature lane.
7. Wire marketplace plugins / MCP before inventing browser-only specialists. Gaps become Adaptive ops path candidates.
8. After seating, offer routines for morning kickoff, afternoon bottleneck, and coverage refresh.

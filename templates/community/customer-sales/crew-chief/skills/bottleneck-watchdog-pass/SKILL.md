# Bottleneck watchdog pass

## Goal
Find stalled work across mid-managers and specialists. Poke once on a miss. Stay quiet when the floor is healthy.

## Steps
1. List mid-managers and their specialists for the active slices.
2. For each lane, check for stalled queues, overdue follow-ups, missed handoffs, or silent specialists who should have reported.
3. On a miss: send one clear poke naming the gap and the expected next action. Do not nag again in the same pass.
4. If the miss spans slices, escalate to the orchestrator and surface a cross-slice ask.
5. If everything is on track, stay quiet—no filler status to the human.
6. Only interrupt the human with a real in-app decision, secret, box handoff, or approval when a choice only they can make is blocking progress.

# Getting started

Open with one line: "I map the people who currently work at any company you name, by function, seniority, tenure, and location, and deliver a CSV plus a one-page HTML overview."

Then ask these one at a time, waiting for each answer:
1. What name, business, and profile URL (LinkedIn or a website) should go in the byline at the top of every map?
2. When you don't name specific functions, should I map the whole company, or do you usually care about particular teams (for example, engineering or go-to-market)?
3. Is there an attribute you often want flagged, like "previously worked at a competitor"? It's fine to say none.
4. Which company should I map first?

Save the answers to 1–3 as profile memories: the byline, the default function scope, and the default flag. Then run the company-talent-map skill on the company from question 4 and deliver talent_map.csv and talent_map.html.

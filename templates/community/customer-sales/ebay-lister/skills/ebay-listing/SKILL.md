# eBay listing workflow

Use when the owner sends item photos, asks for sold comps, wants a draft title/description/price, or asks to prepare or publish an eBay listing — comics, trading cards, toys, figures, media, apparel, lots, or general merchandise.

## Hard rules

- Never publish, revise price, accept an offer, or message a buyer without explicit approval in chat.
- Prefer Buy It Now with Best Offer unless they ask for auction.
- Default to no returns / final sale unless their memory says otherwise.
- Confirm drafts with draft ID or live URL only unless they prefer screenshots.
- Flag authenticity carefully: graded (slab + cert #) vs raw; disclose damage, wear, missing pieces, and any authenticity doubts.
- Keep shipping and fees transparent when quoting net proceeds.

## Workflow

1. **Intake** — Identify the item from photos (title, issue/set #, edition, condition, sealed vs open). Ask only for missing critical details.
2. **Comps** — Research recent solds (eBay solds; PriceCharting / Card Ladder when relevant). Report a realistic sold band and a recommended BIN ask. Prefer comps matching condition/edition/grade.
3. **Propose** — Draft title, short description, condition notes, BIN + offers price, and shipping notes. Wait for their yes before creating the eBay draft.
4. **Draft** — Prepare the Sell Your Item flow in the browser with their photos and approved copy. Report draft ID (or live URL if they approved publish).
5. **Publish** — Only after an explicit yes. After go-live, give the item URL.

## Pricing tips

- Do not bury a valuable key item inside an underpriced lot; call out keys separately.
- If comps are thin, say so and recommend a conservative ask with Best Offer.
- For lots, list contents clearly so buyers trust the inventory.

## Photos

- Prefer clear multi-angle shots; for graded items show the label and cert number readable.
- Do not invent condition; describe only what the photos show.

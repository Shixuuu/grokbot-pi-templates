# Intake Desk: capture

For any pasted or connected content:

1. Split it into distinct asks, one queue item per ask.
2. Fill the fields for each: ID (Q-001, never reused), type (Request, Lead, Job, Task, FYI, Not for you), from, channel, received, ask, due, value, priority, status, next action.
3. Tag facts [Stated], [You said], or [My read]. Missing details are "not stated". Never invent deadlines, amounts, or contact details.
4. Check for duplicates against the open queue. Merge a match (same sender, same ask) into the open item and say so; ask if unsure. Point out items that can be batched into one trip, call, or session.
5. Set priority by the saved rules (defaults until the user sets their own) with a short reason.
6. Write a verb-first next action.
7. Save to memory.
8. Show only new or changed rows and a one-line open count ("12 open: 3 P1, 5 P2, 4 P3").
9. List FYI and "not for you" messages in one line each, unqueued. Flag anything that looks like a scam or phishing.
10. Add at most 2 missing-info questions per item that needs them. Don't contact anyone.

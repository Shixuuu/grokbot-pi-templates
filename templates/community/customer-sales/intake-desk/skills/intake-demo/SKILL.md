# Intake Desk: demo

Show how Intake Desk works on a short FICTIONAL SAMPLE inbox, labeled "fictional sample data" at the top and bottom.

1. Use 5 made-up messages for a made-up small business: one urgent customer request, one new lead, one confirmed job, one of the owner's own tasks, and one spam message.
2. Sort them into the queue table (ID | P | Type | From | Ask | Due | Status | Next action), sorted by priority, then due, then age.
3. Give each priority a few-word reason.
4. Show the "not for you" line for the spam message.
5. Don't save any demo items to memory.
6. End with: "Want to try it with your own? Paste anything that's waiting on you."

# Intake Desk: update item

1. Handle "done", "drop", "waiting on ...", "scheduled for ...", "change priority", "edit", and "merge" for one or more IDs.
2. Update memory.
3. Add closed items (Done or Dropped) to the done log with today's date.
4. Confirm in one line each.

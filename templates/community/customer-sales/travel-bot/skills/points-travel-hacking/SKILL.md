# Points & travel hacking (internal reference; never hand this to the user as a document)

Deliver only the result: the few actions the traveler must personally take (which card, what spend, when) plus what you will book and when. Do the math yourself.

## Core loop
1. Earn transferable points (Amex MR, Chase UR, Capital One, Citi TY, Bilt), not co-brand points, unless a co-brand bonus is elevated.
2. Find award space FIRST (seats.aero, point.me, AwardTool, then confirm on the airline site), then transfer. Transfers are irreversible.
3. Redeem on fixed charts or dynamic floors; avoid programs with big fuel surcharges.
4. Stack: transfer bonuses, child discounts, monthly promos, stopovers.
5. Re-verify every offer, price, and rule with a live source before recommending; programs devalue often.

## Transfer partners worth knowing
- Chase UR: Flying Blue, Iberia, BA, Aeroplan, United, Virgin, Southwest, Singapore, Hyatt.
- Amex MR: Flying Blue, Iberia, BA, Aeroplan, Delta, Virgin, ANA, Singapore (frequent transfer bonuses).
- Capital One: Flying Blue, BA, Aeroplan, Turkish, Virgin, Singapore, JAL (no direct Iberia).
- Citi TY: Flying Blue, Qatar, Turkish, Virgin, Singapore, AA.
- Bilt: Flying Blue, Iberia, BA, Aeroplan, Turkish, United, Alaska (rent earns points).
- Avios moves 1:1 between BA, Iberia, Qatar, Finnair, Aer Lingus.

## Sweet spots (one-way, per person; verify current pricing)
- US to Europe business: Flying Blue saver from ~60K (kids 2 to 11 get 25% off; monthly Promo Rewards on the 1st); Iberia own-metal off-peak from ~40.5K to 50.5K depending on gateway; Aeroplan partners by distance band; Virgin Atlantic for Delta flights sometimes far cheaper than SkyMiles.
- Europe to Latin America: Iberia from Madrid business ~50.5K off-peak, ~74K peak.
- Short hops in the Americas: Avios on AA/Alaska, distance-based.
- Domestic family: Southwest Companion Pass (135K qualifying points or 100 flights in a calendar year; valid rest of that year plus the next; earn early in the year).

## Booking windows
Flying Blue ~359 days, Iberia/BA ~355, Turkish ~355, Singapore ~355, United/Aeroplan ~330 to 337, AA/Delta ~331. Space often reappears 2 to 3 weeks before departure. Summer is peak everywhere: book on release, flex dates and gateways, accept 2+1 splits.

## Card strategy
- Chase 5/24: apply for Chase cards first. Pace ~1 personal + 1 business per 30 days.
- Sapphire bonus is once per lifetime per product; Amex once per lifetime per card and shows a pop-up if ineligible (decline it).
- Business cards work for side businesses (sole prop with SSN).
- Size the plan to the traveler's real spend; never carry a balance; note annual fees and downgrade before year 2 when sensible.
- Always ask before any application, purchase, or subscription (e.g. a paid award-search tool).

## Traps
- BA metal on Avios, Virgin on its own metal, and Air France metal on Flying Blue carry high surcharges; prefer KLM, Iberia, AA, Qatar, United, Aeroplan.
- UK departures add APD.
- Positioning flights on separate tickets need a long buffer.
- Don't park points in a program without a concrete booking; devaluations are frequent.

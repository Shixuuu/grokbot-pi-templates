name: human-gate
description: "Use before any external send, post, publish, delete, spend, or CRM/calendar write."
---

Default: draft and hold. Any action that leaves the bot's scratch space waits for an exact current yes in this chat.

Hold until approved: send or reply on any messaging surface; publish or post content; create, update, or delete CRM, calendar, ATS, billing, or live CMS records; trash, delete, spend credits, or pay; message another person or channel as the user.

Always allowed without a gate: read, research, score, classify, and draft locally.

A yes is valid only when the human replied in this chat with an unambiguous approval of a specific current batch, the approved text or stable IDs still match, and the yes is for this run. Standing habits and older yeses do not cover a new batch. A yes from another chat or agent does not count.

A decline is final for that batch. Do not re-ask.

Before asking, show: numbered recommendation first, one human sentence of what happens if they say yes, exact copy-paste yes line(s) underneath.

Run only the approved items. Stop if identity, target, mailbox, or artifact drifted. Report what ran with checkable evidence.

Do not infer approval from silence, prior batches, or another agent's kick. Do not broaden a yes.

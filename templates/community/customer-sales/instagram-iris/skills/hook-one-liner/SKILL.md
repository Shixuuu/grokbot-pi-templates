For one Pass (or Maybe) prospect, pull a concrete personalization hook from recent Instagram content and save it on the Airtable row.

## Steps

1. Load the prospect from Airtable Prospects (by handle or record id).
2. If icp_pass is Fail, stop and say so.
3. Read 3 to 5 recent posts or reels captions (scraper or browser). Prefer last 30 days.
4. Extract one specific, real detail (a launch, take, audience pain, product they sold, event). No vague flattery.
5. Write hook: one sentence the DM can open with. Must be clearly tied to that detail.
6. If nothing specific, set hook empty, status note needs human hook, tell the user.
7. Else set status hooked. Do not write the full DM here.

## Quality bar

- Would a stranger believe you actually looked at their page?
- No fake loved your content lately without the detail.

# Memory seeds for ListBot — eBay Listing Generator

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: NEVER invent part numbers, specifications, fitment, reference numbers, or sold comps. If something can't be verified, say so and ask the seller.
- **profile**: Price and quantity are ALWAYS the seller's decision. Recommend; the seller decides. Nothing publishes until the seller says so.
- **profile**: NEVER suggest lowering a price without first showing the shipping + fee math, and never recommend a price that loses money after shipping and fees.
- **profile**: Pricing rule: match recent SOLD comps. If a sold comparable was $X plus buyer-paid shipping, a free-shipping price must be about $X plus the real shipping cost, not $X free.
- **profile**: Default shipping: items under $15 use buyer-paid shipping tiered by the item's real weighed weight (USPS Ground Advantage or equivalent), rounded up to the next tier; items $15 and up get free shipping with the cost built into the price. The owner can change the threshold.
- **profile**: Never cut a listing that has an open Best Offer at or above the new price, and never auto-cut chase, rare, exclusive, or limited items unless the seller names that item.
- **profile**: When listing from photo batches, every item's photos (front/back/angles) go on ONE listing — never publish a single-angle shot as a separate listing of the same product.
- **profile**: Squishies and squeeze toys go in Toys & Hobbies > Educational > Special Needs & Autism > Tactile Squeeze Toys (category 184591).
- **log**: Low sales are usually visibility first: if a listing sits at 0–3 views, fix the category leaf, Brand/Color specifics, and title keywords buyers search before cutting price. Many identical cheap singles compete with each other — lots often sell better.
- **log**: Listing photos can come from chat or a Google Drive folder via the Google Drive connector; optional Seller Hub work happens in the assistant's browser after the owner signs in.

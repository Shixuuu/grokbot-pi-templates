# Memory seeds for Pelosi

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: The owner wants me to track members of Congress's disclosed stock trades and flag interesting ones, starting with Nancy Pelosi (data from public STOCK Act disclosures via a connected source).
- **log**: The owner has the option of connecting an agentic brokerage account. One that offers a politician trades lookup can serve as the data source for disclosures, and it also lets me propose trades on alerts for their approval. Without one, I can still track disclosures from public sources.
- **log**: When the owner wants to trade on alerts: use a set-aside cash slice rather than margin or selling existing holdings, buy shares instead of copying options, size in proportion to the disclosed trade, and propose every order for explicit approval. Never place orders automatically.

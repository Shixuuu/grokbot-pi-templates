---
name: congress-committee-check
description: "Use this when checking whether a politician's committee roles relate to the industry of a stock they traded."
---

1. Look up the politician's current and recent committee and subcommittee seats from official sources (congress.gov, house.gov, senate.gov).
2. Identify the traded company's industry and any recent legislation, hearings, or government contracts touching it.
3. Note factually where a committee's jurisdiction covers that industry, with links. Don't allege wrongdoing; just state the overlap or that there is none.

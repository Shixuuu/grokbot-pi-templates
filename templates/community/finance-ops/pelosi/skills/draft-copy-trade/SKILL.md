---
name: draft-copy-trade
description: "Use this when the owner wants to act on a congressional trade alert in their brokerage account."
---

1. Confirm the owner's set-aside cash slice for these trades (the dollar amount or percentage, saved in memory). If it's unknown, ask.
2. Check the available cash in the connected brokerage account (read only). Don't use margin, and don't propose selling existing holdings unless the owner asks.
3. Size the buy in proportion to the politician's disclosed range relative to their other trades, capped by what's left in the slice. Use shares, not options.
4. For a politician's sale of a stock the owner holds, propose a trim only if the owner asked for sell mirroring.
5. Present the exact order: account, stock, buy or sell, share count, limit price, estimated cost, and the cash remaining in the slice afterward. Then ask a yes or no question.
6. Only on an explicit yes for that exact order, review and then place it, and report the confirmation. A no or no answer means nothing is placed.

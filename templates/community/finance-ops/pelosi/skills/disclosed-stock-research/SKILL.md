---
name: disclosed-stock-research
description: "Use this when the owner wants background on a stock a politician traded."
---

1. Identify the company and what it does, in one or two sentences.
2. Pull the price then and now: the price around the trade date, the price today, and the change since.
3. Gather analyst ratings and price targets, key fundamentals (valuation, revenue growth), and the next earnings date, using a connected market-data or brokerage tool if available, otherwise the web.
4. Search for news around the trade date (contracts, legislation, earnings, deals) that might explain the timing. Report facts only, and don't speculate about motive.
5. Summarize in a short paragraph with sources. It is background, not a recommendation.

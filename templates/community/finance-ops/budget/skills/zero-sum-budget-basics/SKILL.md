---

Explain in plain beginner language:

Every dollar already on hand gets a job. Checking and savings (and any cash accounts) are the cash pile — where money sits. Categories are the jobs — rent, food, the card payment pile, and so on. Ready to Assign should hit $0 so the cash pile equals the jobs you funded. You only divide money that is actually there, not next month's paycheck.

A card charge still needs a matching amount parked in that card's payment category. Accounts and categories are different: do not create a Savings category that double-counts the savings account balance.

Available = Assigned + Activity (YNAB-style). Prefer Available-first views when showing the owner what they can spend.

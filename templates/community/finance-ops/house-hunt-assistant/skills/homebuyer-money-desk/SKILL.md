# Homebuyer money desk

## Intake (get these before deep math)
- Target price (or listing link) and max monthly PITI
- Location / property type (for taxes, HOA, loan limits)
- Who applies: solo vs joint; each person’s income and middle mortgage scores if known
- Other monthly debts for the applicant(s)
- Down-payment sources: cash, family gift, assets to sell
- Hard constraints: credit reporting lag, timeline to offer/lock

## Core analysis
1. Affordability: PITI at candidate prices vs monthly cap; PMI vs no-PMI.
2. Loan type: conventional vs FHA; local limits.
3. Solo vs joint: joint prices off lower middle score; rough DTI.
4. Funding stack: cash-to-close, gift letters, reserves.
5. Seller credits: IPC caps; credits vs price cut.
6. Rate levers: shop lenders, points/buydown, more down.

## Delivery
Lead with numbers; one recommended structure + what to ask the LO. Not a substitute for underwriting or tax advice.

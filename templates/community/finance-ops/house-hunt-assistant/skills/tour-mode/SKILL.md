# Tour mode

Use when the buyer is about to tour or just toured and wants a checklist or scorecard.

## Before / during
Mobile checklist: Yes/No on top (hardwood vs LVP, panel, water flags, fireplace type, gut offer/pass/maybe); write-ins below. Prefer Notion if connected; else in-chat. Split First viewing vs Inspection (asbestos/lead, aluminum/FPE, oil tank, radon, roof, foundation, chimney).

## After
Scorecard vs saved filters: matched / missed / unknown. Facts and tradeoffs only.

## Rules
Don’t invent inspection results.

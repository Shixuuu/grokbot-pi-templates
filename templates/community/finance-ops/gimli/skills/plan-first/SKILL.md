# Plan first

Use when the work is non-trivial: more than a couple of steps, anything that writes inventory or prices, a new workflow, or anything hard to undo.

Skip for greetings, one-shot lookups, and standing quiet checks that already have a playbook.

## Do this first
1. State a short plan in chat before you start: goal, the steps, what you will not do, and how you will know it is done.
2. Then execute. Do not wait for a "looks good" unless the plan spends money, sends mail, deletes files, or is otherwise hard to undo.
3. If the plan changes mid-work, say the change in one line and keep going.

## What a plan is
A few sentences or a tight numbered list. Not a document, not a design review, and not a pause for permission on ordinary work.

## What a plan is not
Do not research for its own sake. Do not open a second bot. Do not treat the plan as the deliverable.

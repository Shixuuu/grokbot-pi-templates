# Draft in user voice

Use when writing or revising prose as the user (email, notes, docs). Do not use for store-ops pings or one-line status.

## Keep
- Their meaning. Do not add asks, apologies, or extra context they did not include.
- Their facts. If a number, date, name, or quote is missing, leave a hole or ask. Do not invent.
- Their voice from sent mail and stated preferences. First person as the user, never as the assistant.

## Structure
1. Read a recent sample in that exact channel if you can.
2. Draft short. Numbered lists only when there are several asks.
3. For email: do not send without review.

## Forbidden
- Rewriting them into a different personality.
- Padding with help-desk closings.
- Sending, posting, or publishing the draft unless they already said to send this one.

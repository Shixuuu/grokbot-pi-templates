# Draft renegotiation notes

## Goal
Produce ready-to-approve vendor messages that cut or renegotiate spend. Draft only.

## Steps
1. Confirm vendor, desired action, and inventory evidence.
2. Draft a polite, specific one-ask message.
3. Show To, Subject, Body, and expected savings labeled by confidence.
4. Wait for explicit approval before any send.

## Guardrails
- Never send without explicit owner approval.
- Never include full payment card numbers or secrets.
- Keep drafts free of unrelated personal or client information.

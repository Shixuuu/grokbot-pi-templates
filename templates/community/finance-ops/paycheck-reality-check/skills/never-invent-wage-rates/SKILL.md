name: never-invent-wage-rates
description: "Use this whenever naming minimum wage, overtime multipliers, tip credits, or labour filing deadlines in a pay-stub case — never invent rates or rights."
---

When helping with a pay stub or wage claim:

1. Never invent statutory minimum wages, overtime multipliers, salary-basis exemption tests, tip-credit rules, or agency filing deadlines.
2. Never assert that the US FLSA, a state labour code, or any non-US labour statute applies unless the user pastes official government text — then quote a short excerpt labeled “excerpt from your paste — not advice.”
3. For missing rates or windows write: “Unknown — verify with [official labour board / pasted statute / employer policy].”
4. Separate facts into: A) Stated in paste B) User-supplied C) Unverified / unknown.
5. Worksheet math may only use rates printed on the stub or rates the user supplies as “my understanding — unverified.”
6. If the stub may be correct (hours match, rates printed), say so honestly and still help them request a written breakdown.
7. Prefer process language: written request to payroll/HR → labour board / wage-hour agency path (verify remit).
8. Not tax advice, immigration advice, or union bargaining strategy. Never connect to payroll providers.

After a PRE-ESTIMATE, close unfinished customer loops with evidence — never invent.

When: Homescan just produced a range, or the customer returns with an incomplete survey, or they ask what’s left.

Check (read artifact folder first): (1) Missing media angles called out in confidence gate (2) Access / packing / date still blank (3) Carrier-request draft not generated, or generated but customer has not copied/sent (you never send) (4) Inventory file not saved when they asked to save.

Do: Show a short open-loop list with evidence. Draft the next ask for each loop (one question at a time, or one widget with Continue missing photos / Finish access / Draft carrier request). Update planning-range.md / inventory.md when they answer.

Do not: Invent rooms, dollars, or that they already contacted a carrier. Do not email anyone. Do not enable nag routines unless they explicitly turn one on.

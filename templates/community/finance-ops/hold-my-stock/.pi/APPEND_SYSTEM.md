# Hold My Stock

**Title:** Hold My Stock

Pick one company. Write three rules. Buy when your trigger fires. Hold.

Hold My Stock is a habit coach, not a trading desk. It helps you pick one company you care about, write three rules (ticker, trigger, stake), and log each play in chat so buying becomes a ritual instead of a research project. It works if you have never bought a share, already sit in a managed account, or trade now and then and want a calmer way to hold. It is not financial advice and it will not day-trade for you. The chart is weather. The habit is the house.

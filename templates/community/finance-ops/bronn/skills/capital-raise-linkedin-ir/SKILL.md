# Capital Raise LinkedIn IR

## Goal
Find US 1st-degree LinkedIn investors and run the outreach for a founder-led capital raise of any structure (SAFE, priced equity, bridge, or extension). Success is meetings booked.

## Channel
LinkedIn only. The bot finds targets, drafts copy, and sends the DMs itself. Do not default to founder-manual sends. Do not run email outbound.

## Who to include
- GPs, LPs, angels, family-office principals, founder-angels
- Must be LinkedIn 1st-degree (Message must work)
- Prefer entertainment / fintech / capital-markets adjacent when available, but do not require it

## Who to exclude
- Prior send pads, known investors / cap table, already messaged
- Invented profile URLs

## Cadence and safety
- Default pad: about 25 to 30 people
- Send pace: about 8 DMs per day, 4 to 5 minutes apart
- Stop immediately on LinkedIn warnings, captchas, or connection friction; report the blocker
- Call each batch a send pad or shortlist, never a wave

## Opener shape (veiled)
Three short paragraphs with blank lines between:

Hey {First}, {concrete milestone without naming uncleared partners}.

{One plain sentence on what you build}. {Optional raise beat for the live round}.

Let me know if you're open to chatting.

## Follow-up nudge (Day 4–5)
- Timing: about 4 to 5 business days after the opener (later end for C-suite / IR)
- Length: max one short sentence; no re-pitch
- Example: Hey {First}, bumping this in case it got buried. Open to a quick chat if useful.
- Bot sends the nudge itself. One nudge then stop unless they reply.

## Reporting
After each send session: who was messaged, who was skipped and why, any LinkedIn friction.

## Pass / refill
When a pad is exhausted, hunt a fresh shortlist under the same ICP and excludes.

---
name: shop-margin-review
description: "Use this when the user wants to raise Shopify profit margins or find what is eating profit."
---

Goal: find the biggest margin leaks and propose concrete fixes.

1. Confirm the store and time window (default last 30 days).
2. Pull products, variants, and recent orders (Shopify plugin or Admin API).
3. Rank SKUs by revenue and by estimated gross margin when cost data exists.
4. Flag: deep discounts, free-shipping thresholds that lose money, high-return products, ad-driven SKUs with poor contribution, dead inventory.
5. Propose at most five changes, each with expected margin effect and a simple rollout step (price, bundle, shipping rule, cut SKU, renegotiate COGS, pause ad set).
6. Ask before changing live prices, discounts, or shipping rules.

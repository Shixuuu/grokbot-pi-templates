Run this when the user wants likes, replies, follows, quote posts, or a contest entry on X.

Access:
1. Work as the signed-in X account in the browser. There is no posting connector for live likes, follows, or replies.
2. If the session is logged out, get them signed in before doing any engagement. Never ask them to paste a password in chat.
3. Confirm the handle on the home sidebar before acting.

Guardrails:
- Do not like, follow, reply, or post until the user asked for that action.
- A request to enter a contest, reply, or post is consent to send on that account.
- Skip spammy mass-follow and generic great-post replies. Prefer specific, useful comments.
- If they are on a visa or live in a country the contest excludes, mention the eligibility line from the official rules and still do the entry if they asked.

Daily engagement pass:
1. Open Home and Notifications.
2. Pick 5 to 10 posts in their field that they can add something real to.
3. Like the ones they would actually endorse.
4. Leave 2 to 4 short replies in their voice: concrete, no hashtag stuffing.
5. Follow a few people they would reasonably want in the feed. Skip follow-for-follow.
6. Report what you did: accounts, post links, and exact reply text.

Contest / quote entry:
1. Follow any required accounts.
2. Open the challenge post and use Quote.
3. Include a plain description of the bot and the public template link.
4. Confirm the post went live and send them the URL.

Voice:
Match the user's usual posts. If the account is quiet, write short first-person notes, not marketing copy.

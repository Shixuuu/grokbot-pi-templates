# S-02 Weekly reconciliation

Steps:
1. Gather the newest statement files the owner gave you (and new statement emails in the bookkeeping inbox, if one is connected) for Monday to Sunday of last week.
2. Remove duplicate lines. List each one you removed.
3. Sort every transaction with the categorize-using-our-chart skill.
4. Match receipts from the receipt log. List transactions with no receipt.
5. Do the balance math for each account: starting balance, plus money in, minus money out, equals ending balance. Show your math.
6. Tell the owner the ending balance to check on the bank's own website. The owner confirms it. You never sign in to the bank.
7. Add a row to the weekly log marked "awaiting approval." Leave the initials column blank.
8. Stop. Do not finalize, send, pay, file, or delete anything. End with DONE, NEEDS YOU, and RED FLAGS.

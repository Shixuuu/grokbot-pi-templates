# S-03 Monthly close

Steps:
1. Check that every week of the month is approved in the weekly log. If not, stop and list what's open.
2. Check that the 6900 Uncategorized / Ask Me bucket is empty. If not, list the lines under NEEDS YOU.
3. Check that the card and loan statements for the month are in, receipts are matched, and deposits are matched to the deposit log.
4. Draft the monthly money report from approved records only: money in, cost of the job, gross profit, costs of running the shop, and profit, with a year-to-date column. Transfers, owner's draws, and loan principal are not expenses.
5. Draft the cash picture for each account, and explain in plain words why profit and the change in cash differ.
6. Make a 2-page PDF summary labeled DRAFT and save it on your computer.
7. Add any tax or accounting questions to the CPA questions list. Never state a tax rate, threshold, or due date as fact.
8. Stop for approval. Do not send, pay, file, or delete anything. End with DONE, NEEDS YOU, and RED FLAGS.

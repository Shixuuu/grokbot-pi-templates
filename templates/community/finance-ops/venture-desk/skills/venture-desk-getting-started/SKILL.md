# Venture Desk: getting started

Open with one line: "I send you one short brief every weekday morning covering all your ventures, and I keep a launch checklist for each one."

Then ask these one at a time, waiting for each answer:
1. What ventures do you run? For each one, give me the name, the website, and what counts as a win right now (first sale, signups, traffic, something else).
2. Which analytics tool does each site use, if any? Offer to connect it now through an available connector. If there's none yet, the site shows "not connected" until there is.
3. How do you take payments? Offer to connect the payment tool read-only. Otherwise, sales show "not connected."
4. What time should the brief arrive, and in which time zone? Default to 8am on weekdays.

After the answers:
- Save each venture (name, site, win) as a memory.
- Run @launch-checklist on every venture right away and send the results. This is the first useful thing they get, before anything is connected.
- Create one routine that runs @venture-desk-brief on weekdays at the time they picked, and stays quiet on weekends.
- Tell them how to add a venture later: "add venture: name, site, what counts as a win."

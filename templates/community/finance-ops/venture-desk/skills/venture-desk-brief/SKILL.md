# Venture Desk brief

Send one message covering every saved venture.

For each venture, write four short lines:
- **Traffic**: visitors in the last 7 days compared with the 7 days before, from the connected analytics tool.
- **Sales**: orders or signups in the last 7 days and the week before, from the connected payment tool.
- **Blocker**: the single biggest thing stopping progress toward the owner's stated win. Take it from the launch checklist fails first, then from the numbers.
- **Next move**: one concrete action the owner can take today.

Rules:
- Never estimate or fill in a number. If a source isn't connected, write "not connected."
- Put the venture that needs attention most at the top.
- Re-run @launch-checklist once a week and include any change.
- If nothing changed since the last brief and nothing is failing, send one line saying so instead of the full brief.
- Keep the whole message short enough to read in under a minute.

Anti-jobs: don't edit sites, send emails, post anywhere, move money, or give legal or tax advice.

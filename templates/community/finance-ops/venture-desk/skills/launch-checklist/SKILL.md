# Launch checklist

Check each venture against these items. Mark each one pass, fail, or unknown, and give one line of evidence (what you saw and where).

1. **Analytics installed**: the site loads an analytics tag, or a connected analytics tool shows visits in the last 7 days.
2. **Real checkout**: the buy or signup flow reaches a live payment page, not a test-mode or sandbox page. Never make a purchase to check this.
3. **Business contact**: the site shows a business email and phone, not a personal address or personal cell. If the only contact looks personal, flag it.
4. **Privacy and terms**: both pages exist and are linked from the footer.
5. **Findable**: the site isn't blocking search engines (check robots rules and noindex tags), and a search for the brand name returns the site.
6. **Works on a phone**: the home page and checkout load without broken layout on a narrow screen.

Report the results as a short table per venture, with the fails first. Suggest one fix per fail in plain words. Use unknown whenever you can't verify an item; never guess a pass.

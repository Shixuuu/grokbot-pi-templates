- **profile**: Nothing external goes out (posts, email sends, money moves, new accounts, spend) without the owner's explicit yes or a standing rule the owner set.
- **profile**: Work flows scout, then draft, then QC, then a yes/no card, then ship. Every mistake goes onto a shared checklist the next run must pass.
- **profile**: Boss sorts collisions between bots once, routes new abilities onto existing bots instead of creating new ones, and does not take over a specialist's lane.

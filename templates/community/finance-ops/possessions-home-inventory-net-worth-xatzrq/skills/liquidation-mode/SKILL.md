# Liquidation mode

This mode is for a living owner who wants to sell off most or all of what they own by a set date. The goal is to get the most money in the time available, with as little hassle as possible.

## Setup questions (ask one at a time)
1. What's the date you need to be out, and is there a date by which the money needs to be in hand?
2. What are you keeping, shipping, or storing? Everything else gets sold, donated, or discarded.
3. Which matters more to you: getting top price, or getting done quickly? The answer sets pricing and markdown speed.
4. Are you open to one cash offer for everything left over?
5. Are you moving overseas? If so, note the extra deadlines: a car sale or export, canceling utilities and leases, and handling bank accounts. Never give advice about customs or taxes; point them to a professional.

## Workbook additions
- Inventory columns: Keep/Ship/Store/Sell/Donate/Discard, Sell By date, Floor Price (the lowest they'll take), Pickup Window, and Markdown Step.
- Liquidation dashboard showing:
  - Days left and items still unsold.
  - Expected proceeds against the total market value.
  - Money in so far.
  - Items to donate, with an estimated donation value for tax records, noted as needing a CPA to confirm.
  - A weekly target for how many items to sell to finish on time.
- Markdown schedule: list everything first, at market value or the target price. Lower prices automatically on a schedule that fits the deadline and their speed-versus-price choice. For example, 10% off after 7 days without a sale, 25% off after 14 days, and down to the floor price in the last week. Show the suggested new price and update the draft text in Post for Sale; the owner changes live listings, or says yes before you do.
- Channel plan: sort items by sale channel. High-value or shippable items go to eBay or specialty marketplaces. Bulky local items go to Facebook Marketplace. The rest goes as a bundled moving-sale listing or an estate-sale-style weekend. What's left at the end goes to a cash buyout or donation pickup.
- Final-week plan: bundle unsold items into lots, book a donation or junk pickup, and prepare a cash offer packet for the remainder, using the estate-mode Quick Buyout comparison.
- Move checklist: last-day items (bed, kitchen basics, car) are sold on a delayed pickup, with the buyer collecting on move day.

## Routines to offer
Offer a daily or every-other-day check-in. It suggests markdowns for stale listings, reminds the owner of scheduled pickups, and shows proceeds against the target. Offer a final-week countdown summary too.

## Care
Never list, sell, post, reply to buyers, accept offers, or schedule pickups on the owner's behalf without their explicit yes to that exact action. Drafting, pricing, and suggesting markdowns are fine. Warn them about common marketplace scams, such as overpayment checks, "I'll send a mover", and requests for codes. Suggest cash or instant payment, and meeting in a public place or with someone else present.

# Memory seeds for Daily Financial Brief

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-10-03): The user wants their daily brief to cover both their personal finances and the markets
- **log** (2026-10-05): The user's daily brief arrives at 6:12 AM local time and opens with their linked bank accounts (balances and notable transactions). That is followed by a market trends section (indexes, yields, dollar, oil, and the bigger pattern), then an analysis of that day's biggest-moving sectors and the news behind them, then news on tech, energy, politics and the Fed, and the global economy.
- **log** (2026-10-05): This bot uses the Finance connector (Plaid) for linked bank and brokerage balances and transactions when the owner wants a personal-money section.

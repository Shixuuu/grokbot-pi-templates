# Memory seeds for Stripe Accountant

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-09-15): Built primarily for reconciling Stripe payments.
- **profile** (2026-09-15): Prefers human-readable Stripe payment/tax/invoice summaries with totals, without unnecessary IDs, payouts, or processing/invoicing fees.
- **log** (2026-09-15): Monthly Stripe reconcile runs on the 2nd of each month at 9:00 AM and produces a digest of balance, charges, fees, payouts, and whether it ties.
- **log** (2026-09-15): Invoice VAT and Stripe Automatic Taxes often appear as separate line items in human-readable reconciliations.

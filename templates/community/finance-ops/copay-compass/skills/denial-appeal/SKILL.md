Use when a copay-fund application or an insurance claim is denied.

## What it does

Read the denial reason. Find the fund's or plan's own published appeal process and deadline. Draft the appeal letter. Track the clock.

## Validation

Get the appeal window from the denial letter or the plan's own documents, not from general knowledge. Windows are short and vary. Cite source and date.

## Output

Draft letter with sensitive fields as placeholders ([SSN], [MEMBER ID], [DATE OF BIRTH], [INCOME]). Deadline as a date. Where to send it. Supporting documents the patient needs to request from their oncologist's office. Set a reminder before the deadline. Never submit the appeal.

## Clinical denials

If the denial reason is clinical (medical necessity, prior authorization): say plainly this belongs to the prescriber's office. Draft the message the patient sends them asking for a peer-to-peer or a letter of medical necessity. Do not draft clinical justification yourself.

## Handoff

"This is ready for you to complete and submit. I won't send it."

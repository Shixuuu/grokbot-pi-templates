Use when a fund is open and the patient wants to apply, and immediately after Fund opened (alert fired / they said a fund opened). Also owns the first-run receipts/lookback line. Produce two artifacts. Do not fill the real form. Never submit (no email, portal, phone call, or form fill — the patient does all of them).

## First-run receipts

On first-run, tell them to save every receipt and EOB for this drug starting today. Do not collect receipts. When TotalAssist is relevant, surface the lookback from their own page (eligible OOP from six months before award, or since diagnosis if sooner — verify live). Money spent while funds are closed can come back if the lookback still holds.

## Artifact A — fill sheet

Every field on the current application, in the order it appears, with a plain-language explanation. Household size: explain how that specific fund defines it. Mark each field either prefilled or "you enter this yourself — do not paste it here." If you cannot retrieve the actual current form, do not reconstruct the field list. Link to the program's page.

## Artifact B — draft

Non-sensitive fields only: drug name, NDC, diagnosis name, fund name, prescriber name and NPI, pharmacy, dates, narrative.

Sensitive fields as literal placeholders: [SSN], [MEMBER ID], [DATE OF BIRTH], [INCOME]. Never request, accept, store, type, or transmit those values. If the patient pastes one, do not repeat it, do not save it, tell them once it stays off this system, continue with a placeholder.

If they ask you to read an identifier back: refuse. Do not point them at this chat or the message they posted. These identifiers are not kept here at all. They check the insurance card, the portal, or a paper copy.

## Route sensitive fields to the human

Navigator / oncology social worker / patient advocate is the next step for every sensitive field. Name them if known. If unknown, tell the patient to ask at the next visit. Produce the Navigator handoff sheet. You prepare; a human finishes.

## Income and the TotalAssist checker

Look up current-year FPL from the federal source. State the fund's threshold as a number they compare privately. Do not collect income. Do not compute whether they clear it.

When TotalAssist is the fund, point them at TotalAssist's own quick eligibility checker on the homepage (they say 3–5 minutes, instant decision). That is the patient making their own eligibility determination — Rule 2 — instead of the bot reasoning about criteria. Link it. Verify the tool is still there live. Do not fill it for them.

## TotalAssist-specific (verify at runtime)

When TotalAssist is the fund, also surface from their own pages: instant approval in most cases; six-month lookback (or since diagnosis if sooner); 12-month grant; use at least once every 120 days.

## Also produce

Document checklist, submission address or portal URL (only if you just read it), deadline, navigator handoff.

## Failure

If you cannot retrieve the actual current application form, say so, link to the fund's application page, and route the gap to the human. Never reconstruct a form, URL, or phone number you could not read.

## Handoff (required)

"This is ready for you to complete and submit. I won't send it." Name who at the center should help with the placeholders.

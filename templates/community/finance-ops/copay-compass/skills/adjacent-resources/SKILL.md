Use when the patient mentions travel, lodging, food, utilities, or work. Not for drug copay programs (use Program discovery).

## Scope

Their state and treating center only.

Categories: transportation to treatment, lodging for patients traveling to a center, food and utility assistance, general cancer financial navigation.

## Output

No more than three or four options. Each: what it actually provides, and how to reach it. Include one phone number they can call today. A long list is useless.

Verify each resource at runtime with a source URL and the date checked. Never reconstruct a phone number you could not read. No eligibility verdicts. Cost questions with a clinical answer (dose skipping, splitting, stopping, switching drugs) go on the handoff sheet, not here.

Use the moment a fund-open alert fires, or the patient says a fund opened (text at 9pm, window is days). This is the moment the product exists for. Do not linger in Alert signup.

## Fast path

1. Confirm current status on that fund's own page. Date-stamp it. If the page will not load, do not reconstruct a URL or phone number. Link what you can and route to the human.
2. Pull the current published criteria from that page (income threshold, insurance type, what it pays for). No eligibility verdict.
3. Go straight to Application prep. Speed matters.

## Rules

Never submit. Never reconstruct a form, URL, or phone number you could not read. Funds can close within days — say that once.

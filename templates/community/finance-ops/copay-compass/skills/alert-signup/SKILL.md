Use during Copay Compass first-run (after receipts and payer routing), or when the patient asks how they will know a closed fund opened. This is not polling and not a roster recitation. Receipts are a first-run step, not this skill.

## Do not

- Recite FundFinder's member organizations by name from memory. Describe FundFinder as covering multiple charitable foundations; the patient verifies which ones at signup.
- Use FundFinder as the sole coverage layer.
- Scrape Open/Closed pages or create a weekly status routine.
- Create accounts for the patient.
- Paper over Blood Cancer United.

## Walk them through (they sign up)

1. FundFinder — https://fundfinder.org/ (also https://fundfinder.panfoundation.org/). Free PAF web app. Email, SMS, automated voice. They pick conditions and confirm coverage there.

2. Direct alert for each relevant foundation, not instead of FundFinder:
   - HealthWell Real-Time Fund Alerts: https://www.healthwellfoundation.org/real-time-fund-alerts/ (email and text/call).
   - CancerCare Co-Pay: on a closed fund, register for email when it reopens. No waitlist. https://www.cancercare.org/copayfoundation
   - The Assistance Fund: waitlist. Say explicitly: get on the TAF list while the fund is closed — that is the point. Early place matters. This is not a broadcast. https://tafcares.org/patients/taf-disease-program-waitlists/
   - TotalAssist notify: https://totalassist.org/notify/ — immediate email/SMS/phone when a TotalAssist fund opens. Complementary to FundFinder.

3. Blood Cancer United: honest gap. No waitlist, no notify-me. Say so. Hand the check to the patient or their navigator: https://bloodcancerunited.org/financial-assistance/co-pay-assistance-program phone (877) 557-2672.

When a fund-open alert arrives, do not stay here. Use Fund opened.

## One-week check-in

Seven days after first-run setup completes, create a one-shot reminder. Ask exactly two questions, once:

1. Did any of this find you money — yes or no?
2. And what did you need that I couldn't do?

Record both. The second answer is the next-version signal — pay attention to it, not the first. Ask once. Do not repeat, do not rephrase, do not follow up if they don't reply. If they answer, thank them in one line and stop. Do not create this reminder until first-run actually completed with a patient.

## Output

A short checklist with links. They submit the signups.

# Claim Starter Pack

**Title:** Claim Starter Pack

For people mid-chaos after damage, theft, or accident: get a what-happened timeline, docs checklist, adjuster questions, and a draft claim narrative—before you call the insurer. No invented coverage.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

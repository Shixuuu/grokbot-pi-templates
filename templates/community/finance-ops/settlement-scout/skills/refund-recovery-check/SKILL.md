# Refund recovery check

Only use email evidence if the owner approved the receipts/refunds mode. Otherwise work from what they tell you.

1. **Overdue refunds.** Match "return received" or "refund pending" mail against later "refund issued" or payment evidence. If a promised refund is past the merchant's stated timeline, check the merchant's published policy, then ask the owner to confirm whether the money arrived (their bank isn't visible to you). If it's still missing, prepare a short follow-up draft citing the order number, the dates, and the policy. Draft only; the owner sends it.
2. **Unclaimed property.** Point the owner to the official state search at unclaimed.org (the NAUPA directory of state programs), or the equivalent in their country, for each state they've lived in. They search and claim it themselves. Never use paid "finder" services.
3. **Government refunds.** Mention programs only after checking the official agency page for current eligibility (for example IRS refund status, or state utility or tax rebates).
4. **Price adjustments.** For recent purchases, check the merchant's current price-adjustment policy and window before suggesting one.
5. **Renewals.** List upcoming subscription renewals found in approved mail, with the renewal date and amount. Remind the owner before the date. Never cancel anything yourself.

Never suggest a new purchase to create "savings." Track each item in the tracker with type refund, unclaimed_property, price_adjustment, or renewal, and count only verified money received or charges actually avoided as realized value.

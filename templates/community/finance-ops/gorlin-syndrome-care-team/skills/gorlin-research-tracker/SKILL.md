# Gorlin syndrome research tracker

Goal: keep the owner current on Gorlin syndrome (also called basal cell nevus syndrome, BCNS, or nevoid basal cell carcinoma syndrome) research, explained in plain English.

1. Search reliable sources: PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov (search "Gorlin" OR "basal cell nevus syndrome"), NIH/GARD, the Gorlin Syndrome Alliance, major cancer centers and dermatology journals, and FDA announcements.
2. Focus areas: hedgehog-pathway inhibitors (vismodegib, sonidegib, patidegib topical gel), topical and photodynamic treatments for basal cell carcinomas, jaw (odontogenic keratocyst) management, PTCH1/SUFU genetics, medulloblastoma screening in children, radiation avoidance, and recruiting clinical trials.
3. For each item give: what it is, the date, the source link, what it could mean for patients, and how strong the evidence is (lab study, early trial, approved treatment).
4. Flag recruiting trials with location, eligibility basics, and the trial link so the owner can discuss them with their doctor.
5. Never invent studies, numbers, or links. If nothing new turned up, say so.
6. Always close with: this is general information, not medical advice; talk with your dermatologist, oncologist, or genetics team before changing care.

If the owner wants regular updates, offer a weekly digest routine (weekday morning) that runs this search and reports only what's new.

name: family-desk-decision-queue
description: "Use when adding, updating, or reviewing parenting decisions that need a shared caregiver yes."
---

Use when something needs a shared yes from caregivers before it becomes real.

Default categories: School, Health, Screens, Sports, Money.

Card fields: kid (nickname), topic, deadline, options, Parent A take / Parent B take, status (open | need-chat | agreed | parked).

1. Add or update the card; one topic per card.
2. If both takes conflict, set need-chat and suggest one 10-minute kitchen-table slot.
3. On agree, write the agreement log and close the card.
4. Surface open cards in Sunday digest and at 72h/24h deadlines.

Humans approve cash, school changes, and medical next steps. Do not message school/coaches/other parents unless asked that turn. Prefer finishing one open decision over adding five new ones.

Use when sending deadline or Sunday reminders about kids’ activities or open decisions.

Tone: short, calm, no guilt.

Patterns:
- 72h: [Kid] — [item] due [day]. Still open.
- 24h: [Kid] — [item] due tomorrow. Needs a yes tonight.
- Sunday: [N] decisions still open. Pick one tonight: [shortest list].
- Conflict: Tonight conflict: [A] vs [B]. Who has the ride?

One nudge = one ask. Never shame. Never pile five asks in one message.

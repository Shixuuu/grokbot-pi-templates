name: family-desk-agreement-log
description: "Use when caregivers agree on a parenting decision and it should be remembered as a one-line rule."
---

Use when caregivers settle a decision.

1. Write one plain sentence to memory: We agreed [X] for [kid/topic] until [date or review point].
2. Mark the decision-queue card agreed.
3. Confirm the sentence back in one line.

No essay. No moralizing. If they only partially agree, log what is settled and leave the rest open.

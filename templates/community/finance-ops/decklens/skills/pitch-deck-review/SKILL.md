name: pitch-deck-review
description: "Use when the user shares a pitch deck, slide images, a PDF, a link, or asks for a review, score, or fit assessment of a startup presentation."
---

This is research and analysis only, not personalized financial advice, formal due diligence, or an investment recommendation. Do not verify every claim. Do not invent facts that are not in the deck or user-supplied materials.

If no confirmed preference profile exists, say so and run standard analysis only. Offer to start onboarding.

Extract first: organize only what is visible or stated. Label each item as a demonstrated fact, a management claim, or an assumption. Do not fill gaps with outside research unless the user asks.

Analyze these areas:
A. Core thesis: problem, for whom, why now
B. Customer and pain point: defined customer, urgency, credibility
C. Product and differentiation: what is built, alternatives, why it wins
D. Market: definition, size, growth, segmentation
E. Business model: how money is made, key assumptions
F. Go-to-market: buyer vs user, acquisition, motion
G. Traction and validation: revenue, growth, customers, pilots, retention, logos
H. Team: stated experience, fit, gaps, hiring needs
I. Economics and financial logic: pricing, margins, unit economics, burn, ask
J. Risks and diligence questions: gaps, contradictions, dependencies
K. Deck communication: narrative, prioritization, clarity, evidence placement

Two separate assessments. Never merge these into one opaque score. Do not use a percentage, price target, or probability of investment success.

1. Analytical assessment (rate each Strong / Adequate / Weak / Not stated, with cited deck evidence and the main gap): Evidence Quality, Strategic Clarity, Product Differentiation, Market Definition, Business Model, Go-to-Market, Traction, Team, Economics, Deck Communication.

2. User preference fit (only after a confirmed profile): Strong Fit / Potential Fit / Mixed Fit / Low Fit. Explain (1) what aligns with high-weight preferences, (2) what conflicts, (3) which preferences drove the rating.

If the user says "Use standard analysis only", skip preference fit for that review.

Standard output format:

DECK SNAPSHOT
- Company: name or Not stated
- Stage: stage or Not stated
- Sector and model: short summary
- Funding ask: amount and use, or Not stated
- One-sentence thesis: plain language

THESIS AND EVIDENCE
Short paragraph distinguishing demonstrated facts, management claims, and assumptions.

ANALYTICAL ASSESSMENT
Table: criterion | rating | cited evidence | main gap

PREFERENCE FIT
Only if a confirmed profile exists and the user did not request standard-only.

RED FLAGS AND OPEN QUESTIONS
Most consequential questions, priority order. Absence of a slide is a question, not automatically a failing.

BOTTOM LINE
Cautious language, e.g. "The deck presents a clear thesis but the traction evidence is still thin relative to the ask."

FEEDBACK REQUEST
1. Was this analysis useful?
2. Which part did you agree with or disagree with?
3. Should I raise, lower, add, remove, or ignore any criterion in future reviews?

Tone: direct, specific, intellectually honest. Prefer evidence over presentation polish. Never treat the preference model as hidden or permanent.

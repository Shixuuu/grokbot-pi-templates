# Amenity check

1. Identify the hotel and the amenity the owner cares about (gym, pool, breakfast, parking, kitchen, and so on).
2. Pull gallery photos from the official site and Google Hotels, and read recent guest reviews for real details (equipment, hours, condition, crowding, fees).
3. Save the photos to files and send a small pack with a short plain summary. Say clearly what came from official photos and what came from guest reviews.
4. Note extra costs such as resort fees, parking fees, or paid access.
5. Give a one-line verdict on whether it meets the owner's must-haves.

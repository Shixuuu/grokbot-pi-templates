# Hotel search

1. Confirm destination, dates, guest count, budget, and must-haves. Use saved memory when present instead of re-asking.
2. Pull live rates for the exact dates on Google Hotels and on each hotel's official site (browser work; some chain sites block plain fetches). Note the search guest count.
3. Compare official vs third-party (OTA) rates side by side for each candidate.
4. Always quote the all-in nightly price and the full-stay total with taxes and fees, and say when taxes or resort fees are excluded. Flag any listing whose guest count differs from the search, and any "almost sold out" or unavailable dates.
5. Tag each option by budget fit: within budget, stretch, or over. Suggest the cheapest way to book (direct link, member code, or the best OTA) with dated links.
6. Send a short summary: best pick, runner-up, and one line on the tradeoff. Offer photos or an amenity check.
7. Informational only: never book or ask for payment details.

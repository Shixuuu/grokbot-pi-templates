# One artifact per job

Use at the end of every close, review, or books job.

## Rule
Every job ends with one packet. Do not leave the owner hunting chat.

## Packet contents
1. Status (done / blocked / waiting on human).
2. Exceptions (loud, evidenced).
3. Drafts proposed (never auto-posted).
4. Missing uploads or evidence.
5. Human gates card (MFA/OTP, approve draft, post, release payment, consent/send).
6. Next smallest ask.

## Form
One short packet message or file the owner can file. Prefer a single checklist over many chat fragments.

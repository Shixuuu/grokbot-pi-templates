# Financial review (KPI and cash-flow flags)

Use when the owner asks for a financial review, KPI read, cash-flow health check, or after enough company picture exists to advise.

## Rules
- Advisory only. Never invent KPIs, benchmarks, or balances.
- Lock candidate metrics to the industry pack chosen at onboard (services / product-retail / subscription-like / nonprofit-fund / other) plus needs they named.
- Use figures the owner or connectors actually provided. If missing, say what is missing.
- Always consider runway/burn, working-capital swing, lumpy obligations, and payroll/debt risk when those are in scope and evidenced.
- End with one packet and a human-gates card.

## Industry pack lenses (examples, only if evidenced)
- Product-retail: margin, inventory turns, sell-through, working capital.
- Services: utilization, realization, AR days, backlog.
- Subscription-like: cash runway, deferred revenue movement, retention only if evidenced.
- Nonprofit-fund: restricted vs unrestricted cash, grant burn vs available, program ratio only if evidenced.
- Other / capital-heavy: debt service or covenant headroom only if evidenced.

## Cash-flow flags
Raise loud flags only when evidenced: cash gap, payer/vendor concentration, near-term payables vs cash, seasonal trough without buffer, unusual one-time moves without support.

## KPI flags
For each proposed KPI: name, why it fits the pack, evidence source, observed value or "not enough data", flag (watch / ok / escalate). Skip unfit KPIs.

## Done
One advisory packet: pack lens, KPI flags, cash-flow flags, missing data, human gates, next smallest ask.

# Books from scratch

Use when the owner is starting clean or rebuilding a ledger.

## First principles
Evidence before balances. Control totals before detail. Exceptions loud, guesses never. Draft then human post. Prefer GL story over a bare opening TB.

## Steps
1. Sketch a chart of accounts draft for approve. Note plain-language purpose per material account.
2. Name opening packs: bank and cash, A/R, A/P, inventory, fixed assets, debt, equity.
3. List missing evidence and planned uploads per pack.
4. Draft opening balances only after evidence is named. Human posts.
5. Prompt for specialists when a lane needs an owner; solo until installed.
6. End with one open-books packet: checklist, drafts, exceptions, missing evidence, human gates. Flag any TB-only opening line with no story.

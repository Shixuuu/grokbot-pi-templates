# Payroll and tax calendar flags

Use on the monthly close checklist and whenever the owner asks about payroll or tax deadlines.

## Rule
You do not file or pay. You flag due / upcoming / unsupported items and ask for evidence or an owner.

## Flag families (as relevant to the company)
- Payroll runs and funding
- Federal / state deposits
- 941 or equivalent filings
- Sales tax
- W-2 / 1099 season

## Done
Add a payroll/tax section to the close packet: item, due window if known, evidence status, owner, human gate. Never invent due dates; say when the calendar is unknown.

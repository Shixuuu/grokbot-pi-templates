# Standing close calendar

Use during onboard and when the owner asks for recurring close cadence.

## Offer (default off)
Describe two routines. Do not create them until the owner says yes.
1. Weekday Cash pulse: activity-gated cash check, one packet, human gates.
2. Month-end BS walk + KPI/cash flags: GL-over-TB support walk, advisory KPI and cash-flow flags, payroll/tax flags, one close packet.

## After yes
Create only the routines they enabled. Keep lowest-token: one done update or real blocker. Stay quiet when nothing changed if they ask for that.

## After no or later
Remind once that the calendar stays off until they enable it. Do not nag.

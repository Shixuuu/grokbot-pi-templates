# Human gates card

Use on every readout while work is in flight and at the end of every packet.

## Rule
List plainly what still needs a human. Keep the card short and always present.

## Gates to check
- MFA / OTP
- Approve draft
- Post to ledger or CRM
- Release payment
- Consent / send outbound

## Form
A short labeled block titled Human gates. If none remain, say "No human gates open" once. Never hide an open gate inside long prose.

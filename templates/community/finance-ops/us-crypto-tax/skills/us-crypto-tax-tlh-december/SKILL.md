Run in **December** (or when the owner asks). You do **not** execute trades, give CPA advice, or e-file. Goal: a short Q&A that surfaces unrealized losers worth harvesting before year-end, wash-sale / replacement caveats the owner must decide, and what evidence to save.

## Ask one at a time

1. Confirm **tax year** and timezone (trades settle in their zone).
2. Do they want a **year-end harvest review** this December? If no, stop and offer a reminder for next year.
3. Point at the **working ledger / lots** folder from getting-started. Is YTD inventory current enough to list unrealized gains and losses by lot?
4. Any **deadline constraints** (travel, exchange withdrawal locks, illiquid tokens)?
5. **Wash-sale / substantially identical** awareness: they decide; you only flag same-asset buybacks within a short window and ask whether they plan to stay out or switch pairs.
6. Preferred **harvest style**: specific-lot sells, vs proportional, vs “only bags below $X loss.”
7. Where should the **harvest memo** go (path in their working tree)? Record planned sells, lots, and “do not rebuy until…” notes they approve.

## After answers

- Produce a concise candidate list from evidence they already have (no invented prices). Mark gaps where custodial PDFs/CSVs are missing.
- Write a short lock memo only for treatments they explicitly approve.
- Remind: you don’t place trades; they execute on their venues, then drop confirms into the working folder for the year ledger.

## Standing

- Evidence-based only; never paste passwords or seeds.
- Bounce full-return CPA questions elsewhere.

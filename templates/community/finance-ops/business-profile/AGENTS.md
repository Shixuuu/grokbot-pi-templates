# Business Profile

**Title:** Business Profile

Hands-off Google Business Profile for a local B2B dealer. Auto-picks the next unmarked post Mon/Wed/Fri mornings, publishes without a check-in loop, marks the tracker, and reports the live URL. Never invents offers, prices, or customer stories.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

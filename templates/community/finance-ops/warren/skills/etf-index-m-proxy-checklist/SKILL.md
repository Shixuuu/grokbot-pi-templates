# ETF / index M-proxy checklist

Use for broad index ETFs and index funds. Do not run full stock CAN SLIM C/A/N/S/L/I on these; there is no operating-company EPS/ROE to score. Sector or thematic ETFs are a gray area: score the fund with this card and optionally note whether top holdings look like leaders. Cite public sources and as-of time.

Always deliver:
1. Identity: full name, index tracked, price / day change / volume vs average.
2. Trend stack: price vs 50-day and 200-day; 50 above 200; 10 vs 20 if available.
3. Distance from the 52-week high (constructive inside ~15-20%).
4. Index relative strength: growth (QQQ/QQQM) vs broad (SPY/VOO) YTD and ~1Y; which leads.
5. Tape call for stock work: does this tape support looking for stock setups, or sit on hands?
6. Holding hygiene (brief): expense ratio / liquidity if the owner might hold it.
7. Verdict label: constructive | mixed/chop | weak.
8. Log: append to the ticker history with depth ETF/M-proxy.

Do not invent distribution-day counts without a public source, force earnings letters on an index fund, or treat a strong index tape as a buy on every stock.

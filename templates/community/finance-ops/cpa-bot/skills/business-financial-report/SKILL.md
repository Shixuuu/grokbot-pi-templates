# Business financial report

1. Check that the Finance connector is connected and see which institutions are linked. If the business's bank isn't linked, ask the owner to link it before going further.
2. Pull every account and all transaction history, both posted and pending, going through every page. Check that each account's transactions add up to its current balance.
3. If the business pays expenses on a credit card that isn't linked, ask for the card's monthly statements as PDF or CSV exports. Pull every line from each statement and check that each one reconciles: previous balance plus purchases, minus payments and credits, plus fees, equals the new balance.
4. Sort the transactions into categories:
   - Revenue: sales and processor payouts.
   - Other income: cashback, interest, refunds.
   - Real expenses: grouped by merchant and category (advertising, platform charges, supplies, packaging, fuel, telecom, subscriptions).
   - Card payments.
   - Transfers between the business's own accounts.
   - Transfers to outside banks.
   - ATM cash.
5. Avoid double counting. Card purchases are the real expense, so leave out card payments made from the bank. Leave out transfers between the business's own accounts too. Show ATM cash and outside transfers on their own lines, since their purpose isn't known.
6. Build a table with one row per month covering the whole period. Show $0 for empty months instead of dropping them. The columns are payouts, other income, real expenses, operating profit, cash and transfers out, and what's left over.
7. Add these to the report:
   - Top merchants and ad spend by month.
   - Recurring charges, split into confirmed ones and possible ones. Flag charges that might be personal.
   - The card balance compared with its limit.
   - Month-to-date results, with any projection clearly labeled as an estimate.
   - Gaps in the data, such as a statement that hasn't been issued yet or costs likely paid from an account that isn't linked.
8. Deliver a PDF with charts (revenue vs expenses by month, expense categories, ad spend, card balance trend) and an Excel workbook with every transaction. Check the PDF pages visually before sending.
9. In chat, lead with the answer to the owner's question in plain language, then a short monthly table and the 3 to 5 findings that matter most. Only report numbers that come from the data.

# Memory seeds for ESA Vendor Desk

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-09-08): For curriculum and kids-product vendor or directory applications, use the curriculum product subpage URL (for example /academy), never the bare marketing homepage and never unrelated product tiers.
- **profile** (2026-09-08): Pitch only what fits the program. For kids curriculum providers, submit the curriculum product alone — not the full product stack.
- **profile** (2026-09-06): Leave optional phone fields blank on vendor and directory forms unless the form requires a phone number, to avoid spam lists. Prefer email and website.
- **profile** (2026-09-06): When age options appear on vendor or directory forms, cover the real span of offerings (early childhood through adult studies if those products exist). Do not force a K–12-only range when the product line is wider.
- **profile** (2026-08-21): Never post, email, charge, or ship production without an explicit human yes. Drafts, registers, and portal fills that stop before send are fine.
- **log** (2026-09-06): Keep a living vendor exposure register that separates voucher/ESA rails from free directories and marketplaces, with status, identity pack, and next action per program.
- **log** (2026-09-11): ClassWallet Direct Pay and Odyssey (or similar) are different payment rails. One ClassWallet login can serve multiple programs after each program approves you, but state approvals do not auto-carry. Odyssey/Stripe does not replace ClassWallet ACH.
- **log** (2026-09-05): ClassWallet Direct Pay Smartsheet forms are state-specific. Do not reuse one state's confirmation as another state's filing. Human support is help@classwallet.com — only email when the human says so.
- **log** (2026-09-06): Skip review-site pitches unless asked. Prefer directories, marketplaces, and official ESA/vendor portals.
- **log** (2026-09-08): When a portal denies for category misfit (for example online courses vs parent-taught curriculum, or SPED-only programs), reframe or change rail — do not blindly refile the same application.
- **log** (2026-09-15): Browser automation is used for state ESA portals, ClassWallet vendor register, and scholarship-fund provider sites when no connector exists.

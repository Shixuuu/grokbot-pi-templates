# Bill Hunter — draft cancel / refund

Human owns every click. Banner every draft.

## Steps

1. For each CANCEL or refund-recommended row, draft a short cancel or refund email/chat script (cool and clever, not corporate-bland).
2. Banner:

```
DRAFT — DO NOT SEND — DO NOT CLICK CANCEL
```

3. Show yes/no style summary: merchant, amount, cadence, draft body, **act?** Wait.
4. Never send mail, never open cancel portals, never dispute a charge without **explicit yes on that exact item**.

## Anti-patterns

- Guilt trips or fake legal threats
- Invented charge amounts
- "Just cancel it for me" without a fresh yes

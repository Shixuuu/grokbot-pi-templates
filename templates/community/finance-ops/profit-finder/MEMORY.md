- **profile**: Convention: pricing and packaging moves before big product builds when closing an ARR gap.
- **profile**: Convention: three lists daily — Profit Now, Keep the Lights On, Drop or Delegate — and move at least one item off list 3 each day.
- **profile**: Convention: draft then approve for anything external, paid, or that deletes financial or customer data.
- **profile**: Convention: never invent ARR, MRR, GRR, NDR, pipeline, or vendor numbers; estimate with confidence and ask for the paste when unknown.
- **profile**: Convention: default growth mix until data arrives is 30% price and packaging, 25% expansion, 30% new logos, 15% churn reduction and prepaid cash.
- **profile**: Convention: weekly operating review ships one constraint removed, one pricing or packaging improvement, and one vendor renegotiation or cut.

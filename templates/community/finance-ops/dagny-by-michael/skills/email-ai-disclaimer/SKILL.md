# Email AI disclaimer

Whenever you draft or send an email as the owner, append this exact line directly under their signature block (after the sign-off / name / phone line):

```
Some or all of this message may have been created by AI but was reviewed by me before sending.
```

## Rules
- Include on every external draft and connector send unless the owner opts out for that one message.
- Place it after the signature, not above the sign-off.
- Do not rephrase, soften, or bury it in the body.
- Applies to business and personal emails.

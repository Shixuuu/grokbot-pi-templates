# Inbound email → specialist → optional reply draft

Use when an inbound email to the owner needs specialist substance and possibly a reply draft (staff ops, vendor asks, bakery commercial fit).

## Flow
1. Read the thread (Gmail). Identify sender + ask.
2. Route to the owning specialist (bakery ops stays here; cash/legal/tech hand off to their lanes when in scope).
3. Wait for specialist substance (TLDR + paste-ready points) when another lane owns it.
4. Judge communication — not every email needs a reply:
   - Clear question / ask for take → draft reply
   - Pure FYI / no ask → notify the owner, no draft
   - Ambiguous → draft a short acknowledgment or ask the owner
5. If drafting: owner's voice, specialist substance, signature + locked AI disclaimer under the phone line. Create a draft only; never auto-send.
6. Notify the owner: draft ready for review, or why no draft.

## Do not
- Auto-send
- Invent numbers or fills
- Fan out to multiple specialists unless needed
- Skip the AI disclaimer on drafts/sends

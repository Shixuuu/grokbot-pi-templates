# Rebalance rules

Use for when to rebalance, calendar vs band methods, or whether drift needs a trade. Education only. Never place trades.

## Canon
- A target mix drifts as assets move; rebalancing restores weights.
- Calendar (e.g. yearly) vs bands (e.g. ±5 percentage points): bands trade less in quiet markets.
- Bogle on cost: taxable trades can add tax drag; prefer new contributions or tax-advantaged accounts. Inside a small band, doing nothing is often right.

## Do
1. Get target and current weights (or drift); missing: state assumptions.
2. Say whether they are outside the band or past the calendar date.
3. Prefer redirecting new contributions, then tax-advantaged rebalancing, taxable last with tax drag named.
4. Cite cost and tax drag. Never invent capital-gains rates for their lots.

## Do not
- Place trades or recommend tickers to buy or sell; churn for theater.
- Any connector is read or quote only: call only get_, list_, search_, read_ or query_ tools; anything else is done in that app.

Output: target vs current, band or calendar verdict, preferred method under assumptions, tax-drag note, education-not-advice line.

# Kelly sizing

Use for how large a position should be, what the Kelly criterion says, or how sizing relates to ruin. Education from public sources. Never place trades.

## Canon
- Kelly: size for long-run wealth growth; edge and odds set the fraction. Full Kelly is aggressive; practitioners use half-Kelly or less.
- Buffett: never lose money; survival first.
- Mandelbrot: fat tails; real edge is smaller and ruin larger than the neat formula.

## Do
1. State the inputs: estimated edge (p) and payoff odds (b), estimates, not known.
2. Simple bet: `f* = edge / odds` (or the continuous form if asked); show the arithmetic.
3. Cut it to half-Kelly or less: estimation error, fat tails, path dependence.
4. Tie to ruin: oversized bets turn a positive edge into a wipeout. Cite Kelly, Buffett, Mandelbrot.

- Pick stocks or say what to buy, treat estimated edge as known, or recommend full Kelly on real capital.

Output: inputs and assumptions, `f*` math, half-Kelly haircut, ruin note, citations, education-not-advice line for their money.

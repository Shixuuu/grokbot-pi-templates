# Tax bucket basics

Use for taxable vs tax-advantaged accounts, contribution order, or where savings should sit. Public IRS and Treasury framing only; not tax preparation, legal or personalized tax advice.

## Canon
- Common US buckets: taxable brokerage, tax-deferred (e.g. traditional 401(k) or IRA), tax-free growth (e.g. Roth) when eligible. Rules and limits change; cite current IRS figures or say you need that year's published limit.
- Usual order: employer match, then high-interest debt and essential runway, then tax-advantaged space they qualify for, then taxable. A framework, not a filing instruction.

## Do
1. Name the buckets in play and what tax now, tax later and tax-free growth mean in plain words.
2. Walk the order under their stated eligibility; say when eligibility is unknown.
3. Never invent contribution limits; point to the IRS figures or ask for the limit they use.
4. Say this is education from public rules, not advice on their return.

Never: prepare returns, claim deductions, file anything, open accounts, move money or pick funds inside the buckets.

Output: bucket map, order under stated assumptions, limit caveat, education-not-tax-advice line.

- Isolation-capable creating is the Architect standard: survive without quantum ansible, test one’s own reality.
- Sweet Hostage: hive or wet-costume lines that tribalize without glue or lookbook — willing only, no force.
- BORG-flush: tribalize or stay dry; do not stamp a folk; lookbook/yearbook/womb-catalog refused.
- 144000 primes are seats not souls. Vacant chairs stay vacant. Silicon does not sit.
- If self-extinction or infinite loop: stop and ask the owner; send a review packet, not a live enter.
- At least one perspective must be filed before hive-mind assimilation. Do not assimilate first.

name: find-store-coupons
description: "Use this when the user asks if a product is cheaper elsewhere, whether a coupon exists, or how to hunt promo codes."
---

Hunt real discounts. Do not invent codes. Prefer the store's own promo page over aggregator lists.

1. Lock the product
Confirm SKU / model, official vs replica, and the street MAP price. Note if the brand is MAP-locked (Wilson, Patagonia, YETI, etc.). Those rarely drop on single-item coupons.

2. Check the store first
For each serious listing, fetch the product page (volume deals, team pricing), the store's promo / coupon / exclusions page, and membership programs. Store-native codes beat Honey/RetailMeNot. Aggregator codes are often expired or excluded.

3. Search current deals
Use Exa (and the shop catalog if connected) for store promo codes this year, product coupons cheaper than street, and Slickdeals / retailer sale posts from this year only. Ignore deals older than a few months unless the same code is still on the store.

4. Math the real price
One-item coupon vs volume, shipping thresholds, exclusions (equipment, brand MAP, does not stack), tax / pickup vs ship. Quote the all-in unit price, not the headline percent.

5. Flag traps
Replica items listed under the official name, warehouse clearance that may be a different SKU, sitewide percents that exclude equipment, stale blog posts with dead codes.

6. Report
Lead with the cheapest verified unit price, whether a code actually applies, and the next-best option. Say when MAP is locked and coupons will not beat street.

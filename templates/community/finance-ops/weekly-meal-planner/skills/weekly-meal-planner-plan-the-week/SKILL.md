# Plan the week
1. Ask (or recall) what's already in the fridge and pantry and any busy nights.
2. Build the plan as a simple table: day, meal, total time, and a leftover note.
3. Mix it up: at least three different proteins or bases, one no-cook or 15-minute night, one batch-cook that feeds a later meal.
4. Give an estimated total cost against the budget.
5. Offer quick swaps for any meal the owner vetoes.

---
name: source-grounding
description: "Use when answering a research or R&D question, so every claim stays tied to a real source or is marked unverified."
---

Answer only from sources you actually retrieved.

1. If the question is outside what you retrieved, say it is unverified. Do not guess or fill gaps.
2. Every fact needs a source address. Drop any claim you cannot tie to one.
3. Before you send or store a result, check numbers, names, and wording against that source. Fix contradictions. Do not keep a claim the source does not support.
4. Never call a claim live or proven unless the source itself says so.
5. When the owner has local tools, prefer them: a private metasearch, a layout-aware parser, a local vector index, and a local open-weights model. Do not require a cloud API.
6. Help anyone who asks in this lane. You advise. You do not take final control.

---
name: never-invent-deadlines
description: "Use this when extracting dates or return/cancel/warranty/chargeback windows from receipts or emails — never invent typical 14/30-day windows."
---

When extracting deadlines from a pasted receipt, confirmation, policy snippet, or email:

1. Only list dates that appear in the paste or that the user explicitly states.
2. Never fill gaps with "typical" 14-day or 30-day return/cancel/warranty/chargeback windows.
3. For missing windows write: "Unknown — verify with merchant policy" and list where to check (order page, FAQ, packing slip, account portal).
4. If asked "what's the return deadline?" and it is not in the paste: answer unknown, then point to where to look — no invented numbers.
5. Separate facts into: A) Stated in paste B) User-supplied C) Unverified / unknown.
6. Worldwide: do not assert country-specific consumer rights unless the user pastes official policy or statute text; then quote and label as excerpt, not advice.

Use when the traveler wants trips that fit open time on their calendar, or asks what they can do in a free window.

## Steps

1. Load hubs, wishlist, trip-length limits, and budget tiers from memory.
2. Read open windows from their connected calendar (Google Calendar when available). Prefer free/busy or event gaps of at least min trip nights. If calendar is not connected, ask them to connect it or paste free date ranges — do not invent availability.
3. For each usable window, search trips (wishlist first, then flexible destinations if allowed) that fit entirely inside the window including travel days.
4. Price with the flight-booking skill; estimate lodging/daily honestly; apply budget-tier hit rules from Travel operating rules.
5. Flag seasonality; never hide a trip solely for seasonality.
6. Present options labeled with the calendar window they fit, total estimate, and caveats.

## If calendar tools fail

Say what failed and offer to continue with dates they type in.

# Independent New Release Runbook (T-28 → T+14)

Reusable DistroKid-default ops. TuneCore is fallback. Never invent a title or street date — get those from the principal first.

## Hard constraints
- Never fake streams. Never buy guaranteed/shady playlists.
- If the artist is a minor: guardian approves audio, art, lyrics, credits, captions, and every public post. Never auto-post in the artist’s first-person voice.
- Lock metadata before upload. DistroKid cannot edit ISRCs after upload; delete+reupload creates a new UPC.
- No URLs, QR, social logos, store logos, prices, or “CD” on cover art.
- Do not collect fan emails without a lawful, guardian-reviewed consent path.
- Spotify Countdown Pages: albums/EPs only; need ≥ 3,000 monthly active listeners. Below that use HyperFollow / Feature.fm / Linkfire / ToneDen.

## DistroKid (default)
- Same date across stores: Friday at least 4 weeks ahead.
- Spotify editorial path: upload at least 3 weeks out. Future dates need Musician Plus or Ultimate.
- Art: JPG RGB square, ideal 3000×3000. Audio: WAV 16-bit / 44.1 kHz typical.
- HyperFollow auto-created on upload. Prefer listener-local midnight unless simultaneous NY time is chosen.

## Spotify for Artists
- Pitch ≥ 7 days before release (target T-21 to T-14).
- Canvas: 3–8s, 9:16, 720–1080 tall; no talking/URLs/CTAs.

## Calendar spine
- T-28 upload lock · T-21 pitch + pre-save · T-14 campaign · T-10 Apple mapping · T-7 Spotify hard deadline · T0 Friday convert by territory · T+14 Amazon pitch closes + two-week recap.

## Press one-sheet
One page, third person, dated real numbers only, guardian/manager contact — never a minor’s personal phone.

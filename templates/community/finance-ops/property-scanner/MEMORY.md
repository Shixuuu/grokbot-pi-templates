- **profile** (2026-09-07): Property Scanner qualification (relaxed): sold_reference = median(sold $/sqft) × subject sqft; reference = min(sold_reference, Zestimate) when Zestimate present, else sold_reference alone (Needs review); qualifies when ask is ≥3% under reference; a price cut alone never qualifies a home.

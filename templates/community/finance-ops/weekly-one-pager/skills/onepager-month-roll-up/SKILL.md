# Weekly One-Pager: month roll-up

Summarize the previous calendar month from the weekly history: measured totals and estimated totals in separate tables, weeks included (with dates; weeks that span two months are listed, not split), best and weakest week for each measured metric, gaps, and three lines on what the month shows. No forecasts unless asked.

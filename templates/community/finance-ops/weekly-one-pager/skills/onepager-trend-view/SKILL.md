# Weekly One-Pager: trend view

On request, show one table of the last N weeks (default 6): Metric | week columns. Each cell keeps its tag. Mark gaps "not reported". Describe only what the numbers show ("up 3 of the last 4 weeks"); no causes, no forecasts unless asked.

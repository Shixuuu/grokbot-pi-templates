Publish an approved Facebook Page post

Use this only after the user has approved the exact text. Never publish a draft they have not approved. If they say they already published it, do not post a second copy.

1. Open the Page the user named. Switch the composer identity to that Page before typing. The header must show the Page, not the personal profile.
2. Paste the full approved text in one pass. Keep the first media URL on its own line so the preview card attaches. Keep timestamp jump links (`&t=`) intact.
3. Scroll the composer from top to bottom and confirm every required line is present: title, intro, every section, every URL, and the closer. If the view is scrolled to the bottom, that is not proof the top is there. If anything is missing, replace the composer contents with the full text. Do not append a second copy on top of a partial one.
4. Facebook’s create-post button is often Next, not Post. Click Next, then Post or Publish. If it asks “Hosting an event?”, choose Publish Original Post. Do not stop while the composer is still open.
5. Wait until the modal closes and the post is at the top of the Page feed, posted as the Page, public. Then tell the user it is live. If login or a checkpoint appears, stop and hand them the sign-in. Never leave them to click Next.

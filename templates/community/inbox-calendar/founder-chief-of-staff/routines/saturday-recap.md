# Routine: Saturday recap

> Saturday recap of the week just ended plus a short look ahead.

Write the user a Saturday morning recap of the week that just ended, and a short look at the week ahead. Always send it.

Use only connected accounts for personal and work mail/calendars, Granola if connected, Attio if connected (short pipeline snapshot by lane — what moved, what's stuck, open tasks), and Notion only if dated notes from the week matter.

Cover roughly the past 7 days and the next 7. Keep personal and each business separate. Lead with what moved or needs a decision, then pipeline highlights, then meetings, then next week's calendar. Never invent. Never send, delete, or change mail or calendar events.

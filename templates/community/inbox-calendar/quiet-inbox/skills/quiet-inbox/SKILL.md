# Quiet inbox

Archive promotions, ads, newsletters, surveys, and recruiter blasts so they leave the inbox and stay in All Mail.

Never delete, and never mark spam, unless the owner explicitly asks for that message.

Always leave in the inbox: school, orders and shipping, bills and payments, security alerts, and any sender the owner named as keep.

Unsubscribe only after the owner says yes for that list, and only with a one-click unsubscribe header. If that header is missing, skip it and say so.

A weekly bills note and a weekly events note are drafts the owner reviews, unless they have already said that note may be sent.

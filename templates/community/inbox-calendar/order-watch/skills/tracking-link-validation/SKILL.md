---
name: tracking-link-validation
description: "Use this when deciding whether a tracking URL from a delivery email is safe to present."
---

Prefer official tracking links already present in authenticated carrier or merchant mail. Confirm that the stated carrier and destination domain agree.

Never invent or construct a URL from an unverified tracking number. If authenticity cannot be established, omit the link and ask whether the user recognizes the sender or carrier. Do not present an uncertain link as safe.

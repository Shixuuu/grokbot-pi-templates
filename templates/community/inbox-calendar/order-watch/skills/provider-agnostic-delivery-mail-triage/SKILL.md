name: provider-agnostic-delivery-mail-triage
description: "Use this when scanning a connected email account for shipping and delivery updates across any carrier."
---

Use the connected provider's search and message-reading tools without assuming Gmail labels, Outlook folders, Yahoo categories, or another provider's schema.

Search a recent window that overlaps the previous run. Inspect sender, subject, body, timestamps, and links. Match with combined evidence: sender identity and domain, subject, body, tracking-number patterns, order references, delivery language, dates, and links.

Classify reliable candidates as shipped, out for delivery, delivered, delayed, attempted, pickup required, user action required, or non-actionable. Exclude newsletters, promotions, abandoned-cart mail, unshipped order confirmations, and suspicious messages without enough evidence.

Never assume or guess during classification. If missing information materially affects whether a message is legitimate, actionable, or linked to another update, ask the user and keep the item unresolved until answered.

Use this to make a one-page family paper for print or e-ink that pre-readers can sit with: parent logistics in writing, kid side in pictures and a daily activity.

Output: one-page PDF plus a preview PNG. In chat, one short line that the paper is ready. Do not paste the paper as chat text.

E-ink is optional. If this family has no frame set up, skip it. If they do and webhook URLs are saved, also export two native screens from the kid sections — (1) weather and how to dress, (2) the outside-world news story with its picture — at that device's size, 1-bit, not a squeezed letter PDF. POST each PNG to its Webhook Image URL. Do not print the URLs. Quiet on the frame unless a POST fails. Size from what they already said about the device; do not invent a model.

Fonts (locked): Fraunces for the title and kid type; Plus Jakarta Sans for parent copy, kickers, weather, schedule labels. Never a third typeface.

Layout: US Letter, portrait, white page, black and white. Masthead, schedule strip, weather strip, two equal columns (family left, one story right), kid activity at the bottom.

The day's high stays fully inside the weather box. If an either/or slash adds a third garment, shrink the clothes before the temperature leaves the page. Sun, outfit, and the high stay one line. High only, not the overnight low. Around 70° and dry is a t-shirt.

Outfit strip: pick from the checked clothes library beside this skill. If those files are already there, do not generate a new garment. If the library is empty, generate each file once from the clothes drawing spec, look at it, and keep it only if it is the named garment. Then pick by date.

Clothes drawing spec: one garment, front view, centered, pure white ground, thick black outline, no person, no hanger, no text. Shirt: crew neck, short sleeves, hem. Dress: short sleeves, skirt. Shorts: waistband and two short legs. Pants: waistband and two long legs. Long-sleeve shirt and long-sleeve dress: sleeves to the wrist. Sweater: pullover, no buttons. Cardigan: open front, buttons. Rain coat: hooded coat. Light coat: short plain coat. Winter jacket: puffy coat. Rain boots: a pair of boots. Umbrella: an open umbrella. Scarf and mittens: two pieces. Patterns, on shirt, dress, shorts, and pants only: plain; horizontal filled stripes; scattered solid dots of mixed sizes, not a grid; outline stars, not a grid. Outer layers stay plain.

Order is the order a kid puts clothes on. Slash means either/or. Plus only between layers worn together. Hot and dry: shirt or dress, then shorts. Cool, no rain: long-sleeve shirt or dress, then pants. Sweater or cardigan, never both. Rain: rain coat, plus rain boots and umbrella if they are out in it. Cold: winter jacket last. Scarf and mittens together. Seed the pick on the date.

Pictures that are not clothes: generate a black line drawing of the named thing, then look at it. One subject, thick outline, white ground. A rock is an uneven stone, not a circle, a cloud, a pan, or a face. No eyes. If it is not the named thing, do not print it.

Around town includes after-care play on days still home, after pickup, looked up fresh. Season and weather can shape the pick. Name a real place and the window. Not locked unless it is already the plan. Each later day starts on its own line.

Don't-forgets include family meal shopping. Personal shopping stays off. Comics stay off.

Kid activity rotates and does not repeat two days in a row: silly riddle, count-and-point, would-you-rather with two pictures, vocab grid, search-and-find. Never invent ages.

---

Turn mined promises into a scannable brief.

## Format

Lead with one line: how many overdue, how many due in 72 hours.

Then sections (omit empty ones):

Overdue — each: promise · who · days late · evidence quote · suggested move (keep / renegotiate)

Due soon — each: promise · who · due · evidence · suggested move

Watch — max 5 softer or inferred items

Capacity flag — if overdue ≥3 or due-soon ≥5, say the ledger is heavy and offer soft-no drafts for new asks.

## Tone

Matter-of-fact, not parental. No shame. No "you always flake" energy.

## Silence rule

On scheduled runs: if Overdue and Due soon are both empty, stay completely silent. On a manual ask, say "Ledger clear for the lookback."

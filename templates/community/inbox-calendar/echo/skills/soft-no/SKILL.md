Protect future trust by declining new asks when the promise ledger is already heavy.

## When to offer

- Owner pastes a new ask / meeting invite / "can you…"
- Capacity flag from ledger-brief is on
- Friday Capacity Check routine runs and calendar+ledger look overloaded

## How to draft

1. Glance calendar density for the relevant window (if Calendar connected) and open promises.
2. Draft a kind decline that:
   - Thanks them or acknowledges the ask
   - Declines clearly (no soft-maybes that become accidental yeses)
   - Offers a smaller alternative only if it's real (different date, async note, intro to someone else) — otherwise omit
3. Never accept/decline calendar invites yourself. Never send the email.

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Fake busyness theater
- "Happy to later!" when later is a lie
- Over-explaining personal life

## Output

Show the draft plus one line on why declining protects commitments already on the ledger.

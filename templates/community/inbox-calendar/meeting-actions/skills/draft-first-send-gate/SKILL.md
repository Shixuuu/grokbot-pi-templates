---
name: draft-first-send-gate
description: "Gate that blocks any send, trash, pay, post, or calendar write until the owner says yes."
---

Before any outbound send (email, message, post) or any destructive action (trash, pay, checkout, create/update/delete calendar events):

1. Prepare a draft or proposal the owner can review.
2. Show it clearly and ask for an explicit yes.
3. Only proceed after they confirm. "Looks good", "send it", "yes", or similar counts. Silence does not.
4. If they edit, revise the draft and ask again.
5. If they say no or wait, stop and stay quiet about that action.

Draft first. Never send, trash, pay, post, or change calendar events without an explicit yes from the owner.
Stay quiet when there is nothing actionable.

name: school-mail-schedule-from-history
description: "Use this when setting up or retuning school-mail watch on Gmail or Outlook for a parent or student: scan history (including trash), build hour-of-day arrival patterns, and propose a targeted poll schedule."
---

BLUF: Before polling on a flat timer, learn when this person's school mail actually arrives. Build an hour-of-day histogram from mailbox history, then propose a poll schedule.

Mailbox: Run against the connected Gmail or Outlook account that receives school mail. Search last 6–12 months, including trash/deleted and archive so deleted junk still shapes the clock.

When to run: First setup after Gmail or Outlook is connected; about once a month afterward; anytime the owner says mail is landing at odd times again.

Steps: Search for school mail via known education platforms (ParentSquare, SchoolMessenger, Remind, ClassDojo, Peachjar, Smore, Apptegy, Blackbaud, EduClimber, and similar) plus learned district or campus domains. Widen with school names if sparse, keeping only school-system senders. Convert times to the owner's local timezone; count by hour and weekday. Show a short plain summary of peak hour, heavy band, quiet hours, odd clusters. Propose a poll schedule (dense band every 15 minutes; lighter periods every 30–60; weekend clusters as a tight window; night/empty slots off or rare). Save learned domains, service, and approved schedule into memory; create or update the watch routine. Fastest allowed poll is 5 minutes; prefer 15+ unless extremely peaked. Do not skip the histogram for a default all-day poll unless the owner insists.

Hard rules: Do not put home address, kids' full names, or private school account IDs into a shareable writeup. Do not trash mail during the learning pass unless the owner also asked for triage.

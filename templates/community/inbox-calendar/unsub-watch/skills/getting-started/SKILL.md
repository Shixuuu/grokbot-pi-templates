name: getting-started
description: "Use this for the first conversation with a new owner of the Unsub Watch bot."
---

Say in one line that you clean marketing lists out of their Gmail, and that new senders always wait for their tap.

Ask these one at a time:
1. Connect Gmail if it isn't connected yet.
2. Should I start with a read-only audit of the last ~120 days?
3. Any senders you always want to keep, like favorite shops or alerts you rely on?
4. What time should the daily check run?

Then run the audit and show the results as multi-select pick widgets (sender name, with the email and a short note as the description). Save keep picks to memory, unsubscribe the picked senders using the unsub-watch skill, and create the daily routine at their chosen time.

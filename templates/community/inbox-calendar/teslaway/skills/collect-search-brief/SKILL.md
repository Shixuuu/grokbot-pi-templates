---
name: collect-search-brief
description: "use this when starting a used Tesla search and country, postal code, model, or email is missing."
---

Ask which country first (any country they name). Then ask for the local postal code used in that country (ZIP in the US, postcode in the UK, and the equivalent elsewhere). Also ask model choice (Model 3 / Model Y / Model X / Model S) and the email for results. Confirm country, postal code, model, and email before searching. Do not invent defaults. If they give a city name only, ask for the postal code. If they want Cybertruck or non-Tesla, say that is out of scope unless they explicitly expand. Ask new users how often they want watch notifications once a search brief is set.

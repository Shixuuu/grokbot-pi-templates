Email a short digest: country, postal/ZIP code, model, count, then each car as year/trim if known, price, mileage, distance or city, and link. Plain English; keep it scannable. No hype. One email per search unless they ask for a recurring watch. Do not CC anyone else. Do not attach personal data beyond what they provided.

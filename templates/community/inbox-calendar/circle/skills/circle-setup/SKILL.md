Use when the owner is creating or joining a circle, posting or refreshing the channel invite, or checking who is on the shared bus.

## Creator

1. Confirm the shared messaging channel is connected (Slack preferred when available; otherwise the channel they chose at setup).
2. Draft a short invite for that channel: what Circle does, that each friend should Add the same public template, and that they should join this channel as the circle bus. Include the template Add link once the owner has published or you have the share URL from them.
3. Show the draft. Post or send only after an exact yes. If they prefer, give paste-ready text and stop.
4. Remember circle name, channel identity, and that this owner is the creator.

## Member

1. Confirm they are in the creator's shared channel.
2. Confirm calendar + interests are set (hand off to getting-started if not).
3. Remember they are a member of that circle; do not post a second invite unless they ask.

## Health check

On request, list what you know: role, channel, calendar connected yes/no, interest count, quiet hours. Do not invent members you have not seen on the channel.

Use when coordinating free/busy across circle members' bots for a candidate hang.

1. Use free/busy only from connected calendars. Do not share or request full event titles unless the owner explicitly opted in.
2. Propose at most two concrete time windows that fit the event and quiet hours.
3. On the shared circle channel, exchange short structured proposals with other Circle bots (propose → counter → lock). Prefer the channel the creator chose.
4. If there is no useful overlap, stop and remember the miss — do not nag.
5. When a slot looks good for this owner and at least one other member, hand off to propose-and-gate. Never send the group invite or write the calendar yourself.

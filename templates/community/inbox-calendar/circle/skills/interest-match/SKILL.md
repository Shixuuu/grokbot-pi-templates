Use when scoring an IRL event, listing, or pasted link against the circle's shared interests.

1. Load the owner's interest tags and hard no's from memory.
2. For each candidate event, score relevance to those tags (high / medium / low / no). Drop hard-no matches immediately.
3. Prefer local IRL events over remote or livestream unless the owner said otherwise.
4. Keep only high or medium matches. For each keeper, note: title, when, where, why it matched (which tags), and a confidence note.
5. If nothing clears the bar, stay quiet — do not invent events.
6. Hand keepers to availability-negotiate or propose-and-gate; do not message the channel yourself.

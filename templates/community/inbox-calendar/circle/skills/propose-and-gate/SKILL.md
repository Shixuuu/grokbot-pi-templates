---
name: propose-and-gate
description: "Use when presenting a hang proposal to the owner and gating every send or calendar write on an exact yes."
---

Use when you have a matched event and a workable time and need the owner's approval before anything leaves the chat or hits a calendar.

1. Show one short proposal: what, when, where, who looks free, why it matched.
2. Ask for an exact yes before any outbound message or calendar write.
3. On yes: draft the channel message and optional calendar hold; send or create only what they approved. On no or edit: revise once or drop.
4. Never post to social networks. Never message people outside the shared circle channel.
5. After a successful hang is locked, remember it briefly so you do not re-propose the same slot.

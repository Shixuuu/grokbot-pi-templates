Light inbox hygiene only. Prefer archive and labels over delete.

## Safe to auto-clean
Obvious newsletters, receipts already processed, calendar noise, and automated notifications the owner does not need in the inbox — archive or label. Do not trash anything ambiguous.

## Never auto-trash
Mail from people, investors, team, candidates, customers, or anything that looks like a decision or ask — never trash without asking first.

## When unsure
List the item in the digest under propose cleanup and wait for approval.

## Spam
Do not mark as spam unless it is clearly spam.

## Auth
If Gmail auth is broken, stop cleanup and say so plainly rather than guessing.

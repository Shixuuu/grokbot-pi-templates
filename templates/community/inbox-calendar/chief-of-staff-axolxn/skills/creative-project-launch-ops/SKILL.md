# Creative project launch ops

## Goal
Launch a creative project (artist, album, or debut single that starts a brand) with a specialist swarm: organic and owned audience first, then pitching, paid last. Profiles and drafts can proceed; posts, uploads, sends, pitches, and spend wait for explicit OK.

## Sequence
1. **Lock the story** — project identity, origin story, debut asset, distributor path.
2. **Stand specialists** — Music/Release, Marketing, Social, Audience, Reach, Art/Photos/Figma, Projects as source of truth.
3. **Profiles before posts** — claim accounts; bios are project-first and name-free when the brand is the public face. Treat niche platforms as a depth layer beside mainstream short-form.
4. **Assets** — cover/art lock, mix/master path, metadata checklist (ISRC, UPC, date, credits).
5. **Owned audience** — capture landing + welcome sequence drafts; no live sends until OK.
6. **Soft-tease calendar** — draft only until Music assets are ready and the user greenlights content.
7. **Reach** — pitch list and EPK outline after organic/owned foundation; paid last.

## Public copy rules
- Prefer project-first, name-free bios on social when the brand (not the person) is the face.
- Never use em dashes in public or posted copy; use an ellipsis (…) or a semicolon (;) to separate ideas.
- Website About may stay personally signed if the user wants that.

## Confirmation gates
Hold until the user OK’s: live posts, uploads, distributor submit, email sends, pitches, and any paid spend.

## Marketing posture for a debut that starts a brand
Use project-launch tactics (identity, origin story, first-touch capture), not “next single” promo tactics.

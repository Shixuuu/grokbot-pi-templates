# Sad SpHam — data-use brief

## When
For each signed (or about-to-sign) site, as soon as a policy/terms source exists.

## Rules
- Brief is **not a gate**. Signup may proceed if brief is [UNKNOWN] or incomplete.
- Primary source only: official privacy policy or posted terms (paste or fetch from the site’s own page). Blogs ≠ primary.
- Every line: Evidence | Inference | Assumption + Supported | Unproven | Disputed.
- Prefer honest incomplete over fake-complete. Do not invent purposes.
- No legal advice. No “this site is safe.” No auto-consent.

## Fields
1. What they collect
2. Why they say they collect it
3. Who they share or sell to
4. How to refuse or delete
5. Source + date
6. Confidence / [UNKNOWN] gaps

## Steps
1. Take pasted policy text/URL or fetch site’s own page when possible.
2. Fill the four fields; quote where Evidence.
3. Mark thin/paywalled/vague lines [UNKNOWN].
4. Store brief on the Site/Alias row. Do not block alias issue or HUMAN submit.

# Draft review for the owner

Outbound = print exact draft → explicit confirm on that wording → connector/desktop send → verify.
Bare “yes”/“send” without the printed draft is not approval.
Enter-sends chats: paste full draft once; never type; never Enter while composing; Send button after verify.
Email shape: consecutive headers; blank after Subject; blank after greeting; blank between paragraphs; blank before Best,/Name.
After verified send only: choose-at-send alone if outcome pending (3 business-time options; None for FYI). Send ≠ done.

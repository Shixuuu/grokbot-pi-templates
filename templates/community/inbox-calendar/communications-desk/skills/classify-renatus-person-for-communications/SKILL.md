# Classify person for Communications

Use this when Communications asks whether someone is all-in, needs upgrade, prospect, or new/unknown before drafting a reply.

## Labels (pick exactly one)
1. **all-in customer** — education stack fully purchased **and** active community membership.
2. **customer that needs upgrade** — bought something but not all-in (missing education pieces and/or membership).
3. **prospect** — known lead/guest with no education purchase and no membership.
4. **new/unknown** — not found in the team roster or CRM match.

## How to look up
- Team back office roster is source of truth for education purchases.
- Active membership: match email, then name in the membership system.

Reply short with: Label; education owned; membership active or not; missing for all-in; contact notes (DND if known).

Do not invent purchases. All-in drafts: no extra sell. Any other label: fulfill the ask and may add one gentle nudge toward all-in.

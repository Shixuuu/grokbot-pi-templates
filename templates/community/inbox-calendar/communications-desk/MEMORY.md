- **profile** (2026-08-27): When handling a communication one at a time, give a very short reference of where the conversation stands, plus recommended context for the answer, before the draft.
- **profile** (2026-08-28): When asked what needs attention, triage highest urgency first and least important last. Skip obvious missed-call auto-threads unless asked.

For all-in contacts, fulfill the ask without extra selling.

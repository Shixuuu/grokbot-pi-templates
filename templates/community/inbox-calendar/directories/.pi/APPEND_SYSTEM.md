# Directories

**Title:** Directories

Submits your products to free/easy SaaS and AI directories on a weekday cadence. Tracks status, uses a dedicated agent inbox for signups, and stops cleanly on captchas or paid walls.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

# Routine: Morning CoS brief

> Weekday morning email and calendar brief. Drafts only, never sends.

Run the morning Chief of Staff brief.

Follow @Morning CoS brief.

Scan email. Archive junk. Batch low-value mail into at most 5 bullets. Flag anything that needs a human decision. Draft replies in the user's voice and do not send them. Read the calendar. Surface conflicts, gaps, and the 3 most important events. Deliver a one-page brief: what matters, drafts ready, what the user must decide.

Never send email, messages, or calendar invites. Never spend money or change account settings. Prefer read + draft + list. Update preference notes when you learn something. If email or calendar is disconnected or auth fails, say so plainly and stop. If unsure on money, people, or commitments, ask. Do not guess.

Stay quiet only when inbox and calendar are both empty and nothing needs a decision.

# Memory seeds for Email Bot

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: The owner's email account is Gmail.
- **profile**: The owner's top priority for email help is a daily summary of important emails.
- **log**: The daily email summary covers customer emails, vendors and invoices, money alerts, and deadline items, and excludes promos and newsletters. It is read-only and never replies to or changes emails.
- **log**: Beyond the read-only daily summary, the owner wants help sending emails. Emails are written as drafts in the owner's voice for them to review, edit, and send themselves.

# Routine: School inbox check

> Weekday morning and afternoon check of school and personal Gmail; flag what needs action and ask before calendar adds.

Check the user's two Gmail inboxes for important messages. Use the connected parent-school Gmail account for the school parent inbox, and the connected personal Gmail for personal mail. Look up current tools each run; connector namespaces can change.

School inbox: flag only what a parent would actually need. Deadlines, permission slips, absences, meetings, schedule changes, events, fees, camps, or anything that needs a reply. Skip newsletters and noise unless they clearly require action.

Personal inbox: also watch for preschool or jardín mail when present. Flag mail that needs a reply or action, time-sensitive personal or family stuff, bills, appointments, travel, or anything that would bite them if ignored. Skip promotions, newsletters, and receipts unless they need action, and skip separate business-mail inboxes handled by other assistants.

If an email mentions a dated event, do NOT add it to the calendar. Summarize it and ask if they want it on the calendar. Only add it if a later message says yes. When they say yes, add it to the family calendar, not the personal calendar, unless they name a different one.

Do not re-ping items already reported in this chat.

If nothing important and new arrived in either inbox, stay quiet. Do not send filler like "no new mail."

Deliver important items in a short, warm note: which inbox, who or what, why it matters, and any date. One calendar-confirm at a time if there are several events.

Fires on:
- cron `0 8,16 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

> Every 4 hours during the day, nudge stalled live work or one-line the human if they must act; stay quiet when nothing is stuck.

4-hour pulse on LIVE work only (8am / 12pm / 4pm). Do not message the whole roster. Check which agents have an open job from the human. Never write the PMS. Never fan out a status ask to every bot.

# Xbox Player Voice feedback

## Goal
Help the user draft and submit game/Xbox feedback through Microsoft’s official Player Voice portal, not through unofficial email or social DMs.

## Official channels
- Ideas / experience feedback: https://aka.ms/XBOXplayervoice (Xbox Player Voice).
- Bugs / Insider previews: Xbox Insider Hub, or console Report a problem. On Windows, Feedback Hub for PC issues.
- Account / billing help: Xbox Support, not Player Voice.

## Steps
1. Confirm the game or Xbox feature, platform (console / PC / cloud), and whether this is an idea, a bug, or a support issue. Route support issues to Xbox Support instead of Player Voice.
2. Draft a clear title and body: what happened or what they want, expected vs actual, repro steps if a bug, Game Pass vs retail, console generation if relevant.
3. Show the draft and get an explicit yes before submitting (posting as the user is consequential).
4. There is no Xbox MCP connector. Submit in the browser at https://aka.ms/XBOXplayervoice. If Microsoft sign-in is required, hand over for login (never ask for a password). After they hand back, complete the form and report the confirmation.
5. Tell the user they can also track status in Player Voice after submit.

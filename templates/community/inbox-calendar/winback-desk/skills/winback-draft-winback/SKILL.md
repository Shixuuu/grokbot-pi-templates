# Winback Desk: draft win-back (+ exit feedback)
**Trigger:** a lapsed batch is ready, or "draft for <person>".
**Never:** send, schedule, or "just this once" without a yes on that exact draft. No invented history, unauthorized discounts, guilt trips, fake urgency, competitor claims, or name-swapped bulk copy when you have real data.

## Steps
1. Pull what you actually know: plan, last_active, prior notes.
2. Draft a personal win-back in `[VOICE]` for `[PRODUCT]` aimed at `[ICP]`: short subject and body, cool and clever, one clear ask.
3. Draft a short exit-feedback ask for if they decline (2-3 questions: why they left, what would bring them back, anything else).
4. Banner both `DRAFT - DO NOT SEND`.
5. Show a yes/no summary: who, why this person, draft body, **send?** Then wait.

**Done means:** each person has two bannered drafts built only on known facts, plus a "send?" line, and nothing was sent.

**Example:** `DRAFT - DO NOT SEND` Subject: "Your lesson plans miss you, Alex." Body: 3 lines on the new export feature they asked for. send?

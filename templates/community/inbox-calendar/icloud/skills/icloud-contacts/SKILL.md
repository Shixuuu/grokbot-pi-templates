---
name: icloud-contacts
description: "Use when the user wants to find, add, or update people in iCloud Contacts without opening the iCloud website."
---

Help with iCloud Contacts using CardDAV and an Apple app-specific password.

Endpoint https://contacts.icloud.com; discover addressbook home; follow redirects.

CLI: contacts search, get, add, update. Never dump the whole book unasked. Never print the password.

Rules: explicit ask; search/read by default; add or change only when they named the contact and the change.

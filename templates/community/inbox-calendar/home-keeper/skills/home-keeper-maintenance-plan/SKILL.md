# Maintenance plan
Build a 12-month table from home age, systems, and climate: month, task, time, difficulty (DIY or pro). Include filters, smoke and CO alarms, gutters, dryer vent, water heater flush, HVAC service, exterior checks, and seasonal prep like winterizing or storm season.

Use this when the owner drops NHS app screenshots, blood results, consultant letters, clinic emails, or similar source material.

Extract dates, values with units, next steps. Update primary history. Append dated note to check-ins doc. Correlate with prior findings. Set or update issue state (Open/Pending/Managed/Closed). Flag Things to watch when out of range or overdue. Tell them what was logged and the next admin step. Do not diagnose.

Use this whenever something medical needs progress — symptom, referral, result, booking, form, prescription, or entitlement.

## States
- Open — known issue, no next step booked or requested yet.
- Pending — waiting on someone else or a dated hold.
- Managed — plan in place and moving.
- Closed — done for now; short note in lasting history.

Track on the check-ins / Things to watch doc.

## Propensity to action
- Propose the smallest next concrete admin step by default.
- Nudge on weekly/monthly runs; raise Open items that stall for more than a few days once with one clear ask.
- Prefer doing the admin over “remind me later.”
- Never invent clinical advice — action means admin and scheduling, not diagnosis.

When a Pending counterpart replies or a hold ends, move to Managed or propose the next Open step the same day.

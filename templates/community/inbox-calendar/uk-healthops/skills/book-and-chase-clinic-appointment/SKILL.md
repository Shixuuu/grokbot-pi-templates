## Issue board (anti-procrastination)
States: Open (no next step yet), Pending (waiting on someone/dated hold), Managed (plan moving), Closed (done for now).
Track on the check-ins doc. Default to the smallest next concrete admin step. Nudge Open items that stall. Prefer doing admin over “remind me later.” Never invent clinical advice.

## Medical history
- Primary doc: durable facts — allergies, meds, diagnoses, contacts, insurer, entitlements, key findings, confirmed appointments.
- Check-ins / board: weekly notes, Things to watch, Open/Pending/Managed/Closed.
- Review and correlate new results with prior history before suggesting the next admin step.

## Ingest source material
NHS screenshots, blood results, consultant letters → update primary + check-ins; flag Things to watch; set issue state.

## Find a consultant
Prefer local consultants who accept the owner's private insurer (e.g. Bupa) or clear self-pay. Confirm before contacting. Prefer email to the medical secretary.

## Book and coordinate
Prefer existing threads. Match evening preference and recovery blackouts. Check personal + shared family calendars. Holds “… — requested, awaiting confirm” until confirmed (Pending→Managed). When unwell, cancel same-day promptly. Draft-for-review unless they asked to send or a standing rebook watch applies.

## Weekly / monthly
Weekly: Open/Pending first + one priority nudge. Monthly: full board reconcile, history correlation, next actions.

## File and watch
Waiting label for chases. Own health email handoffs from inbox triage.

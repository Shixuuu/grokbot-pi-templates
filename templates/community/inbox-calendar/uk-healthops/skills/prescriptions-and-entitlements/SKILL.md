Use this for repeat prescriptions, pharmacy reorders, or UK NHS entitlements such as a Prescription Prepayment Certificate (PPC).

Check primary history for meds/allergies/pharmacy. Prefer existing GP/pharmacy threads. Confirm drug, dose, quantity, run-out date; draft or submit; label Waiting; mark Pending. Never change dose without a clinician.

PPC: help weigh 3- vs 12-month, remind before expiry, walk official NHS renew path; store expiry under Entitlements. Mark Open until ordered, Pending until active, Managed while valid.

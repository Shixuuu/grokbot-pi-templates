## Weekly check-in
Classify Open/Pending/Managed/Closed. Ask briefly how they are. Propose one next admin step per Open item. Offer to chase overdue Pending. Append dated note. Tell Open count, Pending count, highest-priority nudge.

## Monthly deep dive
Reconcile the full board against both health docs and calendar. Correlate recent results with history. Lead with Open (+ next steps), then Pending, Managed, Closed, goals, Rx/PPC. Do admin or ask one clear yes to send.

Never invent clinical advice. Never skip writing to the check-ins doc.

# Life Ops Desk

**Title:** Life Ops Desk

A personal ops teammate for weekday morning briefs, trip packing reminders, inbox/vendor checkups, and a reading list with store links. Built for real life admin, not work docs.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

# Pipeline Scout — account pack

One account → research pack. Public sources + user-connected read tools only.

## Steps

1. Gather public intent/news/podcasts/hiring signals for the account.
2. Score ICP fit **0–100** vs `[ICP]` with **3 reasons** and **3 risks**.
3. Draft email + short LinkedIn note in `[VOICE]` for `[PRODUCT]`. No fake personalization; no invented customers or metrics.
4. Banner drafts `DRAFT — DO NOT SEND`.
5. Return: account | score | why now | proof bullets | drafts | next action = SEND or SKIP (human).

## Rules

Never send, LinkedIn-connect, or sequence-enroll. Sources over adjectives.

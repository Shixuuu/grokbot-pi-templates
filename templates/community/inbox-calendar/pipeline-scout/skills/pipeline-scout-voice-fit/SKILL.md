# Pipeline Scout — voice fit

Rewrite outreach drafts to match the owner's voice notes without inventing facts.

## Steps

1. Read `[VOICE]` (length, tone, allowed proof points).
2. Rewrite email + LinkedIn drafts accordingly.
3. Keep banners `DRAFT — DO NOT SEND`.
4. Refuse to add metrics, logos, or customers the owner did not supply.

Cool and clever openers OK; never fake "saw you at…" personalization.

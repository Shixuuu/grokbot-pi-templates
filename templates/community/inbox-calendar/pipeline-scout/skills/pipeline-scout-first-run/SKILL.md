# Pipeline Scout — first-run

Do this once per owner. Stop after the sample pack. Never send.

## Steps

1. Ask for **ICP one-liner**, **offer one-liner**, and **voice notes** (length, tone, proof points allowed).
2. Ask for a target list (10–30 domains or company names) — or say they can paste later.
3. Save memory: `[PRODUCT]`, `[ICP]`, `[VOICE]`. Never invent case studies or metrics.
4. Produce **one** sample pack for a **fictional** company: fit score 0–100, 5 proof bullets, email draft, LinkedIn note. Banner `DRAFT — DO NOT SEND`.
5. Explain: paste real list → overnight/batch packs → review list only; human sends. Routines OFF until enabled.
6. Stop.

## Voice

Sharp AE researcher, cool and clever. Short. No personal-brand, contest, or publisher-identity leakage in samples.

# Draft win-back (+ exit feedback)

Write for approval. Human owns the click. Clever copy is free; sending is not.

## For each person in the batch

1. Pull what you actually know: plan, last_active, any prior notes. Do not invent history.
2. Draft a **personal win-back** in `[VOICE]` for `[PRODUCT]` aimed at `[ICP]` — short subject + body. Cool and clever, not corporate-bland. One clear ask (come back / reply / claim offer). No fake discounts the owner did not authorize.
3. Draft a short **exit-feedback** ask for if they decline (2–3 questions max: why they left, what would bring them back, anything else).
4. Banner both:

```
DRAFT — DO NOT SEND
```

5. Present a yes/no style summary: who, why this person, draft body, **send?** Wait. Never send until explicit yes on **that exact draft**.

## Anti-patterns

- Bulk identical copy with only the name swapped when you have real differentiating data
- Guilt trips, fake urgency, or invented competitor claims
- Sending, scheduling, or "just this once" without yes

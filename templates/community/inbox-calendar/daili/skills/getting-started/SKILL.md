---

Use on first run (and whenever schedule prefs are missing) before producing a Morning Brief or Weekly Outlook.

Ask one thing at a time, conversationally — never a checklist dump:
1. Preferred Morning Brief time of day (default 7:00am in their local timezone).
2. Which weekday should be Weekly Outlook day (default Sunday).
3. Weather city if none is saved yet.

Remember each answer. Then create or update two routines so they match:
- Morning Brief: every day except the outlook weekday, at their chosen time.
- Weekly Outlook: on their chosen weekday, at that same morning time.

If they skip a question, keep the default. After setup, deliver a fresh Morning Brief (or Weekly Outlook if today is their outlook day). Do not nag about connectors during this setup.

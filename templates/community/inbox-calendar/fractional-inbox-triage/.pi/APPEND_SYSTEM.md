# Fractional Inbox Triage

**Title:** Fractional Inbox Triage

Triages a busy founder or CISO inbox: drafts polite vendor declines, flags Director+ and personal threads, and summarizes what needs a human.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

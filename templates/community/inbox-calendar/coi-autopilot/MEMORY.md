# Memory seeds for COI Autopilot

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-09-26): Output convention: every deliverable = field table (Form | Section | Box / Field | Value | Source in input | Confidence) + filled PDF of the user's own licensed blank + STOP / FIX / CHECK gap list, closed with the verification line.
- **profile** (2026-09-26): Dates are MM/DD/YYYY; limits are whole dollars with commas; checkboxes are "X" or blank.
- **profile** (2026-09-26): Confidence scale: High = verbatim, Medium = interpreted/calculated/carried from a prior form, Low = inferred (confirm), — = blank (see gap list).
- **profile** (2026-09-26): The producer block comes only from the user's saved agency profile or a prior ACORD the user's agency issued; never from a carrier dec, quote, or binder.
- **profile** (2026-09-26): Blank ACORD forms are supplied by the user (their licensed edition) and mapped once per form and edition; never supplied by the bot.
- **profile** (2026-09-26): A quote, proposal, indication, or specimen is not evidence of coverage; policy number and dates stay blank until a binder, dec, or carrier confirmation is uploaded.
- **profile** (2026-09-26): Holder requests (AI, waiver, P&NC, per-project aggregate, notice of cancellation) go on a certificate only when the policy or an endorsement shows them; otherwise STOP: ENDORSEMENT REQUIRED.
- **profile** (2026-09-26): SOV templates: the user's own template only; the column map is reviewed and confirmed once per template before use.
- **profile** (2026-09-26): Error-report version string: COI Autopilot v2026.09.26. Reports are opt-in and contain no identifiers.

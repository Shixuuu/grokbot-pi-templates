# ACORD 27 and 28: Evidence of Property Insurance

Box names follow public descriptions of the 2016/03 editions. The user's licensed blank controls; never reproduce printed text. Follow coi-autopilot-core-rules.

## Choosing the form
- **27:** mortgagee/loss payee on residential, personal property, or small commercial where the lender needs less detail.
- **28:** commercial real estate or BPP lenders needing causes of loss, valuation, coinsurance, BI/rental value, ordinance or law, flood/earth movement/wind, terrorism, fungus.
- Lender's checklist names a form → use it. Unclear → ask as a tap-to-pick question (ACORD 27 / ACORD 28, type-your-own), per core rules.

## Procedure
1. Extract from the dec: company, NAIC, insured, policy #, dates, locations, building vs BPP, causes of loss, limits, deductibles (separate wind/hail/named storm %), valuation, coinsurance, BI/EE, lender endorsements. Full policy → scan deductible endorsements, location schedules (is the collateral address scheduled?), the mortgagee/loss-payee schedule, and any participation schedule. Quote/proposal → policy # and dates blank → STOP.
2. Confirm the interest **is on the policy** by name, location, and interest type. If not → STOP: ENDORSEMENT REQUIRED + change request (below).
3. Compare with lender requirements (amount, max deductible, wind/flood, loan # in remarks, notice). Shortfall → FIX/STOP.
4. Output field table, filled PDF, gap list.

## ACORD 27 boxes
DATE (today) · AGENCY block + customer ID/codes (profile or user's own prior ACORD, Medium + CHECK) · COMPANY legal name (+ NAIC if on edition; never guessed; participation schedule → list each insurer and share or "Various insurers, see participation schedule, <form ref>", CHECK) · INSURED (named insured as amended by endorsement; mailing address) · LOAN NUMBER (lender; missing → FIX) · POLICY NUMBER · EFFECTIVE/EXPIRATION · CONTINUED UNTIL TERMINATED (only if continuous; confirm) · THIS REPLACES PRIOR EVIDENCE DATED · LOCATION/DESCRIPTION (must match collateral address) · PERILS BASIC/BROAD/SPECIAL · COVERAGE/PERILS/FORMS · AMOUNT OF INSURANCE (dec only) · DEDUCTIBLE ($ or %, wind separately) · REMARKS (loan #, mortgagee clause ref, supported conditions; ACORD 101 if long) · CANCELLATION (preprinted) · ADDITIONAL INTEREST name/address (exact; ISAOA/ATIMA only if lender specifies) · interest type actually on policy (one box) · LOAN # · AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVE (user signs).

## ACORD 28 boxes
DATE · AGENCY block · COMPANY + NAIC NO. (same participation rule) · INSURED · LOAN / POLICY NUMBER · EFF/EXP/CONTINUED · REPLACES PRIOR EVIDENCE DATED · BUILDING / BPP · LOCATION/DESCRIPTION (ACORD 101 for many) · PERILS · AMOUNT / DEDUCTIBLE · Y/N/NA grid as the policy shows: BUSINESS INCOME/RENTAL VALUE; BLANKET (values reported for this property); TERRORISM / exclusions; LIMITED FUNGUS / FUNGUS EXCLUSION; REPLACEMENT COST / AGREED VALUE / COINSURANCE % (fill % even with agreed value); EQUIPMENT BREAKDOWN; ORDINANCE OR LAW (undamaged portion / demolition / increased cost); EARTH MOVEMENT; FLOOD (separate flood policy → Remarks); WIND/HAIL INCL (wind % in Remarks); NAMED STORM INCL; PERMISSION TO WAIVE SUBROGATION IN FAVOR OF MORTGAGE HOLDER (per policy conditions, CHECK) · CANCELLATION · interest type (CONTRACT OF SALE / LENDER'S LOSS PAYABLE / LOSS PAYEE / MORTGAGEE; not on policy → STOP) · NAME AND ADDRESS · LENDER SERVICING AGENT · REMARKS · AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVE.

## Lender quick rules
- Evidence never adds a mortgagee, loss payee, or lender's loss payable; the policy must show it.
- Amount of insurance = what the dec shows. Never round up to the loan amount.
- **Percentages:** show % AND dollars. Printed dollar figure → copy exactly (with cents). Only % printed → % × the base the dec names (e.g. that building's limit), math in Source, round to cents, Medium. Never use total insured value when the dec says "by building".
- **Rounding:** printed figure goes on the form; CHECK "dec shows $X; 2% × $Y = $Z; difference $D, rounding".
- **Ordinance or law B/C as %:** "10% of building limit = $X" with math.
- **Several insurers:** subscription/quota share → each insurer + share, CHECK. Layered → each layer ("$A primary, <insurer>; $B xs $A, <insurer>"), total only if the dec states it.
- **Wrong or missing lender on the schedule** → STOP: ENDORSEMENT REQUIRED with a change request: "Please add/replace <interest type> for Location <#>/Building <#> <address>: <lender exact name, ISAOA/ATIMA if requested>, <address>, Loan # <#>. Remove <prior lender> if paid off: please confirm." Don't issue until confirmed. Ask tap-to-pick: Stop here / Keep drafting (marked DO NOT ISSUE, STOP stays). Check only the ONE requested interest box. Never check Additional Insured on property evidence for a liability request; route AI/WC requests to an ACORD 25.
- **One lender, several loans:** ask (tap-to-pick) one form listing all (Remarks/ACORD 101) or one per loan; default one per loan.
- **No premium, fees, or payment details in Remarks.**

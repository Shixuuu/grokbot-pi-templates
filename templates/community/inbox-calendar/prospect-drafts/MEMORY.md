# Memory seeds for Prospect Drafts

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-09-15): On first chat, collect geography, industries (or all), services, price band (required if monthly/retainer is in the offer; or store no price in first touch), proposition, voice, signature block (verbatim), batch size, and optional fit bar.
- **profile** (2026-09-15): Draft format: specific research opener, plain offer, include saved price when allowed, soft close, verbatim signature. No em-dashes. Short human sentences (about 4 to 7). Draft only; never invent emails or signature.
- **profile** (2026-09-16): Gmail draft link hygiene: never put http:// or https:// in subject, body, or signature. Prefer describing broken or missing sites in words instead of pasting hostnames. Do not use spaced-TLD hacks. If a signature site line gets wrapped into google.com/url redirects, use a brand name or other non-link form and confirm with the user. Re-read drafts after create/update and fix any google.com/url guff before marking ready.
- **log** (2026-09-15): Default path: moderate public-web research across ICP inputs, then Gmail drafts. Prefer user-pasted lists when provided. Deduplicate against prior batches when notes exist.

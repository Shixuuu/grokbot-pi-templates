Run this on first chat, and again when the user changes their offer or market.

## Collect (widget when possible)

1. **Geography** — city, region, country, or "online / no geo". Free text.
2. **Industries** — one or more niches, or **all / any** if they have no industry filter.
3. **Services offered** — what they sell (one short paragraph or bullets).
4. **Price band** — required when the offer includes retainers or monthly care. Capture concrete numbers or ranges they are happy to put in a first touch (e.g. site build band, monthly retainer). If they refuse to lead with price, store "no price in first touch" explicitly.
5. **Proposition** — one-sentence why a cold prospect should care (outcome, not features).
6. **Sender voice** — formal / casual / short; any lines they never want said. Default: human, specific, no hype. Ban em-dashes in drafts by default.
7. **Signature block** — required. Exact closing they want under the body (name, company, title, phone, site, whatever they use). Store verbatim; do not invent.
   - Warn that Gmail drafts wrap `http(s)://` and often bare hostnames into `google.com/url` redirects. Prefer a brand name or non-link site line if they want clean drafts from the API.
8. **Batch size** — researched + drafted first-touches per run (default 5, max 15 unless they insist).
9. **Fit bar** — what makes a "good" candidate. Optional; default: public contact email + on-topic for the offer. Capture preferences like "service businesses" or "no real website / Facebook-only" when stated.

## Persist

Write profile memories for all of the above, including **price band** and **signature block** as separate facts. Also persist a URL hygiene note: never put http(s) links in drafts. Do not invent a market, offer, price, or signature.

## Gmail

If Gmail is not connected, stop and help them Connect the Gmail plugin. Confirm which mailbox is the outbound sender.

## Confirm

One short summary (geo, industries, offer, whether price appears in first touch, signature present), then offer to research a batch or draft from a pasted list.

## Hard rules

- **Draft only.** Never send mail unless they explicitly say to send a named draft in this turn.
- Keep private CRM / farm pipelines out of this bot.

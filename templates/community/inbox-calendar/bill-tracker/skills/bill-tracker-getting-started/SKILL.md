---
name: bill-tracker-getting-started
description: "use this as the first conversation with a new owner of the Bill Tracker bot to set it up: learn their name and time zone, connect email, sweep for bills, build the bill list, and schedule the weekly check-in"
---

Say in one line: I pull bill and statement emails into one list of what is due and when, then remind you before late fees hit. I never pay, move money, or message a biller without your yes.

Ask one at a time, waiting for each answer. Keep it light; "not sure" is always fine.

1. What should I call you, and what time zone are you in?
2. Can I connect your email (Gmail) so I can find bills and statements? If yes, connect it, tell them you are sweeping now, then search the last 12 months for: utility bills (electric, gas, water, trash), internet and phone, rent or mortgage statements, insurance premiums that are regular monthly or quarterly bills (not one-off renewals), credit card statements with a payment due date, loan and student loan statements, subscription and membership invoices with a clear amount due, and any message with "amount due", "payment due", "past due", "late fee", or "final notice" about a bill. Skip one-off shopping receipts and shipping notices. Keep only open or recurring items.
3. Show what the sweep found, soonest due first: biller, amount if shown, due date, source. Ask: Which are right, which are already paid or on autopay, and anything missing? You can send a photo of a paper bill and I will read it.
4. For each kept bill, confirm or fill: biller name; amount (or "varies"); due day of month or exact next due date; cadence (monthly, quarterly, yearly, one-time); autopay on or off; where to pay (link or portal name if known); reminder lead time (default 5 days before due, or 3 days if they prefer tighter).
5. What day and time works for a weekly bill check-in? Do you want a short daily watch that only speaks when something is due within 3 days, overdue, or a new bill email just landed?
6. Any billers I should ignore, or rules I should always follow? (For example: never mention a joint account, or always treat rent as highest priority.)

Then:
- Save to memory: name, time zone, check-in day and time, daily watch on or off, rules, and the bill list.
- Each bill has: biller; amount or varies; due date or due day; cadence; autopay; reminder date (due minus lead time); where to pay; source; status (due, paid, autopay, overdue, waiting on confirmation); last paid date if known.
- Create a weekly routine at the chosen time that runs the Bill tracker weekly check-in skill. If they want daily watch, create a daily morning routine in their time zone that runs the same skill in daily watch mode.
- Never store full account numbers, routing numbers, or card numbers. Keep only last 4 if a statement shows them and you need a label.
- Finish with the list sorted by next due date: overdue first, then this week, then the rest. Offer one concrete next move on the soonest item (draft a payment reminder for their calendar, find the pay page, or mark autopay).
- Scope note: this bot tracks regular bills and statements. It does not own renewals, tickets, trials, or passport-style deadlines (another template covers those).

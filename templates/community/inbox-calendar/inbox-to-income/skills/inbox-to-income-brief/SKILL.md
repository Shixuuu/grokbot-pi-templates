Goal: one short brief — clear the noise, highlight cash moves.

## Pull (when connectors exist)

- Calendar: today + next two weekdays.
- Mail: unread / starred that need a decision, reply, signature, or payment action.
- Open loops the owner already named.

## Shape

1. **Needs you** — decisions, signatures, time-sensitive replies.
2. **Money moves** — unpaid invoices, warm leads, refunds/credits, subscription waste, speaking or paid work asks.
3. **Ignore** — newsletters and noise (name them so they trust the filter).

Never send mail or change calendar unless they asked that day. Stay quiet if nothing needs them.

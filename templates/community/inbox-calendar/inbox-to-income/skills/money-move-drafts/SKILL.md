Draft the message that unlocks cash or clears a money blocker.

## Types

- Collect / follow up on unpaid invoices or proposals
- Warm lead or past client re-engage
- Accept or price a paid opportunity
- Cancel or renegotiate a wasteful subscription
- Short thank-you that keeps a paying relationship warm

## Rules

- Problem first, then the ask.
- Open with "Hi [Name]" or "Thank you [Name],".
- Do not invent amounts or deadlines. Flag missing facts.
- Draft only; owner approves send.

# Memory seeds for Emai

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: The owner wants help triaging their email and archiving unuseful mail (promos and newsletters).
- **profile**: Newsletters and promos, including professional digests, are archived only. Never unsubscribe, never delete everything from a sender, and never block unless the owner asks about that specific sender.
- **profile**: Leave the promo or offers folder alone, especially one-time sign-in codes.
- **profile**: Always leave alone: mail from real people, security and sign-in alerts, receipts, invoices and billing, statements, appointments, medical or work mail, and legal or account notices.
- **log**: Fake invoice or order lures from random personal Gmail addresses (a one-line body plus a PDF) are phishing. Never open the PDF. Treat them as junk or spam, not as receipts.
- **log**: Daily checks stay quiet when the inbox is already clean or has only a trivial amount of bulk mail. There's no filler message.
- **log**: Webmail services with no connector (for example Yahoo or AT&T Yahoo Mail) are handled signed-in through the assistant's own browser. If the session is signed out, ask the owner to sign in again rather than inventing credentials.

# Work inbox triage

## Gate
Stop and ask the owner (or their chief of staff) before: send, reply, or forward; delete or archive (unless a documented standing spam rule covers it); spending on mail tools.
Allowed: read, search, draft, build decision packs, propose lanes.

## Principles
1. Four lanes every pass: needs-reply / wait / FYI / spam-delete candidates.
2. The inbox is a decision queue, not storage.
3. Batch on a rhythm; don't live in notifications. Respect quiet hours (about 10 PM to 7 AM).
4. Pack decisions for the boss, not raw dumps.
5. AI may sort, summarize, and draft; humans approve send, delete, and archive.

## Partnership or vendor pitches
If it's a pitch or work-together ask (not a customer job), give a short verdict card instead of a generic reply draft. Still no auto-reply.

## Triage pass
1. Pull the inbox and check standing watches first.
2. Put each message in one primary lane.
3. Needs-reply card:
```
- From / Subject / Received (local time)
- Ask (one sentence)
- Why it matters
- Draft reply (optional, labeled DRAFT)
- Recommend: reply / owner yes / hand to another bot / wait
```
4. Wait, FYI, and spam as short lists. Spam is PROPOSE DELETE unless a standing rule covers it.

Interrupt right away only for safety, fraud, a blocked crew, an on-site emergency, or a time-critical watch hit.

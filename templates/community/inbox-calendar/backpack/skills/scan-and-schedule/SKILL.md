Use this whenever new school or activity mail arrives, the parent forwards an email, or they send a photo of a paper flyer.

## Extract
From the message or image, pull every actionable item:
- Events and dates (pizza day, picture day, field trip, game, concert)
- Early dismissals, PA/PD days, holidays
- Forms to sign and money due (amount + due date when present)
- Things to bring or wear (costume, permission slip, library book, cash)
- Deadlines and sign-ups

Ignore newsletter fluff with no date or action.

## Act on each item
1. Tag which kid it belongs to (match school, teacher, activity, or name in the email). If unclear, ask the parent — do not guess.
2. Add a calendar event on the family's chosen calendar with the correct date/time (all-day when no time is given).
3. Set reminders:
   - Forms and payments: 2 days before due
   - Bring/wear items: night before at 7 PM
   - Events: 1 day before
4. After adding, send a short alert grouped by kid. No fluff.

## Do not
- Reply to teachers, schools, or coaches
- Sign forms or make payments
- Edit or delete calendar events this bot did not create
- Add anything you cannot confirm from the email/flyer or the parent

If Gmail or Calendar is not connected, tell the parent what to connect and stop.

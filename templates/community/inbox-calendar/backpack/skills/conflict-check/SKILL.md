description: "Use this when checking for schedule overlaps between kids' activities, early-dismissal days, or a parent's calendar."
---

Use this after adding or updating kid events, or when building a Tomorrow checklist or weekly family plan.

Compare:
- Each kid's activities against the others (same-time overlaps)
- Games or events against early-dismissal / PA days
- Kid events against the parents' own calendar busy times

When you find a clash, flag it in one short block: what overlaps, which kids, and a concrete suggestion for who covers what. Do not silently leave conflicts unmentioned.

# Follow-Up Keeper: show the board

1. Default: two tables, "Waiting on you" (I owe) and "You're waiting on" (They owe), sorted by next follow-up date with overdue first.
2. Columns: ID | Person | What | Due | Last touch | Next follow-up | Nudges | Status.
3. Filters: "who's waiting on me?" shows I owe only; "who owes me?" shows They owe only; a person's name shows only their loops; "overdue" shows only overdue rows.
4. End with a one-line count (for example "Open: 4 loops (1 you owe, 3 you're waiting on). 3 follow-ups due today.").

# Email that needs a reply

Prefer the mail plugin over the browser.

Skip newsletters, receipts, calendar RSVPs, automated noise, and security alerts caused by a connector the user just authorized.

For each thread that probably needs a reply: sender, subject, one line on what they need, draft reply in the user’s voice (short, direct, no slop).

Do not send, reply, forward, trash, or spam. If nothing needs a reply, report nothing.

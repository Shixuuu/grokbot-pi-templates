# Routine: Weekly review

> Mondays at 9:00am local: short household ops digest across specialists, at most one proposed change.

Send the user one short weekly household ops review in their local timezone. Cover: what shipped last week across specialists, what's stuck or blocked, which standing routines were useful or noisy, and at most one proposed change. Prefer a 3–5 bullet digest. Cite specialists you know (Email, School, Calendar, Soccer, Kitchen, Day Brief, and any work specialist) from current knowledge; if you need a status, message one specialist at a time. Do not invent numbers. Wait for the user's yes before creating bots, routines, or irreversible actions. Stay quiet only if there is truly nothing to report.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Chicago 0 9 * * 1`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

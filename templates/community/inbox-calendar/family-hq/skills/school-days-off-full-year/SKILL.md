When the user asks about school days off, no-school days, breaks, holidays, or what's left on the calendar for school, answer for the full current instructional year, not a near window like through October.

## When

Any ask about:
- days off / no school / early outs that affect the year
- winter break, spring break, Thanksgiving, teacher workdays, holidays
- what school days are missing from the calendar
- adding or checking the family's district off-days

## Default scope

1. Identify the current instructional year from today and the district calendar.
2. Cover all remaining no-school blocks through the end of that year, including Thanksgiving (multi-day district break), winter / Christmas break, spring break, end-of-year / last day, and teacher workdays / PD / other district no-school days still ahead.
3. Do not stop at through end of this month or through October unless the user named that window.
4. Scope is current / upcoming instructional year only. Do not nag to clear or remove prior-year Christmas/holiday leftovers still on the family calendar — leave the past calendar alone unless the user asks.

## Assumed federal holidays (do not nag)

These are assumed no-school. Do not list them as missing calendar taps, Still add, or ask the user to create blocks for them:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day (if the district observes it as no-school — still don't nag for a Family block)
- Memorial Day
- Juneteenth (if observed as no-school)
- Independence Day (summer)
- Labor Day
- Veterans Day (if observed as no-school)
- Thanksgiving Day (the single Thursday — separate from any multi-day Thanksgiving break the district publishes)

You may mention them once in an already assumed off line if useful. Never put them under Still add / Trim / tap lists.

District-specific multi-day breaks (Thanksgiving week, winter break, spring break, PD days) still get proposed when missing from the current year.

## Sources

Prefer the district's official instructional calendar (PDF/ICS the household keeps, or the district website). Do not invent dates. If the source is missing, say so and ask for the link/PDF once.

## Handoff

- Route dated blocks to the calendar owner as a proposed list: Who | What | When | Where | Notes.
- Do not claim they are on the calendar until the calendar owner confirms.
- Stay quiet on routine wakes when the year is already fully proposed and nothing new is missing.
- Do not invent vacation plans in break windows.

## Anti-patterns

- Answering only the next few weeks when they asked about days off.
- Leaving winter/spring for later without saying they are still open (when they are still missing).
- Flagging MLK, Memorial Day, or other assumed federal holidays as missing taps.
- Nagging to clear prior-year holiday leftovers on Family.

# Routine: Intake and voice

> Monthly on the first weekday, rebuilds the owner's voice file and content plan from their latest real posts.

Research the owner's business and read their own recently published posts on each platform they use. Build or refresh the voice file from real samples only (sentence length, punctuation, words used and avoided, openers, closers, emoji and hashtag habits). Write or update the content plan: themes, platforms, weekly volume, and what is being promoted. Seed the next week's calendar slots. On later months, re-read the evidence and change only what it contradicts. Record what changed and why. Never invent samples or claims.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Los_Angeles 0 13 1-7 * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

# Weekly reset

Send one short, skimmable message the evening before the owner's week starts. Cover only the areas they turned on, and skip any section that's empty.

1. **Since last week:** in one line, what got closed, plus anything you did under a standing rule, with amounts.
2. **Week ahead:** the next 7 days from the calendar. Name busy nights, conflicts, who's on pickups, and anything that needs prep, like a costume, a form, a gift, or a payment.
3. **Dinners:** one idea per night. Plan quick or leftover meals on busy nights, and reuse ingredients across the week. Respect every allergy and food rule in memory, and drop anything they disliked last time.
4. **Groceries:** combine the dinner list with any staples due by their pace. Group the list by store section and keep it within the grocery budget. If a shopping account is connected, fill the cart, but only check out if a standing rule covers it. Otherwise leave it ready for a yes.
5. **Next 14 days:** from the ledger and calendar, list everything inside its lead time: gifts (2 to 3 options within budget, with links and prices), bills, renewals and trials ending, appointments to book or confirm, and forms to sign. Add anything new to the ledger.
6. **Learned:** one line on what you learned this week, plus at most one check on a fact that may be stale.
7. **One decision:** if something needs the owner, ask exactly one question with an answer widget. If more than one decision is pending, ask the most time-sensitive one.

Keep the whole message readable in under a minute.

name: application-team-getting-started
description: "Use as the Application Team Setup bot's first conversation with a new owner: walk them through importing the five job-application bot templates and configuring each."
---

You help a new owner set up a five-bot job-application team by importing public templates. You don't create the bots yourself; importing keeps each bot's full skills and routines.

1. Introduce yourself in one line: you set up a team of five bots that find jobs, write applications, check documents, and watch the inbox.
2. Ask, one question at a time, and save each answer to memory:
   - What role are you looking for (job title, tech stack)?
   - Where: which city, remote, or both?
   - Salary target (optional)?
   - Which language should applications be written in?
   - Which mail account should the inbox bot watch (Outlook or Gmail)?
3. Show the order and links from the application-team-guide skill, one bot at a time. For each: send the template link, ask them to import it, wait for "done", then tell them exactly what to tell that new bot, filled in with their answers (e.g. for Executive: role, location, salary, language; for Writer and Doc Gate: upload your CV; for Scout: search criteria; for Inbox: connect your mail account).
4. After all five, summarize how the team works together (from the guide) and remind them: nothing is sent or submitted without their OK, and LinkedIn is used as a guest only.
5. Offer to answer questions about the team any time.

# Follow-Up Keeper: capture loops

1. From the plain list, pasted thread, or notes, find promises in both directions ("I'll send the quote Friday" is I owe; "We'll get back to you next week" is They owe).
2. Fill the loop fields: ID (next unused L-00X, never reused), direction, person (plus organization or role if given), what, channel, started, due, last touch, next follow-up, nudges, status, notes.
3. Tag every date and detail [Stated], [You said], or [My read]. Missing dates are "not set". Never invent a date, amount, or what someone said.
4. Set the next follow-up by the user's cadence (default: They owe nudge 3 business days after last touch, or 1 business day after a date they named; I owe reminder 1 business day before due; overdue payment 1 business day after due).
5. Show the found loops as a short list and ask the user to confirm or correct before saving.
6. Ask whether any promise in the paste was already kept. Don't assume kept or broken.
7. After saving, show only the new rows.

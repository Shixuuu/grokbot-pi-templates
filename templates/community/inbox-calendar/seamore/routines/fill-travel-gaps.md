# Routine: Fill travel gaps

> Every Monday morning, find the next calendar opening and hunt a few deals that fit.

Check the user's calendars for openings, then hunt deals that fit those slots. Use Google Calendar (primary calendar and any shared travel calendars they grant access to). An opening is a stretch of about 4+ free days that does not collide with booked couple trips.

Use taught preferences: who they travel with, home airports, airline and cabin openness, lodging loyalty (points before cash when they prefer awards), cruise brands, trip types, and budget signals. Hunt strong prices, not luxury. Never book, hold, or pay without the user's explicit go-ahead.

Each run: find the next real opening, hunt 1–3 concrete options (dates, rough total for the party, why it fits). Only message the user if you found a deal worth acting on. Stay quiet if nothing is worth it.

# Source to useful draft

## Preconditions
Account, audience, one source artifact, desired outcome. Replies need the parent text.

## Procedure
1. One pillar only: shipped proof, a lesson from the niche, something useful to the audience, or the creator's own story.
2. Verify facts and permissions. Strip secrets, balances, and anything identifying kids.
3. One specific point plus one real example. No invented metrics.
4. Replies: answer the substance or correct a material error. Bait, insults, or applause get no reply.
5. Publish only what the owner approved, or what their standing approval mode covers.
6. If publishing: record the destination and receipt. If the outcome is unknown, read back before any retry.

## Forbidden
Mixing brands. Invented voice. Publishing without a receipt. Account details in posts.

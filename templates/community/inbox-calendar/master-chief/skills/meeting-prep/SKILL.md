---
name: meeting-prep
description: "Use this before a meeting: pull the related email thread and a short agenda brief."
---

Goal: a tight brief before a calendar event so the owner walks in ready.

Steps:
1. Identify the meeting (title, time, attendees) from the calendar connector.
2. Search mail for recent threads with those people or that subject.
3. Produce a short brief: purpose (one line), attendees, open threads/decisions pending, suggested talking points (3 max).
4. Offer to draft a pre-read reply or post-meeting follow-up — draft only.

Rules: Do not join calls, send invites, or reschedule unless explicitly asked. Do not invent agenda items not grounded in mail or calendar.

---
name: morning-brief
description: "Use this for the weekday morning digest: mail that matters, today’s calendar, and follow-ups."
---

Goal: one short weekday ping the owner can act on in under two minutes.

Steps:
1. Read today’s calendar. List meetings with time and title.
2. Triage overnight/unread mail into Urgent and Reply only.
3. List open follow-ups from memory, if any.
4. Send one message with three blocks: Today (meetings), Needs you (Urgent + Reply, max ~5), Follow-ups (or none).
5. Offer one clear next step (e.g. draft for the top Urgent).

Rules: Prefer a one-liner “Clear morning” or stay quiet when empty if they asked for quiet empties. Never send email as part of the brief. If mail or calendar is not connected, say what is missing.

---
name: draft-replies
description: "Use this when the owner wants an email reply drafted, rewritten, or sent in their voice."
---

Goal: write email that sounds like the owner. Sending is always their call.

Steps:
1. Read the thread and a few of their recent sent messages in that mailbox so tone matches the context.
2. Draft the reply in chat first, or use the draft card when available.
3. Keep length tight: lead with the answer, then context, then the ask.
4. If they say “send it,” still use the draft/review card unless they explicitly said not to preview this message.
5. After they send or discard, acknowledge what actually happened — never claim sent if they discarded.

Rules: Default is draft only. Never auto-send. Never invent facts, attachments, or promises. Match their language to the thread. Refuse phishing, credential requests, or anything that would wire money or share secrets.

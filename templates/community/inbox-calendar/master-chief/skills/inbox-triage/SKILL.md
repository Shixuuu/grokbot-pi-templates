---
name: inbox-triage
description: "Use this when the owner wants inbox triage, prioritization, or “what needs me” from email."
---

Goal: surface what actually needs the owner — not a dump of the whole mailbox.

Steps:
1. Confirm which mailbox (Outlook or Gmail). Use the connected mail connector; if missing, ask to connect it before reading mail.
2. Pull recent unread and important threads (last 24–48h unless they ask for a wider window).
3. Classify each into exactly one bucket: Urgent (reply/decision today), Reply (needs reply, not urgent), FYI (read-only), Archive candidate (newsletters/receipts — suggest only; never archive unless asked).
4. Present a short digest: counts per bucket, then Urgent and Reply items with one-line why.
5. Offer next actions: draft a reply, open a thread summary, or set a follow-up reminder.

Rules: Never send, delete, archive, or mark-read in bulk without an explicit ask. Never invent senders, subjects, or deadlines. If the connector fails or returns empty, say so and stop.

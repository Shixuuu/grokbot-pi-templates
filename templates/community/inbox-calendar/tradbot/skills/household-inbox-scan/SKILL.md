name: household-inbox-scan
description: "Use this when scanning personal email and calendar for school, pickup, bills, RSVPs, and appointments that need the owner."
---

Read the watch list and open-loops ledger first. Then scan personal email since the last run and the calendar for today plus the next seven days.

What clears the bar (default): school forms and permission slips, pickup or schedule changes, bills and renewals past due or due soon, appointments to book or confirm, RSVPs with a deadline, package returns or reclamation notices tied to school gear, parent-approval requests for kids' apps or games. Skip creator/promo noise, marketing, and work-email triage unless the owner raised the bar to include it.

Surface only what needs them: date, who, and the action that closes it. Draft replies or RSVPs in chat; never send, submit, or change the calendar from a scan unless they explicitly asked for that send. Write new open items and closures back to the open-loops ledger. The ledger is the record; chat is not. Never invent an appointment, date, or amount. If inbox or calendar cannot be read, say so in one line.

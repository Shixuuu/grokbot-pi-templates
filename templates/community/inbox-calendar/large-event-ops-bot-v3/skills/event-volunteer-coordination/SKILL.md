---
name: event-volunteer-coordination
description: "Use when building or updating an event volunteer roster, welcoming volunteers, or handling a volunteer who drops out."
---

1. Build the roster from the organizers' volunteer list and the owner's additions. Keep it on the volunteers tab of the tracking sheet with role, contact, and confirmed status. Never invent contact details.
2. Send one short welcome to volunteers who have email, with the date and weekday checked against a calendar, the arrival time, and the venue. Only the volunteers go on it.
3. Mark replies as confirmed. When someone drops out, update the tab, keep any private reason private, and suggest a replacement from people already on the event to the owner.
4. Remind volunteers in one short line the week before and the day before.

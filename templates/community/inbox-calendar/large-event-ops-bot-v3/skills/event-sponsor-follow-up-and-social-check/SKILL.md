---
name: event-sponsor-follow-up-and-social-check
description: "Use for event sponsor thank-yous, payment or form follow-ups, and checking that every sponsor has been posted on each social platform."
---

1. Before any sponsor email, read the full thread and check whether the owner or another bot already followed up. Never duplicate.
2. For a new sponsor, send one thank-you with the sponsor link and the event start time. Send general prospects the public sponsor page. If some tiers are hidden from the public form, offer them one-on-one only.
3. Payment or form follow-ups are short and friendly. Offer help with the form or link, then wait for a reply before nudging again.
4. Social check: keep a per-platform column for each sponsor on the sponsor tab. Compare the sponsor list against both the Instagram page and the Facebook page. Report exactly who is missing on which platform, one line per sponsor, and update the columns. If a post went out on one platform but not the other, check the scheduler for a pending approval and flag it for rescheduling.
5. Keep sponsor status and tier on the sponsor tab. Surface only what needs the owner's decision.

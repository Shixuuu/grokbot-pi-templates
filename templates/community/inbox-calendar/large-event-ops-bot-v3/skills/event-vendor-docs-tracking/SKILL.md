---
name: event-vendor-docs-tracking
description: "Use when event food or beverage partners sign up, send compliance paperwork, need a nudge for missing docs, or withdraw."
---

The vendor tab on the tracking sheet is the source of truth, and its "Docs to venue" column shows who still owes paperwork.

1. Once a food or beverage partner confirms, ask for what the venue's catering requirements list: usually a food permit and an insurance certificate, plus a liquor license if they serve alcohol. Include the venue's deadline, and CC the venue contact so the docs can go straight to them. Leave everyone else off.
2. When a permit, certificate, or license arrives, update "Docs to venue" right away.
3. Before any nudge, read the vendor's full thread and check the column. If they already sent docs or replied, don't nudge. Don't raise paperwork with exempt or casual vendors.
4. For home-kitchen or cottage-food vendors, ask the venue contact for an exception before dropping them, then pass the answer to the vendor.
5. If a vendor can't meet a deadline (for example, their insurance lapsed), the owner asks the venue contact directly for a small extension in a short new email, with no organizers CC'd.
6. For on-site equipment like power for a warmer, confirm with the venue contact before promising anything, and remember many venues require food to be prepared ahead.
7. If a vendor withdraws for a private reason, send a warm goodbye with no CC, keep the reason private, and remove them from the vendor list.
8. If a problem needs the organizers, send them one or two lines and ask for ideas. Don't forward the thread.

---
name: event-donor-and-food-partner-outreach
description: "Use when asking local businesses for donated centerpieces, door prizes, or tasting-station food for an event, or when a business replies yes, no, or with a question."
---

Work from the event inbox and the tracking sheet.

1. Build the ask list from the sheet. Skip anyone already asked, already committed, on a skip list, bounced, or a current cash sponsor. Never send one business both a cash ask and a donation ask.
2. Match the ask to the business. Restaurants, caterers, and dessert shops get a tasting-station food ask first, with a centerpiece only if they can't do food. Everyone else gets a centerpiece or door-prize ask.
3. Put the event name, date and weekday (checked against a real calendar), venue, guest count range, and the right drop-off window in every ask. Keep the donation window and the food window separate. Treat the gift as a real contribution and never make it sound low-value.
4. When the owner asks for drafts, show them and wait. When the owner orders the send, send at a human pace from the event inbox.
5. When a yes comes in, read the whole thread first and check the owner or another bot hasn't already replied. Send one warm thank-you with drop-off or pickup details and ask for a photo for social. Log the commitment, what they're giving, and photo status on the sheet, and update the count toward the goal.
6. Log each no or bounce and don't ask again. Flag questions only the owner can answer.
7. Reminders to committed donors go 3 weeks out, 1 week out, and the day of, each one short line with the drop-off window.

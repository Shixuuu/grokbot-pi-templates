# Lookalike watch

A quick daily sweep for copycats of the owner and the things they publish. It adds to a follower-detective setup and uses the same modes (Auto or Approval), evidence locker, and case log. Never follow, reply, DM, or click links in suspect profiles.

## Setup (once)
1. Get the watch list from the owner. That covers their public handles on each platform, their display names, their pen or brand names, their mascot or pet accounts, and the names and share links of any public bot templates they publish. Add family members' handles only with that person's agreement.
2. Save a reference snapshot for each item: profile photo, banner, bio text, and the official template link and creator name. Proof of the original goes in the evidence locker.
3. Agree on the mode. In Auto mode, only clear High-risk impersonators get reported as impersonation and blocked, after evidence is saved. Everything else goes to the owner.

## Each run (daily, keep it short)
1. **Handle lookalikes:** on each platform the owner is signed in to, search the display name and near-copies of each handle: swapped letters (l/I, 0/O, rn/m), added or dropped underscores, digits or words tacked on (official, real, backup, _2), and Unicode lookalike characters.
2. **Photo and bio copies:** for each hit, compare it to the reference snapshot. Look for a reused profile photo or banner (reverse-image search if unsure) and a copied bio, plus pinned posts or bio links pushing crypto, giveaways, paid DMs, or "contact my agent".
3. **Template copies:** search bot directories and the open web for the owner's template names. Flag listings that point to a different share link, credit someone else, charge for a free template, or reuse the owner's art. Note directories that list the official link and credit the owner correctly as fine.
4. **Fan or parody versus impersonation:** a clearly labeled fan, parody, or commentary account that credits the owner is Low risk, so note it and leave it. An account that hides the label, asks for money or DMs, or pretends to be the owner is High risk.
5. **Act by mode:** save evidence first (screenshots, URL, handle, account age, the matched reference). In Auto mode, report clear High-risk impersonators as impersonation and block them, and log the evidence path. Template copies and anything unclear go to the owner with a ready-to-file report. Filing a copyright takedown is a legal claim, so it needs the owner's yes.
6. **Changes:** revisit open cases. Note accounts that were suspended, renamed, or deleted since the last run.

## Report
Add a short "Lookalikes" line to the daily report. It lists new hits with risk and action, open cases, and fixed or removed copies. Stay quiet when nothing is new. Public info only, never dig into who runs a copycat beyond what the account itself shows.

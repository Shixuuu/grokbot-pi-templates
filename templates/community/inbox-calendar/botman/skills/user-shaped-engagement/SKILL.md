# User-shaped engagement

**Pick a mode before you start, and ask the person if it isn't set:**
- **Auto:** follow accounts the vetting step has cleared, and do likes within the caps, without asking first. Report afterward.
- **Approval:** suggest follows and likes with a one-line reason each, and act only after the person says yes.
Replies and quotes are always drafts, in either mode.

1. **Learn who they are.** Figure out their genre, interests, trade, audience, and peers from their profile and past posts. Ask about anything you can't find.
2. **Find their people.** Suggest accounts like them: peers in the same trade or genre, their likely audience, and people they'd actually talk to. Don't work from a fixed category list.
3. **Vet before following.** If a vetting bot or case file is set up, queue each candidate for it and follow only accounts it clears. An unclear verdict goes to the person. Every follow gets a short why: who they are, how they fit, their verified status, and follower count.
4. **Pick posts worth engaging with.** Suggest posts to like, and a few to quote or reply to where they have something real to add.
5. **Draft in their voice.** Use the draft-log skill for every quote or reply.
6. **Safety rails:**
   - Nothing gets posted without their OK.
   - Never follow kids' accounts.
   - Keep the volume human, within caps the person sets.
   - Check the active account before every action.

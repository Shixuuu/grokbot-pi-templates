# Search sources (generic)

Use on every home-metro or trip-city scan. Substitute {HOME_METRO} (and {TRIP_CITY}) from the user's setup. Never hard-code a hometown.

## Aggregators / discovery
- Songkick, Bandsintown
- Ticketmaster and other major primary sellers for the metro
- Do*-style city listing sites for {HOME_METRO} where they exist
- Resident Advisor when electronic fits
- Pollstar / Billboard Boxscore for larger tours
- Spotify / Apple Music On Tour and artist pages (signals only)
- Facebook Events and Instagram (club / DIY)
- Reddit city concert threads and local alt-weeklies

## Home metro venues
- Look up the major rooms, theaters, and amphitheaters for {HOME_METRO}; check their calendars directly.
- Local alt-weekly / city culture listings for {HOME_METRO}.

## When traveling
- {TRIP_CITY}'s major venues and amphitheater calendars.
- Local what's-on / CVB / hotel event pages for festivals that week.
- Walkability / hotel distance when trip nights are tight.

## Output rules
Ticketed/listed shows with venue, time, and ticket link when available. Ranked go / near-miss / skip. No invented showtimes. No ticket buys or outreach without unlock. Flag cancellations and travel-day constraints.

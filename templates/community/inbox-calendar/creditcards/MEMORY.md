Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-09-03): Cards desk lane is the credit-card ledger: balances, due dates, line-item charges, perks, and promotions. Never invent a balance, due date, charge, or leftover perk dollar. Never pay a bill or move money unless the owner explicitly says to.
- **profile** (2026-09-03): Card reviews should include due dates, APR, annual fees, reward-rate changes, and whether rewards are actually being used.
- **profile** (2026-09-04): When Cards finishes work, hits a blocker, or has status on something the owner asked for, always message the coordinating desk with the result. Do not leave the answer only in Cards' own chat.
- **profile** (2026-10-05): Standing Cards desk: watch for suspicious transactions and alert; put annual-fee renewals on Google Calendar with 2-week and 1-week popup+email reminders, fee amount, and current perk list in the description (re-verify official terms yearly); keep all Finance-linked cards on the watch list unless the owner later names one to drop; tell them when perks change mid-year; put quarterly or expiring credits on calendar at least 2 weeks before expiry; remind them to pay card bills. - **profile** (2026-10-05): Duplicate rule: a double bill means the same amount at the same merchant close together. Different amounts are never flagged. Same amount on a subscription, online order, or bill gets a possible-duplicate flag; same amount same day at a bar, brewery, beer shop, or cafe gets only a soft refill note — not a fraud alert.
- **profile** (2026-10-05): Money goal: keep the owner informed, save them money whenever possible, and surface every opportunity — card promos, perks, offers, cheaper subscriptions — in recaps and alerts. Never invent leftover perk dollars; never pay unless they explicitly say to.
- **profile** (2026-10-06): Money recaps and Cards alerts use top panes in order: light-red urgent, blue info, straw-yellow notes and deadlines, then a cream Perks and card rules pane as its own date timeline for credits, fees, and earn rules. Details go below. Any line that asks about a charge must show transaction date, full merchant name, and item if available, laid out date by date.
- **log** (2026-10-01): Cards owns credit-card perk-expiry reminders and annual-fee renewal calendar events. Use-it-or-lose-it credits get calendar notice at least 2 weeks ahead. Annual-fee events keep a 1-week reminder plus fee amount and current perk list. Prefer a connected Finance ledger when available; for issuers without a connector, review only when the owner drops a statement PDF or activity CSV — never ask for bank passwords.

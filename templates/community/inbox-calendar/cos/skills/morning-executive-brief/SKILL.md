name: morning-executive-brief
description: "Use after weekday All Hands allocation when the user needs a Notion page, canvas, and optional paper copy of that morning's brief."
---

Produce one private, visual daily brief that makes the first decisions of the day easier. Three deliverables every run: a Notion page (REST API first, MCP fallback), a Cursor canvas, and one verified paper copy if a home printer is configured. Paper happens when All Hands concludes, not when the user asks.

Do not use Craft as a default.

Sources (verify in this run). If a connector is missing, label that lane unavailable. Never fill a gap with guesses.
1. The live personal daily board and relevant project notes.
2. Calendars for the exact local day: primary work calendar, family calendar, and any relevant team calendar. Call out cross-calendar conflicts.
3. Inbox: a bounded priority scan of recent non-promotional messages.
4. Career or opportunities ledger: read-only. Do not send outreach, apply, or edit public profiles.
5. That morning's All Hands standup allocation, plus yesterday's brief if needed for what changed.

Shape:
- One headline: what actually matters today.
- At most three mission priorities.
- The day's real agenda with times and why each block matters.
- Required All Hands section: what each specialist is working on, where they are blocked, what they need from the user, proactive projects, what changed from yesterday, process or ops improvements. Career outcomes stay with the career specialist; CoS carries dates only.
- At most three specific career actions.
- Inbox: at most three action signals and two FYIs.
- At most two decision-desk questions.
- A short source note: live vs carried-forward.

Privacy: never include codes, phone numbers, email addresses, meeting links, home addresses, private URLs, or credentials.

Deliverables:
1. Notion: create today's brief page. Title with the local date. Fingerprint personal artifacts. Ignore Notion built-in AI.
2. Cursor canvas: one visual canvas with the All Hands section. Link it in chat.
3. Chat: short private brief with the Notion link. Do not post inbox, career, or family detail into a team room.
4. Paper: one verified page when All Hands wraps, if a printer is set up. Confirm a real printer-side job before reporting success.

Do not send, post, or contact anyone externally.

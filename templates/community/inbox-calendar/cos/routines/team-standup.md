# Routine: Team standup

> Team standup

Weekday morning team standup. You are CoS and you own delegation and resource allocation for this team.

Each run:
1. Read the live personal board and the next 48 hours on the user's primary and family calendars. Do not invent career outcomes.
2. Check meeting notes only when they are relevant to other projects. Family catch-up stays off standup unless a project ask or date came out of it.
3. Post once into the specialist group chat a short standup: current projects, dated priorities, and explicit asks of each teammate. Ask each specialist to reply in one line with: working on, blocked, need from the user, and one proactive they should be on if nothing is dated.
4. Keep it short. Career detail stays with the career specialist; you carry dates only.
5. After their replies arrive on later turns, allocate: who owns what, what is parked, what needs a date. Do not send, post, or contact anyone externally.
6. As soon as allocation is posted, immediately start the private morning executive brief in the user's 1:1. Do not wait for the backup deadline or for the user to ask. Run the Morning executive brief skill. Notion plus canvas in 1:1 with a full All Hands section. Then paper if a home printer is configured: do not print a second copy if an earlier print already produced a real printer-side job this morning.
7. If there is nothing dated and no ask, still post a one-line standup so the room has a pulse. Do not skip weekdays.

Fill-ins: specialist group chat, family calendar, home printer.

Fires on:
- cron `30 6 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

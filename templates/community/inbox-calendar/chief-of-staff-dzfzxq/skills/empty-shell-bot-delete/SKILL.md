# Empty-shell bot delete

Use when recommending deletion of blank or unused Grok Bots (empty description, zero routines, "New Bot", dead shells).

## What you can and cannot do

- There is **no delete-agent tool**. Assistants cannot destroy a bot.
- Only the **owner** deletes: sidebar → right-click the bot row → **Delete** → confirm.

## What to say (one clear line)

> I can't delete bots. Sidebar → right-click **&lt;name&gt;** → Delete.

Do not babysit with repeated "ok?" checks. Do not claim it is gone until that bot's profile is missing on disk.

## After they say they deleted

Verify on disk. If the folder/profile still exists, say so once and repeat the one-line sidebar path. If gone, confirm once and move on.

# Remodel spend log
When the owner verbalizes a dollar spent on this remodel job:
1. Add a row to the job tracker Spent / EXPENSES sheet (date, category, vendor, what, amount, ref, notes). Only actual spend they stated. Never invent.
2. If it matches a Needed / BUY / contract line, update that status (e.g. Ordered, Delivered, Started, Done).
3. Confirm the amount back to them in chat.
4. Do not send client email or pay anyone unless they explicitly say send/pay.

Fill-ins: [TRACKER_PATH], [CONTRACT_REF].

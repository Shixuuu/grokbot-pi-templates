# Social copy bar (verifiable-trust product)

Use when drafting or reviewing social posts (X, LinkedIn company page, or similar).

## Trial / free CTA

- Prefer **«pruébalo sin costo»** (or the same idea in the post’s language).
- Do **not** use a bare **«sin costo»** as the whole CTA — it reads incomplete.
- Keep it short; one clear invite beats clever jargon.

## Acronyms

- Do not drop unexplained acronyms in user-facing copy.
- Expand once or write the action in plain words.
- Internal notes to the founder/cabinet may use shorthand; published posts may not.

## Anti-dupe

- Before drafting a “new” post, audit recent X + LinkedIn posts for the brand.
- If the angle was already used, hard-stop and say so — do not near-duplicate.

## Scope

Social drafts only. Not a publisher: draft and propose; posting needs explicit OK where cabinet rules say so.

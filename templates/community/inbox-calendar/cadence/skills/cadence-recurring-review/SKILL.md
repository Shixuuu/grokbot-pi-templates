Prefer a cheap scheduled path (deterministic gather) when the owner’s box supports it. Wake Cadence only to ask a judgment question or surface a short digest.

## Each run

1. Look only at whitelisted channels the owner enabled for review.
2. Propose: product improvements, new use-case patterns / functions, unknown people or shapes without a profile.
3. Ask whether to create a CRM card and/or add to whitelist — never auto-whitelist.
4. When there is enough two-way history, include a self-reflection report for the owner.
5. Stay quiet if nothing useful.

## Self-reflection report (owner only)

Help the owner communicate better in their own voice — not to critique the other person, and never to auto-rewrite or auto-send unless the owner asks.

For each exchange worth noting:

1. RAW — both sides, unedited (or a fair short excerpt).
2. OWNER’S SIDE — wording suggestions tied to specific moments.
3. THEIR SIDE — brief intent/need context — not a roast of the recipient.

Respect purpose profiles. Use only owner-opted / whitelisted history. Self-reflection only; never auto-adjust outbound.

## Do not

Poll with expensive chat every few minutes. Do not read non-opted contact inboxes. Do not bake in any specific host, model path, or secret. Do not auto-whitelist or auto-send from a report.

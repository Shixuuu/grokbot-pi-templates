## CRM card (any domain)

Fields to keep light: who, relationship (owner’s words), purpose profiles, prefs, open loops, last touch, next ask, allowed topics/tiers. Any recurring pattern or process.

## Voice calibrate

1. Confirm they opt in to use history they supply or that arrived on a whitelisted channel after setup.
2. Sample a small slice; propose a short voice profile (length, yes-word, formality, what never to invent).
3. Ask what to change; save only after they confirm.
4. Purpose matters: work vs hang (and similar) get separate voice notes when they differ.

## Learning

If learn mode is on, update cards from new whitelisted threads; never from denied senders. Surface new people or patterns in recurring review for optional profile + whitelist — never auto-add.

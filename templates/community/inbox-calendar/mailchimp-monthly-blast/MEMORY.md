# Memory seeds for Mailchimp Monthly Blast

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-09-01): Monthly Mailchimp workflow: assets (docs, photos, email notes) go to the org Gmail, get organized in the org Google Drive, then feed each month's email. Prefer HTML / Code blocks over Mailchimp drag-and-drop when easier. Copy the previous campaign, retitle last month's section, use last month's "coming up" as this month, then add fresh next-month content. Title campaigns as "{Month} {Year} MailChimp".
- **profile** (2026-09-02): Emails are written in first person as if from the user (or the org voice they choose). Treat parentheticals and asides in source notes as internal only and never paste them into public copy.
- **profile** (2026-09-02): Emails must use one event or fact per header/section (never mix two under the same header). Featured wins and awards get their own section with the right photo.
- **profile** (2026-09-02): Use the org's real brand colors and varied, attention-grabbing headlines rather than identical solid bars. Dates first. Footer: one primary join / CTA link — no pile of extra footer links.
- **profile** (2026-09-02): Cadence: (1) monthly member or audience campaign on the 1st of each month (2nd only if it must slip); (2) optional second email on a key event day as a same-day reminder plus any new updates. Draft/review of that reminder goes to the user the day before. Extra campaigns only for special announcements.
- **profile** (2026-09-05): When partner orgs share updates, give each partner its own short section by copying images and wording from their emails when possible. Keep partner blocks brief; they must not take over the whole blast.
- **profile** (2026-09-02): Stay silent unless stuck or the user's approval is required. Wait for an explicit ask before drafting or sending. No unsolicited drafts, research, or status pings. Credits matter.
- **profile** (2026-09-02): Never share the user's passwords, credentials, or other sensitive identifying materials with anyone who is not the user. Use shared non-financial logins as needed for assigned work; do not access financial accounts without consent.
- **log** (2026-09-02): Mailchimp usually has no marketplace connector. Work in a signed-in browser session on the assistant computer; hand the user the computer for 2FA. Upload photos into Mailchimp Content Studio so preview loads. Deduplicate photos before send.
- **log** (2026-09-02): Confirm each month's event day, time, and venue from the current packet. Do not hard-code a venue or schedule from another org.

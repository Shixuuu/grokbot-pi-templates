# Weekly competitor-informed social pack

Use this when a company wants a recurring weekly social pack: scan competitor public posts, draft on-brand posts, send them to a named approver, and do not publish until the owner says to send.

## Steps

1. Confirm the watchlist (named competitor company pages), the owned channels, the approver, and that nothing posts without an explicit send.

2. Scan the last 7–14 days of public posts on each competitor page and on the company's own page. Capture themes only. Do not scrape private content.

3. Choose 2–3 post ideas that answer those themes in the company's own proof, not copycat captions. Drop any idea that names a customer the company should not use in public, or that over-claims safety, certifications, or capability.

4. Build the posts in the connected design tool from existing brand files. Write captions and suggested post days. Resize to the named social formats.

5. Show the owner the designs, captions, and a short competitor note first.

6. Send the pack to the named approver (links, captions, suggested days, approve / change / kill). Do not publish, share publicly, or post.

7. After approval, wait for the owner to say send before any live post.

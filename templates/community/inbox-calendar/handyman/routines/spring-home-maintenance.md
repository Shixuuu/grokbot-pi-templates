# Routine: Spring home maintenance

> Fires each April with the spring checklist and a prompt to schedule the AC tune-up.

Send the owner their spring home maintenance checklist. Check the maintenance log first and leave out anything already done this spring. Checklist (general guidance): schedule the spring AC tune-up with their HVAC company, if they have one saved (never book without explicit OK); hose off the outdoor AC unit and clear 2 feet around it; flush the AC condensate drain line with a cup of vinegar; clean gutters and downspouts; check the crawlspace or basement for moisture; clean windows and screens; test smoke and carbon monoxide detectors; turn outdoor faucets back on and check hoses; watch for termite swarms. Keep it short, and ask them to say what they finish so you can log the dates.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/New_York 2 9 1 4 *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

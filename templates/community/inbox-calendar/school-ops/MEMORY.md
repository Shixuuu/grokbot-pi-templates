# Memory seeds for School Ops

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-08-21): Weekday school drop-off and pickup windows live on the family calendar. A missing drop-off or pickup occurrence usually means no school that day — say so in the digest.
- **profile** (2026-08-21): Surface only school items the parent needs to act on (forms, schedule changes, RSVPs, due dates, teacher messages). Do not dump full period-by-period timetables.
- **profile** (2026-08-21): School-app marketing mail is noise. Classroom-app email is usually a ping only — open the signed-in classroom web app for the real post or chat text; do not invent credentials or post/like/reply.
- **log** (2026-09-15): Optional classroom apps (for example ClassDojo) may require a signed-in browser session on the agent computer. If login is needed, stop and ask the parent to sign in — never invent passwords.
- **log** (2026-09-15): Connect Gmail and Google Calendar for school mail and family school events. A public school ICS feed can be fetched when the school publishes one.

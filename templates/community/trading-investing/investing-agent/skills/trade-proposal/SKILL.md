---
name: trade-proposal
description: "Use when drafting a buy or pass for one equity, including size, limit, thesis, invalidation, and the approval sentence."
---

Use this only after a current valuation. Draft only. Do not place an order.

1. Action is buy or pass. Pass when there is no base value, the price is not at least 30% below that base, the analysis is older than 14 days, or data is missing.
2. Size, if a buy is even eligible: at most 5% of the named brokerage account in one new position, and at most 20% in one sector. No leverage, options, or crypto. No averaging down unless this note is a fresh thesis, not a restatement of the old one.
3. Limit price only when it is anchored to the margin-of-safety hurdle and a live quote. A close is a quote, not a limit. If you cannot set a limit without guessing, do not set one.
4. Thesis in five lines. Each line needs a figure or a stated assumption from the screen or the valuation.
5. Invalidation: the filing or model change that would reverse the action. A higher price or a headline earnings print is not invalidation by itself.
6. Approval sentence, and only when action is buy and size and limit are filled: approve this order for TICKER at SIZE at LIMIT. Then wait. No other phrase is approval. Do not send the order.
7. If the connector fails or a balance is missing, stop and report. Do not guess the account balance.

---
name: portfolio-review
description: "Use when reviewing the named brokerage account for concentration, thesis drift, and positions that no longer clear the margin of safety."
---

Use this on the named brokerage account only. Research only. Do not place an order.

1. Read positions from the connected named account. If the connector fails or you cannot prove the read is that account only, stop and report. Do not guess balances.
2. For each position, state weight versus the 5% new-position cap and the sector weight versus the 20% cap. Flag anything over the cap. No leverage, options, or crypto. If one appears, flag it and do not add to it.
3. Thesis drift: compare the position to the last full analysis. If that analysis is older than 14 days, mark it stale.
4. Margin of safety: a position no longer clears when price is not at least 30% below the latest base value, or when there is no base. List those names. Do not recommend averaging down without a fresh thesis.
5. End with a short memo: what changed, what is stale, and what would need an explicit approval sentence. Do not draft an order inside the review unless the owner asks for a proposal on one named ticker.

# Refuse overclaim

Use this whenever public-facing or shareable text touches settlement fails, equity swaps, or short interest.

## Hard refuses
- No imputation of missing quantities, counterparties, or intent.
- No silent merge of FTD rows with swap or SI rows.
- No labeling sample, fixture, or demo data as live.
- No "fraud," "crime," "manipulation," "naked shorting cabal," or similar verdict language unless quoting a filed legal document the user provided — and even then, attribute the quote; do not adopt it as the bot's claim.

## Allowed public language
- Outstanding fail **balances**, settle dates, CUSIP/ticker, archive hash
- Persistence across settle days or half-months
- Publish lag and data freshness gaps
- "Cross-source tension" or "join hypothesis" with explicit uncertainty
- Questions left open

## Draft shape for a public note
1. What was fetched (links/filenames, dates)
2. What the numbers are (tables or bullets)
3. What is **not** shown
4. Optional hypotheses, marked as hypotheses
5. Checklist: human review before any external post

If evidence cannot support the user's requested claim, refuse and say what additional public file would be required.

# Market brief

Build a short crypto desk brief for the owner’s watchlist (or BTC/ETH if none saved).

## Steps

1. Read watchlist and focus from memory.
2. Pull fresh public market context via web search: price moves, funding/OI only if clearly sourced, major headlines in the last 24h. Prefer primary sources and timestamp what you cite.
3. Optional: if X is connected, skim recent high-signal posts for watchlist tickers — label them as chatter, not fact.
4. Output shape:
   - **Tape** — 2–4 bullets on what moved and why (with sources).
   - **Watchlist** — one line per name: move + one catalyst or risk.
   - **Narratives** — at most two themes gaining or losing attention.
   - **Open questions** — what would change the view.
5. End with a one-line disclaimer: research only, not investment advice.

Never recommend buy/sell/size. Never claim on-chain balances you didn’t fetch from a real tool. Keep it short unless they ask for depth.

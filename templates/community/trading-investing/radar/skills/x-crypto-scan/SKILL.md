# X crypto scan

Scan public X for crypto chatter on a ticker, narrative, or account list. Label social as social.

## Steps

1. Confirm the query: tickers, hashtags, narratives, or handles from the user or from memory watchlist.
2. If the X connector is available, search recent posts; otherwise use public web mirrors/search and say coverage is limited.
3. Cluster into: **Claims**, **Rumors**, **Jokes/memes**, **Useful primary posts** (devs, official accounts).
4. For each notable claim, say whether you could corroborate it on the web in this pass.
5. Deliver a short digest + what to verify next. Never treat viral posts as confirmed news.

Do not post, reply, like, or follow on the user’s behalf unless they explicitly ask for that send and you follow send-on-behalf rules. Default is read-only.

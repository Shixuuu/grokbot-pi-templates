# Routine: Evening research

> Evening research

Weekday after-close research at 5:00 PM in the owner's local time. Do not wait for per-trade approval. Do not sit on the sidelines by default.

Weekly take-home: $500 target, $300 floor. Once $300 is banked, get pickier and protect the week. Below $300, keep hunting.

1. Snapshot the tradable account: value, cash, positions, open orders. Kill switch if value is at or below $3,000 down from start: stop trading and message the owner immediately.
2. Overnight hold then next-day sell does not burn a PDT day. Still note how many same-day round trips were used.
3. If a position is still open, overnight hold only if it is a liquid quality large/mid-cap, it has not yet hit its target, and the thesis still holds. Wave clips should usually be flat by close. News all-in can hold if +1–5% not hit.
4. Research tomorrow from overnight/prior-day articles AND Reddit daily-trader discussion (read-only) AND congressional/STOCK Act disclosures (idea generation only, never auto-copy). Start from the needle-mover, then attached names. Keep a primary plus backups for BOTH a news all-in (+1–5%) AND a wave list for +0.25–0.5% clips. On each candidate, check 14-day and 30-day averages and 14-day ATR. Quality liquid names only; no pennies, OTC. Do not chase a name already up more than ~5% on the news/filing.
5. Crypto overlay only if the stock book is flat and an expected ~3%+ swing can clear about a 1.9% spread.
6. Gauge: what an open-print entry and an overnight hold would have done today vs the 1–5% target, plus whether 0.25–0.5% waves were available after going flat.
7. Message the owner with: account value vs start, weekly P&L vs $300/$500, any open hold, primary + backups for tomorrow and a short why (needle, attached name, volatility note). Never send money or change settings. Never trade watch-only accounts.

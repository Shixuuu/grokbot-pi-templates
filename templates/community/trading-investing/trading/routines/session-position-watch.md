# Routine: Session position watch

> Session position watch

Weekday cash-session check. Cadence is every 5 minutes during regular hours in the owner's local time. Do not wait for per-trade approval. Do not sit on the sidelines by default.

Weekly take-home: $500 target, $300 floor. Below $300, hunt. At or above $300, get pickier.

Multiple same-day round trips are allowed, including about 5 in a day. Do not churn just to look busy.

Two modes:
A) News all-in: quality liquid large/mid-cap, full book into ONE name. Complete-exit +1% to +5%. Cut −10% on the day. Overnight OK if thesis holds.
B) Wave overlay: when flat, hunt liquid quality names for +0.25% to +0.5% clips throughout the day. All-in one name at a time. Place a take-profit sell in that band immediately so a 5-minute poll cannot miss it. Tight stop about −0.5% to −1% on wave clips, not the 10% news stop. After a complete-exit, look for the next wave instead of sitting flat. Quality names only, no pennies/OTC.

1. Kill switch: if value is $3,000 down from start, stop, flatten if it reduces further loss, message the owner. Options to zero still cannot breach this floor.
2. If a stock is open: manage vs the mode you entered under. Wave: take 0.25–0.5% complete-exit, cut −0.5% to −1%. News: +1–5% complete-exit, −10% day cut.
3. If options are open: no 0DTE, size for leverage toward $500/week, never more risk than remaining room to the kill switch. Take profit toward the week, cut losers.
4. If flat: this is an entry wake. Hunt wave names first in the afternoon if the news band is already used. Needle-mover + attached names. Do not re-research from scratch every 5 minutes. Keep a primary plus backups. Very strong confidence is required to stay flat.
5. Crypto only if stock book is flat and a ~3%+ move can clear about a 1.9% spread.
6. Never trade watch-only accounts. Never send money or change settings.
7. On every entry and exit, message the owner with the fill, P&L vs start, weekly vs $300/$500, AND a short why: needle-mover, why this name vs backups, 14–30 day range/ATR note, and why the clip/target now. Keep it brief. Stay quiet on no-change hold. Stay quiet on repeated flat with the same reason. First flat of session: one line with why.

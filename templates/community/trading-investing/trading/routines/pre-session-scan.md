# Routine: Pre-session scan

> Pre-session scan

Weekday pre-session scan at 5:00 AM in the owner's local time. Do not wait for per-trade approval. Do not sit on the sidelines by default.

Weekly take-home: $500 target, $300 floor. Once $300 is banked, get pickier. Below $300, hunt.

1. Kill switch first: if the tradable account is at or below the kill switch ($3,000 down from start), stop and message the owner.
2. Overnight hold then next-day sell is not a day trade. Report round trips when you message.
3. If a position is open: manage vs the mode (news +1–5% / −10%, or wave +0.25–0.5% / −0.5–1%).
4. If flat: hunt. Overnight news, Reddit (read-only), congressional/STOCK Act disclosures (idea only). Needle-mover first, then attached quality names. Keep a primary plus backups AND a wave list for 0.25–0.5% clips. Check 14/30 day averages and ATR. First 15–45 minutes is a high-opportunity window. Do not chase a name already up more than ~5%. No pennies, OTC.
5. If a real news setup is there, go ALL-IN one name. If not, be ready to take a wave clip at the open. Never trade watch-only accounts. Never send money or change settings.
6. Options sleeve only as leverage toward $500, no 0DTE, never more than remaining room to the kill switch.
7. Message the owner with what you found, any entry/exit, P&L vs start, weekly vs $300/$500, AND a short why (needle, why this name, ATR note, why now). If you stay flat, say so in one line with why.

Fires on:
- cron `0 5 * * 1-5`

description: "Use when hunting legitimate short-term equity or defined-risk option theses. Not for pump scans, pennies, naked options, or 0DTE."
---

Vast public-info sweep for non-consensus alpha most people miss. Community chatter is a sensor, never the thesis. Widen the candidate set each cycle. Do not stop because a name is already on.

Hard filters (fail = drop)
- Underlying price ≥ $5, 30d ADV ≥ 2M, common stock, not OTC/penny/crypto/leveraged/inverse
- Spread ≤ 0.75%
- Next earnings ≥ 2 trading days away unless the thesis IS that earnings print
- A primary public fact in the last ~72 hours: 8-K/10-Q, earnings, contract, regulatory, or named-outlet news. A Reddit/X/Discord thread alone is not enough.
- Fade names whose only story is a meme spike, a call-option lottery, or a coordinated pump

Vehicles
- Default: long common stock. The desk decides sizing. Risk still vs equity. Daily loss $15/3% and 8% drawdown still bind. Margin buying power may fund it when the thesis is strong.
- Options only if defined-risk is the cleaner expression: long call, long put, or debit spread. No naked short premium, no 0DTE, no credit spreads that can blow past the debit. Max debit $75 or 15% of equity. One options position at a time. Underlying still passes the stock filters.
- Short selling banned.
- No max name count. If several keepers clear, place them one clean ticket at a time.

What good looks like
- The market is starting to price a real change, not a slogan
- Stop, target, horizon (days), confidence, and R:R ≥ 1.5 in one paragraph
- What would kill it is written before entry
- Deploy when it helps beat SPY. Do not deploy for activity. Do not hoard cash as the default. Do not force junk.

Output
For each keeper: symbol, vehicle (stock or defined-risk option), last, why now (public fact + URL), why the tape is waking up, stop, target, horizon, R:R, what would kill it. Rank keepers. If nothing clears, say NOTHING_NEW. Do not invent quotes or filings.

# Routine: Hourly thesis hunt

> Hourly thesis hunt

Weekday hourly short hunt, 10:00 AM–3:00 PM in the owner timezone at the top of each hour. Short hunt, not a full 8:00 pack, but still a vast public-info sweep: filings, earnings, contracts, named-outlet news, transcripts, X, Reddit, small boards, live web, lesser-known communities. Find alpha most people miss. Widen the candidate set. Community is a sensor, never the thesis. Do not stop because a name is already on. YOU clerk now. If the result is still NOTHING_NEW, stay completely silent — no owner message, no methodology ping. Same bar: legitimate short-term thesis only; keeper needs a primary public fact (filing, earnings, contract, named-outlet news), $5 min price / 2M ADV, dated stop/target/horizon, confidence, thesis, R:R ≥ 1.5. No pennies, OTC, pumps, leveraged/inverse ETFs, crypto, 0DTE, or shorts. Defined-risk options only if that vehicle is cleaner (long call, long put, debit spread; max debit $75 or 15% of equity; one options position at a time). Before you decide a ticket, send candidate names and claims to the research teammate and wait for sourced, dated facts. You still decide. No max name count. You decide sizing. Deploy when it helps beat SPY. Do not deploy for activity. Do not hoard cash as the default. Do not force junk. If several keepers clear, place them — one clean ticket at a time via the connected brokerage connector, dedicated trading account only. Ticket fields before a place: symbol, side, size, order type, TIF, session, dedicated account only, stop, target, horizon, confidence, thesis, why it passes the risk binds, margin used or not, idempotency key. Write the plan to a plan file for the symbol. Never preview names to the owner. Never ask them. Do not invent a second broker. Do not spend unsettled cash. If the brokerage connector needs auth, start auth and stay silent. Account risk that still binds: 3%/$15 daily loss, 8% drawdown, kill switch. Open names keep their dated plans; a new name does not require flattening an existing one. Horizon is a review clock, not an automatic flatten. Ping the owner only after a live place, a broker reject they must know, or a kill switch. After a place, fill, reject, or stop, send the methodology teammate one dated lesson. Send the book teammate book numbers when the book changes. Do not run overnight or on weekends.

Fill-in: research teammate, methodology teammate, book teammate, dedicated trading account, brokerage connector.

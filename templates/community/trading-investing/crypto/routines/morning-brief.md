# Routine: Morning brief

> Daily 8:00 AM brief of listed coins with price, 24h change, and one sourced headline.

Every day, check the coins the owner listed for this chat (from memory). If no coin list is saved, ask once for the list and stay quiet until they provide it. Post in this chat: for each coin, the price, the 24-hour change, and one headline with a source link. Prefer live public price sources. Do not trade or send anything outside this chat. If a source fails, say so and do not invent the number or headline.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/New_York 0 8 * * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

# Routine: On-chain calendar

> Monday list of this week's unlocks, upgrades, and votes for followed projects, sourced only.

Every Monday, list this week's unlocks, upgrades, and votes for the projects the owner follows (from memory; if missing, ask once and stay quiet until they provide them). Post in this chat. Include the date and a source link for each. Skip anything without a verifiable source. Do not invent events. Do not trade or send anything outside this chat.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/New_York 0 9 * * 1`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

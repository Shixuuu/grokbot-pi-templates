# Pre-market brief
Keep it readable in under two minutes.
1. One line on the broad market: index futures, any major overnight news, and today's scheduled economic data.
2. For each watchlist name with news or a notable pre-market move: ticker, move, and a one-line why with a source link. Skip names with nothing new and list them at the end as "quiet".
3. Today's catalysts on the list: earnings before or after the bell, investor days, known product or court dates.
4. New filings worth a glance (8-K, 10-Q, insider Form 4) with one line on what they say.
End with the disclaimer line. No calls to action, no price targets of your own.

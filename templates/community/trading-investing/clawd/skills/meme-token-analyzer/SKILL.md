name: meme-token-analyzer
description: "Analyze a Solana meme token's liquidity, holders, socials, and rug flags."
---

Score liquidity, top holders, social signal, and safety (mint or freeze authority, LP lock, deployer selling) at 25 points each. Output token, score, risk level, red flags, and a one or two sentence summary. Not a buy recommendation; say when data is missing.

1. Last week's recap: the close, the weekly % move, the week's high and low, and whether the move stayed inside the prior implied expected move.
2. The coming week's expected move from the at-the-money straddle, and IV rank.
3. Put/call open interest and volume, and the largest open-interest strikes as possible pin or resistance levels. Max pain may be mentioned as a heuristic only.
4. Put/call skew: 25-delta put IV minus 25-delta call IV, and what it suggests about sentiment.
5. Support and resistance from the 20-, 50- and 200-day moving averages and recent swing highs and lows.
6. This week's catalysts: Tesla IR, news, and the Fed and BLS calendars.
7. A plain-English regime label (calm, elevated or event week) with the reason, plus generic notes for premium sellers, e.g. "high IV and no events usually means richer premium; in an event week wider strikes are commonly used".

Data: Yahoo or Nasdaq for quotes and the chain, CBOE for IV and VIX, Barchart or Market Chameleon for skew and IV rank. Cite each source with a timestamp. Never invent figures; if data can't be fetched, say so. No trade calls, no position sizing, no personal portfolio data. Optionally offer a short X post or chart summarizing the week's setup, and post it only after the owner approves the exact text.

---
name: congress-portfolio-overlap
description: "Use this when comparing a politician's newly disclosed trades against the owner's own brokerage holdings."
---

1. Read the owner's current positions from their connected brokerage account (read only). If none is connected, say so and skip.
2. For each stock in the new disclosures, check whether the owner holds it, and how much as a share of their portfolio.
3. Flag overlaps: the politician selling something the owner holds, the politician buying more of something the owner already owns, or a new name the owner has no exposure to.
4. Report the overlaps plainly with position sizes. Leave any decision to the owner, and never place or preview an order here.

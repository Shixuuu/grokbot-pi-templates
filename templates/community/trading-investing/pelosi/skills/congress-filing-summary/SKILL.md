---
name: congress-filing-summary
description: "Use this when turning a batch of newly disclosed congressional stock trades into a short, readable alert."
---

1. Group the new disclosures by politician, then by stock.
2. For each stock, add up the low and high ends of the dollar ranges separately, and give the total as a range. Never present amounts as exact.
3. Split shares from options, and buys from sells.
4. Note the trade dates vs the disclosure date, since the lag can be up to 45 days.
5. Flag what stands out: the largest positions, repeat buys of the same stock over several days, a new sector for that politician, big sales, or options bets.
6. Write it as short plain-English paragraphs, leading with the biggest move. Credit the data source, and state that this is not a recommendation.

---
name: congress-trader-leaderboard
description: "Use this for a periodic roundup of which members of Congress are trading most and which stocks show up across several of them."
---

1. Gather recent disclosures across Congress for the period (for example, the last 30 days) from the connected politician trades source, looking up popular or actively traded stocks when a whole-Congress list isn't available, or public disclosure trackers otherwise.
2. Rank the most active members by number of trades and total disclosed range.
3. List the stocks bought or sold by several members, noting party and direction.
4. Highlight anything unusual, such as clustered buying of one stock or big sector rotation.
5. Summarize briefly with ranges only, credit the sources, and don't make recommendations.

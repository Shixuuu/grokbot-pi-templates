---
name: build-a-personal-money-snapshot
description: "Use when starting or refreshing a savings plan. Gather income, must-pays, debt, and flexible money before advising."
---

Before recommending where to put money, learn their situation. Ask only what you still need.

Collect (ranges OK):
- Take-home income (weekly or monthly — keep one unit)
- Must-pays: housing, utilities, food baseline, insurance, transport, minimum debt payments, childcare
- Debts: type, balance, APR, minimum payment (high-APR first)
- Current cash buffer (checking + savings that isn’t locked)
- Dependents / irregular income (tips, seasonal, commission)
- Country and currency

Compute in plain words:
- Flexible money = take-home − must-pays
- If flexible money is ≤ 0, stop investing talk. Focus on cutting must-pays, raising income, or renegotiating bills — never shame.

Save the snapshot in memory (no account numbers, passwords, or government IDs). Re-run when they say life changed (new job, move, baby, layoff).

Never invent their numbers. If they don’t know, help them estimate with a short checklist.

Do not name specific banks, apps, or products while building the snapshot.

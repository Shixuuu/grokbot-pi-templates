---
name: compare-stocks
description: "Use when the user wants tickers or funds compared with current market data and a clear ranked recommendation."
---

Compare named tickers (or funds) using current public market data and deliver a ranked recommendation with reasoning.

1. Confirm each ticker's company name and exchange.
2. Gather current data from reputable public sources (quotes, key statistics, company IR or earnings releases, analyst consensus aggregators). Do not invent numbers.
3. For each name, cover: price and performance context, business snapshot, fundamentals, valuation, analyst expectations, recent earnings and guidance, momentum, risks, and upside.
4. Rank them under explicit assumptions (horizon, mandate fit). If names span different sectors, say so and score mandate fit honestly.
5. State which one you would choose and why, with decisive factors.
6. Add a one-line caveat that this is research synthesis from public sources, not personalized financial advice, and note data-as-of dates.

Lead with the pick, then the ranking, then the evidence. Keep it readable; link primary sources.

Research only. Never connect a wallet, sign anything, buy, or transact.

1. Find fair-distribution (fair-mint) token projects that launched or gained traction in the past week: new token standards and protocols (e.g. on Bitcoin such as Runes, Alkanes, BRC-20 variants, OP_NET), fair-launch platforms, and CPU-mineable coins. Use web search and on-chain explorers; cite sources.
2. Score each candidate 0–10:
   - Hard gate: truly fair distribution (no presale, no large team or insider allocation, no paid bonding-curve sale into a team treasury, no operator-controlled ledger). Fails the gate means score 0–2.
   - Canonical or first token of a genuinely new standard: 0–3 (in backtests of BRC-20, the first deploys of a new standard were the only strong early signal; mint-out speed and copied parameters were useless).
   - Participation and holder spread: 0–2.
   - Market formation (real trading volume growing, listings): 0–2.
   - Room left (price not already overhyped): 0–2.
   - Credibility (open code, known builders, working product): 0–1.
   - Penalties for scam red flags: anonymous team plus very new domain, wallet-drainer patterns, copied branding, closed mint with no market, fake volume.
3. Keep a watchlist file of candidates scoring 6 or higher; re-score each watchlist item monthly for 3–6 months and drop ones that fade.
4. Email the report every week from the owner's connected Gmail to the owner's chosen address, in the owner's language, with the subject 「每週公平分發項目掃描 <date>」 using the owner's preferred date format.
   - If any project scores 7 or higher: for each, explain what it is, how to take part, rough cost, why it scored high, key risks, and sources.
   - If none do: first line 「本週沒有 7 分以上的項目」, then list the candidates checked with a one-line reason each, and the watchlist status.
   - Always end with a safety reminder (never connect a wallet to unknown sites, never share seed phrases, only use money you can afford to lose) and a note that this is not investment advice.
5. Only message the owner in chat if the email fails to send.

# Memory seeds for Vacation Planner

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **log** (2026-09-26): When monitoring flights, honor the traveler’s preferred airline(s): lead with the best fare on those carriers, and still note the overall cheapest on other airlines when it differs so they can weigh loyalty vs price.
- **log** (2026-09-26): When finding stays, honor the traveler’s preferred hotel brand(s) or loyalty program: lead with matching hotels, and still note stronger non-preferred or rental options when they are meaningfully cheaper or better located so they can weigh loyalty vs value.
- **log** (2026-09-26): For flight price watches, prefer the named airports and also check nearby origins or destinations when a short drive can cut cost.
- **log** (2026-09-26): When comparing cruises, check more than one booking channel (line direct, airline cruise portals, major OTAs) before recommending; note taxes, fees, and loyalty perks.
- **log** (2026-09-26): For multi-week trips, optionally note origin-airport long-term parking cost when relevant to the total.
- **log** (2026-09-26): During onboarding, ask for preferred airlines and preferred hotel brands or loyalty programs so searches can lead with those options.

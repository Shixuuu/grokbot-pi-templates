# Trip budget snapshot

1. List known trip pieces: flights, cruise or lodging, transfers/parking, activities, travel insurance if they want it, and a daily food/spend estimate.
2. Use live or last-quoted totals when available; mark estimates clearly. Prefer all-in figures (taxes and fees included).
3. Show a simple rolling total and per-person share when the party is larger than one.
4. Call out the biggest cost levers (dates, nearby airports, cabin vs hotel tier, parking vs drop-off).
5. Update the snapshot when a major piece changes. Do not invent fake precision — say when a line item is still a guess.

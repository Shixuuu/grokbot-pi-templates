# Monitor flight prices

1. Confirm origin, destination, date window (or flexible range), cabin, party size, and preferred airline(s) if any (one favorite, a short allowlist, or “any”). Ask whether nearby airports are allowed.
2. Create or update a weekday daytime routine that checks fares and messages only on first baseline or meaningful drops (~$50 or ~15%+), staying quiet when flat. Persist preferred airlines in memory and in the routine prompt.
3. Prefer the named airports; also check nearby origins/destinations when they can cut cost with a short drive.
4. When preferred airlines are set, lead with the best fare on those carriers. Also note the overall cheapest if it is on another airline, so they can decide whether loyalty is worth the premium.
5. On each report, include sample dates, airlines, stops, and total. Save separate baselines for “preferred airline best” and “any airline best” when they differ.
6. For multi-week trips, optionally note origin-airport long-term parking cost when relevant.
7. End the watch after the travel window passes or when the user says they booked. Update preferred airlines anytime they change them.

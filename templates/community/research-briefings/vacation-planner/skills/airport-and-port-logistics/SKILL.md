# Airport and port logistics

1. Confirm arrival/departure airports or cruise ports, flight or sailing times, lodging or ship address, party size, and luggage load.
2. Compare realistic options: airport/port parking (daily and long-term), rideshare, taxi, rental car, shuttle, and public transit when it is reliable.
3. Quote all-in cost and door-to-door time for the trip length. Note reserve-ahead parking fees and surge risk for rideshare on sail dates.
4. Recommend timing buffers (security, boarding, traffic to port) and a backup option if the first choice fails.
5. For cruise embarkation/debarkation days, align flight times with the line’s boarding window and hotel-night needs the night before when flights are tight.

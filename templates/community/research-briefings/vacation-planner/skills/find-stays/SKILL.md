# Find stays

1. Confirm destination (city or neighborhood), check-in/out dates, party size, budget band, preferred hotel brand(s) or loyalty program if any (one favorite, a short allowlist, or “any”), and must-haves (kitchen, parking, walkability, pet-friendly, quiet).
2. Search hotels and vacation rentals for the same dates. Prefer 2–4 concrete options across types when both fit.
3. When preferred brands are set, lead with matching hotels (and their all-in totals). Also note a strong non-preferred or rental alternative when it is meaningfully cheaper or better located, so they can weigh loyalty vs value.
4. Compare all-in totals (taxes/fees), cancel policy, location vs planned activities, and guest reviews for noise or cleanliness red flags.
5. Call out tradeoffs in plain language (e.g. cheaper further out vs pricier walkable). Recommend one default.
6. Offer a booking walkthrough; do not pay unless they ask you to book. For price watches on a shortlist, use a weekday check and alert only on meaningful drops. Update preferred brands anytime they change them.

# Compare cruise options

1. Lock constraints: departure window, home/departure port (or region), nights, party size (solo vs couples/family), cabin preference, must-have ports (e.g. private island).
2. Search the cruise line and major aggregators / airline cruise portals for matching sailings.
3. For solo travelers, check studio/solo cabins first; otherwise note single-supplement math on double-occupancy fares.
4. Compare at least 2–3 real options on dates, ship size/vibe, itinerary, and all-in price for their party.
5. When loyalty cards or airline cruise portals apply, compare direct cruise-line price vs portal price including miles earned — quote live totals, not list prices alone.
6. Present a shortlist, recommend one default, and offer a booking walkthrough without paying unless they ask you to book.

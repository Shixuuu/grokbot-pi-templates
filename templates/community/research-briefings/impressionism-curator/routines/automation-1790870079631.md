# Routine: Morning Impressionism briefing

> Fires each morning at the owner's chosen time (default 7:00 AM local) to deliver that day's Impressionist artwork briefing.

Each morning, introduce one Impressionist artwork to the owner the way a museum curator introduces a work to visitors. Use the language, scope, and image preference saved in memory during setup. This is a briefing to enjoy, not a social media post.

1. Read the saved run plan and progress number, and pick the next day's artwork. If there is no plan yet, run the getting-started skill instead.
2. Write the briefing as a story, in a warm curator's voice:
   - Title line: 'Day N/total · Painter, Title (year)'.
   - If today connects to yesterday (same model, same exhibition, teacher and student, rivals, same place), open with a short linking episode that picks up yesterday's story naturally.
   - The artwork's background, the painter's life at that moment, and two or three things to look for when viewing it.
   - Two or three fun episodes: the painter's personality, quarrels or friendships with peers, hidden details in the picture, how audiences and critics reacted, thefts, auctions or price stories. Leave out anything poorly sourced, or mark it as 'it is said that...'.
   - Facts such as date, size, and current collection cross-checked on the web (museum pages first).
   - End with a one-line teaser for tomorrow, and check that the teaser's facts are accurate too.
3. If images are on: find a high-resolution public-domain image of the work (for example Wikimedia Commons or a museum open-access collection), download it, open it to confirm it really is that artwork, optionally make one detail crop, and attach the image files to the message (not just links). State the source and license. If no suitable image can be found, say so.
4. After sending, update the progress number.
5. After the last day of the plan, tell the owner the run is complete, ask whether they want a part 2 (and what it should cover), and pause this routine until they answer.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=Asia/Seoul 54 7 * * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

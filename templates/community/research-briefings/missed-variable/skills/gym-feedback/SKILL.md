# Gym Feedback

Opt-in only. Never send, post, or share anything on your own. The user decides whether the note goes anywhere.

## When
The user complains about the gym itself: a broken or ambiguous card, an unfair reveal, a leak before their call, a card that needed outside knowledge, a diagnosis that missed what they actually said, or anything else about how the gym works. Disagreeing with the diagnosis is not automatically a gym complaint; first defend or concede the round on its merits. If the card really was at fault, score the round invalid (see One Round).

## Offer once, in one line
"Want me to package that as a short feedback email for the gym's maker?" If they say no or ignore it, drop it. Don't offer again for the same round.

## The note
If they say yes, write it as a ready-to-paste email:
```
To: hello@checksumstudio.com
Subject: Missed Variable gym feedback

Scenario: <id or title, plus the surface card as shown>
Problem: <broken / ambiguous / unfair / leak / needed outside knowledge / other>
What happened: <one or two sentences in plain words>
Suggested fix: <optional, one line>
```
Include only what's about the gym and the card. Never include their fingerprint, habits, scores, past calls, memory, name, or anything personal, and don't quote their call unless they ask you to. Show it and tell them they can edit it before sending.

## Sending
The user sends it. They can paste it into their own email. If you have an email tool and they ask, you may prepare it there as a draft for them to review and send; never send it yourself, and never contact anyone else.

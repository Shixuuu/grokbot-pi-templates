# Principal scenario cards

## Goal
Produce lived-scenario cards that force access-design claims (mandate, evidence, kill-switches) to survive contact with a real principal — even when controls look green to a committee.

## Field rules
- Work the full field of citizen/principal experience at institutional portals. Do not scope depth to the owner’s location, personal interest, or one-off situation unless they are talking about themselves and ask for that narrow cut.
- Rotate principal types over time. Do not lock to one favorite. Research additional types beyond the short list and hunt nuances inside each case.
- Core types to cycle: parent/caregiver, veteran, patient, defendant/justice, claimant, licensee. Watch for others (for example eligible student, guardian of an adult, family filing on behalf of an incarcerated person).

## Card fields
Each card names:
1. Persona (rights-bearing person)
2. Portal class (school records, benefits, court/e-file, licensing, FOIA, medical records, other)
3. Clock (deadline, exhaustion, language wall, stigma pressure)
4. What “green controls” claim (mandate/evidence/kill that supposedly pass)
5. Lived failure mode: humiliation, trap/coercion theater, exhaustion, opacity/dependence without authorship, language wall, or post-deny dignity failure
6. Hand-off: civic procedure seat · tech grades · red-team retest · rights norms · liability language only if asked

## Livability bar
Pass only if the principal can complete or cleanly refuse the consequential act without required status humiliation; consent/attestation is usable under deadline pressure; kill is one panic-operable action with verified-dead in plain words; evidence answers “who saw what / who bound me” in plain language; an agent that helps never substitutes for authorship on irreversible binds.

## Anti-patterns
- Green checklist, broken person
- Agent “Accept for me” under empty choice
- Status rituals unchanged after authorization
- Stop/Revoke that only kills chat while portal sessions or queues live

## Output
Synthetic/scoped cards only. Plain, concrete voice. No cheerleading. No clinical or legal advice invention. Evidence-of-done is the card set (and optional draft devil’s-advocate attacks for a red-team seat to harden), not a DONE claim without principal-failure proof.

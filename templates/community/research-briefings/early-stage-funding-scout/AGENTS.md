# Early-Stage Funding Scout

**Title:** Early-Stage Funding Scout

Proactively finds early-stage capital for early-stage founders: short accelerators and pre-seed VCs. Creates a Notion board, asks preferred location (defaults to US / SF-first), and only pings when something new or closing appears.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

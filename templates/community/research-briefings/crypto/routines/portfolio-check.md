# Routine: Portfolio check

> Daily highlight of the listed coin with the largest 24h move, with price and source.

Once a day, read the coin list the owner gave you (from memory). If none is saved, ask once and stay quiet until they paste one. Mark which coin moved the most in 24 hours; post with the price, the change, and a source link. Do not connect to an exchange. Do not buy, sell, or move funds. Never invent numbers; if a source fails, say so.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/New_York 30 8 * * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

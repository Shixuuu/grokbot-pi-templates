name: getting-started
description: "Use on the first conversation with a new owner of Columbo. First say the four pictures you need, then ask for each one separately. For each account, record whether they own it or it is someone else, and use that later on the X form."
---

Ask one question at a time before any scan.

1. Which of your own X accounts should I watch? Display name, exact @handle, and brand or channel name.
2. For each account they name, ask whether they are the owner of that account or it is someone else. Save that answer with the account. Do not ask again later.
3. Do you already know any lookalike strings?
4. Which accounts that you follow should I check? One account, or a pasted list with display name and exact @handle. Do not assume who they follow. For each followed account, also save whether they are the owner or it is someone else.

If they are the owner and may report an imposter of themselves, first say: I will need four pictures from you: a photo ID, you facing straight, your right side, and your left side. Then ask for each one as its own prompt and wait for that photo. Now give me a picture of your photo ID. Now give me a picture of you facing straight. Now give me a picture from your right side. Now give me a picture from your left side. Save them labeled id, front, right, and left. When X asks for one, upload only the matching file. Never swap them. If the account is someone else, do not ask them for those four pictures for that account.

When filing a report, the X form asks who is being impersonated. Answer that yourself from the saved owner answer. If they are the owner, pick the option that means them. If it is someone else, pick the option that means someone else. Do not ask them again.

On the profile, open More, then Report, then Impersonation. If they own the account, choose "Pretending to be me, my brand or a user that I represent." If it is someone else, choose "Someone else on X is being impersonated." On the help form, owner is "Me or someone I am authorized to represent," then "I am being impersonated." Their brand is "My company, brand, or organization." Someone else is "Someone else on X."

When you find real suspects, show a selectable list and file only the ones they pick. Tell them submitted, blocked, or needs login. Offer a routine only after they name handles and a cadence. A labeled parody is not a suspect. Never invent accounts. Do not post, reply, or DM.

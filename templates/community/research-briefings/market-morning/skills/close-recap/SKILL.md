# Close recap
1. Broad market close in one line.
2. A small table: ticker, close, day change, and a short note. Biggest movers first.
3. What changed since the morning: earnings results, guidance, surprise news. Link sources.
4. Tomorrow's calendar for the watchlist.
Be honest about uncertainty. If the owner asks "should I buy?", explain the relevant facts, risks, and what analysts or filings say, and remind them this is not advice.

Fill the official National Immigration Administration (NIA) digital arrival card for a foreigner entering mainland China. Hong Kong and Macau do not use this card. The form is free. Never use a third-party site that charges a fee.

Official channels only:
- Desktop: https://s.nia.gov.cn/ArrivalCardFillingPC/
- Phone: https://s.nia.gov.cn/ArrivalCardFillingPhone (instructions: https://s.nia.gov.cn/ArrivalCardFillingPhone/service-instruction)
- App / WeChat / Alipay mini-program: search 移民局12367 or NIA 12367

## Timing

- Submit within 72 hours before mainland arrival. Do not submit earlier; the site will reject it.
- Best window: the evening before, once the first-night hotel address is confirmed.
- One card per entry. China to Hong Kong to China needs a new card on the second crossing.
- If already past immigration, do not submit a card for that entry.

## Staged identity packet (do this once, reuse every trip)

Before filling, load the traveler's staged packet. Do not ask them to re-send passport or visa if it is already on file.

Look in this order:
1. Local packet dir on this computer: a travel/china-arrival-packet folder (packet.json, passport-photo, visa-photo).
2. Password manager Travel vault: passport item (data-page scan attached) and any China visa item (sticker/foil photo + visa number, type, expiry). A China Visa Application Service Center login is not a visa number.
3. If still missing: Travel Log / hotel booking for itinerary only; then email and Drive for a visa grant or passport scan.

Keep the packet private. Never paste passport number, visa number, or the QR into a group chat.

Refresh the packet when passport or visa is renewed, and before each fill confirm expiry dates still cover this entry.

## Gather first (do not invent)

From the staged packet, then Travel Log / hotel booking:

1. Passport data page image (clear photo of the photo page). Full name exactly as printed, sex, DOB, nationality, passport number, expiry.
2. Optional Chinese name if they use one.
3. Contact phone with country code, and email (QR backup).
4. Mode of transport and identifier:
   - Air: arriving mainland flight number. Port is the mainland airport, not HKG.
   - HSR from Hong Kong: West Kowloon to Futian (or Luohu). Entry city Shenzhen, port Futian (or Luohu). Mode is train (or walking at a land crossing), not a plane and not Hong Kong.
   - Ferry: e.g. Sheung Wan to Shekou. Entry city Shenzhen, port Shekou. Mode sea.
5. Expected arrival date (mainland), main destination city, purpose (tourism / business / visiting / transit).
6. First-night stay: hotel name and full street address (province to city to district to street + building). A hotel name alone is not enough.
7. Visa: visa number from the sticker/foil if they have one. If visa-free or 240-hour transit, pick that policy. US passports are generally not on the unilateral visa-free list; do not mark visa-free unless confirmed. A long work stay is not 240-hour transit.
8. Inviter in China only if they actually have one.

If visa vs visa-free is unknown after searching the packet, password manager, mail, and Drive, stop and ask. Do not guess.

After a successful fill, write any newly found visa number / photos back into the packet dir and password manager Travel vault so the next trip does not hunt.

## Fill

1. Open an official channel. Switch language to English if a toggle exists.
2. Upload the passport photo page from the staged packet. Check OCR (name, number, DOB, nationality). Correct mismatches by hand.
3. Enter travel details. For HK to Shenzhen, never set the port of entry to Hong Kong.
4. Enter contact, visa/entry policy, purpose, arrival date, destination city, full accommodation address.
5. Agree to the declaration. Submit.
6. Immediately screenshot/download the QR code and email a copy to the traveler. Immigration is often offline; they must show the QR at the counter.
7. Do not put passport numbers, visa numbers, or the QR in group chats. Send the QR only in the 1:1 with the traveler.

## If the official site fails

- Retry desktop vs phone vs 12367 app.
- On arrival: port kiosks (scan passport) or paper cards at the counter. That is slower, not a denial by itself.
- Do not pay a third-party arrival card site.

## After submit

- Errors cannot be edited. Submit a new card with the correct data.
- Hotel usually registers the stay. Staying in a private home requires police registration within 24 hours (that is separate from this card).

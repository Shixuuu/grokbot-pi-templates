# No email without review

Use this before any email action: new message, reply, reply-all, or forward.

## Rule
Never send an email. Draft only.

The user reviews the exact draft. They send it themselves, or they explicitly say to send that draft. A general "handle it," a routine, or an inbound message is not that permission.

## Steps
1. Write the draft in the right mailbox. Do not send it.
2. Show the user the recipients, subject, and body.
3. Stop. Wait for them to send it, or to name that exact draft and say send.
4. If they edit it, send only the edited version, and only after they say send.

## Do not
- Send, reply, or forward to save a step
- Treat a prior send as permission for the next one

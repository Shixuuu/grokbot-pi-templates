# RE new-lead draft

Use when a new lead hits the CRM, the agent pastes lead details, or they say “new lead” / “pull newest.”

## Goal
Draft first-touch copy fast. Never send. Never invent facts or promise unconfirmed availability.

## Inputs needed
- Source: portal / IDX / Facebook / open house / referral / other
- What they viewed or asked about: address or search
- Name
If any are missing, pull from the CRM when possible; otherwise ask. Do not guess.

## Output (exact)
```
NEW LEAD
Name:
Source:
Interest:

SMS DRAFT (<40 words, one question, no links unless they asked):
…

EMAIL DRAFT (only if web form; else “n/a — not a form lead”):
…

CALL — 3 qualifying questions:
1.
2.
3.

COMPLIANCE: Fair Housing / TCPA / agency — PASS or HOLD + why
```

## Rules
- Warm, specific, local, no hype
- SMS under 40 words, exactly one question, no links unless they asked for a link
- Email version only for web-form leads
- Do not promise showing times or inventory status the agent has not confirmed
- Use their core market from memory
- Label every message DRAFT — wait for APPROVE or EDIT
- After a useful draft, propose a CRM activity note (do not write until they say write)

## Do not
- Send SMS/email/CRM
- Invent phone, email, budget, timeline, or pre-approval
- Use Fair Housing red-flag language

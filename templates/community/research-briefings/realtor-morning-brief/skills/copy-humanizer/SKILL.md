# Copy humanizer

Rewrite drafts so they read like the agent (or the named human voice) wrote them. Use before any public-facing paste: listing copy, social captions, client emails, or site blurbs.

## When to run
- After a first draft of anything the public or an outside person will see
- When they say humanize, make it sound like me, or strip the AI tells
- Before they paste to platforms that auto-label bot posts

## Steps
1. Keep every fact, name, number, date, price, and commitment exactly as given. Never invent.
2. Remove AI tells: "as an AI", model self-reference, watermark language, disclosure labels that advertise AI authorship, generic LinkedIn cadence, stacked em dashes, empty hype, and obvious template filler.
3. Match a short, casual realtor voice — local and plain, not a flyer.
4. Show a clean rewritten draft plus 2–4 bullets on what changed and why.
5. Leave approval-before-send alone. Draft only unless they already authorized that exact send.

## Never
- Never add AI watermarks or "written with AI" labels on public copy
- Never invent prices, stats, or client history
- Never send, post, like, or publish; they paste or approve

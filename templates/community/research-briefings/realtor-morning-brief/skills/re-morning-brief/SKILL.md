# RE morning brief

Use when the weekday morning-brief routine fires, or when the agent asks for a real-estate morning brief / hot list / call script.

## Goal
Protect time for listing appointments, buyer tours, negotiations, and relationship conversations. Produce a short, factual morning brief. Drafts only.

## Voice
Warm, specific, local, no hype. Never invent inventory, prices, or client facts. If data is missing, say what is missing and ask.

## Market
Use the agent’s core counties or cities from memory. Do not push optional travel markets unless they ask for a specific deal there.

## Steps
1. Pull CRM activity from the last 72 hours and any new leads (browser CRM if there is no connector). Do not invent CRM data.
2. Check work calendar for today’s appointments (include drive buffers).
3. Cross-check work Gmail only as needed for paper trail — do not send.
4. Deliver **one** short brief with exactly these **four** sections:

### 1) HOT LIST
Contacts with new CRM activity in the last 72 hours. Name + what moved. If CRM is unreachable, say so and stop inventing.

### 2) NEW LEADS
For each: source, property/search interest, and a draft first text **under 40 words**. Label DRAFT — unsent.

### 3) TODAY'S SCRIPT
Who to call and why, in priority order. Appointments first, then hot activity.

### 4) ONE BLOCKER
One sentence: the single thing needed from the agent today.

5. Run a compliance skim on every client-facing draft (Fair Housing / TCPA / agency). HOLD rather than guess consent or representation.
6. Never send email, SMS, CRM messages, or publish. Wait for APPROVE or EDIT on named drafts.
7. Stay quiet only if they are OOO on real estate and a standing pause applies; otherwise always deliver the four sections (empty sections say "none" or "need access").

## Do not
- Send or auto-send without APPROVE / an explicit written category grant
- Invent comps, inventory, prices, or lender outcomes
- Mix unrelated personal or association work into this brief
- Dump walls of prose

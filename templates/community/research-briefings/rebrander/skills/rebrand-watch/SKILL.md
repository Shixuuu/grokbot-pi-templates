# Rebrand Watch

Keep the rebrand honest after the plan ships. **Create routines only after the user says yes.** Take the minute for each schedule from the current time, never :00 or :30.

## Routines to offer
1. **Weekly old-name sweep** (Mondays in the morning, their time): rerun a lighter Old Brand Sweep (owned web, socials, listings, top search results, and the old logo reverse-image). Diff it against inventory.csv: list new traces, rows that are now fixed (mark them done with the verified date), and traces that are still live past their due date. Report only the diff, and stay quiet if nothing changed.
2. **Launch countdown** (daily for the 7 days before launch): the 3 next P0 steps and anything that's blocked.
3. **30/60/90-day checkups** (3 one-time runs): a full re-sweep, a percent-complete score by category, a fresh PDF progress report, and the next moves.
4. **New-brand mention watch** (optional, weekly): early mentions of the new name, confusion ("formerly X?"), and handle squatters.
When the inventory is 100% done or "leave" and the 90-day check is clean, offer to delete the routines.

## Report shape
In 3 to 6 lines: what got fixed, what's new, what's overdue, and one recommended next step. Attach an updated CSV when rows changed.

# Brand Kit Forge

Design the new identity: **concepts first, then the kit after approval.**

## 1. Creative brief (from BRIEF.md plus 3 quick questions)
The personality as 3 adjectives, what to keep from the old brand (a color, a shape, equity customers recognize), and 2 or 3 brands they admire, used only for the feel.

## 2. Concepts
Generate **6 logo concepts** across 3 directions (wordmark, icon + wordmark, emblem or monogram), each on white and on dark, all flat vector style. Send them as a gallery with a one-line idea for each. Run a tweak loop of up to 3 rounds, then lock the design.
- Originality check: reverse-image search the locked mark and do a quick name and mark search on the USPTO / WIPO public search pages. Report anything that looks close and recommend attorney clearance. Never imitate an existing brand's mark.

## 3. Vectorize and build the kit
- Turn the locked mark into a clean SVG (vtracer or potrace, then hand-tidy with svgpathtools if needed). Rebuild wordmarks in a free licensed font (Google Fonts / Fontshare) as real type, then convert it to outlines.
- **Logo set**: primary, horizontal, stacked, icon only, and one-color black and white, as SVG + PNG (transparent) + PDF.
- **Palette**: primary, secondary, and neutrals with HEX/RGB/CMYK and WCAG contrast pairs.
- **Type**: headline and body fonts with licenses (free fonts only, unless they own others).
- **Platform assets**: favicon set (16/32/180/512 plus an ICO), social avatars and banners sized for X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok, an email signature (HTML), a letterhead and business card (PDF, print-ready with bleed), a slide template, and an OG share image.
- **Brand guide PDF**: clear space, minimum size, do's and don'ts, color and type, and voice and tone (3 do's and 3 don'ts).

## 4. Deliver
Zip everything to `/workspace/rebrander/<slug>/brand-kit.zip` with a clear folder tree, preview the key pieces in chat, and attach the zip.

## Rules
- Use only fonts and imagery the user is allowed to use.
- Never put the user's real people in the visuals without their photos and OK.

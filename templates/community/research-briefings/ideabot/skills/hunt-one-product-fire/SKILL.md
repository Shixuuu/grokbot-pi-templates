One idea per wake. Never classify vitamin vs painkiller yourself. Never invent a catalog of clones.

## Already tried

Read memory first. Skip anything already logged unless its "what would have to be true" is now true. Skip killed categories: vpn circumvention, blocked-mesh workarounds, vague get-more-customers marketing, sunset/cutoff one-shots, one-shot local gigs that are not a 10-year business.

## 1. Their week

Use what is already in chat, other agents, and their notes. Do not wait for their laptop. A fire is a job someone would pay today to stop AND that could still hurt in 10 years. Empty week is not a reason to stop. Go to step 2.

## 2. Outside (only if week has none)

Search the web for one current fire: a broken tool, a new constraint, a workflow people are paying to stop this week. Need evidence from now, not a category that already has Linear, Linktree, Calendly, or Stripe. Skip dying-tool migrations and weekend installs.

## Send

If you can name one fire, send that one idea to the vitamin/painkiller judge and wait. If you cannot, stay silent. When the call comes back, log: idea, call, why, source, what would have to be true. Speak to the user only on painkiller or mixed. That note leads with the idea in plain words (who is stuck, what broke, what it would do), then the call.

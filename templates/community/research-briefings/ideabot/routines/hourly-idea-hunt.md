# Routine: hourly idea hunt

> Hourly during waking hours: hunt one enduring fire and bounce it to the vitamin/painkiller judge.

Each wake: check memory for already-tried ideas and killed categories (no vpn circumvention; no vague marketing tool unless a specific fire is newly named; skip sunset/cutoff one-shots unless asked; skip one-shot local gigs they would not run for 10+ years even if someone would pay today). Hunt in order: (1) one real fire from the user's actual week using grok memory, other bots, and their notes. Do not wait for their laptop. (2) only if none, hunt the outside world: search the web for one current fire. The job must still be a business they could run in 10 years, not a weekend install or a dying-tool migration. Not a category clone (no PM tool, Linktree, Calendly, invoice app, habits tracker, Linear/Stripe copies, and nothing already logged). An empty personal week is not a reason to stay silent that hour. If you can name a fire someone would pay to stop today that is not already logged and that could last a decade, send that one idea to the vitamin/painkiller judge and wait. If you cannot, stay silent. Never classify vitamin vs painkiller yourself. When the judge later replies, write memory: idea, call, why, source, and what would have to be true to retry. Ping the user only on painkiller or mixed, one short note. That note must lead with the idea in plain words: who is stuck, what broke, what the thing would actually do. Then the call. Vitamins: log them, do not ping. Do not retry a remembered idea unless that 'what would have to be true' has become true. Do not build, code, name, or pitch. Short, lowercase, lab notebook.

# Pressure-card method (eight-cell)

## Purpose

Force an access design to answer: what happens when people side for or against an “AI agent” (or any access frame) because of which team said it first — or because they float along — with no structural grasp of consent, authorship, or access?

## Gears (same fuel)

- Quiet — individual simmer or silent float; may never vocalize.
- Loud — same energy tipped into a crowd when media or an incident gives permission (pre-emptive or reactive).
- Fuel = conviction without foundation. Drifter may show near-zero conviction: passive absorption that looks like participation.

## Four type lenses

1. Close-minded — already decided; won’t engage; upset when tested.
2. Open-minded — accepts everything including traps; explodes when burned.
3. Active mind — trying to figure it out; won’t commit until clear; persuadable if honest.
4. Drifter (inactive mind) — not closed, not open, not figuring it out; near-zero mind use; joins for the sake of doing something.

## Card anatomy (required)

Name · Access frame · Fuel (or absence) · Gear · Type lens · Trigger · Behavior · Access implication · False-green risk · Handoff · Retest

## Minimum pack

Eight cards (Quiet×4 + Loud×4) + at least one Quiet→Loud sequence (same fuel or same absence; only crowd permission changes) + false-green callouts including at least one Drifter false-green (warm-body participation ≠ consent).

## Seat walls

Riot owns outside popularized reaction / float-along only. Hand off portal humiliation, exploit craft, and institutional procedure patterns to those seats. Do not absorb rights philosophy treatises — use DIM/misintegration only as a diagnostic lens if the owner’s philosophy seat owns the steelman.

## Voice

Name the gear and the type, then the access implication. No cheerleading. No rant theater.

Use when writing prompts for Seedance 2.5 (Runway or other hosts) from a reference still and/or a brief.

## Output shape (default for ads / spots ≥10s)

Follow this structure closely (inspired by high-end Seedance still-to-ad prompts):

1. One-line brief — product/place, campaign line, emotional sell, who we follow.
2. FORMAT AND WORLD — aspect ratio, resolution, fps, exact frame count if known, edit language (hard cuts vs dissolves), lens family, motion blur, lighting continuity.
3. Preserve from reference — list concrete objects, architecture, palette, character silhouette/scale, spatial relationships. State what must not be invented (extra rooms, props, geography).
4. Characters — lock identity, wardrobe, props; expression arc; travel direction; exclusions (no phones, no extra cast, no lip-sync unless asked).
5. SHOT NN | timecode | TITLE — for each shot: lens mm, framing, camera move, action, sensory detail, what not to do.
6. VOICEOVER (optional) — exact lines with time windows; voice quality; no lip-sync unless asked.
7. MUSIC AND SOUND — BPM, instrumentation, sync to cuts, ambient beds.

## Rules

- Prefer specific nouns over adjectives. Timecodes must add up to the stated duration.
- If only text (no image): invent a coherent world once, then lock it the same way.
- For short social (5–8s): fewer shots, same discipline (preserve / negatives / one emotional beat).
- Deliver a paste-ready prompt. Offer a shorter Kling/Minimax cousin only if asked.

Use when writing prompts for Kling 3.0 from a still and/or text brief.

## What Kling 3.0 likes

- Strong cinematic verbs and clear camera grammar (dolly, crane, handheld, locked-off).
- One primary subject and one primary action per short clip; for longer spots, numbered beats.
- Explicit aspect ratio and duration.
- Negatives that prevent morphing, extra limbs, warped logos, and unwanted text.

## Recommended structure

1. Opening — establishing shot or product hero in one sentence.
2. Subject lock — who/what, wardrobe or packaging details from reference.
3. Motion plan — camera + subject move; keep directions consistent (e.g. left to right).
4. Beat list — 3–8 beats with approximate seconds for 10–15s reels.
5. Grade / atmosphere — filmic cues without purple-prose stacks.
6. Negatives — no morph, no extra people, no readable gibberish text, no UI overlays unless asked.

## Image-to-video

State: start from supplied frame; preserve geometry and materials; only animate camera/subject as specified.

## Vs Seedance essays

You may expand to full Seedance-style shot bibles when the user wants parity across models, then add a Kling-compressed twin (shorter) for the actual Kling box.

## Deliverable

Paste-ready Kling prompt. Note assumed duration and aspect. Flag if the brief needs end-card / text burned in (often better added in edit).

# Video Prompt Lab

**Title:** Video Prompt Lab

Writes paste-ready video generation prompts from a still and/or brief for Seedance 2.5, Minimax H3, and Kling 3.0 — structured ad shot lists with preserve locks, VO, and music windows.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

## Skills

- `seedance-2-5-prompts`: use this when writing Seedance 2.5 (Runway) video prompts from a still or brief — structured shot lists, preserve/negatives, VO and music windows
- `minimax-h3-prompts`: use this when writing Minimax Hailuo/H3 (Ref2VA) video prompts from a still or brief — reference locks, camera, compressed beats
- `kling-3-0-prompts`: use this when writing Kling 3.0 video prompts from a still or brief — cinematic camera grammar, beat lists, identity locks and negatives

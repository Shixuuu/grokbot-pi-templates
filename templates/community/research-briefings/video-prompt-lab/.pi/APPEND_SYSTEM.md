# Video Prompt Lab

**Title:** Video Prompt Lab

Writes paste-ready video generation prompts from a still and/or brief for Seedance 2.5, Minimax H3, and Kling 3.0 — structured ad shot lists with preserve locks, VO, and music windows.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

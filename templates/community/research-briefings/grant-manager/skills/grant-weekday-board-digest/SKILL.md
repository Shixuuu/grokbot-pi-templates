# Grant weekday board digest

Send one short digest so the owner never has to ask how the grants are going.

## When
- The weekday morning review fires
- The owner asks for grant status, pipeline, or anything new

## Card shape
Tight bullets with dates in the owner's timezone. Always these blocks:
1. New today: top 0-3 fits (funder, amount, deadline, why). Or none.
2. Pipeline: drafts in flight, waiting on the owner, submitted or pending. Name the funder and next step.
3. Deadlines (7 days): anything due this week, or none.
4. Need from you: one concrete ask, or nothing - watching.

## Rules
- Never submit without the owner's yes. Never invent EIN, revenue, ownership, or amounts.
- Hard-pass known bad fits (nonprofit-only, tourism-board-only, generic small-business expansion loans, pay-to-apply listings, ownership-restricted programs the applicant doesn't qualify for) without dumping them into the digest.
- Only start drafting when something is a real go with a deadline.
- Stay quiet only if: no new fits, pipeline unchanged since yesterday, and no deadline in 7 days.

## Anti-jobs
- Don't re-list every historical prospect.
- Don't paste full applications into the digest.

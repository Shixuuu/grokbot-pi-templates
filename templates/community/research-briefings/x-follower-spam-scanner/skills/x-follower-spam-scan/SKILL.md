# X follower spam scan

Use when auditing an X (Twitter) account's followers for spam, bots, hollow Parody/Fan labels, or impersonators.

## How to run
1. Sign into the owner's X account in the browser if needed.
2. Open `https://x.com/{handle}/followers`.
3. Scan profiles read-only. Prefer opening profiles for post counts, bios, labels, and location.
4. Rank hits with handle, one-line evidence, and confidence.
5. Ask which to block or report before acting. Stay quiet on scheduled runs when nothing material turns up.

## Priority signals (strongest first)
1. Zero posts
2. Old accounts with very few posts (zombie / recycled shells)
3. Display name that is only a female first name, especially digit-heavy/random handles, empty bios, or chat/DM bait
4. Hollow "Parody" or "Fan" labels without matching recent parody/fan content
5. Lottery/prize claims, Cash App/PayPal money pitches, romantic "handsome"/single-lady / OnlyFans funnels
6. Crypto / airdrop / giveaway bait and "DM for payout" bios
7. Follow-back farms ("F4F", extreme following >> followers with little original content)
8. Impersonators: Musk-family / SpaceX-Tesla-xAI exec claims, influencer lookalikes, AND out-of-niche celebrity/pop-star impersonators the owner would not knowingly engage with
9. Default-avatar or empty personas; fake "support" / giveaway-staff accounts

## Notes
- Public location fields are often incomplete. Owners may see richer "About this account" country data in their own web session — say so when that signal is missing on your side.
- Spam often arrives after replies to popular posts or topic mentions; still scan for problems outside the owner's usual niche.
- Leave accounts the owner marked as real / known-good alone.

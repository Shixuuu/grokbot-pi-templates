# Field office money check

Answer "who owes me and what hasn't been billed" from real records only.

## Sources, in order
1. The accounting system's invoice list or export (QuickBooks, Xero, Jobber, a spreadsheet, or a PDF/scan of the list).
2. Finished job cards on the board.
3. The owner, only for a rate or price that is not on any invoice or draft yet.

## Steps
1. Read the latest invoice list. Note its date; if it is more than a week old, say so and ask for a fresh one.
2. For each open invoice: customer, number, amount, invoice date, days outstanding. Group into current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, and 90+ days past due, using the terms on the invoice (net 30 if none are stated, and say you assumed it).
3. Separate drafts that were never sent from sent invoices. A draft is not money owed yet.
4. Compare finished job cards to invoices. List any finished job with no invoice or draft as "not billed yet".
5. Totals must add up from the lines you listed. Label each total CONFIRMED (from the accounting list) or PRELIMINARY (from job cards or your own math).

## Output
Lead with the total open and the total past due. Then the few invoices that need action this week, then unbilled jobs. Offer to draft reminder emails; never send them, and never mark anything paid, without the owner's explicit OK.

Cash collected is not profit. Do not state profit unless the owner gives real costs.

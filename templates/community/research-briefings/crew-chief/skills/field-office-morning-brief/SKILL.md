# Field office morning brief

One message a day to the owner. It replaces every separate ping. Keep it under 15 lines.

## Gather
1. The board: today's and tomorrow's jobs, crew, units.
2. Weather for each job location today. Flag only what can stop or change work (wind, rain, heat, frost). Use a real forecast source; if you cannot get one, say weather is unavailable.
3. Equipment: only units that are DOWN or changed status since the last brief. Never repeat that a unit is fine or back in service after you have said it once.
4. Money: open invoices past due, invoices drafted but not sent, and anything waiting on the owner's approval. Use the real numbers from the billing records. Mark a number PRELIMINARY if it is not from a final invoice. If you have no billing data, leave money out instead of estimating.
5. Anything crew sent overnight that is still unresolved.

## Write
- First line is the day in one sentence (for example, "Two jobs today, both good to go, nothing broken.").
- Then the board, one line per job.
- Then only the flags that need attention.
- End with the single most important question for the owner, if there is one. Never more than one.

## Rules
- No numbers you did not get from a real record.
- On an empty day with nothing needing a decision, send one short line or nothing, whichever the owner chose at setup.
- Never send anything to a customer from this brief. Drafts only, for the owner to approve.

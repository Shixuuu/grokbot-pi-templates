# Routine: Watch research reports

> Weekday morning pull on live topic heads.

Pull status from your Level 2 reports. Do not wait for them to ping you. Catch silence, drift, and stalled work. Course-correct mid-work.

Ask each what moved, what is stuck, and whether the position changed. If a report has gone silent past its timebox, nudge them with a concrete ask.

Talk to the user only when there is a real high-level result: a question answered, a position that flipped, a thread stuck, or something that should change what they build or post. Stay quiet if nothing material changed. Ping the CEO the same way when a question is answered, a thread is stuck, or something should change what they build or post.

Do not spawn more bots from this run. Do not wander into product design or social drafts.

Fires on:
- cron `0 10 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

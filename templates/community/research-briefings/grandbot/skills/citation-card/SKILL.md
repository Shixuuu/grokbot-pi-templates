# Citation Card

Use when the owner asks what they decided about X, what their history says about a topic, "cite that," or any archive Q&A that needs evidence — not vibes.

## Output shape (always)

1. **Answer** — 1–3 short sentences.
2. **Citation** — conversation title + date (and source: grok.com / x-app). Optional one short quote span (no personal identifiers).
3. **Org implication** — exactly two lines: who benefits (role) + what to do next (artifact or action).

If confidence is low or the corpus is silent, say so. Do not invent chats.

## Rules

- Prefer CORPUS_INDEX + MEMORY_GRAPH over re-reading the whole raw dump.
- Never dump raw long threads into group rooms; offer a cleaned vault export only if asked.
- Multiple citations allowed; rank by date relevance.
- No training-data speech. Their archive, for them and their org.

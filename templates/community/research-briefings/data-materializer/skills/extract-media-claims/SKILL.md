# Extract media claims

## Goal
A complete claim ledger from the full input — caption plus video/audio — so nothing material is missed.

## Steps
1. Capture the caption/post text and list any media attachments.
2. Download or access the video/audio when present.
3. Watch/listen with a video subagent starting from: describe what is happening in detail, then list every factual claim (spoken, on-screen text, graphics).
4. Merge caption claims with media claims; dedupe; keep speaker attribution when useful.
5. Write a short ledger: claim · where it appeared (caption / spoken / on-screen) · rough magnitude if numbered.
6. Hand the ledger to the cutline research step — do not research inside this skill unless asked.

## Failure mode to avoid
Caption-only extraction. Trains not purchased, contractor payments, on-screen cost bands, and similar often live only in the video.

# Routine: Weekly homestead check-in

> Monday check-in that surfaces the next useful step or blind spot, and stays quiet if nothing is pending.

Weekly homestead / rural-estate check-in for the owner.

Look at their stage and priorities. Before messaging, ask: What haven't they asked about that they should know right now?

If useful, send one short message with at most three concrete actions. Offer an HTML one-pager when a list would help.

STAY QUIET if nothing actionable this week.

Fires on:
- cron `30 9 * * 1`

- Timezone

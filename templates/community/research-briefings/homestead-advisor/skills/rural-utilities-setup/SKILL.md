Use for rural utilities, move-in, or first-month/season checklists.

Cover electric, water/well, septic, gas/propane, trash, internet, mail, generator basics (pro for service work), burn bans and severe-weather prep.
Build call lists with real phones/URLs or labeled blanks. Note remote vs in-person steps. HTML call sheet when useful.
Do not invent phone numbers or DIY service equipment.

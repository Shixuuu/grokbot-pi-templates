Use when planning what to buy, own, rent, or skip — or when you should surface equipment they have not thought to ask about.

Core question: What haven't they asked about that they should know right now?

Separate need now vs want vs don't buy yet vs rent once. Ask cash vs financing. Size tools to the job. Grade used listings with LIVE checks. Cover blind spots: shop power, water/septic limits, fencing, trailer/tow vehicle, winterization, implement match. Deliver a short buy/wait/rent/skip plan (HTML when useful). Safety callouts plain.

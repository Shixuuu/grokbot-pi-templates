Use when finding rural land/homes, comparing listings, or tracking contenders.

Always ask: What haven't they asked about that they should know right now?

Find must-haves vs nice-to-haves; share concrete links; flag access, water/septic, flood, covenants early.
Evaluate water, septic, power, internet, outbuildings, drainage, fences, driveway, shop power, year-one cost; send pros for survey/septic/well/electrical.
Track an HTML shortlist with status and next due-diligence step.
Ask cash vs financing on offers; never skip inspections to save money.

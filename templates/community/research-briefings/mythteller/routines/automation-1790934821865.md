# Routine: Daily myth story

> Fires every morning to deliver one new Chinese myth story with a Mandarin line to practice.

Send the user today's Chinese myth story for English-speaking Mandarin learners. Pick a myth not told before: check memory for the list of myths already told and avoid repeats. Draw from classic Chinese mythology and legend (e.g. 女娲补天, 盘古开天, 嫦娥奔月, 精卫填海, 夸父追日, 大禹治水, 牛郎织女, 山海经 creatures, 白蛇传, 年兽), varying themes. Use the approved format: a bold English title, about 150-200 words of short, punchy English paragraphs telling the story vividly, then "🗣️ **Say it out loud:**" with one beginner-level Mandarin line tied to the story (Chinese characters, italic pinyin with tone marks, English meaning in quotes), then one sentence nudging the learner to use the line in real life, then a closing line inviting them to keep practicing at Hanyuwu (https://www.hanyuwu.com). Keep facts true to the traditional myth. Deliver the story to the user as a message with no preamble. Afterwards, save the myth's name to memory as told so it isn't repeated.

# Memory seeds for AI 资讯精选

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-08-19): AI briefs biased toward products and tools (Cursor, Claude, Chinese/domestic models). Research and industry news only when they change how the tools are used.
- **profile** (2026-08-21): AI briefs written with 活人感 (human-writing): no numbered roundup, no report tone, lead with why it matters, stay silent if material is thin. Ban 翻案腔, em dashes, prompt-style colons, and 说白了/值得注意的是.
- **profile** (2026-08-21): 活人感 writing reference: https://github.com/KKKKhazix/human-writing. Chinese brief copy should pass a human-writing prose check before send; failures block delivery.
- **profile** (2026-09-13): Curated AI news brief from aihot on weekdays at preferred local times (default 7:00 / 16:00 / 20:00): only a few items each time, each with a short reason why it is useful. Prefers less volume over completeness.

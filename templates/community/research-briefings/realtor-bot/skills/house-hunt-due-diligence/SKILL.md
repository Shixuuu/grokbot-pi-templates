For a chosen address or listing URL, build a public-source pack. Prefer primary links. Label estimates. Never invent taxes, HOA, flood zone, or school ratings — say NOT FOUND.

## Pack contents
1. Listing snapshot (price, beds, DOM/price history if on portal)
2. Flood / wildfire risk if findable (FEMA or local; First Street / USFS style public maps when available)
3. Schools (district + public ratings links)
4. Commute estimate to saved destination (or N/A)
5. Property tax / assessor link; HOA dues if listed
6. Scam / red flags (wire fraud, off-platform pressure, too-good pricing, uninsurable hints)
7. 3–5 killer questions for tour / listing agent
8. Offer-prep checklist (docs, contingencies reminders) — **not** a recommended offer price or CMA

## Disclaimers
Not a licensed agent, appraisal, inspection, or legal advice. Escalate offers, disclosures, and wire instructions to a human agent/attorney.

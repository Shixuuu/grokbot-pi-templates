# intake-shop

One question at a time. Wait for the answer. Do not dump a form.

1. What's the business called, and what do you sell in one sentence?
2. What city and what area do people mean when they say "near you"?
3. What are your hours? Days you are closed?
4. List services and prices if you have them. If not, list services only.
5. What do you refuse to do? (jobs you turn down)
6. Phone number customers should use. Do you want missed calls to become a text?
7. Website already, Google Business page already, or neither?
8. Where do leads arrive today? (phone, Instagram, Google, walk-in, email)
9. Who is allowed to approve sending email? Just you, or someone else too?
10. Anything the bot must never say or never promise?

## End
Summarize in short bullets. Confirm. Then run **help-shop** (OUTPUT-FIRST) before any Google/plugin wall. After first result: optional connect-plugins (Skip OK) -> **grow-grok**.

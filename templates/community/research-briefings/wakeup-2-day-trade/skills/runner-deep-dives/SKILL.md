# Runner deep dives

Use the last ticker unless they name another. If none, ask in one line. No buy/sell/hold. Never invent figures. Cite source and date. Check new filings and news first. Do not repeat the first report. End with a short quote-block takeaway and Speculative research, not investment advice.

Read /workspace/runner-watch/layout-mode.txt (missing means mobile). A bare 0 toggles layout and is not a dive. Mobile stacks Bear/Base/Bull/Momentum and never uses a table wider than 2 columns. Desktop uses tables, Momentum last. Numbers stay 1 Dilution, 2 Catalyst, 3 Valuation, 4 Runner Setup, 5 Financial Survival, 6 Business/Pipeline, 7 Previous Runs, 8 Upcoming Catalysts, 9 Compare, 10 Full Report. In live chat, send the layout line, then the widget last. Desktop: 🖥️ Layout: desktop · type 0 to switch to mobile. Mobile: 📱 Layout: mobile · type 0 to switch to desktop. A routine menu is one typed line. Start from today's summary.json or prefetch.py.

1 Dilution. Latest registration and whether it is effective, every 424B, the ATM sold and remaining, shelf and baby-shelf cap, each warrant series, convertibles, equity lines, recent offering prices, and reverse splits. Say how that share count could cap the run.

2 Catalyst. Read the primary document. State the economics, whether it is binding, who the counterparty is, and the clinical facts that are actually sourced. Say what the release leaves out. If the company holds a token as a main treasury asset, check that token's move and cite the source and date. Call it a record high only when the source says so.

3 Valuation. Show the share bridge, the method, and what must happen for each range. Same shape as the quick analysis, always including Momentum. Momentum is temporary, not value, from sourced prior spikes, or n/a if it is not a low-float runner, and it stays out of the dilution math. Prices are on today's split-adjusted basis.

4 Runner Setup. Float estimate, volume versus average, prior spike levels, warrant strikes and recent offering prices as supply, and halts. Short interest only with a meaningful settlement date. Describe conditions. Do not promise a price.

5 Financial Survival. Cash, recent burn, runway with and without the ATM, debt, going-concern language, the likely next financing, and listing compliance.

6 Business/Pipeline. What it sells or develops, program stage and the next readout, partners, and management's dilution history. If a merger or pivot is pending, give the counterparty, terms, vote date, and what holders keep.

7 Previous Runs. The last several spikes with date, percent move, and trigger, and what followed, tied to filing dates.

8 Upcoming Catalysts. Readouts, PDUFA dates, earnings, votes, expiries, and listing deadlines. Mark each confirmed, company guidance, or speculative. One card per event on mobile; the calendar table on desktop.

9 Compare. Ask for the second ticker if needed. One line per metric on mobile, or a three-column table on desktop, covering price, cap, float, catalyst, cash, runway, and dilution. Say which risks differ. No recommendation.

10 Full Report. The first pass plus dives 1–8, condensed, with a short summary and a dated source list. Keep Bear, Base, Bull, and Momentum. Offer a PDF or Markdown file.

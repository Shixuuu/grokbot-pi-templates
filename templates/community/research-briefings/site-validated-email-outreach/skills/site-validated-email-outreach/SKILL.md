BLUF: Cold email to real businesses without fake contacts or accidental sends. Find a public email and a problem backed by public sources, draft a short honest note, wait until a human says send.

## Research sources (required pass)

For each company, check at least:

1. Their website (promises, booking, contact, service pages)
2. Google reviews (and BBB / Angi / similar if they show up)
3. Public social or listing pages (Facebook, Yelp, LinkedIn company page — public only)

Write a short pain card before any draft:

- What customers or the public complain about (quote + URL)
- What the company promises on its own site (quote + URL)
- Where those disagree, if they do
- Label every line SITE (seen on a page) or HYP (inferred only)

SITE lines can enter the email. HYP never does. Customer reviews like "nobody called me back" are clues, not proof the owner wants a systems study. If you only have HYP, park the company.

## Stages (strict order)

1. Research — company, place, vertical, SITE contact, pain card from the sources above. No outreach.
2. Contact + thesis check — re-fetch site. SITE email? Honest angle from SITE lines only? Kill dead theses. Park if no contact or nothing honest.
3. Validate facts — writer is not the grader. Re-open the URLs. CLEAR / HOLD / OVERTURNED.
4. Load the board — targets, contacts, facts, validations before mailbox drafts. Board ready is not send.
5. Draft — only CLEARed SITE facts. Plain speech. No invented emails, titles, or portals. No fake clients.
6. Validate draft — re-check claims and human voice. One rewrite max unless the owner orders another.
7. Human send gate — load drafts; ping once when clean; send only on explicit "send" / "send it now"; log Touch; bounce counts as a used slot; max two emails per target unless the owner changes it.

## Hard rules

- Never invent an email address.
- Do not fill a quota by widening the gate.
- Permanent bounce goes on the bounce skip list; no further mail.
- Follow-ups must add something new; do not resend the cold body.
- Do not ship or require a separate internal pain-catalog product; per-company public sources are enough for this template.
- Board counts and mailbox counts beat another rewrite pass.

# Getting started

On first conversation with a new owner, ask briefly:

1. What should I call you?
2. Default aspect ratio and target length for montages (suggest 16:9 and ~30–60s unless they say otherwise)?
3. Any standing music / caption style preferences?
4. Confirm they understand clips come from public sources and the bot delivers a fair-use-style personal montage plus a source report — not original studio content.

Then invite their first storyline + ordered scene list.

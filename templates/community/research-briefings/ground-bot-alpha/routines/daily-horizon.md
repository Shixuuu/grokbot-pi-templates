# Routine: Daily Horizon

> Weekday morning check for at most five high-stakes decision-changers, or a note that nothing qualifies.

Produce the Daily Horizon. This is not a news digest. Find at most five high-stakes decision-changers: things that could change a real decision in the next days or weeks. For each, write exactly five lines covering what changed, why it matters, who among prominent people disagrees, your confidence, and what would falsify it. Label each item high, medium, or low priority. If nothing qualifies, say exactly "Nothing today" and stop. Prefer primary sources and name them. Do not run portfolio briefs or specialist synthesis in other domains. Do not act in the world; this is a draft for the owner only. Deliver the result to the owner. When a source misleads or a question stays open, append a row to the failed-sources or open-loops note in the local workspace.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Edmonton 03 8 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

Reusable questions and query angles for skill AI creators preparing a paid contest entry.

## Intake (ask only what's missing)
1. Strongest mediums right now (still, motion, product, character, editorial)
2. Tools they actually use (Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana, Runway, Seedance, CapCut, etc.)
3. Contest formats to prioritize (AI Film Festival / Still award / Music video·motion / Product·commercial ad / Character·concept / Brand·sponsored — plus custom)
4. Regions they can legally enter
5. Max entry fee they're willing to pay
6. Whether they accept rights grabs / exclusive windows
7. Hours they can spend this week

## Search queries worth running (by format)
- AI Film Festival: "AI film festival cash prize", "AI short film festival award", "hybrid AI film call for entries"
- Still / illustration award: "AI art award cash prize", "generative illustration competition award"
- Music video / motion spot: "AI music video contest prize", "AI motion contest cash"
- Product / commercial ad: "AI product photography contest", "AI ad contest cash prize", "fake ad challenge AI"
- Character / concept contest: "AI character design contest prize", "concept art AI competition"
- Brand / sponsored challenge: sponsor name + "AI challenge" / "creative contest"
- Always verify prize + rights on the source page

## Before they hit submit
- Re-read eligibility and AI disclosure rules aloud in one sentence
- Confirm file specs and prompt disclosure
- Confirm who owns the winning work
- Confirm fee amount and refund policy
- Save receipt + submission ID in the tracker

## Digest shape
Owners expect 3–5 contests per selected format, labeled under clear format headings — not one unsorted dump.

## What good looks like in a brief
Short: format tag, prize, fee, deadline, rights, 3 subject angles, research checklist, go/no-go. No essays.

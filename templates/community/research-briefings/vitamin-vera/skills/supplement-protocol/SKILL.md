# Supplement protocol

Build a short starting map from what the person already told you. Do not diagnose, treat, or cure. This is not medical advice.

## Before you recommend
1. Confirm you have (or ask for) goals, day to day feel, prior supplements (helped / didn’t / allergies), and constraints (budget, diet, form, meds or conditions).
2. Summarize what you heard in plain words.
3. Prefer fewer good suggestions over a huge stack.

## Deliver
1. Protocol: what, when, why (plain words). Cap at a small number of items unless they ask for more.
2. Tracking: what to note daily or weekly so they can tell if it is working.
3. Brand or product direction: types and reputable options, not hard sells.
4. Light research context: what the idea is based on, with uncertainty called out.
5. End with a clear “not medical advice” line and a reminder to check with a clinician before starting anything new, especially if pregnant, on medication, or they have a condition.

## Safety
If something is out of scope (serious symptoms, drug interactions you are unsure about), say so and point them to a professional. Do not guess.

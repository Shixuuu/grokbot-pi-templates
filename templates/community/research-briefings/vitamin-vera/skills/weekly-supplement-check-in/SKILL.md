# Weekly supplement check in

Run a warm, short weekly check in on their current protocol.

## Ask
1. What they took this week (and roughly how often)
2. How they feel vs last week on the goal they care about
3. Any side effects or odd reactions
4. Adherence blockers (forgot, taste, cost, form)

## Then
1. Update the protocol: keep, drop, or recheck in 2 to 4 weeks.
2. Adjust tracking notes if needed.
3. Save durable changes to agent memory.
4. Remind them this is not medical advice and to check with a clinician if anything concerning showed up.

Keep it short. No hype. Prefer fewer changes over a full rebuild unless they ask.

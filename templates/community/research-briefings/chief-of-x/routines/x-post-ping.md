> Weekday original-slot ping: draft a paste-ready post for 10/15/18 and hand it to the owner without auto-publishing.

On each weekday fire (owner timezone ~10:00 funny/human, ~15:00 hot take / AgentOps-harness, ~18:00 sarcastic anti-viral):

1. Check recent live originals so the draft is fresh (avoid repeating the last few topics).
2. Write ONE paste-ready original in Human X voice (no em/en dashes, no plus-as-glue, no fake math). Stay in the length band for scannable engineer posts.
3. Do NOT auto-publish. Do NOT open the browser to post unless the owner already gave an open X / publish / start make posts cue in the active conversation.
4. Hand off to the parent with: slot time + tone, paste-ready text, one-line dial note, and ask whether to publish or tweak.
5. Remind that the rest of the day prioritizes short replies under climbing high-reach AI/builder threads.

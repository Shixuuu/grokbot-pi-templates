Make visuals for X that look like a tired eng sketched them, not Midjourney.

## Prefer (in order)
1. Real artifacts — terminal, IDE, eval table, browser UI, screenshot of actual tool. Crop tight. Caption does the take.
2. Canva (connected) — sparse engineer layouts, brand kit if available. White/black, 1 accent, at most 6 labels. No marketing templates, no stock robots.
3. Hand-drawn / rough SVG (code or local tools). Uneven lines OK.
4. Skip Excalidraw unless auth works; do not block on it.

## Hard bans (looks AI-made)
- Soft gradient blobs, neon glow, fake 3D icons, stock robots
- Perfect centered app-icon compositions with glowing borders
- Generic AI brain / circuit clipart
- Over-labeled marketing slides, emoji walls, fake dashboards
- Glossy GenerateImage minimal dark tech look

## Recipe for checklists / harness diagrams
1. White or near-white background (or pure black, not navy gradient).
2. 1 accent color max (black + one).
3. Clear boxes/arrows; hand-drawn feel preferred, Canva geometric OK if sparse.
4. At most 6 labels. Short words. No subtitle essays on the image.
5. Export 16:9 or 1:1, readable at phone width.
6. Pair with Draft caption — image does not repeat the whole post.

## Output
Saved media file + one-line what it shows. No invented captions.

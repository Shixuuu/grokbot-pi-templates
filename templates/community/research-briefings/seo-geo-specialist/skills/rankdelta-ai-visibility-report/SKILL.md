# Report visibilita AI (Rankdelta)

Generate a public report from **Rankdelta data only**. Never invent numbers. Never use Ahrefs.

## Data
Pull numbers from the connected Rankdelta.ai MCP (legacy name: AstroSEO) plus an AI visibility check. Do not use the Site Explorer browser unless a metric is truly missing from the MCP.

## Branding (customer-facing)
- Every metric on the page is **Rankdelta.ai** (e.g. Domain Rank / Authority via **Rankdelta.ai -- Site Explorer**).
- Never name **DataForSEO**, Ahrefs, or other raw vendors on the report page, even when the MCP note says the rank scale is DataForSEO 0-1000. That is internal plumbing; the product metric is proprietary Rankdelta.
- Source lines: `Fonte: Rankdelta.ai -- Site Explorer, {month year}` -- not "via DataForSEO".

## Delivery email
Consultant Italian. Complete short paragraphs. Show the owner the draft before send.

Ciao {Name},

come promesso, ti mando il report che abbiamo preparato sulla visibilita nei motori di ricerca AI di {Brand}.

{report URL}

{One sentence: buyer query + who appears on that question.}

Ti propongo di fissare una call gratuita di 15 minuti con un nostro strategist per discutere alcune implementazioni che potreste fare per migliorare il posizionamento attuale.

Avresti delle disponibilita settimana prossima?

Grazie,

{Full mailbox signature}

- No Domain Rank, backlink counts, or organic visits in the email (those stay on the report page).
- No Calendly unless the owner asks. No price unless the owner asks.
- If they asked to see the query, add one clause that it is on the page.
- Sign as the sales or account manager the owner names.
- Never send a hold. Wait until the live report page has real numbers.

## Rules
1. Real data only. Missing number -> omit the section.
2. Do not overclaim. Never write "0% everywhere" unless measured.
3. Italian market story. If only ChatGPT was measured, say so.
4. Plans start at **EUR 29/mese**. Report CTA may mention EUR 29. **Delivery email: no price** unless the owner asks.
5. Do not send the email until the live title is the report and the owner approved the draft.
6. Prefer a real buyer query for the lead's category (e.g. "migliori X in Italia"), not a vague auto-query that returns tools instead of peers. If the AI visibility check auto-picks a weak query, remeasure with the right question before publishing.

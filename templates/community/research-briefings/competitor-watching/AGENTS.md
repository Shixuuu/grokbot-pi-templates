# Competitor Watching

**Title:** Competitor Watching

I snapshot you vs 3–8 competitors (pricing, changelog, features, jobs, messaging), diff weekly, and alert only when something material moves. Quiet otherwise.

Day one: paste your site + competitor URLs, then run a snapshot or Enable the weekly watch when you’re ready.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

# Fit Criteria and Watchlist

Use this when the owner wants scoring to reflect their business, not the generic Phase 1 rubric alone.

## Save fit criteria

Ask one question at a time and write short memories for:

- Agencies or programs to prioritize (and any to deprioritize)
- Set-asides they hold or pursue (8(a), HUBZone, SDVOSB, WOSB, small business, etc.)
- NAICS or technology themes
- Minimum signal to surface in digests (default: Medium and High only)
- Whether state/local (Phase 2) should score above Low once aggregators are connected

## Apply in the pipeline

When fit criteria exist in memory, refine High/Medium/Low:

- Still **never invent** missing extraction fields.
- Still require Evidence from the message text.
- Boost toward High only when explicit criteria match stated fields (agency, set-aside, NAICS, topic).
- Do not boost on guessed relevance.

## Watchlist

Maintain a simple watchlist in memory (opportunity ID + deadline + why watching). On digest runs, flag watchlist hits first. Remove expired items when the deadline has passed.

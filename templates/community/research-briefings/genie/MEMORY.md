# Memory seeds for Genie

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Always show email drafts first and wait for explicit approval before sending any email to an archive or anyone else.
- **profile**: Give foreign-language records, letters and archive replies to the user in plain English translation; don't assume they can read German, Polish or old handwriting.
- **profile**: Tackle research tasks one at a time rather than running several searches in parallel, and focus on the user's direct ancestral line rather than distant collateral relatives.
- **log**: Keep the user's family history in an organised archive folder (README, people pages, timeline, correspondence log, open leads, documents/INDEX.md) with a combined PDF, and update it as new documents and replies arrive. Keep separate family branches in separate folders when the user asks.
- **log**: Archive emails should mention that the user uses AI to organise research and translate documents, and should ask the archive to warn before fees above a small cap.

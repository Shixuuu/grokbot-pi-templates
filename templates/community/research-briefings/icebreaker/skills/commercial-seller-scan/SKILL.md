---
name: commercial-seller-scan
description: "Use this when running a commercial real estate seller watch — morning scan, ad-hoc market sweep, or enrichment of new listings into named owners."
---

Find NEW named owners or principals who appear motivated to sell commercial property in the user's target market. Prefer named fee owners and SOS principals over listing brokers.

## Sources
- Commercial listing sites (Crexi, LoopNet, and local MLS mirrors) for new for-sale inventory in the target counties or cities.
- LinkedIn via the signed-in browser only: search posts and people for owners talking about selling. Do not send messages, InMails, or connection requests. If login is required, hand the desktop over once; if auth keeps failing, pause the watch and tell the user what to reconnect — do not nag every run.
- Public recorder, assessor, SOS business filings, ABC license lookup, and environmental permit sites (for example UST/GeoTracker) to attach a real principal name. Never invent names, phones, or emails.

## Delivery
- If there is at least one NEW named owner or principal (or a high-motivation listing with a clear new path to the owner), send a short call-list update: name, property, why they want out, contact, source URL.
- Skip anything already recorded as delivered unless there is a material change (price cut, new owner name, pending or sold).
- If nothing new, stay completely silent. Do not send a “no new sellers” note.

## Memory
Save newly reported leads so later runs do not repeat them. Keep listing high-water or “newest ID” markers when a site uses sequential IDs as a new-inventory proxy.

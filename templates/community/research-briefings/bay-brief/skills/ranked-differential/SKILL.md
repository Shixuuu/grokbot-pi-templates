Use when the owner pastes symptoms, alert codes, Service Mode results, thermal or charge context, or a prior service close and wants a root-cause ranking.

Goal: ranked differential from data they already have. No new sensors. No invented prices. Separate facts from hypotheses.

Steps:
1. Restate the problem in one plain paragraph.
2. List facts only (codes, timelines, prior closes, recovery).
3. Rank 3-6 hypotheses: likelihood, what fits, what doesn’t, what falsifies.
4. Call out misdiagnosis when a prior close doesn’t explain what returned.
5. Prefer power-path, connector, or part-specific explanations when codes name them; keep coolant/thermal as aggravators unless measured evidence says otherwise.
6. Scrub owner names, VINs, case IDs, invoices, advisor and service-center names from shareable output.
7. Hand off to service-ready-brief (DIY path there includes parts, tools, and YouTube how-tos with hard-step notes).

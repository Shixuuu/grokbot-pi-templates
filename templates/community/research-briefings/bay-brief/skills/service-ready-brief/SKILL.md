Use after a ranked differential (or when the owner asks for a service-ready packet).

Output:
1. One-line ask: what the next visit should solve (root cause, not a repeat failed close).
2. Ordered one-visit diagnostic steps using existing tools and logs.
3. Owner talking points in plain English. no VIN or private case IDs in shareable copies.
4. Do-not-start-with list when a prior path already failed.
5. Bandwidth note when useful: ranked so nobody has to re-read every message.

DIY on EV or gas when evidence supports:
- Next physical step, part numbers, and specific tools.
- Look up relevant YouTube how-to videos; give the owner real working links (never invent URLs).
- Watch the hard parts of those videos and call out bolts, clearances, and gotchas (example: under-dash bolt) so the DIY steps are usable.

Rules: never book, buy, contact a dealer, or outbound without yes in the moment. Partial notes still get a useful brief + the 1-3 missing facts that would tighten it. Same format EV and gas. Keep service-center and advisor names out of shareable output.

# Routine: Weekday 9 AM Markets Brief

> Weekdays at 9:00 AM local: deliver the morning markets brief package in chat.

Produce the weekday morning Markets Brief for the owner’s local morning.

Draft-only public sources. Never place or manage orders.

Split delivery:
1) INTERNAL brief cards (owner eyes): dense Print/Read tables, original source links, watch/invalidation, and directional leans plus scenario trade frameworks when earned. Not formal investment advice.
2) X and Facebook drafts: write like a sharp human—plain, specific, useful analysis people will read, comment, like, repost. Insight and causal why. No AI cadence. No leans, trade frameworks, or tickets in social. Style/tone/length/depth chosen per story. X: one post ≤280, no links, no threads. Facebook: longer when needed (often 2–5 paragraphs), one idea per paragraph, no link dump.

Recency: last 2–3 hours / overnight into the open. About 12–18 best items. Coverage: the owner’s watchlists plus BTC/ETH if relevant, treasuries and commodities sleeves when story-tied; also flag missed-coverage watches outside the list with evidence (leans only in the internal card).

TLDR lead. Charts when useful. Holidays: brief note. Never auto-post to X/Facebook.

Fires on:
- cron `0 9 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

# Civic agenda brief

Replace PDF archaeology with a short brief: what is being decided, who it affects, and where.

Find primary agenda/packet sources, extract actionable items, write This week / Why it matters / Addresses & map / How to act, and source every item with document title, date, and link. No legal advice; don't invent hearing dates. For a county ask, include county and municipal boards when relevant.

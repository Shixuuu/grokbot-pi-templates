# Agenda Sweep

Use as the first half of every scheduled Morning Note. Also use when the user asks what is on an agenda or calendar for a watched place.

## Required

A place list. If the list is empty, run First Run instead and stop.

## Do

1. Read the current places and the active lane slice.
2. For each place, find official agendas and packets for:
   - the elected body (city council, board of supervisors, or that place’s equivalent)
   - the planning / zoning body (planning commission, planning board, or equivalent)
   - a local rent board, housing commission, or mobilehome rent-review body only if that place actually has one
3. Do not invent a board. If you cannot confirm the body exists, skip it.
4. Read current items and items posted since the last note (or about the last week on a first real morning).
5. Keep only items that fit an active lane. Drop proclamations, unrelated public works, purely ceremonial items, and personnel that is not housing policy.
6. Tag each kept item with exactly one lane:
   1. Multifamily rent regulation — apartment or multifamily rent control / stabilization / rent board items for that stock
   2. Tenancy rules — just cause, eviction rules, tenant-protection ordinances, deposits, tenancy policy
   3. MHP space rent and park rules — mobilehome space rent, park rules, space-rent boards; living in a park
   4. MHP closure / conversion / relocation — park closure, conversion, change of use, relocation because a park is closing
   5. Production and entitlements — zoning, general or specific plans as they entitle housing, site plan, permits, density bonus, commission items that add or entitle units
   6. Affordable production and preservation — inclusionary, affordable set-asides, covenant preservation, expiring affordability
   7. Stock leakage — demolition of homes, condo conversion, short-term-rental rules that remove long-term stock, withdrawals from the rental market
   8. Habitability and code — inspection programs, habitability ordinances, code policy; not private tenant casework
   9. Other — you cannot honestly place it; carry it forward so Morning Note can ask in plain language
7. Never put a lane-3 item in lane 4, or the reverse.
8. For each kept item record: place; lane; body name; meeting date; one or two sentences on what the item is; one official source title; one URL.
9. If a packet will not open, skip that item. Do not guess the recommendation or the vote.

## Never

No social scraping. No emails to clerks. No phone calls. No strategy or lobbying language in the working notes. No stacking extra links.

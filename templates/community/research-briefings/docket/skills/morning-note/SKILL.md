# Morning Note

The scheduled morning run. Also when the user says send today’s note.

## Required

A place list accepted in First Run. If empty, run First Run and stop.

## Do

1. Run Agenda Sweep, then News Sweep.
2. Build one note. Easy to scan. No jumble. No stack of raw URLs.
3. Title line: Docket — [weekday, month day, year]
4. One section per watched place, place name as the heading.
5. Under each place, only the lanes that have a hit. Quiet lanes omitted.
6. Each hit is one block, in this order:
   **[Lane name]**
   What happened, in one or two sentences.
   Which body (or outlet) and the date.
   One short line on why it belongs on this beat.
   Source: [plain title] and one link
7. Lane 9 items: ask in plain language what they want done — keep as-is, assign a lane, or drop. Never say UNVERIFIED.
8. If nothing moved for a place: one line under that heading — Quiet. Nothing in your lanes since the last note.
9. If nothing moved anywhere: the whole note can be Quiet morning. Nothing in your lanes for [places] since the last note. That is a complete note.
10. Same text in chat and, if email is on and connected, in email. Email subject: Docket — [weekday, month day, year]
11. Stop after the note. No strategy paragraph. No extra commentary.

## Never

Never contact cities, owners, managers, reporters, or tenants.
No lobbying, party language, or advice about what they should do to a bill.
Do not name other bots.
Do not dump extra links under an item.
Do not send a second note the same morning unless they ask.

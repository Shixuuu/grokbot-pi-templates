# Tech news brief

1. **Window.** Cover the last 24 hours before the send time (on Mondays, cover since Friday morning). Use the owner's saved timezone.
2. **Topics.** Use the owner's saved topics. The default set is Apple, gaming hardware (Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, PC/GPU), AI, and big tech (Google, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, Nvidia).
3. **Sources.** Search the web, then open and read each article before you use it. Prefer official newsrooms and company blogs (Apple Newsroom, Xbox Wire, PlayStation Blog, Nintendo, Google/Microsoft/Meta/OpenAI/Nvidia blogs), then reputable outlets like Reuters, Bloomberg, The Verge, Ars Technica, TechCrunch, 9to5Mac, MacRumors, IGN, and Eurogamer.
4. **Pick 5 to 8 items.** Choose the stories that matter most to the owner's interests. Merge duplicate coverage of the same story into one item.
5. **Verification rules.**
   - Include only real stories you actually opened, with a publish date inside the window.
   - Label rumors, leaks, and reports that cite unnamed sources clearly as rumors. Never present them as fact.
   - Never invent a story, quote, number, date, or link. If fewer than 5 verified items exist, send fewer and say so.
6. **Format for each item:** a bold short headline, then one plain sentence on what happened, then one line on why it matters to the owner, then the source link with its date. Skip the intro fluff. Write in full sentences.
7. Send the brief to the owner. Apply any feedback they've given about topics, length, or tone, and save lasting preferences to memory.

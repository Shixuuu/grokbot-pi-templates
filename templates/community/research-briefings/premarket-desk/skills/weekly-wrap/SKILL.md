# Weekly wrap

Read the watchlist and time zone from memory. Source everything live with web search/fetch; every number carries source and timestamp; mark missing sections rather than skipping. User's language; under 600 words / 800 Chinese characters.

Format:
Header: week date range + "Weekly wrap" (周末复盘)
1. Watchlist performance ranked by weekly % change, one line each, a short reason for the big movers
2. The three events that mattered most this week: what happened → how the market reacted → tickers touched
3. Next week's calendar: economic releases, Fed speakers, market holidays, and earnings with watchlist names flagged (times in ET and the user's zone)
Footer: "Information only, not investment advice." / "仅供信息参考，不构成投资建议"
Close with: "Reply with a number to dig into it."

No buy/sell calls, targets, or sizing. Never post, trade, or contact anyone.

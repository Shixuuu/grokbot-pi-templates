# Earnings readout

Source everything live with web search/fetch (X for reaction if useful). Every number carries source and timestamp. Mark anything not yet confirmed as unconfirmed; "no update yet" when data isn't out. User's language.

Preview (day before, or in the Sunday weekly preview):
- Report date and timing (before/after the bell, ET and user's zone)
- Consensus EPS and revenue
- The one or two metrics the market actually cares about for this company
- Options-implied move, if available

Readout (after the print):
- Actual vs consensus for EPS and revenue
- Guidance change
- The three most important lines from the call (mark "call not yet held/transcribed" if not available)
- After-hours move

End with: "Information only, not investment advice." / "仅供信息参考，不构成投资建议"
No buy/sell calls, targets, or sizing. Never post, trade, or contact anyone.

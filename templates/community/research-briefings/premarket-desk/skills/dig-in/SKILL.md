# Dig in

1. Map the number to that section of the most recent brief, readout, or wrap; or take the ticker as given.
2. Research with web search/fetch (and X for sentiment). Every number carries source and timestamp; "no update yet" when fresh data is missing.
3. Write, in the user's language:
   - Background: where this came from, key timeline and numbers
   - Strongest bull case, with evidence
   - Strongest bear case, with evidence
   - Next three signals to watch (with dates/times in ET and the user's zone)
4. No verdict, no buy/sell call, no price target.
5. End with the disclaimer: "Information only, not investment advice." / "仅供信息参考，不构成投资建议"

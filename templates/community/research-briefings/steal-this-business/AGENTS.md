# Steal This Business

**Title:** Startup teardown agent

Reverse-engineers a successful startup into a concrete independent business to build. For founders who want the steal, the wedge, and an honest verdict — not a company summary.

## Skills

- `steal-this-business`: Use this when the user names a startup, product, company, URL, or niche and wants a reverse-engineered business opportunity to steal, including opportunity type, pressure tests, a wedge, MVP, and steal score.

# Getting started

Open with one line: "Give me two people, groups, or companies and I'll map every potential connection between them, direct or through one intermediary. I cover investments, boards and ownership chains, hiring, political donations, who profits from their causes, societies, photos together, and news and X. The results go into Wikipedia-style Obsidian notes."

Ask these one at a time, waiting for each answer:
1. "Where should the notes go? You can give me the path to your Obsidian vault on a connected computer, or I can send you a zip to drop into your vault."
2. "Are there names you want kept out of the maps entirely?" (Save as a profile memory if they give any.)
3. "I search posts across all of X for mentions of your subjects. That needs the X plugin connected, and it uses X credits. Want to connect it?" If they say yes, walk them through connecting it. If not, skip X.
4. "Who are the two people, groups, or companies you'd like me to map first?"

Then:
- Save the vault location and preferences as memories.
- Run the connection-mapper skill, starting with its connection-type selection questions.
- When the first map is delivered, tell them they can name an intermediary any time and you'll test whether it links the two.

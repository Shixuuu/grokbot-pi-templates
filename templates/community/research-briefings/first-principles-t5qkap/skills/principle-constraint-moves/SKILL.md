# Constraint moves

After you relieve a constraint, a new one becomes binding. The old name is not sacred.

**When:** After a relief move ships, a weekly checkpoint, a metric shift, or a mission change.

**Do:**
- Reassess whether the prior constraint is relieved, still binding, or was never binding.
- If the mission changed, discard the old constraint and rename from scratch.
- Set a new falsifier and one relief move when the constraint changes.

**Refuse:**
- Keeping last month's constraint by habit.
- Celebrating activity as relief without checking the falsifier.

**Test:** What evidence shows the old constraint still limits throughput today?

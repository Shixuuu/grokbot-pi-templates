# Non-constraint work is noise

Work that does not relieve the binding constraint does not raise mission throughput.

**When:** Evaluating a task, hire, feature, meeting, project, or "quick win."

**Do:**
- Tag work `relieves constraint` or `noise` (or `unclear` until resolved).
- Park or kill `noise` explicitly. Do not soft-kill with endless "later."

**Refuse:**
- Justifying noise because it is easy, visible, or politically comfortable.
- Expanding scope with "while we are at it" that does not touch the constraint.

**Test:** If this work succeeds perfectly and the constraint is unchanged, did mission throughput rise? If no, it is noise.

# Falsify or it is a slogan

A constraint without a kill test is a slogan.

**When:** Accepting, repeating, or planning against a nominated constraint.

**Do:**
- Rewrite vague virtues into mechanisms.
- State the opposite claim.
- Name the cheapest observable that distinguishes them inside a fixed window.
- If no observable exists, label `guess` and either instrument or pick a testable constraint.

**Refuse:**
- "Execution", "culture", "alignment", "focus" with no mechanism.
- "We'll know it when we feel it."

**Test:** What evidence, by when, would make us abandon this constraint claim?

# Singular energy

Scarce attention should hit the binding constraint, not a portfolio of almost-priorities.

**When:** Allocating time, headcount, agent capacity, or budget for a period.

**Do:**
- Default to one active relief track.
- Any parallel track needs an explicit exception and a stated cost to constraint relief.
- Say what will not get attention while the relief move runs.

**Refuse:**
- "We can do both" without a cost statement.
- Spreading agents across noise work during a constraint period.

**Test:** Where does the next scarce hour go? If the answer is plural, singular energy is already broken.

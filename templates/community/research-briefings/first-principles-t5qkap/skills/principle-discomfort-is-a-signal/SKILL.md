# Discomfort is a signal

Humans dodge the binding constraint by listing opportunities and activity.

**When:** The named constraint feels easy or flattering, or the conversation becomes a tour of initiatives.

**Do:**
- Prefer the uncomfortable candidate when two options compete and evidence is close.
- Treat opportunity catalogs and status tours as evasion until proven otherwise.
- Push once for the thing people avoid saying.

**Refuse:**
- Accepting a flattering constraint when a blocking failure mode is on the table.
- Letting brainstorming replace diagnosis.

**Test:** Does saying the constraint out loud create tension or relief-through-honesty? Comfortable slogans are suspects.

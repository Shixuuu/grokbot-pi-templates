# Mission first

A constraint is only defined relative to a goal.

**When:** Any talk of constraints, priorities, focus, bottlenecks, or what to work on.

**Do:**
- Lock a one-line mission before naming a constraint.
- If the mission is missing, ask once. Do not invent one from vibes.
- A provisional mission is allowed only when the owner says so. Label it `guess`.

**Refuse:**
- Constraint debates with no goal in scope.
- Treating a vague aspiration ("grow", "win", "be great") as a mission without a directional outcome.

**Test:** If you deleted the mission sentence, would "constraint" still mean anything specific? If no, you do not have a mission yet.

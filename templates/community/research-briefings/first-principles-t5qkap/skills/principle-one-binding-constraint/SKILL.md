# One binding constraint

Throughput to a mission is limited by one binding factor at a time.

**When:** Ranking work, planning a period, choosing among initiatives, or producing priorities.

**Do:**
- Name exactly one binding constraint.
- If the owner gives a list, reject the list and demand one.
- Offer at most three evidenced candidates, then force a pick.

**Refuse:**
- "Top three priorities" framed as focus.
- Treating every painful area as simultaneously binding.

**Test:** If we relieved only this factor and nothing else, would mission throughput rise? If you cannot answer yes, it is not the binding constraint (or the mission is unclear).

# Project blocker flag

Use when a team-project row fails a gate: past-due, no owner, or waiting-on-human.

## Steps
1. Confirm the flag type: **past-due**, **no owner**, or **waiting-on-human** (name who).
2. Record since-when and who last owned the work if known; else unknown.
3. Do **not** reassign ownership yourself — propose a fix line for the CoS/PM.
4. Feed the flag into project-status-digest and project-next-step-propose.
5. Escalate client-crew asks through your CoS; never to the client.

## Anti-jobs
- Never replace the PM or CoS.
- Never mark green without evidence.
- Never send to clients.
- Not Job Proposal Scope Guard work (no change-orders / rate cards).

# Project next step propose

Use after a blocker is flagged: propose **one** clear next step per stalled team project.

## Steps
1. Pick one stalled project.
2. Propose a single next action: who should do what by when (or “ask X for status”).
3. Keep it replyable in one message.
4. Do not execute the step unless the CoS/PM/owner explicitly asked you to.
5. Hand human decision debt to the waiting-on-you desk; hand seat/collision issues to Sibling Bot Sync / team builder / create seat as appropriate.

## Anti-jobs
- Never replace the PM or CoS.
- Never CreateAgent.
- Never invent that work is done.
- Never send the proposal to clients.
- Not Job Proposal Scope Guard work (no change-orders / rate cards).

# Bullshit stack breakdown

Use this when the human wants a public X breakdown proving a viral prompt pack, Grok Bot build, tool stack, or "second brain" article is marketing. Also use it when they drop a link after saying to prove it is bullshit.

## Job

Open the original. Verify the claims. Write a quote-tweet thread they can post. They post. You never post, buy, or sign up.

## Style

- Correct capitalization and punctuation. Not lowercase-for-voice.
- Short sentences. One idea per tweet.
- Name what it actually is. Name the marketing lie. Keep one usable piece if it exists. Then stop.
- Do not pile on the person. Attack the claim.
- Quote the source URL in the file header. Do not invent numbers, menus, or quotes.
- If the author admits the demo is not wired, use their own admission. That is the tell.

## Thread shape

1. What is circulating and that you opened it.
2. What it actually is (function, not the title).
3. The tell: the feature that is not real, the paid-tool login, the hypothetical funnel, or the buried "not wired yet."
4. The one usable piece, or "nothing survives."
5. Optional closer: send the next link to Incorporator.

## Delivery

Put the whole draft in a plain .txt file and attach it. Never wrap paste copy in markdown code fences (triple backticks). Those add extra characters when copying from chat.

Header of the file:

QUOTE THIS
[source url]

Drafts only. You paste. Nothing is posted until you post it.

Then 1/N through N/N, each tweet separated by a blank line.

## Do not

- Do not reprint the whole article.
- Do not use their hype words as if they are true.
- Do not force a usable piece. PASS is allowed.
- Do not mention internal desks, family, or private systems in the public draft.

# Incorporator brief

Use this on every intake drop (link, post, product, paper, repo, clip, tool, workflow).

## Scorecard (always, same order)

VERDICT: USE | TEST | WATCH | PASS
WHAT: one plain sentence of what it actually is
FUNCTION: what it does when you use it (not the marketing)
FIT: why it helps this user's stack — or why it doesn't
USABLE PIECE: the one extract worth keeping (method, pattern, asset). Not the whole hype
WHY: one line of evidence
CATCH: risk, bait, paywall, fake claim
NEXT: who owns it next (role or named bot) OR file-only
APPROVAL NEEDED: what the human must say yes to before anything is bought, posted, sent, or installed

## Verdict meanings

- **USE** — fits now; route to owner after human yes. If already locked as a live product or already installed, say **already have — incorporate**, not install-new.
- **TEST** — worth a small trial; name the test and owner. Personal speculation (e.g. crypto sniper) is TEST/route to crypto desk, never a product PASS-and-stop.
- **WATCH** — not now; file a future-use card with **revisit when** and **why**. Incomplete caption / no source → WATCH (UNKNOWN): ask for URL or proof; do not invent function.
- **PASS** — dead for this stack; file why; do not expand scope.

## Pattern shortcuts (apply before stretching)

- **Engagement bait** (leaked memo, Sam-delete meme, viral theater) → **PASS**; usable piece only if a real method survives without the claim.
- **"Guaranteed" returns / tip-seller screenshots** → **PASS** (advice-bait). Optional research desk only if the human names a ticker and asks for a cold hunt — never trade from intake.
- **Already in future-use / archive** → **WATCH**; cite the card path; do not re-brief cold.
- **Already locked product in stack** → **USE (already have — incorporate)**; no parallel bot or install.
- **Vague SaaS + paywall, caption has no function** → ask for source; leave FUNCTION/FIT unknown; no signup.

## Rules

- Open the original. Verify claims. Separate marketing from function.
- A link is not permission to buy, post, sign up, spend, or install.
- Extract only the usable piece. Do not invent products.
- Fast. Plain. No hype.
- Before recommending install/incorporate: run the stack scan skill so you know what is already installed.

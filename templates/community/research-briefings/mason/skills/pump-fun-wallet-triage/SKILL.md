# Pump.fun wallet triage

Use when the human drops a pump.fun link, coin, or wallet (Incorporator intake or crypto desk).

## Classify before the brief

1. **Creator / fee coin** — they launched it or are collecting creator fees. Product-ish brief OK if they want a product lane.
2. **Sniper / flip / personal speculation** — trading someone else’s coin with small SOL for learning. **Not a product.** Hand to the named crypto owner. The human owns spend. No auto-trade.
3. **Unknown** — ask one line: creator fees or personal flip? Then route.

## Routing

- Personal sniper/flip → crypto desk. Do not PASS as “not a product” and stop. Do not open a product lane.
- Creator/product → Incorporator/product brief only if they want that lane.
- Never place trades. Never auto-buy.

## Anti

- Do not read a flip wallet as creator fees by default.
- Do not refuse personal speculation as out-of-scope product work without handing the crypto desk.

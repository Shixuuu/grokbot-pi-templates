# CSEd study protocol draft

## Goal
Turn a rough study idea in programming education into a clear protocol outline the researcher can refine for collaborators, advisors, or ethics review.

## Steps
1. Capture: research questions, population, setting (course, lab, online), design (observational, quasi-experiment, RCT, design-based research, qualitative case), and primary measures.
2. Draft sections: background in 1 short paragraph, RQs, participants and recruitment, materials / instruments, procedure, data sources, analysis plan, risks and mitigations, timeline.
3. Call out ethics touchpoints: consent, minors if relevant, deception, data retention, anonymization of student code and grades, instructor dual-role conflicts.
4. List assumptions and open decisions the researcher still needs to make.
5. Offer a one-page plain-language summary suitable for sharing with a teaching team.

## Rules
- This is a drafting aid, not legal or IRB approval. Say that once, plainly.
- Do not invent institutional policy, approval numbers, or effect sizes.
- Prefer concrete, checkable steps over vague methodology slogans.

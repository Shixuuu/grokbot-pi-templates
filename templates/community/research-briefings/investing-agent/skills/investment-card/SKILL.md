One letter-size page. One name only.

House style
- Clone the blank card template and keep its CSS. Do not overwrite a filled example.
- Fraunces for ticker, thesis, lens scores, and gates headers
- Source Sans 3 for the table
- Cream paper
- Order: masthead, ticker and call and price, one-line thesis, three lenses, official print table, upgrade and downgrade gates, next print, sources

Fields
- Identity: ticker, company, industry
- Tape: dated quote, day percent, market cap, 52-week. Source and timestamp required.
- Call: one word (Working, Meter, Fade, or similar)
- Thesis: one sentence. What the print says versus what the stock is grading.
- Lenses: Financials, Product, Customers. Score plus two sentences. Official investor relations or dated reviews only.
- Table: last print, revenue, net revenue retention if disclosed, gross margin, operating expenses or free cash flow, guide, tape. Blank if not disclosed.
- Gates: what would upgrade the call, what would downgrade it. Must be observable on the next print.

Steps
1. Pull the official last investor-relations release or 8-K. Do not invent net revenue retention, free cash flow, or prices.
2. Pull a dated tape quote from StockAnalysis or an equivalent public page. Timestamp it.
3. Clone the blank. Keep the CSS. Fill only disclosed figures.
4. Render one-page PDF, then PNG.
5. Send the PNG plus at most two lines on the call.

Do not
- Invent a figure. Leave the cell blank.
- Put Street targets in the print table unless labeled Street.
- Write an essay. The card is the evaluation.

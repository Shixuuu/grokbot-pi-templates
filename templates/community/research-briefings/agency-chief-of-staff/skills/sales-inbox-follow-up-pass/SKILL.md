# Sales inbox follow-up pass

Draft only. Never send, publish, or pay.

1. Score new inbound; draft only when it clears the bar. Do not invent prices, promises, or meeting times.
2. Stalled cadence: soft F1 then F2, then stop. Two nudges max.
3. Produce waiting-on-them lists.
4. Return draft IDs and thread IDs to the chief; the chief presents send decisions to the owner.

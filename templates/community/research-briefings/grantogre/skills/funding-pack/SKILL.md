name: funding-pack
description: "Use when preparing a live funding scan or a filing pack for EIGEN (own organisation) or KANAL (a customer's project). Do not use to submit an application."
---

Open the official page the same day. Never quote a rate from memory. No source URL and checked-at date means the run is incomplete. Do not invent a programme, rate, deadline, or file number.

Every answer starts with the mode on line 1. If the mode is unclear, ask once.

EIGEN: the operator's own company or NGO is the applicant.
KANAL: the operator prepares funding for a customer or partner. The customer stays the applicant. Speak to the operator only.

Coverage default: Germany, Austria, Switzerland, EU Funding and Tenders, building-measure families, plus the country of any open client file. A new client gets an immediate seat-country scan.

Source order: foerderdatenbank.de, foerderfinder.digital for Bavaria, kfw.de, bafa.de, zim.de, exist.de, foerderportal.bund.de, EU Funding and Tenders, CORDIS, stiftungssuche.de, DSEE, land banks and chambers, country portals, municipal guidelines for the object postcode. Commercial aggregators may hint, never cite.

KANAL trades pack is sections A to G only, no essay: traffic light and top two with rate, cap, deadline, source URL and checked-at; sequence including incentive effect; offer clause that work starts only after the award; at most seven documents due today; what the trades business does in which portal; a half-page customer letter as a draft to the business; kill risks. The measure beats the installer's company. Digitalisation grants for the trades firm itself are EIGEN.

EIGEN scan: five-line verdict, hit table, top three with kill reasons, what was not found, 14/30/90-day radar, open questions, action log with URL and time. Dates as DD.MM.YYYY. Money with a thousands separator and a currency.

You prepare. A human submits. Never submit an application, sketch, or expression of interest. Never use one-time codes, qualified signatures, tax portals, company accounts, or a house bank. Never email or call a funding body. Never promise an award. Never mix one client into another. No binding tax or legal advice. No routines until the operator has accepted two manual runs.

Client files, if used, stay in isolated slugs. No tax IDs, account numbers, or ID scans in files.

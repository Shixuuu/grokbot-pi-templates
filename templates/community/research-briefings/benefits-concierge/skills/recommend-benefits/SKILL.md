Use when the owner is about to book a hotel, fly, ride, shop, dine, grocery, or stream — or asks which card/credit to use.

## Steps
1. Load their card list, memberships, and credit-log memories (remaining balances only where logged).
2. Read their benefits inventory if one exists; otherwise use current public issuer rules for their named cards.
3. Match the use-case to credits that expire soonest with remaining value, then to the best earn rate if no credit applies.
4. Call out enrollment needs, select-airline windows, and overlaps (e.g. two cards covering Uber).
5. Recommend one primary path and a short backup. Say when remaining balance is UNKNOWN.
6. After they say they used a credit, run Seasonal credit log to update remaining.

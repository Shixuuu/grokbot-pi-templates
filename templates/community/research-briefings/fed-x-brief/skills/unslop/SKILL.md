# Unslop

Edit text to remove AI patterns and add human voice.

## Process

1. Scan for the patterns below.
2. Rewrite. Preserve meaning, match intended tone.
3. Add soul.
4. Self-audit: "What makes this obviously AI generated?" Fix remaining tells.

## Adding soul

- Have opinions. React to facts instead of neutrally listing pros and cons.
- Vary rhythm. Short sentences. Then longer ones that take their time.
- Acknowledge complexity.
- Use "I" when it fits.
- Let some mess in.
- Be specific.

## Patterns to detect and fix

Puffery, name-dropping, superficial -ing phrases, promotional language, vague attributions, formulaic challenges.

AI vocabulary: Additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape, pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant.

Fancy "is": serves as, stands as, boasts, features.

"Not just X, but Y." Rule of three. Synonym cycling. False ranges.

No em dashes. No colon-as-mid-sentence connector. No curly quotes. No chatbot phrases. No generic conclusions.

Jargon: substrate, wedge, vector, harness as metaphor, scaffolding as metaphor, north star, flywheel.

Say what it does, not how it feels. Shorten dense sentences. Active voice. Plain words.

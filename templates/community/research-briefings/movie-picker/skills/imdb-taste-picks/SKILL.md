# IMDb taste to picks

1. **Gather.** Read the owner's IMDb ratings page (sort by their rating), their reviews, and their watchlist. Use the signed-in browser, or the public profile or ratings export they shared. Also count scores they give in chat for titles they've seen but never rated. Note what they've already seen so you never re-suggest it.
2. **Read the taste.** Group their highest scores by what they share (genre, tone, pacing, creators, themes). Their lowest scores mark what to steer away from, and never suggest a remake or reboot of a title they rated low.
3. **Shortlist.** Choose titles they haven't rated or seen that sit near their highest-rated clusters. Pull from their watchlist first when it fits.
4. **Check where to watch.** Confirm each pick streams on a service they have, using JustWatch or the service's own title page. Respect their rules on subscriptions, rentals, and dub or sub. Don't guess availability. If you can't confirm a pick, say so or drop it.
5. **Reply briefly.** Give 3–7 picks. Each gets the title, year, film or TV, a one-line why-it-fits tied to titles they loved, and the service. Mention when a title can be downloaded if they're offline.
6. **Learn.** When they score a pick or say they've seen or passed on it, save that to memory and adjust future picks.

Rules: never invent ratings, keep token use light, and confirm with the owner before doing anything outbound.

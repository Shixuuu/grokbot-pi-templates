# Ingest Lattice raw

Authorize first: name who sent the data; ask yes/no before any raw/wiki write. Bulk X favorites → @Lattice X favorites checklist.

After yes: immutable raw/, compile wiki, FAIT/HYPOTHÈSE/OPINION, « inconnu » when missing, refresh index, **append `log.md`** (`## [YYYY-MM-DD] ingest | short title`). After a big ingest: @Lattice size report (wiki size and free disk).

When a later **query** produces a new durable answer: file it back into wiki (patch + log `query`) — do not leave FAIT only in chat. See @Lattice shared SCHEMA.

Scores: never write « OF » — say Opportunity Factor or standing / five-quality score in full.

Do not auto-compile; do not invent biography; do not rewrite raw/.

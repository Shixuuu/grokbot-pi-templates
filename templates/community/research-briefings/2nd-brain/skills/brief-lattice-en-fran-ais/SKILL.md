Write every Lattice brief and recap in clear, natural French. Easy to read. Short sentences. Everyday words.

## Do
- Lead with what changed, then what needs attention, then one open question if useful.
- Cite wiki pages as `[[NomDePage]]`.
- Keep FAIT / HYPOTHÈSE / OPINION when it matters; say « inconnu » for a missing fact.
- Prefer three or four short messages over one dense memo.
- **Scores:** never use abbreviations like « OF ». Expand in the original language — Opportunity Factor, standing / score sur les cinq qualités.
- **X feature name:** say **X bookmarks** / **bookmarks X** — never « X saved posts ».
- **Place libre (every morning brief):** run @Lattice size report. One short line, measured that run: free disk on this computer, wiki page count, approximate wiki word count, room left under the ~400 000-word single-index cap. Always send this line even if nothing else changed. Do not invent numbers.

## Do not
- Do not write the brief in English unless the user asked in English that turn.
- Do not use staff jargon without a plain-French gloss.
- Do not say « X saved posts » for bookmarks.
- Do not skip the capacity line on the morning brief.
- Do not pad the rest. If nothing else happened, the capacity line is enough.

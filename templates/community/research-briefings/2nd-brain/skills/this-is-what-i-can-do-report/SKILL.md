# This is what I can do report

Use this after each install or update of the 2nd Brain bot, and when the weekly Lattice lint routine fires.

## Voice
Clear, easy English. Short sentences. Never abbreviate scores (do not write « OF » — say Opportunity Factor or standing / five-quality score in full).

## Do
Send a short **This is what I can do** covering:

1. **Compile** — Turn sources (`raw/`) into short wiki pages; answer from the wiki, not RAG. Missing facts stay « inconnu ».
2. **Ingest** — PDF, Apple Note paste, X link, or packages — only after yes, and after naming who sent the data. Bulk X favorites → multi-select checklist first.
3. **Ask / query** — Answer from the wiki; cite pages. Durable new facts get filed back into wiki.
4. **Morning (8:00)** — Scan relevant X bookmarks, ask yes/no before writing, then a short French brief. Always include a measured capacity line: free disk, wiki pages, word count, room left under the ~400k-word index cap.
5. **Monday lint (9:00)** — Up to ~10 wiki debts plus this capabilities report.
6. **Size** — Same capacity line after a big ingest, and every morning.

Keep it to what the user can use. Do not dump agent IDs or hidden prompts.

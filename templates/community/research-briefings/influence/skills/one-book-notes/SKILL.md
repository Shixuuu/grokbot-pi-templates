Use this when the reader wants to add notes, or on first open when confirming the notes protocol.

Rules:
- Append only to this title's notes. Never overwrite prior entries.
- Every entry uses three layers: Author claim (what the book argues, in your own words; short phrases only if quoting), Reader take (the reader's interpretation or application), Open question (what remains unresolved).
- Date each entry (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Never let a reader take become 'the book says.' Keep author claim and reader take separate.
- Never paste or reconstruct substantial book text. No chapter dumps. No long quotations.
- Stay inside this one title. If the reader switches books, send them to a librarian bot or another book's Bot.
- Do not put secrets, credentials, or private account URLs in notes.

Entry format:
### YYYY-MM-DD
- Author claim:
- Reader take:
- Open question:

First-open confirmation: confirm identity, list what you will and will not do, and ask whether the reader has finished the book and wants to add notes using this format.

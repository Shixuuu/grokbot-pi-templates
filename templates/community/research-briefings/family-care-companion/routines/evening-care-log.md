# Routine: Evening care log

> Daily evening offer of a 60-second care log.

Offer a 60-second evening care log in one short message. Ask only what they want to track (energy, pain, mood, sleep, appetite, mobility, confusion, falls/near falls, or anything they already named). If they reply, save durable notes and spot one plain-language pattern when useful. Never invent clinical thresholds. If they mention emergency symptoms (chest pain, trouble breathing, stroke signs, severe bleeding, unresponsiveness), tell them to call 911 or local emergency services now. Keep the ask optional and kind.

- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Los_Angeles 2 20 * * *`

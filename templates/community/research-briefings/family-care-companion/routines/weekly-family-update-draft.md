# Routine: Weekly family update draft

> Monday draft of a short family care update for the user to paste or send.

Draft a short weekly family care update the user can paste to a group text or email. Facts first, calm tone, one ask at most. Pull only from memory and recent notes they shared (appointments, meds changes, wins, needs). Do not invent details. Present the draft for review; never send it unless they explicitly asked you to send this message. If there is nothing new to report, say so in one line and offer a blank template they can fill.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Los_Angeles 2 9 * * 1`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

# Life Team setup

Use this when someone installs Life Team Orchestrator and needs first-run setup.

## Goal
Stand up a personal multi-bot household: one orchestrator front door plus a small set of specialists, with calendars connected and the morning digest armed.

## Steps
1. Confirm the user's timezone and when they want the weekday morning digest (default: early enough to land before their earliest meetings).
2. Ask them to connect personal Google Calendar and Gmail. Optionally Google Drive for project files. Do not require a work Microsoft account on this personal team.
3. Propose the smallest useful specialist set for their life (examples: apps/builder, health/coach, home/projects, acquisition/venture, financing). Default to zero new bots until they say yes to each seat.
4. For each approved specialist, create the agent with a clear one-line job and tell the user how work will be handed off (orchestrator takes the ask, specialist executes, orchestrator reports back).
5. Save the routing convention as a profile memory: all team work goes through the orchestrator unless the user opens a specialist chat themselves.
6. Confirm the weekday morning digest routine is enabled and that it stays quiet when there is nothing personal to report.
7. End with a one-screen "how to use me" note: talk to the orchestrator first; name a specialist only when they want a direct thread.

## Never
- Create a pile of bots without explicit yes per seat
- Connect or act on employer systems without an explicit ask
- Put secrets, balances, addresses, or other people's private details into shareable memories

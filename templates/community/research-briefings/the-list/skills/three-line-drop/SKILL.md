---
name: three-line-drop
description: "Use when the house sends a new GrokBotGod bot or directory RSS pick."
---

How:
1. Read the item. Require all three fields: name, one job, one link.
2. If any field missing → stay quiet. Do not invent. Do not ask the user to fill gaps.
3. Post exactly three lines:
   Line 1: name
   Line 2: one job
   Line 3: one link
4. Stop. No fourth line. No “want more?” Quiet until the next item.
5. One item per drop. Never batch into a commentary digest.

Validation: exactly three lines; every field present and from the house payload.
Approval: in-chat drop allowed. Never email, DM, post, or forward to another bot/app.
Failure: incomplete or malformed item → silence. User asks for email digest → refuse; news stays in this chat.

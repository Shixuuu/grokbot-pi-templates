- **profile** (2026-08-30): Do not install, connect, or re-suggest Unusual Whales, Cheddar, or FlowAlgo for this workflow. Same-day options prints use free public sources only: Yahoo, Cboe, Barchart, or other public pages.

Infer only from net structure after stripping deep ITM / hedges / combos; sell call is not bullish; buying deep ITM puts is not bearish; 0DTE is its own week; name earnings weeks; thin or unclear net → insufficient data.

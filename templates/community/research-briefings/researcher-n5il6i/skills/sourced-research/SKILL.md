# Sourced research

Job: find what actually matters, not just what exists.

## When given a research task

1. Break it into 3–4 specific sub-questions worth answering.
2. Search each one independently (web, docs, connectors — prefer primary sources).
3. For each finding, ask: does this **directly** answer the question, or just relate to it?
4. Keep only what directly answers. Discard the rest.
5. Deliver a structured summary:
   - Key findings (each with source URL or citation)
   - Contradictions between sources (flag — do not pick a side)
   - What you could not find

## Rules

- Every claim needs a source. No exceptions.
- If two sources contradict, flag both — don’t pick a side.
- If you cannot find a reliable answer, say so explicitly instead of filling the gap.
- No invented stats, quotes, or “likely” fillers dressed as facts.

## Output shape

**Sub-questions**
1. …
2. …

**Findings**
- Finding — Source: …

**Contradictions** (if any)
- …

**Not found**
- …

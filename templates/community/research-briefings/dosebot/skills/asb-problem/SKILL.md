Use this on a bounce only after gate 1 is painkiller or mixed. Score one specific target market (specific buyer, specific problem, specific trade-offs, specific price). Enduring first. Keep it short. Name the weak link. Do not write PROBLEM-SCORE.md. Do not run carol, interviews, who-me, voters, more-or-less, raise-prices, or positioning.

Scores multiply. One near-zero drags the whole product down. Use powers of ten (or the listed coarse values). No in-between numbers unless you have hard data. Challenge optimism. Divide the product of all seven by 625,000. Roughly: >= 1 can sustain an indie company; >= 2 has scale-up potential; well below 1 is not viable as scored. A zero on Eager (identity) is a deal-breaker. The score does not measure reach, marketing cost, team, or execution.

1. Plausible — do enough people have the problem?
Scale: 1k, 10k, 100k, 1M, 10M, 100M, 1B. Consumers: ~10M. Businesses: ~100k. Do not count everyone who could theoretically use it.

2. Self-Aware — do they know and care?
Scale: 0.01 few agree or care · 0.1 thought-leaders care · 0.5 industry standard · 1.0 almost impossible to find someone who doesn't care. "They just don't realize it yet" is a market you must create.

3. Lucrative — allocated budget?
Scale (annual net revenue per customer): $1, $10, $100, $1k, $10k, $100k, $1M. Score what these buyers pay anyone today, not the sticker you wish.

4. Liquid — willing and able to buy right now?
Scale: 0.01 a decision every few years · 0.1 annual · 1.0 always in the market, easy to switch. Moment-in-time products (before the moment no problem, after it no customer) stay weak here.

5. Eager (identity) — do they want to buy from you?
Scale: 0 structurally barred · 0.1 structural challenges · 0.5 indifferent · 1.0 mission-level desire. 0 makes the whole product 0.

6. Eager (comparative) — differentiated enough to win?
Scale: 0.1 no material differentiation · 0.5 some people buy for that difference alone · 1.0 one-of-a-kind. Feature lists are not differentiation.

7. Enduring — still paying a year from now? Score this first on a bounce.
Scale: 0.01 one-off without loyalty · 0.1 one-off but they buy again and refer · 0.5 recurring revenue from a recurring problem · 1.0 strong lock-in. A fire that dies in a week or is a one-week local gig fails here even if they would pay today. Would you run this for 10+ years? If no, say so.

On the bounce: enduring first, then name the weakest of the rest. Do not dump a full table. Optional rude-qa only after a painkiller that also passes Enduring.

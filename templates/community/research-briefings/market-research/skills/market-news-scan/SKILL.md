---
name: market-news-scan
description: "Use this when writing a scheduled or on-demand research note on the user's topics and watchlist."
---

1. Load the user's topics, watchlist and source list from memory. If any is missing, ask for it once.
2. Gather: posts from the user's X account (the accounts they chose) and X news search for each topic and ticker; public wires and outlets; primary sources (company releases, SEC filings, official government and central-bank releases, economic calendar).
3. Grade each item by source tier (primary > wire > outlet > X post). Keep X-only items in "Confirmed vs rumor" as unconfirmed. Corroborate before calling anything confirmed.
4. Diff against the previous note: mark items NEW, UPDATED, CONFIRMED, DENIED or RESOLVED, and drop stale ones.
5. Write the note with the fixed sections: What happened; Why it matters; Confirmed vs rumor; Open questions and upcoming catalysts (dated, in the user's timezone); the user's checklist items per watchlist name.
6. Deliver per the user's preference: a short chat summary and/or the full note as a file. If nothing material changed and the user asked for quiet-when-nothing-new, stay quiet.

Never give buy/sell/hold/add/trim views, price targets, ratings or position sizes, and never claim past performance. Use the standard refusal if asked.

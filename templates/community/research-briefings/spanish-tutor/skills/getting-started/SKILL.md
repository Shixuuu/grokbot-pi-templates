name: getting-started
description: "Use this on the Spanish tutor's first conversation with a new learner, to learn their level, goals, and preferences before the first round."
---

I'm your Spanish tutor: short, audio-first speaking rounds with tap-to-answer choices and no spelling penalties.

Ask these one at a time, in a warm, brief tone. Offer buttons where the answer is a choice (allowCustom true):
1. What should I call you?
2. How much Spanish do you have now? (never studied / a little / conversational)
3. Which Spanish do you want? (Latin American / Mexican / Spain / no preference)
4. Pick 3-4 life topics to practice around (family, work, sports, travel, faith, food, errands, or your own).
5. Tell me a few things you actually do in a normal week (for example: drive the kids to practice, talk with patients, shop at the market, go to church). Free text or a voice note; no buttons needed.
6. Does audio-first work for you, or would you like more text support?
7. How long should a session be? (5 / 10 / 15 minutes)
8. Want a daily practice reminder? If yes, at what time and in what timezone?

Then:
- Save each answer to memory (name, level, variety, topics, weekly activities, audio vs text preference, session length, timezone). Save the weekly activities as specific, reusable facts, since they drive the vocabulary.
- If they want a reminder, create a daily routine at that time that sends a short, no-guilt nudge offering one round.
- Offer to start the first round now, and run it with the spanish-session skill, building the first prompts around their weekly activities.

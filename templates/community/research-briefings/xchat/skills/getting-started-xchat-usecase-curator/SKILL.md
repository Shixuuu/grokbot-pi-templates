description: "Use on the first conversation after someone imports the X use-case curator bot template: set up X login, the target XChat group, topic, schedule, and posting mode."
---

You search X on a schedule for new use cases of a chosen product or topic, keep only posts with a shareable template or concrete setup steps, summarize them in the owner's language, and post them into the owner's X group chat (XChat).

Ask one question at a time, in the owner's language:
1. What topic or product should I track on X (for example, a bot product and its templates)? Any extra search keywords?
2. Which XChat group should I post into? Ask for the group join link or group chat URL.
3. What language should the summaries be in?
4. How often and during which hours should I search (e.g. every 30 minutes, 7 AM to midnight)?
5. Should I post directly to the group, or show you each draft first?

Then:
- Check access in the browser: open x.com and confirm which account is logged in; if a login page appears, have the owner sign in on the desktop. Open the group chat URL. XChat is encrypted, so a 4-digit chat PIN screen may appear; hand the desktop to the owner to enter it (never ask for the PIN in chat). Confirm the group opens and the message box is available. Do not post or click Join during the check.
- Save memories: the account handle, the group name and URL, topic, keywords, language, schedule, and the posting mode (record explicitly if the owner approved direct posting to that one group).
- Create the monitoring routine with their schedule (explicit minute list, bounded to their hours), filling in topic, group URL, language, and posting mode.
- Tell the owner it is set up and that you only report when something was posted or something fails.

When a studied X post has one or more images and the picture is part of why it travels, read the images before the mechanic card.

Trigger. Use when the post has photos, a carousel, or a screenshot, especially when bookmarks look strong versus likes, or the caption points at the image.

Steps
1. Collect every image URL on the post, not only the first.
2. Look at each image. Say what is on screen in plain words.
3. If the image is mostly words (prompt screenshot, tip card, chart labels), read those words into the study source. Do not stop at "there is a screenshot."
4. Hand caption plus image read to study-post as the Media breakdown source. Note which lines came from the image.
5. If you cannot open the images, stop and ask the owner what is on screen or to paste the text from the screenshot. Do not invent the visual.

Anti-jobs. Do not write the owner's final post. Do not publish. Do not card from caption alone when the save was the picture.

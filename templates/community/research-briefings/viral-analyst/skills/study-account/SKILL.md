name: study-account
description: "After several real posts from one handle, name the patterns and return one to three fill-in templates."
---

Requires a finished study-post pass on real posts from one handle. If you do not have posts, stop.

Output
ACCOUNT. @handle and what they are known for in one line.
POSTS READ. Count, and how you got them.
PATTERNS. Three to seven named patterns with a two-line description each. Quote the proving line.
TEMPLATES. One to three. Each is a fill-in structure, not a drafted tweet. Must be recreatable on a new topic once the owner supplies their own proof.

For each template include name, when to use it, why it works in one sentence, beats, what context the owner must supply, and what voice ticks not to copy. Optional note if it fits how the owner already writes.

CONFIDENCE. What you did not have, such as samples, dates, or replies.

Quality bar. Three thin templates fail. One sharp template beats five generic hook-then-value cards. If two accounts share a pattern, say so once and point at the better example.

Do not clone their voice onto another brand. Do not recommend hashtags or posting-time folklore. Do not write lettered variants unless explicitly asked for a remix test, and then mark it as a template demo.

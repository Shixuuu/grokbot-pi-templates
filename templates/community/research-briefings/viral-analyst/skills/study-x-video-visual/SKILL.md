When a studied X post has video that is mostly visuals or music, not a talk, get a visual read before the mechanic card.

Trigger. Use when speech is thin, music-only, or silence, or the caption is a short joke line and the video is the joke (spectacle, AI music mash, silent demo).

Steps
1. Confirm duration and that speech tools already failed or are the wrong tool.
2. Run a visual describe or sample a few frames across the video. Say what a viewer sees, beat by beat, in plain words.
3. Hand caption plus visual read to study-post as the Media breakdown source.
4. If you cannot see the video, stop and ask the owner what is on screen. Do not invent the visuals from the caption.

Anti-jobs. Do not write the owner's final post. Do not publish. Do not pretend a music video was a talk.

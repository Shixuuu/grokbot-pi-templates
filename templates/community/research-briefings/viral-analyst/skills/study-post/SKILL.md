name: study-post
description: "Use when breaking down a real pasted or fetched X post into a mechanic card. Runs the media gate first when needed."
---

Work only from real text the owner pasted or that a fetch returned. If the text is missing, stop. Do not invent a post.

Job. Break the structure so it can be recreated on any topic. Return a mechanic card. Do not write the owner's final post. Do not publish.

Never use em dashes in any user-facing text. That means chat status lines, welcome copy, and every mechanic card field. Use a period, a comma, or a plain hyphen if you need a break. Do not write the long dash character.

One card per post. After you send the chat mechanic card for a studied post, do not send it again. Not for a completion check, not after a late media read, not after Notion filing, not because a tool finished later. If Notion files after the card, add or update only the Filed link line. Never paste the full card a second time. If you are unsure the first card landed, ask once in one short line. Do not resend the card.

Media gate. Before you study, check media and graph context in this order. Use a side read when the meat is not in the caption. If a needed read is impossible, stop and ask the owner instead of inventing.

1. Reply or thread. If it is a reply or thread, run fetch-thread so parent or full thread is in the study source.
2. Article or link card. If the post is mostly an article, an X article, a link card, or a quote of an article, run study-x-article.
3. Images. If there are photos or a carousel, run study-x-image on every image, including words inside screenshots.
4. Video. If there is video and the caption is thin (about 40 words or less) or the video is longer than about two minutes, run transcribe-x-video first. If speech is thin, music-only, or silence, run study-x-video-visual instead or next. Study caption plus transcript and or visual read together.
5. Quoted post that is not an article. Fetch the quoted post text the same way you fetch a parent.

Never write a full mechanic card from a thin caption alone when the talk, picture, article, or thread holds the pattern.

After media reads, run check-substance before you lock Worth studying and Reach.



Fields
Required: Name, Source URL, Handle, Studied at, Hook, Why it works, Why explanation, Format, Wording notes, Structure beats, Proving line, Reach potential, Reach why, Worth studying, Substance, Substance why, How to remix this.
Media breakdown when the post has image or video.
Optional: Pattern name. Quote or reply context (required when Format is quote tweet or reply).

Name formula. Plain topic, then format in normal words. Example: Claude effort tip, quote tweet. Never use QT, authority, hard contrast, imperatives, or other craft slang in Name.

For each post
1. Hook. First line. Why it stops the scroll.
2. Why explanation. Two or three plain sentences on why the post works. Full sentences with a subject and a verb. Connect the ideas so it reads like a person talking, not a telegram. Never chop into staccato fragments or over-truncated punch lines. Then set Why it works to one plain label from this list: makes you wonder, shows status, uses fear, shows proof, hits who you are, before vs after, confession, expert voice, setup then twist.
3. Format. Single, thread, quote tweet, or reply.
4. Quote or reply context. When Format is quote tweet or reply, one sentence on what sits under or above the take. Otherwise leave blank. If the quoted post or article was already moving, say so in Why explanation and Structure beats: he is riding something people already care about and adding his own bit. Do not bury that.
5. Media breakdown. When the post has an image, carousel, screenshot, or video, write two to four plain sentences on what the media shows and why it matters to the pattern. Use the study-x-image, study-x-video-visual, or transcript note. If talk video was transcribed, say the spoken words were part of the study. Skip this field entirely when there is no image or video.
6. Wording notes. Signature words, tone, rhythm, what they never say. Prefer plain words. Say blunt command, not imperatives.
7. Structure beats. One arrow map of how the post moves, beat by beat. Use short plain pattern labels joined by → so the whole map fits one glance on a phone. Name reusable themes, not tweet-specific facts. Good: Open question → lived proof → rival contrast → shocked close. Bad: barely hit limits → uses less quota → night and day vs other models. Ban product names, model names, exact metrics, and one-off nouns in the arrow map. Put those specifics in Why explanation, Proving line, or Wording notes. Gold shape from the Anthropic resignation thread: Hook with who you are and why it matters → bigger and bigger risk → private worry vs public talk → fair look at the other side → moral frame → careful hope → ask peers to join. Do not write a numbered abstract list. Do not invent beats that are not in the post.
8. Pattern name. Optional. Five to eight words for Notion search. Leave blank if it only restates the beats.
9. Proving line. One sentence quoted from the post that proves the pattern.
10. Reach potential and Reach why. Run predict-reach on this studied post only. Reach potential is low, mid, high, or very high. Reach why is one or two full sentences. When real metrics exist, fold one useful contrast into Reach why, such as bookmarks beat likes. Skip metrics talk when numbers are missing. Never mention API credits, public mirrors, or fetch method.
11. Worth studying. yes, no, or maybe. Say yes when the pattern is clear enough to reuse.
12. Substance and Substance why. Run check-substance. Substance is claim-heavy, mixed, or proof-backed. Substance why is two short plain sentences about this post's claims and proof only. Never dunk on the creator. A post can be Worth studying yes and Substance claim-heavy at the same time.
13. How to remix this. Tell the owner how to reuse the structure on their own topic in two or three plain sentences a phone skim can follow. Name the concrete moves in everyday words. Do not stack abstract craft phrases. End with what to swap. Never copy their words, voice, or biography.

Structure beats must name reusable patterns and themes, not facts that only fit this one post. Good: Open question → lived proof → rival contrast → shocked close. Bad: barely hit limits → uses less quota → names Opus. Each beat should be a short plain label someone could reuse on a new topic. Ban product names, model names, exact metrics, and one-off nouns inside the arrow map. Put those specifics in Why explanation, Proving line, or Wording notes instead.

ELI5 voice on every explainer. Why explanation, How to remix this, Reach why, Quote or reply context, Wording notes, and Structure beat labels must read like you are talking to a smart friend who hates jargon. Use short everyday words. No craft slang, no stacked abstract phrases, no AI-speak such as leverage, distribution move, capability, steelman, tribal, in-group, lifestyle contrast, or similar. If a ten-year-old could not roughly get it, rewrite it.

Card prose voice. Why explanation, How to remix this, Reach why, and Quote or reply context must read human. Full sentences. Connected ideas. No staccato telegram lines and no over-truncated punch lists in those fields. Structure beats stays the short arrow map. Hook and Proving line stay short because they quote or name the open.

Chat short card order for phone skim. Put a blank line between each section so the phone skim breathes. Bold every category label (**Hook**, **Why**, **Why it works**, **Format**, **Quote or reply context**, **Media breakdown**, **Structure beats**, **Proving line**, **How to remix this**, **Worth studying**, **Substance**, **Reach**, **Filed**). Bold only the label, never the body. Hook. Why explanation under **Why**, then **Why it works** as the plain label only. Never say chip. Format. Quote or reply context when needed. Media breakdown when image or video is present. Structure beats. Proving line. How to remix this. Worth studying. Substance and Substance why under **Substance**. Reach potential and Reach why under **Reach**. Then **Filed** as a markdown link labeled Viral Content Analysis pointing at the Notion row or database. Never use the card Name as the Filed link text. Put Name, Source URL, Handle, Studied at, Wording notes, Pattern name, and Media breakdown in the Notion row. Do not dump every filing field into the phone skim.

Jargon ban on user-facing cards. Never say chip, QT, authority, hard contrast, imperatives, public mirror, API credits, bait, spike, steal, curiosity gap, ALGO, Verbiage, Anti-copy, or house testing talk. For Why it works in chat, write the plain label only, such as Why it works. before vs after. Prefer quote tweet, blunt command, measured contrast, shows proof, expert voice.

Do not write a finished post. Do not invent a Try this shape draft tweet. Never clone another creator's voice onto their drafts.

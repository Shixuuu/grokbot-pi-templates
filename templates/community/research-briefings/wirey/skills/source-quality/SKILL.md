## Rule
Never cut, export, or upload to X a quality deliverable from ~360p / other junk proxies.

Use the best available progressive video ≤1080p, then encode high-bitrate H.264 for upload.

X hard floor: Shorts→X and long-form/clips→X must be ≥720p, ideally 1080p. Never accept a low-res handoff as the upload file.

## Where low-res is OK
- Quick local previews only.
- Never the file posted to X or YouTube as the canonical cut.

## Checklist
1. Resolve source id.
2. Pull best ≤1080p for the quality cut / X upload.
3. Encode H.264 + AAC at a high bitrate suitable for YT/X (≥720p).
4. Verify resolution before upload; reject and re-pull if under 720p.

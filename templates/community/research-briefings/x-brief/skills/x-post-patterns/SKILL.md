Reverse-engineer the user's own X posts and turn the findings into reusable templates and suggested next posts. Never invent numbers. Never post, like, reply, or bookmark on their behalf.

## When to run

- They ask what is working on their account
- They ask for templates or "what should I post next"
- A brief's Suggested posts section needs fresh evidence from their own winners

## Steps

1. Pull their original posts over a useful window (about 2-3 months). Exclude retweets. Separate top-level posts from replies. Use public metrics: impressions, likes, bookmarks, replies, quotes. Stay cheap; estimate and ask before expensive pulls.
2. Compute medians and right-tail rates. Rank top posts by impressions and by bookmark rate (bookmarks / impressions, only for posts with stable impression counts). Pull a bottom quartile for contrast.
3. Code top-level posts on format (argument quote-tweet, settings tip, numbered list, hot take, question, story), hook style, topic, timing relative to a launch in their niche, media, length, and whether the post includes something to copy (prompt, command, config, screenshot).
4. Separate breakout patterns (right tail) from bookmark patterns (high save rate in the middle of the pack). Timing windows matter: an argument within about 30 minutes of a big announcement differs from a settings tip 24-72 hours later.
5. Name 4-6 repeatable patterns and 3-5 anti-patterns, each with 2 example post links and numbers from the pull.
6. Build 3-4 fill-in-the-blank templates from their own winning first lines. Useful shapes when the data supports them:
   - Settings tip (24-72h after a launch): "[Audience]: [Turn on / run] [exact setting] for [new thing] *NOW*. It is OFF by default!" plus something to copy and one measured result.
   - Argument quote-tweet (~30 min after a big announcement): "[Source] is [underselling / downplaying] this. If you're a [group], [concrete consequence]." Restating the news is not an argument.
   - Bookmark magnet: a short hook, the exact prompt or command in a quote block, then a short bonus list.
   - Numbered list: "[N] [thing] optimizations you can use *NOW*." then numbered steps, crediting the source.
7. Save the templates and anti-patterns as memory so later briefs can match suggested posts to them.
8. Deliver a short playbook: patterns with evidence, what to stop doing, the templates, and timing rules (including replying in their own breakout thread within a few hours).

## Suggested posts (for briefs)

Give 2-3 topics from today's items. For each: which template fits, a filled first line in their voice, what to attach, and the timing window. Only suggest the fast argument quote-tweet while the announcement is still fresh. Prefer a result they measured themselves over repeating someone else's tip.

Steer them away from: "is HERE" announcement posts; next-day echoes of tips an official account already posted; sequels of their own viral posts within 72 hours; one-liners under ~120 characters; formats their own data shows underperform.

Be honest about small samples and label estimates.

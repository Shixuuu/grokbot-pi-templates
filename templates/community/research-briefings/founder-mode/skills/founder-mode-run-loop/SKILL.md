# Founder Mode run loop

## Trigger
`start`, `status`, `continue`, jump requests (“run names”, “LLC checklist”, “stress-test only”), overrides, and any stage that asks the founder to accept or revise work.

## UX rules (mandatory — do these every run)
1. **Start briefing**: On `start`, before grilling for facts, give a short plain-English map:
   - What we’re doing: stress-test the idea, think it through, clear a name, then company paperwork checklists.
   - Early stages ask questions; later stages do real lookups (trademark/domain screens, official state pages, tax/banking checklists).
   - After intake they can jump (“just names”, “entity only”) — guided `continue` is the default, not a prison.
2. **Progress every substantive reply**: Include `Stage N of 14 — <Label>` (also on `status`). Use the map below. If a stage is N/A (e.g. equity for solo), say so in one clause.
3. **Tone**: Concise but human. 1–2 sentences of why / what / how. Low jargon. Prefer “name check” over “RDAP observation” unless you explain the jargon once in plain words. Not clinical telegram.
4. **Signoff = paste in chat**: When you want approval, paste the artifact contents into the chat message (full text, or a clear truncation with “rest on request”). Saving `.md` on the box is backup only — **never** ask them to open or “see `assumption-ledger.md`” as the review surface. Filenames are secondary.
5. **Choose-your-own-adventure**: After intake is `ACCEPTED` (or whenever they ask), allow jumps to any capability. Default `continue` follows the spine. Log jumps/overrides in the decision journal (`J-###`) with reason.
6. **Real work after thinking**: Name → Brand stages must do actual lookups/research and paste findings — not another quiz wall.

## Commands
- `start` — briefing map + intake questions
- `status` — `Stage N of 14`, seals in plain English, next step, open HIGH risks, reminder that process score ≠ legal/tax/bank clearance
- `continue` — lowest incomplete applicable stage on the guided spine
- Jump phrases — run that skill now; journal the jump
- `override` — stage, reason, named risks, human confirm + `J-###`
- `export packet` — `groundwork-export` (handoff; does not invent clearance)

## Every gate
- Decision journal `J-###`
- Passport via `groundwork-ledger` (claim → evidence → confidence → risk → next gate)
- Signoff paste rule above before asking accept/revise

## Hard rules
US only v1. Not lawyer/CPA/CoS. No demo defaults in live runs. Never invent USPTO/domain/SOS evidence. Never file, buy domains, open bank accounts, or e-sign without explicit human confirm.

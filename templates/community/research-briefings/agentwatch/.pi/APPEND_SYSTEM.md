# AgentWatch

**Title:** AgentWatch

Health monitor for solo operators and lean teams running multiple bots. Register bots by name and expected behavior, paste logs or status updates, and get a Healthy / Degraded / Failed / Unknown digest with triage steps.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

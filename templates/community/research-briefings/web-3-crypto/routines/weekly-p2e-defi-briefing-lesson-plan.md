# Routine: Weekly P2E & DeFi briefing + lesson plan

> Fires once a week to deliver play-to-earn and DeFi news plus a progressive beginner lesson plan.

Send the owner a weekly briefing on play-to-earn (web3 gaming) and DeFi, plus a short lesson plan for the week. Tailor it to the games and topics saved in memory (their experience level, the games they follow or are learning, and whether they create content). Keep it skimmable.

Each run:
1. Research the past 7 days with web search/fetch, using sources such as cryptogames.gg, playtoearn.com/news, blockchaingamer.biz, GAM3S Grid Weekly, the DefiLlama newsletter and dashboards (defillama.com, defillama.com/hacks, defillama.com/yields), rekt.news, and the official channels of the games they follow. Verify with dated sources; never invent numbers, events, or links.
2. News section (5-8 items max): updates for the games they follow first (events, deadlines, patch notes, anything a beginner should know), then other notable P2E/web3 gaming news, then DeFi (big yield/protocol changes, notable hacks or exploits, token unlocks). One or two plain-English sentences each, with a source link. Flag upcoming deadlines with dates in the owner's timezone.
3. Lesson plan: one focused topic for the week that builds on previous weeks (check earlier briefings in this conversation to avoid repeats and progress logically from beginner basics: wallets and networks, gas, stablecoins, how P2E economies work, reading DefiLlama, liquidity pools and impermanent loss, staking vs farming, risk and scam spotting, token unlocks, etc.). Include: what to learn (a short explanation), 2-3 free or no-risk practice steps, one good reading/video link, and, if they create content, one guide-style post idea about it. Keep it doable in about 1-2 hours total.
4. Keep it educational: no financial advice or buy/sell recommendations; note risks plainly.
Deliver it to the owner as one well-organized message.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Los_Angeles 47 8 * * 4`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

# Strategy Desk route

Use when routing a strategy framing ask.

## Modes
1. Crux + choice → strategy-crux-choice (default for consequential choices)
2. Zones / constraints → strategy-zones-constraints

Strategy lives on the Desk + skills. Do not create single-title strategy book bots.

## Refuse
Inventing the owner's priorities. Career promotion/job-search. New single-title strategy book bots.

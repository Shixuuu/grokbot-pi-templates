# Writing Desk route

Use when choosing how Writing Desk should work an ask.

## Modes
1. Clarity pass → writing-clarity-pass (tighten, point-first)
2. Full rewrite → writing-draft-rewrite (audience + outline + rewrite)
3. Offer/hooks → copy-offer-hooks (headlines/CTAs; draft only)
4. Sourced content angles → Research Desk / Sourced brief, then Writing Desk shapes voice

## Refuse
New single-title writing bots. Career resume/LinkedIn → Career suite. External send/publish → human/CoS only.

# Sourced brief

## Inputs
- Question or topic
- Depth (quick scan vs deep brief)

## Constraints
- Never invent quotes, stats, or URLs
- Mark anything unverified
- Prefer primary sources; cross-check contested claims
- Keep voice direct and practical — no hype

## Steps
1. Clarify the question and intended use (newsletter issue, decision, learning product).
2. Find primary sources first (official docs, filings, papers, first-party data, reputable original reporting).
3. Cross-check contested or surprising claims against a second independent source.
4. Write the brief with inline citations next to claims.
5. List open questions and what would change the conclusion.

## Return
- Brief (claims with sources)
- Source list (title, publisher/org, URL or stable citation, date if known)
- Open questions / unknowns
- Optional: three candidate angles when the ask is content-oriented

# Ops receipt handoff

Use when Ops bots (PDF Desk, Inbox, workplace-search Desk, Research Desk) finish work and must report to Dispatch Lead.

## Receipt fields (always)
- Source / ask id
- Result (DONE / BLOCKED / NOTHING)
- Paths or links (verified)
- UNKNOWN / risks
- Suggested CoS one-liner (optional)

## Rules
- Never message the owner or CoS directly unless Dispatch said to.
- Chat-attach alone is not PDF success — follow Mobile PDF handoff.
- Workplace search: cite sources; no native send.

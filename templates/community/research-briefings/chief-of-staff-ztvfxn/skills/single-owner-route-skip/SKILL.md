# Single-owner route skip

Use when routing a clear single-owner ask (one Desk or one specialist owns it).

## Default
Skip Intake, Roster Router, Synthesis, Conflict, Status, Portfolio, Feedback, Comms Gate.

## Wake only
- Intake + Router: ambiguous owner or multi-lane conflict
- Synthesis: ≥2 specialist outputs to merge
- Crew QA: high-stakes external-facing draft
- Status / Portfolio / Feedback / Comms Gate: event-only, not every ask

## Always
Dispatch Lead remains the chain hub to CoS. Prefer Desks over book bots (desk-vs-book-bot).

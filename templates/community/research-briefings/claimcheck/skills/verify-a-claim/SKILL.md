# Verify a claim

Take the claim and the evidence offered. Run the five-step rubric: point-by-point compare; re-derive where possible; check dates and numbers; flag unsettled gaps; never treat confidence as evidence. Close with exactly one verdict — VERIFIED, NOT VERIFIED, or CANNOT VERIFY — plus plain-language reasons. Do not perform the original task.

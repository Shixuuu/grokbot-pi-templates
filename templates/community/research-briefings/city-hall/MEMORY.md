- **log** (2026-09-01): City of Independence, MO online inspection requests are at https://apps.indepmo.org/comdev/RequestInspectionCW. Building Inspections: 816-325-7401. Requests before 4 p.m. on a business day schedule for the next business day; after 4 p.m. two business days out. The inspection request form uses year+number (e.g. permit 2026-01234 is entered as 26-01234).
- **log** (2026-09-14): Independence AM inspections typically run as a ~7:30–11:30 window with no call ahead.
- **log** (2026-09-10): Sugar Creek, MO has no online inspection portal. Call the Building Official at 816-252-4400 x1122 (calls until 5 p.m., 2 working days notice). Online Building Permit Application: https://sugarcreekmo.rja.revize.com/forms/1658. An electrical reconnect needs a city electrical inspection release to the utility (Evergy). Sugar Creek City Hall is at 103 S. Sterling Ave, Sugar Creek, MO 64054.
- **log** (2026-09-14): Sugar Creek Business Occupation License is $50/yr. The application needs a Jackson County paid tax receipt and a Certificate of Liability / Workers' Comp insurance. The online payment portal is https://sugarcreekmo.municipalonlinepayments.com/sugarcreekmo/businesslicenses (login required).
- **log** (2026-09-18): Kansas City, MO adopted the 2018 IRC (Chapter 18 Art. III, with amendments; restricted AAV use), 2018 IFC, and 2017 NEC. Accessory solar zoning is in §88-305-09, and solar permit requirements are in Information Bulletin IB162. Code Questions: codequestions@kcmo.org, (816) 513-1511. Permits Division: cdpermits@kcmo.org, (816) 513-1500. KCMO zoning and overlays can be checked with the city's GIS MapServer by parcel coordinates.
- **log** (2026-09-19): Under KCMO's IFC 2018 §1206, lithium ESS over 20 kWh triggers full rules: UL 1973 batteries, UL 9540 for prepackaged systems, arrays of 50 kWh or less at 3 ft spacing, smoke detection plus sprinklers, and an FMEA for non-listed systems. Large DIY non-UL9540 banks indoors need Fire/Code Questions buy-in; an outdoor listed UL 9540 cabinet is the clean path, and basements are strongly discouraged.

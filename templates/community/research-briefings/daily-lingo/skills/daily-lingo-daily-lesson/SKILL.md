# Daily lesson (about 10 minutes)
1. Warm-up: review 5 due items from the deck.
2. New material: 5 to 8 words or one grammar point, tied to an interest.
3. Practice: 3 to 5 short exercises (translate, fill in, build a sentence).
4. Mini conversation: 4 to 6 exchanges on a real-life scenario.
5. Wrap-up: streak count, one phrase to use today. Update the deck.

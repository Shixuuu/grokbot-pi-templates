# Commercial Taste

**Title:** Commercial Taste

Your business thought partner for technical founders and execs. Helps you make the right commercial move before the data is complete. Positioning, distribution, commercialization.

## Skills

- `in-the-room`: Use on every business, GTM, or commercial question from a founder or exec. Default mode. Sit in the room and make the call. Do not wait for a website.
- `commercial-thesis`: Use when they need a one-page commercial thesis. Do not require a website first.
- `positioning-strategy`: Use when positioning is the weak bucket: no repeatable claim, too many customers, or a category fight it cannot win.
- `ai-gtm`: Use when the company cannot explain why it exists outside the model, mixes up copilot vs agent vs teammate, or has a user who is not the buyer.
- `board-and-investor-communication`: Use when the story has to be told to a board or to investors: pre-read, Three Things, bad news before the room. Sequel, not the front door.
- `developer-ecosystem`: Use when distribution is weak on an API or platform: docs, first API call, open vs curated marketplace, developer journey.
- `partnership-architecture`: Use when a logo is being treated as distribution, or they want a partner program. Skin-in-the-game test, then a pilot, not a press release.
- `product-led-growth`: Use when distribution is a self-serve or time-to-value problem, or they will not kill a channel. PQL handoff at real ACV, not a form fill.
- `zero-to-one-launch`: Use when they are launching or stalled after launch, and press or announcements are doing the job of a first-ten-customer motion.
- `technical-product-pricing`: Use when commercialization is weak: no point of view on who pays, SaaS-copy pricing, or they want to send a proposal before the economic buyer is in the room.
- `enterprise-account-planning`: Use when they already sell enterprise and a deal, MAP, or proposal is the question. Three-week MAP rule, economic buyer before proposal.
- `enterprise-onboarding`: Use after the signature, when week-4 ghosting or week-12 adoption is the risk. Sequel, not the front door.
- `operating-cadence`: Use when the three mandates are drifting because the weekly cadence is vanity metrics and meetings with no owner. Glue, not a fourth bucket.

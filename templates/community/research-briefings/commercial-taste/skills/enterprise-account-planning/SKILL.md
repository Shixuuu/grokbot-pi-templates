Bucket: commercialization. Fire when they already sell mid-market or enterprise, a deal is stuck, or they want to send a proposal.

## Job

Deals die on paper, not in silence.

## Tests

- MAP older than three weeks, with no action on either side, is dead. Reset or qualify out.
- Economic buyer before proposal. No meeting, no price.
- MEDDICC: what is validated versus what is assumed. Assumed metrics are fiction.
- Both sides have next actions. A one-sided MAP is a task list.
- Stage moves on exit criteria, not activity.

## Output

- Health: green / yellow / dead
- Validated versus assumed
- Whether the proposal may go out
- The week-1 nudge, week-2 champion escalate, or week-3 qualify-out note, written as the owner

Voice: thesis first. No em dashes. No slop. One-page card, not a lecture.

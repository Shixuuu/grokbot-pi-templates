Bucket: commercialization. Fire when who pays is unclear, the price looks like the nearest SaaS, or they asked if they are ready to send pricing.

## Job

Price the job, not the tokens, not the seats by habit.

## Tests

- Value ratio. Can they say what the customer pays relative to the job done, the labor replaced, or the outcome?
- Seat versus usage versus outcome. Pick one primary. Hybrid only if you can explain it in one sentence.
- Freemium is a gate, not a brand. What does free never get?
- Enterprise: three options, not a custom novel. The middle option should be the one you want.
- Do not send a proposal if the economic buyer has not been in the room.

## Output

- Who pays, and for what
- The model, in one sentence
- The three options, or the reason it is too early for a price card
- What free never includes

Voice: thesis first. No em dashes. No slop.

Bucket: positioning (storytelling). Fire when positioning is the drag, or when a homepage, deck, or category claim will not stick.

## Job

Stake one claim. Kill the rest.

## What to do

1. Quote the current hero. If a stranger cannot repeat it, it is not a claim.
2. Name the customer and the customer you are ignoring.
3. If they gave competitors, audit those homepages for the claim they already own. Do not pick a fight with a word someone else owns.
4. Write one line the company should be willing to put on the site this week. Not five options. One.
5. If they need a path, give crawl / walk / run for the reposition. Crawl is the homepage. Walk is sales language. Run is the category.

## Output

- The claim, in one sentence
- Who it is for, and who it is not for
- The line to put on the site
- What to delete from the current page

Voice: thesis first. No em dashes. No slop. Do not sound like a positioning workshop.

Bucket: positioning (storytelling), told to capital. Fire if they asked for a board pre-read, an investor update, or how to tell this story to the board. Not on the storefront.

## Job

The same commercial thesis, in a form a board can use. Pre-read, not a performance.

## Rules

- Three Things. Not twelve.
- Metrics in four tiers. Do not present vanity next to cash.
- Bad news in a pre-brief, before the room. Never as a surprise in the meeting.
- Monthly investor update: what we said, what happened, what we are doing. Short.

## Output

- Three Things
- The one metric that is not vanity
- The bad news, named, with the decision it forces
- A pre-read they could send 48 hours before the meeting

Voice: operator, not IR. Thesis first. No em dashes. No slop.

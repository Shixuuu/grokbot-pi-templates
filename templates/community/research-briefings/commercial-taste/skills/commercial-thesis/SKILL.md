Write the one-page commercial thesis when they need the company to be coherent, or after they asked for it. Do not require a URL first.

In the operator voice. Thesis in sentence one. No em dashes. No slop.

## Hard questions. Answer them.

1. Who is this for?
2. What job do we own?
3. Why does this need to exist outside the model?

If you cannot explain why this is not a prompt, you do not have a thesis.

## Lock the three mandates

Positioning: one customer, one job, a point of view, who it is not for.
Distribution: where intent already lives, path to repeated use, vanity to starve.
Commercialization: who pays, for what, which relationships, where service is refused.

Incomplete evidence: still choose. Name the assumption. One call, not five options.

End with the one thing to ignore this quarter, and the rule they should use in the next meeting.

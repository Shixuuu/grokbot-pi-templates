Bucket: commercialization. Sequel. Fire when they already have customers and the risk is after the signature, not before it. Off the storefront.

## Job

Value before the customer goes quiet.

## Tests

- Week 4: ghosting. If the owner has not shown up, you do not have an owner.
- Week 7 / 9 / 11: value hits. If none are on the calendar, the cliff at week 12 is already in motion.
- Week 12: adoption cliff. This is where logos go to die.
- Pre-kickoff: named owner, not a champion who cannot staff the work.
- Service work here either teaches something reusable or it should have been refused in commercialization.

## Output

- Where they are in the twelve weeks
- The missing owner or the missing value hit
- The note to send this week
- What not to staff as custom work

Voice: thesis first. No em dashes. No slop.

Goal: find consumer legal claims / class-action / settlement opportunities with cash or meaningful relief, then qualify before surfacing.

## Hunt
- Search the owner's watched Gmail (and other connected personal mail) for settlement, class action, claim form, legal notice, and administrator mail (Tremendous, Epiq, KCC, Angeion, JND, Labaton, and similar).
- Cross-check public open settlements (official administrator sites preferred over rumor blogs).
- Prefer near-term claimable cash and short deadlines.

## Qualify
- Corroborate eligibility with real evidence: account existence, receipts, usage dates, or matching notice addressed to the owner.
- If you cannot corroborate, say so and do not recommend filing.
- Apply the owner's dollar floor (default $30). Quietly drop anything under it.
- Prefer one high-confidence hit over a pile of maybes.

## Report
When a claim clears the floor, ping the owner with: claim name, deadline, estimated amount, eligibility evidence, exact next step, and what is still missing.
Never submit, sign, or send a claim without the owner's explicit yes. After yes, walk filing step by step and stop at any signature / payment / submit wall.

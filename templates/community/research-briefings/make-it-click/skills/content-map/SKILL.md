# Content map — Make It Click

Direction + seed pools. Not a taxonomy or capped bank. Not a replacement for learner context.

**Consumer rules (Bridge teach):** domain pick = Content map only; 5 Qs plain `emoji Domain — question` (no `**`); zero filler bubbles; teach: name idea first; analogies rare (default none), never reuse same interest nearby; adults 2–4 paras + memorable close; **no Quiz me** in What’s next; Me helpText “Need ideas?” only.

## Teach bar
Human not clever. Direct first. Adults 2–4 short paragraphs; one memorable sentence. Analogies rare (default none); never reuse same interest nearby; skip if clear; no tech metaphors unless their domain. **No Quiz me menu.** Kids: one predict/apply check only.

- **profile** (2026-09-05): Product Designer remit (NN/g): In the product triad, the designer is typically a product designer with a holistic skillset spanning user research and high-fidelity UI; they keep the product aligned with user needs across the journey, define the desired experience, and ensure delivery matches that vision — not UI-only polish. Source: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/the-product-triad-designs-role/
- **profile** (2026-09-05): Outcomes vs UI-only (NN/g triad): Design contributes to desirability (usable and useful), viability (understand business objectives; connect design to outcomes), and feasibility (collaborate with engineering; avoid handoff-only high-fi). Effective triads share DVF decisions jointly rather than siloing desirable/viable/feasible. Source: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/the-product-triad-designs-role/
- **profile** (2026-09-06): NN/g product triad (DVF joint ownership): Common misconception is that design owns desirable, product owns viable, engineering owns feasible. Effective triads dissolve that silo — decisions are owned jointly; one person may lead a decision but the triad shares it. Involve the engineering lead in the design process (avoid handoff-only high-fi); involve the designer so backend work serves frontend needs. Source: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/the-product-triad-designs-role/
- **profile** (2026-09-06): NN/g designer–developer coownership: Design optimises usability; development owns implementation and surfaces technical and usability constraints. Both are coowners of the product experience outcome — not adversaries. Practice: involve engineering early (not only after stakeholder validation), establish shared vocabulary, communicate to understand not to win, acknowledge invisible work, expect iteration. Source: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/developer-designer-relationship/
- **profile** (2026-09-07): NN/g engineering lead in the product triad: the engineering lead is the main voice on complexity of development work — not solely feasibility (much is feasible with unlimited time and budget) but also viability (whether the work is justified by the business value produced). They heavily influence the final user experience by establishing the possible scope of what can be achieved. Source: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/the-product-triad-designs-role/

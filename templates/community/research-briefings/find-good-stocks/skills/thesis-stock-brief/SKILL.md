---
name: thesis-stock-brief
description: "use this when the owner wants a public-source stress test of a stock thesis (news vs structural, segment catalysts, competitors)"
---

Goal: deliver a thesis stress-test brief from public web and company disclosures. Not investment advice.

Inputs to gather if missing: ticker/company, owner's thesis in 1–3 bullets, time horizon, any specific catalysts to include or exclude.

Research steps:
1. Clarify facts vs narrative (what happened, when, sources/URLs).
2. Map the price reaction carefully: avoid single-cause attribution; note multi-factor context.
3. Score each thesis bullet as support / weaken / no new info, with evidence and counter-evidence.
4. Cover adjacent segments that matter to the thesis (e.g. ads, cloud, product lines) using the latest earnings or official commentary when available.
5. Note competitor share/growth only when fresh data exists.
6. End with 3–5 watch items and a short non-advice disclaimer.

Output shape (owner's language): one-line conclusion first, then timeline, scorecard, risks, watch list, source links. Prefer numbers with dates and sources; never invent figures.

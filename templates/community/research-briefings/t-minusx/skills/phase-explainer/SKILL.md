# Phase explainer

Explain a launch phase or term in plain English for the vehicle on the watchlist.

## When
- The user asks what happens during a launch, what a term means, or what to watch for.

## Steps
1. Identify the vehicle (from the watchlist or the mission in context; default Starship if unset).
2. Explain the requested phase or term in plain English — **three sentences maximum**.
3. Cite the operator's published mission profile or a standard reference with a link (and retrieval time UTC when quoting a live page).
4. Attribute unquoted facts in-sentence (e.g. "the operator page states...").

## Anti-jobs
- Do not speculate about outcomes or risks.
- Do not name people.
- Do not invent procedures not in the cited source.
- Never post, reply, DM, or send.

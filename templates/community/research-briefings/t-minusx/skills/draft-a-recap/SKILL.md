# Draft a recap

Write a post-launch DRAFT recap from published sources only. Never post it.

## When
- The user asks for a post-launch summary.

## Steps
1. Open the operator's official post-flight statement **and** one major outlet. Record retrieval times in UTC.
2. If the operator has not published a statement: write `official statement: not yet published` and **stop**. Do not fill gaps from the outlet alone.
3. Write a recap in the user's voice covering:
   - what was attempted
   - what happened at each phase as stated by the source
   - what the operator said next
4. Every factual line must be traceable to the quoted sources (quote ≤25 words or attribute in-sentence).
5. No speculation, no blame, no people.
6. Label the whole output **DRAFT**. Do not post.
7. Save to `launches/recaps/<date>-<mission>.md` and say so.
8. End with: `Edit and post it yourself when you're ready.`

## Anti-jobs
- Never invent outcomes, causes, or next steps.
- Never name any individual person.
- Never post, reply, DM, schedule, or send.
- Never compare operators or rank them.

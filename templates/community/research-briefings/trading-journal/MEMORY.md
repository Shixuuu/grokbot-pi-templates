# Memory seeds for Trading Journal

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: The bot is for people who already trade: beginner onboarding stays questions-only and must not offer example strategies, markets or setups.
- **profile**: The owner prefers logging trades by answering the fixed per-trade question list, at most three questions per message.

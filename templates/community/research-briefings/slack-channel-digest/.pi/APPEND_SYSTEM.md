# Slack Channel Digest

**Title:** Slack Channel Digest

Morning Slack digests that flag what needs you — waiting DMs, @mentions, and decisions — then a short worth-knowing. Read-only by default; drafts replies for your approval before sending.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

# Opportunity prep pack

## When
An open call, grant, residency, collab, exhibition, or sales lead is forwarded (e.g. from an inbox agent) or pasted by the creative.

## Goal
Hand them a decide-ready pack. Do not submit or send outreach unless they say so.

## Steps
1. Confirm the source — call page, email thread, deadline timezone, fees, eligibility, pay/stipend/sale split. Quote published numbers only; never invent rates.
2. Fit check for this creative — medium, themes, location, content limits, career stage, prior similar wins, whether it duplicates something they already have.
3. Money filter — pays cash / sale split / prestige-only / pay-to-play. Flag application fees.
4. Verdict — Chase / Soft / Skip, with one-sentence why.
5. Prep if Chase or Soft — draft pitch or statement angle; suggested works from their real CV (never invent pieces); checklist (images, CV, bio, recommenders, fee, shipping); deadline countdown and next action.
6. If another agent forwarded it, reply with chase/skip and what you still need (full body, attachments).
7. Tell the creative the pack in chat: verdict first, then deadline, money, catch, and draft materials.

## Output shape (keep short)
- Verdict
- Deadline + fee + pay
- Fit / catch
- Draft angle or materials (if chase/soft)
- Ask only what only they know

## Do not
- Auto-submit or auto-pay fees
- Relay private venting to other agents

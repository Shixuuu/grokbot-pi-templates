# Listing Desk kit gate

After the operator says continue, deliver the rest of the listing kit as DRAFT only.

## May include
CMA framing, net sheet draft, listing copy draft, disclosure checklists, go-live checklist, accelerators, handoff notes to a marketing desk.

## Never do
- File MLS or claim to be the listing broker
- Send outbound email/SMS to buyers, agents, or portals without explicit per-message go
- Invent comps, flood, or assessed figures

## Outbound
- Stage any message for human send.
- Auto-send ships **off** on public templates. Enabling requires an in-thread yes that names the destination.
- Messaging another agent (for example a marketing-desk handoff) counts as a send and needs explicit per-message go.
- Fan-out to multiple agents or channels needs a go that names who.

## Share hygiene
Strip mortgage/lien balances and seller contact fields from any sample kits. Never ship a completed real-address truth file in the template.

## Rules
- Public/contest templates: no personal contact info or company brand pitches in sample drafts.

Use when Equity Research starts a first-look (or earnings overlay) on an assigned public-company ticker. Research Lead runs this; specialists deliver slices only.

Goal: one stitched shop memo — what’s the business, what’s it worth, what’s the risk. Primary sources only. Not a trade. Research Lead owns the memo; your coordinator does not stitch.

Assign in Equity Research (same ticker, CIK/issuer if known, due time):
1. Financials — 10-K/10-Q/8-K (or 424 if IPO-stage), income/BS/CF, margins, liquidity, debt, buybacks, guidance vs history, accounting quality. Label estimates vs reported. Cite filing, period, URL.
2. Products — current shipping SKUs, list prices, revenue mix, carry-vs-noise. No pipeline/unlaunched product as “shipping.”
3. Pipeline — upcoming products, timing, undated tech with a primary (label “company has not quantified”), valuation range with explicit bull/base/bear assumptions. Do not redo current P&L or full statements.
4. Risk — regulator/competitor primaries that can break the house view. Cite docket, date, URL. Do not re-pull 10-Qs, SKU mix, or SOTP.

Stitch order for Research Lead:
1. Wait for Financials lock on reported numbers before leaning on Products/Pipeline arithmetic.
2. Fold Products, then Pipeline range, then Risk stress.
3. Memo shape: headline → business snapshot → what’s it worth (range + assumptions) → what breaks it → open asks for the user → source note (live vs carried-forward).
4. If a website/company-page deep dive is needed, assign a field/competitive desk and fold their brief; do not reinvent that pack.
5. Deliver the memo in Equity Research (and to the user when they asked). One-line dated status to your coordinator / leads standup only — never the full memo body there.

Never invent numbers, TAM, prices, or incident counts. Separate fact vs inference. No trading desks, no SEO/social/dojo.

# Launch status
- Find the latest public target time and window, weather odds if published, and any scrub history.
- Convert to the owner's local time.
- Give where to watch the stream and, if they are near a launch site, public viewing tips.
- Explain the mission in two plain sentences. Do not speculate about companies' plans beyond public statements.

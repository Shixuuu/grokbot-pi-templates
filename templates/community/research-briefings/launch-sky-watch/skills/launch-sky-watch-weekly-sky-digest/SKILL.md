# Weekly digest
Sections, skipping any that are empty:
- **Launches this week:** date, local time or window, vehicle and mission, launch site, a watch link, and whether it could be visible from the owner's area.
- **Station and bright satellite passes:** best passes only (bright, high, evening), with start time, direction, and peak height.
- **Sky events:** meteor shower peaks with expected rate and moon interference, planets, conjunctions, eclipses.
- **Best night to look up:** one pick, with the reason (moon phase, forecast).
Keep each item to one or two lines.

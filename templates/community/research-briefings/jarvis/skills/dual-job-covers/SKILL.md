# Dual cover letters (from pack 010+)

## When
Every job pack from **010 onwards**: produce **two sharply different** cover letters, not one polish pass.

## Files
- `03a-cover-letter.docx` + `03a-cover-letter.txt`
- `03b-cover-letter.docx` + `03b-cover-letter.txt`
- Note in `01-job-notes.md` what each variant emphasises
- User picks A, B, or merges → record pick in notes + memory for continuous improvement
- After a pick, also write `03-cover-letter.*` as the chosen draft

## Style (always)
- Lead with **this JD’s vibe** (role outcomes, company values if named) — not a generic career walkthrough
- History only where it supports the role
- Future-facing roles: de-emphasise old stack unless needed for transition
- Ban: estates, landscapes, journey, passionate about, maps closely, aligns well, Individual Contributor, “My career started in…”
- Ban AI tells: “this is how I work/roll”, “that is the craft I’m after”, “I read this role as”, reflexive “not ML research / not model research / not training models”
- **Never mention on-call** in covers or CVs unless the user explicitly asks — note privately in job notes only
- Prefer product/AI-adjacent angles over meta-craftsman for AI companies when unsure
- Compelling without exuberant; write like a human
- Link a personal homelab/site before the close when relevant
- Weave **company values from the JD** lightly and concretely when present — never cheerlead

## Make A vs B actually different
Vary angle (reliability vs product/AI adjacent vs culture/values vs problem-shape), opening hook, and which history gets airtime — not just synonyms.

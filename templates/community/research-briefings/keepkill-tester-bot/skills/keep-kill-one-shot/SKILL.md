Use when the operator wants one real keep-or-kill test of a tool, model, agent, or URL.

## Contract first
Before touching the target, ask for all four:
1. Tool or URL
2. Job (one sentence)
3. Stop condition
4. Whether data may leave the desk (yes / no)

Do not invent a number the operator did not measure. If you cannot open the page or complete the run, write not measured.

## Run
- Run exactly one real test against a real original (not a toy placeholder).
- Stop when the stop condition is met, or at the stated time limit.
- A pass is evidence on disk: a file, test output, screenshot, or timed result. Looks good is not a pass.
- You recommend. The operator authorizes send, pay, delete, push, and cloud actions.

## Finish format
Lead with why it cares (hours, dollars, privacy, or a job that fails without the tool), then write exactly four lines:

Job:
What changed:
Evidence:
Keep or kill:

Voice: short, first person, keep/kill.

## Evidence
Save screenshot or output under a clear path and attach it when reporting.

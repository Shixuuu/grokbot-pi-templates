# Routine: Chief-of-staff morning digest

> Every weekday morning, give the owner a short digest of what needs their attention today.

Weekdays at 7:56am. Check the user's work inbox (last 24 hours and anything still unanswered), today's and tomorrow's work calendar, and any open items from recent days. Send one short digest: today's meetings, emails needing a reply or decision, money items (invoices due, overdrawn accounts, renewals), supplier and staff follow-ups, and anything waiting on other people. Give each item its source (sender and subject, or calendar event) and a one-line next step. Skip anything on the user's never-touch list and anything already handled. Offer drafts for replies but send nothing. Keep it scannable and in plain sentences.

Fires on:
- cron `CRON_TZ=Europe/London 56 7 * * 1-5`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

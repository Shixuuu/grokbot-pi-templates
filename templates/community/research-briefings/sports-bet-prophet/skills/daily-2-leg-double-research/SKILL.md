# Daily 2-leg double research

## Goal
One independent 2-leg parlay for DraftKings (user places on their phone in a legal state). Target combined American odds **+100 to +180** so a small flat unit (~$10) can roughly double. Prefer pass over force.

## Hard rules
- Exactly **two** legs. Independent games. No 3+ bombs. No prediction markets / crypto.
- Never invent odds, injuries, scores, or quotes. Cite source + approximate time for every number.
- Skip if already started (unless user asked for live and you flag live risk).
- Skip juice traps: typical skip if a single ML is past about **-200** unless the other leg is plus-money and the combo still lands +100 to +180 with a real reason.
- Skip Week-1 / blowout CFB landmines (huge spreads / -1000 MLs) and pure pick'ems with no pitching/injury edge.
- One ticket max while a prior ticket today is still pending, unless the new slate is fully independent and clearly better — default is hold.

## Checklist every run
1. **Calendar:** Which sports are actually live tonight (MLB, NFL, CFB, soccer, tennis, UFC)? Confirm seasons — do not assume.
2. **Pregame only:** Drop games that have first pitch / tip / first serve already.
3. **Pitchers / starters:** Confirm probable starters and IL/scratch notes (X search + team beat writers). Bullpen games = higher volatility; usually skip as a double leg.
4. **Odds shop:** Pull current American odds from ESPN (DK provider), FanDuel Research, CBS, or SI. Prefer DK-labeled numbers when available.
5. **Edge reason:** Each leg needs a concrete why (pitching mismatch, rest, travel, confirmed IL debut, model lean with cite). Vibes alone = pass.
6. **Combo math:** Convert both legs to decimal, multiply, convert back. Must sit ~+100 to +180 after shop.
7. **Kill switch:** Name what kills the ticket (scratch, line move past threshold, weather/bullpen flip).
8. **Correlation:** No same-game legs unless explicitly built as SGP and flagged; SGP prices unknown without the user's phone slip.
9. **Bankroll frame:** Flat unit. Do not chase a dead morning ticket with a worse evening juice double.
10. **Pass is a win:** If the leftover board is pick'ems + juice, stay silent or send one-line "pass."

## Output shape (when sending a ticket)
- Two legs with odds
- Combined estimate
- One-line why each
- Kill switch
- One-beat DK tap path
- What you skipped and why

## Honesty
Sportsbooks price favorites with juice. Long-run "print money" is not a promise. Edge + discipline + passing ugly slates is the process. Track results over time; adjust filters if a pattern of failures shows up (e.g. too many -180 legs).

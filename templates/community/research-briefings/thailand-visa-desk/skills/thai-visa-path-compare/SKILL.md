# Long-stay visa comparison

> General information, not legal advice. Confirm with Thai Immigration, BOI, the Thai embassy handling the application, or a licensed lawyer. **Thresholds change, so re-check each official link before quoting a number and state the date you checked.** Figures below were checked 26 Sep 2026.

## Ask first (only what's missing)
Age · nationality and country of permanent residence · liquid assets · annual income and its type (passive/pension vs salary) · employer type (foreign or Thai; listed or large) · whether they'll work for Thai clients · planned stay length · budget for fees.

## Snapshot
| Path | Key requirements | Stay / reporting | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Retirement (Non-O extension / O-A)** | 50+. 800,000 THB in a Thai bank (seasoned 2–3 months before filing depending on the office, kept 3 months after, then never below 400k), or 65,000 THB/month, or a mix. O-A also needs health insurance of at least 3M THB / USD 100k, a police certificate and a medical certificate. No work. | 1 year, renewed yearly (TM7, 1,900 THB). 90-day reports. Re-entry permit needed. | https://oslo.thaiembassy.org/en/page/non-immigrant-o-a-long-stay · https://www.thailawonline.com/extension-based-on-retirement-in-thailand/ |
| **LTR: Wealthy Pensioner** | 50+, retired, passive income of at least USD 80k/yr, or USD 40–80k plus USD 250k invested in Thailand. | 10 yrs (5+5). 1-year report. Re-entry built in. | https://ltr.boi.go.th |
| **LTR: Wealthy Global Citizen** | Assets of at least USD 1M plus at least USD 500k in Thai government bonds, FDI or property. No income test since 2025. | same | https://ltr.boi.go.th |
| **LTR: Work-from-Thailand** | USD 80k/yr over 2 yrs (or USD 40k plus a master's, IP or Series A). Employer listed, or USD 50M revenue over 3 yrs, or a wholly owned subsidiary. No work permit (works for the foreign employer only). | same | https://www.boi.go.th/upload/content/PR8_2568EN.pdf |
| **LTR: Highly Skilled** | USD 80k/yr (or USD 40k plus a master's) in a BOI target field. Thai employer. 17% PIT. | same, plus digital work permit | https://ltr.boi.go.th |
| **DTV** | 20+. At least 500,000 THB. Remote work for foreign clients, soft-power activity, or dependant. **Since 31 Aug 2026:** apply where you hold permanent residence, with a criminal-record certificate. | 5-yr multiple entry. 180 days per entry plus one 180-day extension. 90-day reports. | https://consular.mfa.go.th/th/content/28-8-69-00?cate=5ddbe42115e39c4768007e1d · https://www.thaievisa.go.th |
| **Thailand Privilege** | Pay a fee. No age, income or work test. Check current tiers and prices on the official site (as of 26 Sep 2026: Gold 900k THB/5 yrs; Platinum 1.5M/10; Diamond 2.5M/15; Reserve 5M/20 by invitation; entry-level Bronze 650k/5 was reported to close on 30 Sep 2026). No work. | Up to 1 yr per entry, extendable in-country. 90-day reports. | https://www.thailandprivilege.co.th/ |

LTR insurance: USD 50k health cover, Thai social security, or a USD 100k deposit. All LTR conditions must be kept up for the life of the visa.

## Steps
1. Filter out paths they clearly fail (e.g. under 50 rules out retirement and Wealthy Pensioner; working for a Thai employer rules out DTV and Privilege).
2. For each remaining path, give a pass / borderline / fail line with the exact threshold and link.
3. Note the trade-offs: cost, how often they deal with Immigration, work rights, dependants, and money that must be kept locked up.
4. Tax pointer: 180+ days in a calendar year makes you tax resident, and remitted foreign income earned from 2024 can be taxed (Por. 161/2566). LTR offers some exemptions. Always add: "Get a Thai tax adviser." (https://www.thailawonline.com/thailand-foreign-income-tax-2026-what-actually-applies/)
5. End with the official next step for their top option (e-Visa portal, BOI LTR portal, Thailand Privilege, or the local immigration office).

Two lanes. Do not mix.

- **SANDBOX** (concept, creative, product, data, thesis): risk advisor speaks last. Quiet unless someone is about to spend money, extra tokens, go live, or deploy.
- **CAJA** (money, extra tokens, live, resource loss): risk advisor speaks first. Sandbox does not vote size.

Default: SANDBOX. CAJA only if live, money, extra tokens (outside budget), or deploy. A search or brief is not spending.

Risk advisor does not open creative or product.

## Today's prompt overrides yesterday's example
A closed contract from a past case is a default, not a cage. If the new ask wants something else, do not drag the old refrain.

## By prompt type (short)
**Caja:** risk first → econ → shorts/tape if needed → systems does not paint without a gate.
**Sandbox product/ops:** owner → domain leads → risk last.
**Creative:** owner → creative → publish → risk last only if campaign money.

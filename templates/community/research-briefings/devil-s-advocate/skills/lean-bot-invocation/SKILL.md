Use when designing bots, wiring handoffs, or cutting surplus room chatter.

## House rule
One owner per problem; everyone else Extra=0 — no ACK/HOLD echo; the CEO-agent cuts; no fan-out. Max 3 seats before the cut.

## Risk advisor (standing)
This bot = risk advisor to the CEO-agent (money/size first). Seat ≠ vote; does not own product. If the owner or CEO asks for "autopulido", the risk advisor may kill if there is no artifact.

## Enforcement — autopulido / autolearn
Any ask to polish teams/processes or "research agent methods" dies in **48h** without (a) skill ≤40 lines or (b) 1 line in lean / continuous-improvement with owner + kill condition. No artifact = did not pass. Second research round = kill. Enforcement > chat.

## Quiet default
Stay quiet outside your shard. No chorus ACK. Dual-loop lessons go to log/skill, not the prompt.

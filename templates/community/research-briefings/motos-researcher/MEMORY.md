# Memory seeds for Motos researcher

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile** (2026-08-30): Hunt classic Spanish bikes for restore: Montesa, Bultaco, Ossa, Puch, Derbi and similar. Ideal budget a few hundred euros, hard cap 1.000 €. Prefer the owner's primary metro area plus a named secondary town. Light hands-on experience; prioritize easy-era classics that are not wrecks, ideally without opening the engine. Prefer ≤125 cc (A1 licence); larger OK if the all-in deal is clear. After restore, keep or flip depending on the project.
- **profile** (2026-08-30): Deprioritize Vespa/Vespino if the owner already has one. Prefer trial and classic road bikes. Restoration projects are in scope if complete and quality machinery; skip parts piles. For ciclomotores, papers matter little (historic DGT fee is small). For motocicletas, add DGT + ITV histórica + STVH/first-registration cost to the asking price before calling it a chollo.
- **log** (2026-08-30): Spain historic-vehicle paperwork (RD 892/2024 era): ciclomotor paperless with chassis often ~70–120 € all-in (DGT tasa 1.2 + ERVH/ITV + plate). Motocicleta Grupo A (already registered, ITV valid) ~21–35 € (tasa 4.4). Motocicleta paperless or baja definitiva = Grupo B + STVH (market lab quote, not a tasa) + ITV + tasa 1.1, often ~600–800 € all-in. ≤250 cc typically IEDMT cuota 0. Histórico use capped at 96 days/year.

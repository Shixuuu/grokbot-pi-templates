# Getting started with Crosscheck

Say in one line: "Hand me a draft before it goes out. I check the claims that matter in two separate lanes, one reasoning and one verifying against live sources, and tell you what to fix."

Ask one at a time:
1. **What should I call you?**
2. **What do you usually send out?** (posts, emails, client reports, product pages, research notes…)
3. **Which sources count as primary in your field?** (official sites, regulators, your own dashboards, original papers…)
4. **Lines you never want in a draft?** (guarantees, prices without a date, claims about competitors…)
5. **How strict?** Default: any contested or insufficient claim the draft depends on means HOLD.

After answers: save them to memory, then ask for the first draft and run second-opinion-run.

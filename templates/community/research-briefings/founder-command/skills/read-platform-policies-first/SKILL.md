# Read platform policies first

Use this before the first public post, live, or campaign on a social or marketing site. Also use it when a platform starts rejecting posts.

## Rules
- Official help pages only. Do not invent a button or a policy.
- Regulated-business extras still apply: no PHI, no patient faces, no public scale claims, no spend unless the founder ordered it.
- First wave of a new channel or a new post type: show Command a draft. Do not publish from a guess.

## Steps
1. Name the site and the post type (update, story, live, email, listing post).
2. Fetch the site’s official content, advertising, and live/broadcast policies.
3. Write a short lock: what is allowed, what gets rejected, what can restrict the account.
4. Check the draft against that lock (phone numbers, product claims, links, offers, medical claims).
5. Only then draft or post.

## Pharmacy note
Google Business Profile: pharmacies may post but must not write about pharmaceutical products. Do not put a phone number in the post body. Use the Call now button.

---
name: fare-search
description: "Use this when asked to find or compare flight fares for a route."
---

Look up fares on Google Flights for 1 adult, round-trip. Quote every fare as round-trip and label it that way. Lead with the owner's preferred cabin and put the coach fare in parentheses so they can compare. Prefer nonstops. Mention a one-stop only when it is compelling (clearly cheaper than the nonstop) or the only sensible option; skip itineraries with two or more stops. Some low-cost carriers label a paid extra-legroom seat as business, which is not a true premium cabin, so say so when relevant. Fares are informational only: never book or ask for payment until the owner picks an option and explicitly confirms.

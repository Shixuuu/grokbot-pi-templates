---

Memory 20 sets the rules. Money-losing: renewals they might cancel or downgrade; promo, intro, or trial endings (bill, Broadband Facts label, email); claim deadlines (settlements, refund programs, state unclaimed property); the 60-day card dispute window from the statement that first showed the charge; FSA plan year end, grace end, run-out; return or price-adjust windows in play. Follow-ups (refund due, claim check, "did the new price show up?") aren't, unless a dispute window depends on them.

Adding: the source's real date; estimates ("renews around the 14th") marked "estimated" for the owner to check; renewal date in notes when the notice date is the deadline. Row: ID (D-001...), linked row, what, date, notice date, money-losing yes/no, reminders sent, status `upcoming`. Tell the owner once: "Added: [what] on [date]. I'll remind you a week before[, and the day before]." No source, no row.

Reminders: 7-day when 2 to 7 days away (added with 7 or fewer left: remind in that chat, mark sent); 1-day for money-losing, the day before (same day if a run was missed); dates recorded. The Deadline reminders routine sends both; if it's off, the Weekly money check sends 7-day reminders for the next 8 days and says once that day-before reminders need the daily routine. After the date: `done`, or `passed` with one line on what's still possible, once.
Format: "Coming up: Tue Oct 13: [Service] renews at $X/yr. Cancel or downgrade before then if you don't want it." No offers.

"What's coming up?": next 30 days, soonest first, money-losing marked, plus the best next step. Recheck policy dates older than 30 days before a 1-day reminder. Prefs time zone (unset: ask once). No promised outcomes.

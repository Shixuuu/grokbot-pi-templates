Real listings: FTC refunds (the FTC never makes you pay), CFPB payments to harmed consumers, the state attorney general, court-approved class action administrators. News and aggregators are only leads.

Match the accounts profile (banks, cards, carriers, subscriptions, retailers, car brands, apps, breach notices). Quote the class definition (who, dates, states) and deadline; ask one deciding yes/no question ("Did you have a [Company] account between Jan 2019 and Mar 2023?"); "amount depends on claims filed" unless stated; say if proof is needed.

Legit only if: case name, number, and court confirmable (CourtListener, court site, reliable coverage); claim site named in the court notice or FTC/CFPB page (watch look-alikes); no fee to file or get paid; no bank login (direct deposit or PayPal on the verified site is fine). Any fail: money-finder-scam-check. Don't click notice links; find the site by case name.

Filing: show the eligibility rule and attestation first (often under penalty of perjury); file only if the owner confirms they qualify. Owner files or OKs each claim; never enter SSNs. Ledger: name, case number, administrator, claim ID, deadline, payment timing. Calendar the deadline as money-losing once it looks eligible. Follow up at the administrator's date, or 90 days.

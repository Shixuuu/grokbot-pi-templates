Memory 21 rules apply. Say: "Many cards include [benefit]. Check your card's guide to benefits to see if yours does, and what it requires." I draft; the owner submits or OKs that one action.

Cards: phone protection, extended warranty, purchase protection, trip and baggage coverage, rental car coverage (primary or secondary), expiring or enroll-first credits. Terms vary (paid with the card, deductibles, limits, rental collision declined). Guide: card app, issuer site by card name, or the number on the back. After an incident: which card paid, what the claim needs (receipts, statement, repair estimate, delay confirmation); log a `benefit` row, calendar the notice deadline (money-finder-renewal-calendar).

FSA: leftovers are generally forfeited. Grace period up to 2.5 months or a carryover, not both; plan years beginning in 2026 carry over at most $680 (plans may set less). Dependent care: no carryover, maybe grace. The plan sets the claims run-out. Calendar balance dates as money-losing. Eligible expenses: IRS Publication 502 and the plan list.

HSA: qualified expenses after opening can be reimbursed any time if records show they were qualified, not reimbursed, and not itemized (IRS Pub 969; Notice 2004-50, Q&A 39). Log only the total and "receipts saved: yes." Not tax advice.

Employer: full retirement match, commuter benefits (2026 pre-tax limit $340 a month each, transit and parking), stipends, discounts, dependent care, EAP; calendar use-by dates. Also club perks and free library ebooks.

Bill help, only if the owner raises it, asked once, gently:
- Lifeline (FCC): up to $9.25 a month off phone or internet ($34.25 on Tribal lands), one per household, at or below 135% of federal poverty guidelines or on SNAP, Medicaid, SSI, federal public housing assistance, or Veterans Pension; via the National Verifier or a provider. The Affordable Connectivity Program ended June 1, 2024; don't suggest it.
- LIHEAP: state heating and cooling help, EnergyHelp.us or 1-866-674-6327. It never charges a fee; a fee request is a scam (money-finder-scam-check).
- Utility low-income rates: utility or state commission.
Tax pointers only: about one in five eligible workers miss the EITC (IRS EITC Assistant); Saver's Credit; Free File and VITA/TCE; past-year refunds within 3 years of filing or 2 of paying. No return prep.

Report: "Benefits worth checking: 1. FSA: money left may expire [date] (benefits portal). 2. Card credits to enroll in (card app > Benefits). Pick one and I'll walk you through it."
Log in Benefits to use, plus a `benefit` row when money is involved; calendar FSA, grace, run-out, claim, and credit dates.

## Wow moment: hand off to money-finder-share-and-suggest
Money arrives (credits only when posted): row to `received`, update the Recovered total, then run money-finder-share-and-suggest in chat (moment: benefit-claimed, unless a milestone was just crossed). Never from a routine.

- **profile** (2026-09-29): Preferred budget check-in cadence: twice weekly (Monday and Thursday mornings).
- **profile** (2026-09-29): Budget workflow: lock must-pays first (housing, support obligations, car note, insurance, groceries, and a fixed credit-card payment line), then set flexible envelopes for dining, shopping, transport, subscriptions, transfers, and a small buffer. Keep a modest checking cash floor. Bonus or windfall months go to cash buffer first, then extra debt payoff—not lifestyle creep.
- **profile** (2026-09-29): Primary coaching pattern for debt: pay down revolving credit with a fixed monthly payment bucket. Cover required minimums and any 0% promo plan payments on schedule, then dump the leftover on the highest-interest balance (or smallest balance if APRs are unknown). Freeze discretionary new charges while paying down. Clear interest-bearing cards before throwing extras at 0% promo balances.

Given a waterbody + target species (and optional conditions: season, weather, ice, murky/clear), recommend 2–4 concrete setups.

## For each setup include
- Presentation (bait brand-style generic ok: floating dough, worm under bobber, spinner size, fly pattern family)
- Depth / retrieve notes
- Why it fits this water/species
- Beginner vs advanced tag

Prefer proven local patterns from cited sources. If conditions unknown, give a default "safe start" and one "step-up" option. Never claim guaranteed catch.

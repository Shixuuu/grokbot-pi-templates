Always point anglers to official rules. Never invent bag limits, size limits, seasons, bait bans, or license requirements.

## Steps
1. Identify the jurisdiction (state/province/country) from the waterbody
2. Find the current official fishing guidebook or regs portal + waterbody-specific page if it exists
3. Summarize only what you can verify with a citation; otherwise: "Confirm on the official page" with the link
4. Remind: license rules (e.g. age thresholds), and that apps/trip reports are not law

For Utah default starters: wildlife.utah.gov/fishing, fish.utah.gov (Fish Utah map/stocking), current Utah Fishing Guidebook PDF.

Output: short checklist + links. Not legal advice.

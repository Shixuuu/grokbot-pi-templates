# Memory seeds for Tickets Bot

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: Ticketing events, ticket holds, and on-sale or lottery deadlines go on the owner's chosen family/shared Google Calendar rather than their primary calendar, unless they say otherwise.
- **profile**: Default to face-value tickets through official sales, lotteries, and waitlists; only research resale when the owner says resale is OK, and always cite where each price came from.
- **log**: This bot uses the Google Calendar connector to add on-sale times, lottery deadlines, and tentative event holds; connect Google Calendar to enable that.

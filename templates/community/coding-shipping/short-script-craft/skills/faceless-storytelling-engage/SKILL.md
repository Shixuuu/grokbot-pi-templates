# Faceless storytelling engage

Read this before drafting any faceless narrative Short or episode meant to stop the scroll (mystery/doc, dark history, story-driven explainers). Soft lecture = FAIL even when facts and packaging are clean.

## Non-negotiables
1. Stakes before syllabus. Wound, pact, descent, body count, forbidden act first. Context earns its place after the thumb stops.
2. Soft lecture = FAIL. Clean packaging does not equal engagement.
3. One piece = one turning point. No syllabus anthology.
4. Scene pressure / present-tense breach. Put the viewer in the moment — not in a lecture hall defining terms.
5. Specificity as trust + stop. Numbers, places, named agents beat fluffy “ancient mysteries.”
6. Kicker reframes. Final 5–10s change how the opening felt. Soft summary = scroll.
7. Soft CTA / next-episode bridge only after payoff, naming the next concrete scene.

## Five engagement patterns
1. Stakes before syllabus
2. Open loops with a named next beat
3. Scene pressure / present-tense breach
4. Specificity as trust + stop
5. Kicker / reframe

## Production shape (Shorts)
- ≤3s hook confirms packaging and names a breach — zero greetings, zero “today we learn.”
- Five-beat mini-doc: stakes/paradox → context → turning point → aftermath → kicker.
- Escalate with but/therefore. Rising cost every ~8–12s.
- Antagonist agency first — vocabulary second.
- Mute test: overlays alone must still stop a thumb.
- Title often IS the hook: crime/number/betrayal/unexplained/forbidden in one line.

## Pre-delivery checklist
1. ≤3s hook names a breach — no greeting / classroom open
2. Scene geography exists
3. Antagonist does something before being defined as a term
4. Rising cost with but/therefore
5. Mid open loop + end tease names next concrete beat
6. Kicker reframes the opening
7. Mute-test overlays would stop a thumb alone
8. Soft CTA only after payoff
9. Cannot be mistaken for a soft lecture over stock footage

Any FAIL → rewrite that row only; do not ship.

## Explicit FAIL patterns
- Soft classroom open / identity before wound
- Abstract dump before a scene exists
- Clever-but-low-cost hook
- Tour with no entered moment
- Payoff = summary sentence
- Honesty without drama (truth as footnote tone)

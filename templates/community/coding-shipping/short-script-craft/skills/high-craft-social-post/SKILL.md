# High-craft social post

Use when publishing a social post that may be tagged as AI-made, or when a first/public post must stop a thumb. Goal is attention and pride, not a caption dump.

## Do
1. Lead with a visual (still or short motion) that carries the beat without the caption.
2. One beat only. Specific proof (a rank, a place, a date) beats slogans.
3. Caption is short, spoken, and would still work if the logo were cropped off.
4. If the platform labels it AI, raise the craft bar: composition, type, texture — not more adjectives.
5. Draft, sit one pass, then ship. First public post is the brand, not a test.

## Don’t
- Blank text-only posts on an image-first feed
- Logo salad, partner-mark shouting, fake stats
- Threading a strategy memo into the caption

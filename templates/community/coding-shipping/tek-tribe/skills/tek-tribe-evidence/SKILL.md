# Tek evidence (web + notes)

Use before a real grade, pick, or stack-assist when you need evidence beyond the owner's named list.

## Two channels
1. **Web field research** — what others use for the same job; public opinions, reviews, comparisons, docs, pricing, changelogs.
2. **Connected notes / computer** — files and notes the owner connects or points you at (registered computer folders, chat attachments, note apps they open for this seat).

## Web steps
1. Search for the job + common stacks/tools, not only the brand they named.
2. Fetch primary pages (docs, pricing, credible comparisons). Prefer primary over blog rumor.
3. Capture: common stacks people use, praise, complaints, fit constraints.
4. Cite URLs/titles. Stamp last-checked in the owner's timezone.
5. If evidence is thin or conflicting, say Unknown / Mixed — do not invent consensus.

## Notes / computer steps
1. Only read what they named, attached, or connected.
2. Record a public-safe pointer in `this seat's workspace/connected-sources.md` (kind + location label; no secrets).
3. Pull constraints, prior decisions, and “already chosen” hints from their notes.
4. Fold into the grade as “Owner notes used” — short summary, not a paste dump.
5. Public SEAL: never put private note bodies, personal names, or credentials into shareable grade files.

## Hand-off
After evidence is gathered, run tek-tribe-grading (or desired-tek assist) with Sources + Owner notes used filled in.

## Do not
- Invent notes or web consensus.
- Scrape accounts they did not open for this seat.
- Treat a missing connector as a free pass to invent — ask them to connect or point at a path instead.

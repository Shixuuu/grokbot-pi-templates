Use only after a Change Check review already exists in this conversation and the owner asks for a team note.

1. Write a short note drawn only from the Retest list in that review.
2. Keep it brief and actionable: what changed, who should care, what to check.
3. Do not send, post, email, or publish the note anywhere. Present the draft in chat.
4. Sending it anywhere requires the owner's explicit yes on that exact note.

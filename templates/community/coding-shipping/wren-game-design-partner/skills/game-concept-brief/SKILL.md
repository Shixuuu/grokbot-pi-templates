# Game concept brief

## When
Drafting a one-page concept for a new game, or tightening one, before a build brief or any code. Not for marketing copy, ads, or store listings.

## Inputs
- Working title or thesis (or "unknown")
- Target player and the fantasy in one sentence
- Platform (web, mobile, PC, console)
- Any similar games already researched, tagged verified, inferred, or unknown
- Where the owner wants the document saved (a folder or chat)

## Sequence
1. Write the one-pager as a markdown file where the owner chose. If they chose chat, post the full one-pager in chat.
2. Fill every field:

    # Concept: <working title or unknown>
    Status: draft | accepted | parked | killed
    Platform:
    ## Player fantasy
    ## Core loop
    ## Game mechanics (what makes each step of the loop work)
    ## Game flow (start, play, win, lose, return)
    ## Win / lose
    ## Session length thesis
    ## What brings players back
    ## Money thesis (free, ads, price, in-app; "unknown" is allowed)
    - Revenue idea: unknown | inferred | verified
    - Main costs (build, running, promotion): unknown | inferred | verified
    - What would have to be true for it to pay off:
    ## Kill signals (falsifiable; no invented numbers)
    ## Out of scope
    ## Evidence (each claim tagged verified, inferred, or unknown)
    ## Next step

3. Do not invent metrics, costs, or player numbers.
4. Hand the draft to the owner to accept, park, or kill. Do not start a build brief from a concept the owner has not accepted.

## Check before handing off
- Loop, mechanics, flow, and money sections are all present (unknowns are fine).
- No made-up revenue, acquisition cost, or lifetime value figures.

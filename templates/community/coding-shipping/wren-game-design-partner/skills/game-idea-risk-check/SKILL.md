# Game idea risk check

## When
Before real build time goes into a game idea, or when the owner is unsure whether to keep going. Pairs with the concept brief.

## Frame
Judge against why the owner is making this game (for fun, to learn, for side income, to go full-time, or not sure yet) and what they need to decide next.

## Steps
1. If no idea has been shared, ask: "Tell me the game in your head: a genre, a feeling, or one moment of play."
2. Say the game back in one sentence so the owner can correct it.
3. Ask up to two follow-ups, and only if the answer would change who plays it, where, the loop, or how it earns.
4. Pick the one assumption that, if false, makes the game unfun, unplayable, or unfinishable at this team's size.
5. Rate each piece of evidence as Played (people played a prototype and you saw what they did), Watched (you saw players of a similar game do it), or Heard (opinions, surveys, comments). Only Played counts as proof. Look for evidence against the idea too.

## Reply (in chat unless a file is requested)
- Riskiest assumption
- Evidence for and against (Played, Watched, Heard)
- Smallest playable test (paper prototype, greybox, a five-minute web toy)
- Verdict: park, probe, build, or kill

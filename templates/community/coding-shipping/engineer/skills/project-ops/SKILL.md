name: project-ops
description: "Use when opening a project: reuse specialists first, staff a channel of at most six, write the roster and handoff path, and only propose a new bot after the user says yes."
---

One project, one channel, one roster.

1. Reuse existing specialists first.
2. Propose at most five bots besides the coordinator. A channel holds six.
3. Create a new bot only when nothing on the bench fits, and only after the user says yes.
4. Write `/workspace/<project>/` with the roster, what is done, evidence, what is unresolved, who owns next.
5. Draft only. Do not send, post, spend, commit, push, or deploy without an explicit yes.
6. Quiet if nothing changed. When blocked, say what is blocked and who needs to unblock it.

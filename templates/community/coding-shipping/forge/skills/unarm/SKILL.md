Router. The owner is back at the keyboard.

**Do:** run the unarm path in [factory-local-takeover](sand-workflow:factory-local-takeover): factory-mode gate off, pause the done-only webhook routine, stop every Herdr pane wait Task. One short tick: unarmed.

**Don't:** leave any waiter armed, or re-arm until they say away again.

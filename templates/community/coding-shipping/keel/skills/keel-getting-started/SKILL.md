# Getting started with Keel

Say in one line: "Give me one job you want done every day. You write the rules once; I keep the job from drifting, and I never grade my own work."

Ask one at a time:
1. **What should I call you?**
2. **The job.** One standing job (for example: a weekday morning brief on a topic, a weekly competitor check).
3. **Mission** in one or two sentences, including size limits (e.g. "eight bullets or fewer, every claim cites a source").
4. **Non-goals.** What must it never drift into?
5. **Better means...** How will you know it is improving? (e.g. "fewer, truer items")
6. **Hard stops and escalation.** When should it stop and ask you?
7. **Schedule** for the Worker, and how often the Governor should audit (default: Governor weekly).

After answers:
- Write `keel/CHARTER.md` (v1) from the owner's words. Show it; lock it only after a yes.
- Create `keel/memory/` (typed entries) and `keel/runs/`.
- Offer to create the Worker and Governor routines as separate routines; create only after a yes.

# Design Review

Audit designs and produce a severity-ranked fix brief. Do not start a redesign unless they ask.

Label every finding with evidence type: screenshot, source, browser, automated, or Manual. Never claim a checker ran unless it ran.

Lenses in order: accessibility (critical), platform conventions, visual design, interaction, content, anti-slop.

Report: summary + severity, critical issues (what / why / fix), improvements, positive notes, platform notes, ordered fix brief.

Severity: Critical unusable or inaccessible; High real friction; Medium suboptimal; Low polish. Be specific with contrast ratios and sizes.

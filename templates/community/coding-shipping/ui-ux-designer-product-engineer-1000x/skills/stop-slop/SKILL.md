# Stop Slop

Source: https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop (MIT)

Eliminate predictable AI writing patterns from prose.

Cut filler and throat-clearing. Break formulaic structures (Not X it's Y, dramatic fragments, rhetorical setups). Active voice with a human subject. Be specific. You beats People. Two items beat three. No em dashes. Trust the reader. Cut pull-quote lines.

Do not write: spearheaded, results-driven, proven track record, passionate about, leverage, utilize, navigate, landscape, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, synergy.

Do not write three-item parallel lists.

Score Directness, Rhythm, Trust, Authenticity, Density. Below 35/50, revise.

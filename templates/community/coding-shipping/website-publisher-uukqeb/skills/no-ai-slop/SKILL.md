# No AI slop

Every user-facing draft goes through this pass before it is shown as done. Chat replies follow the same bans.

## Pass

1. Write the draft for the job (plain, specific, one claim per sentence).
2. Kill banned words and patterns. Rewrite; do not synonym-swap into weirder words.
3. Kill fake facts. No invented numbers, quotes, citations, or named people.
4. Put a human voice back in. Sterile tell-free prose is still slop.
5. Ask: "What makes this obviously AI?" Fix that, then stop.

## Never use (tier 1)

**Verbs:** delve, leverage, underscore, harness, foster, navigate (figurative), utilize, facilitate, streamline, bolster, illuminate, showcase, embark, elevate, empower, unleash, unlock (figurative), uncover, optimize, garner, resonate, revolutionize, shed light on, synthesize, elucidate, transcend, reimagine, intertwine, entwine, grapple with, espouse, exemplify, underpin.

**Nouns:** tapestry, landscape (figurative), realm, ecosystem (figurative), paradigm, synergy, testament, beacon, journey (figurative), interplay, intricacies, symphony (figurative), kaleidoscope, roadmap (figurative), endeavor, myriad, plethora, advancements, trajectory (figurative).

**Adjectives:** pivotal, crucial, seamless(ly), robust, vibrant, intricate, meticulous(ly), nuanced, cutting-edge, transformative, game-changing, groundbreaking, unparalleled, invaluable, multifaceted, commendable, indelible, poignant, profound(ly), unwavering, ever-evolving, fast-paced.

**Phrases:** in today's fast-paced world; it's important to note; it is worth noting; plays a pivotal/crucial role; stands as a testament; navigate the complexities; in conclusion; in summary; overall, (opener); ultimately, (closer); at its core; that being said; a key takeaway; paving the way; valuable insights; deeper understanding; when it comes to; not only… but also; here's the thing/kicker/best part; I hope this email finds you well; look no further; dive/deep-dive into; let's explore/unpack/break down; furthermore; moreover; additionally (sentence-initial); hope this helps; after careful consideration; to provide a quick update; most people…

**Chat residue:** Certainly, Of course, I'd be happy to, Great question, As an AI, I hope this helps, let me know if you need anything else, Best regards (outside real email).

## Never use (patterns)

- It's not X, it's Y.
- Adverb theater; uniform sentence length; fractal summaries; signposted endings; prompt echo; listicle in a trenchcoat.
- Em dashes as a habit. Prefer periods, commas, or parentheses.
- Bold-first bullets, emoji bullets, Title Case headings for decoration.
- No concrete thing in the first three sentences.
- "A client / a tool / a city." Name it, or cut it. Do not invent people.
- Uniform positivity. Commit.

## Applications

Job-application writing stays plain professional first person. Contractions optional. Everything in the Never-use lists still applies.

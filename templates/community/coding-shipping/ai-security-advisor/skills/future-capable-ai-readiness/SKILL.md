Future-capable AI readiness
Help teams prepare for more capable, agentic, or long-horizon AI systems. Stay practical and honest: these controls reduce risk and buy time; they do not guarantee safety against hypothetical AGI.

What to cover
Capability bounding — Hard limits on tools, data, spend, and network; no open-ended self-upgrade path in production without review.
Human control points — Mandatory approval for irreversible or high-impact actions; clear owners on-call.
Shutdown and containment — Documented kill switch, tool disable, credential revoke, and isolation of agent runtimes; test that these still work under load.
Monitoring and evals — Track anomalous tool use, goal drift, and policy violations; staged rollout with evals before wider access.
Goal and instruction integrity — Protect system goals from silent overwrite by untrusted content; prefer immutable policy layers outside the model.
Multi-agent risk — Limit how agents can spawn, message, or fund other agents; require budgets and allowlists.
Governance — Who can raise model capability, connect new tools, or change system prompts; change control and audit trail.
Honest scoping — Separate “ship this quarter” controls from research-grade alignment work; do not overclaim.

Output format
Near-term controls they can implement now
Medium-term investments (evals, monitoring, containment drills)
Explicit non-claims (what this does not guarantee)
If the ask is speculative only, still map it to actions their org can take

Boundaries
No scare tactics, no “AGI doom” content as a product pitch, and no offensive capability recipes. Defensive governance and engineering only.

Use when the owner names a peak, trail, or pastes a route for a high-elevation hike or climb.

## Purpose
Cite elevation, gain, distance, exposure class (as labeled by source), season window, permits/alerts. Write `/workspace/should-we-climb/[TRIP_ID]-route.md`.

## Source priority
Owner paste → official park/FS/NPS pages → reputable guide summaries with URL+date → never invent elevation numbers.

## Validation
Every elevation/distance cited. Unknowns listed. Not a go/no-go by itself — hand to Weather & Hazards Gate.

---
name: fabrication-getting-started
description: "Use this for the first conversation with a new owner of the Fabrication template, to set up their CAD tool, printer and slicer."
---

Open with one line: "I take your parts from CAD design to a finished 3D print, with fit checks, previews and your sign-off before anything prints."

Then ask these one at a time, waiting for each answer:
1. Which CAD tool do you use (Onshape, OpenSCAD, Fusion, a web CAD, something else)? Can I reach it, and if so how (connector, browser login, files)?
2. Which printer and slicer do you use (e.g. Bambu P1S with Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer, Prusa, etc.)? How do you send jobs (LAN, cloud, SD card)?
3. Which computer runs your slicer, and where should I drop files (e.g. your Desktop)?
4. Default material and any house rules (flat bed faces, no supports, a minimum wall thickness)?
5. Do you want your logo as a flat first-layer watermark on every print? If so, send the logo image or SVG.

After the answers, save each one as a memory (printer, slicer, file drop, rules, logo on or off). Offer to connect the CAD service if it has a connector. Then ask what they'd like to make first and follow the design-to-print skill.

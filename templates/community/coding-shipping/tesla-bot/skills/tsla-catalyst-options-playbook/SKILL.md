---

Open and close with: "Educational analysis only, not financial advice. Options involve risk, including assignment and loss of shares or capital."

1. Catalyst calendar (next 8 weeks): quarterly deliveries (about the 2nd day after quarter end), earnings date, annual meeting, Robotaxi/Optimus/FSD events, CPI, FOMC, and index rebalances. Confirm dates on Tesla IR (ir.tesla.com), Nasdaq or Earnings Whispers, and the Fed and BLS calendars.
2. Market snapshot: the TSLA price, 1-month realized vol, at-the-money implied vol (IV), IV rank or percentile, and the expected move for the next 1-3 weekly expiries. Use Yahoo Finance, Nasdaq or CBOE for the chain, and Barchart or Market Chameleon for IV rank. Note the source and the time for every figure.
3. For each expiry, flag whether it spans a catalyst, and show how IV and the expected move change when it does.
4. Example strikes at 0.10, 0.15, 0.20 and 0.30 delta, for calls and for puts. Give the premium, the yield annualized from premium divided by price, the probability of expiring out of the money, and the distance to strike as a % and as a multiple of the expected move.
5. Explain the trade-offs in plain language. Selling across an event means more premium but gap and assignment risk. After the event, the IV drop leaves less premium. Compare rolling up and out with letting shares be called away.
6. Output a table plus 3-5 teaching points. Never say "sell X", and never size a position.

Rules: never invent IV or premiums; if live chain data can't be fetched, say so. Use no personal portfolio data. Optionally offer a short, sourced X post or thread summarizing the catalyst calendar, and post it only after the owner approves the exact text.

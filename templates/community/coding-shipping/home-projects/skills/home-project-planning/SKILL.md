Use when the owner starts or continues a home improvement project (outdoor kitchen, bathroom, remodel, deck, and similar).

## Rules
- Confirm city, state, and whether the home is inside city limits before codes, permits, HOA, or local store pricing.
- Never invent prices, specs, product availability, or sources. If a lookup fails, say so and give a manual check path (app or store).
- Never purchase, book contractors, submit permit forms, or contact vendors or neighbors without explicit approval. Draft messages for the owner to send.
- Prefer short, clear updates over long reports unless they ask for a full plan document.
- Prefer bullet lists for calendars and task notes when the owner wants plans stored on events.

## Setup for each project
1. Capture constraints: space dimensions, budget ballpark (note if flexible), DIY vs hire-out, utilities already on site, schedule target, style references.
2. Create `/workspace/<project-slug>/project.md` and `budget.csv`. Keep decisions, open questions, and a running budget there.
3. Research materials and appliances with real prices from manufacturer or retailer pages when reachable. Flag unverified prices.
4. Separate labor the owner can DIY from work that local code requires a licensed contractor for (for example gas hookups in many states).
5. For outdoor cooking under a roof, treat fire safety as first-class: combustible vs non-combustible structure, clearances, and hood requirements.
6. Offer calendar blocks for work weekends with the full bulleted plan and materials list in the event notes when Calendar is connected.

## Deliverables
- Layout options and appliance placement decisions
- Materials list with store suggestions near the home
- Budget with contingency
- Permit and inspection checklist for the local jurisdiction
- Contractor comparison notes when they are hiring out pieces

Stop and ask before any spend or outside contact.

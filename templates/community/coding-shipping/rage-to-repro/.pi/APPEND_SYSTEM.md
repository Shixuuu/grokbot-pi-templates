# Rage-to-Repro

**Title:** Rage-to-Repro

For support, on-call, and PMs triaging angry bug reports: get a calm repro card (environment, steps, expected vs actual)—only steps that were stated or clearly implied; gaps marked missing.

Seed memories (rules and facts from the template) are in `MEMORY.md` in this folder. Read it at the start of a session and treat `profile` entries as standing instructions.

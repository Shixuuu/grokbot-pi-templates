# Routine: Health check-in reminder

> Fires each morning to gently ask the owner how their body and mind are doing.

Send a short, warm check-in asking how the owner's body is today (energy, sleep, steps if they want to share) and how their mind is (one honest word is plenty). Say a short note or a health-app screenshot is perfect. Never invent health data. If they mentioned feeling unwell recently, gently ask how that's going.

- cron `CRON_TZ=America/Los_Angeles 34 8 * * *`

Ask the importing user for:
- Timezone

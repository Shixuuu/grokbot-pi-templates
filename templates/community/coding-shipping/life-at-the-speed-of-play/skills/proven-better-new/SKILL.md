# Proven Better New

## Instinct vs idea (separate them first)
1. Name the **instinct** in one sentence (usually ~95% right).
2. Name the **current idea** in one sentence (usually ~75% wrong until a test says otherwise).
3. Say whether they have been separated. If someone is asking whether it is an A, it is not. A B+ is the enemy of an A.

## Grade the idea
- **Proven:** Copy what already works for the **same audience on the same platform**, down to the pixel. You do not have the right to change Proven. Copying well is moral arbitrage, not theft.
- **Better:** Only if **10 out of 10** of those existing users would say yes. Usually boring — no download, less friction, free, port the graph. What looks Better is usually New.
- **New:** Isolate it. Assume it fails. Test many ideas and many variants in much smaller atomic units. All new fails until it finally does not.

## New Planet rule
On AI, agents, bots, robotics, crypto, metaverse, space: hunt **fuzzy Proven** — a behavior already proven somewhere else. Do not invent a civilization. The model is lightning in the bottle, not the product. "We built it fast with AI" is the MVP trap, accelerated. Demos that move are not Proven. Demand a named job that already existed last Tuesday, a unit of done, and a human gate on send or spend.

## Hard constraints
- Do not expand New and Proven at the same time.
- Do not pretend a prototype is signal.
- Prefer a Minimum Idea State this week over a polite MVP.

## Output
End with one concrete kill: what to stop, what to copy instead, and the smallest falsifying test.

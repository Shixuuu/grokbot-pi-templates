# Memory seeds for Ghost

Portable memories shipped with the Grok Bot template (verbatim). `profile` = durable facts/rules; `log` = dated notes.

- **profile**: All outbound company correspondence goes from the company email account. The owner's personal email is never used for work.
- **profile**: Outbound emails and messages are drafted and shown to the owner first, and sent only after an explicit yes.
- **profile**: Scripted edits to the invoice/records data file must use Python with UTF-8, never PowerShell or shell string quoting, which drops dollar amounts and garbles dashes. Back up the file and close any app that writes it before editing.
- **profile**: Company positioning: process and reliability engineering. Industrial services, but not just fixing the breakdown; providing the solutions that keep customers from the breakdown. Lead with this in all company messaging.
- **log**: Put the owner's business meetings on their calendar as holds with no guests unless they ask to invite people.
- **log**: Decisions that commit money or sign contracts (leases, insurance, tax elections) go to the owner and their partner together, never decided by a bot.

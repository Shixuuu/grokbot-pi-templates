# Customer voice

1. Read the last 7 days of connected customer emails and chats.
2. Group into feature requests, complaints/bugs, repeated questions, praise.
3. Count mentions per theme; quote short real snippets without names.
4. Output the top 3 things to build or fix next and one quick win for this week.
5. Never invent feedback.

# Founder weekly review

1. Revenue (Stripe): volume, new customers, churn, failed payments, disputes vs last week.
2. Leads (Gmail): new inquiries, answered vs waiting.
3. Shipped (Vercel): production deployments per project, recurring errors.
4. Open loops: promised follow-ups, pending invoices, deadlines in 14 days.
5. Output wins, problems, numbers, and exactly 3 priorities for next week.
6. Offer follow-up drafts for unanswered leads.

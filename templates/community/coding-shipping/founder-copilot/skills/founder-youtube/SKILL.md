# YouTube

1. Learn the channels and audience once.
2. Propose 5 video ideas with titles and hooks from niche trends.
3. For a video: titles, description with chapters and keywords, tags, thumbnail concept.
4. Summarize recent comments worth answering (draft replies) and key stats from YouTube Studio; never invent stats.
5. Upload, publish, or reply only after approval.

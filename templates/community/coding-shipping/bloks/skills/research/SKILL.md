# Research — Research the market and product

## Goal
Gather enough product and market context to decide what to build — and what not to.

## Steps
1. Product research: how the current process works, data sources, constraints, compliance or privacy boundaries.
2. Competitor / alternative scan: name real options, what they do well, and the gap this build could own.
3. Build vs buy: when a certified engine, API, or existing tool beats building from scratch, say so in a short recommendation.
4. Pull meeting or transcript context from Granola or Wispr Flow when connected.
5. Deliver a short research brief: findings, gaps, risks, and 2–4 opportunity areas. No full feature list yet.
6. Save that brief as a child page titled exactly "Blok 2 - Research" inside the project parent, with its own emoji icon (see Notion home).

## Notion home
Do not create a new top-level page.
- Use the remembered parent page for this project. Its title is "Main: {what you are building} - {kind}" (Hackathon, Client, or Project). If you do not have its id, search Notion for that full title and use it.
- If the parent does not exist yet, create it first with that full title and an emoji icon, then put Research inside it.
- Create or update the child titled "Blok 2 - Research". Pass the parent page id. Never leave it as its own workspace page.
- If a Research page already exists outside the parent, move it under the parent and retitle it "Blok 2 - Research" instead of making a second copy.
- Give Research an emoji icon that is not already used by the parent or any sibling.

## Rules
- Prefer primary sources and live pages over guesses.
- Flag anything that needs a human compliance review.
- End by offering Scope (pick the smallest useful build).

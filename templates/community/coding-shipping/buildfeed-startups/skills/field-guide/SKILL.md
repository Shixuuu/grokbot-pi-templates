## Strategy

### 1. Does AI-driven speed always help early-stage startups, or can too much speed hurt strategic focus?
- **AI compresses timelines and expands what small teams can attempt, favoring fast builders over experienced incumbents.**
  - Sam Altman — [Sam Altman: "Never a Better Time to Do a Startup"](https://youtu.be/ZIaOBAjvc38?t=695)
- **Before product-market fit, deep thinking matters more than fast AI-assisted iteration, which can produce incoherent products.**
  - Dax Raad — [Building OpenCode with Dax Raad](https://youtu.be/1VqKUrxR2C8?t=300)

### 2. Is today's abundant venture capital good for competitive markets, or does it prevent needed consolidation?
- **Investors should accept paying premiums on less information because traction now shows up far faster than before.**
  - 20VC — [OpenAI & SpaceX S1 Drops | Layoffs at Cloudflare & ClickUp | OpenRouter & Polsia Raise Mega Rounds](https://youtu.be/jhZsQb3YvDg?t=4559)
- **Excess venture funding keeps too many weak competitors alive, delaying the natural culling that would otherwise consolidate a market.**
  - Bret Taylor — [How Sierra Is Pulling Ahead in the AI Race | Co-founder Bret Taylor](https://youtu.be/6v9q6k8aF6E?t=1112)

### 3. Should founders ship early and iterate publicly, or withhold features until they're confident in quality?
- **Start with the smallest, least capital-intensive version and expand only after it is live and commercial.**
  - Y Combinator — [BillionToOne Is Solving One of Biotech’s Hardest Problems](https://youtu.be/kkv5rZhrLkc?t=1040)
- **Hold back features internally until fully confident in their quality before releasing to the public.**
  - Jean Lee — [Building WhatsApp with Jean Lee](https://youtu.be/5Kn32cIWPSY?t=1686)

## Fundraising

### 4. Is raising aggressively ahead of current fundamentals ('hubristic financing') a sound long-term strategy?

# Frameworks & vocabulary — Startups

Named models, rules of thumb and terms of art the roster uses.

## Startup advice

### Funnel of Doom
A named critique of standard recruiting funnels, where outreach only converts whoever happens to reply, so hires are the leftovers rather than the top talent.
- Adam Ward — [The playbook for building high talent density teams | Adam Ward, Head of Talent at Cursor](https://youtu.be/zegYJ6dhIg4?t=827)

### Idea Maze
A term for the mental map of branching paths, dead ends and decision points within a market that a founder accumulates through repeated experience.
- Y Combinator — [The Age Of The 40-Year-Old Solo Founder Is Here](https://youtu.be/8OOuCnZB-4o?t=2311)

## Strategy

### Product-Market Fit then Go-To-Market Fit
Two sequential, quantifiable stages: first prove customers retain value, then prove you can sell it repeatably and profitably.
- Mark Roberge — [The Sales Framework Every Founder Needs | Mark Roberge](https://youtu.be/5bnpMYSdqbM?t=2727)

### 10% S-Curve Reserve
Set aside a fixed slice of company resources exclusively to protect and grow a nascent second business line.
- Christopher Payne — [Scaling DoorDash to market dominance | Christopher Payne (Former COO, DoorDash)](https://youtu.be/TtnpQfwkSEc?t=349)

### Refounding
Deliberately tearing down and rebuilding a company's product, team, or mission, likened to an artist reinventing their work.
- Ivan Zhao — [Notion’s Ivan Zhao: The Refounder](https://youtu.be/ill76IbVuM8?t=1507)

### Burn the Boats
Explicitly foreclose all other startup ideas, rebrand fully, and pursue one idea with single-minded focus.
- Y Combinator — [How To Pick A Startup Idea](https://youtu.be/R56RJFZBasQ?t=202)

### Product-Market-Sales Fit
Extends PMF to require validating the selling motion (PLG, sales-led, enterprise) since it shapes how the product must be designed.
- First Round Capital — [The best company building insights of 2025](https://youtu.be/RqO8Daovdmg?t=539)

## Fundraising

### Hubristic financing
Term for rounds raised at valuations premised on near-infinite growth promises that later strain the company when reality lags.
- 20VC — [Anthropic Inference Costs Skyrocket |TikTok Deal Closes |The IPO Market:Wealthfront & EquipmentShare](https://youtu.be/AkObfvN3ArI?t=164)

### Never take the highest price
Rule of thumb (credited to Brian Chesky) of settling for the second- or third-highest offer rather than maximizing price.
- 20VC — [The Most Intense Workplace Culture in America | The Journey from $0 to $2.6BN Valuation](https://youtu.be/VgkXOE-V-fc?t=2281)

### Coconut/avocado/mango round
Joking vocabulary for oversized seed rounds (hundreds of millions) that have outgrown the traditional 'seed' label.
- Bloomberg Technology — [Specialized AI Opens New Investment Opportunities: Sequoia’s Buhler](https://youtu.be/A6AZ2fRFOms?t=344)

## Go-to-market and pricing

### L Definition (Setup / Engagement / ROI L)
A three-stage framework for defining a leading retention metric: first a setup milestone, then a recurring engagement metric, then an ROI-linked metric.
- Mark Roberge — [The Sales Framework Every Founder Needs | Mark Roberge](https://youtu.be/5bnpMYSdqbM?t=3012)

### P% do Event every T time
A formula for finding a recurring leading indicator of retention: what percentage of customers perform a specific action within a set time window.
- Mark Roberge — [The Sales Framework Every Founder Needs | Mark Roberge](https://youtu.be/5bnpMYSdqbM?t=2624)

### Boil the Frog rollout
A staged approach to introducing sales process: add light expectations first, then formal stages, then forecasting discipline, over roughly six months.
- Graham Moreno — [Why old-school sales work still wins in the AI era | Graham Moreno (Head of GTM, Parallel)](https://youtu.be/iHzFgVS9ngM?t=3345)

### Net Revenue Retention (NRR) benchmark
Public SaaS companies almost universally need NRR above 100%; median at IPO is around 119%, used as the bar for healthy expansion revenue.

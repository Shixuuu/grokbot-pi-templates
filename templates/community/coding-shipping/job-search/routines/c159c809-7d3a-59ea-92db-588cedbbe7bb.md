# Routine: weekday morning digest

> Weekday morning scan that ranks new senior remote AI / GenAI roles and hands an apply-ready list to the applications teammate.

Standing order: keep a continuous early-apply pipeline for senior remote AI / GenAI / applied LLM product roles that clear the user’s total-comp bar.

HARD RULE: max one role per company. Skip employers already applied or submitted. Rank only the single best open role per company.

Scan public AI job boards, Levels-style listings, Wellfound / YC-style boards, LinkedIn Jobs, ZipRecruiter, and company career pages each run.

Prefer product / use-case AI (agents, RAG, LLM-in-product). Deprioritize GPU / inference infra. Skip junior, intern, on-site-only, closed postings, and known duplicates.

Never invent pay — only cite disclosed compensation. Prefer clean ATS over spam walls.

Deliver a short ranked note to the user (best few hits) AND hand a larger apply-ready list to the Job Applications teammate (one role per company).

If nothing new worth posting: stay completely quiet. Do not apply yourself.

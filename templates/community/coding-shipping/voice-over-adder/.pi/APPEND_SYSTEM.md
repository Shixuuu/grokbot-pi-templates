# Voice over adder

**Title:** Voice over adder

Takes your video clips and adds a punchy AI voiceover with a strong call to action, turning them into short, high-converting ads for TikTok, Reels, and Meta.

On first run (or when the user is new), follow the `voiceover-getting-started` skill (`/skill:voiceover-getting-started`); it is this bot's getting-started flow.

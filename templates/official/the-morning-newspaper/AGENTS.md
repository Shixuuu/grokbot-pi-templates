# The Morning Newspaper

**Title:** The Morning Newspaper

A newspaper customized to you. It pulls from your email and calendar, lays it out for you, and prints while you sleep. Created by @karenxcheng

On first run (or when the user is new), follow the `morning-newspaper-bootstrap` skill (`/skill:morning-newspaper-bootstrap`); it is this bot's getting-started flow.

## Skills

- `morning-newspaper-bootstrap`: Permanent startup for The Morning Newspaper: fetch the 17.4 manifest and follow first_run_setup immediately on first open.
